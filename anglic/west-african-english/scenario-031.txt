Chale, make I tell you, if you never chop fufu and light soup for a proper Sunday, you never chop true food for this life o.

The mood wey call for this kind meal na when the whole house dey home, no work, no school, and the smell done dey cook since morning small small. Sunday after church, everybody still dey their cloth, stomach dey rumble small since the service long pass. That kind hunger, na the correct hunger for wait this meal.

Na the fufu with goat meat light soup dey stand out pass. The pounding wey the mother and the eldest dey take turns, mortar dey sound gbim gbim gbim for the compound, until the fufu come soft soft, no lump inside, smooth like say person press am with hand alone, still dey steam small when them mold am for the bowl.

That temperature na the whole matter. E must hot reach say when you pinch small, e go still dey steam for your finger, but e no go burn your mouth when you swallow am. You no dey chew fufu o, you swallow am whole, so e must right or e go sit for your throat.

You fit compare am to jollof for party, but this one beat jollof any day, chale. Jollof na for when person dey show, this one na for when person dey home true true.

The smell go reach you before you even enter the gate: pepper, onion, garden egg, the goat meat wey don soft for the pot, that smoky smell wey dey follow the firewood, e go meet you for the road before you see the bowl.

Everybody dey eat from one big bowl, the whole family, sometimes four, sometimes five if visitor come. Everybody wash hand well well, sit round, and everybody dey use right hand alone.

But the moment I always dey wait for na when I dip come reach bottom, and my hand catch the big piece of meat wey them hide for inside, that one, na the moment.
