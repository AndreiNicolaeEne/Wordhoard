Me: Make I tell yo the whole thing, from how e start this morning self.

I wake before cock crow, my belly wa turn small-small. I di dress, I di undress, I di dress back again, my hand dem wa shake small. I no know wetin go meet me for that place, I just di pray make God carry me safe reach there, no wahala.

I reach the gate before seven, the compound still quiet quiet. Then one big man come out from the office, na him bi the oga for the whole section. He shake my hand well-well, he say "welcome-o, we glad say yo join us today." Straight so he hand me over one desk, say make I start sort the files, arrange them by date. I go tell yo true, I wa think say na small-small work dem go give me first day, but no-o, e ova plenty pass wetin I brace for. Paper everywhere, and the man say make I finish before lunch self.

As I di rush so, I mistake, I put one important paper for wrong place, and one senior see am, e call the whole office attention make dem come check. I shame small-small, but the oga just laugh, e say "take-time, take-time, nobody born knowing," e no vex at all.

For break, I di size the rest of dem workers quiet quiet, who friendly, who serious. Na one junior girl come meet me, she no wait make I ask am, she just show me husay the good file dem dey, and how the printer di jam if you rush am. She sef sorry for me true true, she say make I no fear, everybody dae pass there before.

The work self di move faster pass wetin I brace for, no rest reach, nine hour dae go like nothing at all, my leg dem di pain me when I di go home.

All the way I di walk, I di replay everything for my head, the mistake, the junior girl kindness, the oga laugh.

The mother: So how e be, my pikin, dem people good-o?

Me: Mama, the work na plenty, but the people bi good. I go manage am.

The father: Na so, sorry for the stress today, sha.

Me: Thank you, Pa. Tomorrow go better, God willing.
