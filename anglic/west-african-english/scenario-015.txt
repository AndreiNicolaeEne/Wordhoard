My love,

I no fit hold this thing again, so make I just talk am plain: I love you, true true, no be small love.

I dey remember one evening, nothing special dey happen. We just dey sit for your veranda after the rain don pass, and you dey peel orange for me small small, quiet quiet. Na there I sabi say this thing don pass friendship. No party, no big day. Just you dey peel orange and me dey watch your hand, and something for my chest turn like that.

You know how you dey rub your left ear anytime somebody vex you, before you even open mouth? Nobody go notice unless dem dey close to you like me. That thing dey make me smile anytime I see am, even when you dey vex with me sef.

Since you come my life, my days don change. The small-small things I used to pass by, I dey notice dem now, because I dey imagine how I go tell you. But when you no dey near, chale, the days dey heavy for me pass how I fit explain. I dey miss you well-well, no be small thing at all.

I never tell anybody this one, but sometimes I dey fear say maybe I no dey enough for you, that one day all my wahala go tire you. I dey write am here because paper no go judge me for am.

But make I promise you one thing, no be empty talk: whatever come, I go dey stand for your side. We go build this thing small small until na permanent, until na solid ground. That one na my word, I no go shift for am.

Make I stop here before I write whole book for you.

Yours, my own.
