Me: Senior, that roof, e don beat me now o. The wahala dey worse for the corner husay the bed stay, right behind the kitchen. That corner bi always the wettest one; small rain, e just drip small-small, but when the big rain fall, water ova-run the whole floor, e go soak everything.

The friend: Ehn, sorry o, my friend. Wetin the landlord say when you first raise am?

Me: The first time, e wa tell me make I wait small, e go send man come fix am. I na ask am the second time already, same answer e wa give me: "next week, next week." E never send nobody sef, e just dey chop the rent and leave the roof so.

The friend: So both time e promise you the same excuse, e no so? How long exactly this thing dey go, and wetin e really tell you?

Me: E don be four month plus, since June self. Both time e wa promise the same thing, say e go fix am "small time", but e no fit come near the house sef.

The friend: OK, listen me well. You get three road here. One, you stop pay the rent till e fix am, risky, e fit use it throw yo whole family commot. Two, you call man yosef come patch the corner and knock the money off the rent, abeg, no need dash am make e fix wetin e suppose fix already, but if e no gree with the price, e go say you spoil him property. Three, you carry the matter go the community leader make dem call am, sit am down, make e hear wetin people talk.

Me: Hmm. Which one you go pick, if na you?

The friend: Sha, make I tell you my own. My landlord bin do me the same way one time, promise, promise, till the whole ceiling don collapse for my sitting room. I no wait for am again, I just carry man come fix am, keep the receipt well-well, and knock am off the rent straight. E vex small, but e no fit say anything because I get paper for everything.

Me: That one sound better pass to just dey wait for am.

The friend: Abeg, make we do that one. Write am the letter first, so if e vex, you get your proof. Take-time, but that na the safest road.

Me: OK, senior. We go do so.
