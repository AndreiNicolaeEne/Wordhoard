Oh God, na you I dey call dis evenin time.
I dey thank you fo dis day, how di work wa go fine, no bad ting no come near me.
I dey hold my ol ma close to my heart tonight, my sweet ma, kip her strong-o.
One ting I dey ask you: abeg mek tomorrow road smooth small-small, no leh wahala meet me.
Dis na my prayer, oh God. I don finish-o. Amen.
