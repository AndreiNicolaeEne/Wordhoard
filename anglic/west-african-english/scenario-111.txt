Chale, where dis boy dey sef? He talk say by eight he go reach house, straight eight, no be story. I dey look the wall clock now and na eleven e don pass, e dey creep small-small to twelve. So na three hours plus wey the boy don owe me, three hours abeg, and person no even fit call to greet me.

Maybe na the trotro delay am. Traffic for Kaneshie side dey mad this time of night, or the car don break down for road, that one dey happen too, e no strange. Make I no worry myself for nothing sha.

But I no fit sidon well-well. I dey stand, I dey sidon, I dey stand again. I go check the window, nothing dey there, only the street lamp dey blink, then I go check the door like say my checking self go bring am quick quick.

Ei! Then the bad thing wey I no wan think, e just waka enter my mind: wetin if accident don happen for road? God forbid o, I no even wan hear my own mouth talk am, but e don enter now, e no wan comot.

I remember the time wey my junior brother bin do this same thing, years back, whole house dey wait like this one, and when e come back the person dey fine, only say the taxi bin get flat tyre for the way. Make I hold that one for heart, make e comfort me small, even as the fear still dey sit for background.

I want carry my slippers go outside, go stand for junction make I see road well, but I no go, not yet, I go tell myself again. That boy, he never come. Make I wait small more.

Now na past four hours. Four hours, chale. My chest dey tight like something dey siddon on top am.

A sound for outside, footstep, my heart jump go my mouth. Na nothing, na cat wey knock the bucket for gutter side.

Then real key dey turn for the gate.

Me: Chale, wetin took you so?

The son: Sorry o, phone bin off since, the car break down for road.

Me: Thank God o, thank God say you well. But wahala dey wait you o, you hear me? Next time make you call small, abi you wan kill me with this kind worry?
