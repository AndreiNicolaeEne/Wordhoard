My friend, no vex make I yarn you small, abeg. I know say na you dey grieve now, na your own turn, and I no wan make e be like say I dey take the moment come put my own inside. But since you ask me how the whole thing be for me, make I tell you true-true, no add water for am, because I done pass that road already and I no go lie for you.

The first days, ehh, e no even feel like anything sotay. Na numb. People di come, people di go, food di enter the house, one aunty di arrange this, one uncle di arrange that, phone di ring every minute, and you di just answer, you di nod, you di shake hand with person wey you no even sabi well, but the reality of the whole matter never even reach your mind. Your body di do the work but your mind never sabi say the person don go. Wahala of arrangement na him occupy the space wey suppose be for feeling something direct. No time to sidon feel anything, because too many hand dey for your head.

Na the first time the house empty, that one na the thing wey I no fit forget. Everybody don pack their motor, go home, the church people, the neighbors, senior and junior wey come show face, all of them don comot. And na that time the silence land, heavy heavy, like load wey person carry put for your chest. No knock for door again, no phone di ring, na you and that silence, face to face, and I di tell you, that silence na him first show me say the thing real, real-real.

For the weeks wey follow, my body come strange sotay. I no fit chop. People go vex, dem go say make I chop something, but the food no wan enter, e go sotay evening reach and nothing never pass my mouth since morning. Sleep sef come strange same way. I no di sleep whole night again, na small-small, broken pieces. I go wake for midnight, my eye go just open by itself for three for morning, no reason, no dream, nothing, just wake. Sleep wey suppose be whole thing come be like torn cloth, patch here, patch there.

The first month na the one wey pain me well-well, because the world no wait for anybody o. People di go work, di go market, di di laugh for road, everybody di march front, but for inside me, time neva even move sef. Na like say the clock stop only for where I dey, and everybody else don pass go ahead. That kind feeling fit vex person pass anything, to see life continue as if nothing happen.

I remember one ordinary Sunday, nothing mark am, nobody put am for any calendar, jus common Sunday like any other, and that Sunday na him catch me finish. I no even sabi why that particular day of all day, no warning, no build-up, e jus land like fresh news and I break down proper, as if say na that same morning dem tell me.

Even reach some months later, small thing fit still trigger you. One smell, perfume, cloth wey still carry the person scent small, or even one voice for radio wey resemble their own, e go jus grab you anyhow. No pattern, no way to predict am, e go come like wave, knock you flat, then e go pass, leave you there dey breathe hard.

The middle part, my friend, na fog, real fog. Even the days wey suppose sweet you, e dey dull small, like say you dey watch your own life from outside glass. You di go work, you di laugh with people, you di do everything wey person suppose do, but inside na cloud, e no clear at all.

One person come tell me one time, "you go soon forget, time go heal am", as if I dey wait to forget the person! That kind talk pain me pass anything, e pain me sotay I nearly vex for the person for real. But one next person, wey no even close to me reach, na my senior for work, she jus dash me one plate of food one evening, no long talk, she jus knock, say, "I know say you no get mind to cook today," drop the food, comot. That small small thing, e helep me pass any big grammar wey people talk for that period.

When the anniversary near reach, weeks before, fear catch me proper. I no sabi how I go survive that day at all, I di dread am small-small every day wey pass. But the day come, and I jus manage am, however I fit, no beautiful, no organize, I jus waka through am anyhow, and when evening reach, I still dey stand, still dey breathe. That na the whole point, sha, say you pass am, no matter how clumsy the passing be.

Small small my body come normal back. The appetite no return for one day, e come one week better, one week bad again, up and down like that. Sleep sef the same, e no come back whole in one night, e jus dey build itself small small, brick by brick.

One day I catch myself dey laugh, real laugh, from my belly, and just like that, guilt come grab my neck from behind, as if I no get right to laugh again since the person don go. That guilt na strong thing o, e follow the laugh like im own shadow.

By the time the year end reach, I di carry the thing different way, lighter for some corner, heavy still for another corner, but e neva go completely, e neva go anywhere at all. E jus siddon quiet for one side of my heart, and I don learn how to carry am without am ova-weighing me every single day like before.

So make I tell you plain, my friend: e no go hard like this forever. E go loosen, small small, but nobody get fix time for when e go loosen, no be like say after so many months you go just dey fine, e no work like that at all. So no ova-compare your year with anybody yeah own, not your neighbor, not your workmate, not even with mine. Your own grief go take the road wey e wan take, and that na enough, abi? Take-time, my friend. Take your time.
