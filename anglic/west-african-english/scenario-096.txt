Everybody dey quiet, dey wait, nobody wan talk first. My throat dey tight small before the words fit come out. This one, everybody wey stand here go recognize am one time: how him dey laugh loud pass everybody for the whole area. Chale, that na the word I always dey call you. Rest well o, my friend, we no go forget you here. I open my hand, make the sand fall small small, e don finish.
