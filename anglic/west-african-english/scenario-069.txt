My cat dey missing three days now. Chale, abeg if you see am tell me sharp. She be small grey one, white for the chest, one ear dey bend small for the tip. Last I see her na near the gutter, evening time. She no shy for stranger o, she go rub for your leg. Whoever bring her come, small something dey for you. Number dey for the strip below, tear one carry go.
