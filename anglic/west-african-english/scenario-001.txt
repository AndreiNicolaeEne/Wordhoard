That evening the pain start small, and me I dey think na normal thing, na so pregnant woman dey feel am those last weeks, I no gree take am serious. My wife she just dey shift for the chair, dey rub her back, and I dey tell am make she rest, sha, e go pass. But by nine, ten o'clock, the thing don change, e no be that small-small pain again, e dey come like wave, come go, come go, and na so I know say today na today, this one no be practice again.

We call around, my sister don carry the first one go stay for her place since morning, so na just me and my wife for the house that night, the two of us, waiting. I dey pace up and down the parlour, I no fit sit down well-well, my heart dey do double-double. Around one, two for the morning, things turn hard proper, my wife dey hold my hand tight, dey shout, and abeg I no know wetin to do again, I just dey pray, dey rub her waist, dey tell her make she hold on small.

Na around that time the woman we call come reach, the one wey sabi this work, she come meet us for the middle of everything, and immediately she take charge, she calm us small-small. She dey talk to my wife like sister, dey encourage her, "push, push, make you no fear."

The last hour was the hardest one, my wife don tire well-well but she no stop, she just dey push, dey push, and then everywhere quiet, no sound at all, just some seconds wey feel like forever, I dey hold my breath, my chest tight. Then the cry come, small voice but strong, and na so relief pour for my body.

Before one minute pass, they don put the baby for my hand, my second child, warm and crying, and I just dey look am, dey thank God, my hand dey shake sef.
