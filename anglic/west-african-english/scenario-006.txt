The older one: Come here small, make I explain the game well well before the rest of them reach o, so you no go dey lost.

The younger one: Okay o, I dey listen.

The older one: First thing, na minimum five of us we need before anybody fit start, if we no up to five the game no go sweet, everybody go just dey stand like mumu.

The younger one: Five, okay. And where the line dey?

The older one: You see that gutter side, na there be one boundary, and that mango tree wey dey opposite, na there be the other one. Anything wey pass that mango tree, you don comot from field, no more inside there.

The younger one: And who go be the chaser first?

The older one: We go do am like this, everybody go put one leg for the middle, I go count, the last person wey remove leg, na him go chase. No wahala, na fair fair thing.

The younger one: If the person wey dey run comot outside the line small small, wetin dey happen?

The older one: Once you cross that boundary, you don disqualify yourself, you no fit come back, you just siddon watch until the round finish. So make you no dey too greedy to run far.

The younger one: And this tag, how e go be? If the chaser just touch my cloth, that one don do?

The older one: No no, e must touch your body proper, your hand, your shoulder, anywhere sef, but cloth no count, cloth dey too loose. Once e touch you like that, you don freeze, you no fit move again until another person wey still dey run reach come tap you loose.

The younger one: So the round go end when?

The older one: The round go end when everybody don freeze, and the last person wey the chaser catch, na him go become chaser for the next round.

The younger one: Okay, I dey ready.

The older one: No worry, this first round, if you miss anything, e no go count against you at all. Just enjoy am.
