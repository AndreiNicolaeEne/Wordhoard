The auntie: Abeg make we yan riddle. Weti di waka but e no get leg?

The nephew: E fit be wind?

The auntie: No be wind, my pikin, na shadow, e di waka anytime sun show face.

The nephew: Ehen. My turn: weti get key but e no fit open door?

The auntie: Na padlock?

The nephew: No be padlock, na piano sef! You sabi well-well o.

The auntie: Abeg one more, or your head don tire small-small?

The nephew: My head don do work o, make we rest, next time we go continue.
