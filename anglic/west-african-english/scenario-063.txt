I na tell you dis one from the start so you go know how e really go, because people wa hear the end part and think we just wake up and be friend again, like magic. E no go so.

Dat morning the wind wa blow the whole night, roof zinc dey rattle like somebody dey fight up dey, and when day clean I open the back door and my heart drop small. The fence between me and the neighbor own bi flat, flat-flat on the ground, wood split, wire twist up like something vex chop am. And right on top the whole mess, one big tree, roots and all, lay down cross the line like e tire and just decide to rest there.

I walk go look husay the tree root stand before, and no lie, the root bi for the neighbor side, small small over the line but bi for their side true-true. That tree wa lean for long time, I no dey make story. Anybody who pass dat road for morning walk sef go tell you dat tree wa lean toward my yard since God knows when. So I no surprise when e come down, only I surprise say e take dis long.

I go knock the neighbor gate dat same morning, because wetin better pass to settle small thing before e grow big. First thing the neighbor tell me, before I even finish greet am proper:

The neighbor: Sorry-o for your fence, but you know na the wind do dis one. I no get hand in it. Tree fall na tree fall, na God business dat.

Me: I hear you, but dat tree wa lean since way back. E no just fall today because wind blow today. Wind only push wetin already ready to fall.

The neighbor: Wind dey blow every year for dis country, e no mean say every tree suppose fall. Dis one just be accident.

I no vex quick quick, but I no gree with dat one either. And right dey so, my own wife, she wa stand by the door, she come say the thing wey settle am for me:

My wife: You forget say I tell your madam last rainy season, when the tree first start to bend, say make she call person come check am before e cause trouble? She nod her head dat time, she say she go see to it. Nobody see to nothing.

The neighbor's wife stand quiet small, then she no deny am, she just look ground. Dat one alone tell me say the warning bi real, e no be something I invent for argument sake.

So now the quarrel move from whose fault to who go pay. The neighbor say make we split the cost half-half, but only for wood, not for the wire, because "wire na small thing." I say no, if we go split, we go split everything, fence bi fence, e no get wood-price and wire-price for dis kind matter. Neighbor say I dey ova-do the thing, say I dey make small trouble big. I say na dem dey ova-relax on top warning wey somebody give dem already.

We stand for two, three day like dat, neither side wan back down first. Yo know how e go for our place, once person back down first, person go feel say dem lost, like say the whole town go talk say "oh, na dat one fold." So we both dey hold hard, greeting still dey happen but e cold-cold, no warmth for it.

Na so Old Pa from down the road, the one wey build house for dis area before any of us born sef, e come stand by the fallen tree one evening, unask, nobody call am. He just dey pass, he see the mess, he stop, he look the tree, he look me, he look the neighbor side, he shake e head small.

Old Pa: Make una hear me small. Dis tree, e no ask permission before e grow on the line, and e no ask permission before e fall either. So how una go fight like say person plan dis thing? Make una hear wetin I say: the man wey own the ground wey the root stand, make he handle the tree part, wetin be timber, wey e fit sell small or use for firewood, dat one na him own to clear. And the fence itself, since e serve both side equal, make una split am equal, materials and labor together, no wood-price, no wire-price, just one price. Dat way, nobody dey pay for "wind fault," nobody dey pay for "who neglect," una just dey pay for una own fence wey both a una dey use.

Nobody talk for small time. Then the neighbor nod, slow slow, and I nod too. E wasn't say either side win, e wasn't say either side lost. Old Pa just find road wey both a we fit walk down without shame. Dat kind wisdom, e no cheap-o.

So we start the work dat Saturday. Early morning, both household come out, but I go tell you straight, the first hour or two, e stiff well-well. We dey work side by side, dragging branch, cutting am small small with the cutlass, but nobody dey talk beyond wetin the work require. "Pass dat one." "Hold am dey." Like dat. No joke, no laugh, just work.

Round midday, the neighbor wife bring out food, rice and soup, enough for both household, she never wait nobody ask am. She just carry am come, set am for the small table under the mango tree, and say make everybody chop before the sun finish person. Dat one soft the thing small. And when we start back the fence itself, my own boy hand the neighbor son the hammer before he even ask for am, because he see the man dey struggle with the nail. Small thing, but e mean something, small gesture like dat.

By the time we finish, we don dey laugh small, dey tell story about other thing, weather, market price, nothing to do with the fence anymore. The new fence, when we finish am, e stand sturdier pass the old one sef, because we use better post dis time, we dig deeper, we no rush am like the first one wey person build quick quick way back.

Now, how thing stand between the two household? I go say e better pass before, not just even. Before, we wa just greet-greet for gate, nothing more. Now the wife dem dey borrow pepper from one another, the children dem dey play together for the yard wey the fence separate. We still get our own line, our own side, but e no dey feel like line wey dey keep person out. E just dey mark wetin bi wetin. And when wind blow strong again, as e go blow again one day, I no fear say we go fight again. We done learn wetin fence really be for.
