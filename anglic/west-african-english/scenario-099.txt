The one: Wait, wait... na you dis? I no dey see well o, make I look you well well.

The other: Na me sef, my friend! Long time no see, abi? I dey pass now when I see you, I say make I no just waka pass you like dat.

The one: You for don waka pass, I for run come catch you self. How you dey now, body dey kampe?

The other: Body dey inside cloth, no wahala. Aw di work, aw di family, everybody dey fine?

The one: We dey manage, we dey push am small small. You self, you dey work for dat place still, or you don change?

The other: I don change o. Dat place, dem chop all our sweat no gree pay overtime, so I comot since. I dey do my own small small business now.

The one: Ehen, dat one better pass. As long as e dey put food for table, na dat one matter.

The other: True talk. So wetin bring you dis side today?

The one: Nating o, I just dey pass, I see person wey resemble you, I say make I confam.

The other: I glad say you confam am, sha. Look how time dey waka. Na like yesterday we dey wear our school uniform, abi?

The one: My friend, no remind me. As you talk school so, my mind don gallop go back go meet dat teacher.

The other: Which teacher you dey call now, abi na dat one everybody used to fear?

The one: Na him sef. Just to hear person mention am, my whole body dey tense small till now.

The other: (laughs) Dat teacher no be small ting o. If you open your mouth to talk while e dey teach, na wahala full package.

The one: Na so. E get one rule wey e no gree bend for anybody: if e dey teach and you make even small noise, forget am, you don kill yourself.

The other: And di punishment, you no go forget am even if you try. "I must not talk when the teacher is teaching." One hundred times, every single time, no negotiation.

The one: Every time o! Not once, not two times, na hundred. Your hand go swell finish before you reach seventy.

The other: I remember say I used to count am for exercise book, one, two, three, going all di way, my hand dey shake finish.

The one: And you remember di line e used to tell us, almost word for word? "Una tink say una mama send una come here to play? I no be una mate, make una hear me well: silence na gold, and una go pay for una gold with una own hand."

The other: (laughs loud) Ah, na exact di same ting e used to talk! You still get am for your head like say na yesterday.

The one: How I go forget am. Dat voice still dey ring for my ear till now.

The other: You remember dat day wey dat boy get punishment for cheating for exam, di whole class dey watch from window?

The one: I no fit forget dat one, sha. Teacher carry di boy comot, make e kneel down for di gravel outside, under di sun, holding two block, one for each hand.

The other: And we all dey look from window, nobody dey talk, everybody just dey pray say make e no be we next.

The one: Di boy face wen e come back inside, e no fit even sidon well, e dey shake small small. We all dey feel am for we own body sef.

The other: I still remember how I used to freeze anytime e call my name for question. My mouth go just lock like padlock, even if I sabi di answer.

The one: You self? I taya for freeze o. My leg go just dey shake under di desk, my heart dey beat like drum.

The other: But you remember di one time dat one boy talk back to am and nating come happen? Till now I no fit believe am.

The one: Ah, dat one! Teacher ask am one question, di boy just answer am cheeky, e even talk say "Sir, na you sef teach us wrong di last time." Everybody just freeze, we dey wait for wahala to burst.

The other: And instead, teacher just look am, e even smile small, e no touch am at all! We all dey ask ourself, na wetin dis one mean, how dis boy waka comot free like dat.

The one: Till today, if you tell person dat story, dem no go believe you, dem go say you dey lie.

The other: Real amazement, sha. We used to talk say dat boy get person for spirit protecting am.

The one: (laughs) You remember di habit wey e get, wey we used to mock behind e back?

The other: Wetin, di cane wey e name?

The one: Yes o! "Enter and Win," na so e name di cane, and any time e wan use am, e go hold am up, e go call am by name like say na person.

The other: We used to mimic am for back of class, "Enter and Win don wake up today, make una fear." We used to laugh tire.

The one: And e used to clear e throat before every sentence, "ahem, ahem," even when nobody talk anything.

The other: True! Na like say e need permission from e own throat before e fit talk.

The one: But you remember dat one time e show kindness wey nobody expect?

The other: Which one you dey mean now?

The one: Dat girl wey e papa lost work, she no get money for school fees, dem almost send her comot. Teacher pay am from e own pocket, nobody sabi till later.

The other: Ehen, I remember dat one now. E no even tell anybody, one small small talk just leak comot after.

The one: Dat time, we all just look each other, we no fit believe say na di same person wey dey give us punishment fit do dat kind ting.

The other: People na complicated ting, my friend. Wetin you hear about am since, sef? Anybody know wetin become of am?

The one: Some people talk say e don retire go im village for provinces. Other people talk say e don become pastor.

The other: I hear say e travel go abroad, America side, dem say e dey stay with im pikin dem. But nobody know for certain, everybody just dey guess.

The one: True, nobody sabi exact di story. But wherever e dey, I hope e dey fine.

The other: As we don grow so, and we dey look back, all dat fear wey we bin get, e don turn to something like respect, you no go lie.

The one: Na true talk. Dat time we tink say e wicked, but now we understand say na for we own good e do all dat wahala.

The other: (laughs) Imagine if e no push us so, wetin we for be today.

The one: (laughs too) Maybe we for still dey run streets, no discipline for we body.

The other: Na so, sha. But I go tell you one ting, if I hear say e dey come dis side even now, small fear go still touch my chest, I no go lie.

The one: You no be di only one o, I go still feel am too. Old fear no dey comot easy.

The other: (laughs) We too don grow old, and still fear one old man.

The one: Life funny, sha. Anyway, wetin about di rest of we mates, you dey hear from anybody?

The other: Small small. Some don travel, some dey around still, one even become oga for one big company.

The one: Ehen, na good news dat one. Chale, we go need to gather all of we one day, catch up proper.

The other: Make we do am o, we no go take-time this time, abi we go just dey talk and nating go happen again.

The one: No be so, this time make we serious. Give me your number make I call you.

The other: Take am, I dey wait for your call o, no forget.

The one: I no go forget, my friend. Na today we don start, make we continue.

The other: Correct. Greet your family for me o.

The one: I go do dat, greet yours too. Tenki for stopping today.

The other: No wahala at all, na small ting. We go see again soon.
