The mother: So how the place be now, abi you don forget us finish?

The traveller: Ah, Mummy, no be so o. I never forget anybody for this house. The place... e big well-well, no be small thing at all. You go waka waka the whole day and you never reach where you dey go.

The elder brother: Bigger pass Lagos?

The traveller: Different kain big. Lagos wide, but this one long. Na so the train go, go, go, and you still dey inside am like film never end. First time when I bin land there, I bin think say the place go confuse me finish.

Me: How long e dey take you cross from your side go the next side now? Abi na small thing?

The traveller: Small thing? My friend, the whole hour sometimes, if the train no delay for that day. If e delay, add extra thirty minutes on top, make you just siddon dey pray.

The aunty: Ah, an hour just to cross road? Sorry o, that kind of stress no easy at all.

The traveller: E don become normal to me now. But make I no lie, the noise get power for there sef. Different one though. No be the okada horn or the generator wey go dey shake your head from morning, na train sound, na siren sound, dey pass under ground, dey pass for sky. Sometimes I dey miss the quiet quiet night wey we get for here, before that generator man for the corner go wake everybody.

The mother: So where you dey stay sef? Person no even see where you dey lay your head since you come.

The traveller: Small small place. If I tell una the size, una go laugh. E no reach this parlour we dey sidon now, but na inside am I dey cook, dey sleep, dey do everything, everything happen for that one small room. And the money wey I dey pay for the small place, ah, that flat, e too cost for wetin e be. If I change am come naira, person go faint for road.

The elder brother: How much sef, talk the figure make we hear.

The traveller: Make we no go there now, my brother, the heart no strong for that calculation today.

The elder brother: And the market wey una dey buy thing, e resemble our own market?

The traveller: E no resemble am at all. No bargaining for there o. Wetin dem write for the price, na im you go pay, no room to yarn the woman make she reduce am small. First time I try am, the cashier just dey look me like say I don misbehave for that shop. I don learn my lesson since, I just dey pay wetin dem write, mouth shut.

Me: So how you take dey find work there? Na person go carry you go meet oga, or how e dey work?

The traveller: No be like here at all. No connect, no "my oga know your oga." You go write your paper, send am go, dem go call you if dem like wetin dem see. Sometimes dem go interview you three times before dem talk anything. My senior for the work, na person wey don dey there ten years plus, na him dey teach me small small how the place run. E get patience for that one, sha.

The mother: So no bribe, no dash, nothing at all?

The traveller: Nothing like that o, Mummy. Try am, dem go arrest you sharp sharp. Everything dey by the book there, strict pass anything we sabi.

The aunty: You never talk say you bin dey lost for that place. I hear say person fit lost sef for city like that one.

The traveller: Lost? My aunty, no be one time o. First week I comot, I bin lost reach three, four times, dey enter wrong train, dey come out for wrong side entire. One time I waka reach one hour before I sabi say na the opposite side I dey go meet. But small small, the place don enter my head now. I fit waka am with my eye close small, abi make I no lie, almost.

Me: Wetin of food na? I sabi say na so-so bread and something una dey chop there.

The traveller: E no be so bad o, sef. Some things sweet pass wetin we get here, plenty kain food from every corner of the world, you go chop one thing today, chop another one tomorrow, nobody go query you. But make I no lie to una, the pepper no reach there at all. The swallow wey dem dey sell for that side, e no be swallow at all, na something wey just resemble am small. That soup, e no near this one we chop today. I don miss native soup well well, I no go lie to una.

The mother: So which one better sef, this one or that one, talk true, no dodge am.

The traveller: Ah, Mummy, that na the kind question wey no get one answer o. Both get dia own wahala, both get dia own better side. If I talk say that one better, I don lie against this place wey born me. If I talk say this one better, wetin I dey go back go do for over there?

The aunty: Take-time answer o, this one. You don learn to dodge question well well since you go abroad.

The traveller: Na so life be sha, my aunty. Abeg no vex me for that one.

The elder brother: So make we ask the last one now, the main one wey everybody wan hear. You go come back come settle for good, or na visit visit you go just dey do reach when you old?

The traveller: That one I no fit answer with straight mouth today o. My mind no dey one place. Maybe if work good, if things arrange demself well for here small, I fit come back. E fit happen tomorrow, e fit happen ten years, nobody sabi. Make we just dey see road. Na God go show us wetin go happen, no be me go fit talk am now now.

The mother: Na so o. Make we no press the matter again, food don ready since. Come chop first, we go still yarn tomorrow, this thing no go run comot.
