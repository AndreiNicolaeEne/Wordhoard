The mother: Who break this mirror for the parlour? Make una come here sharp sharp, all of una, now now.

The eldest: Mummy I no touch am o. I no even near that side since morning, I dey my room dey read.

The mother: Number two, na you nko, wetin happen?

The second child: Mummy I swear I no do am o. I think say na wind blow the door, the door just bang the shelf, or maybe na the cat jump enter, e knock the thing, e fall like that, na so e be.

The mother: Wind? That window don close since I dey do morning cleaning, no wind fit enter this parlour. And the cat, I bin carry am go outside before una even wake. So wetin una dey tell me sef?

The eldest: You see now, na you dey lie. I don tell you make you no dey rush enter that side, na you always dey do wayo for this house.

The second child: No be me o, na this one dey always careless when she dey clean, ask am, na her duster bin dey near there sef.

The last born: I no sabi anything o, una dey always blame me for anything wey happen for this house, I tire well-well.

The mother: Just now you talk say na wind. Now you dey talk say na duster. Which one be the correct one, abi you dey change story for my front?

The second child: Mummy... maybe I touch am small small when I dey clean, but I no mean to break am o, e just slip small.

The mother: Ehen. So na lie you don dey lie since. Make I hear true story from una mouth, if not, nobody go comot from here today, take-time, I no dey play.

The eldest: Mummy abeg no vex, na small small mistake, e no need turn big wahala like this.

The last born: Sorry mummy, sorry o, even though na not me.

The mother: All of una sha, since nobody wan talk true, all of una go kneel down for this parlour till evening, no TV for anybody this week, and una go contribute pocket money buy new mirror, equal equal. If nobody wan confess, everybody go carry the load together, abi make I carry am alone?
