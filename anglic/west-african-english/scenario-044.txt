The oga di kneel for front of the moto wheel, spanner for hand, showing the new hand how to loosen the axle nut.

Oga: You see this nut? You no go force am. You go turn small-small, this side, until e come loose. Na so.

He turn the spanner twice, wheel just come free, easy easy.

Oga: You see how e bi? Now you try the other side yourself.

The new hand kneel down, grab the spanner, and start to turn the wrong way, forcing am well-well.

Oga: Ah no. Weti you dey do na? You di tighten the thing, you no di loosen am!

The new hand: Na which side na? I no sabi weti side you talk.

Oga: Left! I don show you the side already. You no bi watching me?

He take the spanner back, show again quick, hand moving fast.

Oga: Loosen. Left side. Small-small. Na all.

The new hand try again, but him hand di shake, e ova-turn the spanner and the nut just dey spin without moving anywhere.

Oga: This boy sef! I don tell you say make you no rush the thing. Two time now you don spoil the same work.

The new hand: Sorry oga, my hand no dey balance well, sha.

Oga: Balance no concern here o. Make we start again, patient patient.

He kneel down proper, put him hand for top the new hand hand, on the spanner together.

Oga: Feel am. You feel say e dey tight? Now we go turn small, together, no rush. You feel say e dey loose small small?

The new hand: Ehen, I di feel am now.

Oga: Na so, continue like that till e come out. You go fit finish the rest yourself.

The new hand turn the spanner slow, steady, and the nut come loose proper, wheel start to shake.

Oga: Na so! No be hard thing o. You don see am now?

The new hand: I don see am, oga. Thank you well-well.
