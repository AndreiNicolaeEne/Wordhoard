Chale, make I tell you how today go, because today no be small today.

Na that big gutter behind our houses, the one dey run from the mechanic corner straight down to where the road dey bend. E don chop rubbish since like two, three months, sand dey pile inside there small-small until water no fit pass again. Everybody know say if rain fall today-today, na flood we go see, e no be small work one house fit do alone. You go carry bucket, you go carry cutlass, you no go finish am before dark meet you.

Na the mechanic first raise the matter. Since dawn, before cock even crow well-well, the man dey knock gate to gate, dey shout, "Make una wake o, this gutter go kill us if rain catch us like this, make we all come out today, no be work for one man." That is how e start. Word no even take one hour, e don spread up and down the whole street like fire dey burn dry grass. By the time sun show face proper, people wey we no even call, dem don show face already. I dey tell you, more than fifteen extra people wey nobody plan for, dem just carry cutlass and shovel from their own house dey waka come, some from the next street sef, because dem hear say we dey work.

So we divide the work. Some pesin dey cut the grass wey block the mouth, some dey carry the basin come remove sand, some form line, dey pass bucket hand to hand like relay. But the heaviest part, na inside the gutter itself, the mud dey reach waist, the smell no good at all, na the mechanic's own boy carry that load. That boy, he never rest small, e dey inside there from morning till the sun change position, digging, digging, waist deep, and anytime dem tell am make e come rest, he dey shake head, dey say, "Leave me, I go finish this side first."

Halfway the work, small disaster catch us. We hit one old pipe wey dey block deep inside, and as we force am, e burst, black water and all the old dirty thing inside spray comot like fountain, everybody wey stand near there don soak finish, one auntie clothes spoil complete, and for like two minutes na pure confusion, people dey run, dey shout, dey laugh at the same time.

Na that same time argument start. Some pesin say make we pile the sand for one side of the road, another one say no, that side go block car from passing, make we carry am go the empty plot instead. The taxi driver and the mechanic almost quarrel proper over it, voice dey rise, hand dey point, until Papa, the oldest man for this street, waka come slow slow, use him stick knock the ground one time, and say, "Both of una wrong. Carry the sand go the plot, but small-small, no dump everything one place, e go pack." Just like that, the matter cool down. Nobody argue with Papa.

Because Papa self no gree sit down that day. Small chair dem bring for am under the mango tree, dem tell am make e rest, oga should relax, but he refuse, he say, "I dey old, I no dey useless," and instead he take small hoe, dey scrape the edge weeds small-small, dey supervise anybody wey slack, dey call the name of anybody wey dey lazy make dem hear. Nobody fit tell Papa make e sit down and win.

For food, e no lack that day at all. My mother bring out big pot of rice with stew, one woman near the corner send kenkey and fried fish come, another house bring banku and light soup, and somebody carry cold water, minerals, one big bucket of sobolo for everybody wey thirsty. Different house dey bring different thing throughout the day, nobody carry list, food just dey come as work dey go.

The children too, dem get their own work. Small ones dem carry basin of water go round make people wash hand, some dey pick the small stones and rubbish wey too small for grown pesin to bother with, dem line up like ant dey carry am go the heap. One small girl take am serious well-well, she dey shout "abeg make way!" every time she carry her small basin pass, and everybody dey laugh, dey give her way like she be oga of the whole operation.

The moment wey everybody laugh together though, na when the goat, the one wey belong to nobody sure who, waka enter the freshly clear gutter, slip for the wet mud, and just dey struggle there, legs up, dey bleat like the world don finish. Even Papa laugh until him stick nearly fall from him hand. Somebody try rescue the goat, e self resist, dey kick, and by the time dem pull am out, that goat don cover in mud head to tail, dey shake body, spray everybody near am again. We no fit work small-small after that, everybody just dey hold belly dey laugh.

We suppose finish before afternoon proper, that na how everybody plan am for their head. But between the pipe wey burst, the argument, the goat, and the sand wey still need arranging, the work no finish until almost three hours pass the time we plan. Sun don begin dey go down small when the last basin comot from that gutter.

But chale, by the time we finish, na clean gutter from the mechanic corner to the road bend, no sand, no rubbish, water fit run free comot reach the main drain. We count the sacks of sand and rubbish wey we carry comot, pass forty sacks o, forty and small change. When the last person throw the last basin, somebody just shout, and the whole street join, one big cheer wey rise up reach the sky, hand dey clap, some pesin dey whistle, Papa dey nod him head like say na him alone win this war.

That evening, the whole street change small. People wey no dey greet each other well before, dem dey sit together for veranda, dey laugh over the goat story again and again. Doors wey dey shut early, dem stay open pass usual, children dey play for road small pass their normal time because nobody rush house. Even if e go go back to normal tomorrow, for that one evening, this street felt like one house, one family, and I no fit lie to you, e sweet me well-well.
