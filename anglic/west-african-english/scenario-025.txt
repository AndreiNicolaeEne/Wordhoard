My love: Weti kind thing be that, your mama di ask if I check salt for the ndolé, like say I no fit taste weti dey my own mouth.

Me: Na so mama be now, no vex. She want make everything correct when visitor dey.

My love: Visitor? Since when I be visitor for that house? Na so she di do me small small, and I no talk anything.

Me: Abeg, na love she get for you o, na so she di show am. She no get bad mind.

My love: E fine, e fine. But your papa sef, that story about the bicycle and the border, na third time this year I di hear am. That story, e don tire me well-well before the man even reach the border for the gist.

Me: True talk, I sef di count am for my head as he di talk. "Na so I take cross the whole border with one bicycle..." That one don tire me small too, make I no lie.

My love: You see now! And when we say make we go, im don start another one, about the market and the oga wey chop the whole community money.

Me: Ehen, that one na fresh gist sha, but he go still repeat am next time we come. And that goodbye for door, my friend, I nearly faint standing there.

My love: Forty-five minute! Cold di catch person, and mama still di find something to add. "Make you take-time for road, no drive fast, greet your in-law, take this small thing give them..."

Me: And she go dash you plantain wey nobody ask for, then go inside, come back carry more.

My love: Na so senior people be, my dear.

Me: You see, na you sef di complain pass me now.

My love: I no talk say I no di vex small. Wahala full that house o.
