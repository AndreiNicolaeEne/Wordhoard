Let me tell you how that storm wa come meet us, from the start self to the end, because up to now when I di talk am I still dey shake my head.

That evening the sky just wa sitting there, quiet quiet, in a way that no normal at all. No bird di call, no dog di bark for the whole street, everything just still like the whole town dey hold breath. I wa inside doing small thing for the kitchen when I notice the light done change, it done turn that grey-green color the old people them always warn you say na trouble sign. I call to my eldest, I say make you come look this sky oh, something no right here.

Before one hour even pass, the wind done start to rise for true. E dey pick up small small, then all of a sudden e just jump like person vex. I run go outside quick quick, because we get chair, bucket, my wash basin, all kind of thing scatter for the yard, and if you no move them the wind go carry them go meet person for another town. I di shout to the last born, come help me carry this thing inside, no stand there dey look! We di run up and down, carry go, drop, come back, carry go again, the both of us soaking small from the first rain wey done start to fall.

By the time the real wind reach, I done gather everybody for the parlor, the whole household together, packed close like sardine. That na when the light just cut, one time, no warning, and the whole house dark like inside pot.

I no go lie to you, that na when the fear start for true, but nobody wan say so for that moment.

The eldest: Ma, this wind go carry the roof oh!

Me: Hold yo self, make we all sit down together, nothing go happen us.

The last born: Fear done catch me small, Ma.

Me: Make we pray small, e go pass, e go pass.

But the wind, ehn, the wind wa blowing so hard that after small time nobody could even hear the person wey sit close to them. Talk just stop, because if you open mouth the wind go swallow the word before e reach the next person ear. We just dey hold hand for that dark parlor, everybody body press for everybody, and the whole house self di shake small small like something wan uproot am from ground.

Na so we hear one big crack, then one heavy sound like the earth self shift, close, too close. Somebody tree done fall, and from how the sound land I know say e no far from us at all. My heart just jump go my throat. Nobody talk anything, we all just hold each other tight, waiting to see if the next thing wey fall na for us.

Then the roof, part of am, just peel off like person di open sardine can. You could hear the zinc dey scrape and bend before e fly, one heavy tearing sound wey pass straight through the wind noise self. Water start to come inside for that corner, plenty plenty, and one window for the back room crack, one long crack wey run from top to bottom, but thank God e no break through, e just hold there shaking in the frame like e too dey fear.

I no go pretend for you, sha. That moment there, I wa think say maybe this house no go stand the night. I no tell nobody that time, because if I talk am out loud e go frighten the children them more, so I just hold my mouth and hold my prayer inside me, quiet quiet, waiting.

Then, small small, the wind start to ease. E no just stop one time, e wa dragging itself away slow, like person wey vex done tire from vexing. And then, sudden sudden, everything just quiet. That silence after wa almost louder than the wind self, if I fit talk am so. Nobody move for some minutes, we just dey sit there listening, make sure the thing self done finish pass, no be small lull before e come back double.

When we sure say e done pass, we come outside to see wetin remain.

Ah, my friend, the yard wa some kind of mess. The tree wey fall, na right there e lie, blocking the whole way out to the road, so nobody for that house could pass with car or even walk free until men come with cutlass and saw to clear am. The roof, that corner wey peel off, was sitting open to sky, and I know say na real work, real money, going to fix that one proper, no small patch go do am. The window still holding, cracked but holding, and I thank God for that one small mercy sef.

I look go my neighbor side, and true true their fence wa flat flat for ground, the whole thing knock down like something just sit on top am. But when I compare wetin dem lost to wetin we lost, I no fit even complain for them, because fence dem fit stand back up in one day with small nail and small wood. Our roof no go finish so quick, and that tree self no go move itself.

The days wey follow, oh, na work self. Everybody for the compound, senior and junior alike, come together, some with cutlass, some with rope, to cut and drag that tree piece by piece till the way clear. We di borrow tarpaulin from the neighbor them to cover the open roof make more rain no soak the room, and the man dem wey fix roof, we di wait for them to come, because everybody for the whole area get the same kind of damage, so make you take-time, dem tell us, everybody dey line for turn.

Even now, when I di tell this story to person, I still dey reach for the exclamation dem, I no fit hold it back. When I reach the part wey the tree fall, I go still say ah, and when I reach the roof peeling, I go still shake my head and say oh Lord. My eldest, if dem dey around when I di talk am, dem go add their own part too, dem go say Ma, no forget say the window nearly break oh, and I go say true true, e nearly break, God just hold am.

Is it not so, that kind of night you no forget quick. We lost plenty, the roof, the yard, small peace of mind sef, but we still dey here, the whole household together, and that na the thing wey I hold onto anytime I di tell the story. Every time, I go still catch myself dey say ah and oh in the middle of am, like say I di live the thing all over again small small, right there while I di talk.
