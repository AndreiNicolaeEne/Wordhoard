My friend: You never hear? Dem say your cousin, na him wey dem chase comot from that oga office. E don dey do funny funny thing with the money wey dem give am to carry go bank, oga catch am red-handed, na so dem just show am door, no letter, no nothing, just carry your bag comot.

Me: Abeg wait first. That one you dey tell na lie-lie version, e no even near wetin happen at all.

My friend: Eh?

Me: Make I tell you the true one, small small. My cousin no thief anything. Na him himself report am say the figure for the account no dey balance, na small small discrepancy from another staff entirely, e no be my cousin work. Oga call am, dem sit down, dem check the book together, dem see say my cousin na the one wey first notice the wahala. Dem no sack am o, na promotion dem give am sef, to oversee the account well well.

My friend: Haba.

Me: Yes now. The one wrong thing your story carry na say dem sack am, the man dey work till today, senior for that office, everybody dey greet am "sir." From "he catch red-handed" to "dem give am promotion", you see the distance the story don waka? Na so rumor dey grow leg for road.

My friend: Sorry o, I no mean anything by am. Where I even hear the thing sef, make I remember... na my workmate tell me for bus stop yesterday.

Me: And who tell your workmate?

My friend: E talk say na from another person for their compound, maybe the one wey dey sell recharge card, the one wey dey always add pepper for story.

Me: You see am. From now, anybody wey ask you, tell them the true one, my cousin no do wahala, dem promote am. No carry that other version again, abi you hear me?
