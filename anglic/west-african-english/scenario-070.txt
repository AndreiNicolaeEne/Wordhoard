The first friend come meet me for house, na Friday evening, e no even siddon well before e don begin de talk.

First friend: My friend, you must hear wetin dis one do me o. Na so e come meet us for the njangi meeting, e begin de talk say I no dey serious with money, for front of everybody! Me wey I don dey pay my own since since. E just come shame me for public, abi that one fine?

Me: Ok ok, slow down small. Wetin exactly e talk?

First friend: E say make I no dey borrow borrow if I no go fit pay back. E no even greet me first o, na so e just open mouth begin talk. I no do am o, na him start the wahala, no be me.

I no reply anything, I just di hear am, because if you don hear one side well-well, the case never done.

Later that same week, the second friend knock for door.

Second friend: Oga, abeg hear me small. Na dat one start am o, no be me. E dey owe me money since last year, e never pay, and any time I ask am, e go dodge, dodge. Na for the meeting I just talk am small, I no even shout, sef.

Me: The person talk say you shame am for front of everybody.

Second friend: Shame? I no shame nobody o, I just talk true. If truth dey shame person, na him business be that.

Me: Ok, make I ask you one thing, my friend. Na the money wey e owe you, or na di way e di carry himself for people wey vex you?

Second friend stay quiet small, e look down.

Second friend: ...Na di way e di act like say I no dey important again. Since e start hanging with the big oga dem, e no dey greet me well-well again. The money na small-small thing sha, na di respect wey pain me.

Me: You see now. The money na excuse, the real matter na say una two don dey lose respect for una self.

Second friend: I fit don over-talk for that meeting sha. Maybe na true.

Me: Make the two of una siddon together, no third person for between. I fit arrange am, una go talk am well-well, face to face, no need to come pass me again. Wetin you think?

Second friend: Ok o, arrange am make we finish dis wahala once, before e grow become bigger palava.
