The grandmother: Ehen, no sweep the house now o, na night.
Me: Wetin go happen now, na small dust dey there.
The grandmother: You go sweep the family blessing comot for door, na so e dey happen, believe am or not.
Me: So make I just carry the dust reach morning?
The grandmother: No, sprinkle small water for the broom first, e go undo the bad luck.
Me: Ok o, make I sprinkle the water sha, just in case.
