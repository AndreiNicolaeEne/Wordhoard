Me: My friend, no vex o, but I need talk to you small bout dat money. Na three hundred dollar you bin barrow from me, back when your small girl wa sick and dey rush her go hospital. Almost five month now e dey pass.

My friend: Ah chief, you know how thing dey for me right now. Work no dey pay like befo, rent dey press me well-well, I sef no get plenty for pocket dis time.

Me: I hear you, my man, but dis na de first time you dey tell me story so. You bin promise say by June you go settle am. June done pass, July done pass, now we dey August self, and de money still dey husay e dey befo.

My friend: Sorry o, I no dey try give you hard time. Once dat contract money come, I go pay you every cent, I swear.

Me: Dat contract talk, you don tell me since March. Every time na new promise, new date, new excuse. You dey ova-promise me, my friend, and I dey tired to dey chase you for wetin bi mine sef.

My friend: Wetin you want make I do, eh? You dey vex for me like say I na chop your money and run. Abeg make we no bring wahala for dis small thing between us two.

Me: Small thing? Three hundred dollar no small for me o. I get bill for pay too. Dis bi true, and I no come here fight you, I just want know husay dis thing dey stand for real.

My friend: Okay, okay, no vex sha. I get hundred dollar for pocket now, I go dash you dat one today, de rest go come before month end, God willing.

Me: Awright, bring de hundred make we start somewhere. But take-time, o, I done hear dat "before month end" talk befo. I go dey wait, but my eye go dey on am dis time.
