Na so dis street be, my friend, make you open ear well well because nobody go write dis one for paper, na for head we di carry am.

You see dat open ground for front di corner shop, husay di old woman di sell garri and cube maggi? You fit carry your moto go park der, no problem, di ground dey free. But dat compound gate wey you see for down, di one wit di zinc fence, no ever leave your ting for front der, even if di place look empty like nobody dey inside. Di owner no go quarrel your face, but by evening you go come see your tire don flat, or water don pour for your seat, and person go just tell you sorry, sorry o, even dough na dem duly do am. Take-time for dat corner, my friend, take-time.

You see dat line of stone wey run from di mango tree reach di gutter? Na di boundary. Nobody cross am, not even pikin. Before before, one man bin try build fence for der, but di neighbor bin vex say e di enter im ground, so dem bin leave am like dat, just stone. Nothing dey mark am for eye but everybody wey grow for dis quartier sabi say dia foot no dey pass dat line, abi dem go hear word from dia mama sef.

From ten for night, di whole street di rest. Generator dem di die down one by one, nobody di ova-shout wit music. No be law dem write, but if you break am, person go just open window, look you, no talk one word, and dat silence pain pass any shout.

For street, if you no know weti to do, just watch senior person: na senior you di greet first, oga before junior, mama before pikin, dat one no dey change, whether na doctor or na di boy wey di sell groundnut.

If you want borrow tool or provision, send small pikin make e go beg, dem go dash you small extra sef when you return am. But dat man wey chop person cutlass and no return am, e no go hear am for mouth, dem go just stop to lend am again, forever, quiet quiet, and dat one bi worse pass any wahala.

Di one rule everybody di break, na dat gutter behind di compound dem, night time una go see people di pour dirty water der, di same ones wey di preach against am for morning o.
