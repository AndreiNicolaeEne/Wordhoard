My brother,

Long time now I owe you this letter, and I no go lie to you, shame dey catch me well well when I think how many months done pass since I promise say I go write, I go write, and I neva sit down do it till today. The year done nearly turn since you gone from here, and see me now, pen for hand, like person wey just wake up from sleep.

Make I start with the house news before I forget any of it. The roof leak wa dey worry us since before you traveled, remember how the water wa drip for that corner by the kitchen every rainy season, and Mama wa put bucket there, and we wa laugh anytime somebody forget and step in the bucket for night time? Well, it done fixed finally, praise God. The man came, he na finish the work good, not one drop this whole rainy season we just pass through. But wetin happen now, the back door wey we neva think about, the hinge done give way, so if you no lift the door small when you push am, it go scratch the floor, and everybody in this house dey vex about it small small. Nothing wey get old for this house wan rest, I tell you.

We plant pepper for the back this year, and Mama put down some bitterball too, she say the ground good now since the roof no dey drip water for that side no more. It dey come up nice, you go see am when you come home.

The big change I must tell you about, your workmate from before, the one wey wa always come here with you those days, he don move from that job. Oga wan cut people small small, and he na among the ones the company let go. So he na find work with a different outfit near the port now, doing something with the shipping people them. It happen so fast, one week he dey tell everybody things fine, next week he dey tell us he don pack him things to go stay near his people side, closer to the new work, save the transport money. Nobody wa expect am, but he say the new place treating him better sef.

You remember the thing we used to say anytime Mama call us for something we no want do, how we used to whisper "the goat done cross the road" and only the two of us sabi why that funny? I no go explain am here, I just want you laugh small when you read it, wherever you dey.

Neighbor news now. The family wey dey stay for the corner house, they done leave, the man get transfer, whole family pack go just so. New people come stay there, quiet people, small pekin them get, two of them, one still dey crawl. The woman by the gate, her daughter don born, make it three pekin for her now, and she say the house getting small small but God go provide, she no worried.

Two people dey always ask after you when I pass. The one selling for the corner, anytime I pass she go stop me for road:

The woman at the corner: How your brother? He never call this side?

Me: He dey fine, he dey busy with the work, I go tell am you ask.

The woman at the corner: Tell am we miss am o, this place quiet since he gone.

And the pastor too, almost every service he go find me after and ask if I hear from you, he say make I tell you the church still dey pray for you, abi you don forget them already.

Something I want confess to you, wey I no fit ever say to your face if you wa here: sometimes I vex when you left, I no show it to nobody, I just wa quiet those first weeks, because this house feel different without person to argue with about small small things. I glad for you sha, I no want you think say I no glad, I just wa miss the noise for a while.

The rainy season wa hard this time, worse than the one before am. The road down by the market wa flood two times, and people property wa spoil for some of the low houses near there. Our side we escape am mostly, just that leak I done tell you about. Sun done come out proper now though, everywhere dey dry small small, and people dey happy for that.

Now make I ask you plenty things, because your last letter never tell me nothing about your life there. How the work dey treat you, they no dey stress you too much, abi they dey fair with you? You done find people to call your own for that place, or you still dey manage by yourself most time? And the food, abeg, you dey find anything close to home food there, or you dey suffer for that side, because if you dey suffer I go find way send something proper for you.

I know I always say I go send the palm oil and the pepper for you, every time we talk I promise am and I never do am, and I sure you don tire to hear that promise by now. This time I mean it well well, I go pack am proper this week self, before this letter even reach you sef, so when the package land no vex if the letter still dey road behind am.

Take care of yourself over there, my brother. Everybody here dey ask after you, everybody dey miss you, this house still dey feel the space where you use to be. Write me back quick this time, no make me wait one whole year again before I hear your voice for paper o.
