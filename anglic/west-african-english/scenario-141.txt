The gathering was at the mother's parlour, plastic chairs pulled in from the veranda, everybody sitting close like for wake-keeping, and before I even open mouth, I could see am for their eyes: they know say something dey come. Nobody talk. Even the small ones wey dem carry come, dem just dey look, sensing say this one no be regular Sunday visit matter.

Me: I gather una here today because there is something wey I don carry since, years now, and I no fit carry am again. Time don reach make I talk am.

Nobody answer. The eldest was sitting by the window, one leg crossed over the other like say he relaxed, but I could see the small tension for his jaw.

Me: Una remember that year wey the business scatter. The buses. The money wey we all contribute for Papa treatment, wey the last born bin dey hold for the house because she na the one wey stay there full time. Una remember how the money just disappear before we even fit book the flight, and how everybody turn face to her.

The elder aunt shift for her seat, her face don already dey change small.

Me: Una remember the anger. How the family gather like this same way we gather now, and everybody dey shout for her, dey ask her weti she use the money do, dey call her thief for her own father house. How Papa himself no talk to her well again till the day wey he pass. How she try explain say she no touch am, and nobody believe her, because who else fit reach that money if not her. Una remember how that thing follow her since, the way people for this family dey introduce her to their in-laws with small hesitation for their voice, the way senior ones dey warn their children make dem no follow her handle money for anything. That reputation don follow her well-well, since, till today.

The one wey dem blame, the last born, was sitting quiet, her hands folded for her lap, her face already tight like person wey sabi wetin dey come but still dey brace for am.

Me: I dey tell una now, plainly, no soft-soft: na lie. She no touch that money. She never touch am from the beginning.

The elder aunt's mouth open small, disbelief dey plain for her face, like say the ground don shift under her.

The elder aunt: Wetin you mean say she never touch am? Na who touch am then, if no be her? She was the one holding the money, abi?

Me: I dey tell una say I sure. I no dey guess, I no dey hear from person. I see am with my own eye.

Silence, thick like harmattan dust, sit for the room.

Me: That night, I bin wake up to drink water, and I see the eldest for the passage, dey come out of her room with the envelope for hand. I no talk anything that night, because I bin tell myself say maybe na something else, maybe he dey carry am go keep am safe somewhere. But by morning, the money don finish, and na she dem blame.

I could see the last born's chest dey rise and fall fast fast, her eyes dey shine but she no talk yet.

Me: Later, small time after everybody don calm down small, she meet me, alone, and she cry, and she beg me make I no tell anybody wetin I suspect, because she sef no fit prove am, and she fear say if I talk am and I no get evidence, e go just cause fresh wahala, fresh division, and Papa go just die faster from the trouble of it. She carry that thing quiet, protecting the eldest without even knowing say she dey protect am, just praying make God vindicate her one day.

Now every eye for the room turn go the eldest. He never talk. He just dey look him hand.

The last born's grievance, the one wey she don swallow for years. I could see am rising fresh for the elder aunt's face too, because she was one of the ones wey talk hard word for that time. I no wait make she start to defend herself first.

Me: I know wetin dey your mind now, that if I bin see am, why I no talk since. Make I answer that one before anybody ask. I dey fear. I dey fear that if I open my mouth, this same anger wey don finally quiet down, e go wake up fresh, worse pass before, and the family go scatter fully this time. I dey fear also for the eldest, no be say I dey protect am because I love am pass her, but because I bin think, foolishly, say maybe if I no talk, the wahala go just die with time and everybody go move on. I no want to be the person wey cause more damage with my mouth.

The eldest still no talk. His hand dey shake small for his knee.

Me: There was one time, years back, for her introduction ceremony, when the in-laws bin hear the rumour and almost call off the whole thing. I remember standing for the kitchen, the words don already reach my tongue, I almost talk am there, in front of everybody, but I look her face, and I look how happy she still try to be that day despite everything, and I just swallow am back down. I no fit forget that day. I don carry that regret since.

The last born's face, as she dey hear all this, na complicated thing to watch. Relief bin dey there, plain, like heavy load wey just drop small from her shoulder. But almost same time, fresh anger dey rise for her eyes too, hotter pass the relief.

The last born: So all these years... all these years wey people dey look me anyhow, wey my own husband people dey whisper for corner, wey I lose that contract because one auntie no gree recommend me, all because of something I never do, and you know since? You KNOW since, and you carry am for your pocket like say na small thing?

Me: I no dey defend myself. I know say sorry no go return the years wey you lose. I know say sorry no go undo the names wey people call you for your back. But I dey sorry. Genuinely. For carrying am too long.

The eldest finally lift his head. His voice, when e come, was low, no fight for am at all.

The eldest: I bin owe person plenty money that time. Bad people, the kind wey no dey play. I bin fear say if I no pay, na my life. I take the money thinking say I go replace am before anybody notice, but the trip date reach before I fit arrange anything, and by then... by then everybody don already turn to her, and I just... I just allow am continue like that. I no defend her. God forgive me, I no even open my mouth.

The elder aunt's anger, the same anger wey she bin direct to the last born for years. I watch am turn, right there, redirect itself fully, like water wey finally find where e suppose flow from the start.

The elder aunt: You allowed us to insult her for years. You sat there, in this same parlour, and watched us call her names, and you never said one word?

The eldest never answer that one. He just look down again.

Me: I dey add my own apology to whatever una wan give am too. I bin fit talk this thing long ago, and I choose silence instead, thinking I dey protect the family. I no protect anybody. I just added more years to something wey bin already too heavy.

Nobody talk for long time after that. The room no settle back into anything comfortable, this one no be that kind story wey end with everybody hugging and laughing sotay make peace quick quick. Something bin break for that room, something wey go take time to mend, if e ever mend well-well. But at least, for the first time in years, the family bin dey stand for the truth, not around am, dodging am, protecting am with silence. Whatever go happen next, the anger, the questions, the long conversations wey still dey wait, at least nobody go carry the wrong load again. That one alone, sha, na something.
