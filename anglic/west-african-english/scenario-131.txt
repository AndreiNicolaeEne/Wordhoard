The first drop hits the roof and someone shouts.

The eldest: Rain! Rain don come o!

They all rush outside into it.

The mother: Ah tenki God, we don wait dis rain tay tay, i dey fall now.

The last born throws up both hands and turns in the yard.

The last born: Make e rain well-well, wash we clean, o!

The eldest: Una go catch cold o.

They stand there, just listening to it fall.
