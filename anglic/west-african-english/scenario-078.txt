E no even fall reach ground o, na me quick quick hold the arm, but that alone tell me say things don pass ordinary drinking. I ask, "You dey fine?" and the mouth just dey open and close, the words no dey come out straight at all, everything just dey mix like garri wey too much water enter.

Of course the person go still insist say, "I'm fine, I'm perfectly fine," but the tongue no gree cooperate with that mouth at all, e come out like "I'm... fahn... perfeckly..." I just look my other friend wey dey with us that night and we both know say na time to move, no more argument.

The person start to vex small, dey shout say we dey disturb am, say the night still young, say una be wicked people wey no wan make person enjoy. Na so we take manage am, small small, one word here, one joke there, until finally the person agree, still grumbling under breath, and we lift am up.

We had to turn back, the person now dey laugh at me, "You self don dey drunk too," meanwhile na the person self dey barely hold body.
