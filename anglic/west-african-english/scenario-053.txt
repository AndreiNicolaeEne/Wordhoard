My friend, make I tell you wetin happen to me for that immigration office, the day I wa go collect my clearance paper. That paper wa important o, because my flight wa book for the next morning self, and if the paper no ready that same day, the whole trip go spoil, ova-spoil sef.

I reach early, but the line long well well. When I finally reach the counter, the official just dey turn my file page by page, slow slow, no dey talk straight. Then he drop the first hint.

The official: My friend, dis kind paper, e get plenty step o. Sometimes if person want am fast, e get way make e move.

I know wetin he mean, but I play like I no hear am at all. I just answer:

Me: Is it not so, senior? Wetin I fit do make the step them short small?

He no push am straight that time, he just dey shift the paper, dey look me small small. Then he say am plainer.

The official: Wahala plenty for this office o. But small something for me, e go help the ting move quick quick.

My heart wa beat, because I no wan pay, and I no wan lose the whole day sha. So I tell am:

Me: Abeg senior, no vex, I no get extra money on me today sef. But na my senior for the head office send me come, make I go check am small, I go come back.

I no call full name, I just mention say person dey watch from head office. E pause. Long pause. Tense one sha. Then e just stamp the paper, dash am give me.

The official: Take time o, next time make yo come early.

I say thank you o, and I carry my paper go, no money change hand.

My friend, if I bin pay am that day, I for don lose myself for nothing. That na how such people bi: they go hint small small, wait make you fall inside, but if you stand your ground and play dumb small, na so you go win am, no wahala at all.
