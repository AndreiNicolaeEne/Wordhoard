The mother: Chale, make we call am after my mother now. The name dey sweet me small-small since.

The father: Sha, that name too old-fashioned o. All the pikin for the compound, if you call your child that one, people go dey laugh am for school.

The mother: Laugh am for what reason? Na fine name, no be so?

The father: Abeg no vex, I just dey talk true. Wetin of the name I bring last week, the one wey mean "God's own gift"?

The mother: That one? No o, I no fit like that name at all. Your cousin don already name her son that same name, sef, and I no want people dey confuse the two pikin dem for family gathering.

The father: True talk, sha. Okay, make we think about naming am after Papa now, God rest am well.

The mother: Ah, that one too get its own wahala o. Your junior brother and your senior uncle, the two of them dey carry Papa name already. If we add this one, na three for the family. Too much sef.

The father: Chale this thing dey hard well-well. Every name we bring, one wahala go show face.

The mother: Sha, I dey tired small. Who even get the final say for this matter, isn't it the mother wey carry the pregnancy nine months?

The father: E no work like that o. Na the two of us dey decide together, na so I know am.

The mother: Then make we sleep on top the matter. My eye don dey heavy well-well, I no fit argue again tonight.

The father: True talk. We go continue tomorrow, but I dey tell you, that first name, I no go gree o.
