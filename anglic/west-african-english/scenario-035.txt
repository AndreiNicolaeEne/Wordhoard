Me: Chale, no be small thing you dey talk. The stall for behind the trotro station, na him get the best waakye for this whole area, no be lie.

My friend: Waakye? Chale you never chop real waakye for your life. Na the one near the taxi rank get am. That woman, her shito dey do wonders, small-small she go put am for your plate, e go still turn your head well well.

Me: Ah ah, that woman? I bin go there last month, the woman no even wash her hand well before she dey serve people. I see am with my own eye, wahala full ground that day, no be small thing.

My friend: Sorry o, but that no mean say the food bad. Every stall get their bad day, na so life be, is it not so.

Me: Bad day? Chale that woman go dash you belle wahala for free, no be gift dash o, na sickness dash straight, sharp sharp.

My friend: You dey exaggerate now, chale. As if say the whole Accra go chop for her hand one day and die finish. Nobody don die o, no be small thing you dey call am like that.

Me: I no dey lie sef. My brother chop there last month, he dey run toilet the whole night, no rest small, ask am yourself.

My friend: Ei, take-time chale, no vex the woman finish now, maybe na the pepper wey no gree with his body.

Me: Sha, make we no argue reach morning. But you go admit say my stall waakye pass her own, no be so?

My friend: I no go admit that one o. But make we talk about the one near the church now, the banku with tilapia, even you no fit find fault for that place.

Me: True true, that place na correct one, nobody fit talk say the food bad there.

My friend: Chale make we go chop both our own again then, once and for all, make we see who dey right.

Me: Ehen, na so we go do am. This weekend, we go settle this wahala for good, chale.
