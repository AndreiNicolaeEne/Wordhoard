That cold wey come hold us for eleven days straight, abeg, I no go forget am for my whole life. E no be say e start small small then e grow, no, e catch us for head one time, first day sef, everybody for compound just di shiver like say una dey inside freezer.

We fire, we no gree leave am rest, morning, afternoon, midnight, na fire di burn for that stove non-stop, day and night, eleven days straight, because if you leave am small time, the cold go just enter bone.

Something wey never happen before happen for our house: the water wey dey inside the tank for back yard don freeze solid, no be small ice for top-top, na the whole thing turn rock. Water no fit come again, we di break the ice small small with matchet to fetch am.

The oldest one for house, na she done see the most year among us, she talk say, "I bin see hard cold before, way back, but this one don pass all of dem, this one no get equal, e don beat everything I sabi."

The worst night na the eighth night. That night the cold just too much, wind di blow like knife, whole street quiet like say town don die. Nobody talk again, everybody just di manage, di pray make morning come quick.

By the time e finish, three of the fowl wey we dey rear don die for that cold, just so, we see dem for morning don stiff for ground. That one pain the mother well well, she no talk the whole day, sorry-o.

Na for the eleventh day the sun come out proper, the ice for tank start to melt, water start to run. When that warmth touch us for skin, everybody rush comot, di shout, di laugh, like person wey just come back from far, far journey. Na so we know say the cold don finally release us, o.
