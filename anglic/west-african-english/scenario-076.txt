Me: Chale, make we sit down small, I wan talk to you serious serious.

Friend: Wetin dey worry you like this? You dey fine?

Me: Na about the bike. That thing wey happen last week, e no dey sit well for my mind at all. You dey ride like say nobody dey behind you, dey weave for that Spintex road, the trotro almost knock you flat, sef the driver bin hoot like mad person. My heart just dey beat anyhow when I see am. My hands never even stop dey shake, up till now when I think am.

Friend: Ah chale, relax now, nothing happen na. I get skill, I know how to dodge. Small small thing you wan make big matter.

Me: Small small? Abi you don forget how you break your hand for that same junction, two years ago? You bin dey hospital tey tey, you no fit even chop with that hand, your mother bin dey cry for that ward every day. That one na small injury sef, imagine if next time na your head go hit the ground. That bike, e no know person o. You go die, my friend, or you go dey bed for the rest of your life, somebody go dey feed you, dey turn you like baby.

Friend: ...

Me: If that thing happen, na who go remain to carry the grief? Your mother go bury her own pikin with her own hand. Me, I go dey your funeral dey cry, dey ask myself why I no talk to you well well when I still get the chance. Abeg, no do this to us o.

Friend: ...Okay chale, e don land, I hear you. I no fit promise you say I go stop dey ride am, but I go take-time small, I go wear my helmet well well, I go slow down for that junction. That one I fit promise, no vex.

Me: That one dey better pass nothing at all. Just make you dey careful, sha.
