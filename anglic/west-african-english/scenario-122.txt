You wan hear why the old ones no gree make nobody pass that bend for night, abi? Na so the thing always begin. Evening go quiet like this one, we don chop finish, lantern di burn small for corner, and na so somebody weh never grow for here go ask the question, weti de for that road weh make grown person, strong person, take-time and change side make dem no pass am after dark. Na so I go start too, the very way my own grandmother start am for me, long time back. So make you hear me well-well, because I go tell you the whole thing, start to finish, and I no go stop for question, I go carry you go the whole way na so dem carry me.

That thing weh de for that bend, nobody never see am proper, sef. Na so dem tell am from before, na so I go tell you now: you no go see am first, you go feel am first, like person weh di stand for your back before you turn. Before e get any shape at all, e get voice. Na the voice come first, before anything, before shadow, before smell, before nothing. Some people weh claim say dem see small-small shape for the corner of dem eye, dem swear say by the time dem turn face am well-well, nothing de there again. So the thing, if e get body at all, na body weh no like show face. Na the voice na im true self.

And weti the voice di do? E go call your name, but no be your name for stranger mouth, e go call your name for the very voice of person weh you love, person weh you go answer quick quick before you even think. Your mama voice, your papa voice, the voice of the one weh you fit trust pass anybody. E go call you soft, from far small, like say the person di wait for you for that road, di find you sotay. And na so e want carry you comot the path, comot from anybody weh fit save you, before you sabi weti di happen.

E no just di call anytime sha. Na after midnight the thing di wake, when the whole place don quiet reach the last person don sleep. And e no di try am on night weh moon di shine. Na the night weh no moon de at all, when road dark pass eye, when even person weh sabi the road well-well fit lose road. Moon de, the thing quiet. Moon no de, na so time don reach.

Na for our own bend, the one weh the road di bend go down near the water, before you reach the bridge side for Bonabéri, na so dem tie the story. I no di talk say e get for everywhere, I no di talk say e get for every water for this country, I di talk about our own bend, the one we sabi, the one weh our people don warn our people about since before I bin small pikin. Other quarter get dem own story for dem own road, na so I hear, but I no fit tell you about other quarter road, I go tell you about ours.

Me, I first hear this story from my grandmother, when I bin small pikin weh di fear dark corner for inside house sef, no talk of outside road. She no tell am like play story, she tell am like say na so the ground be, like say na fact weh no need proof, the way you go tell pikin say fire go burn. Sometimes when she tell am she go laugh small for the end, but that laugh no reach her eye, I tell you true, that laugh na the kind weh person di laugh when the thing e di laugh about no really funny to am, na so e cover the fear small. I bin di watch her face well-well those times, and I know say na something de there weh she no wan make me see.

First proof weh dem go always give you, na the ground itself. For that same bend, get one patch weh nothing no di grow, notin sef. Dem don plant, dem don try, dem don even carry good soil comot from another place go put there, and still nothing no di come up from that patch, no grass, no weed, nothing weh even bird go drop from im mouth fit grow. Everything around am green well-well, but that one patch, na so e stay, bald like head weh hair no fit grow again.

Second proof, na the kind weh person go always hear from another person weh hear am from another person again. Somebody go tell you say im neighbor cousin, weh di drive taxi for night, one time pass that bend and see one woman for road side, dressed fine fine, waving hand make e stop. But by the time e slow down small to look well-well, road empty, nobody de there. E no be say the taxi man himself tell you, na im neighbor cousin story, weh pass through third mouth before e reach your ear. Na so most of the wahala stories di come, secondhand, but people still believe am, because too many mouth di carry the same kind story.

But the one story weh dem go always tell in full, no leave out one part, na my own uncle own story, years back, before you sef born. E bin di trek pass that bend for night, no be foolishness, na work call am, and e hear im own name, clear clear, from off the path, for the water side. E swear na the voice of im own mother weh don die before that time, calling am soft, di beg am make e come small. E no think twice, e turn go the voice direction, e don waka three, maybe four step off the path before something for im body, e no fit explain weti, just hold am, freeze am, turn im head back to the road. When e reach house, shaking sef, nobody for the family blame am for anything, we just tell am sorry, sorry well-well, because na so the thing be, no person to blame.

To this day, if you ask am, e go tell you the very same words, e no ever change am: "Na my mama voice, no be another voice, na my mama voice weh call me, and na my leg self weh no gree carry me the last step." Every time, na so e talk am, word for word, no more no less, and the family go repeat am the same way anytime the story come up, because to change even one word for that one, e feel like person di lie.

The detail weh go always make even the doubter person quiet small, na this: my uncle mama don die before that night, but nobody for that road, nobody weh di near am that time, sabi say im mama don die. E no bin tell anybody yet, di keep the news small for imself. So weti call am for that voice, nobody fit explain weti make the voice pick that particular mama, that particular time. Na that one go make person pause, even the one weh no believe for spirit sha.

But the other detail, just as true, na the one weh give doubter person chance to laugh am off: na night, na dark road, and my uncle bin waka far, tire well-well from work, belly empty sef. Anybody go tell you say tire body and hungry belly fit play trick for ear, fit make person hear weti no de there. So na that one dem go hold, the ones weh no wan believe, dem go say na sleep di catch am for leg, na hunger di whisper for im head, nothing more.

I no go lie to you, the story no come the same from every mouth. Some elder go tell am soft, small warning inside sweet story, make pikin no too fear sleep. Some go tell am hard, make you fear road well-well, make you no ever forget. My own grandmother bin tell am soft small, but my grandfather side, dem tell am hard pass. I no mind the difference, sha, because na the same true thing di stay inside all of dem, na just the mouth weh carry am di change, and every mouth get im own right to carry am the way e sabi carry am.

So today, even now, make una hear me well: if you hear your name call from that dark side, from bend weh no light de, make you no answer o, not one word, not even to ask who di call you, until you don check with your own eye who di call you true true. Na the one thing we all still di do here, big man, small pikin, everybody, no argument, na so we di teach una pikin dem too. Me myself, I no go lie to you, I no fit swear say I believe the whole thing wholesale, sometimes I di think na so old story di grow leg for demself, di get body weh never bin get before. But I go tell you this one true thing: since the day my grandmother first tell me, till today, I never once answer when my name call from dark side weh I no fit see person, not once, and I no plan to start now.
