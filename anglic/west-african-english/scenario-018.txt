Una make I stand up small, make I hold this glass before I sit back down, because if I no tell una the story tonight, una go vex for me, I know am. My husband de sit down there de smile, e no go talk one word, e don leave the whole thing for me, as usual. That na how e be. Even the day e propose, na half sentence e manage finish, so make una no expect too much from am tonight.

So the story. Na nothing grand o, make I tell una true-true. We no meet for no fine wedding or no big church programme. Na for park we meet, straight up. I dey wait for car for Regent Road, de go up-country to see my auntie, and the car full-full already but the driver still de shout "one more, one more" like say the car go stretch. I bin get one book for my hand, de read am small small make the waiting no vex me too much.

Na so this man come stand up beside me, e no even greet me, e just look the book and e say:

My husband: You de read book for park? The car go come now-now and you no go even see am de come.

I no even answer am first, I just cut eye small. But e no move, e just stand there de smile like say e win lotto. That na the thing wey stick for my mind since, how e no fear, how e just waka come stand and talk him mind, plain, no shame for am. Small thing, but I still de tell my children am today.

Now make I confess something. When e start to come around, I no bin ready o. I bin just come out from one bad situation before then, one thing wey almost spoil my mind concerning man business altogether, so I bin dey fear well-well. Anytime e come, I go find one excuse, I go say I busy, I go say make e no rush me. But this man, e no push. E no ever push. E go just come, greet my people well, sit small, laugh with my brother, and go him way. E never once make me feel say I owe am anything. Na that patience wear the fear off me small small, until one day I look and I see say na him I de wait for, not the other way.

The first time we properly go out, outside all this chance-chance meeting for park, na my husband invite me go eat for one place down Lumley side, near the beach. But e no go smooth like that o. My father, God rest am, e no like the idea one bit. E say the boy no get work wey settle, e say na taxi e de drive, today here tomorrow there, e say how man wey no get him own house go fit marry my daughter. My father bin dey fear for my future, and make I no lie, e get him point that time.

So how we take pass am? Nothing quick quick. My husband just continue to come, greet my father proper every time, bring small thing for the house anytime e get, sit down patient when my father talk rough to am. E never disrespect am, not once. E work, e save, e buy him first keke, e come show my father say na so-so hard work e dey do, no gimmick inside. Small small, my father mind change. By the time e come put stone for my family, na my father wey de shout loudest for the compin dem.

Now, una see this umbrella wey de stand for the corner there, the black one wey don tear for two-three place, wey nobody wan trow away? Na that same umbrella e bin cover me with the day rain katch us for Lumley, that first outing. We both wet reach skin, laughing like mad people, and since then anytime rain wan fall we go joke say make we carry "the marriage umbrella." Una don hear the joke before, I know, but I must call am tonight.

Na so time pass, and one day I realize say this thing don pass small small friendship. How I come know? Na my husband him sista come tell me one day for market, she say, "My brother don tell mama say na you e wan marry." I never hear am from my husband own mouth yet o, na so him sista spoil the secret give me for market, in front of fish seller and everybody. That na how I come know say the thing don serious pass play.

The proposal self, e no get plenty style, make I tell una true. We bin de sit down one evening de roast groundnut for road side, near where we first bin dey court, and e just turn come me, e no kneel, e no get no ring for that moment, e just hold my hand and e say make we marry, plain so. I laugh first because I think say e de joke, but e no laugh back, e just wait. So I say yes, and na so we start to tell people.

Na about one year pass before we come marry proper, from that groundnut evening to the wedding day. My husband family welcome me quick quick, him mother love me from the first day she set eye for me. My own side, una don hear the story, my father come around but e no come around fast, e take him time, e watch the man well before e give him blessing full. But by the time the wedding reach, e de call my husband "my son" pass how e de call him own pikin dem sef.

Now, everybody remember the wedding for the fine dress and the cake and the dancing, but the one thing wey still de come back for my mind, no be none of that. Na when light just go off, right there when we wan cut the cake. Whole hall dark, generator refuse to catch, and instead of everybody panic, all of una start to sing "Mr and Mrs" with una phone torch dem up in the air, and my husband just hold my hand for the dark, still smiling like say na so e plan am. Photo no fit catch that kind moment. But I no go ever forget am.

We no go stand here tonight pretend say everything smooth since that day. We done pass one hard stretch, when the business nearly spoil and money no dey come for house, weeks upon weeks. I no go dwell for am tonight, but make I just say, na prayer and na this man beside me carry me through that time, and na the same family wey sit for this table tonight wey no leave us alone. That one, I no go finish thank una for.

So make I close this thing before my husband come tell me say I de talk too much. The one thing wey never change since that day for park, since that book and that "you go come now-now", na this man still de tease me the same way, e still de laugh the same laugh, e still de stand him ground plain and simple like say na so God make am. That thing wey I see for him the first day, na the same thing I still de see today.

So make una raise una glass with me. To this man wey no fear to talk him mind for park that day, to the family wey come around, to the umbrella wey refuse to die, and to whatever God get plan for us wey still de come. Cheers, my people. Tenki make una come. Now I go sit down before e vex.
