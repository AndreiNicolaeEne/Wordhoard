Abeg make I tell you dis story, and no laugh too much for me sha, because as I dey tell you now I dey laugh myself, but that day, I swear, nobody for that road was laughing, nobody at all.

So the whole plan was sweet-sweet, well arranged, better pass any journey wei I don take for this life. My sister wedding bin dey happen for Bo, and me and my brother, we bin plan the whole thing since one month before. Poda-poda ticket book early, small bag pack the night before, everything lay down neat. We say make we leave Freetown early-early Saturday morning, before six, reach Bo before midday, rest small, iron we clothes, dress fine for the evening program. Sound plan, na so we think.

First trouble start before we sef leave the park. Driver say the vehicle dey come, dey come, dey come, but the man just dey siddon for the shed dey chop groundnut like e no get eye for time. Everybody for the park dey ask, "wetin dey hold you now, driver?" and the man just dey laugh small, "small time, small time, e go come." That "small time" turn to one hour wei nearly finish my patience, and we never even move one yard yet.

Finally the vehicle come, we pack ourself inside tight-tight, knee for chest, bag for lap, and off we go at last. Everything dey fine small-small, the vehicle dey move, we dey gist, we dey laugh, until we reach one bad spot near Moyamba junction and the whole engine just cough, cough, and die like that. No warning, nothing. Driver come down, open bonnet, dey look inside like e sef no sabi wetin e dey look for.

Na there the crowd start to grow o. One vehicle full become like fifteen people standing for road, then thirty, then more from another vehicle wei don pack behind us. Some passenger just holla, "una make room small, more people dey come," as if room dey grow from ground. Everybody just dey stand for that hot road, dey ask each other the same question..."how long you tink?", and nobody get answer. Three hours pass so, nobody fit tell you when the thing go move. Some people siddon for the ground, some dey buy groundnut and banga from the small small sellers wei don smell business come find them for road.

And as if that one no bad reach, the sky just open. So-so heavy rain, thunder and everything, no warning at all, and everybody dey run come under the one small shed wei get for that place, packing ourself like sardine, water dey still drip from the roof sef. Na there me and my brother lost each other for that rush, I no dey lie, na true. I dey call am, "wusai you dey, wusai you dey," and I no see am for like twenty minutes. My heart dey beat fast-fast, because for road so, anything fit happen to person. When I finally see am, e dey stand for the other side wei e don help one old mama carry her load come under shelter. I vex small but I glad pass everything.

One senior man wei dey siddon for the bench near us just dey shake im head, dey say make we no vex, patience go pay. And a small girl wei dey sell pure water, she see me dey soaked to bone, no umbrella, nothing, and she just come give me her own extra wrapper, say "cover yasef sister, no bi your fault." I no even know her name, I never see her again after that day, but that small kindness dey stay for my heart till now. God bless her wherever she dey o.

By the time the driver find one mechanic to help patch am small-small, na extra fifty thousand leone commot from my pocket, money wei I never plan for at all, because normal fare no reach that kind of spending. Na there me and my brother start to argue.

My brother: "make we just abandon this vehicle, find another one, no matter the cost. You no see the time? Wedding go pass finish before we reach."
Me: "abeg no vex, make we just hold on small, the man dey almost ready. If we waste more money now, wetin go remain for us when we reach?"
My brother: "hold on for wetin, wusai patience go carry us? Sef if we go pay double, make we find road."

We argue so-so back and forth till we vex reach the point wei we no dey talk to each other, just dey siddon dey vex quiet-quiet, each one dey face im own side. Eventually we agree to try one alternative road, one "short cut" wei one man for the shed say go save us time..."una take time o, that road no easy," na so e warn us, but we no listen well. Na there our real wahala start, because that short cut cost us two extra hours. The road bad pass anything, pothole upon pothole, and at one point the driver sef get lost, dey stop dey ask people for road wetin be the way. Two hours wei bin suppose save us, na the same two hours wei nearly kill us.

By evening time, tiredness don full my body well-well. That was the point wei I nearly cry, I go tell you true, no laughing matter that time. We don dey road since before six for the morning, no proper food inside belly, body dey pain, clothes still wet-wet from the rain, and the wedding wei we plan reach before midday, we no even sabi if e still dey happen by the time we go reach.

But God dey o, because as darkness dey fall, small luck come find us. One private car, wei belong to one oga wei dey travel come from Freetown sef, see us for road dey stand helpless, and the man just stop, roll down im window, ask wusai we dey go, say make we jump in, e get space small. Just like that, our luck change one time. E no even ask us for money, just say "make God bless the journey," and carry us the rest of the way.

We reach Bo nine hours pass the time wei we bin plan, nine hours o, imagine that. When the two of us come down from that man car, you go tink na from war we dey come from. My dress wei I don iron nice-nice for the wedding don crumple, still small-small wet for some part. My brother eye don red like pepper, mouth dry, body tire pass tire, and me sef I no fit even stand up straight one time. But we reach. We meet the last small-small of the program, we chop the cold rice wei remain, and we siddon dere quiet, just dey look each other, too tire even to talk.

Now when I sit down dey tell you this, I dey laugh, I no go lie, because time don pass and everything now just be story we go dey tell for many years. But that day self, no be lie, that day nobody for that whole road was laughing at all.
