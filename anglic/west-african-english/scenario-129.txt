Na for early afternoon I first see am. Smoke di rise for the horizon, small small, grey like pepper soup smoke wey too much. I no bin fear at all, I just di stand for veranda, di watch am, di tell myself say na far far, e no go reach here. My neighbor even come stand near me.

Neighbor: You see am? Na wetin di burn so?

Me: I no sabi o, but e far, make we no worry ourself.

But within one hour the wind just turn everything. The smoke wey bin far don near now, e don thick, e start to sting my eye, my eye di water like say I di cry. That smoke, e no dey play, e dey choke person nose small small until you no fit breathe well.

Na then I know say watching don finish, time to move don reach. I no waste time. Within twenty minutes I don comot from that place, only wetin important I carry, the rest I leave am, wahala no dey for property when life dey for line.

The eldest: Abeg make we hurry, no carry heavy load, run body!

My throat don raw finish, heat di press my skin like fire self dey follow me for back. I look back one time as I di go, and na so I see the light of the whole place, red red, dey climb sky. I no even talk again, I just carry leg, waka fast fast.

Two nights I bin dey outside, no house, no news, everybody just dey ask make God helep us, sha, we no know wetin go happen. I sef bin dey pray small small every night.

Na third day them tell us make we fit go back. My heart dey beat as I di enter, but the place, e don spared. Everything still stand, o. Thank God well well, na so my life still be.
