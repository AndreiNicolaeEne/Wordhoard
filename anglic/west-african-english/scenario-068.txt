Listen na, since you just reach this place make I tell you one thing so wahala no go meet you for road. It get person wa dey work this whole town, dey wait for the new person, the one wey just land. That kind person na oga for wahala business, he sabi how to chop people money smooth-smooth. He always friendly, always get one deal wa dey run, always dey call you 'my friend' before he even sabi your name-o. He go buy you cold drink first time, he go dash you small-small thing, make you feel like he na your padi already, like you two bi old-old friend.

Then he go bring the pitch. He go tell you he get connection, he fit find you house cheap, or job, or dat land everybody dey find for this Monrovia, an the price wa too sweet, wa make you no even question am. He go say, 'Abeg just bring the small deposit make I hold the thing for you, before another person come take am.' Dat na the whole trick, if you pay anything on top before you see the place, before you see the paper, you no go ever see the money again, an you no go ever see the man again sef. He go change phone number, he go change corner, he gone like dat.

Somebody near me, he wa pay ova two hundred US for one house wey no even bi the man own. He run go check the place the next day, an husay the house suppose dey, na empty lot dey there, nothing. He lost the money, he lost the whole week crying for am. Sorry for am o, but that na lesson everybody must hear well-well.

The signs wa dey there before-before: the man too friendly too quick, he no get fixed address, he wan rush you today-today, he no let you bring witness. Take-time o, sha, na small caution save person for this town. If anybody approach you so, no pay nothing, no follow am go anywhere alone. Go find senior for this corner first, di old man wey sell for the shop, or di security wey stand there, dem sabi who dey run wahala for this area. Ask senior before you give your money to anybody-o.
