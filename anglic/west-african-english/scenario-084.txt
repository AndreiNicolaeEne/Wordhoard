The stranger: Abeg, sorry o, dis sun don deal wit me well well. You get small water wey you fit spare?

The one nearby: Na so? No vex, make I bring am one time. Tek am, drink well well.

The stranger: Ah no, na small-small go do, I no wan trouble you.

The one nearby: Wetin, small water go do wetin? Drink am finish, abeg.

The stranger: Tenki o, na God go pay you, you don save my life today.
