Make una come close, make una come sidon small, make wi tok. Na three-nil, e dey for di board, no need make I hide am, no need make I sweet-mouth una. Three-nil. Make e rest for una mind small, then make wi leave am dere.

I no go point finga for one person for wetin don happen dis first half, sef make I no go try am, because na all a wi, evenly, no be one man kill dis game. Wi all dey slow for defence, wi all dey rush wen wi get di ball, wi no dey talk to each oda for di field. Dat na wetin don happen, plain, no more, no less.

Make una remember dat game, di one wi bin down two-nil for half-time, and wi still turn am, wi still carry am home for di end. Dat game no lost, e still dey inside una, abi make wi no forget so quick.

One ting wi go change dis second half: wi go hold di ball small-small, come build am, no more long ball wey nobody dey chase again. Just dat one, nothing more, nothing less.

Make I go round one time. Keeper, na you go tok, make you tok loud make everybody hear. Defence, make una hold di line, no let anybody pass una. Midfield, carry di ball, come give am forward, dat na una work. Frontline, make una dey ready, di first chance wey come, na una go take am. One plain ting each, nothing more, so nobody go carry dis whole match for im back alone, o.

Dis team no be three-nil, sha. Dis team na di kind wey dey play until di final whistle blow, whether e sweet or e no sweet, whether wahala full di ground or nothing. Dat na wetin wi be, and dat one no go change.

Whichever way e go, win or lose, wi go sidon togeda afta, wi go tok am well, sorry or sweet, wi go chop togeda as wi don always do. Dat one na promise, no dash, no lie for am.

Now make una rise up. Make una rise up, come out dere togeda, make wi go show dem wetin wi really be!
