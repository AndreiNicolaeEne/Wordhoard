The traveler: Chale, how much make I pay make you carry me go there?
The driver: Twenty cedis straight, no negotiation.
The traveler: Twenty too much o, make we say twelve.
The driver: Twelve? Chale you wan cheat me, this fuel no dey cheap sef.
The traveler: No vex, abeg, make we meet for sixteen.
The driver: Sixteen dey fine sha. Enter, make we dey go.
