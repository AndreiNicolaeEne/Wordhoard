The eldest: This debt wey you di run up and down for, na wahala go swallow you if you no hear me. Debt na like fire for dry bush: small-small e start, but if you no quench am kwik-kwik, e go burn the whole farm. Make you stretch your leg according to how your cloth long. Borrow-borrow na quick road to shame, my pikin. So abeg, stop dis your running up and down, before wahala go finish you finish.
