The eldest: My brother, na dis knee of mine dey give me wahala since. E don tey well-well. Anytime rain want fall, na like drummer dey beat inside dere, from left one to right one, morning till night. Na since '92 e start, and till now e no gree leave me.

The second elder: Ehen, knee. You dey compare knee wey dey pain small-small with wetin I dey carry? Abeg no make me vex o. This my waist, na proper wahala be that one. Three, four night every month, sleep no fit near me at all. I go dey turn for bed like person wey dem don dash bad medicine, my wife go just dey look me, she no fit even talk again.

The eldest: Sorry o, my friend, that one no easy sha.

The third elder: Una two dey talk as if una don suffer. Make una hear word small. Wetin una dey yarn na pikin matter. This my stomach, e don dey trouble me since una never born sef, since I be young man wey dey run for field. My mama, God rest her, used to warm water for fire, rub my belle with im, before una two sef sabi wetin knee be. So who una think get am worst here, abi make we call vote?

The second elder: Na me o, no be by mouth. This waist go finish me one day, mark my word.

The third elder: You dey hear this one? I don tell una the stomach pass everybody own, e dey since world start sef. Na senior wahala be dis, respect am.

The eldest: Una two dey lie for my front, but who go win, na God go judge o.

The second elder: God self no fit judge dis one, e too plenty for one table.

Nobody gree back down, everybody just dey hold im matter tight, until laughter come carry the whole table, and dem forget who win, dem forget who lose, na only laugh remain for there, well-well.
