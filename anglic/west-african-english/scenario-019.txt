My friend: Ah ah, no try hide am from me o. Yesternight I dey my room and I dey hear una through the wall like say na film dem dey shoot. Wetin sef happen make the two of una dey shout like that?

Me: My friend abeg make I just tell you the whole thing sotay you go understand, no be small thing at all at all, but the way e start, e no even suppose reach that level.

My friend: Na so. Talk make I hear.

Me: OK so e start with light bill. Just light bill, nothing pass that. I don tell my partner say make we pay the bill before month end because last time dem cut the light for three days and we still dey suffer for that one. My partner say make we wait small, say money no dey reach reach, we get other things to handle. I say na so una dey talk anytime we get bill, but when e reach phone credit or when senior brother dey call for help, money dey find from somewhere.

My friend: Ah, so na that one start am.

Me: Na so e start o, small small. I raise my voice first, I no go lie to you. I say una nor dey serious with this house at all, and my partner come answer back the same way, voice up, say make I no dey talk to am like say I be pikin. From there the thing just dey climb up, up, up.

My friend: Na so wahala dey start.

Me: True true. Then na there I say the thing wey I no even mean, I say una nor dey care about this house or about me at all, say if I dey drop dead now una nor go even notice. I no mean am o, na anger dey talk, but the thing don already comot my mouth.

My friend: Ah, that kind talk dey pain person.

Me: E pain am well well. Then my partner come vex more, and na there the old matter come enter, the one wey we no dey touch again. My partner bring up how I never finish that course wey I bin dey do, how I dey always start something and no finish am, how na so I be since. That one nor even get anything to do with light bill, but you know how e dey be, when person vex, everything wey dey inside heart go comot.

My friend: Ehen. Old sore go always find road come out.

Me: Na so. By then I don vex reach where I no fit talk again, I just carry myself go the room and I bang the door hard, sotay the picture frame for the wall shake small. I no even mean to bang am like that, na my hand just dey heavy from anger.

My friend: That na the sound wey I hear o. I hear the door bang and I say ah, dis one serious.

Me: Na the door o, no be anything else, thank God. After that, silence. Full silence. My partner no answer me again, I no answer my partner again. We dey the same house the whole day, but na like say we dey two different country. My partner dey sitting room, I dey the bedroom, we no exchange word. We pass the better part of the day like that, morning to almost evening, each one dey vex for im own corner.

My friend: Ah, that kind silence dey heavy pass shouting sef.

Me: E heavy well well. But na my partner come break am first. Around evening my partner knock for the door small small and say, "I no fit continue like this, make we talk." That na how e start, soft soft voice, no more shouting inside am.

My friend: Ah, good thing.

Me: Yes o. And then my partner come apologize, say sorry for bringing up that course matter, say e know say that thing nor concern the fight for that day at all, say e just dey use am as weapon because e vex, and e shame for am. My partner beg say make I forgive am for that one particular.

My friend: Na fine apology be that, e specific.

Me: Na so, e no just say sorry sorry for everything, e call the exact thing. And I too, I no fit just sidon dey chop the apology alone, I say my own sorry, for the words wey I talk say una nor care about me, I say that one na lie wey vex carry comot from my mouth, say I know say una dey care, I just dey vex reach where I want pain am back. And I add say sorry for the door too, say I no mean to bang am like that.

My friend: Good, good. So how una know say the thing don really settle?

Me: You know the fine thing? My partner call me "my darling" again, small small voice, the way my partner dey call me since way back when everything sweet between us. That word no dey come out easy when person still dey vex, so when I hear am, I know say the ice don start to melt.

My friend: Ehen, that na the sign.

Me: Na so. And then the thing wey really seal am, my partner go kitchen go make tea, two cup, and bring am come the room, put one for my side without saying anything again, just put am there. That small small gesture, na im finish the whole matter finish. We no need talk again, we just dey sip the tea together for the bed, quiet quiet, but na sweet quiet dis time, no be the vex kind.

My friend: Ah, sweet ending o. But make I tell you something.

Me: Wetin now?

My friend: Since that night, anytime I see una, I go just knock for wall small small make una know say I dey there o, make una lower una voice, ha ha.

Me: Ah ah, you go still dey remind me of that one, abi? But na true, since that day things dey fine well well between us, even better pass before sef. We even dey laugh about the whole thing now, my partner go tease me say make I no vex reach where I go start banging door for una landlord house o, because na im go come collect money from us for the door.

My friend: Ha ha, that one true sha. But I glad say una don settle am, because that night I dey worry small for una.

Me: No vex, my friend, the wall get ear but the heart get better memory. We don pass am, and we don sweet again, better pass how we bin dey before.
