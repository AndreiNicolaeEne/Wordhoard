Me: Aunty, abeg, that egusi soup wey you dey make the other day, teach me the recipe now, make I write am down before I forget.

Aunty: Ehen, you wan learn am. Okay make I try remember, my mama never write anything down for paper o, na so she teach me with mouth, na so I go teach you with mouth too.

Aunty: First na the red oil. You go pour am for pot, na like half bottle, the small bottle self, no be the big gallon one. Make e hot well well before anything else enter am.

Aunty: Then you slice your onion, fry am small... ah, wait, I never talk of the crayfish o! That one nearly pass me. You must add the crayfish, if you forget the crayfish the soup no go get body at all, e go just taste like water.

Me: So na after the crayfish you go put the egusi?

Aunty: Yes na so, you put the egusi, make e fry inside the oil small small. And that step, my mama used to warn me plenty times, she go always say make you no rush the frying o. If you rush am, the egusi go raw for inside and the whole pot go spoil, no be small thing.

Me: How I go sabi say the egusi don fry finish, since person no get clock for kitchen that time?

Aunty: The oil go come float for top, e go dey shine like crown, na so you know say e don ready, no need clock at all.

Me: And the pepper and salt?

Aunty: That one, I no fit give you exact measure sha, na by eye I dey judge am, na so my mama teach me, taste small small till e correct.

Me: If ugu no dey, I fit use spinach instead?

Aunty: You fit try am, but I go tell you true, e no go taste the exact same, the ugu get one sweetness wey the spinach no get.
