The child: Mummy, abeg now, that phone I don dey beg you since. Everybody for my class don get one.

Me: I don tell you why we no fit buy that phone now. Na money, plain and simple, no be that I no love you. The money no dey. School fees dey wait, light bill dey wait, everything for this house dey wait for me. I no go lie to you and dodge am, na the truth I dey tell you.

The child: Mummy, if I dey wash plate every day, even Saturday, even Sunday, you go buy the phone for me now?

Me: My dear, washing plate na your work as it is, e no be trade wey you go carry go exchange for phone. But make I tell you one thing, if by December when I don collect my bonus, if the money dey settle small for hand, we go talk about it again. No promise o, but we go see.

The child: Ok o. But wait, my birthday dey come next month, that one no fit work?

Me: Birthday no be ATM machine. Money no go just show because na your birthday, abi you think say naira dey grow for tree?

The child: But that girl wey dey sit near me, her own mama don buy iPhone for her since last month sef!

Me: That girl mama and me, we no dey run the same house o. Wetin dey happen for their side no concern us for here, una different-different families, different-different pocket. Every house get how e dey manage itself.

The child: (mumbling) Sha...

Me: Sha nothing. Make I tell you wetin I fit do, that small earpiece wey you don dey point at for market, I go dash you that one this weekend. E no be the big phone, but at least you go fit dey listen your music well well.

The child: (sighing) Ok o, thank you ma. E better pass nothing sha.

Me: That's my girl. Small-small, we go get there.
