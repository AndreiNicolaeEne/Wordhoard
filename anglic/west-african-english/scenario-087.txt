The neighbor: I hear say your goat bin sick well-well las' night, weti really happen?

Me: Ah my friend, no be small thing o, that night bin hard well-well. Na since evening I first notice say the goat no di chop again, e no di waka fine, e just di stand for one corner, head down, like something di worry am inside. I tell miself say no, I no fit leave this animal for outside night like that make anything happen am, so I carry mat come sidon near im pen, I stay there with am.

For the whole first hour nothing di change at all, I just dey watch am, watch am, watch am small-small, my eye no even blink. I try give am water sef, I di coax am, I put the bowl for im mouth because e no gree drink for imself, e just di turn im head like say e no want.

One senior for the compound wey pass come tell me, "Take-time o, no let the cold catch am for night like this," and I just dey nod, I no even answer well because my mind don dey the goat only.

Round midnight the thing turn bad, e start di shake small, im breathing come rough rough, and na so my heart come jump, I no go lie. Wahala catch me for that moment sef, I di ask miself say abi na wrong thing I do keeping am for house instead to carry am go where dem fit look am well. But I no gree leave am. I cover am small blanket make e warm, I continue di talk to am low low, the whole night, the way you go talk make pikin wey dey fear no cry.

Na near morning, small-small, I catch am, the breathing come steady, im ear come twitch when cock crow, like say normal thing di return for am. By the time sun don rise well, e no fully okay yet but e steady, no be like before.

Na only when I see say e steady, na so tiredness come catch me one time, my whole body just come loose, I sidon there, I no fit even stand.

The neighbor: Sorry o. God don helep.

Me: Yes o, we go continue watch am today sef, make e no relapse.
