That day I close late for work, later than I even plan am, and by the time I dey waka go house the sky don dark finish, no be the dark wey get streetlight, na the real Accra dark wey you fit only see shadow for front. I dey go my usual way, the short cut wey dey pass behind the football park, when I hear say person dey waka behind me, the footsteps dey land almost same time with mine, small small.

I no wan turn round make person think say I dey fear, so I test am small small. First I slow my step, make the person pass if na so be am. The steps too slow down. I no like that one at all. Then I speed up small, my heart don start to dey beat like drum, and the steps behind me speed up too, same time same time, like shadow wey no wan release me.

I no waka the short cut again. I cut go the main road side, where the shop dem still dey open and taxi dey pass, abeg make I no go meet wahala for that dark corner. Just once, before I cross, I look back small, one time, quick quick. I no see anybody clear, only shadow and one gate wey dey swing for wind. That one no tell me anything true, e no comfort me at all, e even shake me small-small pass before.

The last small distance to the house, chale, I no go lie, I waka like say person dey chase me for real, though nobody see me run. I reach, I push the door, I lock am, I stand there small, back for the door, breathing hard well well.

Till today I no know who or wetin was that. Maybe na nothing sha, maybe na my own head dey play me. Whenever I tell the story now I dey laugh small, because how I dey sure that night, as if I don see person with my own eye, e dey shame me small-small.
