The relative: Ehh, weti di wahala self? Dem tell me say you no well since.

Me: Na so, my broda. I no go lie you. Tri days now e start. First na di joint dem, dey pain me well-well, and di throat sef come dey scratch like sand for inside. Yesterday na so di fever join everi ting, e come hot me tire.

The relative: Ah, sorry o. So how you dey today?

Me: Today small easy pass yesterday, sha, but I no fine reach where I go talk say na normal me be. Di body still dey weak-weak.

The relative: Weti you don dey do for di ting?

Me: First ting, I dey gargle warm water wit salt for di throat, morning and night. Di fever no want kolo by eye so I take wet cloth, put am for my forehead, dey change am di whole night make e no ova-hot me. I don dey chop only broth and weak tea, nothing heavy for stomach. Anytime sleep catch me, I dey allow am, I no dey fight am.

The relative: Abeg rest well-well o, no ova-push yasef, you hear? Take-time, abi you wan make dis ting hold you for longer?

Me: I hear you well. But make I tell you my own line sef: dat fever, if e climb pass husay e dey now, or if I start to dey find breath well-well, na so I go waka go hospital, no wait again. Before dat time reach, I no dey go anywhere.

The relative: OK na. You want make I dash you some food, or make I come sidon wit you small, an hour like dat?

Me: Ehh... di food, you fit carry am come, dat one go helep true true. Di sitting sef, no bad, but no worry yasef too much, I no wan make you comot for your own work because of me. Sha, if you get small time, come small, I no go vex.

The relative: Na so. I dey come. Take care of yasef well-well o, my broda.
