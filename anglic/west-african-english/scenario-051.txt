Me: My guy, if money just fall for my hand now now, like ten million wey no get owner, na phone I go first buy. The newest one, the one wey dey shine for shop window since.

My friend: Ah, you dey craze. Ten million just land and na phone na the first thing wey dey your mind? Me I go first pay house. Better house, good quarter, no more this small place wey water di enter anytime rain fall.

Me: House na later matter. Make man enjoy small small first na, life no long.

My friend: Enjoy how? You go chop the whole money finish, come remain with empty hand, dey cry after. Some of am must sidon for bank, make e dey grow small small.

Me: Sha, bank for this our side no even dey give better interest. Better I carry some go start small business, na him bi sensible thing.

My friend: Ah now you dey talk true talk. Okay make we share am: half for business, quarter for house, the remain quarter make we use enjoy, chop small, dash our people small small too.

Me: Abeg no forget say senior for house dey wait him own dash. If we no dash am, wahala go plenty, na so e be.

My friend: True true. But wait small, husay we go even see ten million wey just fall like that one? Nobody dey ova-dash person like that for this Douala.

Me: Ah sorry o, na so we dey take-time build castle for air. We don ova-plan the whole money finish, we never even see the first coin sef.

My friend: (laughing well well) We dey serious like say na real thing. Oga money never even come, we don already fight for how to share am, abi?

Me: Sef make we just laugh am small. If e come tomorrow sha, you go remember dis our plan o.
