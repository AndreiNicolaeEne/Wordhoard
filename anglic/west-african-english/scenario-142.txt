Today na the day o. Since since, since that time wey person dey worry me about this compound wahala, I just vex, I open mouth, I swear say make nobody hear my voice again if I no finish the whole thing in one day, no matter wetin. And na today be that day.

The very moment I wake, before eye even open well-well, everything land for my head one time, the whole stress, the whole long day wey dey wait. My body no even want comot for bed sef. I just dey lie down dey stare ceiling like say ceiling fit help me.

But I don swear am. I don talk am for people ear. So make I no dull myself again, make I rise.

I dey grumble the whole way comot house, dey mutter under my breath. Wetin make sense to swear like that one? Who bin send me message? Nobody. Na my own big mouth. My own mouth don finish me today, abi na my own head wey no dey work well-well that time.

Reach where the work dey, I start am one time. My workmate see me dey frown face, e ask:

Workmate: Oga, you well? You dey vex since morning o.

Me: I dey manage. Na this promise I swear since, I must finish am today.

Workmate: Ah, sorry o, that kain thing dey take strength. Make you no take-time, but no rush yourself sef.

Me: Abeg no even mind me, make I just chop this stress finish today.

Every stage wey I pass, na new thing to complain: the length of am, the sun wey hot pass mate, my own bad head wey agree to this kain thing for the first place. Small-small, na so I dey complain, dey work.

Halfway reach, my body wan quench, my mind whisper say make I just leave am, nobody go know. I stop small, catch breath, I talk to myself flat: I don swear am. That thought, I no even give am road to grow, I cut am for root one time.

I grit my teeth, I continue. The worst part pass, I no fall my hand, I no waka comot.

By evening, I finish the whole thing, exactly as I talk am since. I dey complain throughout the day, true, sha I no quit one single time. Promise na promise o.
