The teller: Chale, wetin I see last night, e no be something I go just forget o.

The skeptic: Abeg no start. You dey sleep small, dream catch you, dat's all.

The teller: I swear I no dey sleep. I dey wide awake, my eye dey open well-well, I dey hold my phone dey chat with my sister sef when I see am.

The skeptic: Okay tell me wetin you see make I hear.

The teller: I dey waka come house from station, di light for di road don off since, only moon dey shine small. Then I see one woman dey stand for di other side, she dey wear di same cloth wey I wear dat day, di same slippers, everything. She just dey look me, no blink, nothing.

The skeptic: Chale relax, na your shadow you see, or maybe person wey resemble you small, dark road fit confuse eye like that, is it not so?

The teller: No be shadow at all! Shadow no fit wear slippers wey get di cut mark I myself do for am with blade, right for di small toe side. I look am well-well, di mark dey there plain plain.

The skeptic: Hmm okay, maybe na low sugar, you never chop since morning, brain dey play tricks when belle empty.

The teller: I don chop before I comot house, full stomach sef. And na not only me see am o, my neighbour wey dey come behind me, she scream first before I even see anything, she point am with her hand dey shake. Two of us no fit dream di same dream together, abi?

The skeptic: Chale this one dey give me wahala for head sha, but I no go believe am still, my sister.

The teller: I swear on my mother life, every word na true, I no dey lie. That thing tilt her head di same way my own mother dey tell me say I dey tilt my head, then she just comot, no trotro pass, no wind blow, she just comot like say she neva dey there.

The skeptic: Hmm, una this story dey too much, but I no go fit believe until I myself see am with my own eye o.

The teller: Believe or no believe, na so e happen. I no go change my story, not even one word, not even small small.
