Groom: Abeg make we face this list o, because as e dey now, e don pass wetin the hall fit take by like thirty.

Bride: I don see am. Na real wahala be that.

Groom: Make we start small small, your third cousins, that your colleague wey you no even dey greet for office again, that your uncle pikin wey we never see since. If we touch those ones, e go come down by half of the thirty sharp.

Bride: Wetin you mean my side? Na only my side get people you no dey greet? You never mention your own aunty wey una no talk since last Christmas.

Groom: That one different na. But abeg, that my senior wey stand for me the day I dey interview, that name gats to stay, no matter wetin happen. I no fit touch am.

Bride: So na so. If that name go stay, na somebody go comot from my side make the number balance.

Groom: Na so I talk am o, no vex.

Bride: This your family sef, una dey always get more space for this kind list, na so e dey be from the beginning, anytime we dey count, na my side dem dey cut first.

Groom: Abi na me carry the whole thing write am myself? Everybody get their own wahala for this one o.

Bride: Make we just do small-small gathering for house for the ones wey no fit enter the main one.

Groom: E no go work at all, forget am. If we start that kain thing, everybody wey no see invitation go ask question, and person go vex pass how e for vex now. That idea don die.

Bride: Okay, okay. Make we recount.

They go through the list again from the top, calling each name out one one and counting am for finger, and by the time dem reach the last page the number still no gree balance.

Groom: This list still pass by like twelve o, e never even reach.

Bride: Chai. Take-time, we go find who go still comot.

Groom: Fine. That colleague, I fit shift am go the small list, sha, but I no dey promise say na final.

Bride: We never finish this thing at all, but at least e don reduce small.
