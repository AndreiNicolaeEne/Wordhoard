Younger neighbour: Uncle, dis sun de hot today o!
The elder: Yes na, e don hot well well, e pass Tuesday sef.
Younger neighbour: Ehen, na so.
The elder: Long time back we bin get one year like dis, heat pass three month, no rain fall at all, we tey tey suffer. Na so life de go.
Younger neighbour: E go rain tomorrow, abi?
The elder: Sha, tomorrow na tomorrow business, make we see o.
