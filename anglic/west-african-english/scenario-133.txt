Dis year wei don pass, na de garden plot dem give me back of de hill I dey tell una about, from how e start to how e end. Make I no waste una time, I go tell am small-small de way e happen.

Around February time, when de harmattan still dey blow and cold dey catch bodi small for evening, na so I dey siddon dey plan wetin I go put dis year. I no rush am o. I dey ask ma senior wei get im own plot near de bottom by de stream, I dey watch how de sun dey behave for morning, I dey check de soil weti I get for dat corner. We agree say make I put okra, garden egg, pepper, and small corn for one side. Sef I bin dey want plant cassava leaf too but I tell masef make I no over-load, one hand no fit carry too many basket.

By de time de cold mornings don pass well-well, na so I start to plant, na like three four week after. Weather bin kind to us dat time, sun dey but e no too hard, small rain dey fall enough make de ground stay soft, no need to carry water go every day. Everything come up strong I tell you, de okra leaf dem green well-well, de garden egg dem no waste time to show demsef. I dey happy for weti I see, I dey tell masef say dis year go better pass last year.

Na so trouble start show face. One kind small beetle dem come dey chop de okra leaf, dey make hole for am like say person don shoot am with pellet gun. I no know wetin to call de insect but I know say if I no do something quick e go finish de whole bed. I mix wood ash with pepper water, na so ma senior bin teach me, I spray am for de leaf dem morning and evening. E work small, I no go lie, de beetle dem reduce but dem no finish clean. Some week de leaf dem still get hole, but de plant dem still dey grow so I let am be, I no fit fight nature every single day.

Den de rain wei bin dey help us just stop, na like say heaven vex. For weeks, I mean weeks o, I dey carry bucket dey go de standpipe morning before I go work, dey water de bed dem one by one before de sun rise reach top. Evening self I dey come back dey do de same thing again. Ma back dey pain me, ma hand dem get callus for de bucket handle. But if I no do am de whole plot go waka commot, so I no get choice, I just carry go, come water, carry go again.

Na dat water wahala bring de trouble between me and de plot-holder wei get im own patch next to mine. We dey share de same tap for dat area, and de arrangement bin say we go dey take turns, morning for one, evening for de oda, switch every week. But one week e come dey fetch water for im plot for morning time when e sabi say na my turn. I no like wahala but dis one I no fit let am pass.

Me: You self you sabi say dis morning na my turn, abi I dey lie?

The neighbor: Ah, I forget, de time just pass me small.

Me: Na so-so forget you dey forget for dis water matter, e no fine at all.

De thing almost cause bad blood between us, we no even dey greet each oda proper for some days. Na de elder wei get de plot for top de hill come call us both, e sit us down, e say make we no allow small water spoil de peace wei dey between neighbor. E say make we write de turn dem for paper, tape am for de tap so nobody go forget again, and if person miss im turn make e do de oda person's chore one time to balance am. We agree, we shake hand, and since dat day de water business no bring problem again. Sorry too much bin dey pass between us before we settle am, but at de end na so-so peace remain.

By mid-year na de garden egg bin dey shine pass all. Dat one no get mercy, e just dey produce and produce, bunch after bunch, I dey pick am tree times for week and e still no finish. I dash some to ma neighbor dem, I dash some to de church, I still get plenty wei I dey sell for market. Na dat one make de whole year worth am sha.

But while de garden egg dey do wonders, de corn come face im own wahala. Bird dem come dey chop de corn for de cob before I even ready to harvest am, dey peck am from de top, spoil de whole thing before I fit reach am. I try tie shiny paper for stick round de bed, I try make small noise-maker with tin can, but bird sef dem too clever, dem just dey shift to de part I no cover. By de time I harvest wetin remain, na half of wetin I bin dey expect.

Den de storm come. E flatten whole rows of ma pepper and some of de corn wei still standing, de wind and rain no gree spare anything dat night. I wake up de following morning I just siddon dey look de plot, I no fit talk. But wetin man go do, make we no cry over am, I call ma junior wei sabi dis kind work, we spend de whole weekend dey pull de stem dem back up, dey tie am to stick with rope, dey pack soil round de root make dem stand again. Some of dem survive, some no survive, but we try, and dat one give me joy small.

By de time year end reach and I tally everything, de garden egg carry de day clear, no argument about dat one. De okra also do fine despite de beetle wahala, de pepper manage after de storm, but de corn na de one wei suffer pass, both from de bird dem and de storm. I trade some of ma garden egg and pepper to anoda grower wei get plenty cassava, we swap am, I collect cassava from am wei I need pass wetin I get. Na so we dey do for dis area, nobody dey waste, weti you no need you exchange am for weti you need.

I clear de plot after dat, pull out de old stem dem, turn de soil, cover am make e rest small before next planting season reach. Looking back now, I go say na good year overall, e get im wahala true, de beetle, de dry spell, de water quarrel, de bird dem, de storm, but e also get plenty win, de garden egg alone make me proud. If you ask me make I do am again, I go still do am, only next time I go plant less corn and more of wetin de bird dem no like to chop. Na so farming dey be, you learn small-small every year, you no go know everything one time, and dat na de truth, is it not so.
