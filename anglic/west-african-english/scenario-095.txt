The elder: Make we start small small. This clock, weti we go do with am?

The younger: Na me im promise am. Papa bin tell me for im own mouth, "when I don go, this clock na for you." You no fit come deny am now like say I di lie.

The elder: Papa never tell me any such thing o. If na so, why im no write am for paper? You just di talk anyhow because na you want am.

The younger: I no di lie! Papa and I close well-well, you sef know sey na true. Na so e be, no be so?

The elder: Sha, leave the clock small, make we talk about the money. The 700 mille wey remain, weti happen to am? I hear say you don ova-chop some already.

The younger: Who tell you dat one? I no touch the money o. Na lie pure, and you know sey na lie.

The elder: The bank paper no dey lie na. E di finish small small since when? You go explain am today.

The younger: You know sef say na not about clock or money. Na because Papa never even sick reach one week and e don leave us just like that. That one pain me pass everything.

The elder: ... I know. Me too, e pain me well well. I no even sabi weti I di fight for again.

The aunty: Wetin dis noise be, eh? Una two, make una siddon small. Papa no go rest fine if e hear say una di quarrel over im property like this. Take-time, abeg.

The younger: Sorry, aunty. We no mean am like that.

The elder: Make we just continue tomorrow. Today no good.

The younger: Na true.

Both of them quiet small, shame di show for their face, because na their own papa dem di fight over, and e just bury last week.
