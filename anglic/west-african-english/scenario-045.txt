I reach the tailor shop early early, because we bin agree say the dress go ready before weekend. The tailor bring the dress out from behind the curtain, holding am up like say na prize work. I look the thing one time and something no settle for my eye at all.

Me: Oga, wetin be this one now o. I order three button for the front, and straight hem, na so we agree, abi? This one get two button, and the hem dey slant like person cut am crooked.

The tailor: Madam, abeg no vex, this na exactly the design we take measurement for that day. Two button, angle hem, na wetin you point for the book.

Me: I no forget sef wetin I ask for. I bin talk am clear the first day, three button, straight hem, e clear for my head still. Take-time, check well well before you tell me na so we agree.

The tailor: Sha, make I no argue with customer for nothing. Make I go check the note self, the one I write that day.

The tailor come back holding small paper, reading am under the light.

The tailor: Ehen, I na check am now. The note say two button true true, that one na wetin you tell me. But for the hem, e write straight, no angle at all. Na me miss that one, sorry o, na my hand slip for the cutting.

Me: You see now sef. The button dem, dem correct, make we leave am so, no wahala for that one. But this hem, e must straight like we agree first time.

The tailor: No wahala, I go fix am. Make I carry the dress come back inside, I go redo the hem make e straight, small small, before two day pass. Yo people just like quality, that's why I no go rush am, make e come out fine well well.

Me: Aright, two day, I go hold you to that o. No more mistake abeg, my oga for house dey wait for this dress.

The tailor: Trust me, e go ready, sorry for the wahala, but we go settle am fine.
