The aunt: Chale, sit down small make I tell you something.

Me: Aunty, I dey here, I dey listen.

The aunt: I bin dey talk to your uncle wife last week, and she say the person wey I get in mind for you, everybody wey sabi am dey praise am well well, no be small talk o. She say the person dey work well, get better work, dey save money, no be lazy person. The temperament sef, patient somebody, no be person wey go dey shout for house like mad man. And the family, chale, respectable people, church people, no wahala for that side at all. Three things, all correct.

Me: Aunty, I hear you o, but...

The aunt: But wetin? Wetin fit wrong with person like that, abi tell me. I dey wait.

Me: Aunty, e no be say anything wrong dey there. Na just say work dey catch me too much now, I no even get breathing space, make I no talk of husband matter.

The aunt: Ah, busy, busy, always busy. Your own senior sister talk the same thing when we bring person come, "I no ready, I no ready." Today she dey thank God we no gree leave her alone, is it not so. She dey happy for that house now, she go tell you herself if you ask am. Time no dey wait for anybody o, this thing no be play-play.

Me: Aunty, I hear you well, and I respect wetin you dey try do for me, sha. But make I take my time small, abeg, I no ready yet.

The aunt: Okay, okay, I no go force you, you don grow, you be big person now. But make you know say the door still dey open. Whenever you change your mind, just come tell me, the offer still dey stand there. I no go chase you sha, but no make time waste am, o.
