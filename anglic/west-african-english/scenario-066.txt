Chale, you don move come this street now and everybody dey mention "the animal, the animal" like na proverb, abi? Sit down small, make I run the whole gist for you from the beginning, because if I no tell you well-well you go dey confuse anytime somebody bring up the story.

So the first time, na my neighbour brother wey tell am. E say na night time, e dey come from the junction, and e see one long thing dey cross the gutter near the mango tree, tail long-long, e run enter the bush behind the wall. E come tell people the next morning, but you know how dat kain gist go: everybody just laugh, dey say the boy don dey watch too much film, or maybe na cat wey e see and e dey exaggerate for himself. Even the boy own mother sef tell am, "abeg go sleep, no come frighten this house with your dream." So the whole thing die there, nobody take it serious. Just imagination, everybody talk am like that.

Then no be long after, na the security man for the blue gate house see am, in daylight this time, broad afternoon. And you know that man, e no be person wey go carry lie come street. E dey watch the gate quiet-quiet since I small, e no dey gossip, e no dey add pepper for story. So when e come out say,

The watchman: I see the thing wit my own eye, e dey by the gutter, e dey turn the rubbish bin, e dey pick something inside, then e look me one time and e waka enter that bush corner behind the empty plot.

, chale, that time the whole street change gear. Because if that man talk say e see am, then na true talk, no be film again.

After that one, sightings come plenty small small. People start dey see am for early morning, near the bins for this side and that side, sometimes for people compound wey dey grow tomato and pepper for their small garden. E go poke the soil, e go scatter the leaves, one time somebody's pepper plants just dey uproot like that, no reason. Some people vex say na the thing don spoil their labour, others say maybe na goat or somebody's dog, nobody fit prove wetin exactly do the garden.

Na from there the street split into two camp proper. One side dey say, leave the animal, e no dey trouble anybody serious, make we just secure our things and life go continue. The other side dey vex, dey say wahala go happen one day if una no deal with it now, before e attack somebody pikin or bite person. Every evening for the corner shop, that na the only topic.

The elder: This bush no be town before. All this houses, all this cement, na we build am on top the animal own land. If e waka come check us, e no dey do anything to offend God, we just need chase am go, but no need to kill.

The corner shop woman: Chase am go where? E go come back tomorrow. Better make somebody deal with it proper, one time, finish. I no want my children dey fear to go outside for night.

Then the thing wey really vex people happen: one household lost their fowl, three of them, one morning them see only feathers scatter for the yard, no proof, no evidence, nothing. Immediately everybody blame the animal, say na him chop the fowl finish. But another neighbour argue say maybe na dog from the next street, because that kain dog dey roam plenty for night, and nobody see the animal near that compound sef. Argument start there, people dey shout for the compound, some dey defend the animal say make una no just dash blame anyhow, other people say who else go do such a thing, wetin animal go dey wander for our yard for if no be looking for food.

That argument follow street meeting straight, and eh, that meeting was hot o. Everybody gather for the open space near the water tank, oga for the area, the landlords, even people wey no dey usually attend, all of them come. Voices dey raise up, one man dey bang table, another woman dey shout say una too dey exaggerate this thing, e no be lion, na small small bush animal wey dey lost, no need to kill am like criminal. The other side dey shout back say make we no wait till something bad happen before we act, take-time no dey work for this kain matter.

One household, the one near the empty plot, that na the household wey lost the fowl, decide say them go handle it themselves, no need call anybody. Them buy net, them set trap for their own yard, them even try block the small hole for the fence with block and cement. All that effort, the animal just find another way come, e no even fall inside their trap, and the wire net them buy just tear finish, waste of money, waste of time. That failure na the thing wey open everybody eye say this thing no be small small work for one household alone.

Then the night wey change everything really happen. One of the young men, e dey come home late from work, e dey walk quiet near the gutter, no torch light, and the thing just jump out from behind one bin, brush pass e leg, before e run enter the bush. That boy shout like person wey see spirit, whole street wake up, dogs start dey bark everywhere. E dey shake when e dey narrate the story after, e say e never fear like that before in e life.

After that night, argument reach head proper. Some people say this na proof say the thing dey dangerous, e fit attack person anytime. Others still hold say no, the animal na just unlucky for where e dey wander, e no chase the boy, e no bite am, e just dey run pass because e too dey fear human being sef, na coincidence wey scare person, no be attack.

Because the street no fit agree, oga for the area suggest say make them call somebody wey sabi this kain thing well, person wey dey handle wildlife for real work, no be person wey go just guess. So them call one man, experienced person wey dey work with animal control before, e come check the whole area, e look the bins, e look the garden, e look the small hole for the fence, e even see the footprint near the water tank.

The wildlife man: This kain animal, na civet, e dey shy naturally, e no dey attack human unless una corner am for tight space. E dey come out because your bins dey open, and your compound dey give am plenty hiding place. Make una secure the bins well, block the small holes, no chase am with stick, just make e no find reason to stay too close.

E advise them no to kill or trap am rough-rough, just make the street secure their environment small small, and if e persist for one particular compound after that, then them fit call proper wildlife people come relocate am. That one settle the street mind, because both camp fit hear something them can accept: the one side hear say no need kill, the other side hear say concrete steps go happen.

So since then, chale, things change small small. Almost every house done buy proper bin with cover, some people even use rope tie the cover extra. That watchman, and one or two other people, dem just dey keep half eye when them dey pass night, no be like guard duty proper, just small awareness. Sightings still dey happen small times, but nobody dey shout anymore, person go just say "ah, the visitor don pass again" and continue their business.

These days when the story come up, like now with you dey ask, people just dey laugh small, dey tell am like film wey dem watch, not like something wey go frighten anybody serious again. Everybody get their own version, everybody go add small pepper for how them tell am, but the real gist na wetin I just tell you now. Alarm don finish, na story remain, and that na how this whole thing settle for our street.
