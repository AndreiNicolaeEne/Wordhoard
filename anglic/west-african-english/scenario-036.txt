Ehen, make I tell you the whole ting from the start, because you no bin dey and I know say you dey wonder how e go so.

This year na me bin say make I be the one to host the Krismes food, first time proper. Since bebi bin dey born, na my mama dey always host, but this year she say make I try am, she don tire small. My mama tell me, "take your time, no rush, food no dey run," but wetin I go do, the day self no gree wait for anybody. Wen she first hand the ting over to me, my belly bin dey turn upside down, sef. I no fit sleep proper for one full week, I dey think am, I dey think am, I dey imagine wetin go happen if e no work.

So I sidan one evening, I draw up the menu wit pen and paper like say I dey plan wedding. I write jollof, I write cassava leaf, I write groundnut soup, I write fried fish, I write chicken, I write dodo, I write salad, I write even ginger beer wey I go make my own self, no bottle wan. By the time I finish write, I look the paper, I say "wetin be this, na who go cook all this?" But I no reduce am. I just say make I try, God go helep.

Then the guest list. First time na me and my sisto and one or two, small small ting. But wen word don go out say I dey host, everybody start to add demsef. My workmate say make she come with her husband. My oga for office say make e no vex if e pass by small. My auntie call, she say she dey bring her three pikin dem. By the time I count am, the ten wey I bin plan don become almost twenty. I no even fit vex, because na so we dey do, but my belly bin dey turn more.

I go market two days before, I don write list wella, I dey go from stall to stall. Everything I see, na so I buy, pepper, onion, palm oil, chicken, fish, cassava leaf plenty plenty. But the one ting wey I no fit find anywhere, na the particular smoke fish wey my mama always dey put for the cassava leaf, the special one. I waka the whole market, I ask this seller, I ask that seller, dem all say "e don finish, come back tomorrow," but tomorrow na the day self, I no get time again. I almost cry for market, sha. Finally one auntie for the corner say she get one wey remain small, she just dash me small discount because she see say I don tire waka.

The night before, I no sleep at all, I dey tell you true. I dey wash rice, I dey pound pepper, I dey marinate the chicken, I dey chop onion till my eye dey water like river. Na midnight I sidan finally, I look the kitchen, everything don scatter for table, and I say to mysef, "make I write out the morning schedule so I no go confuse mysef." I take paper, I write: six o'clock rice, seven o'clock stew, eight o'clock fish fry, nine o'clock everything don ready. E fine well well for paper.

But you know how morning dey do person. Six o'clock come meet me sleep, because na three o'clock I bin close eye. By the time I wake, na almost seven, and the whole schedule wey I bin write don spoil finish. I dey run up and down like fowl wey dem wan kill.

Na so the first disaster come meet me. That chicken, na him bring the whole trouble that day. I put am for oven, I go check the rice, I forget the time, and by the time I remember, smoke don dey come from the kitchen. I run go, I open the oven, the chicken don burn one side black like charcoal, the smell don spread the whole house. I stand there, I no know whether make I cry or make I laugh. I just say "God, no be today o."

I quick quick cut off the burnt side, I season the rest wella, I add extra stew on top to cover the taste, I say make God helep me, if person no look too well dem no go notice.

Na that time one guest come knock, my sisto workmate, she come early pass everybody, before I even bathe. I open door, my face don show finish say ting no dey alright. She look me...

The guest: Sorry o, wetin dey happen, make I helep, no vex.

Me: My sisto, everything just dey scatter, the chicken burn, I never even peel plantain.

The guest: No stress yasef, make we do am together, we go finish.

So she come dey peel plantain, I dey stir stew, both of us dey sweat for that small kitchen wey no even big reach. By eight o'clock the whole house don smell like Krismes proper, but the kitchen self na war zone. Stove get four burner but I get like six pot wey wan use am, so rice dey wait for stew to move, stew dey wait for fish, and fish dey vex say oven no free because chicken still dey there dey heal from im disaster. Everybody wey pass by kitchen dey shake dem head small.

But sef, one ting come surprise me pass everything, the cassava leaf. I bin no even confident for that one, because na the fish I bin worry about pass, but wen I taste am, e come out sweet pass anything I ever cook before. I taste am, I taste am again, I say "abi na me cook this so?"

By the time everything ready, na almost two o'clock afternoon, wen I bin plan say food go don ready by twelve. Everybody don sidan since eleven, dem dey hungry, dem dey drink juice, dem dey manage. But nobody vex, because na so Krismes food dey be, una know say time no dey ever match paper.

Wen food finally land for table, everybody rush take plate. First bite, the room quiet small, everybody dey chew, dey nod. My auntie say...

The auntie: Ah, this cassava leaf, who teach you this one? E sweet o!

My oga say...

The oga: The jollof better well well, no be small ting.

But my sisto, she no fear to talk true, she taste the chicken, she just look me straight...

The sisto: This chicken, wetin do am? E dry small, and I dey feel small burn taste for inside.

Everybody laugh, I no vex, I just say "I know, I know, make we no talk about that one again, abeg."

The cassava leaf finish first, before anything, people dey scrape the pot, dey ask if more remain. That one na the star, no argument. The jollof come second, but the cassava leaf carry the day well well.

By evening, when everybody don chop finish, dey sidan dey gist, my sisto lean come me, she say...

The sisto: So, next year, you go host again, abi you don tire?

Me: My sisto, abeg no ask me that question tonight, make I rest small first, ask me next month.

Everybody laugh, my mama just dey smile for corner like say she know say I go still do am again, sef, because that na how e be, the wahala, the burning, the running up and down, and still, wen everybody chop well and dem dey laugh together, you go forget the wahala sharp sharp, you go just dey wait for the next one to come.
