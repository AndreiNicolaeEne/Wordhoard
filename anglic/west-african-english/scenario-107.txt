She hold me close for the station, small time before she let go.

Mother: Take care well-well for road o, no rush yourself.

Me: I hear, Mama. The moment I reach, I go call you sef.

Mother: My pikin, carry this small money, buy something chop for road.

Me: I go bring something come back for you, Mama, no worry.

Then the bus horn blow and I enter, waving till I no fit see her again.
