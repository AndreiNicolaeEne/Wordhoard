My friend, make I tell you how dis moving day bin go, because na wahala full-full from morning reach night, and you no bin dey there to see am with your own eye.

We bin plan say we go start seven, seven sharp, before di sun vex and start to beat person. But you know how these things dey be. By di time everybody wake, wash, chop small something, e don pass eight, e don near nine. My junior brother nor even wake on time, and my mama dey shout for am from di parlour, "Come out, oya, di day nor go wait for you o!" So already we dey start late, and I dey tell myself, this one no go end well, I fit feel am for my bones.

Then before we even comot from di old house door, first trouble show face. Di key wey we dey use for di padlock, di one for di gate, e no gree turn again. E jam inside there like say e vex with us too, like e no want make we leave dat house. My oga brother-in-law dey twist and twist am, sweat dey run for im face, and e no move one inch. We beg neighbour man come look am, im try with oil, with wire, with everything, before di thing finally gree open. That padlock, e nor even sabi say na moving day, e just dey do wetin e want.

Na so we dey struggle for gate when we see one figure dey waka come down di road, and na my mama junior sister pikin, di one wey dey call am nephew, im just come stand up there like say na so e planned am. Nobody call am o, nobody text am say make e come. E just say im dey pass, e see say we dey carry load, so im join. That kind help sweet person well-well, because di boy strong pass all of us, and im dey lift box like say na pillow.

Now make I tell you wetin break my mama heart. Di plate, di big white plate wey her own mama bin give her since she marry, di one wey we no dey use for ordinary day, only for Christmas and when big person come visit, dat plate bin dey inside one box wey nobody pack well. Somebody put am for di bottom, put pot and pan on top, and when they carry di box comot from di lorry, di whole thing shift, and. CHIA!, di plate break for inside there, into three piece. My mama shout reach heaven. "Ah! Ah! Ah! Wetin una don do na! Dat plate, e don pass fifty years, na my mama own, una don break am finish!" Everybody freeze. Nobody talk. Even di boy wey come help, im just dey look ground. My mama vex small-small, then she just siddon for one chair, and she cry small. Nor be say na di plate self, na wetin di plate carry inside am.

And then, my friend, na one box, one only box, we never see am again till today. We no even sure wetin bin dey inside. Some person say na di one with old photo and papers, another person say na di one with kitchen cloth. All we sabi na say e bin dey there for di lorry, and by time we reach new house, e no dey. We call di driver, we call di boy wey help load am, everybody say dem no sabi. Sorry-o, dat box don waka comot from dis world, we no go ever see am again. E pain me till now sef.

Then na di wardrobe. Di big heavy one, di one my papa bin buy since when, im carpenter make am with real wood, no be dis plastic thing wey dey nowadays. To lift dat thing, na war. My oga uncle say make dem carry am flat, lay am down, push am like so. My junior brother say no, no, if we lay am down di mirror go crack, make we carry am standing up, two person for front, two for back. Dem dey argue for road, dey point finger, dey shout instruction wey nobody dey follow.

Uncle: "I dey tell una, lay am down, e go balance better!"

Junior brother: "You wan make di mirror break? Na so you wan do am?"

Uncle: "Wetin concern you with mirror, carry di ting make we go!"

So they carry am standing up, sweating, cursing under dem breath, and na so we reach di stairwell for di new place, and dat wardrobe, my friend, e no fit pass. We turn am dis way, we turn am dat way, we tilt am, we vex, we try again. E no fit pass, no matter how we turn am. Di stairwell narrow pass di wardrobe by inch, small-small inch, but e still no fit pass. We stand there sweating, breathing hard, everybody dey look each other like say person go find answer for air.

Na when we dey struggle so, di sky just turn. Cloud dey gather since morning small-small, but nobody dey mind am, everybody dey busy with dem own trouble. Then rain just come down heavy, no warning, no small rain to start, straight heavy rain. We dey run with box, dey cover load with tarpaulin, dey shout for one another, "Cover di mattress! Cover di mattress, make water no reach am!" Everybody wet, clothes dey stick for body, shoe dey make sound for road. Dat rain nor gree stop small-small, e just dey pour reach almost one hour.

By afternoon, everybody don tire, hungry too, and na dat time my mama neighbour, di one wey we no even call, bring big pot of rice and stew come stand for compound. She say, "Una no go work with empty belly, make una chop first." We no even sidown, we no get chair, we no get table, everybody just stand up among box and bag, dey chop with hand or with plate wey we find quick-quick, and my friend, dat food sweet pass any food I don chop for long time. Na dat rice save di whole day, I dey tell you true.

By evening, when na last two load remain, dat tiredness wey dey wait since morning, e come meet us proper. Legs dey shake, back dey pain, and every box wey we carry dey feel heavier pass di last one, even when e be di same weight. Nobody dey talk again, everybody just dey move like machine, one foot then di next.

Before we lock di old house finish, I waka go inside one time, go check di rooms wey don empty now. Di parlour wey used to get sofa and table, e just be empty space, echo dey when you talk. My old room, di mark for wall wey show where my bed bin dey, still dey there. I stand for door small time, I no talk anything, I just look, because dat house don hold plenty year for us, and now e go belong to another person. I lock di door, and my hand small-small shake when I turn di key.

We reach di new place well after dark, my friend, past nine, past ten sef. Everybody tire reach ground, voice don finish. And when we count wetin we carry, we sabi say we get less pass wetin we bin start di morning with, one plate broke, one box lost forever, energy finish, but we still reach, we still dey, and by God grace, tomorrow go better. That na how di day go, no be small thing at all.
