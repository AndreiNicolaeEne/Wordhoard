This year na di year wey people go still di call to mind for many year come, make I no lie to wuna. Since I small I never see harvest turn like dis one. Even before we finish cut everything, we don see say na different level dis one be, di corn wey we plant almost double wetin we normally di harvest for normal year, and everybody for di area di talk am.

Na di cocoa sef wey really pass mind. Dat cocoa, e yield pass anything wey we sef don see before, e pass even di best year wey old people still di remember, and dat one na many many year back. When we finish weigh everything, we no fit believe di number wey come out.

Because di cocoa plenty reach like dat, house hand alone no fit finish di work before di ting go turn for tree, so we bring extra hand make dem come help. Make wuna take-time o, so nothing no go spoil for ground. Small-small, well-well, morning till night, everybody di work true-true.

After we don keep wetin house go chop and wetin go carry us reach next season, di rest wey remain, some we store, some we carry go market sell am make money enter house. No be small thing o.

The eldest: I don see harvest for dis ground since I be pikin, but dis one pass all wey I don see. Na di rain wey come di right time, no too much, no too small, and di weather wey stay cool small for di whole season, na dem do am, no be luck.

Even now, if any harvest come and people wan measure am, na dat year dem go still call, abi na so? Na dat year become di mark, till today, e still dey there o.
