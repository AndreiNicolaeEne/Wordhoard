My friend, make I tell you well-well how di whole ting go, because you no bin dey there to see am, and I want make you hear am from me one, straight, from beginning reach where I land.

You know say na dis year I decide say I go learn to ride bicycle. Everybody wey I sabi don learn dat ting since dem small small, since dem na pikin wey dey run about di yard, but me I bin dey reach dis age before I even try am one time. People bin dey laugh, some sef bin dey shake dem head, but I say make I no mind dem, I go do am. Sha, I never know wetin I bin dey enter.

Di first day I go, di instructor look me well, look di bicycle, look me again, and e tell me straight, e no soft-soft am for me at all:

The instructor: At your age, e go take you longer pass dis small ones wey dey come here. Body no dey learn quick again like when you young. But make we try.

I hear am, I no vex, but inside me someting bin turn small. And true true, within days, small small pikin dem wey their leg no even reach di pedal well, dem don dey ride pass me, dey wheel about like say na play dem dey play. Me wey be big pesin, I still dey wobble like say ground dey vex for me. Dat one pain me pass wetin I fit tell you. I no go lie.

Di whole first week, my body dey ache for place wey I even forget say body get. Muscle wey I no use since God knows when, dem all wake up, dem all dey vex, dey shout for me anytime I move. I dey waka like ol ol woman, dey siddon like say bench dey punish me. Every morning I dey wake, I dey ask myself wetin I put myself for.

But na one fall wey I never go forget. Dat day di bicycle just throway me, I go down hard, I hear my own breath comot from my body like person don pull am comot with hand. I lay down for ground for small time, no fit talk, no fit rise, and true word, for dat moment I bin don finish with di whole ting for my mind. I tell myself, dis one no be for me, I don too old for dis kind wahala. I even tell people around me say I no go come back again.

Me: I no go do dis thing again o. Na my age dis, no be pikin business.

But my padi wey bin dey there, na she tok me make I no give up so quick.

My padi: Come back one more time. Just one more time, make you see. If you still no like am after dat, na den you fit stop.

I no know wetin push me, but I come back. And I glad say I come back, because na from dat small small step everything start to move, even if e move slow well-well.

Progress no come quick at all, my friend. E dey come small small, one small ting today, another small ting after weeks of getting am wrong, wrong, wrong. And na so we bin dey, small crowd of people dey stand up dey watch, some dey cheer for me, dey clap small when I manage to balance for two second, others dey laugh, dey nudge each other, dey enjoy di show wey I dey give free. I no go lie, some day di laugh dey pain me, but I learn to carry am, to just laugh with dem sef, because wetin els I go do.

Di only ting wey bin dey help me continue, na when I stop to dey compare myself to dem small ones wey don ride since dem born. Dat one no dey help me at all, e only dey vex me more. Wetin dey help na when I compare myself to how I bin dey one month before. If last month I no fit balance for even one second, and dis month I fit balance for five second, den na progress dat, whether anybody else dey ride round di whole compound with eye close or not. I hold dat one tight, I no let am go.

So na so we continue, month one, month two, month three, still wobbling, still falling small small, still dey ache for body, but less and less each time.

Then na fourth month someting happen wey I still no fit explain am well with mouth. E no be say I sidon think am out. Na my body wey just know am one day, like say something inside settle down for its own place. I dey ride, and for di first time, I no dey think about wetin my hand dey do or wetin my leg dey do, di bicycle just dey go, dey carry me go, and I dey balance without even trying. One pikin wey dey watch me shout out loud:

One small pikin: Look am o, e don sabi am! E don sabi am now!

And true, I don sabi am. Di ting wey bin dey impossible for four whole month don just turn to someting difficult, and difficult I fit manage. I no go lie, I nearly cry dat day, but I hold am, I just laugh instead, laugh well-well, because na so joy dey show sometimes.

I never catch up to di fast ones, up till today. Dem still dey ride pass me, dem still dey do trick wey I no even go try. But I don make peace with dat one long time. Na wetin I fit do now wey I couldn't do before I dey proud of, not wetin person else fit do wey I no reach yet. I remember how I couldn't even sit for di bicycle without person hold am for me, and now I dey ride go market, dey ride come back, dey carry small load for back, dey do di ting like say na small small business.

When I sidon think about di whole season now, e no dey vex me again, e no dey shame me again. Di fall, di ache for body, di small pikin dem wey dey laugh at me, di day I bin want quit, all of am, dem don turn to someting I dey smile about anytime I remember. I glad say I try am, even if I try am late pass everybody. Take-time dey teach person plenty ting wey quick quick no fit teach.

So dat na di whole story, my friend, from start to where I land now. Sorry say you no bin dey there to see di worst of am, but I hope dis one make you see am small small, di way e really go, well-well, no lie for inside am.
