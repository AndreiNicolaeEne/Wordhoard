Tenant: Oga, good afternoon o. I hope I no come meet you for bad time.

Landlord: No o, my friend, you dey welcome. Sit down, sit down, make you rest small. How the family dey?

Tenant: Everybody dey fine sha, thank God. And madam and the children nko?

Landlord: We dey manage, we dey push. This Lagos no dey easy for anybody, but God dey.

Tenant: True talk, na so e be. Oga, na small matter bring me come see you today.

Landlord: I dey listen, talk make I hear.

Tenant: Na about the rent. I don dey this house now reach four years straight, and no be one day I ever late for the payment. Every January, before you even open mouth ask, money don enter your account.

Landlord: That one I sabi. You no dey give me wahala like some tenants wey dey this compound.

Tenant: E dey there. But that kitchen tap wey dey leak since last year, I don beg you plenty times, nobody come fix am. I just dey manage am with bucket.

Landlord: Ehen, that one we go look at am. But this rent wey you wan make I reduce, I no fit o. Na still small pass wetin dem dey charge for this area sef.

Tenant: I no wan trouble anybody, abeg, but make we shave the price small. Make you knock off one-fifth, e go help me well-well.

Landlord: One-fifth?? Ah, oga, that one too much o, you wan kill my pocket. Make I even see ten percent, and na because na you, no be everybody I fit do am for.

Tenant: Sha, think am well, no vex, na my mouth I take beg you.

Landlord: Make I sleep on top am, I go give you answer before the week end.
