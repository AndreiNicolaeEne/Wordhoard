The friend: Oga, how market? How the new baby dey do?

The mother: We dey manage o. Baby dey grow, we dey cope small small.

The friend: Na so? Everything dey alright true true?

The mother: To be honest, my friend, e no easy at all. This pikin go wake me up like four, five times for one night. Sometimes na three hours sleep I go see, if I even see am well well.

The father: And no be only that one o. My back don dey pain me anyhow, like say I don carry cement block whole day. My hand sef dey shake small small, e no dey stop, na so tiredness be.

The friend: Ah, sorry o, sorry well well. This thing no be small thing at all, abi? Una don try o, God go help una.

The mother: You know, I go tell you something wey go make you laugh sha. Yesterday I dey find my phone everywhere for this house, I don shout like say person steal am, I don near cry. Na inside baby bag I keep am since morning, I no even remember when I put am there.

The friend: (laughs) Ah, tiredness na real thing o, e no dey gree person rest even to think straight.

The friend: Make I help abeg, no vex. Give me the baby make I carry am go afternoon, make una two sleep small. Or make I bring food come, so una no go stress about cooking again, una don suffer reach.

The mother: My friend, I no go lie again, we don tire to dey refuse people wey wan help.

The father: Na so, oga. Abeg come o, we go gladly take the help, because we need this rest well well, no be small thing we dey pass through.
