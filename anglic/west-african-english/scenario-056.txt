Make I tell you how dat house treat me, seven whole days. Di people bin travel go up-country for one funeral, and dem beg me make I come stay, mind di place, feed di goat, water di small garden dem plant for back, nothing more than dat, dem say. I say okay, no wahala, na small small work, I go manage. Di people no sabi wetin dem dey send me go meet o.

Di very first night I sleep for dat house, something no just right already. Di corridor light dey blink, blink, like current dey vex for inside di wire, on off, on off, and I dey lie down for di parlour sofa dey watch am, I no even fit close eye well. I no dey fear ghost or nothing like dat, na just di way di whole house dey feel strange around me, like di walls sef no know me yet. I bin dey tell myself, make you sleep, tomorrow go better. E no better.

Second day, before I even brush teeth, na di goat don loose. Di rope wey dem tie am for backyard, I no check am well di night before, and by morning di animal don comot through di gate wey I no lock well well, don waka enter street, dey chop people neighbour's leaves for dem fence. I run come out, my slippers still for hand, I dey shout, dey call di goat like e go answer me, and people for street dey laugh me small small, dey point, "oga, na your goat dat?" I no even get time vex, I just dey run. But God dey. Na less dan one hour I catch am, for near di kiosk junction, one small boy hold di rope for me and I dash am small change, I thank God well well dat morning.

By third day, na di garden wey dey trouble me small small, small small, like sickness wey dey creep. Di pepper leaves dem start dey yellow for edge, and I no know if na too much water or too small water I dey give am. I dey pour water every morning, but by evening di soil don dry again like I no do nothing. I ask di neighbour woman wetin dey wrong, she come look, she shake her head, she say...

Neighbour: My sister, di sun dey hot pass wetin dat plant fit take for dis season, you go water am morning and evening, no leave am under dat hot sun for afternoon.

I try am so, but by di time I remember to move di pot, di leaves don already curl. I no fit tell di people wetin exactly happen to dem pepper, I just dey pray say make e recover small before dem come back.

Now na di fourth day wahala start proper. Di standing fan for di sitting room, di one dem dey use every night, e just stop, e don spoil, like dat, under my own watch. I dey seat down dey watch television, di fan dey turn, den one kind smell come, small smoke, and di whole thing stop, dead, finish. I no touch am, I no do nothing to am, but I know say if I no fix am before dem come, dem go think na me kill am with bad handling. I take-time o, because current for that house sef no dey stable, and I no want touch anything wey go shock me. I call one man wey dey fix electrical for near, he come, he open di fan, he say di motor don burn small, e need new part. I pay for am from my own pocket, because how I go tell dem say di fan just spoil for my hand like dat, dem no go believe me sef.

Same week, di senior, one big auntie for di family, dem call her senior for house, everybody dey respect her, she just show for gate one afternoon, no call, no warning, nothing. She knock, I open, I dey shock small.

Senior: Ah, so na you dey mind dis house? Where dem dey travel go?

Me: Dem don go up-country, ma, for di funeral. Dem no tell you?

Senior: Dem no tell me anything o, I just come check house.

I offer her water, I offer her food, I no even sabi wetin dem keep for dis house sef, but I try, I no want her go tell people say house-minder no get manners. She sit small, she look around di sitting room like she dey inspect, she talk small talk, den she comot again, same way she come, sudden sudden.

Na around fifth day di real jolt catch me. I bin dey clean di shelf wey dem keep dem glass plates, di special ones dem no dey use every day, and one of di plates just slip from my hand, I hear am hit floor. My heart just jam for my chest, I close eye, I dey pray say make e no break. When I open eye, na small crack for di edge, no be full break, but I know say dis plate na something dem value well well, I don see how di woman dey handle am with two hand anytime. I sit down for floor small time, my hand dey shake, I dey think how I go explain dis thing. I no fit sleep well dat night, I just dey turn di plate for my hand, dey check di crack, dey hope say maybe dem no go notice.

Sixth day bring me new kind wahala. I comot go throw rubbish for outside bin, I no carry key, I just say make I run go and come, small small time. Di door lock behind me, na spring lock, I no know say e go lock like dat by itself. I stand for dat gate for almost three hours, dey call people, dey try climb small window for back, until one boy wey sabi lock come help me, he use wire, he open am. I dey vex for myself well well, but wetin I fit do, na so life be.

Dat same evening, rain just come down heavy heavy, without warning, and I forget say I bin leave one window open for di small back room, di one dem dey keep boxes and dem old things. By di time I remember, water don already soak di floor, some carton don wet, some paper for inside carton don spoil. I take towel, I dey mop, I dey pack di wet things go dry for veranda, I dey pray say di things wey spoil no dey too important.

Seventh night, before dem due to come back, I hear one loud noise for outside, like something fall or something push door. I no fit sleep, I get up, I take torch light, I go check, my heart dey beat fast. Na cat, wey don climb enter di kitchen through small hole for window net, dey turn over one basin. I chase am comot, I laugh for myself small, but sha, dat night I no sleep again till morning.

Na on di sixth day sef I finally land one fix wey work for di gate rope wey di goat keep dey loose, because e don try comot again two times more after di first day. I take stronger rope, I double am, I tie am for one iron post wey no fit shake, and I put one extra lock for di gate itself. From dat day, di goat no comot again, thank God.

Last day before dem come, I dey run up and down like mad person, dey clean, dey arrange, dey hide di cracked plate for back of di shelf where light no reach am well, dey water di garden one more time, dey check say every light dey work, every door dey lock well. I dey sweep, I dey wipe, I dey tell myself make everything look like nothing happen for dis house dis whole week.

When dem come back, di people dem happy, dem dey thank me well well, dem say house look fine, goat fat, garden dey survive. I tell dem small small wetin happen, I no hide everything, I no fit lie to dem straight face. I tell dem about di fan, about di window and di rain, about di goat wey comot but I catch am quick. Di plate, I tell dem small small too, I no fit hide forever, I just say sorry, sorry well well, and dem say no wahala, e don happen, thank you for minding di house for us.

I tell my friend later, I say, my friend, make dem no ever ask me to mind house again o, dat one week teach me pass wetin I sabi before. She just laugh, she say, "una people wey dey mind house for una people, dis kind thing dey normal," and I just shake my head, because true word, e no easy at all.
