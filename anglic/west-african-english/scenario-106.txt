Make I tell you how di ting bin go, dat time wi bin dey go Bo fo go si mi mama wey bin sick. I no go make am big pas wetin i bi, but I go tell you strehet, from di time wi rich di checkpoint to di time i don don.

Na so-so checkpoint dem bin mount pan dat road, near di junction wey pipul dem sabi say plenty roba bin dey happen dat mont, motor dem dey stop for night an dem dey rob pipul dem tin. So army an police mix togeda dey stand for dat corna, dey check tra-veller papa dem an dema bag, make sure no bad tin no dey pas.

Wen wi rich, di line don tay well-well. Motor bin line-up wan aata anoda, poda-poda, taxi, big trailer, all a dem dey wait. I count, i bi like half wan hour befo wi rich front, mi one, sidon inside di poda-poda, dey watch dem dey check pesin wan aata anoda, slow-slow, no rush.

I no go lie, mi belle bin dey turn small-small befo wi rich front. I no bin do notin bad, mi papa dem bin correct, but dat kind waiting, wusai you dey si di soja dem faces, gun for dem shoulda, i dey mek yu tink tink notin wey no dae for tink about. I bin dey pray small inside masef, jus make e pas quick.

Wen i rich mi turn, di officer wey bin dey check papa dem call mi make I bring am. I bring mi ID card an di small paper wey mi broda bin give mi wen i dash mi money fo mama medicine, cos dat time dem dey ask fo see wetin you carry sotay dem sabi say no bad ting.

Officer: Wusai you dey go?

Me: I dey go Bo, fo go si mi mama. I sick.

Dat one na plain queshon, i no tay mi. But den e come put anoda one, an dat one need mi tink small befo I ansa.

Officer: Wetin dey inside dis bag? Na who money dis?

Dat queshon, i get weight. Cos I dey carry big-big money fo mama hospital bill, an if I no ansa am right, dem go tink say na some kind wahala money, or say I dey carry am fo som pesin wey no correct. So I hol misef, I no rush mi mouf.

Me: Na mi broda give mi dis money, fo pay mama hospital bill for Bo. E work Freetown, e no fit come, so e sen mi go carry am.

Officer: Show mi wetin dey inside.

I open di bag, e look inside, e look di small envelope wit di money, e look mi ID again. Den e keep quiet.

Dat quiet wey e keep, i long pas wetin i bi. E dey turn mi ID dis side an dat side, e dey look mi picture, e dey look mi face, back an fot, back an fot. I no talk, I jus dey stand, mi hand dey sweat small-small.

Den I member, di paper wey show say mi mama admit for hospital, I neva bring am out. I bin put am for di bottom part of di bag, foda paper dem, an now e dey ask mi.

Officer: You get eni paper fo show say na true wetin you tok?

Me: Yes, senior, i dae, make I find am.

I bin dey search, mi hand dey shake small, but I find am, I bring am comot, I gi am. E read am, e nod, e no tok notin.

Wen dat one don pas, I look di young man wey bin dey infront mi for di line. Dem hold dat man back. Dem dey look am well-well, dem dey ask am plenty queshon, dem dey check am from head to leg, dem open im bag two time. Dat man, i face bin dey change, but I no si eniwan tok notin bad to am out loud, dem jus dey watch am, dey check, dey take dema tem wit am. I no ask notin, I jus si am for corna mi eye, an I face front, cos na so wi dey do, wi no dey put mout for oda pesin business fo checkpoint.

Trowout all dis, I bin dey try hol mi face steady, mi voice steady, no let dem si say mi belle dey turn. Cos if dem si say you dey fred, dem go tink say som ting dey wrong, an dem go hol you moretem. So I hol misef, I ansa plain, I no laf, I no fred-fred pan mi face, but insaid, mi hat bin dey beat well-well.

One ting I go neva forget, di officer, wen e don don check evritin, e look mi, e come soft small.

Officer: Tek yu tem, road dey bad wia you dey go, mama dey wait fo you.

Dat one, i simple, but i sweet mi. I no bin expect am fo tok notin like dat, e jus bin gi mi back mi papa dem, wit dat small word wey mek mi hat siddon.

E hand mi papa dem back, e wave im hand.

Officer: Go.

Dat wan word, "go", i carry mi comot from dat place. I nod, I tank am small, "Tank you, senior," an I waka go back to di motor, di driver don start di engine, wi pas.

I no go lie, we don pas di checkpoint, but I no bin feel say notin loosen inside mi. I bin siddon quiet, dey look outside, road dey pas, tree dem dey pas, an na aata wi don waka far, maybe twenty minit down di road, na den, na den, I feel say di tension wey I bin carry, e comot from mi body, small-small, like breeze wey blow comot smoke. I feel mi shoulda drop, I feel mi hand no dey shake again.

Na dat time I know how e bin ton mi. Notin bad no bin apin, mi papa dem correct, dem let mi pas, but di kain waiting, di kain queshon, di kain quiet wen dem dey look you papa, i tek som tin comot fom yu, wey you no know sotay aata.

So dat na why, even now, wen I tell dis story, I dey tell am careful. I no go mek am big pas wetin i bi, cos notin bad neva apin to mi, straight. But I no go mek am small pas wetin i bi, sef, cos I know wetin I bin feel, an I know say dat young man wey dem hold longa dan mi, i no sabi wetin apin to am aata. So I dey tell am jus so, plain, no bigger, no smaller, di way i bin go, o.
