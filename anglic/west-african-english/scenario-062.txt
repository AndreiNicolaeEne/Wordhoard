Me:

Make I tell you the thing as e bi, no be as people di carry package for this street, adding pepper for where no pepper dey. You di ask me weti bi the trouble between me and dem people for next door. Fine, I go tell you, but I no go take question for inside am, abeg, because once I start this thing I must finish am the way e dey rest for my chest all this time.

Before you come this street, sef before you born small small, me and dat house, we bi like one compound. Wall di separate us true true, but nothing else separate us. Their pikin and my pikin dey grow together, dey chop for the same plate, dey sleep for the same room when rain too much and light don go. Their mami and my mami dey go market together every Saturday, dey share pepper, dey share salt when person's own no reach, dey carry gossip come dump for my veranda for evening time. If sickness catch me, na their mami go come cook soup bring for my house. If their pikin born, na me go carry water go help wash am. Ova ten years so we bi like that, no wahala, no bad blood, nothing. People for this street go tell you say those two houses, na one family wey fence separate.

Na so e go until this small small thing come start everything.

The oga for that house, e decide say e wan build one kitchen extension for the back, small block wall, small zinc roof, nothing big. But e build am too close, too close to the line wey separate our two plot. So the water wey suppose run go the gutter, e don begin block am, and my own side na him di receive all the flood when rain fall well well. I no rush go quarrel, no. I take time, I go greet the man proper, I talk to am softly, I tell am, oga, abeg, the wall dey too close, make we move am small, make water fit pass well, we no wan spoil relationship for something small like this. E say okay, e go see to am. Weeks pass, nothing happen. I go again, this time I carry the mami inside am, I talk make e no vex, I just di remind am, and again e promise say na next week, na next week. Next week no come. E just forget, or e no forget, I no sure, but nothing move. So when the third time come, I no fit talk soft again, my voice carry small edge, I tell am say this wall must move before the big rain reach, because water don start to enter my kitchen self.

Na there wahala start proper, because story wey I send, na different story wey reach that house. We get one neighbor, the one wey both houses dey trust, e dey carry word from one side go the other since old old time. So I send my message through am, small message, just say make dem move the wall. By the time the message reach the other house, e don grow big. Dem talk say I bin call the man senior for insult, I bin talk say e no respect nobody, I bin threaten say I go break the wall myself if dem no move am. Ehen. All that, I never talk. The messenger neighbor, either e add salt for the story or the story just grow leg for road, I no fit tell you which one, but by the time e land for that house, na different matter entirely.

So when I go finally go meet the oga face to face, expecting say we go settle am small small, na denial straight straight I meet. E stand for im own door, e say, "I never hear any request from you o. Nobody come tell me anything about any wall." Just like that. Flat. Like I no send message before, like the messenger neighbor no dey walk between our two houses every day since God knows when. That one pain me more pass the wall itself sef, because to build something too close, that na mistake person fit make. But to stand for your own door and lie say the person never even ask, that na different matter entirely.

I no fight am for there. I no shout. But that night, small small, I take my own cutlass, I cut the branch from my plantain wey dey hang small into their side, the one wey I bin dey allow to hang there for years, free of charge. Small thing. Nothing big. Just to show say if you no want give, I too no go give.

Then the comment reach me. Na through the same messenger neighbor am, because word must pass through somebody for this street, abi. Dem say the mami for that house bin talk say people like us, we like to suffer for other people wall, na jealousy dey worry us because dem house dey grow well well and ours no dey grow reach there. Chai. That one enter me well well. Ova all the things wey dem talk, that one na the one wey I no fit forget, because we never once, not one day, dey jealous that house. We bin dey happy for dem, genuine happy, before all this. But once that comment reach me, something inside close.

I still try one time. I send small dash go their house, one basket, plantain and fish, through the same messenger, just to show say I no wan carry this thing too far, make we settle am like people wey used to be family. Dem send the basket back. Untouched. Even the fish never open. That one talk louder than any word dem fit send back to me.

By this time the whole street done pick side. Half of dem dey stand for my side, dem remember how the wall bin dey close from the beginning, dem remember the flood. The other half dey believe say na my pikin nature wey too proud, wey no fit let small thing pass. Even for church, people dey greet one side more warm pass the other, small small.

And then the day everybody dey remember, the argument wey happen for road self, for where every person on this block fit hear. E no start with me sef, na the mami for that house wey come stand for her gate and begin call me name, loud, telling everybody wey want hear say I na the one wey start everything from the beginning, that I na wicked person, that my own generation no know how to respect neighbor. I no fold, I answer am back, loud too, and by the time we finish, half the street done come out to watch, some dey beg make we stop, some dey just watch quiet quiet.

Na for that same argument the accusation come out, the one about the zinc roof wey bin missing from my own store small time before. Dem stand there for everybody hear, say na my side wey come thief the zinc for night when nobody dey watch. I deny am flat, I tell dem say if una get proof, bring am come, show the whole street, because I no thief for my life, my mami raise me well. Nobody bring proof. To today nobody bring proof. But the accusation, once e land for road like that, e no ever fully wash off.

Na since then, silence. Ova three years now, three good years, we no greet, we no talk, our pikin dem no cross that fence again, though sometimes I go still see their small one peep through the hole for the wall dey look my compound, dey remember old old days maybe, I no know. The wall still dey there, still close to the line, water still dey pool small small anytime heavy rain fall, and I still dey clear am myself every time, alone, because to ask am now, no be something my mouth fit do again.

And to talk true, my friend, even now, even after all this long silence, I no fit swear for you say I know who really start the whole thing. Sometimes I think na the wall wey start am. Sometimes I think na my own edge that third time wey I go meet the man. Sometimes I think na the messenger neighbor wey carry word wrong. I no sure again o. Both houses done suffer for something wey used to be sweet, and up till today, nobody for either side fit stand up and say na so e start, without one part of dem heart di whisper say maybe, just maybe, dem own self bin add fuel too.
