Me: Senior, come sidon small, make we count dis month well-well. Na nine days remain before e turn, and dis money wey remain, na only twenty.

The roommate: Twenty? Ah, dis life sha. I bin think say we get pass dat one.

Me: I don count am reach three times, na so e be. Twenty must cover us reach when the month go turn.

The roommate: Okay, make we no waste time, weti we go cut first?

Me: The meat wey we dey buy every evening, we go leave am. Na beans and garri go carry us, we go cook big pot one time, e go last us reach four, five days if we manage am fine.

The roommate: Beans again? I don tire for beans o, but no wahala, make we do am, belly no get choice.

Me: Na so. Then the rest of the days we go add small rice make e no be beans beans every day, but small-small.

The roommate: Sha, one thing dey worry me. My hair don spoil finish, I wan buy small cream, e no reach even two thousand.

Me: Ah ah, you wan spend on hair when we dey count kobo for beans? No, abeg, that one na luxury, e no fit enter dis budget at all.

The roommate: E be like say una don forget say person still need to look like person o!

Me: I no dey argue say make you look bad, I dey talk say na priority we dey settle now, take-time, the hair go still dey there next month.

The roommate: True talk sha, make I no vex, na so life be. Weti we go do for the light bill wey don reach?

Me: We go beg make dem push am, we go tell dem make dem wait small, we go pay am when the new month enter.

The roommate: If dem agree, na relief o, because as e be now na this twenty one must cover us the whole nine days, no extra.

Me: E don already dey feel small possible, because my sister sef bin promise say she go send small something for weekend. If e come, we go breathe small.

The roommate: God go do am. We go manage, we don manage before, we go manage again.
