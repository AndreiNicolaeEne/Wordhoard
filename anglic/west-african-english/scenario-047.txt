The buyer: Chale, na seven you dey owe me, but na only five you put for my hand o. Two dey miss.
The shopkeeper: I no do any mistake, I count am well well before I give you.
The buyer: Two cedis dey miss, I no dey lie you, abeg count am again make you see.
The shopkeeper: (counting again) Eii... sorry o, small-small mistake dey. (hands it over) Take, no vex.
