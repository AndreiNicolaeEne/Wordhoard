Mother: Shh-shh, my pikin, wetin dey vex you so, eh? Make you rest your eye, sleep go come now-now. I dey here, I no go anywhere o, I go dey till morning show face. See moon dey shine well-well for sky, e dey watch us small-small. Shh-shh, sleep o, my pikin, moon go still dey shine when you wake. Shh-shh, make sleep carry you go.
