Me: You no go believe how the reunion be o. That our senior for class, the one wey used to dey fine well-well back then, I no even recognize am for gate! The guy don change reach, na fat overtake am small-small, beard full face, I dey greet am like say na complete stranger.

The friend: Ehen, na so I see am too. But you know who no change at all? Our junior, the small girl wey dey always dey library that time. She still be exactly the same person, same laugh, same waka, na only her voice don deep small.

Me: Ehn ehn, and you remember how that senior used to dey shy for back then, e no fit even talk to person well? Today the man na oga for one big company, e dey command people up and down, na work don change am be that.

The friend: Chai, life sha. But make I tell you wetin shock me pass, na word wey we hear say the guy wey used to dey run mad for campus, na him don relocate abroad, e don marry, e get pikin sef. Nobody for that hall fit believe am.

Me: True talk. And that other one wey suppose come, dem talk say e no fit make am, work no gree, oga for office no release am, na so I hear am.

The friend: Sorry for am o, e miss well-well thing sha.

Me: But us two sef, abi we don change reach? I no think say I don change pass small-small grey for head.

The friend: Na so I dey feel am too, you and me still be who we be, no wahala. Make we no allow long time pass again before another one o, abeg, make we plan am before next year reach.

Me: Na so o. Make we even go chop something small next time before we go.
