The customer no even reach the stall well-well before the owner don dey scoop the rice.

The owner: Ah, na you dis. Groundnut stew, plenty pepper, small fish on top, no onion. I know say na so you like am.

The customer: Na so, na so. But today make you put one extra fish for me, abeg. I don hungry pass mark.

The owner: Aha, wahala don start. You wan chop me profit finish. Okay okay, make I add am, no vex.

The customer: Thank you o. How business dey?

The owner: Sha, e no easy. Customer dem no dey come like before, everything dey cost pass wetin we dey sell am. But God dey, we go manage small-small.

The customer: Sorry o. But you know say I dey your customer three times for week, sometime four if I no travel. I no fit chop for anybody else stall again, my belle done get used to your hand.

The owner: Na so? Then na why you dey come dis time everyday like say na oga you dey inspect me work. Una customer sef, una no dey give person rest.

The customer: (laughing) No be my fault, na your food dey call me. Even my workmate dem dey ask say who dey cook for me house, I tell dem na here I dey chop.

The owner: You go make me famous for this junction o, before person go come dash me plenty customer and I no go get time again.

The customer: Na so business dey be. Make e continue like dat.

The owner: Amen to that. Here, your food don ready, careful e still hot small.

The customer: How much all together?

The owner: Give me the usual, plus the extra fish wey you beg for.

The customer hand over the money, the owner count am, give change, both of dem dey smile.

The owner: Take care o, no forget say tomorrow you go come back.

The customer: You know say I go come. Na here my belle dey rest.
