Listen here now, b'y, I got to tell you about this one.

I needed a paper stamped over to the office, the transfer paper for me father's boat, and it had to go in that very day or the whole licence lapsed on us and we'd have lost our spot on the water for the whole capelin season, no messing.

So I'm up to the counter and the one there's flipping through it, slow, slower than he needs to be, and he goes, "This kind of thing now, it usually takes a good while to come through, unless a fella's in a rush, if you knows what I means." And I just looked at him. Didn't say a word. Let it hang there, waiting to see would he say more.

The one at the counter: "I'm only saying, some people finds a way to make it go faster."

Me: "Is that right. What way's that, then."

He only smiled at me, right smug, and wouldn't answer it straight, just kept on with the "you knows how it is."

I told him no, b'y, I don't know how it is, and I'm after coming a long way for this, and me father's fishing rep is on the phone this minute if there's some rule holding it up, sure he'd know it better than either of us. That put a right damper on him quick. Told him too I'd no trouble coming back another day, no rush on my end at all.

He went quiet a minute, shuffling the paper round like he was deciding, then just stamped the thing and shoved it back over the counter at me.

Never paid a red cent. That's the whole of it: some crowd, they only wants it easy for themselves, and the second you stops playing along, b'y, they folds right up like nothing was ever the matter.
