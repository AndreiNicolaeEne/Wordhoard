The one counting: One two three, out goes she, up the tickle and down to the sea, catch a cod right by the knee.

The one landed on: Luh, that's not fair, b'y, ye skipped right over me, ye went some fast.

The eldest: Ye knows the rule, arguin means she starts it over from the top.

The one counting (louder): ONE TWO THREE, out goes SHE, up the tickle and down to the sea!

The one landed on: ...

The eldest: That's shockin fair now. She's out, b'y.
