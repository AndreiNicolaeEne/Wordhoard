The day started off some quiet, sun splitting the rocks, and round after dinner I looks off towards the hill and there's this line of smoke standing up grey against the blue, way off yet, just sitting there like nothing at all. I says to meself, that's a fair ways off, no odds to me, and I kept on with what I was at, never give it a second thought.

Wasn't an hour after that the wind come around and hauled the whole of it right down over top of us. I'm after telling buddy next door, luh, that's closer than it were a minute ago, and next thing me eyes is stinging something shockin, watering up so's I could hardly see straight, and the air gone thick and yellow like duckish come early in the middle of the day.

That's when I knowed watching was done. Didn't stand round wondering about it neither, b'y, twenty minutes and I was out the door with what mattered, the important papers and that, never even boiled the kettle for a mug-up, no time for it. Me throat gone raw as an old rope from the smoke, the heat pressing in against me face and arms the whole way down the road like standing too close to a stove that's roaring. I looked back the once, only the once, and the whole hill was lit up some bright, orange right up into the sky.

The neighbour: Where ya to?
Me: I'm to me sister's, b'y. Safe enough, no more than that.

Two nights I was away, not knowing one blessed thing, not a word on whether the place still stood or was gone to nothing. Oh me nerves, the waiting near done me in worse than the running ever did.

Went back the third day and there it was, standing same as ever, spared entire, never touched. I stood there some while just looking at it, hardly believing me own eyes.
