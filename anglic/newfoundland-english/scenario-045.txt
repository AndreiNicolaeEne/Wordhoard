The customer: Luh, b'y, what's this now. This ent what I ordered a-tall.

The tailor: Whadda ya mean, my love, that's exactly what we settled on, sure. I had it right in me head the whole time.

The customer: No, no. Three buttons and a straight hem, that's what I asked for, and this here's got two buttons on it and the hem's all angled off to the one side. I'm after waiting two weeks for this, I'd have it right in me mind.

The tailor: Sure I done what we agreed, love, I don't make things up outta nowhere.

The customer: I knows what I ordered. I've had it thought out since before I even come in the door the first time. Three buttons, straight across the bottom, plain as anything.

The tailor: Well now, hold on, let me have a look, I got it wrote down somewhere in the back. Sit down there a minute, I'll put the kettle on while I'm at it, have a bit of a mug-up while I finds it.


The tailor: Right, here 'tis now... oh, me nerves. Says right here, three buttons, straight hem. I must've had ye mixed with the other one come in the same day, wanted the angled hem and the two buttons on it.

The customer: There, see, I told ye. Some shockin mix-up, that.

The tailor: I'm some sorry now, love, I won't lie to ye. I'll get it took apart and done right, won't take long a-tall. Two days, that's all I needs, and it'll be sitting here waiting for ye, three buttons and straight as ya like.

The customer: Right so. Two days, Thursday then. I'll be back in for it.

The tailor: It'll be here, on me life. Mind yerself going out now, it's some mauzy out there, and duckish besides, ye can hardly see the tickle from the window.

The customer: Go way, b'y, I've me umbrella. Thursday, now.
