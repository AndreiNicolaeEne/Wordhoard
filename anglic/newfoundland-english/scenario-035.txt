Me: Luh, itself, the best feed in town is the harbour chip stall, no arguing it, b'y.

My friend: Get out, b'y, that crowd wouldn't know a good chip if it bit them. Your one up by Rawlins Cross, now that's the feed.

Me: Rawlins Cross? Sure I'm after eating there one time and me stomach was in knots the whole night after, some shockin. I was up sick till duckish the next evening.

My friend: That's not on them crowd, b'y, that's on you eating like ye never seen food before in yer life.

Me: No b'y, I'm serious. The chips down the harbour is done twice, crispy right through, and the fish is fish, not some mystery bit of nothing like your crowd tries to pass off.

My friend: Mystery, is it. At least mine don't charge ye an arm and a leg for a scoff the size of me thumb.

Me: Some cheek, talking small portions when your one's poutine is half curd, half air, and the buddy that runs it is a proper sleeveen, short-changed me a dollar last time and looked me straight in the eye doing it.

My friend: I'll have ye know that's the best chip in St. John's, everyone knows it, ask anyone.

Me: Everyone don't know nothing, b'y. I'd say that place'd put the whole town in the hospital if they served it out in July heat.

My friend: Now hold on, that's some exaggeration.

Me: Yes b'y, shockin exaggeration, but you started it going on about my chips.

My friend: Whadda about the new one up on Duckworth, though, the fish and brewis one. Nobody's got a bad word to say about that one, scrunchions done right and all.

Me: True enough, can't fault that one, not a bit.

My friend: Right so, how about we goes and does the rounds again the day, yours and mine both, and settle it proper this time.

Me: Fair enough, b'y, but I already knows who's winning.

My friend: Go way b'y, we'll see about that.
