Me: What's the fruit going for the day, b'y?
The seller: Twelve for the batch, and worth every cent.
Me: Twelve? Luh at the bruise there, I'm not paying full whack. I'll give ye eight.
The seller: Eight? Go way b'y, ye'd have me robbed.
Me: I'm after looking, half of it's soft. Eight, or I'll take me custom on.
The seller: Some hard done by, but I'll do it for ten.
Me: Ten it is, b'y, fair enough.
