The eldest: May ye be after finding fair wind and fish enough all your days, some good luck riding home besides, that's swore true, b'y, not wished. But now, whoever begrudges it, may the mauzy never lift off their door, and every tickle they rows run foul on them, every time out, swore just as true. What's swore in the blessing goes down the same as what's swore in the curse, and neither one lies.
