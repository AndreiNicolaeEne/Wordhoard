The youngest: She never said no such thing, b'y. You're after inventin that in your head same as you does everything else.

The eldest: I'm not inventin nar a word of it. I'm after hearin it with me own two ears, plain as plain.

The youngest: Well I never heard it and I was here just as much as you was.
