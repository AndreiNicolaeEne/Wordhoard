I left later than I meant to, some later, and by the time I got out the door it was already duckish turning to full dark, that quick October dark that don't give you no warning at all. Wasn't but two minutes down the road and I got the sense of someone behind me, footsteps landing near enough with mine that I couldn't be sure was it an echo or was it a person walking. I never turned around, b'y, not straight off. I slowed up a bit, casual as you like, like I was only taking me time, and the steps behind slowed too. Then I picked it up again, quickened right up, and sure enough they quickened along with me. Me nerves were gone shockin at that.

I always cuts through by the lane past the old shed, the short way, but not the day, not with someone matching me step for step in the dark like that. I went round by the main road instead where there's streetlight still going and a bit of traffic passing, figured buddy behind me wouldn't try nothing with cars going by. I let meself have one look back, quick, just the once, and there was nothing certain in it either way, just a shape, could've been anyone, could've been nobody, mauzy enough out that you couldn't rightly swear to it.

Last stretch I walked faster than I'd ever own up to, near a trot if I'm honest, and when I got the door shut and the bolt on I stood there with me back against it letting me heart settle. Oh me nerves, b'y.

Never did find out was it anything at all. Might've been some fella walking the same way home same as meself. I tells it now half laughing at meself, how sure I was there was someone after me, how I near ran the last bit of the road home over nothing, probably nothing, and still not rightly knowing to this day.
