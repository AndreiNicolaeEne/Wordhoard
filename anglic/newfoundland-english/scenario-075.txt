Me: Luh, hold on now - b'y, is that you?

The friend: Oh me nerves, get out of it! I never thought I'd see ye standin' in the middle of an airport in Ontario of all places.

Me: I know, I'm after nearly walkin' right by ya, didn't credit me own eyes. How long has it even been now, b'y?

The friend: Sure it's got to be more than ten year, easy. Since before ye moved up to town, I'd say.

Me: Some right, ten year and then some. Look at the state of us.

The friend: I knows, I knows. So whadda y'at these days anyway? Ye still down home or what?

Me: No, no, I'm out in Alberta now, workin' the rigs. Married too, got a young one on the way, if ya can believe that.

The friend: Go way b'y! That's some good news. I'm still in town meself, doin' the electrical, got two youngsters now, the both of them shockin' busy.

Me: Good on ya, b'y. Here, do ya still remember the time we got caught out past duckish down by the tickle, and yer mother come lookin' for us with the flashlight, roarin' blue murder?

The friend: Stop it, I'm after forgettin' all about that till just now. And we come home soaked to the skin and tried to tell her we only went for a mug-up.

Me: And she never believed a word of it neither.

The friend: Not a word, b'y. Here, do ya ever hear tell of the one we used to knock around with, the fella from up the road?

Me: No, not a peep out of him in years now. Someone said he was out west too, but sure I couldn't tell ya where.

The friend: Some strange, that. Anyway, luh, we can't be losin' each other again, give me yer number there.

Me: Now I will, and I'm actually gonna use it this time, not like the last go.

The friend: Ye better, b'y, or I'm comin' to find ya meself.
