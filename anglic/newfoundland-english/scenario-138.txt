The workmate: So I hears tell chummy was some full drunk out on the tickle Saturday night, run the skiff right up on the rocks, and the young fella with him gone in the water, nearly drowned before anyone got out to...

Me: Stop right there now, b'y. That's not what happened, not even close.

The workmate: No?

Me: No, b'y. I was down the wharf meself when they come in. Chummy wasn't even on the boat, 'twas his son had her out, and he wasn't drunk, he don't drink a-tall, ye knows that. Engine cut out on him in the mauzy, that's all, thick fog rolled in off the tickle and he couldn't see the shore to save his life. Drifted a bit, hollered, buddy from the next wharf over heard him and went and towed him in. Nobody went in the water. Nobody near drowned. Cold and some shook up, that's the height of it.

The workmate: Some different than what I heard, then.

Me: Some different, is right. By the time that got to you it's turned into a whole other story, b'y. Drunk driving, boy overboard, none of it happened.

The workmate: Oh me nerves, I'd have sworn to it. Where'd I even hear that from...

Me: Who told you?

The workmate: Her workmate, at break. Said she got it off her sister.

Me: And her sister's crowd is thick with the fella runs his mouth down the shop, sure he can't tell a story straight to save himself, always some embellishment on it by the time it leaves him.

The workmate: That'd be it then.

Me: That'd be it. So tell it the true way from now on, whoever asks you. No drunk, no drowning, just a bit of fog and a good tow home.
