Buddy: Whadda y'at, love. Luh, here's one for ye, what goes round the house all day and never gets in?
The eldest: Is it the dog?
Buddy: No, b'y, 'tis the road.
The eldest: Go way b'y, that's a good one. My turn now, what has a mouth and never talks, a bed and never sleeps?
Buddy: A river?
The eldest: That's it, b'y, you knows your stuff.
Buddy: Right, some sharp tonight. Another round, b'y?
