Oh me nerves, my friend, I'm only just after sitting down and me whole body's after seizing right up on me. Sit down a minute, I'll tell ya how the day went, start to finish, 'cause you weren't there for none of it.

Started this morning way before I was ready for it, still stiff from the day before, and straight off we're into hauling the old lumber off the back of the stage. That set the pace for the whole rest of it, b'y, 'cause once ya gets going like that ya can't slow down or ya loses the rhythm and it only gets harder.

Wasn't an hour in and me back started up on me, right down low, and I knowed then how the rest of it was gonna go. We took a mug-up round ten, just tea and a touton apiece standing up in the yard, no time to sit, and that was barely enough at all, ten minutes and buddy's hollering we're back at it.

The afternoon now, my friend, that was the worst of it. Some hot out, no shade going, and I'm after thinking to meself more than once I could just walk off and nobody'd say a word. But ya doesn't, do ya.

My workmate: b'y, we'll never see the end of this afore duckish.

Me: go on now, we will, keep at it.

One thing went some quick though, clearing the old gear out down by the tickle, I figured that'd eat the whole afternoon and we had it done in under the hour. Small mercy in an otherwise grinding sort of day, that. Got forty for that piece alone, which I weren't counting on.

By the time it come on duckish I was done in altogether, hands blistered, back roaring shockin. Ten hours, start to finish.

Tomorrow's another day the same, far as I knows, unless it turns mauzy and keeps us off it. Then it's a day to rest the body, and Lord knows I could use it.
