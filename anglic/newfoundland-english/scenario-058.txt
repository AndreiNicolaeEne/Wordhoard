Me: Luh, I'm after coming home in the duckish and there's water pouring in over the back bedroom again, right in the corner by the chimney. Ceiling's gone soft as a sponge there now.

The friend: Some shockin, that. How long's it been at ya?

Me: Going on four months now, b'y. I'm after asking him twice already and got nothing for it.

The friend: What'd he say the first time?

Me: I calls him up, tells him there's a stain spreading across the whole corner, and he says he'll get a fella out to have a look. That's it. Never heard another word after.

The friend: And the second time?

Me: Same excuse, word for word. I says it's worse now, running down the wall by the window, and he says he'll get someone out to look at it. That was two month back and still nar sign of him.

The friend: So four months altogether and he never give ya a date, nothing?

Me: Ne'er a date. Just the fella-out line every time. Sure he's a proper sleeveen, that one.

The friend: Well ya got a few roads ya can go. Write him a letter, put down what's wrong and when ya told him, and keep a copy for yerself. Or go to the tenancies crowd, only that drags on and he might get some vexed and go after ya over the rent. Or get a man in yerself and take it off what's owed, but if he don't agree after, that's trouble too.

Me: Oh me nerves, I don't want no trouble with him.

The friend: I had one just like him, wouldn't fix nar thing till I sent it registered, dated and all, laid out plain. Fella showed up inside the week after that.

Me: Right, that's what I'll do then. Get it wrote down proper and send it off.

The friend: That's it, b'y. And don't sign nothing till it's mended.
