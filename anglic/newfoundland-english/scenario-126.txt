Me: Luh, I got to tell ya what happened to me last night, and don't you go sayin I was dreamin, b'y, cause I wasn't.

Buddy: Go way, you was probably half asleep on the chesterfield after that mug-up, dreamin some foolishness is all that was.

Me: No sir, I'm after tellin ya, I was wide awake, walkin home along the shore road in the duckish, thinkin straight as e'er a thing, and I sees a light out over the tickle, low down right over the water, and it weren't movin like no boat light moves.

Buddy: That's just someone out in a punt checkin their nets, the headlight bouncin off the water is all that is.

Me: No b'y, there weren't no punt out, the tickle was iced right over that week, nar a boat could get through it, and there weren't no engine sound neither, nothin, just the light sittin there still as a stone.

Buddy: Well then it's the mauzy playin tricks on ya, fog does that, catches the light off the poles and throws it some funny.

Me: Weren't no mauzy that night, clear as anything it was, stars right out and a silver thaw on the wires, and that light never once caught in the water, no reflection, nar a shimmer to it, just hangin there like it belonged to nothin at all.

Buddy: I still says ya seen what ya wanted to see, half convinced yerself of it walkin home alone.

Me: I swears on me nan's rosary, God rest her, every word of it's true, right shockin as it sounds, I seen what I seen and nothin ye says is changin that.

Buddy: And I still don't believe ya, not one word of it, not one.
