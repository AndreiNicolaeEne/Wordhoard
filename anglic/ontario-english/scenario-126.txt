Me: So we're up north at the cottage last weekend, eh, and I'm out on the dock like eleven at night, and I see this light out on the water just moving straight up the lake, no boat under it or nothing.

The friend: Yeah okay, you were probably half asleep out there, dreaming it up.

Me: No, I'm dead serious, I was wide awake, hadn't touched a drop, just had a coffee, thinking clear as anything.

Me: It went straight past the point for like ten minutes, no sound at all, no wake behind it neither, then it deked in behind the big rock and it was just gone.

The friend: Probably some guy out in a canoe, that's all, no motor so you don't hear nothing.

Me: Can't be, sorry, the lake's got a no-wake bylaw after eleven so nobody's supposed to be out there anyway, and there was no paddle splash, not even a ripple off it. Water was dead flat calm the whole time.

The friend: Maybe it was the moon then, reflecting weird off the water, tricks your eyes something wicked.

Me: There was no moon, hydro'd been out on our side since supper so it was black as anything, socked right in with cloud, couldn't see one star up there. I'm telling you, I know what I seen.

The friend: That's a beauty of a story for sure, buddy, but come on.

Me: I'm not chirping ya, I'm not making this up for a laugh, I know the difference between dreaming and sitting there wide awake watching something move against the current with my own two eyes.

The friend: Still sounds like something your head cooked up, no worries though.

Me: I swear on the cottage, I swear on my nan's butter tarts, every single word of that is true.

The friend: shaking head. Sorry, still not buying it.
