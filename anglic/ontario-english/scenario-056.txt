Okay so they go up north to the cottage for the week, right, and I say sure, no worries, I'll mind the place, how hard can it be, it's a house and a dog and some tomato plants, I got this. Famous last words, eh.

First night already something's off. I'm lying there and the furnace is doing this click-click-click thing, not the normal hum, and I'm like, is that supposed to do that. Texted the owner right away.

Me: hey sorry to bug you, furnace making a weird clicking noise, that normal?

The owner: oh yeah it does that, ignore it, it's been doing that since like February lol

Okay. Fine. Sorry for asking. Lay there another hour convinced the house was gonna go up, then just gave'r and fell asleep anyway.

Day two the dog gets out. I'm bringing in the recycling bin from the curb and he just deked past me, full breakaway, gone down the street like he's got somewhere to be. I'm out there in my socks going buddy, buddy, c'mere, and the neighbour's out on his porch having a laugh at me.

The neighbour: he do this a lot?

Me: I don't even know this dog that well, I'm just minding him

The neighbour: no worries, c'mon, I got treats in the truck

Took about forty minutes, forty-five tops, cornered him by the schoolyard fence with a piece of hot dog, brought him back looking real pleased with himself like he give'r out there. Didn't tell the owners that one till after, obviously.

Tomatoes in the backyard I was supposed to water every day, and I did, mostly, but somewhere around Wednesday I noticed the leaves going yellow and droopy and I genuinely could not tell you if that's overwater or underwater, I'm not a plant person, I just kept dumping water on them and hoping. By Friday they looked worse. Sorry, tomatoes.

Then the dishwasher packs it in Thursday. Mid-cycle just stops, dead, water sitting in the bottom, and I'm standing there going, did I do that, is this my fault. Turned out the breaker tripped for some other reason entirely and knocked out the whole kitchen circuit, hydro guy would've had a field day. Had to hand-wash everything the rest of the week like an animal, felt bad using their good dish soap for it too fast.

Friday the owner's brother just shows up. No text, no heads up, nothing, doorbell rings and I about jump out of my skin because I'm not expecting a single soul, and there's this guy standing there.

The brother: oh hey, sorry, didn't realize you'd still be here, I just came to grab my golf clubs from the garage

Me: oh, yeah no worries, come on in, sorry, wasn't expecting anyone

The brother: yeah sorry man, should've texted, they still up at the lake?

Me: yeah far as I know

Gave him a coffee out of the Keurig, we had a whole conversation about the Leafs, he took his clubs and left, and I sat there for a solid ten minutes with my heart going.

Saturday's when I nearly had a heart attack for real though. I'm dusting, being a good little house-sitter, and I knock this platter off the mantle, this big ceramic thing, and it hits the floor and I'm positive it's toast, positive, I have ruined this family heirloom, I'm gonna have to leave the country. Picked it up shaking and it's fine, not a scratch, and I stood there in the living room laughing at myself for about five minutes. Turns out later when I mentioned it, sheepish, all apologetic...

Me: so, uh, sorry, I did drop that big platter off the mantle, I think it's okay though

The owner: oh that thing, ha, that's had a crack in it since Christmas, don't even worry about it

, so I nearly had a stroke over a plate that was already broke.

Sunday I lock myself out. Took the garbage and the recycling both out to the curb, door swings shut behind me on its own like it's got a mind of its own, and the keys, all of them, sitting right there on the kitchen counter where I left them being a genius. No spare on me. Neighbour wasn't home. Ended up walking to the Tim Hortons down the street and just sat there for like three hours with a double-double and a box of Timbits, texting everyone I know who might have a spare, eventually the neighbour's wife turns up with one they'd been holding for emergencies for exactly this reason. Sorry to the whole line-up at Timmies who had to listen to me on the phone that whole time.

Somewhere in there it rains too, and I'd left the bathroom window cracked upstairs for the smell, and came home from the lockout fiasco to find a puddle on the tile and the sill all swollen up. Mopped it, propped a towel under there, said sorry to the window like it could hear me.

And then there's this noise, middle of the night, like a scraping, thumping sound from outside, and I'm up at like two a.m. standing at the top of the stairs with a flashlight going is somebody in the backyard, heart absolutely pounding, tip-toe down, peek out the kitchen window, and it's just a raccoon in the eavestrough having the time of its life. Banged on the glass, it looked at me like I was the one being rude, and took off eventually. Went back to bed and did not sleep great after that, eh.

The dishwasher thing kept coming back too, breaker kept tripping every couple days no matter what I did, and it wasn't till Monday, second-last day, that I actually got down there with a flashlight and found it, one of the outlets in the basement had water damage from something years back and it was shorting the whole line whenever the dishwasher and the dryer ran at once. Figured that out, kept them off at the same time after that, and it held, first thing all week that actually stayed fixed.

Last day I am cleaning like a woman possessed. Vacuuming at nine in the morning, scrubbing the dish-soap bottle I'd been going through too fast, hiding the takeout containers deep in the recycling so it didn't look like I'd ordered in five times, watering the poor sad tomatoes one more time for luck, wiping dog nose prints off every window in the front hall. House looked beauty by the time I was done if I do say so myself, or close enough anyway.

They rolled in that evening, tired, sunburnt, dog loses his mind he's so happy to see them, and the owner's standing in the kitchen going, so, how was it, any problems?

Me: nope, all good, real quiet week, dog was an angel, plants are doing great

The owner: beauty, thanks so much for doing this, seriously

And that was that. Told them the funny bits eventually, the raccoon, the golf clubs, the platter, spread out over a couple weeks so it didn't sound like the whole house nearly fell apart on my watch. Which, to be fair, it kind of did, but no worries, nobody needs to know that all at once.
