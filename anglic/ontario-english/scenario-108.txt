Okay so this letter's been sitting half-written in my head for the better part of a year now, sorry, you know how I get. I owe you this one bad and I'm finally sitting myself down eh, so here it is, the whole thing, don't skim it, I mean it.

House first because you'll ask before anything else. The eavestrough finally got done, the whole run around the back where it was pulling off the fascia and dumping water right along the foundation every time it rained hard, remember I was on about that before you even packed the car. Guy came out in the spring, did the whole thing in an afternoon, and it is an absolute beauty, no more waterfall off the corner by the garage, no more standing there going "oh here we go again" every time it poured. Course now the fence on the north side has decided it's had enough of us, two panels leaning like they had a rough night out, so that's next on the list, and honestly knowing this place it'll be something else entirely by the time this reaches you, that's just how an old house goes, one thing gets fixed and it opens up a spot for the next thing to fall apart. We got the tomatoes in finally too, way later than we should've, and a row of beans along that same fence that is one good wind away from taking the whole thing down with it. Hockey net's back up at the end of the driveway for the summer, front tire went flat sometime over the winter so it sat in the garage deflated and pathetic-looking for months before anybody bothered with it.

Big news, the actual big one, workmate, you remember her, the one who used to walk in every morning going on and on about the drive in, she's not with us anymore, work-wise I mean, don't panic. She got moved up to the head office out past the airport, which she is thrilled about, genuinely, but it means she's out on the 401 every single day now instead of the fifteen-minute walk she used to have, and she describes it different every time I ask, some days it's the best thing that ever happened to her career and some days she's got the look of someone who hasn't slept properly in a week. Whole department shuffled around after that, buddy from the third floor is running our section now. He's fine. Little much on the emails, sends three where one would do, but fine, no worries there.

You'll read this next part and know exactly what I mean and nobody else on earth will, so: the raccoon still owes us rent. That's it, that's the whole thing, I'm not explaining further, I know you know.

Neighbors, the young couple on the corner are gone, moved out west somewhere, sold the place quicker than anybody expected, up and gone in about six weeks flat, still can't quite believe it when I drive past. New people in there now, seem quiet enough, waved at me once from the driveway, sorry, that's genuinely the whole update, haven't had a real conversation with them yet, give it time. The family across the road had another one though, the second, so there's two little ones over there now and you can hear it on the street in the good way, bikes and scooters back out the second the weather turned, which it hadn't done in a while around here honestly.

Two people ask after you nearly every time I see them, without fail. There's the one behind the counter at the Timmies on the corner, I'll be honest I don't even know her actual name at this point, but she knows my order before I say it and she always goes:

The one at the counter: How's your sister doing out there, then?

Me: Good, busy, you know.

The one at the counter: Tell her I said hi, eh. And your usual, double-double?

Me: Please, yeah, for sure.

Same thing every single time, and I always mean to say more and never do. Then there's the neighbor, the older one who's been on the street since before either of us were born practically, still calls you by that ridiculous nickname of his that you hate and won't admit you secretly like, cornered me at the mailbox a few weeks back:

The neighbor: You hear from her much?

Me: Every couple weeks, she's good.

The neighbor: Tell her to eat properly out there. And tell her I still say she cheats at euchre.

Me: I will pass that along word for word, no worries.

So consider that passed along, word for word. Eat properly, and he says you cheat at euchre, whatever that means to him, take it up with him yourself next time.

Here's the thing I would never actually say to your face but I can write down because it's easier this way and you can't see me saying it: the house is quieter than I let on when we're on the phone, and some evenings I catch myself missing the noise of you being around more than I ever admit to you directly, sitting there doing nothing, arguing about nothing, that's it, that's the confession, don't make a whole production of it when we talk next, I mean it.

Winter was rough on the place, rougher than the last few, ice storm took a whole branch clean off the big tree out front and the hydro was out for two days straight which nobody on this street has let go of yet, people are still bringing it up at the mailbox like it was some kind of war they all survived together. Spring made up for it though, mild and long for once, everything came up early and stayed decent, felt like an actual spring instead of the usual two weeks of mud straight into humid with nothing in between.

Now you have to actually answer these ones properly and not just fire back "good, you?" like you always do, that doesn't count as an answer and never has. What's the weather actually like where you are, is it anything close to here or a whole different animal entirely? Are you finding real people to sit down with out there, proper sit-down time, or is it still mostly passing-through so far, because that's a different kind of lonely and I want to know which one it is. And is there anywhere near you that does anything close to a decent butter tart, because I have my doubts, deep doubts, and I want your honest opinion on it, not a diplomatic one, don't spare my feelings about the place you live now.

Last thing, and you already know it's coming because I say it every single letter and never once follow through. I am sending you a proper tin of butter tarts, real ones, this time for actual real, not just talk, give it a couple weeks and keep an eye on the door. Might throw in a bag of ketchup chips too since you always claimed you didn't miss them and we both know that's a lie.

Write back when you get a chance, no rush on my end, I clearly have zero room to talk on that front after this wait. Love you, talk soon, and yes I know it's my turn again after this one too.
