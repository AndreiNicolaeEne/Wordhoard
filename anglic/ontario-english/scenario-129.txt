So we were up north at the cottage, eh, just a normal Saturday, and around lunch the husband goes, hey, look at that, out past the tree line, this haze sitting on the horizon, brownish, real far off still. We just watched it for the better part of an hour, way you'd watch weather rolling in, not even worried, figured it'd just sneak past the way these things sometimes do.

Then the wind switched right up on us. Within the hour that smoke's right on top of the place, deking around the ridge like it had a mind of its own, and my eyes are stinging, throat's got that scratch starting in.

Me: this isn't looking good anymore.
The husband: no worries, they'll have it contained.
Me: we're going. Now.

That was it, twenty minutes, out the door. Grabbed the dog, she's a beauty, wasn't leaving her, grabbed the photos off the wall, didn't even grab the two-four sitting right in the fridge, don't even ask me why, grabbed the butter tarts though, priorities I guess. Give'r down the gravel, hydro already out next door, embers coming down like snow.

Finally the call comes, place is fine, fire went round it, spared. Drove back up shaking the whole way, eavestrough full of ash, but the cottage was standing, still standing, and I just sat down on the dock and didn't move for a while. Sorry, I know that's not much of a story but that's it.
