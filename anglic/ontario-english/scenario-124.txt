I left way later than I meant to, and by the time I actually got out the door it was that kind of dark where the streetlights aren't doing much for you yet, sky still that deep blue but every yard looking like a tunnel. Not two minutes in, off the main drag, I get this feeling, eh, someone's back there, footsteps landing right when mine do, like a shadow with its own two feet.

So I don't turn around, obviously, you don't do that. I slow down a hair, like I'm checking my phone, sorry, my bad, watch where you're going kind of shuffle, and the steps behind slow down too. Okay. Then I speed up, not full give'r, just a bit brisker, and same thing, matched me step for step. That was enough for me right there.

I bailed on my usual shortcut through the park, the dark little cut past the school where nobody's ever around, and went the long way instead, out to the main street where the plaza's still lit up and there's actual traffic, cars wearing their brights down the road like it's the DVP at rush hour. One glance back at the crosswalk, quick, casual, like I was just checking for cars, and there was somebody there, buddy in a dark jacket, hood up, but for all I knew he was just some guy walking home too, no worries, minding his own business, going where he was going.

Last block I was basically power walking, would never admit that to anyone at the time, arms swinging, no chill left in me at all. Got in, deadbolt, chain, stood there with my back against the door a second just breathing.

The roommate: you're all red, you good?

Me: yeah, for sure, just cold out.

Never did find out if it was anything. Probably nothing, some guy heading the same direction I was, that's all it probably ever was. But I still tell it like I was two seconds from getting jumped, eh, half laughing at myself even saying it, how sure I was right in that moment that it was something.
