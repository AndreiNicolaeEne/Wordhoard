The old hand had the ladder up against the eave before the new hand even had his toque off, double-double going cold on the tailgate. "Watch first, eh, before you touch anything." He hooked the hanger against the fascia, top hole first, one clean turn of the drill and it was in. "That's it. Top hole first, snug to the board. Now you try 'er."

New hand climbs up, drives the bottom hole first, hanger sitting crooked as anything.

The old hand: Nah, bud. Top hole first. You did the bottom one there.

The new hand: Oh, sorry, my bad.

The old hand: No worries, just back it out and go top first, that's all.

He backs it out, and then, halfway to trying again: "Wait, so does the hanger go inside the trough, or the trough inside the hanger?"

The old hand, terser now: Hanger holds the trough. Trough sits down in the hanger. Top hole first. I just did it right in front of you.

New hand goes again, hangs it upside down this time, hook facing the wrong way entirely.

The old hand, sharp now, banging his elbow on the ladder rail and muttering sorry to it out of habit: I literally just told you that twice, buddy. Same spot both times. You gotta actually watch, not just stand there.

The new hand, a bit defensive: I was watching, I just. I thought you meant flip it, I dunno, sorry.

The old hand, letting it go, resetting his voice: Okay. Forget top and bottom for a sec. Think of it like hanging a coat on a hook, eh, hook's up, coat hangs off it. Hanger's the hook. It goes up into that top hole and the trough just hangs off it after that.

New hand tries a third time, top hole first, hanger dead level.

The old hand: There it is. Beauty. Give'r the same way right down the whole run and we're laughing. And quit standing right under me, I'm just gonna sneak past ya with the level.
