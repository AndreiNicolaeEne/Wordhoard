So this past year, eh, was the best harvest anyone in the family can remember, hand to God. We knew early on it was gonna be a beauty of a year, like by the time the garden was even half in you could already tell the tomato plants were doing something different, and by the end it come in at almost double what we normally get off that patch, no word of a lie.

The zucchini though, buddy, that's the one that just went nuts. Usually we're happy with maybe forty, fifty off the vines, this year we lost count past a hundred and some, way past the old record, which was my uncle's row from years back, before they redid the highway even. Everyone had to give'r just to keep up, we had cousins out on weekends that don't normally do garden stuff at all, just to get it all in before it turned on us.

Once everybody's freezer, and the neighbours' too, were sorted, whatever was left over we canned a bunch of, gave a ton away, and my uncle traded a truck load for a case of beer and, I'm not even kidding, a mickey that ended up living in somebody's glovebox for later.

The eldest in the family's been doing that garden longer than I've been alive. She stood there at the end of it looking at the pile on the table and just said it straight out.

The eldest: In my whole life I never seen a year like it.

Credited it all to the rain landing right when it needed to, and a mild stretch clean through the summer, nothing too hot, nothing too wet, no worries the whole way.

Still, any bad crop year somebody brings up now, it's, well, no comparison to that one, eh. That's just the year everything else gets measured against.
