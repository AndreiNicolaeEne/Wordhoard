Okay so like six in the morning I go downstairs for coffee and there's water right at the door, just barely over the threshold, ankle deep, no more than that. I'm like, huh, okay, that's not great, eh, but it's not the end of the world either. Went back up, figured I'd deal with it after coffee, you know how it is.

Hour later I check again and it's already up past where it was, and I'm like oh no no no, this is not slowing down. Started hauling stuff up off the floor while I still had time, the totes, the boxes, whatever was down there, up onto the workbench, up onto the stairs, anywhere higher.

By like ten it's at my knee, I kid you not, and I'm just moving everything I can onto the highest thing I own at this point, dressers, the freezer on cinder blocks, doesn't matter, it kept climbing anyway. Called my neighbour.

Neighbour: You getting this too, buddy?
Me: Oh for sure, it's up to my knee down there.
Neighbour: Beauty. Just what we needed, eh.

Early afternoon it peaked, higher than anything I'd raised anything to, past the workbench even, and I just stood at the top of the stairs listening to it slap against the walls for hours, couldn't do a thing, just wait it out. Give'r, I kept telling myself, nothing else to give though at that point.

Few hours after the peak it finally started dropping, slow, but dropping, and I about cried, no worries though, held it together.

Next day I went down and there's this line right across the drywall, right where it stopped, like a tide mark, sorry to the whole basement honestly, it's toast.
