I left later than I meant to, and by the time I got out the door the dark had already come down plumb thick over the ridge, hit was the kind of dark where you can't hardly see your own hand, and I knowed I ought to have started sooner. Was a right smart ways yet to go. Wasn't far down the road before I heared it, footsteps a-falling right about the same time as mine, like an echo that wasn't quite an echo.

I didn't want to look back outright, so I slowed my walk down some, easy, like I wasn't studying on nothing. The steps behind me slowed too. Then I sped up, just a hair, and them steps sped right up along with me. That was enough for me. I quit the shortcut through the holler right there and cut over toward the main road instead, where the porch lights was on and a body could see something coming if it come.

I let myself have one look back, just the once. Wasn't nothing there I could swear to, just shadow and the shape the trees make of theirselves in the dark, and that told me nothing a-tall, not yes and not no.

The last stretch home I walked faster than I'd ever own up to at the time, near about running by the end of it, and when I got that door shut and the latch throwed behind me, I could have sat down right there on the floor from the relief of it.

Me: I never did find out what it was.

The eldest: You reckon it was anything atall?

Me: I don't know to this day. Might could've been nothing but my own feet a-echoing off the bank, or a possum, or nothing at all. But I still tell it the same, half red in the face at how sure I was that night that something was walking right behind me, matching me step for step, all the way home.
