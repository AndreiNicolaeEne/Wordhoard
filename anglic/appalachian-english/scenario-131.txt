The rain hit the tin roof at midday, and the youngest heared it first.

The youngest: Hit's raining, you'uns come look, hit's a-coming down for real!

Everybody rushed for the door, the eldest running out in it, arms flung wide.

The eldest: Lord, I could stand here and let it drown me, we needed it that plumb bad.

Mother: Right smart of it, whole holler's been dry as dust.

They stood out there, listening to it fall.
