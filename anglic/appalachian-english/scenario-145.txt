The granny: What has a heart that don't beat, and hit's got a face but no eyes?
The grandson: A doll?
The granny: Naw. Hit's a clock, settin' on the fireboard.
The grandson: Fine. Here's one back at ye, and hit's a right smart puzzler. What flies all day and never lights, and hit's got no wings a-tall?
The granny: Time?
The grandson: You got it.
The granny: Plumb good un. Go again?
