Me: You ain't gonna believe what come in the mail today.

My friend: What.

Me: Money. Real money, more'n I done seen at oncet in my whole life. Letter was just a-setting there on the fireboard when I got in and I like to fell plumb over reading it.

My friend: Well I'll be! That is just wonderful, I am so happy for you, I mean that!

Me: I'm still a-shaking over it, I ain't gonna lie.

My friend: How much we talkin, if you don't mind me asking plain.

Me: Right at eight thousand dollar, might could be a little more onced everthing's settled out.

My friend: Eight thousand. Lord have mercy. How'd a thing like that even come about?

Me: Insurance money, off the truck getting tore up back in the spring. Took em forever but hit finally come through.

My friend: Must be right nice. I'm over here working myself half to death down at the holler and can't seed two dollars ahead of me the whole year through.

Me: You alright? Hit don't sound like you at all. Something wrong?

My friend: Naw, hit ain't you, hit's me. I reckon I'm just plain envious, if I'm honest with you. Ain't nothing more sinister to it than that. Seeing you get a break like that when I been a-scraping and can't hardly keep the lights on, it done got to me for a minute there.

Me: I understand.

My friend: I do mean it though, the happy part. You've heared me complain about money since we was young'uns, and here you finally got a little ease. That's a good thing and I'm glad of it, truly. You deserve it more'n most anybody I know.
