Buyer: What're ye askin for these?
Seller: Twelve for the batch, and hit's worth ever penny.
Buyer: Twelve's plumb too high. This'un's sigogglin and bruised clean through. Give ye eight, and I'll haul off the whole poke.
Seller: Eight won't hardly cover what I put in raisin' 'em. Hit ain't right.
Buyer: Ten, then, and I'm still takin the lot off ye.
Seller: Ten it is. Reckon that'll do ye fine.
