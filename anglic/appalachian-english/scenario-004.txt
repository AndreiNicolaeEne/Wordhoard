Hush, little un, quit your a-fighting sleep,
hit don't do no good to holler so.
Hush, hush, the moon's a-rocking easy on the ridge tonight,
and she'll rock till you go still.
I ain't a-going nowhere, I'll set right here plumb till morning,
hush, hush, that old moon's still a-rocking,
so let them eyes fall shut now, hush, hush, and sleep.
