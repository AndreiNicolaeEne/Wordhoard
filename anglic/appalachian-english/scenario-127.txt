The younger: Lord, hit's hot enough to fry an egg on the porch rail today.
The elder: Hit is. Weren't much better Tuesday neither, laid on ye like a wet poke.
The younger: Sure was.
The elder: Puts me in mind of the summer I was knee-high, stayed plumb dry three weeks a-running, crick down the holler nigh went dry. Reckon tomorrow might could bring us a storm.
The younger: Hope you're right.
