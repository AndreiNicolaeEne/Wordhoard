Traveler: What's it gonna cost me clear to town?
Driver: Twenty, and I ain't haggling much on it.
Traveler: Twenty? Hit ain't but a few mile up the holler, I'll give ye twelve.
Driver: Twelve? Now don't be a-lowballing me, I got to buy gas too. Might could do fifteen and that's plumb generous.
Traveler: Sixteen, and we're square, right smart fair for the both of us.
Driver: Sixteen it is then. Climb on in, we're burning daylight.
