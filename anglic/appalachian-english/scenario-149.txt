The eldest: Blessed be the one who bides right, hit'll foller you plumb to the far ridge, good come home to your door like water runs downhill, sworn on the creek, sworn on the kin. Now hear the turn. Cursed be the one who don't: sorrow'll foller you plumb to the far ridge, ill come home to your door like water runs downhill, sworn on the creek, sworn on the kin. What's spoke over water don't wash off.
