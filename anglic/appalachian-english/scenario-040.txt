Me: Well, y'uns wanted the whole of it, so here it is, start to finish.

I was up and gone way before I needed to be, couldn't hardly choke down no breakfast for my stomach was a-churning so bad. Set out near on an hour early, just to be safe, and ended up a-setting in that lot by myself watching the sun come up, nerved up over nothing.

The one in charge come out first thing, older feller, easy in his talk, didn't make me feel low at all. Walked me through quick and had me started before I even got my coat off good.

Daddy: What'd they set you to doing first off?

Me: Sorting and stacking, nothing hard on the mind, but they had it coming steadier than I looked for, hit never let up the whole morning. I figured they'd ease me in slow. No sir. Right smart more of it than I braced myself for.

And I did mess up, right out where ever'body could see it too. Stacked a whole load wrong and it come down sigogglin every which way across the floor. I like to died. But nobody fussed on me none, they just laughed some and we set it right together.

Mother: Was any of them decent to be around?

Me: On my break I just set back quiet and sized the bunch of em up. Most seemed alright, kept to theirselves some. But there was one older feller come over without me asking a thing, showed me a trick to reading the tags so I wasn't a-hunting all day like a fool. Didn't have to do that for me.

The eldest: How long'd they run you?

Me: Nine hours dead, and by the end my feet was hollering at me plumb the whole way home. Drove back just a-replaying every bit of it in my head, over and over.

But setting here with y'uns now, I'll tell it plain: it went fine. Rough in spots. I believe I might could make something out of it yet.
