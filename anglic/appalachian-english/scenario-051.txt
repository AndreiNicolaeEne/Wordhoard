The husband: Now if that money was to land in my lap come tomorrow, first thing I'm doing is driving down and getting me a truck that don't rattle like a poke full of rocks.

The wife: Lord, you and that truck. Hit ain't been three year since the last one and here you go a-hollering about a new one already.

The husband: Well I heared Cousin's boy got him one with the heated seats and all, and mine's plumb wore slap out.

The wife: You'd do better to set some back first. We was always taught you don't blow it all in one place. Put you back a right smart of it and let it set.

The husband: Set where at? In a coffee can up on the fireboard?

The wife: Might could put it in the bank same as anybody does. Or buy that piece up the holler, the one with the old barn gone all sigogglin, and fix it up decent.

The husband: Now that's more sense than I figured you had in you tonight. But a body's got to have a little fun with it too.

The wife: Fun's fine, long as it ain't foolish. I used to could talk sense into you.

The husband: You'uns -

The wife: You'uns? Hit's just me and you setting here.

The husband: I was fixing to say, we ought to buy the whole ridge and build us a house with a swimming pool clear around it, like a moat.

The wife: A moat. Lord have mercy, I do believe you been borned again a fool since supper.

The husband: I seed one on the television one time. Rich folks had it.

The wife: Rich folks ain't got the sense God give a goose neither.

The husband: Alright, alright. We'll split it down the middle then - half put away, half for something good, and no moat.

The wife: That there I can live with. Now get up off that step before you set here all night a-dreaming on money we ain't never even got yet.
