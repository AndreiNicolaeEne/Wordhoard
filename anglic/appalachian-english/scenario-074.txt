Me: She cancelled on us again. Called it off not two hour before we was s'posed to meet up at the diner.

My friend: Lord, not again. What's it this time.

Me: Said her back was a-hurting her so bad she couldn't hardly set up.

My friend: Hit's always somethin with her, hain't it. I remember that one time she done the same to us right before the fair, said she'd done throwed her back out haulin wood, and then her sister seed her up on the ridge that very evenin walkin around fine as frog hair.

Me: That's right, I plumb forgot about that one. So that's, what, one at the fair, one at Christmas when she said her car wouldn't start, one when she just quit answerin altogether that Sunday -

My friend: And now this'n. That's four.

Me: Four times, and every time it's somethin you cain't hardly argue with. Can't tell nobody their back don't hurt.

My friend: I don't know, I reckon it might be true this round. She has been complainin on that back a right smart while now, more'n just talk.

Me: Could be. But even if it is true this time, don't change the other three.

My friend: No it don't. I used to could just let it roll off me but here lately it's a-wearin on me some.

Me: You'uns think we ought to even ask her along next time, or just let her be.

My friend: I don't want to be the one that quits invitin her. That ain't right neither, cuttin her out like that.

Me: I ain't sayin cut her out. I'm sayin we might could ask her one more time and see.

My friend: And if she cancels again.

Me: Then we go on without her and don't wait around leveling with ourselves over it no more. We go, she comes if she comes.

My friend: That's fair. I can live with that.
