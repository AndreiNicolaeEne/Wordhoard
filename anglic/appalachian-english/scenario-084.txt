Stranger: Ma'am, hate to bother ye, but I been a-walking since noon and I'm plumb parched. Reckon they's a little water to spare?

The woman: Set right there, hit ain't no bother.

She hands him over a whole cup, brimming full.

Stranger: Aw, I didn't need all that, just a swaller woulda done.

The woman: Hush now and drink, they's a-plenty more.

Stranger: Well I thank ye, I mean it now, right kindly of ye.
