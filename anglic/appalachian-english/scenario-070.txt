I was set out on the porch a-shelling beans when the first friend come up the holler path, walking fast and talking before she even got to the steps good.

The first friend: I ain't even mad no more, I'm just done. She stood there in my yard yesterday evening a-hollering at me in front of everbody like I'd stole something off her, and all I done was tell her what her own sister told me. Hit ain't my fault if the truth don't sit good.

Me: Well now. Set down a minute and get your breath. I ain't taking up for nobody yet, I'm just listening.

The first friend: I know it. I just needed somebody to hear it that wasn't gonna go running back and telling.

She didn't stay but a little while, and wasn't two hours passed before the second friend come through, still got dirt on her knees from the garden, madder than a wet hen.

The second friend: She's telling it all backwards. I never hollered first, she come at me first, plumb ugly about it too. I might could've said something I oughtn't, but I never started hit.

Me: Both of you'uns swear the other one throwed the first word. So let me ask you something plain. Was this here about what her sister said, or was it about her not asking you before she went and told it to somebody else?

The second friend: ...It was that. She never even asked me was it true fore she went spreading it around like right smart of gossip.

Me: That's what I figured.

The second friend: I reckon I could've asked her private instead of blowing up in her yard. I ain't saying I'm all wrong, but I could've done that part different.

Me: That's something anyhow. Look here, you two ain't gonna get nowhere with me a-carrying tales back and forth between you like some old sigogglin fence, leaning first one way then the other. Why don't you'uns just talk to one another, face to face, and I'll set right here on the porch with you if you want a body between you while you do it.
