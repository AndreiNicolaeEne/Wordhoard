The neighbor: You heared that racket out in the road last night? Sometime after midnight, weren't close to neither house.

The other neighbor: Hit woke me plumb up. I laid there a-listening but couldn't tell what it were.

The neighbor: I figured some critter got in something, knocked it over.

The other neighbor: Could be, but hit sounded right smart bigger than a coon or such.

The neighbor: Might could been somebody's dog got loose, or a deer even.

The other neighbor: Well, I don't rightly know. If it happens again tonight we'll both keep an ear out.
