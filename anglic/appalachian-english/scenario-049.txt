I caught him down at the store of a evening, since he lives down the holler from me and don't come up this way much no more. He's been owing me sixty dollars since his truck broke down on him back in March, and I hadn't said nothing about it, but hit had gone on long enough now.

Me: I ain't wanting to be ugly about it, but you still owe me that sixty dollars from when your truck went down back in March. That's going on five months now.

My friend: Aw, I know it, I know it. I been meaning to get that to you, I just been slammed with work here lately, a-working overtime most every night.

Me: That's near about what you told me before Easter too. You said you'd have it to me by the first of the month and that month come and went a long time ago.

My friend: Things is different now, I can't just pull sixty dollars out of thin air ever' time you get to worryin' about it. You act like I'm fixin' to cheat you out of it.

Me: I never said that. I'm just saying hit's been going on since March, and I've asked you more than the once now, real polite each time.

My friend: I don't need you keeping score on me like I'm some kind of deadbeat. I said I'd pay you and I will, when I get to it.

Me: A body ought to be good on his word, is all I'm saying. We was raised better than that, both of us.

My friend: I got twenty on me right now. Take that much, and I'll have you the rest of it directly.

Me: Directly like last time?

My friend: I mean it this time. I swear I do.

Me: Well. I'll take the twenty. But don't make me come askin' again.
