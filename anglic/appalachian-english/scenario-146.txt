Now hear me, you'uns, my granddaddy, plumb the finest that ever drawed breath on this ridge. He'll out-plow any mule a-working that bottom and hit ain't even a contest. Hit's the gospel truth. He might could outlast the creek its own self, run on longer than water off the mountain. Ain't no man from here to Knoxville fit to tote his poke. Hit's the gospel truth. Borned in this holler, and right here he'll die a legend.
