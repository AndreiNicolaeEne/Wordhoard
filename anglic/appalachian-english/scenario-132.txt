Eleven days that cold set in and held, and I don't misremember a hour of it. Come down colder than any of us looked for right from the first morning, hit dropped so fast the well bucket had a skin of ice on it before we ever got out to the barn, and I like to froze my galluses off walking to the woodpile. We knowed then this one weren't gonna be no ordinary cold snap.

I kept that stove a-going round the clock the whole stretch, banked of a night, up every couple hours to feed it, for if you let hit die back even a little the pipes and near everything else in the house was fixing to freeze. We went through right smart of wood, cutting and splitting faster'n we could burn it, and still come up short some nights.

The spring line that ain't never failed in all the years we been up this holler, hit froze plumb solid, and the pipe run under the porch busted wide open where nobody could see it till the thaw. We heared it let go in the night, a crack like a gunshot, and the porch step's sat sigogglin ever since, heaved up outta true. Come morning we was hauling water in a poke off the springhouse just to have enough for washing.

The eldest set by the fire of an evening and studied on it, said you'uns don't know what cold is, said she ain't seed the like since she was knee-high, back when folks lost fingers to it and a body might could freeze setting too near a open window. After a while she said:

The eldest: I heared tell that old spell took the paw-paw trees clean down to the root. This one's beat it though. I ain't never seed the mercury set where it's set of a night, not even that year.

The ninth night was the worst of the whole stretch, wind a-hollering round the eaves fit to pull the fireboard off the wall, and we lost the young calf to it, borned too soon into all that and couldn't be saved no matter what we tried.

Eleven days, and then the thaw come of a sudden, ice a-letting go of the tin roof in sheets, water a-running everwhere down off the ridge, and it felt like the whole mountain let its breath out at last.
