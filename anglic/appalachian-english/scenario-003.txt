The husband: I been a-thinking on it all evening and I still like Wade. Wade's a good strong name.

The wife: Wade. Lord, no. That was your uncle's dog's name, hit was, the one that got hisself run over up on the ridge road. I ain't naming my baby after a hound.

The husband: Well fine then. What about Dallas.

The wife: Dallas ain't even a person name, hit's a place clear off in Texas somewheres. I don't want folks asking why we named him after a city we ain't never been to.

The husband: You're plumb hard to please tonight, you know that.

The wife: I'm just tired is all. We was up half the night with him a-hollering and I ain't got the patience for foolishness.

The husband: What if we was to name him after your daddy then. Give him the old man's name, carry it on.

The wife: Now see, that sounds sweet till you remember my brother already named his boy that, and cousin up the holler done the same thing two year back. We'd have three of him running around every family dinner and nobody knowing which one you was hollering at.

The husband: Might could shorten it some, call him by the middle.

The wife: Might could, might couldn't. That ain't fixing nothing.

The husband: Well who's even supposed to say on this anyhow. I carried half the worry same as you did.

The wife: I heared that same argument from my own mother about me and my sister, and it never did get settled between her and Daddy neither, and here we are.

The husband: Reckon we just sleep on it again.

The wife: Reckon we do. He ain't got a fireboard to lean his name on yet, he can wait one more night same as us.
