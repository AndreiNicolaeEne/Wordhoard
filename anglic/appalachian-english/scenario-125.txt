The neighbor: Lord God, hit's the barn up the holler, roof a-caving in, struck plumb through!
Me: What? Hit cain't be.
The neighbor: I seed it my own self, was, was standing right there when the lightning hit her, and the mules a-hollering fit to wake the dead.
Me: Is anybody hurt?
The neighbor: I don't rightly know. My breath's plumb gone, might could be worse than we know yet.
