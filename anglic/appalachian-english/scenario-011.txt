Me: I was fixing to rock that empty chair, to set it a-going.
The eldest: Quit that. Rock a empty chair and you're a-calling death up the holler.
Me: Hit don't do nothing though, does it, if I go on and rock it?
The eldest: It surely does, believe it or don't. Still it dead and lay your hand on the seat, that undoes it.
Me: Rolled my eyes, but stilled it and touched the seat, just in case.
