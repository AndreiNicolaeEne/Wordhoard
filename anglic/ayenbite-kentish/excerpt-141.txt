Þe þridde þing is / ssarpnes of metes.
Vor ase zayþ saint bernard. þys spilþ ine lostes. alsuo ase he spilþ ine þe wetere / þet zuo longe he may by þer onder: þet he lyest þet lif.
Non ne may habbe his heaued. þet is his herte / longe ine þe wetere / of lostes of þise wordle / þet he ne ssel lyese þet lyf / þet is þe grace of þe holy gost. be huam þe zaule leueþ ine god.
