Þe zixte boȝ of auarice / is symonye. þet is zuo ycleped / uor ane wychche / þet hette Symoun / þet wolde begge / of seynte peter þe apostel / þe grace / uor to do miracles / and byad grat guod. and þeruore / hi byeþ y-cleped / Symoniaks : alle þo / þet wylleþ zelle / oþer begge / þe gostliche þinges. þet is amang / alle þe dyadliche zennes : on of þe grateste.
