Nou we willeþ zigge uerst of þe yefþe of onderstondinge / be þan þet þe holy gost wile ous teche.
Þes yefþe þet is y-hote yefþe of onderstondinge / ne is oþer þing / be þe halȝen / and bi þe maistres / bote a lyȝt and a grace of briȝtnesse / þet þe holy gost zent in-to þe herte / huerby onderstondinge of man is arered uor to knawe his sseppere / and þe gostliche þinges þet ne moȝe naȝt by y-zoȝe bodilich ine alle þe þinges þet be-longeþ to þe helþe of zaule. mid skele kendelich ne onderstondinge of man of him / ne uor him / ne may come.
