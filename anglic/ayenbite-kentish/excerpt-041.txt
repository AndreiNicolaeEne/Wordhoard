Þe oþer boȝ : is of mete / and of drinke / be to moche / and wiþ-oute mesure.
Þe ilke byeþ / propreliche glotounes / þet al uorzuelȝeþ / ase deþ þe kete of
his sperringe.
Hit is grat wyt / to loki mesure / ine mete / and ine drinke / and grat helþe. uor moche uolk sterueþ. and ofte þer comeþ / greate ziknesses.
