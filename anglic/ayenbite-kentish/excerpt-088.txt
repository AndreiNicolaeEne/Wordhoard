Þet bread we byddeþ to oure uader. and him we biddeþ / þet he hit ous yeue to-day. ine þise daye / þet is ine þise dyadliche liue. zuo þet we moȝe maky ane guode iornee / and to abyde þe gledlaker oure ssepe.
Þet is þe peny þet he yefþ to his workmen / huanne euen comþ. þet is þe ende of þe liue.
Dimitte nobis debita nostra sicut et nos dimittimus debitoribus nostris.
Ine þise bene we biddeþ oure uader of heuene / þet he ous wylle uoryeue / oure misdedes / ase we uoryeueþ to ham: þet ous mis-doþ. oþer habbeþ mis-do. þanne zigge we þus. 'Leue uader / uoryef ous oure dettes: ase we uoryeueþ oure dettours.' Oure dettes: byeþ oure zennes / þet we habbeþ ydo wexe ope oure zaulen. þet is þet beste wed of þe house.
