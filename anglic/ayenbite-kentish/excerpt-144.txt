Vor þet is a uirtue huerby zaule onderuangþ specialliche more loue / and þe fauour of oure lhorde Iesu crist.
Þanne saint Ion þe ewangelist þet wes mayde / wes amang þe apostles / þe meste belouede of oure lhorde. and him ssewede oure lhord þe meste tokne of louerede / ase hit sseweþ ine þe godspelle.
