Þe midel guodes / byeþ of kende: and of techinge.
Of kende: ase uayrhede of bodye. prouesse. strengþe. zuyfthede. myldenesse. clyer wyt. sleȝþe. onderstondynge. and alle zuyche guodes þet kende berþ.
Be techinge : ase grat clergye. ine alle oþre guodes: þet me wynþ be studye / oþer be guode wone / ase byeþ guode þeawes / and zome uirtues.
