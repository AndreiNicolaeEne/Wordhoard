Þe holy writ comparisoneþ þe zaule and of þe guode manne / an of þe guode wyfmanne : to ane uayre gardyne uol of grenhede / and of uayre trawes / and of guod frut.
Huerof god zayþ ine þe boc of loue. 'My zoster / my lemman / þou art a gardin besset / myd tuo ssetteles.' þet is þe grace of god / and of angles.
