Wel more he him ssel loki / þe ilke þet comþ to cherche / þet is godes chombre / and godes hous / to done / and to zigge / to-uore god / and to-uore his angles / þing þet him ne liki.
God nele naȝt þet me maki his hous marcat / ne boþe / huerout he wrek / þo þe zyalde / and boȝte / ine þe temple. uor he nele / þet me maki uorewerdes / ne noyses / ne nyedes seculeres / þerinne. ac raþre wile / þet me onderstonde / to bidde god deuouteliche / and him herie / and þonki of al his guode.
Þer me ssel wyþ-clepie his herte to him / and do al out / alle seculere niedes. yemere þoȝtes. and þenche an his sseppere / and ane his guode guodnesses þet god him heþ y-do. and him deþ communliche. recordi his zennes / and his lackes / and him-zelue mildi / to-uore god / and bidde uoryeuenesse / and grace / him to loki uram zenne. and bleuinge ine guode liue / al to þe ende.
