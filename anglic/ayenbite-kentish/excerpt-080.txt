Nou ous sseweþ / huer-of þis word / oure. þe largesse / and þe cortaysye / ofgod oure uader. þet ous yefþ more bleþeliche / ynoȝ / þanne lyte and to uelen : þanne to onen allone.
Huer-of saynt gregorie zayþ. 'þe bene / þe more þet hi is commun : þe more hy is worþ. ase þe candele is betere bezet þet serueþ to ane halle and uol of uolk: þanne zy þet ne serueþ / bote to onlepy manne.' Þis word hat ous to yelde þonkes myd al oure herten. of þise grace þet he ous heþ ydo / huer-by we byeþ his children / and his eyrs. and þet moche ardontliche louye oure ealde broþer Iesu crist / þet ous uelaȝeþ wyþ him ine his grace.
