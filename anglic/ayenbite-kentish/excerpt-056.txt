And þous geþ al oure lyf.
Nou we were ybore: and an haste dyad. ne al oure lyf: nes naȝt bote a lyte prikke. nou we byeþ ine zorȝe wyþ-oute ende.
Oure blisse / is ywent in-to wop. oure karoles: into zorȝe. gerlondes. robes. playinges. messinges. and alle guodes byeþ ous yfayled.' Zuyche byeþ þo zonges of helle. ase þe writinge ous telþ. ous uor to Ssewy: þet þis lyf / ne is bote a wendynge: wel ssort. and þis wordle: ne is bote wendynge. and libbe ne is bote a wendynge.
