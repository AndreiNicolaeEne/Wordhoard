Ah left later than ah meant tae, that's the first thing, cause ah wis sittin blethering longer than ah should huv, and by the time ah wis oot the door it wis pure black already. No even dreich, jist black. Wisnae far up the road when ah got that feelin, ye know the one, the wee hairs oan the back ae yer neck gaun up. Fitsteps ahint me, landin close tae the same time as ma ain, like somebody matchin me stride fur stride.

Ah didnae turn roond. Ah'm no daft. Ah slowed doon a wee bit first, like ah wis jist takin ma time, and the steps ahint slowed an aw. Then ah sped up, no runnin, jist a wee bit quicker, and same again, they sped up tae. Baith times matchin me exact. That wis enough tae decide me.

So instead ae cuttin doon ma usual shortcut, the wee lane ahint the shops that's fine in the day but pure deid efter dark, ah went the long way roond, up by the main road where the streetlights are aw workin and there's still folk aboot, somebody walkin a dug, a wee queue ootside the chippy. Ah let masel one look back, jist the one, as casual as ah could make it look, and there wis naethin certain there either way, jist shapes and shadows and a burd flappin oot a bush that near gave me a heart attack oan its ain.

The last bit ah walked faster than ah'd ever admit tae onybody, no runnin exactly, but no far off it either, and gettin the door shut and the latch oan wis the best feelin ah'd had aw week.

Never found oot whit it wis. Might huv been naethin, jist some other soul walkin the same way hame, mindin their ain business.

The mother: Ye were shakin comin in that night, hen.

Me: Wis probably naethin.

But ah still tell it half laughin at masel noo, how sure ah wis in the moment that there wis somebody right ahint me the whole way.
