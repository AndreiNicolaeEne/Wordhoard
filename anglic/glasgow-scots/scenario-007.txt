The counter: Ickle ockle, black bottle, ickle ockle oot, and that's you, wee yin, oot ye go, hen.
The one caught: Naw, that cannae be right, ye went ower fast and skipped past her, dae it again.
Another wean: Ye ken fine, stert a stooshie ower it and it's back tae the top, the whole rhyme again.
The counter, louder: Ickle ockle, black bottle, ickle ockle oot, and that's you, wee yin, oot ye go, nae arguin this time.
