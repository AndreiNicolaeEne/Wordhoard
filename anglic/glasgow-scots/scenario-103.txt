The traveler: Sorry tae bother ye, hen ,  whit's the quickest way tae the market fae here?
The stranger: Nae bother, love. First left doon there, then keep gaun till ye pass the wee chemist oan the corner ,  that's yer landmark, second corner efter that and ye're near enough there.
The traveler: How long's the walk, roughly?
The stranger: Aboot fifteen minutes oan foot, gonnae fly in.
The traveler: Brilliant, thanks ever so much.
The stranger: Aye, nae bother, hen. Away ye go then.
