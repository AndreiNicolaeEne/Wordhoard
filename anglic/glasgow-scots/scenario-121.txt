Neighbor: Here, did youse hear that stooshie ootside last night? Well efter midnight, oot in the street somewhere.

The other neighbor: Aye, I heard it awright, near jumped oot ma skin. Whit dae ye think it wis?

Neighbor: No a scooby. Thought mibbe a fox or a cat knockin the bins ower, but I'm no convinced, hen, it was too loud for that.

The other neighbor: Could've been some bampot stumblin hame drunk. Or a motor backfirin.

Neighbor: Mibbe. Well, if it happens the morra's night an aw, gie's a shout and we'll baith keep an ear oot.

The other neighbor: Aye, right you are.
