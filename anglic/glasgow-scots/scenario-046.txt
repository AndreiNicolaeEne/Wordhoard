Buyer: Whit's the price on they apples the day?
Seller: Twelve for the batch, hen.
Buyer: Twelve? Half ae they's bruised, gie's them for eight.
Seller: Naw, cannae dae that, ye'd have me workin fur nothin.
Buyer: Ten then, and I'll take the wee poke an aw.
Seller: Aw right, ten it is. Away ye go, hen.
