It's a dreich sort of night in the wee kitchen, the two ae them sat wi a cup ae tea gaun cold.

Me: Right, hen, ah'm no wantin a stooshie ower this, but ah've tae say it. It's forty quid ye owe us, an it's been since afore Christmas. That's gettin on for eight month noo.

My mate: Aw here we go. Ah know whit yer gonnae say afore ye even say it.

Me: Ah'm no sayin it tae be crabbit aboot it. Ah'm jist sayin whit's true, so ah am.

My mate: Ah've no forgot, right. Things huv just been mental. The weans huv been greetin aff their heids wi some bug aw week, an ah've had tae get the messages in a poke every second day jist tae keep the hoose gaun on nothin.

Me: Ah hear ye. But mind, this isnae the first time. Ye told me ye'd huv it tae me by the end ae February, ye stood right there an said it yersel. That date's come an went an aw, same as the wan afore it.

My mate: Ah didnae say that tae string ye alang.

Me: Ah'm no sayin ye did. Ah'm jist sayin ah cannae keep hearin a new date every time ah open ma mooth aboot it. It's no the money that's the sair bit, hen, it's bein made tae ask.

My mate: So whit, ye think ah'm some gallus wee eejit sittin on it, laughin tae masel? Ah'm skint. Ah'm no a bampot, ah'm daein ma best here.

Me: Ah never said ye were.

My mate: Well it feels like it. Ah'll tell ye that for nothin.

Me: Ah'm no tryin tae start a stooshie wi ye. Ah'm jist tired askin, that's aw it is.

My mate: Look, ah've got twenty ah can gie ye the day. The rest by the morra, ah swear it.

Me: Aye. We'll see, eh.
