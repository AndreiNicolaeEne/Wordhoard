The borrower: Here, is that rain no gie'in up the day. Drookit the whole walk up.

The friend: Aye it's been like that since the morra was yesterday, if ye know whit ah mean. Sit doon, get that jaicket aff ye.

The borrower: Cannae stop long, just got the weans' messages tae get, poke's already burstin oot the boattom of it. How's yer maw daein onyway, still crabbit wi the hip?

The friend: She's aye crabbit, hip or no hip. You're no here tae ask after ma maw's hip but, whit's up wi you, ye've that face on ye.

The borrower: Whit face.

The friend: That face. C'mon then, oot wi it.

The borrower: Right, naw, it's nothin big, honest, just, could ye lend us a hunner quid. Just till the end ae the month. Ah widnae ask ye normally, ye know that.

The friend: A hunner? Whit's it for, ya eejit, ye're no in some stooshie are ye.

The borrower: Naw naw, nothin like that, just the leccy's went funny and it's a wee bit mair than ah thought, that's aw, it's manageable, honest tae god.

The friend: Manageable. Right. Ah'll gie ye it, but ye're payin it back the Friday after next when you're paid, no the next month efter that, no excuses this time, mind.

The borrower: Friday after next, on ma life, first thing oot the wage. You're a pure diamond, d'ye know that, an angel, an actual saint walkin aboot Glesga in a cagoule.

The friend: Aye aye, calm doon, it's a hunner quid no a kidney.

The borrower: Might as well be, state ae ma bank the day.

The friend: Away ye go ya bampot, and take yer weans' messages afore that poke goes an aw.
