God's truth, my friend, I'm done in. Ten oors on the go the day and I'm only sittin doon the now.

Started at seven and the body wisnae even awake yet, still half in the bed in ma heid. First job was liftin the auld flags aff the back green, and that set the pace for the whole day right there, cause it was dreich aw mornin and they were drookit frae the rain the night afore, every yin heavier than the last. Like an eejit I forgot ma gloves the first oor an aw, so that didnae help.

By aboot nine ma back's lettin me know it's no happy, this wee nip right at the bottom that just sat there aw day gettin worse and worse.

Took a break aboot half ten, ten minutes wi a cup of tea and a piece, barely enough tae draw breath never mind rest, and back at it we went.

The efternoon was the killer but, I'm tellin ye. Aboot two, three o'clock, shiftin rubble roond the back, hands that sair I couldnae hardly grip the shovel, and I'm no gonnae lie tae ye, I thought aboot chuckin it and away hame early. Crabbit as onything by that point, so I was.

There was wan bit though, the guttering roond the side, I thought that would take the rest of the efternoon and I had it done in aboot forty minutes, dead easy, wee lift in an otherwise grindin day, that.

The gaffer had forty owed tae me for it an aw, so that's no bad.

The morra's either mair of the same or I get tae rest the body. Naw, I'm hopin for the rest, cause I'm peely-wally and knackered the now, hen. Could sleep for a week.
