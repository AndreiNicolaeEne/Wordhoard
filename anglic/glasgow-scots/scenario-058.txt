Me: Awright hen, ye got two minutes? Ah'm at ma wit's end wi that roof.

My friend: Aye, come away in, whit's up noo.

Me: It's the corner abin the kitchen, ower by the windae. Been drippin since the back end ae the year, and noo when it's pure dreich oot, like the day, it's no drippin, it's pourin. Ah've a wee basin oot and everything, the ceilin's aw gone broon and bubbly, pure drookit up there.

My friend: Whit did the landlord say when ye first tellt him?

Me: First time, months ago noo, he says aw aye, he'll get a man oot the week. Naethin. Ah phoned again a fortnight later, same story, "aye hen, ah'm ontae it, gie's a few days." That wis weeks and weeks back.

My friend: And ye asked him again since?

Me: Aye, jist the other day. Exact same patter, word for word. "The joiner's snowed under, hen, soon as." Hasnae even sent naebody roond tae look at it, never mind fix it.

My friend: Right, so how long's this been gaun on, aw telt?

Me: Since afore the New Year. Three, four month noo.

My friend: And whit did he actually promise ye, in they words?

Me: Jist "a man'll be oot." Never a day, never a name, nothin ye could haud him tae.

My friend: That's the thing, ye've nothin on paper. Ye could write it aw doon, every date ye phoned, and send him a wee letter sayin fix it by such a time - slow, but it's on record then. Or ye go tae the council, environmental health, get somebody oot tae see it wi their ain eyes - quicker tae shift him, but some ae they landlords get right crabbit and start lookin for reasons tae get ye oot. Or ye stop the rent yersel, but ah widnae, hen, ye end up the one in bother, no him.

Me: Whit did youse dae, wi yer auld hoose?

My friend: Same carry on, exact same excuse. Roof leakin ower the bedroom, he kept sayin the morra, the morra, months ae it, the wean greetin every time it rained. Got the council in the end and he had a man oot inside the week. Funny that.

Me: Right, that's whit ah'll dae then. Phone the council the morra.

My friend: Aye, dae that. And keep every text he sends ye an aw, hen.
