The niece: Auntie, auntie, she's had it, she's had the wean!

The aunt: Whit? Come in aff the step, hen, whit time?

The niece: Just efter six the morn, a wee lassie, healthy as anythin.

The aunt: Aw thank god fur that. That's the third yin noo intae the family.

The niece: Aye, third wean, they're chuffed tae bits.

The aunt: Right, get yer coat, we're no waitin till the morra, we're gaun the noo.
