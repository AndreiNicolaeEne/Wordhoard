Ah went tae the tin ahint the flour where the messages money sits, the wee bit by for the messages and the leccy, and it wisnae right. Ah counted it twice, that's how ah kent it wisnae just me imaginin things, and there wis a good few notes short, no just loose change. Checked the sideboard drawer as well, in case somebody'd shifted it there like they dae sometimes, but naw, nothin there either.

So ah started askin, quiet like, gaun roon the hoose.

Me: Here, has anybody been in ma tin?

The eldest: Naw.

The da: No me, hen, ah've no even been near that press.

The youngest: Whit tin?

Naebody kent nothin, aw the same flat answer, like they'd rehearsed it aff each other. But it wis only the eldest that wis in the hoose their lane the day it must've went, everybody else oot at work or the school, so ah went back tae them, and this time ah wisnae mild aboot it.

Me: Right, you were here yer lane aw day Tuesday. That money wis fine the night afore and it's no fine now. Whit happened tae it?

The eldest: Ah'm tellin ye, ah never touched it.

Me: Naebody else wis even in the hoose!

The eldest: That disnae mean it wis me! Mibbe you're just wrong aboot how much wis in there tae start wae.

Me: Ah counted it twice, ah'm no glaikit.

The eldest: Ah never took yer money, right, ah'm no sayin it again!

Me: Somebody did, and youse are the only one that wis here, so whit am ah meant tae think?

The eldest: Think whit ye like, ah didnae dae it!

It just sat there efter that, naebody backin doon, the two ay us stood in the kitchen wae the stooshie hingin in the air and nothin sorted, nothin near it.
