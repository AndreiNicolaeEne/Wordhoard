Guest: That broth's got a richness aff it the day, hen, that wee smoky bit at the back ay the throat, whit is that?
Cook: Trick? There's nae trick, just an auld pot that's seen better days than us baith.
Guest: Away ye go, don't be a wee eejit wi us, there's something in there yer no sayin.
Cook: Aye awright, there's a bit ay bacon goes in early doors, the rest yer no getting.
