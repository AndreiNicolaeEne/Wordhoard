Woke up the day and it hit me like a bus afore I even opened ma eyes. The garage. The whole garage, top tae bottom, aw they years ay boxes and rubbish, and me swearin blind months ago, sittin there wi ma tea gaun cauld, "right, that's it, gie's a day and I'll dae the whole lot, nae messin." Should huv kept ma mooth shut.

Lay there a good five meenits jist starin at the ceilin thinkin naw, naw I cannae be bothered, surely there's a version ay the day where I don't. There isnae. Got up onyway, grumpy as sin, pu'in on ma auldest joggies and moanin tae masel the whole way doon the stair.

Me: whit wis I even thinkin, swearin tae dae this in the first place, eejit that I am.

The mother, at the door wi the kettle on: aw good, ye're up. The morra widnae huv done, hen, ye said the day.

Me: aye aye, I know whit I said.

Intae it then. First hour it wis fine, second hour I'm cursin every box, third hour ma back's away and I'm shoutin at nathin in particular aboot how long this is takin and how it should huv been the morra, no the day, no this dreich freezin day ay aw days. Roon aboot two, drookit wi sweat and covered in stoor, I nearly chucked it, jist stood there thinkin I could walk away the noo and naebody would say a word.

Me: naw. Ye swore. That's that.

Didnae stop again efter that. Grumbled the whole rest ay it, aye, moanin aboot ma back, ma hauns, the state ay the boxes, but I didnae quit. Finished the lot by the dark, every last bit ay it, exactly like I said I would.

The eldest, comin oot tae see: ye actually done it then.

Me: ay course I did. I said I would, didn't I.
