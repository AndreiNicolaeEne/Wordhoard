Hear me, aw ye gathered, this yin's for the eldest, gallus tae the bone, hen o the hale toon. Nae boy roond here could haud a candle tae her, wisnae in the runnin. Nae city frae here tae Aiberdeen could raise her marra, no wi aw their siller. She's the sun ower Glesga when the rest o us is drookit and dreich, whit a wumman. Stand up for her, stand ah says, whit a wumman, there'll no be anither.
