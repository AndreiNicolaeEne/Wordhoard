Ach, ma hen, ye're deid, and the hoose is that quiet withoot ye rattlin the poker in the grate. Ye'd gie us a row fur mucky boots, then shove a plate o soup doon in front ae us onyway. Gone, gone, and naebody tae shout the messages in fur the morra. Gone, gone. The wean asks when ye're comin back through, and A cannae tell her naw yet. Sleep easy, ma hen, ye're no forgot, jist away afore us.
