The eldest: Whit hus a heid, a bed, and nivir sleeps a wink?
Her workmate: A burd?
The eldest: Naw, hen, ya glaikit wee thing, it's a river.
Her workmate: Right, gonnae. Whit gits mair drookit the mair it dries?
The eldest: A shirt?
Her workmate: Naw. It's a towel, ya eejit.
The eldest: Ah cannae believe ye got me. Go on then, dinnae leave me doon, gie's anither yin.
Her workmate: Naw, that's me done, ma heid's done in efter that stooshie.
