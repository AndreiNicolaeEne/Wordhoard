The neighbour: Oh God, there's been a smash ootside the shop, hen, I seen it wi ma ain eyes!

The mother: Whit? Whit dae ye mean, is it the wean?

The neighbour: Haud on, the motor come right up ower the pavement, it wisnae even slowin, and she wis staunin there wi her messages in a poke...

The mother: Is she hurt? Tell me, is she hurt!

The neighbour: I cannae mind, it aw happened that fast, ma heid's away wi it.

The mother: Right, right. C'mere, hen. Sit doon.
