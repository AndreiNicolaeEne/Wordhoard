Woke up early doors and there wis a wee trickle of water right at the front door, no even an inch, just lapping ower the step like it wisnae sure if it wis wanted in or no. I thought aye, that'll go doon on its ain, rivers dae that. Went back tae ma bed for an oor.

Checked again efter the oor and it wisnae going doon at all, it wis up tae ma ankle in the hall and creeping. That's when I started liftin things, the wee telly off the flair, the boxes fae under the stairs, onythin that wisnae nailed doon went up higher.

By midmorning it wis at ma knee and I wis roarin at the weans tae get the messages aff the flair and ontae the worktop, never mind whit's in the poke, just get it up. Neighbour come chappin the door, drookit tae the bone.

Neighbour: Is yours in as bad, hen?

Me: Aye, it's gonnae be a stooshie the night, I can feel it.

It kept climbin efter that, didnae stop for naethin, past every level we'd raised stuff tae, we ran oot ae high tae put things on. By early efternoon it peaked, right up the wa, past the highest shelf in the place. Ma mother wis on the phone near greetin.

The mother: Is the hoose gonnae be alright, son?

Me: I cannae tell ye that, I'm just sittin here listenin tae it batter aff the walls.

Sat there for oors, listenin tae it slosh and knock, feart tae move in case it come in ower the windaes an aw. Naebody said much, just sat, peely-wally wi the cauld and the waitin.

Several oors efter it peaked it started tae go doon, dead slow, but goin doon.

The morra I went back doon and there it wis, the line right roond the wa where it had sat, plain as onything, higher than I'd ever have believed if I hudnae watched it climb there masel.
