Right, so ah says ah'd mind the hoose for the week while they were away, seven days, how hard can it be, ah'm thinking tae masel, water the plants, feed the cat, dinnae burn the place doon. Famous last words, so they were.

First night and ah'm no even settled, cases still by the door, and there's this smell aff the fridge that's no right. No manky exactly, just... aff. Ah'm standing there wi the door open at midnight gieing it a good sniff like that's gonnae tell me anything, and ah'm thinking, is that meant tae be like that or is that a sign of things tae come. Turns oot it was jist some cheese that had seen better days, but ah didnae sleep a wink for worrying the whole fridge was gonnae gie up the ghost on me and ah'd be explaining tae them how aw their messages went aff in a poke of guilt.

Second day and the cat's oot. Ah'd left the back door on the latch for two seconds, two seconds, tae bring the bins in, and that was it, away, over the fence like it had somewhere tae be. Ah'm oot in ma sliders in the street shouting pfft pfft like an eejit, the neighbour's leaning oot her windae:

The neighbour: You lost that cat, hen?

Me: Naw, naw, it's jist... exploring.

The neighbour: Aye, exploring. It's under my hedge.

Ma hert was in ma mooth the whole time, ah'm no gonnae lie.

Then there was the gairden. Then the boiler, or no the boiler exactly, the wee immersion thing, went and packed in on the Thursday, nae hot watter, and me trying tae work oot which switch was which in the cupboard under the stair, aw these labels worn aff wi age, and ah'm standing there wi a torch in ma teeth going, is it this yin, is it that yin, feeling like a right bampot. Had tae go a whole day washing wi watter boiled in the kettle like something oot the auld days, ma granny would've had a field day.

And in the middle of aw that, a visitor. Nae warning whatsoever, jist a chap at the door on the Wednesday teatime and it's some cousin or other of theirs, up frae doon south apparently, expecting a bed for the night because naebody had telt her they were away.

Visitor: Is she no in?

Me: Naw, sorry, they're away, ah'm jist minding the hoose.

Visitor: Away where?

Me: Ah don't actually know, they never said.

Then the Saturday, the scare. Ah couldnae find the spare set of keys they'd gied me for the shed, looked everywhere, turned the whole hoose upside doon, ma stomach away doon tae ma boots thinking ah've lost the man's keys, that's it, ah'll have tae get them cut, how am ah gonnae explain that yin, whit if there's something else on that ring ah dinnae even know aboot. Found them in ma ain jaicket pocket forty minutes later and near greeted wi the relief of it, honest tae god.

The Sunday was its ain disaster, locked masel oot the hoose for near three hours. Went oot tae put the bin oot, door shut ahint me, and it's yin of they doors that locks itsel, and there's me in the back gairden in ma socks wi nae phone, nae keys, nothing, for the best part of an efternoon, sitting on the back step like a right eejit waiting on somebody, onybody, tae come hame so ah could get in a windae or something. The neighbour: You again.

By the Thursday, next tae last day, ah finally sorted the hot watter, turned oot it wisnae the switch at aw, it was a wee reset button roond the back ah'd never even seen, and the second ah found that ah near danced roond the kitchen. Wan thing sorted, at least.

Then the Friday itsel was jist a frenzy, cleaning like a mad wumman, hoovering, dusting, trying tae make that bit of deid gairden look less deid by chucking a load of leaves ower it, scrubbing the sill where the rain had been, fluffing every cushion in the place tae within an inch of its life, so that by the time ah heard the taxi pull up ah was standing there in the hall like nothing at aw had happened aw week.

And when they came in, cases and aw, dead happy tae be hame, ah jist says tae them, aw fine, hen, no bother at aw, cat's grand, hoose is grand, quiet week.
