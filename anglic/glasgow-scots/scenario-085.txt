Three of them on the wall ootside the shops, messages in a poke aside each pair of feet, and it's no long afore the talk goes where it aye goes.

The first: Ma knees, hen, dinnae even ask. Ten year this has been gaun on, mibbe mair. Cannae get up oot a chair withoot a groan comin oot me like an auld door. Every mornin it's like somebody's poured gravel in the joints owernight.

The second: Gravel. Ha. That's nothin tae whit ah huv. Ma back's that bad three, fower nights every single month ah'm up walkin the flair at three in the mornin, cannae lie on ma side, cannae lie on ma back, cannae dae nothin but sit in the chair and greet wi the pain of it. Peely-wally aw the next day and aw, so ah am, good for nothin.

The third: Youse two dinnae know yese're born, so yese don't. Ah've had this hip since afore either o yese even kent me, since ah wis a lassie oot in the rain gettin drookit tae the bone. That's a langer complaint than the baith o yese put thegither.

The first: Aw here we go. Right, come on then, who's got it worst the day, oot the three o us, eh? Naebody's sayin.

The second: It's me, obviously. Ye've no had a night's sleep like the nights ah've had.

The third: Away and bile yer heid, it's me, ah've had mines langer than yer maw's had you.

The first: Ah'm no backin doon either, these knees are murder.

The second: Whit a stooshie owre naethin.

The third: The pair o yese are a right couple o eejits.

And there they sit, nane o them gonnae give an inch, the three o them laughin fit tae burst till the tears is comin doon.
