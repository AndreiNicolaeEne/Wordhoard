Me: Right, hen, I'm tellin ye the noo, that stall wi the rolls and square sausage, that's the best in this whole market, nae contest.

The pal: Away and bile yer heid. That yin's pure mince. It's the wan doon the end wi the mac and cheese ye want, so it is. The best in Glesga, that.

Me: Mac and cheese fae a burd that cannae even melt the cheese right? Away ye go. Ah had a poke o their chips wan time and they were that drookit in grease ah couldnae eat hauf o them. Pure boak.

The pal: Aye well at least ma stall disnae gie ye a roll that's gone cauld by the time ye've paid. Yours is aw fur show, wee squint rolls stacked up lookin gallus and then they're peely-wally inside, nae flavour at all.

Me: Peely-wally? Ah'll tell ye whit's peely-wally, your face when ye tasted that curry sauce last week and near boaked it back up in front the weans. Ye were crabbit for the rest the day, don't think ah didnae notice.

The pal: That's cause the sauce wis aff, no cause it wisnae nice. Ye're a right bampot if ye think that yin sausage roll is better than a whole plate o comfort like whit ah get.

Me: Comfort? It's gied me the runs twice noo. Honestly it's that bad it should be shut doon by the cooncil, they should be tellt tae stoap servin food tae the public, that's how bad it is.

The pal: Away, dinnae be sic an eejit, naebody's shuttin doon naethin. Whit aboot the wan wi the fish rolls though, naebody's got a bad word tae say aboot them, no even you.

Me: Aye, true enough, that yin's solid, canny fault it.

The pal: Right, so how aboot this, we go back the morra, baith stalls, yours and mine, and we settle it wance and for aw. Nae mair stooshie ower it.

Me: Aye, gonnae dae that. The morra it is, hen.
