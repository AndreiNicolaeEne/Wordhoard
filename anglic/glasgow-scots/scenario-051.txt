Me: Right, say the morra you win it, big yin, hunners a grand, whit's the first thing aff yer list.

My pal: Easy. New telly. Massive wan, takes up the whole wall, so it does.

Me: A telly? Ye've won a fortune and yer buyin' a telly like some peely-wally wee man that's never seen the sun. Naw. Ah'm gettin' a motor. A proper motor, no' this rust bucket ah'm drivin' the noo.

My pal: Aye, a motor that'll sit ootside gettin' drookit every day, dead sensible that.

Me: Better than a telly.

My pal: Naw it's no', we should be pittin' some away tae, no' blowin' the lot like a pair ae eejits.

Me: Away and pit some by, hen, whit are ye, ma granny? Fine, fine, we save a bit. But the rest ah'm spendin' gallus, no half measures.

My pal: Here's an idea for ye then, we buy the pub. Oor pub. Then it's free drink for us every night till we're auld and grey.

Me: Ye're a bampot, that's whit that is. Runnin' a pub, up at six wi' the deliveries, some crabbit auld eejit greetin' at ye ower a warm pint. That's no' a dream, that's a joab.

My pal: Aye, ye're right, forget that yin, that's glaikit.

Me: Right, here's the compromise. Motor for me, telly for you, wee bit put away safe, and a night oot for the both ae us tae celebrate, messages and a carry-oot, the works.

My pal: Deal. Dinnae tell the weans though, they'll be greetin' for a share.

Me: There's nae weans tae tell, it's just you and me sittin' here dreamin' up a lottery ticket neither ae us has bought.

My pal: ...Aw naw. It's still sittin' on the table frae Tuesday, intit.

Me: Dreich. Absolutely dreich.
