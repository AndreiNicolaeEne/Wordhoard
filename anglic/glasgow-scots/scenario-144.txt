The elder: Sit doon a meenit, hen. It's this job ye've packed in again, the third yin this year. See a rowin stane, it gathers nae moss, but it gets naewhere either. Stick in at whit ye stert, dinnae jump ship the meenit it's hard. Better a bad wage than nae wage. Ye're no unlucky, hen. Ye're feart o stickin at things, that's the hale o it.
