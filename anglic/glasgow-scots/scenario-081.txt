Cousin: Awright hen, whit's the story wi you? Ma maw said ye'd been badly since the weekend, ah've been worried sick ower here.

Me: Aye, it's been a right pain in the arse if ah'm honest. Started three days back, joints aching like ah'd ran a marathon and ma throat aw scratchy. Then yesterday the fever came on top of it, felt like ah'd been hit by a bus.

Cousin: Christ almighty. How ye the day though?

Me: The day's a wee bit better than yesterday, no gonnae lie, still feel peely-wally and ma heid's chocka, but at least ah'm no shivering under three duvets any mair.

Cousin: So whit huv ye been daein aboot it?

Me: Started aff wi a gargle, warm water and salt, for the throat, every couple hours or so. Fever widnae fully break itsel so ah've been pittin a cool cloth ower ma forehead, changin it through the night when it goes aw warm again.

Cousin: Ye eating awright at least?

Me: Just broth and weak tea, cannae stomach much else the now. Sleepin whenever the body lets us, which isnae aw that often if ah'm honest, ah'm up and doon like a fiddler's elbow aw night.

Cousin: Right well ye need tae actually rest hen, no just lie there crabbit wi yer phone oot, dinnae push through it, ye'll only drag it oot longer on yersel.

Me: Ah know, ah know. Ah'm no gaunny go daft aboot it. If the fever climbs any higher or ma breathing goes hard, ah'm away tae get seen, nae messing aboot, that's ma line. But no before that, it's just a rotten cauld or the flu daein the rounds the noo.

Cousin: Dae ye want us tae bring ye ower some messages, or ah could just come sit wi ye for an hour?

Me: Ah, ye don't huv tae dae aw that, hen, ye've enough on yer plate... but aye, actually, if ye're passing, a wee bit soup wouldnae go amiss. Don't fash yersel ower it though, ah'm no at death's door.
