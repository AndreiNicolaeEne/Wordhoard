Whole stoack room wis in a stooshie ower the delivery, boxes gaun tae the wrong shop and half the messages ended up doon at the ither branch in a poke naebody ordered. The gaffer come stormin through fae the office, face like a skelpt bahookie, and went right up tae the new start afore onybody else got a look in.

The gaffer: It wis you that logged they orders, wisnae it. Aw that stock's went tae the wrong place and ah've a van oot the noo chasin it doon the length ae the toon.

The new start: Wisnae me.

The gaffer: Aye it wis. You're the only wan that hud the sheet the day, so don't stand there and tell me it wisnae.

The new start: Wisnae me.

The gaffer: This has cost us hunners, ye ken that.

The new start: Ah swear tae God, ah'd nothin tae dae wi it.

No anither word oot her efter that. The twa that had actually filled in the sheet wrang kept their heids doon ower the boxes, sayin nothin, lettin it sit.

Her workmate: (under her breath, tae naebody) Leave it, hen.

The gaffer stood there a wee minute langer, waitin oan greetin or a flinch or onything at aw, but the new start's face never budged, peely-wally under the strip lights but stull as onything, gien him nane ae whit he wis efter.

The gaffer: Somebody's gonnae answer for this afore the morra, ah'm tellin ye the noo.

Naebody said a cheep. The new start lifted her coat aff the hook, turned, and walked oot intae the dreich wi the denial still sittin exactly where she'd left it, and no a soul rushin efter her tae say different.
