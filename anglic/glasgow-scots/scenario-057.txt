Tenant: Awright.

Landlord: Awright yersel, hen. Some dreich day, eh, ye near drookit comin up they stairs.

Tenant: Aye, it's been like that aw week. Wee bit of a walk fae the bus an aw.

Landlord: Sit yersel doon. Ye want a cuppa afore we start?

Tenant: Naw, ye're awright, I'll no keep ye long.

Landlord: Whit's on yer mind then?

Tenant: It's the rent. Been thinkin on it a good while noo, an I want tae ask ye tae bring it doon.

Landlord: Go on then.

Tenant: I've paid ye bang on time every month for three year runnin, never missed once, no even the month the wean was in the hospital. And that boiler's never worked right since the day I moved in. I've telt ye aboot it a dozen times.

Landlord: Aye, I mind ye sayin. It's on the list.

Tenant: It's been on the list a lang time noo. So I'm askin for a fifth aff whit I'm payin the noo.

Landlord: A fifth? Naw, I cannae dae that, hen. Everythin's gone up on my end an aw, I'm no made o money.

Tenant: I'm no askin for charity. I'm askin for whit's fair, considerin.

Landlord: I hear ye, I dae. But a fifth's too steep for me. I could manage aboot a tenth, mibbe.

Tenant: A tenth's barely onythin aff at aw.

Landlord: It's whit I can dae the noo, wi things the way they are.

Tenant: Whit aboot somewhere atween the two? Meet us hauf road.

Landlord: ...Let me think on it right, eh. I'm no sayin naw ootright, I'm just no sayin aye the day. Gie us a few days tae look at the numbers.

Tenant: Fair enough. I'll no chase ye up. Jist dinnae let it slide tae the morra an the morra efter that.

Landlord: I'll no. I'll gie ye a ring aboot it, promise.
