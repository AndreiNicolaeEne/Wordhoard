Gonnae aw haud yer wheesht. We're aw here the night for one reason - tae stand roond this wee table for my friend, hen. If wan thing's true, it's ye'd gie onybody the coat aff yer back and no say a word. Mind when the bus wisnae comin, us baith drookit, greetin wi the laughin. Here's tae ye - health and luck, and nae mair dreich days than ye need. Raise whit's in yer hand, youse aw, and get intae it.
