She's went missin three days noo, oor wee cat. Tabby, white paws, one ear torn frae a scrap wi a dug. Last seen doon the back lane by the bins - she didnae come hame that night. She's no feart o strangers, comes up gallus as ye like, so if she's turnt up at yours she'll no be shy. There's a wee reward for whoever finds her, hen. Number's on the tear-off strips below, gie a ring.
