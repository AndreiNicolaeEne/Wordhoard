Me: Right, youse wantin the whole story or just the good bits.

Mother: The whole of it, hen, sit doon and tell us.

Me: Aye awright. Was up pure early, couldnae sleep a wink, heid gaun like a washin machine aw night wi whit ifs. Oot the hoose before it was even light, drookit already fae the dreich walk doon tae the stop, guts in bits. Get in and there's this wee wummin on the desk, turns oot she's the one runs the whole flair, dead brisk, nae messin aboot, straight in wi "right you'll be shadowin for the morra, the day you're wi me." Gied us the first job strite away and honest tae god it wasnae hauf as bad as ah'd built it up tae be in ma heid, ah'd near made masel sick ower nothin.

Da: Telt ye. Aye buildin it up like a stooshie afore it even starts.

Me: Aye aye, ah know. Anyway made a right eejit ae masel aboot eleven, dropped a hale tray ae the wee bits right in front ae everybody, felt ma face gaun het, but naebody battered an eyelid, jist "happens tae us aw hen" and on ye go. Break time sat there sizin up the rest ae them, some ae them lookin at ye like ye're glaikit jist for bein new, others no botherin at aw. But there wis this yin, didnae huv tae say a word tae her, jist came ower and showed us the wee trick tae the machine naebody else bothered explainin, saved us a load ae grief.

Mother: That's the yin ye want, hen. Mind her.

Me: Aw day it wis faster than ah thought, nae time tae stand aboot bein crabbit or peely-wally, jist gaun and gaun, nine hours ae it, legs deid by the end, gaun hame on the bus wi it aw gaun roond ma heid ower and ower.

Verdict, honestly. Harder oan the feet than the heid. They're no bampots, mostly. Ah'll manage fine.
