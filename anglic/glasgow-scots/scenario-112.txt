Traveller: How much tae get us doon the toon, pal?
Driver: Twenty quid, hen, nae bother.
Traveller: Twenty? Away ye go, it's ten minutes doon the road, gie's it for twelve.
Driver: Twelve? Dinnae take the pish oot me, ah've tae eat an aw, that's affy cheap.
Traveller: Awright, fourteen then, dinnae be crabbit.
Driver: Split the difference, sixteen, an we'll caw it done.
Traveller: Aye, go on then, sixteen it is.
Driver: In ye get, hen.
