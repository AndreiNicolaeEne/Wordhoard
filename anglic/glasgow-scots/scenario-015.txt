I love you. That's it said straight oot, no messing aboot the edges of it the way I usually dae. I love you, an I've loved ye longer than I ever let masel admit.

D'ye mind that Tuesday, ages back noo, naethin special aboot it at aw. Ye were staunin at the sink wi the wee radio gaun an the rain stotting aff the windae, drookit fae walkin up wi the messages in a poke, moanin the milk had went aff again afore ye'd even got it hame. I wis in the doorway, kettle no even on yet, an somethin just went through me, no fireworks, no big occasion, just a wee quiet click in ma heid that said, aye, this is it, this is the wan. I never said a word tae ye. Just pit the kettle on an stood there wi this thing I'd nae name for yet, warm in ma chest the same as the tea.

Naebody else kens the way ye hum tae yersel when ye're nervous, some auld tune ye couldnae even name if I asked ye, ye don't even hear yersel dae it, but I dae, every time.

The days are different noo. Easier tae get through, even the dreich ones. An I'll say the other hauf plain an aw: the days we're apart huv got harder, no easier, the langer this goes on. I'm crabbit an glaikit the whole time, missin ye like a limb, gid for naethin till I see ye again.

Here's a worry I've never said oot loud tae a livin soul: sometimes I lie there feart I'll no be enough for ye, that ye'll wake up wan day an see somethin peely-wally an ordinary where ye used tae see somethin worth stayin for.

So here's ma promise, an I mean every word of it: whitever comes, stooshie or quiet, I'm no runnin, no ever.

I love ye somethin stupit, hen.
