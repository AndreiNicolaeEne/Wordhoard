The neighbor: Yer goat's oot, hen! Went like the clappers doon the lane, heidin straight fur the road!
The wife: Whit?! Ah never even heard the gate go -
The neighbor: Never mind that the noo, away roond and block the gap it got oot through, Ah'll cut across and heid it aff afore the road!
The wife: Right, Ah'm gaun! Haud that gap fur me!
The neighbor: Aye, hen, get a move on, it's gallus that yin, it'll be at the road afore ye know it!
