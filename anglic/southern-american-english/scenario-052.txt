Nine days left in the month and the counter's got twenty dollars laid out on it, two tens folded up, like it's fixin' to stretch itself if we just stare at it long enough.

Me: Nine days. Twenty dollars. That's about two dollars a day if we're doing it right.

Roommate: Lord. I reckon we're eating beans, then.

Me: We got that bag of pintos in the pantry and a little bit of ham hock left in the freezer, freezer's plumb empty other than that. Do a pot of beans and greens, that'll stretch four, five days easy.

Roommate: A mess of greens'll go a long way if we don't get greedy with it.

Me: Rice too. We ain't touching that rice till it's beans and rice, beans and rice, till it's about coming out our ears.

Roommate: I liketa forgot we still got that little bit of cornbread mix in the cabinet.

Me: Save it. We might need that directly, toward the end, when everything else is done.

Roommate: (long pause) I was fixin' to get my hair done Friday.

Me: With what money.

Roommate: I got twenty put back for it, separate.

Me: That twenty's got to go in the pot, not on your hair. We got nine days to eat off this.

Roommate: Don't be ugly about it, I said it's separate.

Me: It ain't separate no more, not when we're down to the wire like this. I ain't trying to start nothing, I'm just saying.

Roommate: Fine. Fine, I'll push it back a week, it ain't going nowhere.

Me: That's all I'm asking.

Roommate: What about the light bill, that due before the first?

Me: Due the twenty-second, but I already called and asked could they push it. They said they'd let it ride to the third 'fore they cut anything off.

Roommate: Well bless whoever answered that phone, that helps some.

Me: It does. So we got the whole twenty for food, straight through, and the light bill ain't due till after.

Roommate: Might could make it, then.

Me: We might could, long as nobody's showing out buying coke or nothing extra.

Roommate: I ain't buying nothing extra. Sweet tea and beans till the first, I promise you that.

Me: Good, 'cause twenty dollars for nine days is twenty dollars for nine days, and it ain't stretching no further.

Roommate: Lord willing and the creek don't rise, we'll make it.
