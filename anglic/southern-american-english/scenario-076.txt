Me: I got to talk to you and I need you to actually hear me out this time.

The friend: Here we go.

Me: I ain't playing with you. Last Tuesday when you laid that bike down coming off the ramp doing eighty, I liketa had a heart attack standing right there watching it happen. My hands wouldn't quit shaking for a good hour after, plumb shaking, and my heart was going like a jackhammer the whole time they was picking gravel outta your arm.

The friend: Wadn't nothing. I got up, didn't I. Little road rash, that's all that was.

Me: This time. Bless your heart, you always got a "this time." You done forgot what happened to your shoulder three years back, or you just don't want to remember it? That collarbone snapped clean in two, you couldn't lift your arm over your head for damn near a year, couldn't hardly hold your own young'un right for months. You used to could take a spill like that and just walk it off when you was twenty, but that ain't now.

The friend: That was different.

Me: It ain't different, it's the same fool thing, just fixin' to come out worse. Next time you might could end up in a chair the rest of your life, or you might not be nothing no more. You hearing what I'm telling you?

The friend: I hear you.

Me: Do you, though. 'Cause I'm the one that's gon' be standing at that funeral. Your mama's gon' be the one they got to hold up. Your young'uns gon' grow up with you in a picture on the wall 'stead of at the table. You reckon that's worth eighty mile an hour with no helmet on?

The friend: ...Naw. Naw, I don't reckon it is.

Me: So what you fixin' to do about it.

The friend: I'll slow down. Get the helmet back out, quit showing out on that ramp. I ain't promising I'm gon' be perfect about it, but I hear you, I do. I'll be more careful. I promise you that much.

Me: That's all I'm asking. Come here and gimme some sugar, fool, 'fore you go and scare me to death again.
