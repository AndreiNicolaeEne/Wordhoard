Traveler: Excuse me, sir, I'm fixin' to find the market, you know the way?

Stranger: Yessir, you take your first right up past the church yonder, then you'll see a barbershop on the corner, that's your second turn.

Traveler: How far is that, you reckon?

Stranger: Oh, 'bout fifteen minutes on foot, ain't far a'tall.

Traveler: Well I thank you kindly.

Stranger: Yes sir, y'all have a blessed day now, hear.
