The aunt: Hey now, it's me. Your mama said you been poorly. How bad is it really, baby, don't you sugarcoat it for me.

Me: Aw, I ain't gon lie to you, it's been a mess. Started a good three days back, just an ache all through my joints and my throat scratchy as all get out. Then yesterday a fever come on top of all that.

The aunt: Lord have mercy. And today?

Me: Today's a little easier than yesterday was, I'll give it that, but I ain't right yet, not by a long shot.

The aunt: Well what all you been doing for it, you fixin' to just lay there and suffer?

Me: No ma'am. First thing I done was gargle some warm salt water for my throat, done that a few times. And when the fever wouldn't break on its own I laid a cool rag 'cross my forehead, kept changing it out most the night.

The aunt: Bless your heart. You eating anything?

Me: Just broth and some weak tea, that's all my stomach wants right now. Sleeping whenever my body lets me, which ain't been much.

The aunt: Well you rest, you hear me, don't you dare try to push through this like you always do. I mean it.

Me: I hear you. But I ain't going nowhere unless that fever climbs higher or my breathing gets to where it's hard. Before that, I'm just gon ride it out here.

The aunt: I could bring you a mess of soup over, or come sit with you a spell.

Me: You don't have to do all that.

The aunt: I'm coming anyway, don't argue with me. Gimme some sugar when I get there.

Me: Alright, alright. Love you.
