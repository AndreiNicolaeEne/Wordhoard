We was standing right there between the two food stalls, plate line already backed up, and my friend started in on me again about who got the best food on that whole strip.

Me: Ain't even a discussion. Jerk chicken stall right here got the best food out here, plumb the best, hands down.

My friend: Girl, hush your mouth. Everybody and their kinfolk know the rib tips two stalls down got you beat six ways from Sunday. That man's sauce alone worth the trip.

Me: Rib tips? I liketa died last time I ate off there, gristle all through the meat and the man ain't never once mashed the button on that fan, sweatin' all in the food.

My friend: One bad plate and you fixin' to write the whole stall off? Bless your heart, you just mad you don't know good food when it's set right in front of you.

Me: I done told you twice now, that chicken stay marinated right and them plantains sweet as cake, not that dry mess y'all be servin' over yonder.

My friend: Dry? I might could eat over there every single day and never get tired of it, unlike your stall that done gave half this block the runs one Sunday. God willing and the creek don't rise, I ain't lyin' to you.

Me: That there is a bald-faced lie and you know it.

My friend: You're pitchin' a fit right now, madder than a wet hen.

Me: I ain't studyin' you. Don't be ugly.

My friend: Y'all always showing out when you're fixin' to lose an argument.

Me: We ain't finished yet. Now that tamale stall on the corner, neither one of us can rightly call that bad.

My friend: Can't argue with that. His tamales stay plumb perfect every time. Reckon that man could sell clean out by noon if he wanted to.

Me: Alright then. Directly we go on and get us a plate from both mine and yours again, settle this the proper way.

My friend: Fine by me. But you buying the coke this time.

Me: Cattywampus how you finesse a free drink out an argument you done lost.

My friend: I ain't lost a thing yet, sugar.
