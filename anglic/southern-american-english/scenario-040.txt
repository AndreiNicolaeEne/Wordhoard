Me: Y'all set on down, let me tell it right, 'cause it was a whole day.

I was up 'fore the sun even thought about it, stomach in a knot, fixin' to leave the house a good hour early 'cause I did not want to be the one walking in late lookin' a fool. Got out there and it was just me and the dark and the porch light, and I liketa turned around twice just from nerves alone.

Got there and the one runnin' things come out to meet me, real nice, "yes ma'am, no ma'am" the whole way through, showed me where to put my stuff. Handed me this first little task, said just get through that box and holler if I need anything, and I'm not gonna lie, I thought it'd be worse than it was. Went quicker'n I braced for.

Now I did mess up once, right in front of everybody too, mashed the wrong button on that machine and it beeped like the world was endin'. Face got hot as a stove eye. But wasn't nobody ugly about it.

Come break time I just sat back and sized up who was who. Some of 'em quiet, some showin' out a little, way people do the first day they got somebody new to perform for. But there was this one, didn't have to say a word to me, just leaned over and showed me the trick to it 'fore I even asked. Didn't make a thing of it neither.

Pace of it stayed steady the whole nine hours, never let up, never rushed me either, just kept on and kept on till it was done.

Drove home just replayin' every bit of it in my head, over and over, wonderin' did I do alright.

The mother: Well? How'd it go, for real?

Me: It went good. Long as it was, it went good. I reckon I might could get used to this.

The eldest: Told you. Bless your heart, you were worried over nothin'.

Me: Maybe so. Y'all pass that tea, I'm liketa starved.
