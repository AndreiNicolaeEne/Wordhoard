I had the new hand out back on the dock, must not been more than seven in the morning, cool enough yet you could feel it good. Didn't tell him nothing at first, just showed him, set them corner boards up under the strap, run it on through the ratchet, mash the handle down till it clicks about three times, snug and done. Then I stood back and let him have a go at it his own self.

He put them boards on backwards and cranked that ratchet down till the top case folded clean in on itself.

Me: Naw now. Boards go on the outside corners, strap holds 'em in place. You had it backwards, hoss.

He turned the boards around right, but before he even touched the ratchet he up and asked me...

The new hand: Which one's the ratchet again, this handle or the other one?

Me: The one I just showed you not a minute ago. I reckon you might could watch this time instead of studying your phone.

I run it through slow again so he could see it plain. He went at it again and this time cranked it so loose the whole load was already cattywampus before the truck even backed up to the door.

Me: Lord have mercy. I done told you twice now and you still ain't got it. That whole pallet liketa slid clean off before it ever left the dock. We got a truck rolling through here directly, we ain't got all day.

The new hand: I'm sorry, I thought you meant...

Me: Bless your heart, but sorry don't strap the pallet. I don't care what you thought, I care what your hands are doing right now. Come here.

I slowed it way down again, put my hand right over his on the ratchet.

Me: Feel that? Tight till it quits giving you nothing more. Not plumb loose, and don't go choking it to death neither. Right there, that's it.

Third go, he had it, strap snug, boards square, corners tight as they needed to be. I stood back and let him look at his own work.

Me: There you go now. Wasn't nothing to it once you quit thinking so hard about it and just felt it in your hands.
