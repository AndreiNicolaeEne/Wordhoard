The tenant: Evenin'. Hope I ain't catchin' you at a bad time.

The landlord: Not at all, come on outta that, it's fixin' to come up a cloud out there directly.

The tenant: Sure is. Sky's done gone that funny green color.

The landlord: You want some sweet tea? Just made a fresh pitcher.

The tenant: I wouldn't turn that down, thank you kindly. How's the family? Mama an' them doin' alright?

The landlord: Can't complain. Young'uns are 'bout to run me plumb crazy with school lettin' out, but that's how it goes.

The tenant: Lord, I remember them days.

The landlord: What can I do for you?

The tenant: Well, I wanted to set down and talk about the rent, if you got a minute. I been here goin' on four years now, and I don't believe I've ever once been late. Not one time.

The landlord: No ma'am, you ain't. I'll give you that.

The tenant: And I ain't tryin' to be ugly about it, but that back burner ain't worked right the whole time I been in there. I've made do, but it ain't right, and I reckon that oughta count for somethin' when we're talkin' numbers.

The landlord: I hear you, but I can't be droppin' the rent. Taxes alone like to eat me alive this year.

The tenant: I understand that. But I was hopin' we could knock a fifth off what I'm payin'. Seems fair, considerin'.

The landlord: A fifth's a mighty big bite. I could maybe come down about a tenth, seein' as you've been so good about payin'.

The tenant: I appreciate that, I surely do, I just don't know it gets me where I need to be.

The landlord: Tell you what, let me sit with it a night or two and I'll let you know directly. We'll land somewhere in the middle, I 'magine.

The tenant: That's all I'm askin'. God willing and the creek don't rise, we'll get it sorted.

The landlord: We surely will. You get on home 'fore that rain gets you.
