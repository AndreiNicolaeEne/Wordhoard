Guest: I got one for you. What's got a face and two hands and no legs a-tall, and it won't quit tickin'?
The eldest: That there's easy, that's the clock on the wall.
Guest: You got it.
The eldest: Alright, my turn. What's sweet as sugar, cold as ice, and settin' right there in the icebox fixin' to be poured?
Guest: Ain't nothin' but the sweet tea.
The eldest: Bless your heart, you done got both of mine. Let's go on and get us another'n.
