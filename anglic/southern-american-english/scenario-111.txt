He said he'd be here by eight, straight up eight, and now it's fixin' to be midnight and I'm doing figures in my head like some kind of a fool. Three hours over now. Then it's three and a half. First off I told myself, well, he's probably stopped by the store, mashed the button on that ATM, picked him up a coke for the road, y'all know how he does, got to be visiting with ever'body in the whole Piggly Wiggly parking lot like they's all kinfolk. I liketa believed it too, for a good while.

I get up and check that door again, and the window after it, and back to the door, like my feet got a mind of their own. Ain't no sense in it and I know it and I do it anyway.

And then it comes up in me before I can stop it, what if he's done wrapped that truck around something out on the highway, what if some fool run him off the road. I hate my mind goes yonder like that but it does, straight to the worst of it every time.

Puts me in mind of the time his daddy was late comin' in off a job, years back, and I sat up just like this, plumb sick with it, and it wasn't nothing but a flat tire in the end. Comfort in rememberin' that, and dread right alongside it, both together.

I study on goin' out lookin' for him myself, taking the car down the route he'd run. But no, not yet. He'll come on directly, I tell myself. I check the clock again. Past four hours now, sure enough.
