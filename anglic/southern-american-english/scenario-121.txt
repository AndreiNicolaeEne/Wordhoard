Me: Did you hear all that racket out in the street last night, sometime after midnight?

The neighbor: Lord, yes I did. Liketa scared me plumb out my skin, whatever it was.

Me: I reckon it was some animal knocking a can over, but I ain't rightly convinced on that.

The neighbor: Could be. Or coulda been some fool kids showing out. Awful cattywampus loud for just an animal.

Me: Well, if it happens again tonight, we best keep an ear out.

The neighbor: Yes sir, I sure will.
