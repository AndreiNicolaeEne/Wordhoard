Traveler: Sir, what you fixin' to charge me on over yonder?
Driver: Call it twenty and we good to go.
Traveler: Twenty? I ain't studyin' twenty, I got twelve on me.
Driver: Twelve! Bless your heart, that's plumb insultin', gas ain't free, ma'am.
Traveler: Well I reckon fourteen's fair, ain't it.
Driver: Make it sixteen, split the difference, and I might could get you there directly.
Traveler: Sixteen it is then.
Driver: Mash that door shut, we rollin'.
