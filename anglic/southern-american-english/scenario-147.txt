Grandmama, you gone on and left us, and I still cain't hardly say it plain. Lord, Lord, my heart's gone plumb cattywampus without you. I remember you cuttin' them lights on 'fore day, hummin' over a mess of greens like the world weren't ending. Gimme some sugar, I'd say, and you always did. Lord, Lord, I don't know how to go on. Rest easy now, sugar. We'll carry you on directly, one supper at a time.
