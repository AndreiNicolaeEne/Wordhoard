Me: I'm telling you, I seen him. Standing right up yonder in the middle of the road past the old church, plain as day.

My friend: Girl, you were prob'ly half asleep. Nodding off on that drive home, that's all it was.

Me: I was wide awake. I'd just been to the Piggly Wiggly for a mess of greens, buggy still sitting in the trunk, and I wasn't tired one bit. I was thinking clear as a bell the whole way, and I mashed that brake so hard I liketa run off in the ditch.

My friend: Okay then, what'd you see exactly.

Me: A man. Standing cattywampus right in the middle of the road, in some long white coat, just standing there not moving. Soon as my lights hit good he wasn't there no more.

My friend: Bless your heart, that was a deer. Their eyes catch the light and shine like that, it'll scare you plumb to death.

Me: Wasn't no deer. Deer don't stand up on two legs wearing a coat. There wasn't no eyes shining neither, no shine at all, just him standing there and then gone.

My friend: Might could've been fog then, coming up off the field, playing tricks on you.

Me: Sky was clear as glass, not a cloud coming up nowhere that whole night. I done told you twice now. I know what I seen with my own eyes.

My friend: I reckon your mind was just playing games with you out there by yourself.

Me: Don't be ugly. I ain't imagining nothing and I ain't showing out for attention neither. I'm getting madder than a wet hen the way you keep on.

My friend: Quit pitching a fit, I'm just saying.

Me: I swear on my mama's grave, every word of that is God's truth. I seen him standing right there.

My friend: I hear you, but I still ain't buying one word of it.
