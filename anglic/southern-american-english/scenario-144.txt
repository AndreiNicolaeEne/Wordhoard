The eldest: Baby, you done quit that job mad again, fixin' to do the same at the next one. Two dogs the same size, one'll fight, one'll run; it ain't the size, it's what's in him. So mash that temper down 'fore you open your mouth. Anger cooks fast and eats slow. I ain't prettyin' it up no more: keep quittin' on your pride, you'll be plumb out of work, and ain't nobody but you to feed them young'uns.
