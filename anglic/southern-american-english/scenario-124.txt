I left later than I meant to, and I knew it soon as I stepped out, cause it was already darker than I like it to be, that kind of dark that comes up on you quick this time of year, like it's coming up a cloud even when the sky's clear. Wasn't but a block or two in and I got that feeling, you know the one, somebody behind me, feet landing about the same time as mine. I didn't turn around, I wasn't fixin' to give anybody the satisfaction, but I slowed my walk down some, just to see, and directly them steps slowed too. So I sped back up, and they matched that an' all. I told myself it wasn't nothing, told myself twice, but my mind was done made up before my feet were.

I cut off my usual shortcut right there, the little cattywampus path between the buildings that ain't lit worth a dime, and went the long way round by the busier street where the lights stay on and there's people out. I let myself look back one time, one good hard look, and it told me plumb nothing, just shapes and shadows, coulda been anybody or nobody at all. Didn't ease my mind a bit.

That last stretch home I walked faster than I'd ever own up to, faster than I like to admit even now, near about running by the porch steps. Got that door shut and locked behind me and stood there a second just breathing, relieved plumb through.

Never did find out what it was. Might could've been nothing, might could've been somebody walking the same direction is all, I reckon that's probably it. But I still don't rightly know. I tell it now and folks laugh a little, and I laugh too, bless my own heart, cause I sound half foolish telling how sure I was right then that something was coming up behind me in the dark.
