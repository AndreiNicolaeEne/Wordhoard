Friend: Well look at y'all. How's everybody doin' in here?

Mother: Oh, we doin' fine, we blessed. Just adjustin', you know how it is.

Friend: I bet. Baby sleepin' good for you?

Mother: (laughs) Bless your heart for even askin' that like it's a real question. Naw, I'm gon' be honest with you since it's just us, we ain't sleepin' at all hardly. Up four, five times a night, easy. Three hours in a stretch if the Lord is good to us, and most nights He ain't.

Daddy: And that's on a good night. My back is plumb done, I mean tore up, from rockin' in that chair half the dark hours. Look at my hands, they done started shakin' on they own, I ain't even doin' nothin' and they shakin'.

Friend: Aw, no. Y'all poor things, I mean it. That'll wear a person down to nothin'.

Mother: It will. I liketa died yesterday, I'll tell you that. I went and put the car keys in the diaper bag and fixed to hunt for 'em a good twenty minutes, and the whole time I had the buggy half loaded at the Piggly Wiggly and folks lookin' at me cattywampus.

Daddy: She put a coke in the pantry too, forgot it wadn't supposed to go with the cans.

Friend: Y'all don't be ugly to yourselves over it, that's just tired doin' what tired does. Look here, gimme that baby a couple hours this evenin', I'll mind the young'un so you two can lay down some, and I'm fixin' to bring supper by directly too, a mess of greens and some chicken, so don't nobody worry about cookin'.

Mother: We couldn't ask you to...

Friend: You ain't askin', I'm tellin'. Go on and rest, ma'am, 'fore you both fall out.

Daddy: ...Alright. Reckon we could use it.

Mother: Lord, thank you. Gimme some sugar 'fore you go.
