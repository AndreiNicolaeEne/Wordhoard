Stranger: 'Scuse me, sir, you reckon I could get a little water? I'm plumb wore out.

The one at the counter: Hush now, here go a whole cup, ain't no cause to be stingy.

Stranger: I didn't need but a swaller.

The one at the counter: Don't be ugly, drink it up.

Stranger: Well I thank you kindly, sir.
