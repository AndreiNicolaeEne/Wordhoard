The first friend come by around suppertime, didn't even knock good, just come on in and set down at the table like she does. I poured her some sweet tea and let her go.

The first friend: I ain't even fixin to make this a whole thing, but she done went and told everybody at the shop I said something I never said. I ain't studying her, I swear 'fore God.

Me: Mm. Well. I hear you.

The first friend: I'm just saying. She the one showing out, not me.

I didn't say nothing back one way or the other, just let her talk herself out, and directly she left saying she had to get on home to the young'uns.

Wasn't twenty minutes later the second friend showed up, cattywampus as anything, looking like she'd been crying or fixin to.

The second friend: I know she's been over here. I know what she said. But I ain't start it, I don't care what she told you.

Me: Alright now, don't be ugly about it, either one of y'all. Let me ask you something plain. When did this start, what was the first thing, before all the rest of it got piled on?

The second friend: ...She quit coming to my mama's for Sunday supper. That's when it started. Before any of the talking.

Me: You sure that's it? Not the other stuff?

The second friend: I reckon that's really it, if I'm honest. The rest is just what come after.

Me: Bless your heart, both y'all done let this get plumb out of hand over a Sunday dinner.

The second friend: ...Maybe I coulda handled it better. I ain't saying I didn't.

Me: Well. How 'bout this, y'all just talk to each other, straight, none of this coming to me separate. I'll set it up, have y'all both over, make a mess of greens, and we'll get it worked out before it comes up a cloud on all of us.
