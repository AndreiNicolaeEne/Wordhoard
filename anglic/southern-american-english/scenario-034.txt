The regular comes up on the stall same as always, and the owner's already got the tongs in hand before she even says a word.

The owner: There she is. Two hot links, mustard not mayo, and a coke, that right?

The regular: You know it. But gimme a bag of the pork skins today too, I'm fixin' to take some home for the young'uns.

The owner: Ma'am, I got you. You want that coke a orange or a Sprite, we out of the reg'lar again.

The regular: Orange fine. Y'all need to get on that man that runs your delivery, he done let y'all down twice this month.

The owner: Don't I know it. Between him and the weather it's been cattywampus round here lately. Business been alright though, can't complain too much, praise God. Wednesday it was slow as molasses, looked like it might come up a cloud all day and folks just stayed in.

The regular: Well I'm here ain't I. Three, four times a week, rain or shine.

The owner: Girl you might could just move your bed back here. How's your mama an' them?

The regular: Mama doin' fine, still bad about her knee. My oldest been showing out at school though, pitching a fit over his homework every single night. I liketa lost my mind with him Tuesday.

The owner: Bless his heart. Bless yours too, dealing with it. You tell him Miss so-and-so at the stall said he better cut it out or I'm cutting him off the free pork skins.

The regular: I'll tell him, he'll straighten up right quick then, he loves them things plumb to death.

The owner: Here you go now, careful it's hot. That's eight fifty.

The regular: Here, keep the change, don't be ugly about it.

The owner: Well ain't you sweet. Alright now, you tell your kinfolk hey, and I'll see you directly, I reckon.

The regular: You know you will. Bye now.
