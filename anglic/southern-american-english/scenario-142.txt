Woke up this morning and it hit me before my eyes was even good open, today's the day I done swore on. Told mama an' them back in the spring, plain as day, that I'd give up a whole Saturday and clear out that shed once and for all, no matter what, and I meant it loud enough that I can't go back on it now. Laid there a good five minutes just cussing myself for ever opening my mouth. Ain't one part of me wanted to get up.

Got up anyway. Muttering the whole time I was dressing, fussing at myself under my breath about being fixin' to spend my one day off wrestling spiders and rusted-out lawn chairs. Drove over there grumbling the whole way, telling the steering wheel I might could've kept my word some other way that didn't involve a hundred degrees in a tin shed.

Started in on it round nine and found something to gripe about at every turn, the length of it, Lord, that shed is deeper than it looks; the timing of it, middle of July with the sky already coming up a cloud like it wanted to rain on me out of spite. My own bad judgment, mostly, swearing to a thing like that with my mouth open before my brain caught up.

Round one o'clock, dragging out a box plumb full of nothing but old jars, I thought about just walking off and calling it a mess of nothing worth my time. But I stopped myself right there. I swore I would, and that was the end of that thought, didn't let it go no further.

Gritted through the worst of it, the back corner where the wasps had built up, sweating and cussing but not stopping. By supper time I had that shed cleared to the bare dirt, swept out and squared away, exactly like I said I would.

Sat down after with a big glass of sweet tea, still grumbling a little just for the principle of it, but it was done. Done right. Done like I swore.
