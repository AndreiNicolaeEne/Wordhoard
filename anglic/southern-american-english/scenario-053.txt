So I had this title paper had to get signed over at the courthouse, the one for the old truck, on account of I had a man waiting on it that same day, cash money in his hand, fixin' to drive it off soon as I got back to the house. I mashed the button for the elevator and went on up right after dinner, and the one at the counter takes his sweet time looking it over, flipping pages he ain't even reading, one raggedy fan behind him blowing cattywampus circles in the heat.

The one at the counter: You know sometimes these things move along faster when a body's willing to help it along some.

I just looked at him like I ain't caught his drift, on account of he never said it outright.

Me: Move along how, sir?

The one at the counter: Depends on what all a person's willing to do for it, is all I'm saying.

I let that hang there a good while, didn't say nothing, just stood there looking innocent as Sunday, and directly he come back to it a little plainer.

The one at the counter: A little something extra tends to get a stamp done same day 'stead of you coming back around.

I told him, right polite, that I didn't reckon there was any rule said it had to cost extra, and I might could just wait my turn same as everybody, and besides, I had kinfolk of mine used to work in that very office and I might should just give him a call, see could he walk it through directly, no trouble to nobody. Said it easy, no ugliness in it, just laid it out plain.

It got right quiet a minute, him looking at me, me looking at him, and I liketa died standing there thinking I was fixin' to sit through the whole evening and come back another day for nothing. But he just stamped the thing, slid it back over, and told me have a blessed day.

Didn't pay him one red cent. And I'll tell you, some of these folks think a desk gives 'em leave to lean on plain people just trying to get their business done, and that ain't right, not one bit.
