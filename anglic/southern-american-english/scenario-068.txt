Now listen here, I ain't tryin' to scare you none, but you're new to Memphis and I done seen this happen more times than I care to count, so I'm fixin' to tell you plain and you gone want to hear it, 'specially if you got young'uns with you.

There's a fella runs around this part of town, always got a deal goin', always smilin', callin' everybody 'friend' 'fore he even know your name. Real friendly type, the kind that'll ask about your mama an' them like he known you all your life. He hangs round yonder by the corner store, sometimes right where folks pushin' the buggy out the Piggly Wiggly, and he waitin' on somebody fresh off the truck, somebody ain't got their feet under 'em yet.

He gone tell you he got a house, a real nice one, rent so low it plumb don't make sense, and it's ready right now, no waitin'. All he need is the deposit up front, cash, today, 'fore somebody else snatch it up. And ma'am, sir, hear me good: you hand that man one dollar, you might could kiss it goodbye, 'cause him and your money both gone be gone directly, and you ain't never gone see either one again.

My kinfolk's boy fell right into it his first week here, paid the man near eight hundred dollar, and come to find the house wasn't even his to rent. I liketa died when I heard how much he lost. Bless his heart, he was madder than a wet hen, and it cost him plumb near his whole first check.

Signs was there the whole time, if he'd knowed to look, papers all cattywampus, nothin' signed right, wouldn't show the place in daylight, wanted cash only and wanted it fast. Anybody rushin' you like that, reckon you already got your answer.

So if one of these comes 'round askin' for money 'fore you seen a thing with your own eyes, don't you be givin' him nothin'. Y'all go on and ask the old man at the corner store first, he know everybody round here, and he'll tell you straight who's real and who ain't.
