The borrower: Hey now. You get that buggy fixed yet, the one cattywampus on the one wheel?

The friend: Naw, still draggin' it around like a fool. What's goin' on with you, you look like you got somethin' on your mind.

The borrower: Ain't nothin'. Well. Hot, ain't it. Lookin' like it's fixin' to come up a cloud out yonder directly.

The friend: Mm hm. You didn't call me up to talk about the weather.

The borrower: Naw I reckon I didn't. Look, I hate to even ask you this, I do, but could you maybe let me hold two hundred dollars till Friday?

The friend: Two hundred. What's it for?

The borrower: Just, you know, the lights got cut off and I'm a little behind is all, ain't nothin' bad, just got behind is all, mama an' them been on me about it but I told 'em I had it handled.

The friend: Behind how bad? You ain't got the light bill AND the buggy note both, do you.

The borrower: ...I mean. It ain't as bad as it sounds.

The friend: Lord. Alright, alright, I'll let you hold it, but I mash the button on my card so I need it back in my account, not cash, and I need it back by the fifteenth, not "directly," the fifteenth.

The borrower: I'll have it to you the fifteenth, I swear 'fore God, first thing that check hit I'm sendin' it straight to you, I done already got it worked out in my head.

The friend: Better had. I ain't tryna be madder than a wet hen come the sixteenth.

The borrower: You a real one, you know that? I mean it, I ain't never gon' forget this, you the best friend a person could...

The friend: Alright now, don't be ugly showin' out over two hundred dollars, you fixin' to make me cry in this Piggly Wiggly parking lot.

The borrower: Ha! Bless your heart, gimme some sugar then 'fore I go on home.
