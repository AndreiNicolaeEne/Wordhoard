Son: Mama, can I please get that new phone? Please. Everybody at school done got one but me.

Mother: Baby, I done told you twice already this week, we ain't got it right now. Rent due Friday and the light bill sitting right there on the counter too. Ain't no way round it.

Son: I could do more chores for it. I'll take the trash out every single night and cut the grass without you even asking.

Mother: I know you a good hand round here and I love you for offering, but this ain't about chores, sugar. It's about what's in the account, and right now that's plumb near nothing.

Son: What about for my birthday then? Could I get it then?

Mother: If your daddy's hours pick back up and we get caught up on these bills, and you keeping them grades right, I might could swing it by your birthday. I ain't promising you nothing, but I ain't saying no neither.

Son: But cousin already got one, and his mama don't even work but part time!

Mother: What go on in that house ain't got one thing to do with what go on in this one. I don't know what all they got going, what they owe, or who helped them get it. You worry about your own house, you hear?

Son: Yes ma'am.

Mother: And don't be pitching a fit about it neither, don't be ugly with me. Tell you what, we'll go on and get you them little earbuds you been eyeing, the good ones, not that cheap mess off the clearance rack.

Son: ...I reckon that's alright, for now.

Mother: Come here and gimme some sugar then, and quit looking like it's fixin' to come up a cloud right here on my kitchen floor.
