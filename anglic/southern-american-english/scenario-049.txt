We was out on the porch after supper, glass of sweet tea sweating on the rail, sky off toward the river coming up a cloud like it might turn on us directly. I'd been sitting on this a good while and figured I better just say it plain before I lost my nerve.

Me: I ain't tryin' to be ugly about it, but that hundred and forty you borrowed off me back in March, that's going on five months now.

My friend: Man, I know. I know. Things just been tight is all, I'm fixin' to get paid Friday, I'll square you up then.

Me: You told me that back in June too. Said the fifteenth plain as day, had it wrote down and everything. Fifteenth come and went and I ain't heard nothing out of you since.

My friend: I ain't been dodging you. Car note hit, and mama an' them needed help with the light bill on top of it. You know how it is.

Me: I do know how it is, I got my own bills same as you. I done asked about this three, four times now and it's a different excuse every time. That's what's got me right put out, if I'm honest.

My friend: Alright, dang, you ain't got to come at me like that. I said I'm good for it.

Me: I ain't coming at you. A man might could forget once. Three times it starts looking cattywampus, like something else altogether.

My friend: So what, I'm a liar now? After all we been through, you gon' sit there and say that to me?

Me: I ain't said liar. I'm just saying owed is owed, and I'm plumb tired of asking.

My friend sat quiet a minute, jaw tight, then dug in his pocket and come up with two twenties.

My friend: Here. That's forty toward it. Rest of it Friday, I liketa swear it on my kinfolk, God willing and the creek don't rise.

Me: Bless your heart. You said Friday before too. Reckon I'll believe it when it's sitting in my hand.
