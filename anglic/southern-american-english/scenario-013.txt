Me: Y'all, my young'un done went and read that whole book, cover to cover, her own self, wadn't nobody standin' over her.
Friend: The one she kept fixin' to quit on?
Me: That very one. Liketa cried in the kitchen. This time last year she couldn't hardly get past a page 'fore she'd pitch a fit and put it down for good.
Friend: Bless her heart.
Me: Plumb proud, and I ain't holdin' back sayin' so. She come a long way, that young'un.
