The friend: Girl, I got to tell you something 'fore I bust wide open.

Me: What in the world, you look like you seen Jesus.

The friend: I hit some money. Real money. I ain't even playing with you.

Me: Oh my Lord, that is just wonderful, that is so wonderful for you, look at you!

The friend: I'm still shaking.

Me: Well how much we talking, don't leave me hanging.

The friend: Little over nine thousand dollars. Nine thousand and change.

Me: Lord have mercy. So how'd that come about, exactly, 'cause last I heard you was scraping same as the rest of us.

The friend: Long story, but it all worked out.

Me: Must be nice. Some folks work theirselves plumb to the bone and don't never see nothing like that, and it just falls in your lap. I done put in overtime three weekends running and I'm still short on the light bill.

The friend: Hold on now. Something wrong? You sound cattywampus all the sudden.

Me: No, ain't nothing wrong.

The friend: Naw, say it. You been short with me since I told you.

Me: ...Okay. Fine. It's envy, plain and simple, and I'm 'shamed of it, but that's the truth of it. Seeing you catch that kind of blessing when I'm over here fixin' to be evicted, it done something to me for a minute there.

The friend: I hear you. I'd be lying if I said I wouldn't feel some type of way too, it was the other way round.

Me: I reckon you would.

The friend: You still my people though.

Me: I know it. And I mean this now, for real this time. I am happy for you. Plumb happy. God's been good to you and I ain't gon' stand here sour about it. Come here, gimme some sugar, and let's go get us a coke to celebrate 'fore this cloud coming up yonder turns to rain.
