Neighbor: Whew, hot today, ain't it, ma'am.
The elder: It is, hon, hot as Tuesday, and Tuesday liketa killed me.
Neighbor: Mm-hm, sure did.
The elder: Reckon it puts me in mind of a spell back in the seventies, three weeks hot and dry, grass gone plumb brown, mama an' them fixin' to haul water for the garden.
Neighbor: Lord.
The elder: Reckon tomorrow'll bring more the same, maybe coming up a cloud round supper.
Neighbor: Might could.
