"Lauerd," he said, "wat es me best,
Mi hert bes neuer broght in rest,
Bituixand þis iacob be slan,
If he mai oþer be ouertan."
þis esau wit his manace
Oute o þe land did iacob chace;
Quen þe moder sagh it was sua
He soght his broþer for to sla,
Sco send him son in-til aran,
Til hir broþer þat hight laban,
þar-to suiorn for hir sake,
Til þat his broþer wreth suld slake.
Be night flum iordan he wode
And thoru a wildernes he yode,
He loked war þat him war best,
And be þe wai he tok his rest.
He hent a stan he fand him bi,
And þar-on laid his hefd to li.
In slepe he sagh stand vp a sti,
Fra his heued right to þe ski,
Apon þe sti þat þar was bun
Angls climand vp and dun.
Open him thoght þe ȝate of heuen,
And herd o drightin suilk a steuen:
