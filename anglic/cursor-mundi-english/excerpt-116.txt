Saint iohn hir keped & had ful dere,
For he was hir a treu fere,
Ne wald he neuer fra hir gan,
For bath þair willes war als an;
Al þat scho badd gladli he did,
To-quils þai lenged in þat sted.
 Quen scho had tare wellang bene,
þe leuedi, þat es heuen quene,
Hir langed sare hir sun cum to,
And quen scho gernd son was scho.
An angel sent he hir fra heuen,
And gret hir wit a mild steuen;
In þe temple wit her he met,
Anurd hir and tar hir grette,
And said, "leuedi! sua ful grace.
Wel be þe euer in ilk place!
Be þou noght dred for me her,
Ic am þi sun messager,
Fra him i com, to þe i com;
þe gretes wel þi dere sun,
Flour of erth, of heuen quene,
Blisced mot þou euer bene;
Wel be þe time þat þou was born.
For al þis werld it war for-lorn
If þou ne war, and te frut o þe,
Maria leuedi, godd wit þe be.
Leuedi! best of all thing,
Blisced bodes i þe bring,
Tak þis palme her in þi hand,
It es þi dir sun saand;
þe thinc wellang þi sun to se,
Her mon tu noght lang be,
He sal send efter þe wel son,
Ne sal tu nawjght lang her hon.
