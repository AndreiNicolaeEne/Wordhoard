First al o þat angel sight
And siþen o bodword broght fra dright,
"For þis meteing þat i of mett
I did," he said, "þe for to fett."
 Petre opend þan his muth,
"I se," he said, "þat it es cuth
þat godd, þat mad for us ranscun,
Bihaldes noght mans persun,
Ne he ne be-halds queþer it be
Iuen or haiþen, thral or fre,
Bot all folk elles þat him dredis,
Wit luue vntil his lau he bringes.
Ne hope o nan he wil for-hu
þat wil traistili in him tru."
Petre þam said o spelling þan
O baptising þat iohn bigan,
þat was o penance cald baptime,
And siþen þaim tald o crists time.
Hu he him-self þat ilk wise,
For vr ensample did baptise.
Siþen of his lijf he wit þam ledd,
Hu him on rode þe Iuus spredd,
And hu he rais þe thrid dai,
And þar-of wittenes lel ber þai.
"And spak wit us in flexs and fell,
And badd us weind his word to spell,
His fader, þat all has for to yeme,
Has taght him quick and ded to deme.
Noght we allan of his afers,
Bot ilk prophet him wittnes beres.
All to be sauf o sin and scam,
þat wald tru in his hali nam."
Quils þat petre spak þis spell,
þe haligast dun on þam fell;
