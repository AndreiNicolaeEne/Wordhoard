"I wat," he said, "þat þou art clene,
War it not sua if i ne had bene,
Fra toche of hir i saued þe,
þat þou suld not sin in me;
Yeild hir to hir husband schet,
For hir husband es a prophet;
And if þou dos anoþer red
þou and þine all sal be dede."
 Vp ras þe king, on nighter tale,
And cald his men be for him hale;
O þis warning he þam tald,
And þai þam dred both yong and ald.
He did to cal habraham son,
And said, "qui has þou þusgat don?
Quat haue i don, or mi kingrike,
þat þou suld þus us be-suik?"
"Parfai for i me vm-bithoght
Yee war men þat godd duted noght;
Your harm sa wend i best to fle
And said mi sister suld sco be."
Til abraham þan gaue þe king,
In wirscipp, mani riche thing,
And him he made þan his priue
And taght to weld al þat contre.
 Bi þis come sarra to þe tide,
O birth sco moght not ouerbide;
þan ysaac þair sun was born,
þat lang was hight to þam biforn;
Was circumcised þe aghtand dai,
Als was þair heing in þair lay.
