And al þe knau barns þat þai fand
Wit-outen grith þai suld þam sla,
þe maiden barns þai suld lat ga.
þis midwimmen for godd was radd
And did noght als þe king þam badd,
Bot sauued þai þar childer liues.
þan did þe king call þaa midwiues
O quilk þar was hatten tua,
þat phua hight, and sephora;
To þir tua wimmen spak þe king—
"Quar-for did yee noght mi biding
Wit childir of þe hebru lai?"
"Sir, for þis resun," þan said þai,
"þair wimmen, yee sal vnderstand,
Er noght lik wimmen o þis land,
For ilkan þam-seluen stere
Quen þai cum to þat ilk mistere;
Ar þat we cum to þam might
þai are lighter bi þair aun slight."
And for wimmen did sua wele
Drightin þam sent bath happ and sele.
 þan comanded eft king pharaon,
þat wald þat folk war for-don,
Ouer all his king-rik, her and þar,
Quen wimmen ani knaubarn bar,
þat o þe kind of hebru war,
Men suld it in þe flum for-far.
Quat! he was brem wit-in his breth
To þat lede him did na leth!
Bot for noght wend he smor þe sede
þat godd seluen of wald bredd,
For na man mai, for nankins chance,
For-do þat lauerds purueance;
