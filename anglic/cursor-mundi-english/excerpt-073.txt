Quen þe sede was dight and boun
He salde ilkman his porcion,
Sua þat þai moght scilwisli,
þai and þair fernet, liue þar-bi.
In sum bok find i þar a wile
þat ioseph fand þat was sutile;
þe caf he cast o corn sumquile
In þe flum þat hait þe nile;
For-qui þat flum þat rennes þar
Til ioseph hus it has þe fare.
 þis hunger, þat i her of tell,
In landes als a-bute it fell,
Men mist it nour quare in land,
And seuen yeir it was lastand.
þan iacob and his suns warn
For defaut wel ner for-farn;
Soruful war þai, was na selcuth,
þat had noght to put in þair mouth;
þof þai had siluer and gold red
þai moght noght find to bi þam bred;
His childer oft for hunger grete,
O bote he ne wist how þam to bete;
Oft he liftud vp his hend
To godd, þat he helpe þam wald send,
And ar grant þam son menskli to dei,
Ar þat misese lang for to drei.
Bot our lauerd godd o might
Hers mans praiyer þat es right;
For þof he proue his freind wit pine,
þar-for wil he noght him tine.
