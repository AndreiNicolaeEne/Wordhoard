Me: Tittuh, if we bin come on some money, out the blue, wha oona think we should do fust?

My tittuh: Fust thing, I da buy me one boat. You shum the sign by the fish house, say big boat fa sale, cheap cheap, motor strong nuff fa outrun game warden own self.

Me: (cut-eye) Gal, where you gwine keep no boat, and who gwine drive e when you cyan even swim good? Best we tink bout something sensible, like that roof e done leak since last hurricane.

My tittuh: Roof can wait, e ain going nowhere. Money like this ain come but once, I wan enjoy e sweet-mout fo e gone.

Me: Enty you done talk so when the church raffle money come? Where that money now?

My tittuh: (laugh) Gone. Gone like cootuh slide off e log, quick quick, ain nobody shum go.

Me: See there. That how come I say put some by fust, then play after.

My tittuh: Put by how much?

Me: Half. Half we save, half we spend how we please.

My tittuh: Awright, half. But wid my half I gwine buy up a whole field, plant e in benne, sell benne candy to every buckra come through here till I rich rich, richer than the man own the bank.

Me: (laugh) Whole field a benne? Where you gwine plant all that, out in the marsh wid the cootuh dem?

My tittuh: Don't cut-eye me now, I done yeddy bout people make money off less.

Me: I yeddy you too, but you talking day clean fo you even done pick out one seed. Le we just say new roof, little bit put by, and maybe, maybe, one small boat.

My tittuh: One small boat.

Me: One small boat, not the big one wid the motor.

My tittuh: (laugh) Look like we done spend money we ain even bin have yet.

Me: (laugh) True, true. Le we go in now, e getting dark, mosquito da come fa we.
