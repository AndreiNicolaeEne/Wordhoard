Oona yeddy me now, cause we all gather roun dis table fa de one done stood by me since day clean, an nebbah once bin trouble. Tittuh, oona heart sweeter den benne candy, no sweet-mout bout it, jus true. Member dat cootuh we chase down to de creek, enty? God bless oona wid long life an good health. Now oona raise wa in oona hand an drink wid me.
