Tittuh, leh me tell you bout dis night, cause you know um same like me and you gwine laugh and shake ya head same time.

By de end a de night e bin gone, I mean gone fa true. Not just tipsy, not just talkin loud, e bin da lean pon de wall like de wall de one holdin up de whole world. E eye half shut, e mout da run but nutton comin out straight. Dat right dey was de first sign, cause e try fa stand up off de stool and e knee just fold up under um like paper. I grab um quick fore e go down.

Me: Oonuh alright?

E: (mout mumblin) I fine, I fine, ain't nutton wrong wid me, I could walk home by mesef enty I could.

But e leg say different from e mout. E try fa take one step and e list to de side like a boat wid too much load on one side. I bin yeddy people at de nex table laugh, but I ain't studyin dem, I just tryin fa keep dis one upright.

Gettin um fa leave, now dat was a whole nex battle. E ain't want go.

E: Just one mo, just one mo and den I done, I promise.

Me: You done bin done tree time already. We gwine now.

E: Oona wan leave me? Oona ain't my tittuh no mo?

I haffa sweet-mout um a lil bit, tell um de music better down de road, tell um we go somewhe else fun, all kind a story, till e finally let me pull um up off de stool and lead um to de door. E lean heavy on me de whole way, one arm roun e waist, e arm roun my neck like a yoke.

We walk. Oh Lawd we walk slow. E foot da drag, e head da bob, and every few step e stop and say e fine again like sayin um enough time gwine make um true.

Bout halfway home e stop sudden and say e feel sick, and sho nuff e haffa bend over by de ditch and let go everyting e done drink dat night. I hold e shoulder de whole time, rub e back, tell um leh it out, leh it all out. After dat e want just sit down right dey on de curb, say e leg tired, say e just need a minute. I let um sit but I ain't let go a e arm, cause I know how dat go, sit down turn into lay down real quick if you ain't watchin.

While we sittin dey one ol man da come down de road, walkin e dog, and e shum us two, me half holdin dis one up, and e cut e eye at us proper, one long cut-eye like e done seen dis a hundred time and ain't impressed one bit.

Buckra man: Y'all need to get on home now, dis ain't no place fa dat.

Me: Yessuh, we gwine right now, jus catchin e breat.

E ain't say nutton mo, just shake e head and walk on wid e dog, but dat cut-eye stay wid me a while, cause I know how we bin look, two grown people out dey in de dark, one a we can hardly stand.

We git up and walk on, but I tell you de trut, in all dat dark and all dat carryin on I done miss de turn we spose to take. We end up two street further down fore I realize de house ain't spose to be dis way. E ain't help none neither, e just da hum some song to eself, some song I ain't even recognize, might be e done make it up right dey on de spot. We haffa turn back round, and e complain de whole way like I de one who done drink too much.

By de time we git close to home I try fa get some water in um, figure e need um bad. E ain't want it first.

E: I ain't thirsty, git dat away from me.

Me: Oona gwine drink dis water or I gwine pour um on ya head, one or de nex.

E laugh at dat, first real laugh all night, and finally e take de bottle and drink some, den push um back like e done enough. I mek um take a lil mo fore I satisfy.

And right bout dey, walkin de last stretch, arm still roun um, e stop and look at me real serious, e eye all watery, and e say de kind a ting only come out when a body dat far gone.

E: You know oona de only one who really understand me, right? Like fa true understand me. I ain't never gwine forget dis, not never, long as I live.

I ain't say nutton back, just pat e shoulder, cause you cyan reason wid a person dat deep in de bottle, you jus leh dem say dey piece and keep walkin.

We finally mek it to de door and dat door gi we trouble too, cause e own key ain't want find de hole, e hand shakin, and I haffa take de key from um and do um mesef while e lean pon de doorframe mumblin bout how de door done move. Once we git inside I walk um straight to de couch and e practically fall in um, arm and leg every which way, and I haffa arrange um proper so e ain't choke or nutton, turn e head to de side, pull a blanket over.

Right fore e drop off to sleep e grab my hand one more time.

E: I done wid all dat, fa true dis time. Never again. Oona hear me? Never again like dis.

Me: I hear you.

But I ain't believe um, not one word, not even while e sayin um, cause we bin dey befo and we prob'ly gwine be dey again, and dat alright, dat jus how e is.

Next mornin, round day clean, I check on um fore I gone. E bin sittin up on de couch holdin e head like e head gwine fall off.

E: What all happen last night? I ain't remember much, jus little piece here and dey.

Me: Oona remember de ditch part?

E: I remember bein sick, dat much I got.

Me: You remember tellin me I de only one who understand you?

E: (e face screw up) I ain't say dat.

Me: Oona sho did, wid tears in ya eye and everyting.

E: Lawd ha' mercy.

So I fill in de rest fa um, piece by piece, de wrong turn, de ol buckra man wid e dog and e cut-eye, de water e ain't want den ain't want stop drinkin, de door dat wouldn't open. E laugh and groan de whole time, shame-face but laughin still, and by de time I done tell um everyting e shakin e head sayin e ain't never gwine do dat again, same promise from de night befo, and I jus smile, cause I bin knowin all along how much dat promise worth. Dat's my tittuh fa you, dat's how e do, and Lawd knows I love um anyhow.
