Me: Le we call everybody for tonight. Meet down to de landing bout seven, enty dat good?
My friend: Seven ain gon work, my friend. De tittuh still da close up de shop till half seven, she cyan mek it dat early.
Me: Awright, le we push um back den. Seven-thirty?
My friend: Dat better. Wha bout de cousin, e comin?
Me: I yeddy e comin, e done tell me dis mornin down to de dock.
My friend: And de auntie?
Me: E gwine come too, e say e bring de benne cake fa we.
My friend: Good, cause I sho do love benne cake. Wha bout de brother-in-law?
Me: E cyan come dis time. E workin night shift down to de plant, dey done call em in dis evenin.
My friend: Enty dat too bad, e always missin out.
Me: I shum yestiddy, e say e sorry e cyan mek it but next time fa sho.
My friend: Wha bout de neighbor cross de road?
Me: E comin, e say e da bring e own chair too.
My friend: And de old uncle?
Me: E say maybe, e knee been actin up on em, but e gwine try.
My friend: Awright, so seven-thirty at de landing den.
Me: Wait, de auntie say she cyan stay out too late, she gotta be back fa put de chirren to bed. Le we mek it 7:15 stead.
My friend: 7:15 kinda tight for me, I still got de wash to finish. Oona sweet-mout me now, but dat still too soon.
Me: E ain sweet-mout, e de truth. Awright den, le we jus say quarter to eight and be done wid it.
My friend: Quarter to eight gwine work good enough. Ain everybody gwine be dere on de dot no way.
Me: True dat. I gi um cut-eye if oona late doe.
My friend: I gwine pass de word to de rest, ain no need for oona to call everybody yaself.
Me: Preciate dat. So how much we lookin at, bout six, seven?
My friend: Bout dat, six for sho, seven if de uncle knee let em come.
Me: Awright den, quarter to eight, de landing, we set.
