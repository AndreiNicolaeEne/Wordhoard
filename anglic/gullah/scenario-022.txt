The teller: Tittuh! Hol up, gal, I bin lookin fa you since de festival broke up. I got sumting fa tell you.

The listener: Wa now? Talk, wa you shum?

The teller: You ain gwine believe. Down by de cootuh race, off from de crowd where nobody hardly lookin...

The listener: Where, exactly? How close dey bin stand?

The teller: Close, tittuh. E da stand dey side by side wid de other one, and dey laughin at sumting like e too funny fa share wid nobody else. Closer den friend does stand, I tell you dat.

The listener: Enty! Lawd have mercy, dat too much fa be nuttin.

The listener: But wait now, could jus be dey laughin bout sumting silly, could mean nuttin. Or you really tink e mean wa e look like?

The teller: Dat ain all neither. Same day, I shum de two ob dem sharin off one benne wafer, one piece, passin e back and forth, and e give a cut-eye look pon top of all dat sweet-mout and grinnin dey bin doin.

The listener: Sharin off one wafer, cut-eye and all? Enty dat mean sumting den, fa true.

The listener: Who else done yeddy by now, you tink? Oona know how tings travel roun dis island, buckra wid dey camera bin walkin every which way too, half de place probably done see um already.

The teller: Could still be nuttin, dough. We ain fa jump to conclusion off wa we shum one time.

The listener: True, true. Onliest way fa know fa sho, we gwine haffa ax one ob dem straight, next time we ketch um, even if e day clean fo we get de chance.
