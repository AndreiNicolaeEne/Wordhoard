The tenant: Tittuh, I had to come by, cause I done reach the end of my patience wid dis roof. E leaking bad, right over de corner by de young one bed. Every time rain come e da drip drip through de ceiling, done turn de plaster black already.

The friend: How long dis been going on now, enty? Seem like you was telling me bout dis back when it still cold out.

The tenant: Bin telling em since way back, tittuh. Two time now I done raise it wid de landlord. Fus time I call, e say e gon send a man fa look at it, gon fix it directly. Second time I call, same mout come back: e gon send somebody, e gon come see bout it soon. Ain nobody set foot on dat roof yet. Trouble running more than two, three month now.

The friend: Same excuse both time, enty? Dat right dere tell you e cut-eye you, e ain studying you a-tall. What e promise you, in plain word?

The tenant: E promise fa fix it. Dat's all, just fix it, no day, no man name, nothing wrote down.

The friend: Well oona got a few road you could take. Oona could hol back de rent till e do de work, but den e liable fa call de law on oona, and if you ain got nothing wrote down it go hard against you. Oona could call de county fa come look and write em up, dat shame a landlord quick sometime. Or oona keep pushing, call every week till e tired hearing oona voice.

I had a landlord one time done de same ting to me. Roof over my kitchen leaking bad, e sweet-mout me bout Monday, Monday, three month straight, and I never shum a man come.

The tenant: Enty, same story exact.

The friend: Dat's how buckra do when e figure you ain gon push back. Write down what e say and when e say it. Go out day clean and take a picture while de light still on it. Den we go together, knock e door, let em see two face 'stead of one.

The tenant: A'right, we do dat. Yeddy me, I ain letting dis ride past dis week.
