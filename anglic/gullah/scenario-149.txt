The eldest: I swear it, blessing gwine come fa oona house, crop gwine stand tall, e chillun gwine laugh from day clean to dark, sweet-mout gwine ride oona tongue all e days. Dat's sworn, not jus wish. But mark de turn, enty. If oona ever wrong we tittuh, I swear trouble gwine dog oona door like cootuh dog mud, cut-eye gwine meet oona dere, and peace gwine never yeddy oona name. One mouth swear bofe; what stand, stand.
