The counter: Rock, cootuh, rock, buckra pass, one two tree, oona fa run. OUT!

The one it land on: Enty! E count too fast, e skip tittuh, I ain out.

Another child: Oona da fuss, oona start de rhyme from top, dat de rule.

The counter, louder: Rock, cootuh, rock, buckra pass, one two tree, oona fa run. OUT!

E land pon de littlest fa true, ain nobody give cut-eye.

The littlest: E done ketch me. I out, enty.
