The elder: E done hurt ya heart wid e sweet-mout, den cut e eye soon ya back turn. I shum, enty, no need fa hide um. Sweet-mout an salt water is one ting: bof taste good goin down, bof leave ya dry. Tittuh, mind wa cootuh foot da do, not wa e mouth da say. Cut-eye tell mo dan mouth ever gwine tell. Dat gal ain't ya friend. Bin so since day clean, an oona done know it.
