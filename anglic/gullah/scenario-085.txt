Three of dem set on de porch shelling benne, and like always de talk done turn to who ail worse.

The eldest: Oona yeddy dis knee of mine? E bin a-trouble me good ten, twelve year now. Some morning e so stiff I can't hardly bend fa git out de bed, e crack like a old cootuh shell fo e loosen up.

The second: Cut-eye all oona want to cut, but dat ain't nottin side what I carry. Dis ache in my side come like clockwork, three, four night every month, e keep me walking de floor til day clean, can't lay down, can't set down neither, and Doctor buckra over to town ain't never fix it, only take oona money and sweet-mout oona bout come back nex week.

The third: Oona two sweet-mouting oonaself thinking oona got de worst of it. Dis back trouble bin with me longer dan either of oona bin knowing me, e started fore oona was crawling good. Ax my tittuh bout it, e used to rub linament on me back when we was young gal, and dat woman done pass twenty year now, so oona figure how long dat make it.

The eldest: Enty you always got to have de oldest something?

The third: Enty it always true doe?

The second: Y'all ain't hear me good, I say I ain't sleeping, dat worse den any ache dat just set still and hurt.

The eldest: So who got it worst den, oona tell me dat, straight out.

The second: I say me.

The third: I say me, and I bin saying it longest.

The eldest: E ain't gon settle today neither, same as e never settle.

And dem all laugh den, one running into de next, rocking slow on dat porch like dey got de whole evening to spend, cause dey do, and nobody give in one inch.
