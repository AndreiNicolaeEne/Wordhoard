The elder: Tittuh, set down a minute, I got something I been study on a long time now. We gwine do dat trip. De one we always talk bout and never do. We gwine go cross to Sierra Leone and see wha we people come from, but hear me good fore you get too excited, we got a thousand dollar and not one cent mo. Dat's e. A thousand, top to bottom, everything got to fit inside dat.

The younger: A thousand dollar for a trip like dat? Tittuh, e ain't much.

The elder: I know e ain't much, but dat's wha we got, so we gwine make um stretch. Le we start wid de big one, cause if we don't handle dat first, ain't none of de rest gon matter. Getting dey. I done shum on de computer already, e run bout four hundred dollar roundtrip if we book um right and don't wait too long.

The younger: Four hundred? Tittuh, dat's near half wha we got fore we even land on de ground.

The elder: I know, I know, but dey ain't no trip widout getting dey, enty? So four hundred, dat's we first number. Write um down.

The younger: Awright, four hundred fa getting dey. Now how much day we thinking bout stay, cause dat gon push everything else round.

The elder: Dat's wha I been chew on too. Le we say seven day.

The younger: Seven ain't near enough time, not for all de place we want fa see and all de people we want fa meet. Le we make um fourteen.

The elder: Fourteen too much, tittuh, we ain't got de money fa stretch dat far, not on a thousand dollar. We been go back and forth on dis too long a'ready. Le we land on ten. Ten day give we time fa do wha we need widout bleeding we clean dry.

The younger: Ten day. Awright, I can live wid dat.

The elder: Now dey one place we ain't skipping, no matter wha e cost. Bunce Island. Dat's where dem people leave from, dat's where we own people bin stand fore dey put um on dem ship. We going dey, period, don't care wha e cost.

The younger: I ain't fighting you on dat one, tittuh, dat one stay in no question. But if we put dat in solid, we got to take e out somewhere else, enty? We can't just keep adding wid nothing coming out de other side.

The elder: True dat. So le we sleep somewhere plain. Not no fancy hotel wid all dat carpet and air condition running all night. We find we a plain room, cut dat cost down low, and put wha we save toward de island.

The younger: Dat's fair. Plain room fa sleeping, keep de place dat matter.

The elder: Awright, so a plain room for de ten night, dat come to a hundred and fifty dollar.

The younger: A hundred fifty. Dat bring we to five hundred fifty so far.

The elder: Five hundred fifty. Now de boat and de tour out to Bunce Island, dey ask eighty dollar a head fa dat.

The younger: Eighty. Dat bring we to six hundred thirty.

The elder: Six hundred thirty. Now hold on, before we go no further, if I find dat ticket fa less den four hundred, wha den?

The younger: Den wha?

The elder: Den we get we a eleventh day. Whatever we save off de getting-dey, we put toward one mo day dey. Dat's fair, enty?

The younger: Dat's fair, I like dat. Keep looking fa dat ticket.

The elder: Now le we talk bout de splurge, cause I been thinking on dis de whole time. I want fa do dat drum and dance thing dey have in de evening, de one wid de real drummer and de food and all. Dat run fifty dollar.

The younger: Fifty dollar for one evening, tittuh? We could save dat fifty stead, or put um toward de eleventh day.

The elder: I hear you, but dis a once in a life trip. We might not never go back. I don't want fa be setting home five year from now wishing we had done dat.

The younger: I hear you too, but fifty is fifty, and we already tight as e is.

The elder: Don't sweet-mout me into giving up on dis one, cause I ain't giving up dat easy. But I hear your side. Le we set um aside for now and settle e after we see how de rest of de numbers stand.

The younger: Awright. Now food. We got to eat every day we dey, whether we like e or not.

The elder: Twenty dollar a day should feed we plain and simple. We ain't need fa eat fancy every meal, some benne cake and rice and stew gon do we fine.

The younger: Twenty a day, times ten day, dat's two hundred dollar right dey.

The elder: Two hundred fa food. Write dat down. Now le we tally wha we got so far. Six hundred thirty, plus two hundred fa food, dat bring we to eight hundred thirty.

The younger: Eight hundred thirty. And we ain't even talk bout wha happen if something go wrong yet.

The elder: Dat's next. We need money set aside case something happen. Somebody get sick, somebody miss a ride, de rain come and mess up a plan, whatever it be. We can't go dat far and have nothing in we pocket if trouble find we.

The younger: How much you thinking?

The elder: A hundred dollar. Just setting dey quiet, in case.

The younger: A hundred. Dat bring we to nine hundred thirty.

The elder: Nine hundred thirty. Now we forgot one thing, we gon need a ride round while we dey, from de sleeping place to de water and back. Taxi and dem ride, say fifty dollar fa de whole ten day.

The younger: Fifty fa de taxi. Dat bring we to nine hundred eighty.

The elder: Nine hundred eighty. Now, tittuh, we been holding dat splurge aside too long. I still want dat drum and dance evening.

The younger: I done think on um good, and you right, e a once in a life trip. Le we keep de fifty in. We done cut where we could already, we downgrade de sleeping, we going plain on de food, le we keep dis one thing dat matter to you.

The elder: Bless you, tittuh. Fifty dollar fa de splurge, dat bring we to a thousand and thirty.

The younger: A thousand and thirty? Tittuh, dat's over. We thirty over de line.

The elder: We thirty over. We done all dis figuring and we still come up short on de other end.

The younger: We got to find thirty somewhere, or find fifty and gain some back, one or de other, cause a thousand and thirty ain't gon work.

The elder: We can't touch Bunce Island, and I ain't touching de splurge now dat you say keep e, and de food already plain at twenty a day, and dat hundred fa emergency, we can't cut dat, dat's wha keep we safe if trouble find we out dey.

The younger: Den e got to be de taxi. We don't need no ride round every single day. We young, we strong, we can walk some, and we can catch a ride wid whoever we meet, de way we do at home. Cut de taxi money out altogether.

The elder: Cut de whole fifty?

The younger: De whole fifty. Save um, put um back in de pocket where e belong.

The elder: Awright. Cut de taxi. Le we run de whole tally one mo time slow, so we know e right dis time. Four hundred fa getting dey. Hundred fifty fa de sleeping. Eighty fa Bunce Island. Two hundred fa food. Hundred fa whatever go wrong. Fifty fa de drum and dance.

The younger: Four hundred and hundred fifty is five fifty. Plus eighty is six thirty. Plus two hundred is eight thirty. Plus hundred is nine thirty. Plus fifty is nine hundred and eighty.

The elder: Nine hundred and eighty. Dat's e. Dat's right dis time, enty?

The younger: Dat's right. Nine hundred eighty out a thousand, dat leave we twenty dollar to spare.

The elder: Twenty dollar to spare. And if dat ticket come in cheap like I'm hoping, we might even stretch dat eleventh day out dey after all.

The younger: You yeddy dat now. We finally got um fit. Ten day, maybe eleven if de Lord bless we, Bunce Island paid for, de drum and dance paid for, food plain but sure, and a hundred dollar setting quiet in case somebody act de fool and something go wrong.

The elder: Something always go wrong, tittuh. But dis time we ready for um.

The younger: We ready. Twenty dollar to spare and a whole trip planned out to de penny. I can't hardly believe we done um.

The elder: We done um. Now all we got to do is save de money and get on dat plane.
