Tittuh, set down a minute, mus tell you someting befo you git further een dis place. Yeddy me good, cause dey a swindle wa run roun yah, an e ketch de new one dem quick quick, cause oona ain know de face yet.

De one wa do um, e always smilin, always got someting nice fa say bout yuh. E dress good, e talk sweet-mout, e call yuh friend de fus day e meet yuh. E might even bring yuh benne cake fa show e trus wortty, sweet mout an sweet hand together. E always got a deal gwine on, some land, some boat, some job wa need jus a little money to git started. E da come by de dock, e da come by de church, wherever new people stan.

De pitch soun too good fa true, big return, quick quick, no risk, e say. But yuh got fa pay someting fus, jus a lil someting, fa hold de spot, fa show yuh serious. Dat de whole ting right dey. Pay one dime befo hand touch hand, an yuh ain neva gwine shum again. E ain slow like cootuh, e quick quick, by day clean, e done gone, an de money gone wid um.

De old man down de road, e bin fall fa dat. Gi up e whole week pay fa a boat share wa ain neva bin dey. Look fa de man fa mont, ain neva shum since. E learn hard, an dat de reason I da tell yuh now stead a leh yuh learn de same way.

Watch fa de sign befo e git dat far, de deal e too sweet, de man e too quick fa know yuh name, e in a hurry fa oona money an slow fa answer question. Enty dat oughta tell yuh someting?

If somebody come like dat, yuh jus cut-eye um an walk on. Ain gotta be rude, jus firm. Ain no buckra gwine give yuh back yuh money once e gone, so watch fa yasef fus. An befo yuh pay anybody anyting, oona go ax de one at de fish stand fus, e bin yah long time, e know every face wa worth trustin an every face wa ain.
