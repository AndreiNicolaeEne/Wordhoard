Guest: Tittuh, dis benne wafer crak so sweet, e stick to me teet' - wuh mek e taste so good? Enty you put something extra in dey?

Guest: Come on now, don't gimme dat cut-eye - I yeddy you da toast de benne fo' day clean, enty?

The cook: Alright, alright. I done toast um slow till e turn gold... but de rest, dat stay wid me.
