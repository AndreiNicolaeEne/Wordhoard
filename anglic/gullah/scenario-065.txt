The neighbor: Tittuh! Tittuh, come quick, ya hog done bust troo de gap by de benne patch, e da run fa reach de road!

The other: Enty! Which way e gone?

The neighbor: E gone down by de ditch side fa true, e ain stop. Oona head um off 'fore e reach de road, I gwine block de gap so e can't double back.

The other: Awright, I gwine cut cross de field. Yeddy me if I shum fus.
