Neighbor: How de hog make out? Enty e bad off last night?

Me: E bad off, tittuh, real bad. From evenin e off e feed, not movin right, an I say to myself, I ain leavin dat animal by e self dis night, no. I stay right dey from de first hour, watchin fa any change, an fa de longest time nottin change, jes de hog layin dey breathin heavy an slow. I try fa get em fa take a little water, mix a little benne mash in wid it too, coaxin an coaxin, cause e ain gwine drink e own self, e too weak in de head fa dat.

Bout de middle a de night e take a bad turn, e start fa shake, e eye roll back in e head, an my heart drop straight down in my stomach. I sit dey wonderin if I done do de wrong thing, keepin em home stead a runnin fa de buckra doctor in town. But I ain leave em. I stay right dey, wrap em up warm, talk to em low, de way oona talk to anything dat frighten, jes soft talk, nottin fancy, so e know somebody dey wid em.

Round day clean I shum move e ear, jes a little twitch at a sound, an e breathin done come easier, not so hard drawin no more. Dat de first sign, an I hold on to dat sign like it gold.

By full mornin e steadier. Not right yet, mind you, but steadier, standin a little on e own leg, tek a little off my hand. An it ain till I sure a dat, sure e gwine be aright, dat my own self jes give out on me all at once, like somebody done cut de string in me.

Neighbor: Oona should a call somebody fa come see bout em.

Me: Fa what. Cut-eye an sweet-mout don't cure nottin in de dark. Oona got fa stay right dey, plain so.
