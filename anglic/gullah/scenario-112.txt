Traveler: Wuh de fare fa go to town?
Driver: E twenty, enty you know de road long.
Traveler: Twenty too much, oona charge like buckra. I got twelve.
Driver: Twelve? Cut-eye pon me now, I done bin drive dis road since day clean, e ain't fa nutting.
Traveler: I ain't fa vex you, jes de purse light dis mawnin.
Driver: A'right, you sweet-mout me good, leh we say sixteen and done wid it.
Traveler: Sixteen fair. Leh we go.
Driver: Come on in den.
