The niece: Tittuh, e done come! Bawn dis mornin atta six!

The aunt: Wait now, you ain't yeddy me knock? Wha you da tell me?

The niece: De chile done bawn! Him mudda bin labor since day clean.

The aunt: Enty dat mek de third pickney fa dat house now?

The niece: Dat right, third one. We got fa go shum dem dis mornin, not wait til later.

The aunt: Le we go den, 'fore de sun git high.
