I gone by de tailor fa git my coat, de one I done order fa true two week back. Soon as e lay um out pon de counter, I shum straight and someting ain right.

Me: Enty dis ain de coat I order? I tell you three button and a straight hem. Dis yah got two button da sit pon um, and de hem all slant so.

The tailor: Naw, dat exactly wa we agree pon. I ain mix up nutin, e come out just like we talk.

Me: I bin stand right yah and tell you plain, three button, straight hem cross de bottom, not no angle. You yeddy me dat day, enty?

The tailor: (cut-eye pon de coat, den go fa de book) Le me check de note fore we fuss any more bout um.

E flip back de page, run e finger down de line, den e stop.

The tailor: Look yah... de note say two button true nuff, but e write straight hem clear as day. So dat part bin my mistake, not yours.

Me: Dat wa I bin try fa tell you from de start. I ain come yah fa sweet-mout you, I come fa my coat right.

The tailor: I ain gwine argue wid you bout wa my own hand write down. Gimme two day, I gwine take out dat hem and set um straight like oona want fa true.

Me: Two day awright. I ain in no hurry like buckra people, but don't let um sit pass dat.

The tailor: E gwine be ready fore day clean two morning from now, sure as benne grow in de patch.

Me: Awright den. My tittuh gwine come wid me fa collect um, so have um ready fa true.

The tailor: E gwine be ready. I done tell you so.
