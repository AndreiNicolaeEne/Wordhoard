The eldest: Tittuh, oona ready fa riddle? Wa ting e da tote e own house 'pon e back, e slow, enty?
Tittuh: E be de crab?
The eldest: Uh-uh, oona ain fa guess dat quick, e be cootuh, oona bin shum down by de creek.
Tittuh: My turn. Wa small black ting sweet-mout when e roas, oona plant um an e come up wid nutten but seed?
The eldest: E be benne.
Tittuh: E so. Oona win, I done.
