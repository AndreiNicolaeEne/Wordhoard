The mother call de two chillun to she, mus set down close fo she say one word.

Mother: Oona come yah, mus set down right by me, close now.

The eldest: Mama, wha e is? Wha happen?

Mother: I got something haffa tell oona. Oona Grandy done pass. E gone on dis mornin, fo day clean even.

The eldest: Mama, wha dat mean, e gone on? E ain never comin back no mo, enty?

Mother: No, love, e ain comin back, not de way oona know um. Grandy body done tired out, e done wuk hard e whole life, and now e done res. E ain gwine feel no mo pain, e ain gwine breathe no mo. But wha mek Grandy Grandy, dat part ain done gone nowhey. E still da walk wid we, same as e always bin.

The youngest: Mama, e gon sleep een de groun now?

Mother: E body gwine rest een de groun, yes, love, quiet-quiet, same as we lay um down soft. But Grandy own self done gone home to God.

Mother: Come yah, bofe oona, mus hug me now.

She pull dem in close, tight tight, and hold de little one long.

Mother: Come yah, tittuh, mus hug me good.

The eldest: We gon shum again, enty, Mama?

Mother: Not de way oona use to shum um, not yah on dis earth wid oona own eye. But we time gwine come too, and we gwine shum um one mo time. E ain gone fa good, e jus gone on ahead of we.

Mother: Now fo oona go on back to play, I got fa tell oona wha comin. Family gwine start come from all bout dis week, fa set up wid we, cook, pray, sing. Dey gwine carry Grandy body today. Church gwine bury um Sat'day, and de house gwine full up wid people. Dat jus we way of loving on Grandy fo we let um go. Oona ain need fa fret when oona see all dat.
