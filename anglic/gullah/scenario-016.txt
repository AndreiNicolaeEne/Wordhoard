We bin setting on de dock since day clean, e da mend e cootuh trap and me da shell benne fa parch out in de sun. E done stop e hand and look at me straight, enty e ain't never do dat before.

He: I got something on my mind I got fa say plain. I want fa be more than dis to you. I want oona and me together fa true, not just neighbor talk on dis dock no more.

I let e finish out. I ain't cut e off, ain't cut-eye, ain't nothing. I let de whole of it come till e done.

Me: I thank you fa say dat so honest, fa true. Ain't many man walk up and say e heart plain like dat, no sweet-mout bout it at all. But de answer is no, and dat ain't gwine change. Not later, not next season, not when de tide turn different neither.

E ain't say nothing, just look down at e trap.

Me: Ain't nothing wrong wid you, hear me now. You a good worker, you good to you mama, you got a steady hand and a kind way wid people dat come round dis dock. Any woman lucky fa have dat one day. E just ain't fit fa we, de timing ain't dere, and I ain't gwine tell you no story bout maybe.

He: You sure, enty?

Me: I sure. But I ain't gwine stop fa shum you neither, I ain't gwine turn cold. Oona still my people same as before, we still gwine set on dis dock together, still gwine share what we catch. Dat part ain't broke none.

E nod slow, e eye still soft.

Me: You yeddy me, my friend?

He: I yeddy you.
