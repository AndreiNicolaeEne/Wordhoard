Day clean when the first one come to my porch, ain't even wait fa knock proper. E sidown on de step like e own it, start in fa talk before I pour de coffee good.

The first friend: Girl, I got to tell you wa dat one done to me. E tek me fa fool. E come to de meetin and e tun roun and tell everybody I da lie bout de money, when e de one who bin owe me since spring.

Me: Mm. I yeddy you. Wa e say back, when you call em on it?

The first friend: E ain't say nottin, e just cut e eye pon me and walk off. Enty dat show guilt right dey?

Me: Could be so, could be not. I ain't gwine say who right till I yeddy de other side too.

Evening come and de second one show up while I da shell benne on de step, ain't stay long fore e start in too.

The second friend: Tittuh, I know e done been here already, e stay long enough fa tell you e whole tale. But e ain't tell you de half of it. I ain't start dat mess bout no money, e de one bin talk behind my back to de buckra man down de dock, tellin em I steal.

Me: Enty oona two been friend long time now? Wa mek e think you da talk to buckra bout em in de first place?

The second friend: I ain't say nottin to no buckra, dat lie clean through, sweet-mout somebody done tell em.

Me: Awright. Both a oona say oona ain't start um. Let me ax you one ting plain - fore all dis money and dis buckra talk, wen oona last really talk, just de two a oona, no third mout in between?

The second friend: ...We ain't talk proper since e tek my cootuh trap off de creek without ax.

Me: Dey e go. Dat de root of um, enty. All de rest just grow up on top a dat.

The second friend: Maybe I coulda ax fa my trap back stead of let it fester so.

Me: I ain't studyin carryin word back and forth no more like some mule. Oona need fa set down togeda and say to one anudda wa oona just tell me. Oona come by my porch Sunday after church, I'll mek sho oona both dey.
