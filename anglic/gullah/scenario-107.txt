De mother hold em close, den e leggo.
The mother: Tek care, yeddy, e road long.
The traveler: I gwine mind myself.
The mother: Soon as oona reach, send word, enty.
The traveler: Soon as I reach, I write oona.
De mother press a han full a benne cake een e hand.
The mother: Fa de road, love.
The traveler: I gwine bring oona something sweet when I come back.
E turn and gone down de road, day clean.
