The stall down by de landin done open since day clean, and de cook already had de oil hot when de customer come round de corner.

The cook: I shum comin fore oona hit de road good. Same as e always stay, shrimp an grits, hot sauce heavy, benne wafer on de side. Enty?

The customer: Enty right, but leff off de onion dis time, ma belly ain settle good dis mornin.

The cook: No onion den. I da fix um right now, jus set down.

The customer: How business been stayin?

The cook: Slow dis week, slow. De buckra dem ain come round like dey use to, an when dey do come, dey want everyting mild mild. I tell um, oona in a Gullah kitchen now, e ain gwine cook no oder way.

The customer: E true. I bin see dem cut-eye de hot sauce like e gwine bite um.

The cook: Dat's e exact! Den after dey taste um, dey sweet-mout me all over de place, tellin me how good e is.

The customer: Ha! Dat sound jus like um.

The cook: How oona people stayin? Oona mumma still down de creek fa cootuh?

The customer: Yeah, e still ketchin cootuh fa de soup. Say e gwine send you some if e ketch nuff.

The cook: Tell um I say tank e kindly. Oona come see me tree, four time a week now. I know oona order fore oona reach de counter good.

The customer: Cause nobody else fix um like dis, dat's why.

The cook: Yah go now, hot an ready. Six dollar today.

The customer: Yah go. See ya Thursday, den.

The cook: Awright now, tittuh, oona take care. I yeddy oona soon.
