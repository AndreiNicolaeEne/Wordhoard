Tittuh: We bin dey since day clean, and I still cyan git over dat call, cousin.

Cousin: E miss nottin, Tittuh. Dat de right call, maybe de only right one e make all day.

Tittuh: E miss em plain. I shum wit me own two eye, hand pon e shoulder de whole way down, and de ref just stand dey da watch, like e ain got no eye in e head.

Cousin: You forget how e bin shaky from de start, den? First quarter, ball gone out by oona side and e call em fa oona anyhow. Nobody say a mumblin word den, enty?

Tittuh: Dat different, dat was close.

Cousin: E ain no different, e just don't suit you dis time.

Tittuh: Well, e miss de holdin down by de goal too, e miss de foot dat trip e boy right front e face, e ain call nottin de whole first half. E too slow fa see em, dat man, e cyan fa keep up wid e own game.

Cousin: My workmate down de road, e say e t'ink both side got someting fa vex bout, e say de call even out by de end.

Tittuh: Dat ain settle nottin. Dat just sweet-mout de whole business so nobody stay vex.

Cousin: E ain wrong neither, though.

Tittuh: E wrong bout dis one, I tell you dat plain.

Cousin: And I done tell you e ain. Yeddy me now.

Tittuh: I yeddy you good, cousin, I just ain agree wid you.

Cousin: Cut you eye all you want, e still de right call to me.

Tittuh: Have some mo benne cake fore you bust a vein over dis.

Cousin: Buckra up to de league office ain gwine do nottin bout it noway, so wa de use fussin.

Tittuh: Oona ref dem all de same anyhow, blind as cootuh in de sun, ain worth de whistle roun e neck.

Cousin: Dat de one thing we gwine agree pon today. De whole lot o dem ain worth nottin, not one.
