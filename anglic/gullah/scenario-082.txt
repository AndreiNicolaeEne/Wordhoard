The eldest, hollering: I cut me hand, e da bleed too much, come quick!

Her workmate, running: I da come, tittuh, hol on now.

Workmate: Leh me shum. Press dis cloth pon de cut, press e hard, don't leggo.

The eldest, shaky: I can't scarce hold um, e still bleedin.

Workmate: Lif dat hand up over de heart, higher. Yeddy me, hold steady.

They wait quiet, checking de cloth now and again, till de bleed done slow for true.
