Tenant: Mornin to you.

Landlord: Mornin, mornin. Come in, siddown a spell. How oona all farin out dere?

Tenant: We fine, thank God. De chillun grown some since you shum last.

Landlord: Time sho do fly. Storm da come dis evenin, dey say. You want some benne candy fore we start?

Tenant: I take a piece, thankee. I ain gwine tarry too long, I know you got tings fa do.

Landlord: Ain no rush on my end. What bring you by?

Tenant: I come fa talk bout de rent. I done pay you steady, on time, gwine on four year now, never late one time, enty?

Landlord: Dat's true, you been steady.

Tenant: And dat back step still ain fix since de storm blow it loose. I bin ax you bout it plenty time, I just quit askin and fix what I could myself.

Landlord: I hear you. I ain say you wrong bout none of dat.

Tenant: So seein how long I been here and how I pay you every time widout fail, I was hopin we could bring it down some. A fifth off what I pay now.

Landlord: A fifth? Enty you know what dat come to a year? I can maybe do half dat much.

Tenant: Half a fifth ain hardly nothin, a cootuh could beat dat to town.

Landlord: (cut-eye) I got my own bill fa pay too. Buckra done raise de tax on dis property again dis year, and it ain me settin dat number.

Tenant: I ain askin fa charity, I askin fa what fair.

Landlord: Let me study on it a night or two. I ain say no, I just ain say yes neither.

Tenant: Dat's fair enough. Don't sweet-mout me now.

Landlord: I ain sweet-moutin, I'm tellin you true. Yeddy me, come back by day clean tomorrow, we finish dis den, and I'll fix dat step too.

Tenant: Alright den. Tell your tittuh I say hey.

Landlord: I will. Take care now.
