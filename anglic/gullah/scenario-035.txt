Me: My stall dey by de dock beat all oona talkin bout. E open since day clean, e stew de shrimp fresh right dere, ain't no buckra food outta no can.

My friend: Hold on now, tittuh. Dat stall ain't got nottin on de one by de church. E benne cake sweet fa true, e melt right in oona mout fore oona even chew e good.

Me: Sweet-mout, dat all e got, cause de rest ain't fit fa eat. I bin dere one time, I order de cootuh soup, e cold like ice an ain't season right at all. I had fa throw half e out.

My friend: Enty oona just ain't got no taste? I done eat dere plenty time, e never gimme no trouble. But oona stall, I shum one day, de fly dem walkin cross de fish like e own de place.

Me: Dat ain't so, oona lyin now!

My friend: I ain't lyin one bit. I yeddy from oona own cousin say e shum a roach big as e thumb walk cross de counter in broad day.

Me: Well if dat true, oona stall so bad, if a body eat one plate dere, e haffa go straight home an call de doctor fore night come.

My friend: Now oona talkin pure foolishness. I cut-eye oona right now, cause dat ain't true one bit an oona know e ain't.

Me: A'right, a'right. But wa bout de one by de landing, wid de fried fish an de red rice? Nobody ain't got nottin bad fa say bout dat one, not oona, not me, not nobody.

My friend: Dat true. E always good, every single time, rain or shine.

Me: Enty we oughta go together, oona an me, eat at both we stall again, see which one really beat de other fa true, settle e once fa all.

My friend: A'right den, tittuh. We go do dat Saturday. But I tellin oona now, my stall gon win, watch an see.

Me: We go see bout dat.
