Love, I got to say dis plain fore I say anything else: I love you, and I done love you a long time now, longer den I ever tell you straight to you face.

I member de time I first know it bin serious. Wasn't no big day, wasn't no wedding nor no church meetin, jus a Tuesday like any Tuesday. We bin down by de creek at day clean, pullin cootuh out de mud fa we granny to bile fa supper. You ain't say much, jus hand me de rope and cut you eye at dat cootuh when e try to bite, and I laugh so hard I mos fall in de water. Right dey, watchin you laugh too, sumpin in me settle down quiet and say, dis de one, e ain't gwine be nobody else, not ever.

Ain't nobody else know how you hum dat ol song under you breath when you shellin benne on de step, low so nobody but somebody standin close could yeddy it. My tittuh shum us one evenin and ax right out:

The tittuh: Oona two ain't do nothin but grin pon one nother all day, enty?
Me: I ain't say nothin. Jus grin some more.

My days different since dat mornin by de creek. E like de light da come in different now, softer some way. But de days I ain't got you close, dem done get harder than I ever let on, cause I get so use to yeddy you movin bout de house, and when you gone to de mainland, de house too quiet fa me, and every time I shum comin back cross dat causeway my heart jump same like de first time.

I gwine tell you one ting I ain't never say out loud befo: sometime I skeered, skeered dat buckra world out dey, all dem long road and dem big city, gwine pull you from me one day, and I ain't know how I stand it.

But I promise you dis, and I mean fa keep it long as I draw breath: I ain't gwine let no sweet-mout from nobody turn my head, and we gwine build we life right here on dis ground, together, jus us.

I love you, sugar.
