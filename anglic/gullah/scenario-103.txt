The traveler: Scuse me, I da try fa find de market. Which way I go?

The stranger: Ya turn by de church wid de red door, den ya gwine shum de ol well on de corner, dat de second turn, ya cyan miss um.

The traveler: E far? How long fa walk?

The stranger: Bout fifteen minute, enty nothin much.

The traveler: Tank ya kindly.

The stranger: Awright, walk safe now, ya yeddy.
