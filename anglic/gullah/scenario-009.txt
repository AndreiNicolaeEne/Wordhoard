The elder: Tittuh! I jes shum an mos drop! E head look like cootuh crawl up dey an dead, enty?
The younger: Ain nobody ax you nuttin. Dis de style I da wear now, you jes ain ready fa see am.
The elder: Style? Look like buckra bin done try cut grass wid e eye shut.
The younger: Better dan you cut-eye face dis mornin. I gwine tell de mother wuh you say.
