One friend: Tittuh, e do it to we again, enty? Just now e call me say e cyan come fa de cookout, e say e head hurtin um.

The other: Lawd today, not again! I yeddy you good. E done dat same way de last time, wen e say e car done broke down and e ain't call fa tell we till de day almost gone.

One friend: Mmm-mm. Dis mek it de fourth time now, I done count um good. First time e miss de church supper, den e miss we cousin weddin, den de day we bin gwine down to de dock fa fish, and now dis cookout.

The other: Fourth time! Cha, e always got a story fa tell, some excuse e da hand we like e sweet-mout gwine fix everyting up nice.

One friend: Dat's what I say. E head hurtin, enty? E ain't sick, e just don't want fa come. If e bin sick fa true e woulda call befo now, not right when we bin gettin ready fa go.

The other: I shum de last time we bin talk bout dis and I cut me eye so hard. Oona know how e stay, e always got a reason soun good but don't feel right in you belly.

One friend: True dat. But e we friend still, we done known um since we small. Enty we should keep axin um anyhow?

The other: I don't know bout dat no more. Every time we plan someting, e say e gwine come, den e da find a way fa back out. Fa keep hopin every time e gonna change, dat wearies me soul true.

One friend: Well den, next time we plannin someting, we ain't gwine wait on um. If e cancel again, we just go head widout um.

The other: Dat's right. We ain't stoppin we whole life fa nobody sweet-mout excuse. E can come if e come, but we ain't holdin up we plans no more fa nobody.

One friend: Enty. Dat's how we gwine do it from now on, tittuh.
