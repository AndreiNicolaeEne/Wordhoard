The mother: I done make up my mind. We gwine name em Marcus, after your daddy.

The father: Marcus? Nah, e ain fa be no Marcus. Every yard on dis island got a Marcus in em, oona know dat good as me.

The mother: Wa wrong wid a strong name?

The father: Ain bout strong. E too common, dat's all e is. Teacher call de roll and got three Marcus stand up same time.

The mother: Awright den, awright. Wa bout Elijah?

The father: Elijah too churchy fa my taste. Dat ain even we own fambly name, you just like how e sound comin out your mout.

The mother: E do sound sweet do, I ain gon lie.

The father: Sweet-mout ain gon change my mind neither.

The mother: Wa bout we give em e gran name den, since e bin pass dis spring? Chile carry de old people name, dat mean something round here.

The father: Which gran, oona side or my side? Cause we already got two Tittuh totin dat same name, one live cross de creek, one right down de road wid e own benne cake and all. Oona want a third one gwine confuse de whole fambly when somebody call out de name at church.

The mother: Cut-eye all you want to give me, but enty I done try? You shum every name I bring and just shoot em down.

The father: Who say e your call anyway? Who get de final say pon dis pickney name, you or me?

The mother: E come out my body, dat count for something, enty?

The father: E my chile too, I ain no cootuh sit back and let oona decide everything by youself.

The mother: Well we ain settle nutten tonight.

The father: Nah, we ain. Leh we sleep pon it, cause we too wore out fa keep on dis way till day clean.
