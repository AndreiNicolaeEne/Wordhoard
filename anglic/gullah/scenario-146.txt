Eldest, yeddy me, oona all yeddy: e da stand tall tonight!
E bin haul more net dan de whole dock, enty de eldest stand fus?
Enty de eldest stand fus, enty!
E wade deeper dan cootuh mud, e shum fudda dan buckra eye, e da work fo day clean.
Ain no rival fa measure dis one, cut-eye ner sweet-mout move um.
Enty de eldest stand fus, enty!
Mark de word: dis boast done seal, ain nottin lef fa say.
