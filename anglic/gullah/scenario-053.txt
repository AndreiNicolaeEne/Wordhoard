Tittuh, leh me tell you bout de day I had was to go down git dat boat paper stamp, de license fa de net boat, cause my brother-in-law and me had was to haul net come Monday and dey ain't gon let we out on de water widout um. I bin know dat office does get slow bout dis time a year, enty, so I gone down day clean fa beat de line.

I da stand in dat line near bout a hour, slow like cootuh, and when I git up to de counter, de one dere look over my paper long, long time. E ain't say nothing straight out. E just say, "Dis gon tek some time to process, tings kind a backed up dis week." And e sit back and cut-eye at me, waiting fa me to catch on.

Me: I ain't say nothing bout dat. I just say, "Yes suh, I understand, tings slow dis time a year." And I leh um hang right dere in de air, like I ain't ketch on to nothing.

De one at de counter: "Some people, dey does bring a little something fa help tings move along faster, oona understand." E talk sweet-mout, smilin big.

Me: "I ain't bring nothing today, I ain't know I s'pose to. Enty de sign say all dis service free of charge?" I point straight at de sign on e wall.

Shum siddown back den, ain't say nothing. Dat pause dere long, tittuh, e could a gone either way, e could a mek me come back nex week and lose de whole haul.

I say, "I can come back tomorrow if e ain't ready, and I gon tell my cousin who does work to de courthouse oona de one handling dis fa we." E stamp de paper right den, quick quick.

I gone home wid my paper, ain't pay one red cent. Buckra tink we too simple fa yeddy what e mean widout e say um. We done seen dat sweet-mout trick since we pickney, oona hear me?
