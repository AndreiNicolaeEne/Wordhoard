Me: I gwine tell oona wa I shum dis mornin, cause e true, e ain't no dream I mekkin up.

My friend: Enty you was half sleep when e happen? Oona know how e go when oona out on de water too early, e da mess wit oona head fo good.

Me: Nuh, I wide awake, I bin thinkin clear de whole blessed time, ain't no fog in my head at all.

My friend: Awright den, wa e look like?

Me: I gone down to de creek fa check de net befo day clean, and right dey I see a light out on de water, a white light, steady steady, no boat under um, no nutting under um, e just da float.

My friend: Dat easy. Some buckra man out dey early wit e flashlight, huntin de good fishin spot fo de rest come.

Me: Nuh, tittuh, ain't no flashlight. E ain't have no motor sound, no oar splash, nutting. De water quiet like glass, and dat light da move against de tide, headin up creek when de water pullin straight down.

My friend: Maybe e swamp gas den, de foxfire wa rise outta de mud sometime.

Me: Swamp gas stay low and e blink and e die right down in de grass. Dis one climb straight up over de tree line and gone one time, like somebody blow out a lamp. No smell, no sulfur, nutting a-tall.

My friend: (cut e eye) I still say oona been dreamin, or oona eat too much benne candy fo bed and e gie oona notion in de night.

Me: I ain't sweet-mout oona bout dis. I swear on my tittuh grave every word I done tell oona true. I bin standin right dey wit my two foot in de pluff mud, wide awake, and I yeddy nutting but de water movin, and I shum wit my own eye, plain as oona standin dey now.

My friend: (shake e head) Mmp. I hear oona good, but I still ain't believe a word.
