Friend: Tittuh, how oona da do dis mornin? How de little one stay?

Mother: We alright, tank God. E sleepin good right now, jus fall out on de bed.

Friend: For true? Dat good ta yeddy.

Mother: Enty I gwine lie to you, doe. We bin up four, five time las night wid dat chile. Tree hour sleep in a stretch, if dat much, fore e start to holler again and I got to git up.

Father: And dis back a mine, e ache so bad dis mornin I can't hardly stand up straight. Hand dem shakin too, from de tiredness, jus shakin like leaf on a tree, can't even hold de spoon still.

Friend: Lawd have mercy, my heart go out to oona two. Dat too much for anybody to carry and still keep goin.

Mother: I gwine tell you somet'in foolish now. Yestidy I bin lookin all over dis house for de baby bottle, and where e was? I done put um in de icebox mysef, wrap up nice like e gwine keep de milk cold forever. Laugh so hard when I find um, cause I done clean forget I put um dey.

Friend: Don't cut e eye at me, tittuh, I ain't fixin to sweet-mout oona, oona wearied down to nottin. Here now, bring dat baby to me dis evenin, let oona two rest fa true. I gwine bring some rice and fish stew by, oona ain't got to cook one t'ing.

Father: We ain't gwine say no to dat, we too far gone to keep refusin.

Mother: Enty we grateful. Oona too good to we, comin round like dis.

Friend: Hush, dat's what we here for. Oona jus rest when day clean come, I got de baby dis time.
