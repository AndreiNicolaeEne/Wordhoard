My friend: Tittuh, e gone. Pack e bag dis mornin fo day clean even broke good, ain't say more'n two word to me.

Me: Mmm.

My friend: We bin together since las summer, and I tought we solid. But e been actin funny long time, cut-eye all de time when I ax um simple question, sweet-mout when e want someting from me and den cold like stone when e don't. I try fa talk to um bout it, try fa fix wharesoever broke between we...

Me: Mmm-hm.

My friend:, and Friday I shum, down by de dock, wid dat gal from de Point, laughin all up in e face like ain't nothin wrong. I ain't say nothin to um bout it. Maybe dat wrong of me too, enty? Maybe I too quiet bout it, maybe I fuss too much some odder time and drive um dat way, maybe I...

Me: Naw. Stop right dey. Dat ain't your fault, not one piece a it. E been runnin roun wid dat gal long fo e ever say a word to you, dat's de real reason, not your question, not your fussin. E ain't have de decency fa be straight wid you. Dat's on um.

My friend: You tink so.

Me: I know so. And dis ain't say nothin bout who you is, tittuh. You done love um right. E just ain't de one fa hold what you was givin.

My friend: (quiet) I yeddy you.

Me: I stay wid you tonight, we ain't gwine sit up in dis house lookin at e empty chair. Come day clean we walk down by de water, let dat grief have somewhere fa go 'sides settin still in you. I even got benne candy in de house, we gon eat every last piece.

My friend: (small laugh) You slow like a cootuh gettin to de point but you always got a plan.

Me: Every time, tittuh. Every time.
