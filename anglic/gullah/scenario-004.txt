Mother: Hush now, sweet duck, e da fuss so, e da cry so - shh, shh, don't fret, my little one. E slow like cootuh down by the creek, e ain't in no hurry, e pull in e head and rest. I bin here before, I gwine stay fa mind you till day clean, tittuh close by, no cut-eye gon touch dis bed, enty? I ain't sweet-mout you none - shh, shh, sleep now, sweet duck, shh, shh, sleep, like cootuh, sleep.
