The niece: Auntie, Auntie! De boat done turn over, e gone under de water!

The auntie: E ain! Wha you say, who gone under?

The niece: I. I bin yeddy um holler out fore day clean, den I ain shum no more, e, de current done pull um round de bend, I run soon I yeddy...

The auntie: Lawd, Lawd. E ain drown, enty? E swim fa shore?

The niece: I don't know, Auntie, I run, my breath short, I can't hardly talk.

The auntie: Come, we go, we go now.
