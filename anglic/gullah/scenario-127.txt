The young one: Auntie, dis sun hot today enty?
The elder: E hot fa true, hot like Tuesday when e bin scorchin, no wind stir a leaf.
The young one: Mm-hm, e sho is.
The elder: Time I bin young we had a stretch like dis, cootuh crawl out de creek fa cool deyself till day clean gone.
The young one: Oona reckon tomorrow gwine bring rain?
The elder: E might, sky da git heavy round evenin.
