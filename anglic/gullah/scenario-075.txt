I di' gone to de feed store fa buy chicken feed, ain't studying bout nobody, when I shum standing right dey by de counter. I stop dead in de door.

Me: Enty dat you?! Lawd have mercy, look at you standing dey!

The friend: Wait, wait now... it can't be. It really you, enty?

Me: It me for true! Girl, how long it been since we bin see one another?

The friend: Chile, it been more than ten year, I know dat much. Since we was young gal running round de yard from day clean to dark, 'fore I gone off.

Me: More than ten, yeah. Time da fly when you ain't watching e.

The friend: How you doing though? Oona still down by de creek?

Me: Still dey, thank God. Married now, got two chirren, working over to de school. E hard but e good. And you, what oona been doing?

The friend: I done move off to de city for work, but I come back when I can. Got married too, one boy, hardhead like e daddy. I da raise him best I can.

Me: Lawd, you remember when we bin catch dat cootuh down by de marsh and try to keep um for a pet?

The friend: Lawd, yes! E bite you finger and you scream so loud de whole yard come running to see wa happen!

Me: I ain't never forget dat foolishness. We was some fool chirren for true.

The friend: We sho was. Say, you yeddy anything from tittuh, the one used to run wid us, always sweet-mout everybody so e could get e way?

Me: No, ain't yeddy nothing bout um in a long time now. Somebody say e move off somewhere but nobody know where e gone to.

The friend: E just gone, just like dat, and nobody know.

Me: For true. Well, we can't keep losing track like dis no more. Gi' me you number 'fore we part.

The friend: I will, and I ain't gon cut-eye you and never call like last time neither. I mean to call for true dis time.

Me: You bet not fool me again, I done wait ten year already fa hear from you.

The friend: Dis time e different, I promise you dat.
