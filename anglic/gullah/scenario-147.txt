Tittuh, weh you dey now? De house stay quiet since e done gone from we. E da come fo' day clean, benne cake in e hand fo' sweet-mout we chillun. Enty you yeddy me call you? De cootuh still crawl down to de creek but you ain' dere fa shum wid me. Weh you dey, tittuh, weh you dey? Res' easy now, de tide gon carry you home slow.
