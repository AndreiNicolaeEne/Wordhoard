It bin a Tuesday evening when de fus pain ketch me, but I ain t'ink nottin of it. I done had one chile already, so I know how e stat soft, den e stat sharp. I say to myself, dat ain nottin, jes a cramp, jes de body settle down from supper. But by de time night come good and dark, e come back, and e come back stronger, and I know den, dis de real ting dis time, enty.

My oldest been by e granny house dat night, so wasn't nobody home but me and de husband. We pace de floor, up and down, up and down, slow like cootuh, hour after hour, nottin fa do but wait. Round bout de middle of de night, tings turn hard. De pain da come one on top de other now, no rest between, and I start to fraid.

Dat when we call for tittuh to come. E ain been dere from de start, but soon as e walk in de door e take charge, e hand steady, e voice low. Won't no buckra doctor dere dat night, jes tittuh wid me. E bin bring benne cake in e pocket, say I got fa keep me strength up.

Tittuh: Oona alright, enty? Jes breathe. Jes hold on.

Me: I cyan hold much longer.

Tittuh: Oona can. E almost time fa push now.

De last stretch de hardest part, I bear down till I ain got no more breath left in me body. Den e stop. Silence. Long silence, too long, and I fraid something bin wrong. Den fus ting I yeddy, de cry, and soon as I shum, all dat long fraid gone from me mind.

Tittuh cut-eye de clock and sweet-mout de baby soft, and 'fore a full minute pass I done had her in my arm, warm and cryin, right dere close to day clean.
