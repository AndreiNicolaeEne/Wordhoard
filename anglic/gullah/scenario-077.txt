My friend: Tittuh, wait till oona yeddy dis! Money done land on me dis marnin, straight in de bank, mo den I mek in six, seb'n month wuk put together!

Me: Lawd have mercy, tittuh, dat good news fa true! Das GOOD news, enty!

My friend: I still can't b'lieve um myself. E jus drop. Ain come slow like cootuh, e come quick quick.

Me: How much e come to? Tell me straight, no shame in e.

My friend: Ten t'ousand and change. Enty dat someting?

Me: Enty. ...So how e happen, doe? Cause me bin da wuk since day clean all dis week and ain shum a extra dime fa all dat sweat.

My friend: De buckra fa my old job hafta settle wid me. Back pay dey done owe long time, lawyer say e come now or never.

Me: Mm. Buckra always got money when e want fa keep e own self out trouble. Wish somebody bin owe me dat kind a back pay too.

My friend: Wa mek oona ax me so hard? Someting wrong wid oona?

Me: Ain nottin wrong wid oona, tittuh. E wrong wid me. Me bin envy oona, plain so, ain no more den dat. Ain no cut-eye pon oona, jus pon how me own life ain never turn dat way.

My friend: Tittuh, I yeddy oona. Ain no shame in e.

Me: But I glad fa oona fa true dis time, not jus sweet-mout like befo. Oona done wait long fa someting good fa come, and e come. Go buy oona some benne cake and mek merry, den we talk no mo bout e.
