I done lef later than I mean to, and by de time I sot out de walk home already darker than I like it fa be. Wasn't far in when I git dat feelin somebody bin behind me, footstep landin bout de same time as mine own, like e da come right long wid me step for step. I ain gone turn round and let on I scared, so I just slow up one time, easy, like I studying on someding else. I yeddy de step behind slow up too, right when mine slow. Den I speed up a lil bit, and enty, dem step speed up right long wid me same way.

I know my shortcut good, de one cut through by de trees where ain much light back dey, but I ain take it dat night. I cut over to de road wid de streetlight and de stores still open, where people still walkin bout, so whoever back dey ain got no easy way fa follow me where folk could shum too.

I let myself look back one time, just one, quick like a cut-eye and gone. Didn't see nobody for sure. De dark play trick on you, and a shadow movin funny by dat oak don't tell you nothin either way.

Dat last stretch to de door, I walk fast, faster than I ever gone admit I walk. Ain run, but close to it. When I git dat door shut and de latch turn, Lawd, de relief.

Never did find out for sure if it was somebody or just my own self echoin back off dem trees.

Tittuh: Enty you was just scared of your own shadow dat night?

Me: Maybe so. But in dat moment I bin sure as day clean somebody was dey. I still tell it half shame-face now, how sure I was.
