The mother: Who done broke dis buckra plate wa bin settin on de shelf? Oona betta tell me right now, cause I ain't playin dis mornin.

The eldest: Ain't me, Mama. I ain't even bin in dis room since day clean.

The second: Me neither, fa true. I t'ink de wind mus done blow t'rough an knock em off. Or maybe e was de cootuh, e da crawl cross de flo an bump de shelf leg wid e shell.

The mother: Enty dat window bin shut tight since day clean? How wind gwine blow t'rough a shut window, tell me dat. An dat cootuh ain't tall enough fa reach no shelf, oona know dat plain as day.

The eldest: (cut-eye at the second) E lyin, Mama. I shum e stand right dey by de shelf dis mornin, sweet-mout talkin bout how e gwine feed de cootuh some benne.

The second: I ain't stand dey de whole time! Well... maybe I bin dey fa a minute, but I ain't touch no plate, I swear fo God.

The mother: Fus oona tell me e was de wind. Den e was de cootuh. Now oona sayin oona bin standin right dey an still ain't touch nuttin. Which one oona want me fa believe, cause dey can't all three be so.

The second: (quiet, lookin down) I ain't yeddy nuttin fall, Mama. I true ain't yeddy em.

The youngest: Tittuh push me dis mornin when we bin runnin, maybe I bump de shelf an ain't even know.

The eldest: Nobody push nobody, oona hush yo mout.

The mother: Since oona all got a different tale an ain't one oona gwine own up, oona all gwine pay for em together. No play out dis evenin, none oona, an oona all gwine wash dish de whole week till I done fuhget bout dis plate. Enty oona shoulda tell me straight de fus time.
