The first cousin: Gal, dat reunion was someting else, oona shoulda been dey. We been up since day clean just fa get everyting ready.

The neighbor: I yeddy bout it. How e went?

The second cousin: Chile, it went good. But some dem people, I ain gon lie, I shum standing right dey by de tree and I ain know who e be till somebody call de name. E done change so much, e head gray now, e done put on weight, e ain look like de same person no more.

The first cousin: Now de one thing, e own sister ain change one bit. Same face, same walk, same way e da talk fast fast like e always do. You put dem two side by side, back in de day and now, and one you can pick out quick quick, de other one you gotta study on hard.

The neighbor: Enty oona tittuh ain come dis time neither?

The second cousin: No ma'am, e say e knee bad, e couldn't make de trip down. But e sent word e love everybody and e thinking bout we.

The first cousin: And oona yeddy bout de one who used to work fa buckra down at de dock, haul crab and cootuh and whatnot for pennies? E done open e own place now. E own boss, e ain answer to nobody no more. Some people give a cut-eye when e say it, like e sweet-mouthing, but e show de picture and e true, for true.

The second cousin: Dat surprise me too, I ain gon lie. Now look at we, tittuh, we ain change too much, enty?

The first cousin: Girl please, we done got a little slow like a cootuh, dat's all, everyting else de same.

The second cousin: We gotta do dis again 'fore too much time pass us by.

The first cousin: Yeah, don't let it be no next ten year. Next summer we call everybody, bring some benne candy, and we do it again.
