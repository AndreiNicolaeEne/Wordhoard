Right, sit down a minute, cause I'm tellin yer the whole of it, start to finish.

I was up donkey's before the alarm, couldn't eat me breakfast proper, guts going ten to the dozen. Left the house dead early an all, went the long way round past the islands stead of cutting through the entry, just needed the time to walk me nerves down. Felt proper yampy, if I'm honest, walking round outside like a spare part cause I day want to be first through the door but I day want to be late neither.

Got in, and the one runnin the place come and got us. Showed us where to put me coat an that. Said, "yow'll be shadowin me this mornin, bab, don't fret." That settled us a bit, I'll say that much.

First job they give us wor half what I'd geared meself up for, dead simple stuff, and I thought, oh this ay so bad. Then come dinnertime it'd gone up about four gears and I was runnin round like a blue-arsed fly.

Made one right mess of it an all, in front of everyone. Dropped a whole tray of the things I'd only just been shown, clattered everywhere. Felt proper saft, face like a beetroot. One of the older ones just went, "eh, we've all done it, bab, get the dustpan," and nobody made owt more of it.

On me break I just sat quiet and sized the rest of 'em up, suppose you do that first day. There wor one though, her never had to be asked, just come over and showed us the quick way to do a thing that would've took us twice as long else.

Pace of it, I ay gonna lie, it wor faster than I'd braced for, proper flat out some of it, and we did the full nine hour an all.

Walked home after, going all round the Wrekin in me head, over every bit of it, the tray and all, donnies black by the time I got in, washed 'em before tea.

Mother: Well go on then, how'd it go, was it bostin or was it a nightmare?

Me: It wor alright, mom. Knackered, but it wor alright. I reckon I'll do.

Our kid: Yer never dropped a tray on yer first day.

Me: I did an all. Tara-a-bit on askin, I'm eating me tea.
