Landlord: Awright, bab, come in, sit yerself down.

Tenant: Ta. Traffic's murder round the islands this mornin.

Landlord: Tell me an all. And how's ya mom keeping, ay seen her about lately?

Tenant: Mom's grand, ta, gooin like the clappers as usual.

Landlord: Good, good. Boiler's playing up an all here, her from the gas board said her'd come first thing, never showed.

Tenant: Typical, that. Look, I wo go all round the Wrekin about it, I'll just say it plain. It's the rent I've come about.

Landlord: Go on then.

Tenant: Four year I've bin here now, paid on time every single month, never once late, not a day. And that winder in the back room's never shut proper since the day I moved in, cold cuts straight through it in winter. Rest of it though, it's bostin, I keep it lovely, yow know that as well as I do.

Landlord: I know yow do. But I cor be dropping the rent, bab, everything's gone up my end an all, insurance, the lot.

Tenant: I ay asking for the world. A fifth off what I'm paying now, that's all I'm after. That's fair, considering.

Landlord: A fifth? That's proper steep, that is. I might manage a tenth, no more than that.

Tenant: A tenth's next to nothing though, that's hardly worth the askin, is it.

Landlord: It ay nothing to me neither, it's still coming out my pocket same as it's going in yours.

Tenant: I'm not trying to do yer down, honest. Just seems fair, considering how I've kept the place and never give yer a day's bother.

Landlord: I know, and that's why I'm even sat here listening to yer. Let me think on it proper, have a look at the numbers, I'll tell yer end of the week.

Tenant: Fair enough, I'll leave it at that then.

Landlord: Tara-a-bit, bab, mind how yer go.

Tenant: Tara.
