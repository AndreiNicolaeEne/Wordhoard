Me: Eh, our kid, if we come into a load o' money out the blue, what's the first thing yow'd get?

Our kid: Easy one that, bab. One of them big flat tellies, the proper size, fill the whole wall up.

Me: Yow'm yampy. Sat there on yer own watching the match on a telly the size of a door. I'd get a car first, a decent one, not this banger I'm driving now.

Our kid: Ar, and what happens when the tax and the insurance comes in, eh? Yo cor just buy a car and leave it, it costs yer after an' all.

Me: I know that. I ay saft.

Our kid: Coulda fooled me. Look, we ought to put some by an' all, not blow the lot in a week like a couple of yampies.

Me: Put it where though?

Our kid: Post office, savings, summat like that. Me mom always said, save half, spend half, then yer've got summat put by for when it rains.

Me: Yer sound like her already. Alright then, what about this, we get a field out past the cut and we have a donkey.

Our kid: A donkey? Yow'm having a laugh now.

Me: No listen, it could have a gambol on the lawn, the kids'd love it.

Our kid: We ay got a lawn, our yard's the size of an entry. Where's this donkey gambolling then, round the islands?

Me: Alright, alright, forget the donkey.

Our kid: Bostin idea though, imagine telling me mom we spent it on a donkey.

Me: Her'd have kittens.

Our kid: Her would an' all. Right, half away safe, half for summat nice, car for yow, telly for me, and no blartin when it's all gone in a fortnight.

Me: Deal. Tara-a-bit, our kid, I'm off up.
