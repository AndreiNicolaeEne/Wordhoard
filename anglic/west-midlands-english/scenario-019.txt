My friend: Ar go on then, I ay letting yo off with nothing, I heard the whole lot through the wall Tuesday night, what was all that about.

Me: Oh bab, leave it out, I'm still embarrassed.

My friend: I'm not leaving it out. Yow were going at it like a pair of cats in a bag, I had our little one's donnies to see to and her gambolling all up the lawn and I still heard yous over the top of all that.

Me: I know, I know. And it was daft an' all, cos it started over nothing, honest to god. He said he was nipping to the outdoor for a couple of bottles for us tea and he'd be twenty minutes, and two hour later he's still not back and his phone's gone straight to answerphone.

My friend: Two hour.

Me: Two hour. So by the time the key goes in the door I've worked meself up summat rotten, and I just went off on one soon as he's over the step. "Where the hell have yo bin, two hour for a bottle of pop" - didn't even let him get his coat off.

My friend: So who started shouting first then.

Me: Me, if I'm honest. I was the one went in loud. He only came back at me the same cos I hit him with it first, no warning, right in his face.

My friend: Fair enough. Go on.

Me: And then it just snowballed, yo know how it does once it's going. I said summat I day mean, said "yo never think of anyone but yerself, yo never have," which ay even true, he's the most thoughtful get I know normally, he'd do owt for anybody, he'd do it for our kid an' all if it was them broke down on the estate. But it come out, and yo cor take it back once it's out yer mouth.

My friend: We've all said one of them in the heat of it.

Me: And then - this is the bit I ay proud of - I brought his mom into it. Said summat about how he's always got time to run round after her but never no time for me, which first off ay fair, second off we agreed years back we don't drag his mom into a row, that's an old one, that's one we leave alone, and I went and poked at it anyway just to make it hurt.

My friend: Ooh, bab.

Me: I know. And that's the minute the kitchen door went. He never touched me, mind, never touched nothing, he just walked out and pulled it shut behind him so hard the glass panel rattled right in the frame, I thought it'd come off its hinges.

My friend: Blimey.

Me: I went after him into the hall saying summat, I can't even remember what now, and he just wouldn't answer, wouldn't look at us, straight out the front and off down past the entry. I watched him go past the window heading for the cut, walking it off I reckon. And after that neither of us said another word, not properly, not for the rest of the night nor half the next day an' all - him in the front room on the settee, me upstairs, passing each other on the landing like a pair of strangers off the same street.

My friend: The whole day.

Me: Best part of it, ar. I'll admit I had a right little blartin session on me own upstairs at one point, felt that saft and yampy over it by then, over a bag of shopping.

My friend: So who give in first.

Me: Me, in the end, I couldn't stick it no more. I took him a brew down about four, put it right by his elbow without saying nothing at first, then I just went, "I'm sorry for what I said about yer mom, that was well out of order, I know full well what yo do for her and it ay a bad thing about yo, I only said it to hurt yo and that's not on, that ay who I am."

My friend: And what did he say.

Me: He went quiet a minute, turning the mug round in his hands, then he said he was sorry an' all - for the door, for scaring the life out of me with it, and specially for shouting straight back at me instead of just telling me plain where he'd bin. Said he could've sent one text and none of it would've happened. Turned out the road by the outdoor was shut for roadworks so he'd gone all round the Wrekin to get there, right past both islands on the ring road twice over, and then on top of all that he'd stopped to help some fella whose car wouldn't start.

My friend: That's a proper sorry, that, not just a "sorry" and left at it.

Me: It was. And then he looked up and called me bab again, first time since the Tuesday, and honest, hearing that one little word come back I nearly went again, but the good way this time.

My friend: Ahh, that's it then.

Me: And then he got up, put the kettle back on without me even asking, and went out to the outdoor hisself for a bag of chips to split between us, proper bostin they were an' all, and that were it really, that were the bit that sealed it, sat there splitting a bag of chips out the paper on the settee, not saying a lot, just alright with each other again.

My friend: We'm glad to hear it, honest.

Me: We'm sound now, we'm fine. Only thing is I keep telling him yo heard the lot of it through the wall, and now he wo look yo in the eye without going red as owt.

My friend: Tell him it's nothing I ay heard off next door before neither, walls round here don't keep nowt in.

Me: I will an' all. Right, I best get on, bab, things to be doing.

My friend: Go on then. Keep out the oss road.

Me: Tara-a-bit.
