The lock got sorted eventually, turned out to be summat daft, a bit of the mechanism had dropped, our kid had it out with a screwdriver in five minutes flat while I stood there feeling a right wombat for not thinking of it meself.

Last thing, before we locked up, I went round the empty rooms on me own.
