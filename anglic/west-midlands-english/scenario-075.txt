Me: Ey up, ay it who I think it is! Bab, what the bloomin heck are yow doing round here!

The old friend: I never! I day expect to see yow in a million year, not down the cut of all places. Come here, gi's a hug, yow ay changed one bit.

Me: Get off, I have an all, look at the state of me. How long's it bin now, must be over ten year.

The old friend: Ar, gotta be, cause I remember it wo long after we left school and that's donkeys ago now. Ten, eleven year easy.

Me: Mad init. So go on then, what yow bin up to. Yow still with the same lot?

The old friend: Nah that finished a while back, I'm with someone else now, we got two little uns, keeps me on me toes I tell yer. Working over the factory still, same as ever. What about yow, our kid still causing bother?

Me: He's alright him, settled down now believe it or not, got hisself a job on the buses. I'm still where I was, married now an all.

The old friend: Get away. Ey, do yer remember that time yow fell in the cut chasing that ball, soaked to the donnies, yer mom went spare cause yer had to have yer tea in yer vest!

Me: Ohh dow, don't! I was blartin me eyes out and all yow could do was laugh, yow yampy get.

The old friend: Cause it was bostin, that's why. Ey, yer heard anything off our old mate, from up the road? I ay heard nothing in yonks.

Me: Not a dicky bird. Went all round the Wrekin trying to find out and got nowhere. If yow hear owt tell em I'm asking.

The old friend: Will do. Ey look, gi's yer number this time, proper this time, I mean it, I ay gonna let it go ten year again.

Me: Go on then, put it in. Right, tara-a-bit love, mind how yer go.

The old friend: Tara bab, speak soon, honest.
