Right, sit down a minute and I'll tell yer how it went, bab, cause me legs ay gonna hold me up much longer.

Six o'clock this morning, still dark practically, past the two islands and up through the estate before I'd even had a brew, and I wo ready for it whatever I looked like, body still griping from yesterday and all. First job was getting the barrow through the entry round the back, cause you can't get owt bigger down there, and that set the pace for the whole day, up and down that entry with hardcore and sand till me arms didn't feel like mine no more.

Me back went about ten, right across the bottom, went like summat tore in it. Yo cor stop though, not with three loads still to shift and the gaffer wanting it by dinner. Had our break, twenty minutes only, donnies washed under the tap and a brew down yer, and that wo enough, not by a mile, but it's what yer get.

Two till four is where it gets yer proper. Full of dinner, sun beating down, job right by the cut so yer can hear the water and think about jumping in it. My workmate, her said we'm mad carrying on through it like that, and I day argue with her. Had to be yampy, the pair of us, to keep at it. I did think about calling it a day early, honest to God, bab, but yer dae that, do yer.

One bit went bostin though, slabs round the front went down in under the hour, quicker than I ever thought, proper little lift in an otherwise grinding day that were. Forty quid he owes me just for that corner.

Gaffer sent me all round the Wrekin for a bag of sand at dinner and all, forty minutes for what should've took ten.

Tomorrow's the same again, or he says maybe a rest day if me back's as bad as I'm making out. We'm hoping for the rest day, I'll tell yer that. Workmate said keep out the oss road on her way off, and I'm gooin in now meself, our kid's out the back doing gambols on the lawn like nothing ever tired him in his life. Tara-a-bit, bab, mom'll have me tea on.
