I'd stopped on later than I meant, mucking about doing nothing much, and by the time I set off it were already darker than I like it to be of an evening. Not far down the road I got this feeling there were someone behind me, footsteps landing about the same time as mine, close enough it give me the creeps. I day want to look daft turning round for nothing, so I just slowed meself up a bit, casual like, like I were looking in me bag for summat. And the steps behind slowed an' all, same as me. So I picked it back up sharpish, and them steps picked up too, right behind, same as before.

That were enough for me. Instead of cutting down the entry by the cut like I normally would of a night, I went the long way round, past the islands and the outdoor, where there's proper streetlights and folk still knocking about outside the chippy. Going all round the Wrekin for it, I know, but I wo be soft about it, not at that time of night.

I let meself have one look back, just the once, going past the bus stop, and there were nobody I could swear to, just shapes and a car pulling out the island. Told me nothing either way, if anything it made it worse.

That last bit up our road I walked faster than I'd ever own up to, donnies freezing in me pockets, heart going like the clappers, and when I got that door shut behind me and the bolt across I could've blartin with the relief of it, honest to God.

Never did find out if it were owt or nothing at all. Still don't know now, and I doubt I ever will. I tell our kid about it and I go all yampy in the telling of it, laughing at meself for how dead sure I was in the moment.

Our kid: Yow was probably running off from a bin bag blowing about, bab.

Me: Cor say for certain it wasn't, mind.

Our kid: Yampy, you are, proper saft.

Me: Keep out the oss road then, I'm off, I've had enough of standing here telling it.
