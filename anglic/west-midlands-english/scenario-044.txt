Right then, bab, watch us do one and then it's yow.

I built the first pallet meself, bags corner over corner, not straight up the middle like a stack of bricks gone wrong. Donnies underneath, not round the sides, else it's coming off the fork the second yer tip it.

Me: See that, corner to corner. Now yow have a go.

The new one built it dead straight up, one on top the other, no stagger at all.

Me: Nah, that ay it, bab. Corner to corner, I just showed yer.

The new one: Sorry, which corner though, the front one?

Me: ...Any of 'em, long as it ay the same one twice running. Do it again.

Second go, same wrong way, just a different lean to it this time.

Me: How many times we gooin round the Wrekin over this? I showed yer twice now.

The new one: I am watching, honest, it just looks the same to me.

Me: It ay the same, look proper this time.

The new one: Sorry, sorry, I'll get it.

I took a breath, put the temper away where it belongs.

Me: Think of it like bricks, right, one row overlaps the join under it so it cor fall through the gaps. That's all it is.

Third go, the new one staggered every bag, squared the corners, donnies right underneath when they lifted.

Me: There y'are, bostin, that's it exactly.

The new one grinned like they'd won summat.

Me: Don't get yampy about it, it's only a pallet. Wrap that and get it on the rack, then it's teatime.

Me: Tara-a-bit, bab, see yer in the morning.
