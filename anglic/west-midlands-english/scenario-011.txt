Me: Putting me shoes on the table.
The gran: Ay ay, yo cor do that, bab, it's bad luck.
Me: Yampy. What's it do then?
The gran: Somebody in this ouse ends up blartin over a coffin, I ay jokin, believe it or not.
Me: What if I leave 'em anyway?
The gran: Then it's on yower yed. Tek 'em off, tap the wood twice, that undoes it.
Me: Tapped it. Happy?
The gran: Bostin. Donnies washed, tea's out.
