The foreman: Right, before we'm all too far down these and forget summat, tomorrow's job. Seven, ay it, up along the cut.

The mate: Seven? Yo'm jokin, ah, half seven's plenty early enough for anyone.

The foreman: It's seven, bab, I ain't sayin it twice more. In and out before the weather turns, if it does.

Our kid: Wo it rain though, they said dinner time on the telly.

The foreman: It wo matter what they said. We'm getting the footings down early just in case, saft not to.

The young one: So who's on what then.

The foreman: Mixer's in the van already. Yo fetch the ladders and the drill. Our kid's on the wheelbarrows, and the young un nips to the yard for the extra sand, it ay far off the site.

Our kid: And the diggin, who's on that, cause that ground's hard as owt down there.

The foreman: That'll be yow, our kid, seein as yo'm the biggest lookin one of us.

Our kid: Yampy, that's all I am, lumbered again. I'll be blartin by dinner time, you watch.

The mate: Get yer donnies washed early then, save doin it twice before tea.

The young one: We'm a bit short on bricks an all, noticed Friday. Might have to go round by the island for more, don't go all round the Wrekin gettin there mind.

The foreman: We'll manage. Right, headcount, quick, who's in.

The mate: Me.

Our kid: Me, worse luck.

The young one: Me an all.

The foreman: What about the other one then, he in?

The mate: Nah. He day text till just now, sayin he cor make it, summat with his mom.

The foreman: That's one less then. We'll manage between the four of us.

Our kid: So who owes who now, I'm doin his diggin an mine both.

The foreman: Yow owe me a pint from Tuesday still, so we'm callin it square.

Our kid: Bostin logic, that.

The foreman: Seven then, don't none of yer be late.

The mate: Right y'are. Tara-a-bit.

The young one: Keep out the oss road, all of yer.
