The eldest: Right bab, before the others get here I'm tellin yer how we play it proper, so yow ay caught out.

The younger one: I ay stupid, I'll get it.

The eldest: Never said yer was. Right, first off we'm needin four at least to start, else it ay no cop, just two on yer runnin round like a couple o' saft aids. Soon as our kid from next door and the two off the top entry get here, we're off.

The younger one: Where's the boundary then?

The eldest: One side's the entry by the outdoor, don't go past that. Other side's the cut, yow dae go over the wall by the cut, that's out. Top's the islands, near the oss road, and mom'll have my guts if any on us goes near there. Bottom's the fence by the gambol, and the woman two doors down, her'll come out shoutin if we're on her side, so mind that an' all.

The younger one: What if I get chased and I go over one o' them lines?

The eldest: Then it's like yow'd been tagged anyway, stood still where yer crossed it, chaser's got yer fair and square. It wo right to just leg it out the game to save yerself, that's cheatin', bab, simple as that.

The younger one: Who's chaser first though?

The eldest: Dip dip dip on it when the others come. First go it's me chasing, cos yow ay never played before.

The younger one: What's a proper tag then?

The eldest: Two fingers, on the back, gentle, not a shove. Not grabbin yer jumper neither, just a tap and that's it, done. And yow cor say I day tagged when yer was, that's the worst thing yer can do.

The younger one: And once I'm tagged?

The eldest: Yow freeze. Stood stiff, can't move a donny, till someone still runnin' taps yer loose. Don't go all round the Wrekin gettin back to us either, straight there. It's bostin once yer get the hang on it.

The younger one: How's it finish then?

The eldest: Round's done when everyone's frozen. Last one still loose is chaser next go. Now don't fret, first go don't count against yer if summat goes wrong, I ay havin yer blartin over it. Go wash yer donnies, mom's callin tea. Keep out the oss road gettin back over. Tara-a-bit.
