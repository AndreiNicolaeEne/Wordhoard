I swear it on the cut, bab: yer donnies'll never be empty, every day'll be bostin, and them as loves yer will an' all. That's swore, not wished.

Mark the turn now, cause I swear the other an' all: if yer break faith, it wo never go right, every day'll be yampy, and yer own blartin the only company yer get.

Both swore. Both'll land.
