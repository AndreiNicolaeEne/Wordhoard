Relative: Ey up bab, yer wo believe how worried I got when yer day answer earlier. How bad is it, straight up, none of yer usual playing it down.

Me: It ay too clever if I'm honest. Started three day ago now, achin in every joint and me throat gone all scratchy. Then yesterday a fever come on top of it an all.

Relative: Oh bab. And how yer feeling today then?

Me: Today's a bit easier than yesterday, I wo lie, but it ay right yet. Not by a long chalk.

Relative: What yer been doin for it?

Me: Gargled warm salt water first thing, for me throat. Fever day break on its own after a bit so I put a cool cloth on me head, kept changin it right through the night.

Relative: Yow eaten owt at all?

Me: Just broth and a weak cup of tea, that's me lot. Sleepin whenever me body lets me, which ay as often as I'd like.

Relative: Yer wanna rest proper now, bab, not go pushin through it like yow normally do.

Me: I know, I know. I'm stoppin in bed today, promise yer. If the fever climbs any further, or me breathin goes hard on us, I'll get meself seen. Not before that though.

Relative: Yer sure? I could bring yer a bit of soup over, or come and sit with yer for an hour, keep yer company.

Me: Go on then, bring us the soup if yer passing. Yo cor sit here all afternoon though, I'll only be flat out asleep, yer'll be talkin to yerself in the end.

Relative: Alright bab. I'll pop round after, just for the soup then, and I'll leave yer to it. Yow just rest, dow go doin nothing daft like getting up for owt.

Me: Tara-a-bit then. And ta, means a lot that.
