Hush now, bab, hush now, no need for all that blartin,
I ay gooin nowhere, not down the entry, not the outdoor neither,
I'll sit here till the light comes, warm agen yer back.
See how the moon sits low on the cut, still and quiet as owt,
that same owd moon'll still be there come mornin, so shut yer eyes, bab.
Hush now, bab, hush now, the moon's still watchin,
sleep now, my little bab, don't yer fret.
