Now then, our kid,

I'm sat here of a Sunday night finally putting pen to paper and I ay proud of how long it's took me, I'll say that up front. Months and months I've been meaning to, and every time I got the paper out something come up, or I just... day. There it is. No excuses left, so here's the whole of it, the way I'd tell yo if yo were sat across the table with a brew going cold in front of yo.

House first, cos I know that's what yo'll ask before anything else. The back step's done at last, the fella come in the spring and sorted the crack that's been there since before yo went, and it's solid as owt now, no more of that wobble that used to catch yo out in the dark. Course, no sooner is one thing mended than another goes, and the fence panel's rotted clean through where the water's been getting at it. I've had a tarp over it since the last lot of rain and I keep meaning to get somebody round, so if yo know anybody, say the word. Round the front we've put up a proper washing line at last, the rotary sort, and I've got runner beans going up the fence at the back where yo used to have your bike propped. They'm coming along bostin, actually, better than I expected for a first go.

The big change is the dad. Yo'll remember him going on for years about that place shutting one day, well it finally happened back in the winter, the whole floor laid off inside a fortnight. I thought he'd go yampy sat about the house all day but he wo having none of that, he's took on driving for the depot down by the cut now, three early starts a week dodging the islands on the ring road, and he's happier for it if yo can believe that, out and about instead of stuck on a line. Took him a good few weeks of moping about it first mind, but he's settled now.

And no, before yo ask, the tin's still empty. Don't even.

Neighbours-wise there's been comings and goings. The old fella two doors down, the one who used to shout at us over the entry for footballs going over his fence, he's gone into a home now, his daughter sorted it after his fall back end of last year, and there's a young family moved in his place, two little ones who are already terrorising the street on scooters, one of em did a gambol clean across our lawn last week and near took the beans out with him. Down the other way, the one whose family's always been in and out, her eldest's had a babby now, a little wench, so there's a pram outside most mornings and everyone's making a fuss.

Two people keep asking after yo without fail. The one at the counter in the outdoor, every time I'm in for the dad's cans her says "any word from yower kid yet?" like clockwork, and her workmate from the depot, met her once at a do and she still remembers yo from that, asks the dad regular. I tell them what I know, which ay much, so yo'll have to write us summat proper to pass on.

I'll tell yo the thing I wouldn't say to yower face. I still go into your old room some Sundays and just sit on the end of the bed a minute before I go back down. I ay never told the mom that, and I ay telling her now either, so keep it between us. It ay sad exactly, just. I dunno. It's there.

The mom's alright though, don't worry, her keeps herself busy. Her come in the kitchen the other week while I was on the phone moaning about summat and stood there till I hung up.

Mom: Who was that then?

Me: No one. Just moaning about work.

Mom: Yo'm always moaning about summat. Get yower donnies washed, tea's on in five.

Same as ever, her.

Summer's been a funny one here, hot as anything through June, the grass all gone that yampy brown colour and half the street ignoring the hosepipe ban anyway, then it broke into storms the last fortnight and the cut nearly come up over the towpath by the old bridge. The garden took a battering but the beans held on, don't ask me how.

Right, questions, cos I want proper answers this time and not two lines dashed off between shifts. What's the food like out there, anything yo've took a liking to that we wouldn't even recognise? Are yo still walking to work or has that changed now yo've moved digs? And have yo made anybody yo'd call a proper mate yet, or is it still early days? Yo never say enough about any of it and I'm nosy, yo know that.

The eldest asked after yo again the other day and all, wanted to know when yo'm coming back for a visit, so there's another one to add to the list of people badgering me for news.

I'll say this and yo can hold me to it same as every time. I'm sending them photos I keep going on about, the ones from the garden this summer and the do we had for the dad's leaving present from the old place, proper prints this time, not just talk. I know I've said it before and it ay happened yet but I mean it this time, bab, I'll get down the shop for envelopes this week.

Anyway I've gone on for pages now and me hand's aching, so I'll leave it there before I start blartin' at me own kitchen table like a saft article. Look after yowerself out there, don't go all round the Wrekin getting home if there's a quicker way, and keep out the oss road.

Tara-a-bit for now, our kid.

Your sister
