Me: Yow hear that racket last night, bab? Summat went over, out in the street, gone midnight.

The neighbor: I day catch what it were. Thought a fox at the bins, but I ay sure, it wo sound right.

Me: Could've bin a cat, yampy this time o' year, or some saft sod stumbling home.

The neighbor: Dunno. Cor be the wind, still out. The one bang, then nothing.

Me: If it goes again tonight, gi's a shout, I'll keep me ear out.

The neighbor: Aye. Tara-a-bit then.
