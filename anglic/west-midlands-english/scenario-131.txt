The eldest: Bab, it's chuckin it down!

Mother: Ar, about time an' all, it ay stopped moanin for weeks.

Our kid: Come and look, proper pelting now.

Father: Bostin, this is, I could stop out here blartin with joy, I could.

Mother: Yow'll catch your death, yampy aporth.

Our kid: Nah, come on, out the both of yer.

And they all stood there on the wet lawn, donnies out, saying nowt, just listening to it fall.
