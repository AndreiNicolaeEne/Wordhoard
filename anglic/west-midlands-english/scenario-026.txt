Our kid, I've got to tell yer what's gone on, me and the wife have had a right go at each other.

It was never about nothing really, bab, it was the leccy bill. Her'd gone and switched us onto some new tariff without saying a word, and it come out sixty quid dearer than what we had. I clocked it on the app first, and I brung it up over tea, nothing more than:

Me: Why's the leccy gone up again then.

The wife: Cos I sorted it out, seeing as yow never sort nothing.

Me: I day say nothing about that.

The wife: Yo cor even look at a bill without moaning about it.

And then, out of nowhere, we went all round the Wrekin and ended up back at the car I bought off that fella down the cut three year gone, the one that packed in after a fortnight. Had nothing to do with the leccy, our kid, nothing, but out it come again, like it always does when her's losing.

The wife: At least I ay never wasted money on a heap of scrap like that.

Me: You're as saft as a brush and always have bin.

That were the sharpest of it, and it was me that said it, I'll own that much. Her went quiet after, no blartin, no shouting, just sat in the front room on her own. Two days it lasted, our kid, not a word between us, donnies not even washed together at the sink of a night like normal.

Come Sunday her put the kettle on and said sorry for dragging the car in, said it wasn't fair of her. I said sorry an' all for the saft comment. Her backed down first, though I weren't far behind her.

My verdict, our kid, between me and yow: her started it wrong and I finished it worse, but we'm bostin now and it wo happen again. Tara-a-bit.
