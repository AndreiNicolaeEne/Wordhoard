Me: Bab, you got a minute? I'm proper fed up with this roof of mine.

My neighbour: Go on then, what's it doing.

Me: It's that corner over the back bedroom, right above where our kid's bed is. Coming through summat rotten every time it rains, running down the chimney breast and all down the wall. I've asked the landlord about it twice now and nothing's been done, not a thing.

My neighbour: What'd he say the first time you told him.

Me: Said he'd get a bloke round to have a look, sort it within the week. Week come and gone, nowt happened.

My neighbour: And the second time.

Me: Same again. Word for word an' all, "I'll get someone round." He's gone all round the Wrekin with it instead of just sending a roofer, honest.

My neighbour: How long's this been going on then, from when you first said summat.

Me: Since back end of last year, so five, six month now. Wallpaper's all black and bubbled up in the corner, it ay gonna hold much longer.

My neighbour: Right, well yow got options, bab. Yow could put it in writing so he cor say he never knew. Or ring the council, get an inspector round, only that can rile a landlord and he might try and shift yow out instead. Or yow stop the rent till it's fixed, but that's a risk, that, he'll say arrears and try and chuck yow out proper.

Me: That's what I'm frit of, ending up out on the street over a bit of damp.

My neighbour: I had the very same with my last one, roof over the kitchen, blartin' I was some nights, buckets out everywhere. He kept saying, kept saying, never sent no one. Took the council coming round before he moved an inch.

Me: So what am I best doing.

My neighbour: Get it in writing today, give him a date on it. Nothing done by then, ring the council. Don't stop paying yet, whatever yow do.

Me: Bostin, ta bab, I'll get onto it now.

My neighbour: Keep out the oss road, love.
