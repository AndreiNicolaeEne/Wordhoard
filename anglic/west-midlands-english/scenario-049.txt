Been owed forty quid off my mate since back end of last year, and I'd let it ride long enough. Brung it up gentle at first, over a brew.

Me: Not being funny, bab, but that forty yow had off me before Christmas, that's still outstanding, yer know. Gone eight month now.

My mate: Ar, I know, I know. It's just been mad busy, what with one thing and another. I ay forgot, honest.

Me: Yow said that last time an' all. Said yow'd have it to me by the bostin do at our kid's, remember. That's come and gone.

My mate: Look, it ay like I'm dodging yer. Things have just been tight, that's all it is.

Me: I ay saying yow're dodging me, I'm saying it's been eight month of "next week" and next week never comes. That's my money, not yowers to sit on. We'm supposed to be mates.

My mate: Bloody hell, don't be saft, yer going on like I've robbed yer. I said I'll sort it, ay I.

Me: I ay going on, I'm asking. There's a difference. Yo cor keep saying it wo be long and then it is long. Yer going all round the Wrekin instead of just telling me straight.

My mate: Alright, alright, no need for that. Here, I've got twenty on me now, that do for a start? Rest by the end of the month, I swear down.

Me: Go on then. But I'll believe the rest when it's in me hand, not before. Me mom always says a borrower is a bad payer till proved otherwise, and I've heard "end of the month" off yow before an' all.

My mate: Fair enough. I'll get it to yer.

Me: Tara-a-bit, then.
