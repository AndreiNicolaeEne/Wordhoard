We was stood by the cut waiting on the bus when it kicked off again over which stall does the best scran.

Me: I'm telling yer, bab, the one by the market is bostin. Yo cor beat them chips, crispy on the outside and soft as owt inside.

My mate: Gerroff, that place ay all that. It's the one down the entry past the butcher's what's the best, yow know it is. Her behind the counter puts proper gravy on an' all, none of that watery muck like yowers.

Me: Watery muck? I day have watery muck last time, it was lush. But I tell yer what wo lush, when I got a burger off yower stall and it was stone cold in the middle. Nearly blartin I was, stood there twenty minutes waiting on summat raw.

My mate: That's one time though, one time in about a hundred. Yo'm going on like it happens every wik.

Me: It ay just the once neither, our kid had the same off there, said it near enough put him in the hospital, poor sod.

My mate: Yampy, that's what you are, proper yampy. Their chips am saft as anything anyroad, no bite to em at all.

Me: Saft? Yo wanna taste your own then, going all round the Wrekin just to avoid saying mine's better.

My mate: Yo'm the one that's daft. Honest to god, best stall in the whole market and it ay either of ours, it's the one by the island what does the cobs. Nobody, and I mean nobody, has ever said a bad word about them cobs in their life.

Me: ...Yeah, all right, I'll give yer that one.

My mate: Right then. We'm going both again tomorrer, yours first then mine, sorting this out proper this time.

Me: Bab, yo'm on. Tara-a-bit then, see yer in the morning.

My mate: Tara-a-bit, keep out the oss road.
