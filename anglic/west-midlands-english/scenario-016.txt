They'd stopped by the cut on the way back, the water flat and browner than tea, and he'd gone quiet the whole way past the entry, which wasn't like him at all.

The lad: I've got summat to say and I ay gonna dance round it no more. I day think I'd ever get the bottle to say it, but here goes. I want more than this, more than just mates grabbing a pint after work. I've thought on it long enough. I like yer, proper like, and I want to be with yer.

He said the whole of it and her let him, never once cut across, just stood there with her arms folded watching the water go past the lock.

The lass: Right then. Ta for saying it to me face like that, straight up, not everyone's got the bottle for that. And I ay gonna mess yer about neither. It's a no, bab. I ay saying that to be unkind, it's just how it is, and it wo go different, not in a month, not in a year, so don't yo sit waiting on it.

The lad: Fair enough.

The lass: And it ay cause there's owt wrong with yer, dow think that for a second. Yo'm one of the kindest fellas I know. The way yo sat with me mom at the hospital when I day get theer meself, that were bostin of yer, that were. It's just the fit ay theer for me, not the way yo mean it, and I ay saft enough to force summat that ay theer.

The lad: I get it.

The lass: I still want yer about though, proper mates, same as before. None of this going all round the Wrekin to dodge us at the outdoor or nothing daft like that. Yo cor lose me that easy.

He gave a nod and they walked on past the islands, quiet again, but not the bad sort of quiet.

The lass: Tara-a-bit, bab. Look after yerself.
