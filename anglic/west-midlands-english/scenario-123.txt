Me: Go on then, get that brew down yer, I'm proper away with the fairies this mornin.

Our kid: Yow ay half quiet, bab, what's up with yer.

Me: Dunno, I ay bin able to shift it since I woke up. This dream. It's sat on me chest all mornin like a wet coat.

Our kid: Go on then, what was it.

Me: It started dead ordinary like. Just walkin home from work, down our road, nothin funny about it at first, just walkin, past the entry, past the outdoor, past the islands by the shops. Only the road just kept goin. And goin. I'm walkin and walkin thinkin it'll be round the next bend, it'll be round the next, and it wo come, our road just wo end. Felt like I'd gone all round the Wrekin and back twice over and I still ay home.

Our kid: That's proper yampy that is.

Me: I know it. And then eventually, dunno how, I'm outside our house. Only when I'm in it, there's a room in there that ay never bin there, not in me whole life. Off the kitchen, where the pantry ought to be.

Our kid: What sort of room though, go on, what was in it.

Me: That's the thing, I cor tell yer what sort, just a room, and our mom's sat in there.

Our kid: What? Her's sat there??

Me: Calm as anythin, bab. Her's just sat there like nothin's ever happened, like no time's gone by at all, and her's talkin to me. Just nattering away like it's a Tuesday.

Our kid: What was her sayin then.

Me: That's the thing, I cor remember. I've laid there this mornin tryin to catch it before it went, but it's like tryin to hold water in yer donnies, it just runs straight through yer.

Our kid: Ar that's proper unsettlin that is, hearin her talk and then it's just took off yer like that.

Me: I know. I felt like I'd bin told summat important an all, summat I needed to know, like when we was little and her'd tell yer wash yer donnies before tea, summat ordinary like that, just an ordinary thing said in an ordinary voice, only I cor get the actual words back for love nor money.

Our kid: Try again, go on. Was it summat about yow, or about one of us, or what.

Me: No, honest, I've tried it about three times since I woke and it wo come. I'm gonna leave it now, it's doin me yed in.

Our kid: Fair enough, bab. What happened then.

Me: Well then it all shifted, like dreams do, no rhyme nor reason to it, and I'm lookin for summat. All frantic like. Tearin the house apart, in the cupboards, under the bed, out the back by the cut, in the entry even, everywhere yow could think of.

Our kid: What was yer lookin for?

Me: I cor tell yer that either, and that's the maddest bit of the whole thing, I didn't know in the dream itself. I just knew I had to find it, whatever it was, and it wo turnin up nowhere, and the more I looked the worse it got.

Our kid: Bloody hell.

Me: Me heart was goin like the clappers, proper panickin by the end, lookin in the same places twice over, in me own pockets even, and it just kept climbin and climbin till it woke me up with it.

Our kid: Yer sat bolt upright?

Me: More or less. Laid there a good minute an all after, not movin, not sure which one was real, the room with our mom in it or this one here with the curtains and the alarm goin off.

Our kid: Ar that'll do that to yer, that will.

Me: It did. Proper shook me up, that.

Our kid: What d'yer reckon it means then, her turnin up like that, calm as yer like, after all this time.

Me: Dunno, bab. Me nan always used to say if they come back easy like that, all calm and nattering away, it means they'm alright, wherever they am.

Our kid: Or it just means yow had cheese on toast too late last night.

Me: Ar, probably that an all if I'm honest.

Our kid: Her'd have summat to say about that dream herself, mind. Her'd tell yer it's yer conscience, or yer nerves, or summat off the telly yer watched and forgot.

Me: Her would an all. Her'd have an answer for everythin, that woman, whether her knew or not.

Our kid: Well yow ay gonna work it out sat here starin in yer tea, are yer.

Me: No, I ay. I'll just have to carry it round with me today like a stone in me shoe.

Our kid: Yer gunna be alright to gu in?

Me: Ar, I'll be bostin once this brew's in me. Just one of them mornings, that's all it is.

Our kid: Well don't gu blartin over it at work, they'll think yowm properly saft.

Me: Cheeky mare. Right, I ay got time to sit here nattering all day, I've a bus to catch and yow've got the school run.

Our kid: Ar go on then. Yer gunna tell me if yer dream it again?

Me: Course I will. Might get the words back next time and all, who knows.

Our kid: Keep out the oss road, bab.

Me: Tara-a-bit.
