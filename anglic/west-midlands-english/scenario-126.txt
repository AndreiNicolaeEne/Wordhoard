Me: I'm telling yer straight, bab, it happened just as I said, and I ay making a word of it up.

My mate: Yow was half asleep on the sofa, that's all that was. Yow always drops off after tea.

Me: I day. I was wide awake, thinking as clear as I'm thinking now. I'd only just washed me donnies and sat down, kids had stopped gambolling on the lawn, our kid'd gone up, and I was watching the telly with the sound down low.

My mate: Go on then, what happened.

Me: I got up to shut the back door and there's summat stood right at the end of the entry. Dead still. Just looking up the yard at me. I stood there a good minute and it never moved, never made a sound.

My mate: That'll have been her next door's coat, blown off the line against the wall.

Me: Wo have been. There ay no line up our entry, never has been, her hangs out the front same as we do.

My mate: Shadow off a car then, headlights coming round the island.

Me: The island's the other end, yow'd have to go all round the Wrekin to get light down our entry from there. And headlights don't stand still, do they, this just stood there looking at me and then it was gone, like somebody'd switched a light off.

My mate: I'm only saying, bab, there's always summat ordinary behind it.

Me: There ay nothing ordinary behind this one. I was near enough blartin telling me mom about it after. I know what I saw and I'm not yampy and I'm not saft.

My mate: I still don't believe yer.

Me: I swear on our kid's life, every word of it's true.

My mate: I believe yow believe it, bab. I still don't.

Me: Keep out the oss road then, tara-a-bit, I'm saying no more about it tonight.
