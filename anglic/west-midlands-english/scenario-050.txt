Cradle for sale, bab. Bostin little thing, this - two of mine slept in her a good four year between 'em, only reason we're parting is they've outgrown her. Honest with yer, one rail cracked a while since, got it mended proper, solid as owt now. Fifteen quid, no less. Come and see her - past the entry by the cut - or come and fetch her yerself, whichever yer like. Tara-a-bit.
