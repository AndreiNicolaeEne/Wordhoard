I call everybody down that Sunday, tell them come for dinner, no need bring anything, just come. My mother, my auntie, my eldest brother, my second brother, all sit round the table already, plates still got a bit of rice, but nobody eating anymore. They can feel something coming lor, I never even open mouth yet they all quiet like that, sit there listening, nobody wants to be the one to talk first.

I put down my cup. I say, okay, I gather everybody today because got something I sit on for very long already, must say now, cannot anymore.

Everybody just look at me. My mother put her chopsticks down slow slow.

Me: You all remember that time, the money for Ah Ma's operation, the whole account kena wipe out overnight. You all remember how big the fight was.

My eldest brother's face change one kind already, he know where this is going.

Me: That time everybody say is him. Say he the one manage the passbook, say he the one always short of money, say definitely he take. Pa never even ask him properly, straight away scold until the whole block can hear. After that whole family treat him like that, like he owe everybody something. Even now, some of you all still not 100% forgive him inside, I know one.

My mother: (voice already shaking) So what you want to say.

Me: It wasn't him. Whole time it wasn't him. It's second brother.

The room go quiet like that, the kind of quiet where even the fan sound very loud. My second brother look down at his plate, don't dare look up.

Me: I never guess one. I saw. That time I still small, hiding under the void deck play catching, I saw him go and meet that Ah Long runner, pass the money bag straight to the guy. I saw with my own two eyes. Later on he also tell me himself, say don't tell anybody, he owe people money, if never pay somebody going to come chop his hand one. He beg me keep quiet.

The auntie: (leaning forward) All these years you know and you never say?

Me: I know lah. I know it's wrong of me. But that time everybody's anger already so big, house like going to blow up, I scared if I say more, worse things happen. And second brother, he already suffering enough that time, loan shark people paint our door red already, throw paint at the letterbox, you all remember or not. I think if I say is him, maybe worse thing happen to him. I also thought, wait, let the anger die down first, then I tell. Then it never die down, it just... stay there, follow eldest brother like that.

My eldest brother say nothing, just staring at the table.

Me: Actually one time I almost tell already. Ah Ma's funeral, remember, everybody standing round like that, second brother crying very hard, I almost open mouth right there. I stop myself last minute. I think, aiyo, not the time, cannot make things worse at a funeral what. So I swallow it back down again.

Me: But I tell you all, carrying this thing so many years, very jialat one. Every festival, every reunion dinner, I watch eldest brother sit at the corner, everybody still throw him funny look one, still whisper behind his back one, and I know the whole time it's not even him. I cannot say how many nights I lie there thinking should I say or not say. Buay tahan already, that's why I call you all today.

My mother just sit there, mouth half open, shaking her head slow slow, like cannot believe.

Me: Ma, I know your face like that, like don't believe. But I telling you straight, I sure one. I saw it happen with my own eyes, and he confirm it to me himself after that. Not guessing, not maybe. Sure.

The auntie start to open her mouth, I can see the old anger climbing up her face already, the kind where she going to start saying things like "so all these years we treat him like family for nothing ah" about second brother, so I cut in first.

Me: I know what you thinking, auntie. Don't start blaming second brother like how we blame eldest brother last time, don't repeat the same thing. Let everybody just sit with it first.

Eldest brother finally look up. His face very hard to describe, one kind of relief, like finally somebody take the weight off him after so many years, but also, I can see, a lot of fresh anger climbing up too.

The eldest brother: So all these years... all these years everybody call me the one who cannot be trusted, the one who spoil the family, and the whole time you all knew, or she knew, and nobody say anything. You all know how many years I sit at reunion dinner and people don't even want to sit next to me? How many matchmaking aunty hear the story and run away already?

He look at second brother direct.

The eldest brother: And you. All these years you let me carry your shame. You let me.

Second brother don't say anything at first, just sit there, hands shaking a bit, eyes red already.

The second brother: (finally, very small voice) I know. I know what I did. That time I so scared, I think if everybody blame you, at least they don't come after me, the loan shark people don't come after our whole family so bad. I tell myself I will confess when things settle, but things never settle, then more years pass, then I too paiseh already, cannot bring myself to say. I sorry. I really sorry.

The auntie's anger, the one I cut off just now, come back up again, but this time pointed the right direction finally.

The auntie: You sorry? Sorry for what, for taking the money, or sorry for letting your own brother suffer for it for years? Wah lau, how could you.

My mother start crying, not loud, just tears coming down, she keep saying, "why you all never tell me, why you all never tell me," over and over.

Me: Ma, I sorry too. I the one who kept quiet longest. I not making excuse, I know I should have said long ago, I just scared of what will happen, scared it will make things worse, scared people will say why you wait so long, and now I saying it and people still asking why I wait so long, so. I owe you all this, I know. I owe eldest brother the most.

Nobody say anything for a long while after that. The rice getting cold on the table, nobody eat anymore.

Things not settled that night, I telling you straight, not tidy like that, no hugging and everybody suddenly okay already. Eldest brother still very angry, rightfully angry, at second brother and a bit at me too. Second brother just sit there small small, letting the anger come at him finally, like he owe it to everybody after avoiding it so long. My mother just keep crying, my auntie keep muttering under her breath. But at least now, the anger sitting in the right place. At least now we are not standing around the story anymore, we standing on what actually happened. Not fixed, not clean, but true. That's the only thing I can say for it.
