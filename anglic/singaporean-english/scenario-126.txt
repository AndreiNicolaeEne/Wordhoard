Me: I was cutting through the void deck, going back to the lift lobby, must be about two plus in the morning already. Suddenly somebody call my name from behind, very clear, use the name only my grandmother call me one, nobody else call me like that. I turn around, corridor empty, not one soul. Then the motion light, one by one, start switching on, coming towards me, like something walking down the corridor slowly.

My friend: Wah lau, must be a cat lah. Those sensor light so sensitive, cat walk past also can trigger one.

Me: Where got. That light strip pitched quite high one, for human height, not for cat level. And cat trigger one light then stop already, this one switch on in one straight line, one after another, same pace like footsteps coming closer and closer. I stood there watching it come, whole body cannot move.

My friend: Okay lor, maybe somebody from the next block also shout the same name, echo carry over that time of night, you just heard wrongly lah.

Me: Aiyo, I say already, that name, only my grandmother call me that, since I was small until she pass away. I never tell anybody, not even you all, not once. Who else got to shout that one.

My friend: ...

Me: I swear on my grandmother's photo at the altar, every single word true. I did not half asleep, I did not imagine, I was thinking clear clear the whole time. That night was real, I tell you.

My friend: Ya ya, I hear you already, but I still don't believe leh. Cannot be one what.
