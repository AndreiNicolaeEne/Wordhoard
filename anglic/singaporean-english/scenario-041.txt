Two years plus I was there ah, you know. Two years two months to be exact, I still remember because my confirmation letter got the date. Everybody always ask me why I stay so long if I never happy, and I also cannot answer properly, just drag drag lah, one more month, one more project, then before you know it two years liao. Not that everyday also so bad one, some days okay what, got teamlunch, got the pantry got free kopi-o, but the whole thing slowly slowly become sian, until sian become buay tahan.

The final straw very small one, that's the funny part. Not the big things that should have pushed me, like the time they never give me the increment they promised, or the time I got scolded in front of everybody for something the other department screw up. That kind of thing I tahan already, tahan until numb. The one that finally push me was so stupid. Friday evening, five forty five, everybody already tapau-ing their dinner order in the group chat, and the boss suddenly message me, "can help cover tomorrow's shift, the other one MC." Saturday sia. My off day, already got plan with my family, small thing, just going to visit my mother, but planned already what. I never even reply immediately, just stare at the phone. And I thought to myself, wah lau, every time also me one. Every single time. That was it. That night I couldn't sleep, kept thinking, this cannot go on like this anymore.

So the whole week after that I was rehearsing what to say lah, in my head, in the mirror even, paiseh to admit but true. I practise until it sound very smooth, very professional, no emotion one, the kind of thing you say in the movie. Something like, "thank you so much for the opportunity, I've learnt a lot, but I feel it's time for me to move on and explore other paths." Very safe. Very atas sounding for someone like me who normally just talk normal. I rehearse it walking to the MRT, rehearse it in the lift, rehearse it until my own workmate ask me why I keep talking to myself like that, and I just laugh, say never mind lah, thinking out loud only.

Monday morning I go find the HR lady first, ask can I speak to the boss, got something to discuss. She look at me a bit funny, maybe she guess already, but she just say okay, will arrange. Kancheong the whole morning, heart beating like that, cannot focus on work at all, keep checking the time.

Afternoon, finally called in. Sit down, the boss look up from the laptop, ask what's up. I take a breath, and I say my line, the one I practise until liao already:

Me: It's not really about one thing in particular, but I've been thinking about it for a while, and I think it's time for me to move on.

Which, honestly, was only half true. It WAS about one thing, more or less, that Saturday shift thing and the whole pile before it, but I never want to say that out loud. The real reason I held back was simple. I still need this person to give me a good reference next time, and also, paiseh what, if I start listing out every grievance I sure will cry one, and I never want to cry in front of the boss. So I keep it general, keep it polite, keep it safe.

The reaction, quite surprised actually, eyebrows go up like that.

The boss: Oh. I didn't see this coming. Is everything okay?

Me: Yah, everything's fine, just personal reasons.

Then the counter offer came, quite fast also.

The boss: If it's about the pay, we can talk. I can push for an increment for you, maybe even look at your title. Or if the hours are the issue we can look at adjusting your shifts.

I sat there for a moment, thinking, wah, now you want to talk about my shifts, now. But I just smiled and shook my head.

Me: It's not really about the money, I really appreciate the offer, but I think I just need a change of environment at this point in my life.

Where got true one, that answer, but what to do, cannot say "actually it's because you make me work every off day and never once say thank you," right. So diplomatic version only.

We agreed on two weeks notice, standard what, everybody know the rule. Handshake, small smile, done deal. But that same evening, walking to the bus stop, I suddenly got this doubt hit me out of nowhere. Like, wah, did I do the right thing ah? Two years, I got my routine already, I know where everything is, I know all the shortcuts, the auntie at the coffeeshop downstairs already know my order without me saying. New place, new people, must start all over again, must prove myself all over again. Very kancheong feeling, almost wanted to gostan and tell them I changed my mind. But I never. Just chin chai, let it be, already said already.

The two weeks pass quite fast, weirdly. Handover this, handover that, teaching the new person, wait, not even new person yet, they never manage to hire anybody in time, so I just leave a lot of notes, hope for the best lah.

Last day, everybody very nice on the surface, got small celebration even, a cake from the kopitiam downstairs, teh-c peng for everyone, some cards, the usual stuff. But honestly a lot of it felt quite hollow, you know, the kind of goodbye where people say "we must keep in touch ah" and both of you already know, where got, probably never see each other again already. Everybody very sweet in the moment then quiet after.

Except one part. Near the end of the day, my closest workmate, the one who always jio me for lunch, pulled me aside near the pantry, very quiet corner, and she just look at me straight, no more sweet talk already.

My workmate: Eh, seriously ah, you leaving because of that Saturday thing right. Don't have to be paiseh, I saw your face that day.

I nearly, nearly said it all out loud right there, the whole real story, how sian I was, how I felt used, how one small request just broke the camel back after two years of small requests. My mouth even opened. But somehow what came out instead was just...

Me: Aiyo, don't say already lah, past already, don't want to dwell on it also.

And she just nodded, like she catch my meaning without me saying more, she also not blur one. That was the closest the whole day came to anybody saying the true thing, and even that was half swallowed back down.

Since then ah, it's been a few months already, and I can tell you honestly, relief is winning by a lot, maybe eighty-twenty like that. No more jumping when the phone buzz on a Saturday. No more that heavy feeling walking in on Monday. Got some regret small small, mostly for the routine I lost, the familiar auntie, the shortcuts, some of the people I did genuinely like. But the big thing, the freedom feeling, that one is real, not just talk.

If you ask me now, sitting here, would I do it again, die die must try lah, that kind of thing you must try once in your life, to stand up and say enough already. Maybe next time I won't rehearse so much, maybe next time I just say the true thing straight, no need so atas, no need so careful. But heng also I did it the way I did, because at least it ended peaceful, no drama, no fight, just quiet and clean. Suay would have been if it turned into a big scene, and it never. So overall lah, no regrets one, glad I finally spoke up, even if half the words I said that day wasn't the real words in my heart.
