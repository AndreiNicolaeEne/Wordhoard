We four sit down, me, the neighbour, my cousin, and the auntie from downstairs, at the round table under the block, tissue packet already chope the table from six o'clock, kopi-o for the auntie, teh-c peng for my cousin, before anybody even touch the deck. Stakes we settle first, ten cents a point only, small money, chicken feed, nobody going to cry over it, everybody nod already then we start.

First few hands very ordinary one. I catch no ball first two hands, still warming up, don't really know who got what. Auntie win a bit hand one, my cousin win a bit hand two, neighbour so-so, I also so-so. Chin chai, agak agak, everybody just feeling out the table, nobody clearly ahead. Hand three I nearly kena but manage to escape, heng ah. Very normal night so far, the kind where you play until got somebody's stomach growl then go tapao something eat.

Then hand four, the neighbour start to win. Okay can, luck like that sometimes. Hand five, win again. Hand six, win again. By hand seven, four hands running already, and you know when somebody win like that non-stop, the table go quiet a bit, everybody start watching the hands more carefully, check check the deck, look look at how the cards coming out.

My cousin very kiasu one, buay tahan already, say something about the shuffle looking too clean for hand seven, too smooth, like machine shuffle like that. Neighbour just laugh, say why you sabo me, luck is luck what, don't be like that. Everybody laugh too, brush past, continue play, don't want to spoil the mood over one small comment.

Hand eight, the neighbour deal again, and this time, when the cards coming out from the bottom side, the auntie eye very sharp one, she catch something, the way the last two cards leave the deck not the same as the rest. She never say anything first, just stare stare. Then she put down her cards and say it outright.

The auntie: Eh, you dealing from the bottom ah?

The neighbour: What nonsense you talking, where got.

The auntie: I saw leh, just now, the card slide out from the bottom side one, not from the top like normal.

The neighbour: Where got! You blur like sotong is it, cannot see properly then say I cheat.

My cousin: Actually now she mention, I also feel something off just now, the shuffle too clean for hand seven, and this one also.

Me: Wah lau, cannot be lah, we all friends, don't like that.

The neighbour: I never do anything, you all wrongly accuse me for what, so many hands already and now like this.

Then the whole table start to argue, some believe the auntie, some believe the neighbour say cannot be, and we go back over hand seven, hand six, even hand four, everybody trying to remember who dealt what, who won what, whether the shuffle look the same each round or not. Nobody can settle it, because nobody was watching properly before this, everybody too relaxed, too happy just playing, catch no ball what actually happened three hands ago already. My cousin insist he saw something, got lobang somewhere he says, the auntie stand her ground, but the neighbour never budge one bit, deny flat, no give at all, keep saying where got, where got, until everybody a bit sian of arguing already, and my cousin mutter wah jialat this one, cannot end nicely.

In the end nobody walk away lah, we all still want to play what, money already on the table, cannot gostan halfway through like that. So we continue. But the whole feel different already, not shiok like before, everybody a bit more careful, deal more slow-slow, cut the deck properly, some people even ask to cut twice, watch every hand leave the deck. Quietly kancheong the whole table, checking each other more than looking at their own cards.

Hand nine go by okay, I win that one, small only. Hand ten, my cousin finally win a bit, first time the whole night, everybody clap for him a bit paiseh style, trying to lighten the mood back up.

Then hand eleven, the last one, because the auntie say she need to go back up already, liao lah, getting late, tomorrow got work. Die die must play this last hand properly she says, don't want to end on a half. Final hand, everybody play it out, and guess who take it, the neighbour again. Same one everybody suspect. Aiyo, you imagine the face at the table when that happen, some people just shake head, don't even want to talk anymore, my cousin only laugh but the kind of laugh where nothing funny actually.

We tally up in the end, whole night eleven hands played altogether. When we count properly, the neighbour finish up by two points more than whoever second, the rest of us quite close to each other, but the neighbour clearly ahead the whole way through, especially from hand four onward, that streak never really got caught back up.

So la, we pack up, everybody go back their own flat, but I tell you, the mood not the same walking off. My cousin still muttering something not right under his breath, the auntie give one long look before she go up, the neighbour a bit huffy, feeling wrongly blamed. Suay night in that sense, whatever the truth is.

Nobody ever settle it, cannot settle also, because that night nobody catch it clean, no proof, just the auntie's eye and my cousin's feeling and one bad losing streak from everybody else. Getting a bit chim to even explain to new people why we keep bringing up one card game from so long ago. But the story never die, comes up every time, become our table joke already, except not really joke lah, everybody still half serious when they say it, and somebody always chope the good seat away from the neighbour after that, just in case.
