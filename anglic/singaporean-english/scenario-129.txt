That afternoon, still early, I saw the smoke first from my window, just one thin grey line at the horizon, so far still can watch one. I never think much of it leh, everybody outside the void deck also just looking, some uncle even bring out his coffee, chit chat like watching fireworks like that. My neighbour say, "Wah, so far away what, no need panic lah." I also think same, chin chai only, not our problem one.

But within one hour, wind change already, the smoke come nearer nearer, until my eyes start to sting, tearing non-stop, throat also itchy itchy. That time I realise, eh, this one not simple leh. The auntie downstairs shout up from her corridor, "Oi, better pack up already, don't wait until too late one!" That word "too late" like slap my face straight away, I kancheong spider on the spot.

Twenty minutes only, I grab whatever important. IC, some cash, phone charger, my mother's photo, that's all, the rest chin chai leave behind, no time to think so much. Buay tahan the heat already, pressing on my skin like that, throat raw raw, cough non-stop, eyes also cannot open properly. Neighbour ask, "You got take your important documents or not?" I say, "Got got, don't ask already, just run!"

On the way out I turn back for one glance, wah lau, the whole sky orange like that, glow so bright, I never see fire so close in my life before. Heart pounding, legs also weak weak, but must move, buay tahan to stand there anymore, no choice one.

Two nights I stay at my cousin's place, cannot sleep well, keep thinking got or never got already, house still there or not, don't know leh. Everybody also sian, don't know how to comfort me, just sit there with me. My friend jio me go makan, try to distract me a bit, but appetite also gone, eat also blur like sotong.

Third day, they say can go back already. I rush down, heart in mouth the whole way, but heng ah, the place spared, everything still there, never touch at all. Aiyo, that moment I nearly cry, so relieved, really heng lah, cannot ask for more already.
