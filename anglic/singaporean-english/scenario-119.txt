Wah, this one long story sia, but must tell you properly because you never see, so how you understand the whole thing.

Start like this. That night after makan, whole gang go down void deck, like every time also, macam that. Somebody chope the table already with tissue packet, then we sit, order kopi from the corner one, kopi-o for me, teh-c peng for the workmate, some uncle also tapao roti bakar to go with.

Then workmate bring out his chess set from the bag, ask who want to play. I just look look at the board first, then say can, why not, I very free tonight.

Me: "Play a game, want or not?"

The workmate: "Can."

I got warn myself before start, don't be too kiasu, play steady, but where got listen to own advice one. I say where got need stakes, we just play play only what, but you know how heartland crowd like that, once got two people sit down with a board, everybody gather come like free show already.

One uncle: "Eh, must have something on it what, otherwise no drama."

Another one: "Ya lah, just play play like that sian only, no fun."

I still blur at that point, say okay chin chai lah, small small can, ten dollar enough. Then the crowd start bidding up like auction, you never see anything like it in your life.

One of them: "Twenty better lah, must have some heart pain a bit, not shiok if too small."

The workmate: "Twenty can accept, but must be fair leh, no need until jialat for anybody."

One uncle: "Confirm ah, is it? Twenty, no changing halfway after this."

Everybody nod, twenty dollar locked just like that, and mind you nobody even move one chess piece yet ah, that's the funny part I always tell people. I very kiasu at this point, tell you honestly, I look at the board, I very confident, I think I can eat this workmate alive, normally play with him I win seven out of ten. Workmate himself think think a while, not that sure, blur like sotong at the start.

Game start, and true enough, first half hour I ahead, taking his pieces one by one, crowd oohing every time I move. Then somehow, I don't know how, the whole thing turn. One move I make, very chim, I thought clever, actually walk straight into his trap. From there suay already, everything start to jialat, cannot save. Uncle beside watch until catch no ball, don't understand why I suddenly cham like that.

The uncle beside: "Aiyo, this one gone case already lah."

My heart start kancheong, hands a bit shaky, I try to gostan my whole strategy but too late, damage already done. Everybody can see it coming before even the last move drop.

Someone at the side: "Confirm lose one this, wait for it lor."

True enough, few moves more, checkmate, finished. I sit there, buay tahan, just groan out loud, put my head down on the table.

Me: "Wah lau, I lose lah. No point say anything else, I lose is I lose."

Now here the strange part start. I take out my wallet, want to just give the twenty, finish the matter, go home. But the whole crowd shout together, no no no, don't want the money, want something else instead.

Someone: "Eh, got a lobang. Why not make him do something in front of all of us instead, more fun what."

Everybody clap hands agree, even the ones who never even watch properly also clap along.

The eldest uncle: "Money no fun what. Must do something, in front of all of us, that one more shiok."

Then they start deciding, right there and then, exactly what I must do, nothing left to guess. Someone say must wear that auntie's housedress, the flowery one, everybody know which, always hanging on the corridor hook to dry. Another one say must stand up on the plastic stool, cannot stand on the floor, must higher, more drama that way. Somebody else add, must sing the whole National Day song from start to end, cannot skip a line, must do the hand action also, the one all the schoolchildren do every year. Last one throw in, at the end must bow to the pillar like it's the President standing there.

I tried to negotiate a bit ah.

Me: "Aiyo, why not I just pay double, forty dollar, we settle like that, cleaner what."

Everybody laugh until some person nearly fall off the stool.

One of them: "No way lah. Terms already set, cannot change halfway. You want to gostan now? Where got such thing. Die die must try, agreed already what."

So we set a day, same time next week, same void deck. That whole week leading up I kena tease non-stop every time I pass the corridor, and the story already spread far, people who never even there that night also come jio their own friends along.

Somebody's friend: "Wah, bojio, why never tell me earlier."

By the actual night, got maybe twenty over people standing around, some bring their own stool, somebody even tapao extra kopi like it's some show at the community hall.

The day came. A bit paiseh at first, I wear the housedress over my own shirt, very ulu looking, not atas at all, flower pattern everywhere, size also not right, too big for me. I climb up the plastic stool, whole crowd already clapping before I even open mouth. I start singing, loud, trying to be serious about it because want to finish fast, action here, salute there, everybody laughing already at this point, some recording on their phone.

Then, this is the part that still get bring up until today. Halfway through, just when I reach the loudest part of the song, the auntie, the one whose housedress it actually is, she walk past on her way back from the market, plastic bags in her hand, and she see her own dress up there on the stool, on some stranger, mid song, mid salute action. She stop dead, mouth open.

The auntie: "Alamak! That's my dress one! Why you all take my dress!"

Whole void deck just explode. Everybody laughing until cannot breathe, some hunch over holding stomach, the eldest uncle laughing so hard he cough non stop, and I myself also laughing halfway through my own salute, cannot even finish properly liao.

Heng also they never make me do more than that, one round already enough to talk about forever. That's the moment, until today, whenever the whole gang gather again, somebody sure bring it up. And every time also the story grow a bit more lah, now got people swearing the auntie chase me around the whole void deck with her market bag, some say I fell off the stool also, none of that true, but nobody bother correct anymore. Twenty dollar, everybody forgotten liao. The dress, the stool, the auntie shouting, that one never die, that one still here.
