Eyes open, and before I even sit up I remember already. Aiyo. Today the day I promise the mother I clear the whole store room, top to bottom, no more delay one. Months ago she nag nag nag until I fed up, I say okay lah, one whole Saturday I give you, don't play play. Now the Saturday come, and wah lau, my whole body don't want to move sia.

I lie there five more minutes, complaining to the ceiling. Why I swear like that for, so blur one, nobody force me what. But say already, cannot take back. Get up lor, mumbling the whole way to the toilet, brushing teeth also grumbling.

The mother already in the kitchen.

The mother: Wake up already ah. Kopi-o here, drink first, don't kancheong.

Me: Coming coming. Later confirm jialat one, that room got how many years of stuff you also don't remember.

Start pulling things out from maybe nine, and by ten already sian liao. Old fan, broken stool, bags of bags, some things I catch no ball what it is even for. Every box also got something, every corner also more. This one keep, that one also want keep, cannot throw one meh, I ask.

The mother: That one your grandfather's, cannot throw!

Me: Then everything also cannot throw, how to clear like that.

By early afternoon my back buay tahan already, want to just stop, say tomorrow continue lah. But then I think, no, I swear I do one whole day, cannot go back on word, so paiseh later if I never finish. Push through, grit teeth, keep going.

The eldest pass by, see me sweating like that.

The eldest: Wah, today you actually do ah. Not like you sia.

Me: Say already must do what. No choice.

Neighbour auntie lean over the gate.

Neighbour auntie: Heng ah, clearing already! Chope some space for me also can or not.

By evening, room empty, swept clean, everything sorted or thrown. Whole body pain like siao, but done. Die die also finish today, exactly like I promise.
