Rain is falling. The eldest at the window shouts first.
The eldest: Wah lau eh, raining already!
Everyone rush to the corridor, slippers half on.
Mother: Aiyo, so long never rain, now can already.
The youngest throws both arms out to the sky, like receiving a blessing, half laughing.
The youngest: Shiok sia, this one must catch every drop leh.
Father: Don't play play, later catch cold.
Nobody moves after that, they just stand there listening to it fall.
