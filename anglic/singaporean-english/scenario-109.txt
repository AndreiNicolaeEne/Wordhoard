The lift take so long tonight, I stand there with two bags and one backpack, and by the time the door open the corridor light already flicker twice. Mother is the one who pull the gate open, and for one second she just stand there, mouth a bit open, like she cannot process.

Mother: Wah lau... is it... is it really you ah?

Me: Ma, it's me lah.

Mother: Aiyo!! Wah, you really come back one ah!! Alamak, come come come, why you never message you reaching, I would have waited downstairs one!

She grab me before I even put down the bags, and she hug so tight and so long I almost cannot breathe, and behind her I can hear the whole flat suddenly come alive, slippers slapping on the floor, someone shouting into the kitchen.

The eldest: Eh!! She's back!! Everybody come out lah, she's back already!!

By the time Mother let go, my father already at the door too, and behind him the whole living room, must be six seven people all trying to see over each other shoulder.

Father: Wah, look at you. So thin! Or is it I forgot how you look like. Turn around, let me see.

Grandmother: Aiyo, come let me see, come here, don't stand so far.

The eldest: Wah, you cut your hair, and you got taller is it, or maybe I just remember you shorter.

Me: Cannot lah, I never grow after eighteen, must be you all shrink.

The eldest: Where got shrink, I still same height okay.

Auntie: Eight years... eight years like that gone just like that, aiyo.

She say this halfway laughing halfway not, and her eyes already glossy, she keep blinking and fanning her face with her hand like that will help.

Auntie: Wait ah, don't mind me, I just, aiyo, I promised myself I won't cry one, see, cannot control.

Mother: Aiyo you, always like that, cry also must apologise for what.

Somewhere behind everyone, half hidden by the eldest, one small figure just watching, not saying anything, holding onto the door frame.

Me: Eh, this one who? Don't tell me this is...

The eldest: Ya lah, this is her, last time you saw her she still cannot walk properly, now she in primary two already.

Me: Wah, so big already! Come, come say hi, I'm not scary one lah.

The youngest: ...

The eldest: Paiseh ah, she shy, she don't really remember you, we always show her your photo only.

Me: Nevermind, nevermind, take time, we got time now.

Father: Eh you all, why still standing at the door, let her come in first lah, bags also not carried in yet.

Nobody move the bags. They just sit there by the shoe rack for another good ten minutes because the questions already flying non stop, one on top of another, nobody waiting for the answer before the next one come.

Mother: You eat already or not, I got cook, wait I show you...

Father: How is the weather over there now, still cold or...

Auntie: Eh your hair colour also different leh, you dye ah...

The eldest: You still keep in contact with that one from school anot...

Grandmother: Sit down first lah, why everybody asking together, catch no ball like that.

Me: Wait wait, one at a time lah, I only got one mouth.

Something in the air already hit me before anyone even mention food, that smell, I know that smell anywhere.

Me: Wah, is that... you cook the curry ah? The one with the potato?

Mother: Ya lor, I know you like, so I make since afternoon, simmer whole day one, must be why the whole house smell like that.

Me: Same smell like last time sia. Whole corridor even smell like that when the lift door open just now, I thought I dreaming.

Mother: Where got dream, real one, come, wash hand first, we eat.

But nobody move toward the kitchen yet, because the news already start pouring, everyone want to be the one who tell me first.

The eldest: Eh you know the one downstairs, the coffeeshop uncle daughter, she married already, got baby already even.

Auntie: And your cousin, the one who was still studying last time, now working in the bank, very atas one, always busy busy.

Father: We moved the rice cooker to the other counter, and the sofa, we change already, the old one too spoil already, cannot sit properly, always sinking one side.

Me: Wah, so many things happen, I feel like catch no ball already, so many people change, so many things change.

The eldest: You also change what, look at you, all grown up like that, walk also different already, more atas walk.

Me: Where got atas, I still same lah. But eh, the void deck downstairs, they repaint already ah, I saw just now, still that ugly green colour though.

Father: Aiyo, that green colour never change one, from your time until now still the same, town council very chin chai about that one.

Me: And the kopitiam, still the same uncle taking order or not?

Mother: Still the same one lah, still shouting kopi-o one cup teh-c peng one cup, never change his voice even.

Grandmother: Eh, how long you staying this time ah, don't tell me just one week again like last time.

Me: This time longer lah, almost one month.

Mother: One month!! Wah, must plan something then, cannot waste, I want to call everybody come over, do steamboat, or maybe I tapao the good bak kut teh from the one near the market, you all remember which one I mean.

Auntie: Must invite the whole family what, this kind of thing don't do small small one, die die must gather properly.

The eldest: Eh remember what we used to call her last time, before she flew off?

Mother: Aiyo, don't say already lah...

The eldest: The long-legs one! Because she always walk so fast in front, we all cannot catch up, always leaving us behind like that!

Everyone laugh, even the ones who clearly don't know the story, they just laugh because everyone else laughing, and the youngest peek out a bit more from behind the eldest, curious now, still not talking but not hiding fully anymore.

Mother: Wah, this girl, hear the joke also laugh, but she don't even know what happen last time.

The eldest: Nevermind lah, she will find out one day.

For a moment, just for a moment, everybody stop talking at the same time, like by accident, and the whole living room just go quiet, everyone just looking at me, and I looking back at all of them, and nobody saying anything, the curry smell still hanging in the air, the bags still sitting untouched by the door.

Then Grandmother break it, patting the seat beside her.

Grandmother: Okay lah, enough standing, come sit, eat first, talk can continue after, food getting cold already.

And just like that the noise settle down softer, like everyone finally breathe out together, Father going to get extra bowl, Mother fussing was the curry too salty or not, Auntie wiping her face one last time and laughing at herself for it, and the youngest, slow slow, coming to sit just a little bit closer to me than before.
