I go down to collect my pants, the tailor shop just beside the coffee shop, been going there for years already. Uncle pass me the garment on the hanger, I look one time, then I look again, something not right sia.

The customer: Uncle, this one not what I order leh. I ask for three buttons, straight hem. This one only two buttons, and the hem got slant one, you see or not.

The tailor: Aiyo, where got wrong, this is what we settle already what. You say like that, I make like that lor.

The customer: No lah uncle, I never say two buttons one. I very sure, three buttons, straight hem, I even show you my old pants that day to point the style, remember or not.

The tailor: Wait ah, don't kancheong first, let me check my book, I write down one every time, cannot simply say.

He go rummage under the counter, mutter mutter, flip flip the pages, take quite a while, I stand there a bit sian already.

The tailor: Aiyo... ah here, got. Three button, straight hem, this is what write down leh. Wah lau, then how come I cut like that one, maybe I catch no ball that day, take wrong measurement for you.

The customer: See, I say already what, I never blur like sotong one, my memory can what.

The tailor: Paiseh paiseh, my mistake lah, not your side. Heng ah you check properly, if not both of us jialat. Ok, you leave with me, I redo the buttons, straighten the hem, two days can already, you come back Thursday, I chope for you first, don't worry.

The customer: Ok lor, two days can. Thursday I come back, don't let me disappoint again ah uncle.

The tailor: Confirm plus chop, Thursday definitely done one.
