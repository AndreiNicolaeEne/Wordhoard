That night ah, I tell you, from the moment I swipe in already I feel it in my bones, going to be jialat one.

Ten o'clock, report for duty, and the signing sheet only got four names ticked when we supposed to be six. Two people short like that, before anything even happen leh. One on MC, one just never turn up, phone also switch off, not answering. The supervisor look at the sheet, look at me, say...

The supervisor: "Where got enough people to run tonight like this. You all must double up already lah."

So from the start already we running blur, everybody doing two roles, no slack at all, and I thinking, wah, tonight going to be long one.

Eleven plus, first small sign. The chiller for the cold room, the display panel start flickering, on off on off, then the alarm beep one short beep and stop. I look at it, my workmate look at it.

My workmate: "Eh, that thing keep beeping like that, normal or not?"

Me: "Dunno leh. Maybe just a glitch lah, don't scare yourself."

We chin chai pat it, reset the panel, seem okay already, so we continue, no time to dwell, still got trucks coming in.

Then just after midnight, the real thing come. The main conveyor for the packing line, halfway through a run, suddenly jam, the belt make this grinding sound, then just stop dead, whole line backed up in like two minutes. Everybody rushing over, cannot even see what wrong first, boxes piling up at the mouth of the machine, some already fall over, contents spill out.

Me: "Wah lau, now what."

The newcomer, first month only, standing there blur like sotong, catch no ball at all what's happening, don't know whether to pull the boxes off the belt or leave it.

The newcomer: "Should I take out the boxes or not?"

Me: "Take out lah, don't just stand there, later more pile up!"

We scramble, trying to clear the jam without stopping the whole operation, because the trucks still coming, still got deliveries to load, cannot just stop everything. Somebody call the on-call maintenance number. No answer. Call again, straight to voicemail. Every time also like that, this kind of on-call number, nobody pick up when you really need. Half an hour like that, we patching with our own hands, broom handle poking whatever stuck inside, praying nothing worse happen.

Me: "This one really no reply ah, like that how."

My workmate: "Call again lah, maybe he sleeping, don't play play, this line stuck already, cannot tapao the problem for later, must clear now."

Finally the supervisor decide, must call the senior technician direct, the one who stay quite near, even though it's like one in the morning already. He come on the phone, not happy at all, you can hear it straight away.

The senior technician: "Now? You all call me now for this. Cannot wait until morning shift meh."

The supervisor: "Cannot lah, whole line down, trucks still coming, if not clear tonight, tomorrow also mess up one."

Aiyo, the atas office people all sleeping soundly at this hour, and here we are. He come down maybe twenty minutes later, still in his slippers, face black black, straight away go and check the machine, muttering the whole time, fixing it like he buay tahan already this kind of call-out.

While he fixing that, on the other side, second problem stack on top. The chiller alarm from earlier, turn out not a glitch after all, temperature creeping up slowly, nobody catch it in time because everyone busy with the conveyor. By the time somebody check, some of the stock already borderline, spoiling liao if not moved fast, must shift it to the backup unit quick.

In the rush to shift the pallets over, using the pallet jack because the forklift uncle also tied up with the conveyor mess, someone nearly kena hit, the newcomer, pulling a loaded pallet too fast round the corner, almost pin his own foot under the wheel, just missed, everybody shout at the same time.

My workmate: "Wah, careful lah! You want break your leg is it!"

The newcomer, shaking a bit, paiseh, mumbling sorry sorry.

The newcomer: "Sorry, sorry, I rushing too much, never see see the corner properly."

Heng also, if not really jialat that one, could have been serious. After that the supervisor decide, enough, this corner of the operation, the manual pallet moving for that section, must stop first, cannot push through half broken like that, not worth anybody getting hurt over some stock. So we cordon off that bit, focus only on what safe to keep moving, and just eat the delay.

By about half past three, things sort of calming down. Conveyor running again, slow but running. Chiller stabilised after the shift to backup. Everybody sweaty, sian already, but for like twenty minutes there, it look like maybe, just maybe, we going to claw this back, finish the shift in one piece.

Me: "Wah, finally can breathe a bit."

My workmate: "Don't jinx it lah, still got few hours more."

True enough, don't know why I even say that, because not long after, maybe four o'clock, third thing break, this time the loading bay door, the roller shutter, get stuck halfway up, motor make one loud clunk and then nothing, door won't go up, won't go down, just stuck, and there's a truck outside already waiting, driver getting kancheong, honking a bit.

The driver: "Eh, how long more, I got other stops after this one leh."

The supervisor: "Wait first lah, door stuck, we trying."

Alamak, at this point everybody just numb already, don't even shock anymore, just get on with it, force the shutter up manually with the chain hoist, arms aching, take another forty minutes. Our shift damn suay tonight, I tell you, one thing after another like never going to end.

By the time we finally get through all that, we start counting the damage. Some boxes from the conveyor spill, contents damaged, cannot sell already, must write off. Part of the cold room stock, the stuff that was borderline before we shift it, some also must be discarded, cannot risk it. A pallet or two knocked over in the rush also lost some units. Small small things add up, by early morning the tally quite jialat, the supervisor doing the count, shaking his head the whole time.

The supervisor: "Tonight ah, wah lau, going to be a lot of explaining to do lah."

Last two hours before the morning crew take over, everybody just running on fumes, nobody talking much, just doing what need to be done, one task at a time, don't even think about it anymore, body on autopilot already. My legs paining, my eyes stinging, but must finish, must clear, cannot leave it half done for the next shift, that also not fair to them.

Around six, sky slowly turning that grey blue colour outside the loading bay, then a bit of orange creeping in. Somebody open the side door for some air and you can see it, morning coming already. Nobody say anything about it, just glance, and go back to work, because still got things to close out before handover.

By the time the morning crew swipe in, we hand over, tell them roughly what happen, they all wide eye, cannot believe how much thing go wrong in one night.

The morning crew colleague: "Aiyo, one night only, so much thing happen ah? Jialat leh."

Me: "Don't ask already, tell you also cannot believe."

Walk out after that, morning air a bit cool still, sun coming up proper now, and I so tired I don't even feel relief, nothing, just numb, just want to go home, makan a bit, and sleep. Legs heavy, eyes half shut, taking the lift up thinking about nothing at all, just the bed waiting. That kind of tired where even the bad feeling also cannot come out yet, must sleep first, then tomorrow maybe can feel something about it liao. One whole night like that, don't wish it on anybody.
