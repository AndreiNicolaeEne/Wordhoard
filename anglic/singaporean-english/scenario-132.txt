That eleven days sia, until now I still shiver thinking about it. From day one already colder than the forecast say, everybody caught off guard like that lah. Weather bureau announce cold spell coming, but we catch no ball what that really mean until we feel it ourselves. We not used to this kind of cold what, so kiasu already, stock up extra blanket, whatever got in the store also buy. Kancheong the whole week, water heater on nonstop, kettle also nonstop, until the electric bill confirm jialat later.

Every time also like that, weather forecast never accurate one, but this time confirm worse than they say. By the sixth day, the pipe at the service yard, the one for the water heater, just crack like that. Kena spoil by the cold sia, never happen before one, so many years staying here also never like that.

The mother: Where got pipe simply spoil like that. Never like that before one leh.

But it did lor. No water coming out that morning, she blur like sotong standing there not knowing what to do.

The eldest: Aiyo, back then also got one time so cold, everybody wearing long sleeve indoor also.

Me: Is it? Worse than that one ah?

The eldest: Worse lor. That time never so long, only two three days one. This one eleven days, die die also must endure.

Me: Move to the living room, can or not?

The eldest: Can.

Worst night was day eight I think. Everybody under got two blanket, still cannot sleep, teeth chattering, whole family squeeze together in the living room, fan off already, aircon of course off, still cold like that. Buay tahan. Kept peeking peeking out the window see got sun or not, but no.

Our betta in the tank, the one my brother chope at the pet shop before anyone else can grab, dead already by the ninth day, floating there one morning. Everybody sian, nobody talk much that day, paiseh to even mention it.

Then day eleven, the sun came out properly, heat up like normal again. Everybody so happy, stand at the balcony like worshipping the sun. Shiok cannot describe. Strip off the extra clothes already, back to shirt sleeve. Went downstairs order kopi-o hot, first time in eleven days don't need the peng version. Uncle at the coffeeshop shout, finally ah, and everybody laugh, relief until want to cry also got.
