Wah, I tell you ah, until now when I think back to that night I still catch no ball how the whole thing happen, but let me try piece it together for you lah, since you never there that day.

So is my auntie's retirement, forty over years working already, everybody say must celebrate one, cannot chin chai like that, must do properly. Whole extended family in the group chat jio jio until confirm going, even the uncle from Johor also drive down. I tell myself before I go, one two hour only, eat eat, say happy retirement, take photo, then go home, got things to settle the next day what. But you know how our family like that, once the aunties start pouring you cannot say no one lah. My uncle keep pushing this whisky he bring back from his trip, "die die must try, cannot waste one," so I try lor. Then my cousin come pull me, "eh don't be like that, drink some more, celebrating what," so I say okay lah one more, then one more after that one, until I lost count already.

Dinner was at the usual zi char place downstairs, then after that everybody tapao the extra food and moved the whole party up to my auntie's flat, corridor full of people, some uncle bring the karaoke machine, plug in until the whole floor can hear. That part I remember quite clear still, everybody sing sing, my auntie doing her retirement speech half crying half laughing, and me clapping like nobody business. After the food finish they push the table to the side and start dancing, old Hokkien songs, my mother dancing with my auntie, the whole thing very shiok. Last thing I really remember clear is somewhere around there, holding my auntie's hand spinning her around while everybody clapping. After that, must be liao, because whatever happen next only come back to me in pieces, like watching a video where somebody keep cutting the scenes.

Woke up the next morning like being hit by lorry one. Head pounding non stop, like got someone using my skull as drum. Stomach also rolling, just the smell of breakfast cooking outside already want to make me run toilet. The room light, normally never notice one, that morning feel like someone switch on stadium lamp straight into my eye, and every sound, the rice cooker click, the neighbour's gate, all like amplified sotong style. Just sitting up from the bed already take me like five minutes, must go slowly slowly, otherwise the whole room spin.

My mother was already up, moving around normal normal like nothing happen, and of course cannot resist teasing me a bit, not fierce, just that kind of enjoy-my-suffering tone.

The mother: Wah, look at you, walk also cannot walk properly, still remember your own name or not.

Me: Aiyo, don't say already lah, my head like being paint roller, so loud.

The mother: Loud? I never even talk loud leh, you the one dancing until three in the morning like young girl, now blame the volume.

Me: I got dance until three ah? I don't even remember leh.

The mother: Confirm you don't remember, you were like that already halfway through, catch no ball what people saying to you.

I just lie there and let her enjoy the moment lah, cannot even argue back properly, throat too dry. Lying there I make myself a promise, very serious one, that I will never let a night run away from me like that again. Even as I promise I also know, deep down, this not the first time I swear this, and probably also not the last, but at that moment I really mean it sincerely.

The whole morning basically write off. Normally a short walk down to the kopitiam, buy my kopi-o, nothing to it, but that morning felt like climbing a mountain. Even simple chore like hanging the laundry, my hands shaking a bit, everything take twice the effort and twice the time. I just sit on the sofa most of the morning, not moving much, curtains closed, phone brightness all the way down, waiting for the worst to pass. Managed to force down some plain porridge in the afternoon, nothing rich, no oil no meat, just enough to settle the stomach a bit, and stayed quiet, didn't even want to talk to anybody, just wanted the whole house to stop existing for a while.

Bit by bit, pieces of the night come back to me in flashes. One flash was me grabbing the mic from my auntie halfway through her retirement song, cutting her off completely, and belting out my own version louder than her, in front of the whole corridor of relatives. Another flash, I think I also told the neighbour auntie her new curtains "quite ulu" one, which, wah, I don't even know why that came out of my mouth, she never even do anything to me. Every time that flash come back I got that paiseh feeling all over again, cannot shake it off, keep turning it over and over in my head. I know I owe my auntie a proper apology, that was her moment and I anyhow snatch it, not fair to her at all, and probably owe the neighbour auntie one too, though how to even bring that up without sounding more blur.

By evening, finally start feeling like myself again, steady enough to eat a proper plate of rice, think straight without the room tilting. My mother still throwing me the occasional look, half amused half exasperated, but by then I also can laugh a bit at myself, in that jialat kind of way where you laugh because otherwise too paiseh to think about it straight.

So that's the whole saga lah, as much as I can piece together anyway, some parts still permanently gone, gostan cannot even find. Heng thing nothing worse happen, no fall down no lose phone, just the headache and the guilty conscience. I really did promise myself lying there that morning, no more like that, control the drinking, know when to stop, go home when I say I go home. But if you ask me honestly, next big celebration comes around, somebody pour one more for me, somebody say don't be like that come dance a bit more, I also very sure I will say yes again, and end up right back here texting you the same kind of story. Some things you swear off every time also, and every time also you still end up here.
