Behind the counter the uncle don't waste time explaining long long, just pick up the kettle and show. One hand high, one hand low, he pour the teh from tin to tin, the stream got a curve to it, top come out all frothy like that. Somewhere behind, someone shout one kopi-o kosong, one teh-c peng, but he don't even turn.

The uncle: See how I do ah. Must got the height, pour from up there, let it fall down slow slow, den the bubble come up. Not just tip and pour one shot like that.

New hand take over, pour straight down close to the cup, flat, no froth at all.

The uncle: Eh cannot like that lah. Too near already, no air go in one, must higher mah, like I just show you.

The new hand: Higher how much ah, like this can already or not?

The uncle: Aiyo, I just show you what, you watching or not. Higher than that, from up there, then pour slow, dun rush like that.

New hand try again, this time too fast, splash all over the counter.

The uncle: Wah lau, again? Same mistake leh. How many times I show already, three time also cannot get, catch no ball is it!

The new hand: Eh sorry sorry, I trying what. Uncle dun need scold like that, first day mah.

The uncle: (breathe out, softer already) OK OK. Forget the height first, high low all that. You imagine like folding, pour then stop, pour then stop, small small, feel the rhythm one, not force one shot dump down like siphoning petrol.

Third try, slower, stop-pour-stop, and the froth come up a bit on top, sitting there proper.

The uncle: There, see, got already! Like that lah. Dun rush next time, one you get the feel, every time also can already.

The new hand: Wah shiok, finally got it. Thought I really jialat, cannot make it one already.

The uncle: Mm, dun proud first. Do until liao without even thinking, then you really got it one, not just heng this one time.
