Me: Mum, teach me the kaya lah, the one you always bring for Chinese New Year. I want to learn how to make already.

The mother: Aiyo, this one no recipe one leh, all in the head only. My grandmother teach me like that, she also never write down, just watch watch, do do, then slowly you remember.

Me: Okay okay, so what go in first?

The mother: First must have coconut milk, fresh one ah, squeeze yourself better than the tetra pack. How much... agak agak lah, about one small bowl, maybe two hundred ml like that, I never measure one, just by feel.

Me: Then egg right?

The mother: Then egg, but wait, no, must mix the sugar with the egg first, before you even touch the coconut milk, beat until smoo, smooth, no lumps. Aiyo I forgot to say, pandan leaf! Almost left out, cannot forget that one, tie knot knot and throw in, if not the kaya no smell, taste like nothing, very sian one.

Me: Okay got pandan already. Then strain is it?

The mother: Strain everything through the sieve first, must strain ah, if not got bits inside, not shiok to eat. Then cook, but the fire must be very very small. This one my grandmother always warn me, cannot rush this step one. You rush, big fire, the egg curdle, whole pot become like scrambled egg, waste the coconut milk, jialat.

Me: Must stir the whole time?

The mother: Stir non-stop, patient patient, one two hours also must stir, don't be lazy.

Me: How you know when done, you got timer or not?

The mother: Timer for what. You just look, when it turn brown brown colour, thick already, coat the back of the spoon and don't slide down, that's done liao. Cannot rely on clock one, every stove different.

Me: And the sugar, how much exactly ah?

The mother: That one I really cannot tell you exact, I just add add, taste taste, until sweet enough for me, by eye only, paiseh.

Me: Can I use gula melaka instead of white sugar or not?

The mother: Can can, but must put a bit less, and the colour will darker, taste got that caramel smell. Still can one, my grandmother sometimes also do like that when got extra at home.
