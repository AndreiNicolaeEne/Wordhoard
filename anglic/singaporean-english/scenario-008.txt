The child: But why never explain properly one, always just cannot cannot.

The mother: Wash dishes is your job already, not new thing you selling me. Chores don't turn into six hundred dollar leh.

The child: Then wait my birthday, next month, can buy for my birthday?

The mother: Depends. If Papa bonus come in and nothing else jialat happen, maybe can talk about it. But don't fix in your head first, later disappointed then blame me.

The mother: Her mother her business, our house our budget, everybody's got different got and don't got, cannot compare like that one. You go compare, you also compare her got tuition fee we paying for you or not.
