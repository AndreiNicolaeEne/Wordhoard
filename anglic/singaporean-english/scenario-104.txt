Wah, you ask me the worst trip I ever take ah, don't play play, this one confirm plus chop the worst one lah. I laugh now when I tell but that time, jialat, really jialat.

Okay so like this. We plan very early already, like three months before, spreadsheet and everything, my friend one who like to plan, colour code some more. Bus ticket book early bird price, hotel also, itinerary got timing down to the minute one. I tell my friend, this time confirm smooth, we so organised leh, cannot go wrong. Famous last words right.

First sign already at the pickup point. Bus supposed to come seven plus, we stand there like blur sotong waiting, no bus. Ask the counter uncle, he also catch no ball, keep saying "coming, coming" but where got coming, forty minutes we stand there with our bags, aircon not even on yet already tired. My friend keep looking at watch, I tell her relax lah, chin chai, delay a bit never mind, later confirm okay one. Wah, I never know how wrong I was that time.

So bus finally come, we chope our seats, settle down, everybody happy happy for like one hour. Then halfway through, on the highway somewhere ulu ulu place, the whole bus just... slow down, slow down, then stop. Driver come out, open the engine cover, smoke coming out some more. Aiyo. Everybody on the bus go "huh, what happened", start standing up craning neck to see. Driver just shake head, say breakdown, need to call for another bus come rescue.

That rescue bus, confirm plus chop take forever. We all get down, stand by the roadside, and slowly slowly more people gather, because got other buses also break down that stretch that day, or maybe just people also waiting for connection, dunno, but the crowd grow and grow until like fifty over people all squeeze under this small shelter, everybody's luggage piling up like mountain. Some uncle start smoking, some auntie open her tupperware start makan her nasi lemak she pack from home, very calm one, like she anticipate already.

Three hours we wait there sia. Three hours! Nobody can tell us anything, ask the driver he also blur, ask got any update he say wait wait, ask again ten minutes later still wait wait. My friend keep asking me "so what happens now, is it?" and I also catch no ball, I don't know leh, everybody don't know, the whole crowd don't know, we all just standing there like sotong looking at each other.

On top of that the sky decide to turn stress also. Grey clouds roll in, then the rain just pour, real heavy one, everybody rush rush trying to find cover, umbrella here and there, some people no umbrella just get soak, wind blowing the rain sideways into the shelter some more. Wet luggage, wet shoes, wet mood, the whole works.

Then, wah lau, in all that rushing about, I turn around, my friend gone. Not there. I look left right, ask people "you see my friend or not", nobody know what I talking about, everybody minding their own luggage. Twenty minutes sia, my heart really kancheong, I walking up down the shelter area calling, checking behind pillars, thinking don't tell me she wander off somewhere in this weather. Finally see her, she also looking for me, turn out she go find toilet and the queue also stuck because everybody same idea, and when she come back the crowd shift and she cannot find where I stand anymore. Both of us catch no ball where the other one go. Relief when we finally see each other, I tell you, hug like reunion drama.

Now during all this commotion, there was an uncle, don't even know his name, just some uncle also stranded there, see we two looking lost and wet, he go dig in his bag pull out one extra poncho, just like that pass to my friend, say "wear lah, later catch cold, die die must wear". Small thing, but until now I remember, this kind of stranger kindness you don't forget one. I always tell people, in the worst moment somebody's small act can really carry you through, this one I grateful for very long time already.

Then because the wait so long and everybody so cham, in the end we give up waiting for the rescue bus timeline, flag down a private car, some gostan operator offer lift for a fee, but confirm not cheap, extra fifty just like that gone, never planned this expense at all, budget spreadsheet all buay tahan already, useless one.

In the car, that's when my friend and I really clash sia. She want to just continue original plan, push through to the destination no matter what. I say no lah, better we detour to the nearer town, find alternative transport, faster. She say I always like this, over-plan then when things go wrong I panic and want to change everything, kiasu but also blur. I say she the one being stubborn, sunk cost only, must let go. Very sharp exchange one, in the small car, driver also awkward, don't know where to look.

Me: You want to keep going that way for what, we already delay how many hours, still want to insist.

My friend: Because we so near already what, now you want detour, waste more time only.

Me: Waste more time? Continuing like this confirm also waste time lah, you don't listen.

My friend: I don't listen? You the one keep changing mind every five minutes.

We both quiet down after that, sulk sulk in the back seat, but in the end, guess what, her way we take, and true enough, wrong turn, the driver himself also not sure the road, we end up doubling back, that alone cost us two more hours. I never say "I told you so" out loud but wah, the temptation.

By that point, buay tahan already, both of us just dead tired, not talking much, just staring out the window like zombie, eyes half close, stomach also empty because no proper meal since morning, just some biscuit we tapao from the rest stop earlier. That was the real low point, everybody exhausted, nobody got energy even to complain anymore, just sian to the max.

Then heng ah, small stroke of luck came. The driver's friend, some lobang he call up, happen to be doing a run near our actual destination that same night, and he arrange for us transfer over, save us another long stretch. Suay the whole day, then heng at the very end, that's how it go sometimes.

We reach finally, nine hours later than the original plan, nine hours sia, can you imagine. The hotel reception uncle look at us, I think our face already tell the whole story, don't even need to explain. We just check in, drop the bags, and the two of us look at each other, half want to laugh half want to cry, so exhausted we just laugh instead, cannot even be bothered to be angry anymore. First thing we do, order two hot kopi-o and just sit there quiet for a while, don't talk, just drink.

Now when I tell this story I laugh, but that time, wah, no joke, none of it funny at all lah. Every hour we thought "confirm this is the worst part already" and then something else come and top it. But now, sitting here telling you, I think that's the trip I remember best out of all of them, funny how that work, is it.
