My friend: I'm fine what. Fine what, don't worry lah.

My friend: Where got drunk. Where got. I'm ok what, you all so kancheong for what.

Second round I just don't argue anymore, I pull the arm over my shoulder direct, tell the birthday boy I send this one home first, come back if can. Nobody stop me, everybody understand this kind of situation, every time also somebody must do this job one.

So we walk, slow slow. I keep one arm round my friend's waist whole way, other hand holding the arm that's slung over my shoulder, otherwise this one list to one side like the block itself is leaning. I just laugh, say ya ya I know lah, don't be like that, drink your water. But inside also a bit touched, this kind of thing only comes out when the person really that drunk, sober time never say such things one, too paiseh.

My friend: I promise ah, never like this again. Never drink like that anymore, I swear.

I say ok ok, I believe you, sleep first.
