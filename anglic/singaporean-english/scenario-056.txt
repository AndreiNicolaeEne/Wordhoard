Wah lau, that one week I go and mind my neighbour's flat, I tell you ah, from day one until the day they come back also got story. Seven days only, I think what, how hard can it be, feed the cat, water the plants, collect the mail, chin chai lah. In the end I also learn my lesson liao.

First night already not right one. I go in around eight, drop my bag, and the whole flat got a smell, funny smell, not rotten but also not fresh, like something switch off that shouldn't switch off. I check the fridge, can. I check the bins, can, already tied up nicely by the neighbour before she fly off. But this smell keep coming back every time I walk past the kitchen. I message her.

Me: eh your kitchen got a bit of smell leh, everything okay one?

The neighbour: aiyo probably the drain trap dry up already, just pour some water down can already

I pour water, smell gone for one day, then come back the next. Whole week like that, on and off, on and off. Not a big problem lah but it set the tone, you know, like the week already telling you something.

Second day, the cat. I open the gate a bit wide to bring in the parcel, next thing I know that cat shoot out like nobody's business, straight down the corridor, into the lift lobby, gone. Wah, my heart drop to my stomach sia. I run down in my slippers, one slipper nearly flew off, shouting like an idiot in the void deck. The uncle downstairs who always sit there smoking, he help me look, the auntie hanging laundry also help me look. Took about one hour, finally found it hiding under a car in the car park, eyes shining like it very pleased with itself. Caught it, bring it back up, my hands shaking. Kena scolded by nobody but myself lah, I scold myself enough already.

Me: you ah, next time I open door you dare run out again I also don't know what to do with you

The cat just look at me, blur like sotong, like nothing happen.

Plant thing was slow one, crept up on me. Neighbour got about six, seven pots on the corridor, some small some big, one very ulu looking fern that she say is her favourite. I water everyday, don't play play, but by Wednesday the leaves already starting to go yellow at the edges. I don't really have green fingers, agak agak only, so I just water more, maybe too much also don't know. By Friday one whole corner of the fern gone brown, jialat. I don't dare tell her straight, I just quietly move the pot to a bit more shade and hope for the best, pray pray only.

Then the appliance. Wednesday night, I want to heat up my dinner, the microwave inside got this spark, one flash, then dead. Silent. I press the button also nothing, catch no ball what happened, just like that gone. I stand there staring at it like staring will fix it. Buay tahan, I thought wah this also can happen to me. I go check got warranty sticker or not, none, and the thing look like it older than me already. In the end I just tapao my dinner cold, use the stove for everything else that week, chin chai.

Thursday afternoon, visitor. No warning at all, just doorbell ring, I peep through the hole, some guy standing there.

The visitor: eh hi, is the auntie home? I come to service the aircon, she never message you ah?

Me: er, nobody tell me leh, she overseas now, I'm just helping look after the house

The visitor: wah bojio, I already drive all the way here sia

Me: sorry ah, I also catch no ball about this one, you want I call her to confirm?

Turned out the neighbour forgot to tell either of us anything, real blur one, I call her, she paiseh paiseh apologise, reschedule the whole thing. The visitor never come back that week in the end, heng also, one less thing.

The jolt of alarm one came from the spare key. She got a spare house key hanging somewhere for the parcel man or whoever, and one evening I go to look for it to give to a delivery uncle and it's not on the hook. I search the whole flat, drawer by drawer, jacket pocket by jacket pocket, sweating already, thinking wah I lost her spare key how, must I pay for new lock is it. Kancheong the whole hour, heart beating like drum. Finally found it, it had fallen behind the shoe cabinet, must have knocked it off without realising. I nearly die of relief lah, sit down on the floor for a while just breathing.

Then my own key drama. Saturday afternoon I go down to throw rubbish, in slippers, no phone, and the door go and lock itself behind me, self-locking gate she got. I stand there like an idiot outside my own borrowed house. No spare key on me because that one is inside also. I go down to the guardhouse.

Me: uncle sorry ah, I lock myself out from my neighbour's flat, can help me or not

The guard: aiyo, this one we cannot help you leh, you got the owner's number or not, call the locksmith lah

Took maybe three hours all in, waiting for a locksmith to come, sitting at the void deck like a lost soul, everyone walking past looking at me is it. Finally get in, first thing I do is put a spare key in my own pocket and never take it out again the rest of the week.

Sunday night got heavy rain, real heavy, thunder and all. I remember only around midnight that I had left one window open in the store room to air it out earlier. I rush in, floor already wet, some boxes on the floor damp at the bottom, one photo album corner gone soft. I mop mop, wipe wipe, move the boxes up onto the shelf, close the window tight. Aiyo, hope the album can still salvage.

Same week also got a noise in the night, must be Monday or Tuesday, some banging sound from the kitchen area around 3am. I lie there for a good ten minutes deciding die die must try and go check or just pretend I never hear. In the end I get up, walk walk slowly down the corridor, torchlight in hand, heart going kancheong again. Turned out just the kitchen window grille banging in the wind, not latched properly. Relief also come with a bit of malu, scared over nothing.

By the second last day I finally solve the smell thing for good, not just pour water anymore, I actually go buy a small bottle of drain cleaner from the mama shop downstairs, pour it in properly, let it sit, flush. Smell gone for real this time, not coming back. Should have done that on day one honestly.

Last day before they come home, I clean like mad. Vacuum vacuum, wipe wipe, throw out anything looking suspicious, straighten every cushion, water the plants one final time and turn the sad fern pot so the brown side face the wall. Chope my own guilt with a tissue packet lah, just kidding, but really I was that kancheong, moving around the flat non stop trying to make everything look like nothing happen at all.

When they finally walk in, bags still in hand, the neighbour look around and go, wah the house very clean leh, you sure nothing happen ah, and I just laugh and say everything fine what, small small things only, don't worry lah. I never tell her about the fern straight away, or the microwave, that one I confess slowly over the next few days, bit by bit, so it don't sound like one whole week of disaster all at once. In my head though, wah, seven days, but felt like one whole month sia.
