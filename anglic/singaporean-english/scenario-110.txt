Auntie: Wah, you're back already ah! Come come, sit here, I chope already with tissue packet just now, don't worry. Uncle, go order for her, what you want, kopi-o or teh-c peng?

The daughter: Teh-c peng can, thank you auntie.

Uncle: (shouting to the stall) One kopi-o, one teh-c peng, one Milo dinosaur for the boy!

Grandmother: Come come, sit next to ah ma, so long never see you, let ah ma see how you look, got fatter or thinner.

Mother: So how, London, like that is it? Bigger than here or not.

The daughter: Bigger lah, but different kind of bigger you know, not like our Singapore, compact compact one, everything near near.

Uncle: Bigger how big. From your side to the other side, how long?

The daughter: Aiyo, don't play play, sometimes the Tube take one hour also never reach, must change train two three times some more.

The younger brother: One hour?! Here from Woodlands to town also don't need one hour leh.

The daughter: That's what I say also! And noisy, wah lau, the traffic, the trains, everywhere honking, every time also got some kind of siren, never stop one.

Auntie: Here also noisy what, but there worse is it?

The daughter: Worse in a different way lah. Not the hawker centre shouting "chicken rice one plate" kind. More like construction, more like sirens, non-stop one, buay tahan sometimes, especially first few months.

Uncle: So your flat there how, bigger or smaller than here?

The daughter: Smaller leh, you all will shock. My room only can fit one bed one table, no space to walk walk even, and whole flat, change to our money, easily two three times what this flat cost. Rent somemore, not even own.

Grandmother: Aiyo, so ulu meh, pay so expensive for so small?

The daughter: Not ulu lah ah ma, very central, that's why expensive. If ulu maybe cheaper, but then must travel so long already, I just said what.

Mother: People there friendly or not?

The daughter: Hmm. How to say... friendly also can say friendly lah, but different friendly. Not like here, where uncle at the kopitiam downstairs already know my order liao, don't even need to say. There must slowly slowly get to know. First few weeks, everybody keep to themselves, on the train nobody talk to you one, all staring at their phone only. But if you really need help, some will help you also, quite genuine some more. So cannot say all bad, cannot say all good, half half like that.

Auntie: So how you buy your marketing there, got wet market like here or not?

The daughter: Where got wet market like here. Most people just go supermarket only. Want fresh fresh vegetable, must go this one market, weekend only, open air like that, a bit like our old market before they renovate, but everything more ex.

Uncle: Then how, tapao food also got or not?

The daughter: Got, but not the same. Cannot just tapao and get plastic bag hor, they charge you for the bag one. And the food more ex also, one small box rice there already can buy two plate chicken rice here.

Uncle: Your work, how you find, easy or not.

The daughter: At first quite jialat lah. Send so many application, catch no ball who even want to interview me. Must network network, go for this thing called networking event, everybody there also kancheong spider trying to find job, worse than us some more. Kiasu Singaporean confirm plus chop, I even bring my own chilli sauce in my bag that day, wah lau, don't ask me why.

The younger brother: Then got or not?

The daughter: Heng got, a friend of a friend introduce me one lobang, if not confirm suay, still jobless now probably. First week on the job also kena scolded by the boss for some small thing, paiseh sia, whole different culture, must learn again.

Auntie: Work there how, same as here or not?

The daughter: Different lah. They clock in clock out very sharp one. Five thirty time up, everybody clear their desk and gostan out the door already, nobody stay back for boss to see face. Not like our side, where you pack bag on the dot also boss give you the look.

The younger brother: Eh, you never get lost ah, first time go there?

The daughter: Lost sia! First month confirm plus chop lost three four times. One time come out wrong exit at the station, walk walk end up opposite side, total blur like sotong, must gostan back down and try again. Ask people some more, catch no ball what they saying at first, talk so fast. Slowly slowly also learn the layout already.

Grandmother: Food there how, can eat or not?

The daughter: Some things shiok leh, their bread, their cheese, I got try already, cannot say bad. Fish and chips also die die must try one, quite good actually. But their chilli, blur, cannot compare to our chilli. Rice dish there, wah, sian only, tasteless. Must go one area, a bit like our Chinatown there, to find our taste, and price also not friendly, one plate fried rice easily very ex.

Uncle: So overall lah, better or worse than here, you say.

The daughter: Aiya, how to compare leh, different lor. Some things better, some things here better, cannot say one is champion lah. Where got I say bad, where got I say all good also, just different one. Eat first eat first, kopi getting cold already.

Auntie: Eh, you go there so long already, why never jio us to visit, so bojio one you know.

The daughter: Aiyo auntie, next time confirm jio, promise. Come lah, I bring you all walk walk, shop shop, I show you around, chin chai also can, don't need plan so much.

Mother: So... you coming back for good one day or not?

The daughter: Dunno leh. See how lor. Agak agak maybe two three years more, maybe not, everything also not confirm one. Job also, life also, must see how things go first.

Grandmother: Aiyo, come back lah, ah ma also not getting younger you know.

The daughter: I know, I know, ah ma. Don't worry, we'll see how. You not going anywhere too, is it? Still here waiting for me to tapao you your favourite one, right or not.

Grandmother: (laughing) Cheeky. Eat your food lah, don't talk so much, teh-c peng also going to melt already.
