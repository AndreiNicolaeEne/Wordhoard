Early morning like that I already feel not right one, go toilet then see water coming in under the door already. Ankle deep only, not scary lah, I think maybe drain choke a bit, rain too heavy. I message the group chat first.

Me: Eh water coming in already, everybody wake up check.

The mother: Got meh, where got so fast, just now still ok what.

But true enough, one hour later I go check again, wah liao, already higher than before, past ankle already. I start carry things up lor, the fan, the rice cooker, whatever plug got near the floor I quickly unplug, put up on the sofa arm first, don't wait already.

By midmorning water reach my knee, buay tahan, rising so fast I never see like that before. Everything that can climb we climb, stool go on top of table, chair go on top of stool, whatever is the highest point in the house, put there already.

The eldest: Put higher, put higher, don't play play, this one going up non-stop leh.

Water still keep coming, until early afternoon reach the highest, higher than anything we already raise up, some things also kena wet, no choice one.

Then the worst part, just sit and wait, listen to the water go bang bang against the wall, sian only, cannot do anything, phone signal also gone already. Nobody talk much, everybody just stare at the wall watching how high.

Several hours after the highest point, slowly slowly water start to go down. First nobody notice, then somebody say eh going down already, and true enough, bit by bit the level drop.

Next day go back check, the wall got a line left behind, dark brown color, mark exactly until where the water reach. Everybody stand there look at it, don't say anything, just look, heng also we put things up in time, if not jialat.
