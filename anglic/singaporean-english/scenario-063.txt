Wah, that night the wind really jialat one, you know. I never sleep well also, keep hearing things banging outside, the rattan chairs on the corridor fly here fly there, one flower pot also confirm plus chop gone case. But I never expect until I open the gate next morning, the whole side fence, my side, just, gone. Flat. Like somebody sit on it. I stand there in my slippers looking, blur like sotong, cannot process what I'm seeing, until I see the big tree branch, whole canopy, lying right across, and then I trace back the trunk and, eh, this is not even growing on my side leh. It's growing right on the boundary line, half-half, but the roots, if you go check properly, more into next door's plot. So technically their tree lah, even though for years everybody just call it "the tree between", nobody bother whose it is until now it come and crush my fence, my little vegetable trellis, everything flat.

So I go knock on the neighbour's door lor, still in my pyjama, don't care already. Tell them come and see what happen. And the first thing...

The neighbour: Wah, act of God what, wind so strong last night, cannot blame anybody one lah. Insurance also call this "force majeure" what, ask your insurance claim lah.

Me: But this your tree leh. My fence kena smashed because your tree fall on it, how not your problem.

The neighbour: Wind blow, tree fall, everybody's tree also can fall what, is it my fault the wind so strong?

I got a bit heaty already at this point, I tell you. Because everybody on our row know that tree been leaning one, not from yesterday, for years already. Slowly slowly leaning toward my side, you could see the trunk not straight, and every time got bad rain the leaves all drop on my side, I sweep also don't know how many times. My wife also chip in.

My wife: Eh, don't forget ah, two years back the town council fella come round door to door, say this tree got issue, need arborist check, you all also got the letter what.

The neighbour: Aiyo, that time also never say confirm will fall what. Just "monitor" only, the letter say monitor leh, not say cut down immediately.

My wife: But you all never call anybody to monitor also what. Just leave it there.

Aiyo, that one really got me heated up. Because monitor also not do, then now say wind's fault, where got make sense. I say to the neighbour, if you never trim, never check, then leaning tree plus strong wind equals your tree fall on my property, how is that not partly your fault. The neighbour also not willing to lose out, say if you all so sure got problem why never come talk to us all these years also, why wait until now to make noise.

So then we standoff lah, real standoff. Whole morning like that, neither side want to be the one to soften first. I don't want to pay full for the fence because not my fault the tree fall, they don't want to pay full because "act of God", so we end up arguing should split fifty-fifty or should they pay more since it's their tree, whole thing drag on, faces already a bit black black, don't really greet each other properly for about a week. My wife say don't bother already, so sian, but I cannot let it go like that, principle somemore.

Then the old uncle from downstairs, the one who always sit at the void deck in the morning drinking his kopi-o, he hear the whole story from the coffeeshop already, you know how heartland like that, one auntie tell another auntie, by lunchtime whole block know, he just walk up to both of us one evening, nobody call him, he come on his own.

The uncle: Aiyo you two, for a fence also want to fight until like that ah. Listen ah, uncle got an idea, you all just listen, don't need to say yes or no now.

Me: ...

The uncle: The tree, technically boundary tree what, both of you got stake in it, that one nobody argue right. So the wood, the clearing cost, split down the middle, nobody "backing down" what, is just splitting what's fair for a boundary tree. But the fence itself, that one belongs fully to your side...

The uncle:, so the fence panel, the actual rebuilding material, the other family contribute more, not because they "lose" the argument, but because is their canopy that damage a fence that's not theirs, separate matter from the tree cost. Two different things, split two different ways. Nobody owe anybody an apology, nobody admit fault, just fair is fair.

Genius somemore, this uncle. Because that way, my neighbour also never need to say "sorry we never trim the tree", and I also never need to say "okay fine maybe partly bad luck also", everybody just nod nod, can accept the arrangement, and nobody lose face. Heng ah got him, if not I think we drag this thing until Chinese New Year still not settled, then really jialat, whole festive season also cannot see each other's face properly.

After that, come the actual work lah. One Saturday morning, both households out there, my side got my son and me, their side got the neighbour and their grown-up boy, everybody in old t-shirt, gloves, ready to clear branches, saw the trunk pieces, carry to the collection point. First one hour, wah, so stiff. Nobody talking, just "eh pass me that", "this one heavy ah", very transactional, very paiseh atmosphere still hanging there from the whole week of not looking at each other properly.

Then somewhere mid-morning, the neighbour's wife just walk out with a tray, few cups of kopi and teh-c peng, some kaya toast cut into pieces, put on the low wall between us, never say much, just say...

The neighbour's wife: Eh, break first lah, hot already, drink drink.

That kind of small thing only, but wah, the whole mood shift after that. My son also go help their boy carry the heavier trunk piece without being asked, their side hand over the good saw when mine start to blunt, halfway through somebody crack a joke about how heng we all not electrocuted since some wire also got tangle in the branches, everybody laugh, and after that the work just go smoother, more natural already, like normal neighbours doing a Saturday chore together, not like two enemy camp.

By evening the new fence up, and eh, I tell you, sturdier than the old one somemore, because this time both sides chip in for slightly better wood, not the cheapest one like before. Tree also finally get trimmed properly this round, arborist and everything, so no more leaning-tree headache for anybody.

Now, when I see the neighbour at the lift lobby, we chat what, ask "eat already or not", sometimes exchange food during festive season, better than before even, I feel like. Not just back to normal, better than the normal before the storm, if that makes sense. Sometimes a bit of jialat luck like that, if handled right, end up bringing people closer what. Who would have thought, all because of one fallen tree.
