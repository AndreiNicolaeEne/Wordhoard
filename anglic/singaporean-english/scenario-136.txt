The money I keep in the biscuit tin on top of the wardrobe, that one for the whole month, market, kopitiam, kids pocket money, everything come out from there one. Take out to count just now, feel a bit thin already. Count again, still thin, confirm missing some more. Check the drawer under the TV also, the place I sometimes move some in case got emergency, not there also. Wah, jialat.

So I go ask around first, don't want to make big deal.

Me: Eh, anybody move my money ah? The one in the tin.

The father: Never touch, what.

The eldest: Why you asking me, I never even go near your wardrobe one.

The youngest: Huh, what money.

Everybody say the same thing, flat like that, nobody blur, nobody unsure. Then I think back, that day only one person was in the house whole day, everybody else out working, out at school. So I go straight to that one, not mildly already.

Me: That day you were alone at home right, from morning till evening, only you.

The youngest: So what if I alone. Never mean I take what.

Me: I never say you take, I ask you first.

The youngest: Your face like that also call ask nicely.

Me: Where got nicely, I asking straight because only you was there that day, who else can it be.

The youngest: Wah lau, so now confirm chop is me ah? Where got proof, you simply accuse like that.

The father: Eh, don't shout in the house lah, neighbours all hear already.

The eldest: Ya lor, maybe you miscount only, calm down first.

Me: I count twice liao, don't teach me how to count my own money in my own house.

The youngest: Then you go accuse whoever you want lah. I never take, that's it. Believe or not up to you.

Me: I know what I know.

The youngest: Know what, you just kiasu, want to pin on someone so bad.

After that nobody talk to nobody already, each one go back own room, still nothing settled, still nobody say sorry, still nobody admit.
