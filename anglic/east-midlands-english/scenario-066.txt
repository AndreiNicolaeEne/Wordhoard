And straight off everyone's saying it were the fox, must've been, what else would it be, and the other lot going, you dunt know that, you've got no proof, could've been the wind, could've been owt, and it got proper heated over that, folk arguing summat rotten with no real evidence either way, just everyone convinced of their own version.
