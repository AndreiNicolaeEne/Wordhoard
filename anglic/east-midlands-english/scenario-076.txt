Me: Ey up, duck. I need a word and I ent messing about this time.

My friend: Ey up. Worra you wanting now.

Me: Yon bike. Thursday. That wagon pulling out on the bypass and you had happen half a second to gerrout the way of it. Half a second. I were behind thee in't car, I seen the whole thing wi' me own eyes, and me hands wouldn't stop shaking after, honest. Had to pull in the layby, heart going like summat trying to gerrout me chest.

My friend: Ah give over, nowt happened did it. I'm stood here in't I, right as rain.

Me: You're stood there cos you were lucky, not cos you were careful. Dunt get mardy wi' me and all, cos there's a difference and you don't seem to see it.

My friend: It's fine. I know what I'm doing on that thing, I've been riding years.

Me: Do you summat. Cos you said same after you come off it two year back and smashed your collarbone in three place, and that were nowt but you and a wet roundabout, no wagon in it at all. Nowt to do wi' luck that time either, way you told it. And look what it did thee. Next time it int gunna be a collarbone, love. Next time it's your mother identifying thee at the hospital, or your sister, or me, stood there having to say summat at the front cos nobody else can gerrout the words. Think on who's left scraitin at the back of that, while you're not there for none of it.

My friend: ...alright. Alright, gerroff on about it now, I've heard thee. I'll ease off, I mean it, I'll not be doing daft speeds down the bypass no more. That do thee?

Me: It's a start. Sort yersen out and it'll do.
