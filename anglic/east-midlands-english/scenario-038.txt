Ey up, me duck. I'm done in, I'll tell thi that for nowt.

Started this morning and me body wunt even awake proper, straight into it an' all, no easing in like. First job were shifting them blocks off the wagon round the back, and that set the pace for the whole day, right there, cos if tha starts heavy tha's heavy all day, in't no coming back off it once it's set.

Hands went first, about ten o'clock, gerrin all raw where the strap were rubbing at us, then it were me back an' all by dinner, proper complaining. Had me snap sat there in the bag since seven and dint gerra chance at it till gone eleven, quick cob and a brew, ten minutes if that, and back on it before I'd hardly swallowed.

Afternoon were the worst of it, about two o'clock summat, sun still out and everyone flagging, and I thought to mesen, I could just gerroff now, no one'd know, but I dint, kept on with it. That stretch dint seem to move at all, three hour felt like six.

One good thing, mind, the last job, clearing the yard out, I thought that'd tek us two hour and I were done in forty minutes, dunt happen often that does it, felt like a right lift when it come off.

Ten hour, start to finish, and I'm owed forty quid for it, which in't bad for a day's graft round here.

Tomorrow's another one same as this, unless the gaffer says different, and if he does I'll rest mesen proper, cos me body's asking for it now, I'll tell thi that an' all.
