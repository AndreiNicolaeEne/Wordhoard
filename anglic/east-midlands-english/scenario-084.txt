The stranger: Ey up, me duck, ever so sorry to bother thee, but is there any chance of a drop o' water spare? Proper parched, me.

The neighbour: Ey up, love, tha looks fair clemmed. Here y'are, get that down thee.

The stranger: Oh, just a sip'd do, dint want to put thee out.

The neighbour: Gerroff, dunt be daft, tek the whole cup, int nowt to me.

The stranger: Ta ever so, tha's a diamond, that.
