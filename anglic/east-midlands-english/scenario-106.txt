Took him a good while longer than the rest of us, and nob'dy in the queue said a word about it, not out loud anyroad, everybody just looking off somewhere else, minding their own, same as you do, but you could feel it, that everyone had seen it and nobody were going to say.

That one were easy, plain as owt, straight answer, no thinking needed. But then he asks us the next one, and I knew soon as he opened his mouth I'd gorra get this right.

The one at the checkpoint: And how long do you intend to stay?

That's the one I had to be careful with, cos it int just the answer, it's how you say it and all, and I stood there half a second too long working out the words afore I said 'em, hoping it dint look like owt.

Me: Not long. A short visit, that's all it is.

And then he went quiet, looking down at me papers, turning 'em over, checking summat against summat else, and that silence went on a fair bit, longer than owt he'd said to us, and I stood there not knowing where to put me eyes, whether to look at him or away, and me heart going like nowt on earth the whole while.

But it sits with us still, and mekkin it into more than that would be a lie, same as mekkin it into less. It were what it were, and that's the whole of it, duck.
