Ey up, me duck. May tha never go nesh nor short of a cob, and may thi snap allus stretch to share, that's sworn, not just wished.

Now hear the turn of it.

But wrong me kin, and may tha go nesh to the bone, may every jitty tha walks turn black over Bill's mother's, and may tha end thi days scraitin, wi nowt but yersen for company.

Sworn the same road: bless one, curse t'other, both come true.
