Me: Ey up, what's these going for?
The seller: Twelve for the lot, me duck.
Me: Twelve? That one's bruised summat rotten. I'll gi' thee eight.
The seller: Eight's nowt, I'd be givin' 'em away.
Me: Tek ten and I'll have two more an' all.
The seller: Go on then, ten it is, but don't tell folk I let 'em gerroff that cheap.
