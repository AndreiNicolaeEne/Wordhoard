I gone to the tin where it's kept by, back of the cupboard behind the cobs, and summat weren't right, it were light in me hand straight off. Counted it once, then again cause you dint want to be saying owt till you're sure, and it were down the best part of eighty quid. Checked under the telly an all, where it gets shoved sometime if the youth's had his mates round, but nowt there neither.

So I went round asking, easy like at first, not making a thing of it.

Me: Anyone had owt out that tin, only it's gone light on me.

Mother: Not me duck, I've not been near it.

The eldest: Dint even know where you kept it, mesen.

Youth: Worra you asking me for.

Same flat answer off every one of em, no bother in it, no nothing. But it come to me after, only one of em were in this house of a Tuesday when I were at me sister's, the youth, off college with a cold, mardy about it an all, too nesh to gerroff his bed all day he said.

So I gone back and asked him straight, none of the mild about it this time.

Me: You were in this house on your own all Tuesday. Where's it gone.

Youth: I never touched it, how many times do I gorra say.

Me: There's nob'dy else it could be, is there, you were the only one here!

Youth: You're saying I've nicked it off me own mother now, are you?

Me: I int saying nowt else, am I, cause it dunt add up any other road!

Youth: Gerroff me, I've told you straight, I dint take it, int that enough for you?

It just stopped there, the pair of us stood in the kitchen, him gone white as owt and me not believing a word of it, and nob'dy shifting an inch either road.
