The eldest: Ey up youth, come here a minute, before the others get here I'll learn thee the rules proper so tha dunt mess it up in front of everyone.

The younger one: Awright. Worra we playin then?

The eldest: Tig, same as always. Reight, first off tha needs at least four to start it, else it's nowt of a game, just thee runnin round on yer owny-oh. Boundary's the wall by the jitty one end and the lamppost t'other, and up to the twitchel on the side there. Tha gerra past that tha's out the game, dunt matter who's chasin thee.

The younger one: And who's on first then?

The eldest: We all put us feet in and count round, one two three, and whoever's foot gets called last is on. Or stone paper scissors if there's an odd number, dunt matter which.

The younger one: Worra counts as tagged though? Our kid says just brushin past dunt count.

The eldest: He's reight an all. Gorra be a proper touch, flat hand, on yer back or yer arm, none o' this pattin at the air and sayin tha got me. Look, like that. Felt that dint tha.

The younger one: Ey, gerroff! ...And if I run past the wall while he's chasin us?

The eldest: Then tha's out for that go, simple as. So dunt do it just to dodge him, that's cheatin and everyone'll know.

The younger one: Worrappens when tha's tagged then?

The eldest: Tha freezes right where tha stood, arms out like this, and tha stops dead still till someone still runnin taps thee loose. Then off tha goes again.

The younger one: And it ends when?

The eldest: When everyone's froze bar one, and whoever got froze first is on next round chasin. Gerrin picked to chase, that's dead exciting, int it.

The younger one: I'll get summat wrong, I know I will, and I'm not gunna go scraitin if I get caught neither.

The eldest: Dunt matter, me duck, first go dunt count against thee, I promise. If tha needs owt explaining again just ask. Ey up, look at that sky, black over Bill's mother's, best get crackin before the others turn up and it starts chuckin it down.
