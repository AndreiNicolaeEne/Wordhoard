The traveler: Ey up. Worra you chargin into town then, before I get in?
The driver: Twenty, me duck.
The traveler: Twenty? Gerroff, I'll gi' thee twelve.
The driver: Twelve, dunt cover me petrol, that. I int tekkin the mick askin twenty.
The traveler: Aye well it int worth twenty either, is it.
The driver: Split it, go on. Sixteen and we're sorted.
The traveler: Go on then, sixteen it is, me duck.
The driver: Gerrin then, we're off.
