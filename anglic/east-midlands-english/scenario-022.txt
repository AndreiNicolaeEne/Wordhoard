Me: Ey up, me duck! Ent sin thee since t'festival, where'd thee gerroff to?

My mate: Ey up! I know, mad weren't it. 'Ere, I gorra tell thee summat, right.

Me: Worra then?

My mate: Right, me cousin were there right till t'end, and she sin the one off the counter, thee knows, wi' that other one, off her workmate's do.

Me: Never. Where abouts?

My mate: Off to the side, away from everyone, down by the jitty what goes to the car park. Just the two on 'em, laughing their heads off at summat, stood dead close an' all. Not just mates close, like. Closer.

Me: Gerroff! That's nowt, is it, folk stand close.

My mate: That's what I thought an' all, so I asked her again, she said it weren't nowt, it were proper close.

Me: But is it summat or is it nowt, though? Cause thee dint want to be reading into it when it dunt mean owt.

My mate: I know, that's what I said. But 'ere, she sin 'em again after, queuing for a cob off same stall, still together, and he'd gorrer her cardigan round his own shoulders, and it weren't even cold.

Me: Shurrup. That's two things now, that in't nowt.

My mate: I know. Bet half the street's heard by now, way it travels round 'ere.

Me: Mardy if it gets back to either on 'em and it's nowt though.

My mate: Aye, could still be nowt, mesen I'm not saying it for definite. Only way anyone'll know proper is ask one on 'em straight out, int it.

Me: Aye. Someone's gorra ask.
