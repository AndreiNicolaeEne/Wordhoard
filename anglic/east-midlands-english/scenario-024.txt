Me: Ey up, we gorra sort this list out proper, we're over by thirty odd and the venue's not budgin' an inch.

The other half: I know. Right, start wi' the ones we dunt even speak to from one year to the next, yer second cousins, my lot from work I only see at the Christmas do. That's fifteen off straight away, easy.

Me: Fine by me, worra about...

The other half: No. Don't even start on that one. She's stopping on the list whatever else gerrs cut, mesen an' all, I don't care.

Me: Well if she stays then summat's gorra come off your side an' all, in it. It's always the same, your lot get twice what mine do and nowt ever changes about it.

The other half: Ere we go again wi' this. That int fair and you know it.

Me: It is fair, we've counted it before, ask anybody yer like.

The other half: Right, what if we did a little do after, like, down the local, for whoever dint fit on the day? Cheap as owt, no bother to anyone.

Me: No chance. Two do's and everyone left off the first one mardy about bein' put on the leftovers list. Not doing that to nobody, no way.

The other half: Alright, alright, keep yer hair on. Let's count it again then, proper this time.

Me: Go on then.

The other half: ...that's still a dozen over, give or take.

Me: A dozen. Marvellous.

The other half: I'll gi' up two off mine, the uncle's lot, they'll be a bit mardy for five minutes and then get over it.

Me: Right, that's summat. Ten to go then, we'll sort the rest another night, I'm proper nesh just sat here thinkin' about it, gerr us a cuppa on.
