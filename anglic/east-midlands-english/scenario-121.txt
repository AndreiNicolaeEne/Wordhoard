Me: Ey up, duck. Did you hear that racket last night, gone midnight, out in the street?

The neighbor: I did an' all, proper set me heart going. Dint sound reet.

Me: Reckon it were an animal knocked summat over, but I'm not convinced.

The neighbor: Could've bin owt and all, a fox mebbe. Weren't near either of us though.

Me: True that. Might've just bin summat blown over.

The neighbor: Ar, if it kicks off tonight I'll keep an ear out, you do same, me duck.
