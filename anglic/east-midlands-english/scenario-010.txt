Mother: Ey up, all three on yer, in ere now, this minute, come on.

The eldest: What's up, mother?

Mother: What's up is nan's jug off the dresser's in bits on the kitchen floor, that's what's up. So who's done it, cos somebody as. Youth, I'll start wi you.

The eldest: Weren't me. I in't even been through ere since dinner, ask anybody.

Mother: I'm askin you. You gorra tell me summat better than that.

The middle one: It weren't me neither. It just went, dint it, must've been a draught, back door were open a crack and it caught the curtain and knocked it, things fall, that's all it were.

Mother: A draught. The back door's been shut since this mornin', it's been chuckin it down all afternoon, I shut it mesen so nowt got wet in ere.

The middle one: Well summat knocked it then, weren't me anyroad. Might've been cat, he's allus up on that dresser when nobody's lookin.

The youngest: Cat's been out in the yard all day, you shut im out yersen cos he were scraitin at the door, I were nesh wi it open so I know it were shut.

The middle one: Ow do you know when e got shut out, you've never even looked, you're just tryin to gerr im off yersen.

The youngest: I int tryin to gerr nowt off nobody, I'm just sayin what's true, that dunt mek me a liar.

Mother: You said just now it fell off be itsen, now all sudden it's cat. Which is it, cos it dunt add up.

The middle one: I dunno, do I. Mebbe cat brushed past it and it went.

The eldest: Now you're just makin it up as you go, you mardy article.

Mother: That's enough off both on yer. Nobody's ownin up and nobody's tellin me owt worth ownin, so till one on yer decides to say summat true, none on yer's avin any telly this week, and no cob for your tea neither, dry bread the lot. That's not just, that's fair, cos it fell on one on yer and it'll cost all three. Now gerroff upstairs.
