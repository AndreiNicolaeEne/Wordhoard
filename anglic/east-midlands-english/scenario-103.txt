Traveler: Ey up, could you tell us the way to the market, please?

The stranger: Ey up me duck. Tha wants first left down by the jitty, then keep on past the chippy till tha gets to the clock on the corner - market's just after that.

Traveler: Ta. How long's that on foot?

The stranger: Quarter hour, near enough, if tha dunt dawdle. Best get a shift on, it's black over Bill's mother's.

Traveler: Cheers, take care.

The stranger: Ta-ra, love.
