Me: Ey up, black over Bill's mother's out there, I only just gorrin before it come down. Right, I've come for me jacket, is this it here?

The tailor: That's yourn, yeah, all done and pressed and ready for yer.

Me: Hang on a minute, this int what I ordered though. I asked for three buttons and a straight hem on this, and this one's on'y gorra two buttons on it, and the hem's cut all angled off to one side.

The tailor: Nah, duck, that's exactly what we agreed. Two buttons, that's what were said, I'm sure on it.

Me: I dunt forget me own coat. I stood right where you're stood now and said three buttons mesen, plain as owt. Straight hem an' all, not this.

The tailor: Dunt get mardy wi' me, I'll gu and check us note, hang on a sec.

The tailor: Right, well I'll be... note's got two buttons wrote down on it, but straight hem, same as what you're saying. So summat's gone wrong somewhere, but it's the buttons what's wrong on the ticket, not you.

Me: I knew it weren't me imagining nowt. I dint dream that up mesen.

The tailor: No, you dint. It's on us, whoever tret them measurements down got the buttons wrong, and the hem's just been cut on the slant for no reason at all. That's ours to put right.

Me: Well what we gunna do then, I need it for a do next week, in't like I can wear it half done, is it.

The tailor: Gi's two days, I'll gerrit sorted mesen, straight hem put right and a third button on for yer, no charge, that's a promise.

Me: Two days, right, I'll tek yer word on it then.

The tailor: You will an' all, I dunt mess folk about, never 'ave. Mind how you gu now, love, looks proper black over Bill's mother's still.
