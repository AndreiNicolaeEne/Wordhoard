The counter: Ey up me duck, get in a ring. Ickle ockle, blue bockle, ickle ockle out, out goes thee wi' a dirty nose, tha's out.

The one caught: Gerroff, tha went too fast, skipped our kid, dint count, go again.

Another child: Dunt be mardy, youth, if tha argues we gorra start again from top.

The counter, louder: Ickle ockle, blue bockle, ickle ockle out, out goes thee wi' a dirty nose, tha's out. Held, littlest one, out.
