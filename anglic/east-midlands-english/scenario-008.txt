The lad: Mum, can I have one then? One of them bikes off the advert, the mountain one wi' the gears and that.

The mother: No, love, not this side of payday, we int gorra money for it, simple as that.

The lad: Why though? You said happen last week.

The mother: Because your dad's short a shift down the factory again and the leccy's gone up twice this month never mind a bike, that's the real reason, duck, I'm not messin thee about wi' owt else.

The lad: I'll do the bins every week and sort mesen out for school and all, no messin.

The mother: You'll do them any road, that's not extra, that's just living here, int it.

The lad: What if I save up and wait till me birthday then, will you put summat towards it off that?

The mother: If you save your own and there's owt left over after we've sorted the leccy, happen we'll see. I'm promising nowt though, so gerroff askin every five minutes.

The lad: His mam got him one already off the market and he's not even had his birthday.

The mother: That's his mam's business, not ours. We dunt run this house off what next door's spending, we've our own to sort, love, so that dunt settle nowt for us here.

The lad goes right mardy then, arms folded, bottom lip going summat rotten.

The mother: Ey up, stop that scraitin, it dunt suit thee, youth. Tell thee what, we'll get thee a new ball for footy Saturday, off the market, and save the rest proper towards the bike. That do thee for now?

The lad: ...Spose.

The mother: Right then. Get your coat an all, it's looking black over Bill's mother's.
