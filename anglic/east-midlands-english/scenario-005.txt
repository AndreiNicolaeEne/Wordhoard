Friend: Ey up, me duck, how's tha getting on then, the pair of yer?

Mother: Oh, not so bad, y'know. Tired, but we're managing, int it always the way.

Friend: Yeah? Good, good.

Mother: ...go on then, I'll tell thee the truth, it's been rough as owt. Up four, five times of a night, in and out to that cot like nobody's business. Three hour's sleep if we're lucky, and that's not even in one go, that's bits and bobs here and there.

Father: And me back's killing me, duck, from all the bending over the cot, and me hands wunt stop shaking of a morning neither, just from being that jiggered.

Friend: Aw, that's rotten, that is. Bless yer both, you look proper wore out, honest.

Father: I'll tell thee summat mardy an' all. Put me phone in the fridge Tuesday, spent an hour looking for it, nearly rung mesen off the landline before I twigged where it'd got to.

Friend: Ha, nowt to be ashamed of that, we've all done summat like it when we've been that shattered.

Mother: Dunt help when yer that far gone yer dunt know if it's day or night.

Friend: Well look, why don't I have the babby for a few hour this afternoon, give yer chance to get yer heads down, get some kip. I'll bring a few cobs and summat for tea an' all, save thee cooking.

Mother: Eh, y'dunt have to do that, love...

Friend: Gerroff, I'm not asking thee, I'm telling thee. You're both dead on yer feet.

Mother: ...go on then. Ta ever so, that'd be a weight off, it really would.

Father: Tha's a good un, that, honest.

Friend: Nowt of it. Get some kip while yer can, and don't be scraitin over a phone in the fridge, we've all been there one road or another.
