It had been black over Bill's mother's since dinner and then the whole run went out wrong, half the boxes to the wrong depot and the driver ringing through in a right state. Everyone come out the back to see what were gooin on, snap boxes still open on the side, and the foreman went straight for the one at the end of the bay, the one who'd only stacked what she were given.

Foreman: This is you, this is. Whole load, wrong depot, and you was on that bay all mornin.

The one blamed: Wornt me.

Foreman: Dunt matter what you say, does it, you was stood right there when it went out.

The one blamed: Wornt me.

Foreman: Same thing every time with you, int it. Summat gooes wrong and it's never nowt to do with you.

The one blamed: I swear down, I had nowt to do with it.

She said it once and left it there, face give nowt away, no mardy look, no owt. The two as had actually loaded that pallet wrong stood by the racking with their mouths shut, one of em lookin at his boots like he'd found summat fascinatin down there. Nobody said a word for em.

Her workmate: Ey up, leave it now, eh.

Foreman: I'll leave it when I've got some answers.

The one blamed said nowt more to that, just stood there tekkin it, calm as owt, like she'd heard worse off her own mother. When he'd finished shoutin she picked her bag off the hook, said nowt to nobody, and went off up the jitty toward the bus stop, still nesh from standin about in the cold doorway an hour, her denial just sat there behind her, same as she'd left it, and nobody come after to say different.
