Ey up, me duck, sit thi sen down, I've gorra tell thee the whole of it.

It were the tumble dryer that started it. Ours packed in of a Tuesday and the wife went straight on her phone and ordered a new one, four hundred and summat quid, dint say nowt to me first, just "it's coming Thursday." I says, "worra you done that for wi'out askin us," and she says it weren't summat she needed askin about, seeing as it were her wage covered half of it and all.

That were when it turned nasty, black over Bill's mother's if ever I saw owt. I brought up her sister's wedding, how she'd spent a couple hundred quid on a dress wore once and never again, and she went proper mardy, said I always drag summat up that's got nowt to do wi' owt else. Then she come out wi' the one that stuck - "at least I dint blow two grand on a car that dint even go, like some folk did when they were nowt but a youth." That were low, dragging that old banger back in, years back and nowt to do wi' any dryer.

We dint speak two whole day. I heard her scraitin in the kitchen the once, thought I were out, and I felt rotten wi' it, but I weren't gerrin off my high horse first, not for owt.

In the end I cracked, made her a cob and a brew, set it down wi'out a word. She just says "ta, love" and that were the end of it, no more said.

Between thee and me, our kid, I know I wanted to be right more than I wanted it sorted, and that's the truth of it.
