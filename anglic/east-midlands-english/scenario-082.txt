The mother: Ey up, aah, I've cut mesen bad, gerrere quick, duck!
The eldest: (running in) What's up, what's tha done?
The mother: Me hand, it wunt stop, it's pourin proper.
The eldest: (grabbing a cloth) Ere, press this ard on it, love, and keep it up above yer heart.
The mother: I'm all of a shake.
The eldest: Nowt to fret on, just hold it there. Int it slowin now?
The mother: Aye... it is, thank god.
