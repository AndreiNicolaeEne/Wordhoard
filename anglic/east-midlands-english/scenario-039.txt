The foreman: Ey up, don't be sloping off yet, we want sorting for tomorra afore it gets forgot.

The eldest: Go on then, what time we saying, seven?

The youth: Seven's daft early for what we're on, mekk it half eight, me duck.

The foreman: Half eight and the mixer's stood there doing nowt half the morning. Seven, and that's that.

The eldest: Seven it is, no arguing wi' him.

The foreman: Right, it's the wall job down the jitty past the retail park, footings first, then block up the far side afore owt else.

The labourer: I'll bring me levels and the barrows then, you sorting the mixer?

The foreman: Aye, I'll gerr it on the trailer tonight so it's ready for seven.

The eldest: Worra about cement though, there's happen only a few bags left in the shed.

The youth: And if it's black over Bill's mother's like it were today we'll not be mixing owt anyroad, it'll wash straight off.

The eldest: Dunt matter, we'll gerr a tarp over it. Somebody's gorra dig that trench out afore we start though, and it's stone half the way down.

The youth: Not me, I did the trench Tuesday, me back's still mardy about it.

The labourer: Gerroff, you've had it easy all week, quit yer scraitin.

The eldest: Reckon it's yours then, love, you're nesh enough wi'out digging in this, might warm thi up.

The youth: Cheers for that. Dint say I wunt do it, just said me back's had enough. I'll do it mesen, but somebody's buying me snap Friday for it, I int doing it for nowt.

The labourer: Reckon you've got him over a barrel there, he owes you now an' all for missing t'lift Monday.

The foreman: Fair enough, that's summat we can sort between yerselves. Right, where's the driver tonight, is he in?

The eldest: He rung us, cannot make it the morn, summat wi' his mother, he's sorted for next week though.

The foreman: That's four of us then, same as always, just wi' one less pair of hands on the barrows.

The eldest: We'll manage, we always do, yersen included.

The foreman: Seven then, don't be late, and don't forget yer own snap this time.

The youth: Int it always me forgetting. Seven, right, I'm off, night all.
