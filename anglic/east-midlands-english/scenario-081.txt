The relative: Ey up, me duck, just ringin' to see how tha's gerrin' on. Our kid said tha's been badly.

Me: Not too clever if I'm honest wi' thee. Started three days back now, an ache right through me joints and me throat scratchy as owt. Then yesterday a fever come on top of it an' all.

The relative: Oh 'eck. How's tha farin' today then?

Me: Bit easier than yesterday, I'll give it that, but still a fair way off reight. Head's a bit mardy still.

The relative: Worra tha been doin' for it, any road?

Me: Gargled warm salted water for me throat first off, that were the first thing. Fever dint fully break on its own so I put a cool cloth across me forehead of a night, kept changin' it through till mornin'. Gone right nesh under the blanket an' all, shiverin' one minute, boilin' the next.

The relative: And has tha been eatin' owt?

Me: Nowt much, just broth and a weak cuppa tea when I can face it. Slept whenever me body'd let us, which int been that often truth be told.

The relative: Well tha wants to rest proper, duck, and not push thi'sen tryin' to gerroff to work or owt daft like that.

Me: I know, I know. Look, if the fever climbs any further, or me breathin' turns hard, I'll go and get seen, promise thee that. But not before then, no point makin' a fuss over summat that might sort itsen.

The relative: I'll bring a bit o' snap over if tha wants, or come and sit with thee for an hour, keep thee company.

Me: Ah, tha's alreight, don't go troublin' thi'sen... well, go on then, an hour wouldn't go amiss. It's lookin' black over Bill's mother's out there any road, best get here before it comes down.
