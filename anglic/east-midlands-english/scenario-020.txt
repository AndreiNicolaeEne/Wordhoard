Friend: Ey up. You gorra minute, only I'm not right.

Friend: He's gone. Buggered off Friday just like that, tret me like nowt the whole week before an all, wun't even look at me proper, then sat theer ovver a cob and a cup o tea and said it were done. Like he were tellin me it were gonna rain.

Friend: An I dint see it comin, not really, or maybe I did an just dint want to. I dunno.

Friend: I ave been sat here half the day goin ovver it in me head. Must ave been summat I did. I were mardy wi him ovver nowt a few weeks back, an I never rung him back that time when he were poorly, an I...

Friend: I just keep thinkin what if I'd been...

Me: No. Listen. It says nowt about who you are, y'ear me? Not a thing. You're the same lass you always was, worra load of rubbish, blamin yersen for owt like that.

Friend: Gerroff, stop it, you'll ave me proper going now.

Me: I mean it. I'm stoppin ere tonight, I'm not avin you sat on yer own wi it all black over Bill's mother's in yer head. Or we get us coats on, walk the jitty down, get some air in us, whatever you fancy.

Friend: I'm alright, promise.

Me: You int, and that's alreet an all. C'mon me duck, you're not doin this on yer tod.

Friend: You're a soft get, you know that.

Me: Aye. Someone's gorra be.

And that got a laugh out of her at last, small and wet round the edges, but a laugh an all.
