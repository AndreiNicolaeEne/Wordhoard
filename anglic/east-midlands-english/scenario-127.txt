The younger: Ey up, me duck. Bitter out today, int it.
The elder: It is, love. Worse than Tuesday, and that were bad enough.
The younger: Aye, it were.
The elder: Puts me in mind of a spell years back, weeks of it, black over Bill's mother's every day, nowt but cold and wet through. Proper nesh I were an' all.
The younger: Get away.
The elder: Reckon tomorrow'll be worse.
The younger: Aye, rain by dinner, I'd say.
