Workmate: Ey up me duck, wharrit is 'as no legs an' no mouth, but tells thee it's gunna be black ovver Bill's mother's?

Me: Int it t' wind?

Workmate: Nay. It's t' sky, duck, plain as owt.

Me: Reight, mesen's go. Wharrit is tha wraps up of a mornin, tekks wi thee to work, an' if tha's nesh tha's etten it afore dinner?

Workmate: That's nowt but thy snap.

Me: Gorrit first go. Go on then, tha's up, duck.
