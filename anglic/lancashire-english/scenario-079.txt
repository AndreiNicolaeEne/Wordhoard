Reet, give over nattering a sec, all of yer, we're all sat here toneet for one reason, to sup to my friend. She's the most gradely soul I know, allus first through door wi' a brew when tha's mithered summat rotten, chuck. Remember when we were wet through in th' ginnel, skriking us laughing? Aye. Here's wishing yer nowt but good from here on. Go on then, glasses up, let's have it, to my friend.
