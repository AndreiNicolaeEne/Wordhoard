Me: Eh, tell thee summat for nowt, that stall by th' market's the best on it, no messin.

My friend: Give over, which one, the burger one? That's bobbins that is, I'll tell thee that reet now.

Me: Bobbins? It's gradely, that. Their barm cakes, love, proper barm, none o' this cob nonsense, and the meat's dripping off the sides. Best brew and butty combo in town an' all.

My friend: Ta very much but no ta. I'm ganning to t' one on t' corner by th' ginnel, they do a barm that'll put yours to shame, and their gravy's to die for. Yours is just fat swimming in bread if I'm honest.

Me: Fat swimming? Appen thee's forgot thee were skriking last time thee had one off that lot. Went right through thee, didn't it, our kid told me thee were stuck in th' bogs half the afternoon.

My friend: That were one time! One bad butty doesn't mek a stall bobbins, chuck. At least it weren't cold like that muck thee served me off yours, stone cold barm, I were fair mithered waiting an' all.

Me: Cold? I've never known it cold. Honest, mine's the best scran this side of Preston, best in the whole county, best in England if I'm reet.

My friend: Best in England, listen to thee. It's a barm cake stall not the Ritz.

Me: It's gradely and that's flat.

My friend: Aye well, what about th' one by the bus station, the chippy one. Nob'dy can say a bad word about that, everyone loves that.

Me: True that. Can't argue with that one, they do a belting chip butty.

My friend: Reet then, we'll settle it proper. Go to yours Tuesday, mine Thursday, and we'll see who's talking bobbins after.

Me: Made up with that. Ta-ra for now, love, don't be late.
