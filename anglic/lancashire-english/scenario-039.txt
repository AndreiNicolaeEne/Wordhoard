The gaffer: That were a belting shift today, that were. Reet, before we sup up and sod off, let's sort tomorrow while it's still in us heads.

The joiner: Go on then, what time we on site.

The gaffer: Half seven.

The joiner: Half seven, give over, it were dark as owt walkin' up that ginnel this mornin'. Appen eight'd do us better.

The gaffer: Seven then, split th' difference.

The sparky: Seven's fine wi' me. I'll fetch th' cable drum and me test meter.

The gaffer: Reet. Job's th' extension ovver Fulwood, plasterboard and first fix. Sparky's on th' wirin', joiner's doin' th' door linings, and I'll sort th' skip and th' rest o' th' materials.

The young un: And what am I doin'.

The gaffer: Thee's diggin' out for th' footings.

The young un: Give over, that's bobbins that is, me back's still bad from Tuesday.

The joiner: Should've kept off th' brews then.

The young un: I'm not skrikin' about it, just sayin'.

The gaffer: Mind, forecast says gradely and fine, but if it comes on to rain we'll not get concrete down at all, and we're short on ply besides.

The sparky: We'll manage, we allus do.

The gaffer: Reet, headcount then. Thee, thee, thee, and our kid, that's four.

The joiner: Our kid's not comin', he telled me at dinner. Summat about his missus's hospital appointment.

The gaffer: He never said nowt to me.

The joiner: He will. I'll cover his side of it, tha owes me one now, chuck.

The gaffer: Aye, I owe thee a brew and a butty and all, and I'll get barm cakes in at dinner, none o' them cobs.

The young un: I'm made up wi' that, just don't any of yer be late.

The gaffer: I'm never late. Seven sharp, and put wood in th'ole on thi way out, it's freezin'.

The joiner: Ta for tonight then.

The gaffer: Ta-ra, see thee at seven.
