The eldest: Reet, listen in, 'cause I'm not saying it twice wi' th' others due any minute. Six of us wants to be here to start it proper, four's nowhere near enough, you get nowt but runnin' round wi' no one left to catch.

The younger one: What if there's only four turn up?

The eldest: Then we wait, or we play summat else till more come. Four's bobbins for this one, it wants numbers to be owt good.

The younger one: Where's th' boundary, then?

The eldest: Wall by th' bins one end, right down past th' ginnel th' other, and side to side it's them two gateposts. Owt past that and tha's out of bounds, dun't matter who's chasin' thee.

The younger one: Who's on first, though?

The eldest: We do "not it", everyone's hand in, on three tha flicks up or down, whoever's odd one out is chaser. All same, we go again.

The younger one: And what counts as a proper tag?

The eldest: Two fingers, flat hand, whatever tha likes, but it wants to land, not just a swipe at th' air. If tha's not sure tha's been got, tha hasn't. Simple as that.

The younger one: What if I run past th' gatepost when he's chasin' us?

The eldest: Then tha's frozen right where tha stepped out, same as bein' tagged. Can't dodge th' rules by leggin' it out o' bounds, chuck, that's th' whole point of havin' one.

The younger one: What's frozen mean exactly?

The eldest: Give over frettin', it's easy. Touched, tha freezes, stood still like a lamppost, till someone still runnin' reaches over and taps thee loose. Then off tha goes again. Round's done when everyone's frozen at once, and whoever got tagged first is chaser next go.

The younger one: What if I get it all wrong?

The eldest: First round dun't count against thee, appen tha'll skrike over nowt, but tha won't, tha'll be gradely at it in no time. Ta-ra now, here they come.
