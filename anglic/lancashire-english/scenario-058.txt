Me: Now then. Put wood in th'ole behind thi, it's leathering down out there.

The neighbour: Come in out that, chuck, you're wet through. Sit thi sen down, I'll wet a brew and do us a barm cake, you look like you want summat in you.

Me: Go on then, ta. I'm at me wits' end wi' this roof, I could just skrike over it.

The neighbour: What's up wi' it now.

Me: It's coming in bad over t' back bedroom, that corner above the chimney breast, worst it's ever been. I'm catching it in a bucket three nights out of four, and the plaster's gone soft up there, you can push your thumb reet through it now.

The neighbour: How long's this been going on then, all told.

Me: Since before Christmas near enough. First time I told him he says he'd get a fella out "next week." That were January, mind. Asked again in March, same tale, "next week, love, I've got him booked in." Nowt come of it. Asked a third time last week, told him it's coming through the ceiling now, and he comes straight back wi' same excuse again, fella's let down, he'll sort it.

The neighbour: Mithering you wi' nowt but promises, him. Right, you've a few roads you can go down. Ring the council, environmental health, get 'em round to look, that puts it on record proper, only it can take a while. Or you write to him, recorded post, give him a date on it, and if he does owt then you get the work done yourself and take it off the rent, but you want that in writing first or you're on shaky ground. Or keep ringing and hope he cracks, though three month of nowt says that's bobbins as an idea.

Me: It's a right worry, this.

The neighbour: I know, love. I had one years back, same tale exact, water coming through over t' stairs, "next week" every time I rang. Six month that went on. Got onto environmental health in the end and he had a fella round within a fortnight, funny that.

Me: Appen that's the one to try then.

The neighbour: Aye, get it in writing first, then straight onto the council if he don't shift. Ta-ra for now, and give us a knock when you've rung 'em.

Me: Will do. Ta for the brew, love.
