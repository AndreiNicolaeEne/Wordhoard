I were later out than I meant, and by time I set off the sky had gone that flat black it goes this time o'year, nowt like the walk I'm used to. Weren't two minute down the road when I got the sense there were someone behind us, footsteps landing about same time as mine, near enough matching step for step. Didn't want to look daft turning round for nowt, so I slowed myself down, just a touch, thinking if it were nobody the steps'd carry on past. They slowed an' all. So I picked it back up sharpish, and same again, whoever it were kept pace, reet close behind, I could hear the scuff of their shoes on th' flags.

Usual way's through the ginnel at back of the shops but I weren't having that, not that night, so I cut the long way round by the main road where the lights are and there's still folk stood outside the chippy. Allowed myself the one glance back at the crossing, quick as owt, and there were somebody there right enough, appen forty yard off, but you couldn't tell nowt off that, could've been anyone walking home same road as me.

Last stretch up our road I were fair flying, quicker than I'd ever let on to a soul, and getting the door shut and bolted behind us were the best feeling going.

Mother: You alright, chuck? You've gone a funny colour.

Me: Aye. Just walked quick, that's all. Give over asking, I'm reet.

Never did find out if it were owt or nowt. No idea to this day, and I doubt I ever will be. I tell it now half laughing at myself, at how dead sure I were in the moment that someone had it in for us, when for all I know it were just some other bugger walking home same road, same time, gradely nowt going on at all. Made up to be indoors, any road. Ta-ra to the shortcut for a good while after that.
