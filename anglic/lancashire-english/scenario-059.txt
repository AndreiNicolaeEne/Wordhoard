It were just gone dusk when everything went off, telly and all, one big clonk from t' fuse box and then nowt. I thought it were just us at first, till I heard next door shouting summat through t' wall, so it were the whole street, leccy gone down proper.

Mother: Give over stood there gawping, go and find summat to light wi'.

We were rooting through t' kitchen drawer like a right rabble, torch in there wi' no batteries in it, matches gone soft and wouldn't strike. Found one candle stub off back of a cupboard, half melted down already from Christmas, and that were it, that were all we had between the lot of us.

Our kid: There's not enough of that to go round is there.

Me: There is if tha stops mithering and shares it.

So we set it in a saucer on t' table and everyone huddled round it like it were a fire, though it give off next to nowt. The eldest went to fetch summat off t' sideboard and cracked her shin on it, same corner as last time, and skriked summat awful, then went and did it again not ten minutes after, said the room had shifted in t' dark.

Eldest: I'll have a right bruise there, that's twice now.

Mother: Appen you'll learn where it is by t' third go.

Father said there were no sense fiddling wi' t' fuse box in the pitch black, better to leave it and see to it reet in daylight, so we give that up an all. We just sat close in that one bit of light, had us a brew that were gone lukewarm and a butty each off what were left in t' bread bin, no barm cakes to be had, just the end of a loaf, and it were made up enough all the same, considering.

We sat talking a good while, nattering on about owt and nowt, then one by one give in and went up to bed still in t' dark, feeling our way along same as we'd been doing all night. Leccy never did come back on. Put wood in th'ole on your way up, love. Ta-ra then, night all.
