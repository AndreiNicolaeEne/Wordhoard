Right, I'll tell yers it proper, start to finish, cos yer'll only mither us for it otherwise if I leave bits out.

Up dead early I were, before t'alarm even went off, stomach doing summat daft, nerves like yer wouldn't believe. Walked down through t'ginnel in the dark thinkin', what 'ave I gone an' done here.

Get there an' the fella runnin' t'place were stood waitin' on us at the door.

Boss: Yer must be the new one. Reet, in yer come, put wood in th'ole behind yer, it's parky out.

Gave us a tour, quick like, then handed us summat to be gettin' on wi'.

Boss: Just do that for now, nowt fancy, yer'll pick rest up as we go.

I were bracin' missen for summat harder if I'm honest, thought they'd chuck us in deep end straight off, but it weren't owt like that, more or less what I reckoned, appen a bit less if owt.

Went an' made a right show of missen an' all, mind, dropped a whole stack o' the things clean on t'floor in front of everyone. Face like a beetroot I were.

Me: Sorry, sorry, I've got it...

Nob'dy bothered though. One of 'em just laughed, said it happens to everyone first week.

Break time I sat wi' me brew an' me butty, barm cake, none o' yer cob nonsense, just weighin' 'em all up, who's who, who yer don't wanna get on wrong side of. One woman on there were the one though, come over wi'out even bein' asked, showed us proper way to do it.

Her workmate: Do it like this, love, saves yer arm hangin' off by dinner time.

Proper gradely woman that were, made all the difference.

Pace of it were faster than I'd braced for an' all, nine hour on me feet, belter of a shift, legs like jelly by t'end of it.

Walked home goin' over it all in me head, every last bit.

The mother: Well? How were it then?

Me: Belting, honest. Hard graft, mind, but belting. I'm made up.

Our kid: Bet yer were bobbins at summat though.

Me: Aye, dropped stuff everywhere didn't I, but give over, it were reet good day all told. Ta for askin'. I'm shattered though. I'll tell rest tomorrow. Ta-ra.
