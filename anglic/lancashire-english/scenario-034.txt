The one at the counter: Now then, chuck. Barm cake, bacon, brown sauce, no butter, and a brew, milk no sugar. That it?
Me: Aye, that's me, but stick an egg on an' all today, I'm clemmed.
The one at the counter: Egg an' all. Tha's spoiling thysen. Sit thi down, won't be two ticks.
Me: Ta. Bloody freezing out there int it.
The one at the counter: It is that. Put wood in th'ole when tha's in, save us all catching us death.
Me: Been a reet long week, our kid's had me running round after her lot again, mithering me for babysitting every five minutes, and the littlest were skriking half the morning an' all.
The one at the counter: Ha, that's kids for thee. How's tha keeping otherwise?
Me: Can't complain. Tired, but nowt a brew won't sort.
The one at the counter: How's work then, still working thee to death?
Me: Same as ever, love. Nowt changes there. How's trade been with thee?
The one at the counter: Mustn't grumble. Bit quiet Mondays, but weekend were belting, queue right out to th' ginnel, and we're doing butties for the market lot at dinner an' all now.
Me: Get away, that's gradely that.
The one at the counter: Aye, mind you, Tuesday were bobbins, rained the whole day, nob'dy about but a couple of lads off the market.
Me: Appen it'll pick up.
The one at the counter: Appen. Anyroad, tha wants to stop coming in here every day, tha'll have me down to nowt.
Me: Give over, it's only three times a week!
The one at the counter: Three? Appen it's more like four and tha's just bad at counting, chuck.
Me: Cheeky sod, that's slander that.
The one at the counter: There y'are, love. One barm with egg, and thi brew. Get that scran down thee before it goes cold.
Me: Ta very much. What do I owe thee?
The one at the counter: Same as ever, three eighty.
Me: There y'go. Right, best get on, things to do.
The one at the counter: Aye, go on then. See thee Thursday, chuck.
Me: Ta-ra.
