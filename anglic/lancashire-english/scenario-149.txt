The eldest: I bless thee, and this'll come true as I say it: gradely luck on thee, th'door never latched against a soul, th'table allus laid.
The eldest: But it turns now, same oath th'other road.
The eldest: I curse thee then, and this'll come true an' all: nowt but bad luck on thee, th'door allus latched against thy own kin, th'table allus bare.
The eldest: Blessing or curse, chuck, both were spoke true, and both'll stand.
