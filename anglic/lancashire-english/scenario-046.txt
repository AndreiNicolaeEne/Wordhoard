The buyer: How much for them, then?
The seller: Twelve for th' lot, chuck.
The buyer: Twelve? Give over. There's a soft'un in that top one. I'll gi' thi eight.
The seller: Nay, eight's nowt, these are gradely apples them.
The buyer: Appen we meet i' th' middle.
The seller: Go on then, ten, and that's me final word.
The buyer: Ta. Reet, ten it is.
