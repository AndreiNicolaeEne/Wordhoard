Me: Reet, put wood in th'ole, it's parky in here. Get that brew down thee and let's have this out proper, cos I've been chewing it ovver all week and it's doing me no good at all.

The rival: Chewing what ovver? There's nowt to chew ovver. We shook hands at th' end and that were that, weren't it.

Me: Was it though. Cos I've had another think on it since, and that opening o' thine were bobbins from the first move, that were. Tha should never have gone into it that road. If tha'd opened up different, proper different, this whole match goes another way altogether.

The rival: Give over. I opened how I opened cos that's how I'd read thee sat across from me, and at the time it were sound as owt. Tha can sit there now, chuck, wi' all the time in world on thi hands and tell me it were wrong, but in the minute, wi' the clock ticking down and thee stood waiting, that were the reet move and I'd play it again.

Me: Aye, well hindsight's a gradely thing to have, and it's telling me tha'd have had a different match on thi hands altogether wi' owt else played. But go on then. Early doors were even enough between us, no sense argy-bargying ovver them, we both know that much.

The rival: We do. Traded fair, nowt much in it either road, right up to that move, what were it, twenty-two?, when tha reckons it all went wrong for me.

Me: Twenty-two, aye. And it weren't "reckons," love, it were a plain mistake and nowt more complicated than that. Tha moved that piece where tha'd no business moving it and give me the whole board on a plate. Halfway through the match and tha just handed it me.

The rival: It were sound wi' what I knew then! Tha's judging it off what came after, and that's not fair judging at all, that's judging backwards. In that minute, wi' what were on the board in front of me, I'd have done the very same again nine times out of ten and thought nowt of it.

Me: Well it went same as it went. And then a few moves on we had that exchange, the costly one, and tha's still blaming my play for how it turned out rather than owning thine own part in it.

The rival: Cos it were thy play an' all that caused it! Tha forced the whole thing, I only answered how a body answers when there's nowt else for it. If tha hadn't gone in like that, there'd have been nowt for me to lose in the first place.

Me: By the time we got past that little to-do, the game were gone, plain and simple. I'll tell thee straight, it were lost by move thirty, no messing, and there's no arguing me out of it.

The rival: It were nowt like lost. Give over wi' that. I still had chances sat there, I were still in it right up to near the end, whatever tha wants to tell thisen now.

Me: Tha weren't in it, but go on, tell us ovver that little comeback tha tried, the one wi' the pawns, cos even that were too late by then, weren't it. Too late to matter one road or t'other.

The rival: It weren't too late. It were close, it were that close. If I'd had two more moves in hand I'd have had thee stuck, I'd have turned the whole thing round.

Me: Two more moves and tha'd have had summat, appen, but there were one move right there and then, that one wi' the rook swinging round, that could've saved it dead easy if tha'd played it instead o' what tha did go and play. Tha saw it an' all, don't tell me tha didn't, I know tha saw it.

The rival: I saw it, aye, I'll give thee that much. Doesn't mean it were the reet one to play at the time, wi' everything else going off on the board.

Me: Well we both know what the last move were. No arguing that bit, we've never argued that bit. Question's only ever been what it meant, and we're not agreeing on that in a hurry.

The rival: It meant tha got lucky right at the end of a game tha'd no business winning off the way it had gone.

Me: Forty-one moves it ran to, start to finish, and that's about the one thing we've managed to agree on all afternoon, sat here.

The rival: Aye. Forty-one, that's reet. And at the time we just shook on it and said nowt more, didn't we, straight up and off home. No skriking about it, no mithering on, just done.

Me: We did that. And now here we are going ovver it again like a pair o' nowts wi' nowt better to do, arguing whose game were really the stronger the whole way through, regardless of how it ended up on the board.

The rival: Cos mine were the stronger, that's why. I had thee rattled from the first move, tha knows I did, tha's just not saying it.

Me: Tha's dreaming, chuck, proper dreaming. Think on that other match we had, few year back now, went the very same road exactly, tha come at it the very same way and it went the very same way an' all in the end. That's a pattern, that is, plain as owt.

The rival: Give ovver comparing that one to this one. It's nowt like this match, different game altogether, different board, different day. Tha's just after summat to hang thi argument on now cos tha's got nowt fresh left to say.

Me: I've plenty fresh left to say and I'm not budging one inch on any of it.

The rival: Nor me neither, not one inch, not for thee nor for nob'dy.

Me: Right then. Get that other brew on and pass us that barm cake, mine's gone stone cold sat here arguing wi' thee.

The rival: Ta for that, at least. Owt else out of thee and I'm off home, I mean it.

Me: Ta-ra then, love. Same time next week and we'll not have this out again, will we.

The rival: We will an' all, tha knows we will. Every time.

Me: Aye. Every time.
