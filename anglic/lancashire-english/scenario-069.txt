Our cat's been missing three days now. Last seen down th' ginnel end o' our street, tabby wi' one white paw, dead friendly wi' strangers, she'll come reet up to owt that stops and talks soft. No bother, no skriking, just wants a fuss. Small reward if tha finds her, nowt fancy, just made up to have her back. Number's on th' strips below, tear one off and gi us a ring. Ta.
