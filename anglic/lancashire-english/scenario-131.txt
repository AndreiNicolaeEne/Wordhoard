The eldest: Eh up, is that rain?

Mother: It is an' all, get to th' window, quick!

Our kid: It's comin' down proper, look at it!

Mother: Ta God for that, we've needed it weeks, nowt but dust out there.

The eldest: Gradely, that is, gradely! Give it here, I'll have the lot of it!

Mother: Give over, you soft ha'porth, you'll be soaked through.

Our kid: Appen it'll not stop this time.

Mother: Shurrup mitherin' and listen, love.

They stood out in it, saying nowt, just listening to it come down.
