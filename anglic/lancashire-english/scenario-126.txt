Me: I'm telling thee, summat weird happened last neet coming through th' ginnel off Fylde Road.

My mate: Aye, tha were half asleep, mithering theesen over nowt. What time were it, one in't morning?

Me: Give over, I were wide awake, wasn't I. Just had me scran at our kid's, a barm cake and a brew, thinking clear as owt.

My mate: Go on then, what happened.

Me: I'm walking down th' ginnel and the lamp at th' end flickers off, like it does. And there's someone just stood there. Not moving, no face on 'em, just stood. Lamp flickers back on - nowt. Gone.

My mate: Appen it were a shadow off them bins, tha knows how dark it gets down there.

Me: It weren't no shadow. Only way out that end's chained shut, has been since the council locked it after the trouble. Whoever it were, they'd have had to open that gate and it skrikes like owt when it moves, it always has. I never heard a sound.

My mate: Reet, well maybe some kid off next street jumped the wall for a laugh, then.

Me: Six foot wall with glass a top? In two second, no noise, and the dog never so much as twitched - and that dog barks at a leaf falling off a tree. Summat weren't reet about it, I'm telling thee, and it's mithering me summat rotten.

My mate: I'm not being funny, chuck, but it sounds like tha'd nodded off walking, that's all it is.

Me: I swear on our kid's life, every word of it's true. I've never been more awake in me life, I were thinking clear as day.

My mate: Aye well, believe what tha wants, I don't. Sounds bobbins to me.

Me: Belting, more like, and I'll say it till I'm blue. Put wood in th'ole, I'm off to bed. Ta-ra.
