Woke up reet early, appen half five, and there were water at th' door already. Only ankle deep, mind, just enough to wet your slippers if you were daft enough to stand in it, which I were. I thought, appen it's nowt, appen it's just the drain in th' ginnel backed up again like it does. Had a brew, tried not to mither meself over it.

Hour later I looked again and it were up past me ankle proper now, creeping under th' door and across the kitchen lino. That were when I stopped messing about and started shifting stuff up. Our kid's coat off the bottom of the stairs, the toaster, owt that were sat low went up high while there were still time to move it.

By midmorning it were at me knee. Knee height, in me own kitchen. Everything I could lift went onto whatever stood highest, chairs on the table, boxes on the chairs, and still it kept climbing, and it never stopped, not all morning.

The neighbour rang us.

Neighbour: You alright there, love?

Me: Aye, just about, it's up to me knee here.

Neighbour: Ours an all. Give over, it's not stopping is it.

It peaked early afternoon, past the highest owt had been put, right past the table, near topping the worktop, and I just stood there listening to it slap against the walls for hours, nowt to do but wait. Made a butty I couldn't even eat, scran going stale on the side. Then, a good few hours after, it started to go down at last. Slow, but going down.

Went back next day and there were a tide-line reet round the kitchen, dark and proper marked, showing exactly how high it had got. Stood there a good while just looking at it. Gradely mess, that were. Nowt for it now but to get scrubbing. Ta for asking, anyroad. Ta-ra.
