I were sat out back havin me brew when I first clocked it, just a smudge o' smoke reet out on th' horizon, early afternoon it were, nowt to fret about, just summat to watch like. Thought appen it were only some farmer burnin off a field, so I got on wi' me barm cake and give it no more thought.

Within th' hour though wind had turned and it were comin on quick, a lot closer than I'd like, and me eyes started stingin off it, that catches th' back o' thi throat an' all. Our kid come runnin in off th' ginnel shoutin the sky were gone orange over th' field end, and that were th' minute watchin turned to leavin.

I moved reet fast after that, no mitherin about it, twenty minute and I were out th' door wi' what mattered and nowt else, didn't even put wood in th'ole behind us we went that sharp. Me throat were raw as owt, heat pressin on me face like an oven door stood open, and I got clear and looked back once, just th' once, and it were lit up summat fearful against th' dark.

Two nights away not knowin, stoppin wi' me sister.

Sister: Give over frettin, chuck, they'll let thee back when it's safe.

Me: I can't help it, I keep thinkin on th' house.

Sister: Appen it'll be reet. Have a brew and a butty and stop thi skrikin.

Then word come we could go back, and I tell thee I were made up, proper made up, whole street spared, not a brick touched. Belting, that were, after two days o' bobbins waitin about not knowin owt. Give over askin am I alright, I said, I'm reet now, ta, just gradely to be home. Ta-ra to that fright, any road.
