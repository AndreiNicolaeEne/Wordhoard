The sister: Eh, it's come! Six o'clock this morning, on t' dot!
Me: Give over, come in first, put wood in th'ole. Who's had it?
The sister: Our kid's, love. Third one now, a little lass, gradely little thing, appen taking after her mother.
Me: Six o'clock, reet? No bother with it?
The sister: Nowt wrong at all, she were made up.
Me: I'm made up an' all. Get your coat, we're off up there now, none o' this waiting till after brew.
The sister: Ta.
