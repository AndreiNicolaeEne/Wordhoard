Me: Put wood in th'ole, will you, there's a right draught coming up that ginnel.

The friend: Eh, never mind that, wait till I tell thee. I've had a right result, me. Money's landed, out the blue, I'm made up, honest, I've had to tell someone before I burst.

Me: Ooh, get you! That's belting, that is! Dead made up for you, I am, champion news!

Me: Go on then, don't leave us hanging, how much we on about, like?

The friend: Eleven and a half grand, give or take. I've checked it three times, still can't believe it were reet.

Me: Eleven and a half grand. Bloody hell. So how's that come about then, what've you gone and done to land a windfall like that? Must be gradely being you, mind, some of us are still mithering our kid for forty quid he's owed us since before Christmas and we'll be lucky to see it this side of Whit week.

The friend: You alright? You've gone a bit funny there, summat up wi' you?

Me: Nowt wrong wi' you, love, honest, it's me. I'll be straight wi' you, it's envy, plain as that, nowt more sinister to it. Seeing you land that when I'm scraping by proper, it got us, that's all.

The friend: Appen I'd be same road round, if it were t'other way about.

Me: Ta for that. But listen, I do mean it now, not just saying it like before, I am reet made up for you, gradely news that is, best I've heard in ages. Put kettle on, we'll have a brew and a butty, mek a do of it.

The friend: Ta-ra to worrying about owt else today, eh.

Me: Aye. Give over now and drink your brew, you daft article.
