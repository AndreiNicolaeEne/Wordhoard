Me: Ey up, chuck.

My friend: Ey up. Come in, kettle's just boiled, I'll mash thee a brew.

Me: Go on then, ta. Eh it's parky out there in't it.

My friend: Reet parky. Shut door, you're letting all't heat out down th' ginnel and all.

Me: Sorry, sorry. Ooh, is that a barm cake going spare? Don't tell me it's a cob, I'll not have it in't house.

My friend: Give over, course it's a barm, what do you take us for. Get it down thee.

Me: Gradely, that. Owt good on telly last night?

My friend: Nowt worth watching, same as ever. You alright, you? You've been mithering round summat since you got in, going round the houses.

Me: Have I? Appen I have, aye. Look... I weren't gonna ask, but, could you lend us summat? Like, eighty quid.

My friend: Eighty quid. Bloody hell. What's it for then?

Me: Oh, it's nowt much, honest, just th' leccy's a bit behind and I'm a bit short till payday, that's all it is, nowt to skrike about.

My friend: Nowt to skrike about, right. Well go on then, I'll sort thee, but I want it back, mind, not left owt.

Me: Course, course. Friday after next, soon as I'm paid, it's back in your hand, I swear down.

My friend: Friday after next then. Not month after, I mean it.

Me: Friday after next. You're an absolute diamond, you are, honest, best mate anyone could have, I'll not forget this, ever, you're proper gradely...

My friend: Give over, you're going to have us blartin' in a minute. Put wood in th'ole and drink your brew before it goes cold.

Me: Ta. Ta-ra for now, I'll be off, our kid'll be wondering where I've got to.

My friend: Ta-ra, chuck.
