Me: Ey up, put wood in th'ole, it's parky. Right, if we copped that big win tonight, first thing I'd have is a barm cake from t'good shop, none o' your cob rubbish, and I'd have it wi' a proper brew and all.

Our kid: Tha's just won a fortune and tha's thinking about a barm cake? Give over. I'd be straight off to get us a house wi' a decent ginnel out back so nob'dy can mither us, and a car an' all, summat daft and fast.

Me: A car! Aye go on then, I'd have one too, red un, and I'd never walk to t'shop again in me life.

Our kid: We can't spend it all on scran and motors though. I were thinking we ought put half on it away somewhere, savings like, so it's there for when we're old and past it.

Me: Boring get. Appen we should just buy a pub instead, run it ourselves, free brew for us every night.

Our kid: Ha! Tha's bobbins at pulling pints, tha'd have the whole place bankrupt in a week.

Me: I would an' all, that's belting though, imagine it, our own pub, no landlord to answer to.

Our kid: Reet, here's what we do then. Half away safe, quarter on summat sensible like the house, and the last bit we blow on nowt useful at all, just for laugh.

Me: Now that's gradely, that is. Proper compromise.

Our kid: Course, we've nowt actually won yet, have we.

Me: Nay. Not a penny. We're sat here skint as owt planning a pub we'll never own.

Our kid: Give over, we've been on about this half an hour and it's not even a real ticket.

Me: I know, I know. Daft, aren't we.

Our kid: Made up we are though, eh, dreaming.

Me: Ta for the laugh anyroad. Ta-ra, love, I'm off to bed skint and happy.
