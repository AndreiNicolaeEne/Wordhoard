Me: Reet, what about half seven, top o' th' ginnel by chippy, get us all some scran after?

My mate: Half seven's bobbins for our kid, he's not off work till gone seven, still graftin'.

Me: Appen quarter to eight then, give him chance to sort hissen out.

My mate: That'll do him. What about her from work, is she in?

Me: Aye, she were made up when I asked her, said she's free from six.

My mate: Good. And your cousin, he coming an' all?

Me: Nah, give him a miss, he's skint, no bus fare, and his shift dun't finish till nine anyroad.

My mate: Ahh shame that, he's belting company usually.

Me: I know, nowt we can do about it though.

My mate: What about her next door, and the one that were poorly last week?

Me: Her next door's in, said she'll fetch a brew flask an' all. Other one's still mithering on about being under t'weather, best leave her be for now.

My mate: Fair do's. So quarter to eight it is then, at th' ginnel?

Me: Give over, I'd have said eight meself, but quarter to eight'll do, don't want us sat there like lemons.

My mate: Reet, sound. I'll tell rest o' group, save thee mithering everyone thysen.

Me: Ta, chuck, that's gradely of thee.

My mate: So summat like six, mebbe seven if others sort thesens out in time.

Me: Aye, summat like that. Right, put wood in th'ole, I'm off.

My mate: Ta-ra.

Me: Ta-ra love.
