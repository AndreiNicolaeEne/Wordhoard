Eh, chuck, put wood in th'ole and sit thi sen down a minute, I'll tell thee about that carry-on at t'office, when I needed that form of mine stamped that same day or the whole job were off. Had to be handed in before four, else our kid's start date at t'new place went with it, and I weren't waiting another fortnight for nowt.

So I nips down through t'ginnel to save us five minutes, get there dead on dinnertime, no time for a brew nor a barm cake nor owt, and there's this fella behind t'counter going through me papers ever so slow, like he's got all week. And he goes, "These can take a while to come through, you know... unless." And just leaves it sat there in't air, looking at us over his glasses.

I just stood there, played daft as owt, went "oh aye, right enough" and smiled, didn't bite at all.

So he has another go, bit plainer this time, "a lot of folk find it moves same day if they... appreciate the effort, tha knows what I mean."

I says, "Oh, there's a fee list up on t'wall there in't there, I'll pay that one, that's reet enough for us." Told him I weren't in no rush, appen I'd just come back another day if it come to it, and our kid's other half works two doors down at t'housing office anyroad, she'd soon know if owt were amiss with a form like this.

Proper tense few minutes that were, him looking at us, me looking at him, neither one saying nowt, just the clock ticking. Then he give over, stamped it, handed it back, no more said about it.

Got me paper, no bother, no brass changed hands, home for scran and a butty after, made up I were.

That's how you deal with one o' them, love, mithering at folk for a bit extra when they're just after their paper doing. Bobbins, the lot of it. Ta-ra.
