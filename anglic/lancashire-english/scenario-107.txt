She held her close a minute before letting go.

The mother: Reet, get thi off now or tha'll miss it. Take care on that road, chuck, don't be doing owt daft.

The traveler: I won't. Give over fussing.

The mother: Ring us soon as tha lands, I'll be mithering else.

The traveler: I will, promise.

The mother: Tek this, few bob for scran on t'way.

The traveler: Ta. I'll bring thee back summat gradely. Ta-ra.

The mother: Ta-ra, love.
