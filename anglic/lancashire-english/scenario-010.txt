The mother: Reet, who's broken this then?

The eldest: Weren't me.

The mother: I've not asked were it thee, I've asked who's done it. Somebody in this house has broke mi good vase and I want to know who.

The eldest: I said, weren't me. I were up in mi room the whole afternoon.

The youngest: It weren't me neither. Th'window in there were open and there come a right gust through, must've caught it off th'shelf. Or appen it were t'cat, jumping up after summat.

The mother: Cat's been out since dinner, chuck, and that window's been shut since Tuesday. I shut it misen.

The youngest: ...well. Appen it were open a crack, then.

The eldest: She's making it up. She were in there earlier, mithering about, I seen her through t'ginnel window.

The youngest: I never! You're the one always in there wi' your hands on me things.

The eldest: I weren't near it, I told thee. Well. I went past it, but I never touched nowt.

The mother: Near it now, are we. Minute ago tha were upstairs the whole time.

The eldest: I were an' all! I just went past on mi way for a brew and a butty.

The mother: Give over, both on you, going at each other like that's going to help. Nob'dy's owning up so nob'dy's having their scran tonight, you'll share what's going and think on. Next time summat gets broke in this house I want the truth first time asking, not a load o' bobbins.

The youngest: That's not gradely, I never even...

The mother: I've heard enough skriking. Go on, out from under mi feet, and put wood in th'ole on your way through, there's a draught come up the ginnel.
