Neighbour: 'Ere, did tha hear owt outside last night? Summat woke me, gone midnight, out in't street.
Me: Aye, heard it an' all. Thought it were a cat knocked summat ovver.
Neighbour: Appen, but it were too loud for a cat if tha asks me.
Me: Fox then, or some soft sod trippin' home.
Neighbour: Could've been owt, chuck. We'll keep an ear out if it goes again t'night.
Me: Aye, we will. Ta-ra.
