The eldest: Ickle ockle, blue bockle, fishes in th' sea, if tha wants a pretty maid, tek one, two, three, out goes thee.

The one counted out: Give o'er, appen tha's gone too fast, skipped our kid, I'm 'avin a do-over.

Another: Mither on about it an' we start again from t' top, them's rules, reet.

The eldest, louder: Ickle ockle, blue bockle, fishes in th' sea, if tha wants a pretty maid, tek one, two, three, out goes thee.

The one counted out: Aye, fair's fair, I were out then, give o'er.
