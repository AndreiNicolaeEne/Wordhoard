The child: Mum, can I have that bike, the one in the shop window on t' precinct?

The mother: Not this week, love, no.

The child: But why not, though?

The mother: 'Cos we've nowt spare for it, that's the plain truth of it. Your dad's shifts got cut back last month and the leccy's gone up summat shocking. It's not that I don't want thee to have it, chuck, there's just nowt there for it right now.

The child: I could do more chores though. I'll do the bins every week and I'll not mither you of a morning for owt.

The mother: Appen that'd help round the house, but chores don't put brass in the pot, love. It's nowt to do wi' thee being good, it's to do wi' there being nowt there.

The child: What about for me birthday then?

The mother: If your dad's back on full shifts by then, we'll see, and I mean that, I'm not saying reet out no forever. If summat changes before then, I'll not forget thee asked.

The child: But he's got one already. Him from down th' ginnel, he's had a belting one since Tuesday.

The mother: Aye well, what his mam and dad can stretch to is theirs, chuck, it's nowt to do wi' what we've got in this house. Give over measuring us against next door, it gets me nowhere, that.

The child: 's not fair though.

The mother: I know, love, I know. Tell thee what. I'll get them old brakes seen to on thine, and we'll have a proper barm and a brew for us tea, none o' that bobbins out the machine. That'll have to do thee for now.

The child: ...S'pose. Ta.

The mother: Good lass. Now put wood in th'ole, you're letting all t' cold in.
