Now then, sit thi down. Tha's been sat there mithering thysen sick, wondering whether to pack that job in. Tha knows, a barm cake left on th' side's like worry, chuck: leave it too long and it only goes harder, not softer. So put wood in th'ole and get on wi' it, appen it'll come reet. Give over frettin'. Nobody else is going to sort this for thee, love. Tha has to get up and do it thysen.
