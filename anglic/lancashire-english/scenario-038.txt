Eh, chuck, sit thee down a minute, I'm done in. Ten hour I've had of it and I'm only just put wood in th'ole behind me.

Started this mornin' before I were reet ready for owt, I'll tell thee that much. Gaffer had us on site for half seven an' I'd barely had a brew down me. First job were shiftin' a stack o' blocks off back o' wagon, an' that set the pace for rest o' day and all, 'cause he had us at that speed the whole time after, no messin' about.

By about ten me hands were skrikin' off it, proper raw they were, an' not long after that me back started an' all, tellin' me summat about it. We got us ten minutes for a brew an' a butty round eleven, but that were nowt near long enough, gone before you'd sat down proper.

Afternoon were the worst of it, I won't lie to thee. Two o'clock onwards were belting hard graft, arms like lead, and I did think about tellin' him I were off home, appen nobody'd have blamed us. Nearly give over more than once.

Mind, one bit went gradely, had to dig a trench out back an' I thought it'd take us the rest o' the day, but it were done by half three, proper lift that were in an otherwise bobbins sort of a day.

Gaffer's payin' us forty for it, cash, owed me that fair and square.

Dunno what tomorrow holds yet, chuck. Might be same again, might be he gives us a day to let body mend. Either road, I'm off to me bed now. Ta for listenin'. Ta-ra.
