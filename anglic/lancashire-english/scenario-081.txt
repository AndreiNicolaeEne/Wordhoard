The cousin: Now then, chuck, our kid said tha's not been reet. How bad is it, honest?

Me: Eh, I've had worse but I've had better an' all. Started three days back, ached in every joint like I'd been under a bus, and me throat were that scratchy I could hardly swallow me own spit. Then yesterday a fever come on top of it, proper rotten do that were.

The cousin: Bloody hell. And today?

Me: Bit easier than yesterday, I'll give it that, but still nowhere near reet. Not gradely, put it that way.

The cousin: What you been doing about it, owt sensible or just sat there?

Me: Aye, done what me mam always did. Gargled warm salted water for me throat first off, that's the one that never fails. Fever wouldn't fully break on its own so I've had a cool cloth on me forehead, changing it through the night every time I come round.

The cousin: Eating owt?

Me: Nowt much, just broth and weak tea, no barm cake and no scran going down, don't fancy it. Slept whenever me body'd let me, which weren't as much as I'd have liked.

The cousin: Well tha wants to rest proper then, none of this pushing through it like you always do, you're bobbins for that.

Me: I know, I know, give over. I'm not going nowhere. If the fever climbs any further, or me breathing turns hard, I'll get meself seen, but not before that, I'm not mithering the doctor over a bit of a cold.

The cousin: Want us to bring you owt over, or I could sit with you an hour, take the weight off?

Me: Ah, you're alright, ta though, appen later in the week if I'm still bunged up like this. Give us a ring tomorrow and I'll tell you how I am. Ta-ra for now, chuck.
