The stranger: Ey up, sorry to mither thee, but appen tha's got a drop o' water goin' spare? Proper parched, me.

The one at the door: Course I have, love, hang on. (hands over a full cup)

The stranger: Oh nay, that's too much, a sip'd have done...

The one at the door: Give over, get it down thee, tha needs it more than a sip.

The stranger: Ta, that's reet gradely of thee, that is. Ta-ra.
