Woke up this mornin and it hit me before me eyes were even open right, what today were. Lay there a good five minute just starin at th' ceilin thinkin, oh give over, why did I ever open me gob and swear it. Got up moanin the whole way to bathroom, mitherin to nob'dy about me own daft mouth. Should've just kept schtum, shouldn't tha. Swearin things when tha's had enough, that's how tha ends up here. Put wood in th'ole on way out so the cold didn't follow us up the stairs, grabbed a brew and a barm cake off the side, not a cob, not a roll, a proper barm, and set off still grumblin under me breath the length of the ginnel.

Our kid: Well tha said tha'd do it though.
