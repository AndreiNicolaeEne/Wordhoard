Traveler: 'Ow much to town then?
Driver: Twenty, love.
Traveler: Twenty? Give over, I'll gi' thee twelve.
Driver: Twelve's bobbins, that's takin' liberties, that is. I were gonna do thee a favour, not rob meself.
Traveler: Appen we split it, sixteen?
Driver: Go on then, seein' as it's thee - sixteen. Reet, made up wi' that. Hop in, chuck.
Traveler: Ta.
