Just crossing ower by t'ginnel end, same as every day, when this car comes reet at me wi' no warning at all, nowt. I were in t'road one second and jumping for wall t'next, afore me mind had even caught up. Felt the wind of it going past, swear I did, that close. Been home five minutes now and me hands still won't stop shaking, can't even hold this brew steady.
