The traveler: Ey up, sorry to mither thee, but which road's t' market from here?

The stranger: No bother love. Tha wants first left down by th' ginnel, then keep on till tha sees the clock tower, that's thy second corner, tha can't miss it.

The traveler: Champion, ta. How long's the walk, would tha say?

The stranger: Appen fifteen minute if tha's not dawdling. Reet gradely day for it an' all.

The traveler: Ta-ra then.

The stranger: Ta-ra, love.
