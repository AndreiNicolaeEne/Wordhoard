First one were round ours dinnertime, stood in th' kitchen still in her coat, going on before I'd even got kettle on.

First friend: I'll tell thee summat for nowt, I'm proper cheesed off. She stood there in front of everyone at do and said I'd never once bought her a brew back in three year. In front of everyone, chuck. That's just not reet, is it, I bought her one at Christmas.

Me: Right. And what did tha say back to her?

First friend: Nowt. I just give over and walked off up ginnel, left her to it. I'm not one for making a scene, me.

She went not long after, ta-ra and off, and I thought no more of it till t'other one were on doorstep an hour later, mithering to be let in.

Second friend: She's told thee her version then, has she. Well it's not how it were. I never said owt about no brew, I said she never buys her round, that's different, and I only come out wi' it 'cause our kid were stood there having a go at me about summat else. I never started it, she did, going off in a huff like she allus does.

Me: Appen. But tell me this, when tha thinks about it proper, when did it first start feeling off between thee two? Before the do, I mean.

Second friend: Since she got in wi' that new lot at work, if tha's asking. Barely gives me time of day since. Calls a barm cake a roll now an' all, that's how far gone she is.

Me: So it's not the brew at all, is it.

Second friend: Appen not. Appen I do feel a bit shoved out, aye, if I'm honest.

Me: Well don't skrike about it to me, love, talk to her thi sen, face to face. I'll get you both round, brew and a butty, and you can sort it proper between thee.

Second friend: Ta.
