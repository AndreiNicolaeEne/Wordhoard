Aayoh, mek mi tell yu how dis house weh mi da stand up eena come to be, cau nobody neva deh deh fi si di whole ting from start to finish, an it long, bwai, it long.

Wi neva plan fi build back. Di old house mi da stay eena mi grow up eena, an dat house good, but wi outgrow it. Di pickney dem multiply, di eldest married an dehn bring anodda pickney inna di mix, an di whole a wi couldn't fit eena three room no more. Yu deh ova one anodda, yu waan turn yu cyaan turn. So one evening di whole family si dong pon di step an wi reckon it out.

The mother: Unu tink how much time dis wahn tek?

My workmate: If wi get tings right an di weather no trouble wi, six, seven month.

The eldest: An who a go help wi build dis ya house, cau a nuh unu one can lift dis kinda weight.

So wi count it out. Wi seh maybe three, four hand besides mi an di eldest. Wi neva know it woulda tek six different people all total, comin an gwehn as dehn could, an wi neva know it woulda tek eleven month, start to di day it stand finish. Life nuh tell yu dem tings from start.

Wi start di foundation early, di dry season jus a come een, an di very first week wi meck wahn bad mistake. Di man weh mi ask fi help mi lay out di line dem, we neva check di level good, an by di time wi lay two course a block wi realize di whole wall a lean. Steups. Two week a hard hard wuk, gone jus so, cau wi haffi knock i dong an start ova. My workmate come look pon it an him jus shake him head.

My workmate: Yu si dis ya? Dis nuh gwehn stand. Betta wi tear i dong now dan wi wait til di roof pon top an den it faal.

So wi tear i dong. Two week gone. But afta dat wi neva mek dat mistake again, wi check di level like it a exam wi haffi pass.

Den one Saturday, an mi neva call nobody, mi jus deh deh a mix cement pon mi own, an di bredda from down di street come, him bredda come wid him, an mi cousin husband come too, all a dehn jus show up wid dehn tool bag. Mi neva ask dehn.

The one from down the street: Wi si yu deh out ya every day pon yu own, wi seh come gi wahn hand.

Dat day wi get more wuk done dan di whole week befo. Dat a di kinda ting weh mek yu believe eena people, when dehn come widout being call.

But den di rain start. It rain fi like five, six day straight, di kinda rain weh come dong an nuh stop, road turn to mud, an wi cyaan wuk cau di block dem get soak an di lumber gettin wet an everyting jus stall. Mi jus deh deh a watch it rain, vex an tired same time. Da one week wi lose right deh.

The mother: Unu jus haffi wait pon Gaad time. Rain nuh ask nobody permission.

An eena di middle a all dis, wi run short pon material. Wi order block but di load neva come enough, an wi neva have money fi buy more right den. So wi mek do wid weh wi have, wi tek old board from di back a di yard, di same board mi grandaddy use fi build wahn shed long time, an wi work i eena di frame weh nobody woulda si unless dehn look good. Yu learn how fi mek someting from nothing when yu deh eena dis kinda job.

Now, one part weh mi neva forget, mi did up pon di roof frame, way up, an mi step pon wahn beam weh neva nail down proper, an mi slip. Mi grab fi someting an mi kotch mi self pon di crossbeam, but mi drop bout eight foot an lan pon di dirt. Everybody run come.

My workmate: Yu right? Yu right? Talk to mi!

Me: Mi good, mi good, jus mi heart a beat hard.

Neva bruk nutten, jus wahn scare weh stay wid mi long afta, an from dat day mi meck sure everybody test every board befo dehn walk pon it.

Now di argument, dat come ova di roof pitch. My workmate seh wi fi build i steep, cau di rain heavy eena dis place an a flat roof gwehn pool wata an leak. Di eldest seh a steep roof cost more tin an tek more time wi neva have.

The eldest: Wi nuh have time fi build no fancy roof, mek i simple.

My workmate: Simple gwehn leak pon unu head come rainy season, an den unu gwehn come back to mi fi fix i.

Dehn go back an forth good an proper, raise dehn voice, but eena di end wi settle pon someting between di two, steeper dan flat but nuh so steep i eat up all wi tin. Dat kinda argument, i normal pon a job like dis, everybody care bout di house so everybody have opinion.

Den wi find out di back wall, di one wi build early on, befo wi eye did trained fi si crooked, dat wall bow. Di block dem neva set square, an once wi put weight pon i, i start crack. Wi haffi knock di whole wall dong, all di way to di foundation, an build i back fresh. Dat did hut wi bad, cau dat wall represent whole week a wuk gone. But wi neva have no choice. Yu cyaan leave wahn bad wall an hope i hold.

Afta all a dat, di day di frame finally stand up straight, all four wall an di roof beam set, wi jus stop an look pon i. Small ting, but i feel like wahn victory.

The bredda from down the street: Look pon dat, e a stand.

Me: E a stand fi true.

Wi neva celebrate big, jus everybody stand back, wipe dehn face, an nod. But i mean someting.

Now toward di end, wi behind schedule, an wi push di last four night, wuk pass dark wid flashlight an one bulb wi run off extension cord to di neighbor house. Mosquito bad, everybody tired, but wi push, cau wi did want fi finish. Mi remember di last night, hand tremble from tiredness, still a nail down board eena di dark.

An den di day come. Di house stand finish, painted, door hang, everyting done. Wi jus walk eena i an walk out, walk eena i an walk out, like wi cyaan believe i.

Afta, wi si dong an tally i up. Eleven month, start to finish. Six different hand weh help, not counting weh come one day an neva come back, jus di ones weh stick wid i regular. Two week lost to di bad line. Bout a week lost to rain. Wahn wall wi haffi build twice. Wahn fall weh coulda been worse. But we do i, an wi do i mostly wid wi own hand.

Now when mi stand eena i, when mi si di pickney dem run bout eena room dehn neva have befo, when mi lean pon di wall wi build twice, mi nuh tink bout di hard part so much. Mi tink bout di bredda who come widout mi call him, an di argument weh mek di roof betta, an di dark night wid di flashlight. Dis house nuh jus block an tin, i carry every hand weh touch i.
