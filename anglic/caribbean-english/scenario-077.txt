The friend: Bey, wait till you hear dis, I cyan even hold it in. I get a big money today, real big!

Me: LORD HA' MERCY, bey, dat is SOME GOOD NEWS enh! Praise God for you doe, real real good news!

The friend: I tellin you, di check land dis mornin and I still standin here shakin.

Me: So how much it be, straight up, nuh hold back.

The friend: Close to nine thousand, bey. More than what I does make in half a year at di job.

Me: Nine thousand... an how dat even happen to you doe. Cause I deh grindin same as you every day an nuttin nuh fall pon me like dat.

The friend: Girl I nuh know myself, di luck just deh pon me dis week I guess. Some insurance people call me up outta nowhere seh dey owe me from way back, from di accident time.

Me: Mmm. Must be nice doe. Some a we out here strugglin fi every dollar an cyan see a break like dat.

The friend: (catching it now) Wait nuh, somethin wrong wit you? You soundin some kinda way all a sudden.

Me: (steups) ...nah, bey, I nuh go lie to you like dat. Is envy, plain and simple, nuttin more sinister than dat. I see you get blessed an a lil part a me get vex cause it nuh happen fi me.

The friend: Bey, for real?

Me: For real. But fi true doe, I glad for you, I mean dat from my heart dis time, nuh di way I holler it out first. You been strugglin same as anybody, so if blessin gwine drop pon anybody, let it drop pon you. I happy, bey. Real happy.

The friend: Tank you, bey. Dat mean somethin, comin from you.
