The eldest: So tell wi nuh, how it stay up deh? Yuh jus a siddung deh like yuh nuh have nutten fi seh.

The visitor: Mi tell unu already, it big. It big and it nuh stop, man. Yah so everything close, walk lickle bit an yuh deh. Over deh everything spread out wide wide, tek yuh whole day jus fi go one place.

Mother: Whole day? Yuh a exaggerate now.

The visitor: Mi nah exaggerate, mumma. Some day fi cross from one side a di city to di other side tek a good hour, hour an a half wid di traffic, an dat's if di train nuh mash up. Yuh haffi plan out yuh whole day roun one likkle trip.

Auntie: Lawd. An di noise? Mi hear seh it noisy up deh.

The visitor: It noisy, but a different kind a noisy from yah so. Nuh dog a bark, nuh sound system a play from nobody yard, is jus train an car an people rush rush rush pon di sidewalk, phone a dem ear, nuh stop fi look pon yuh nor seh good mawnin. First time mi si it mi couldn't believe how much people can pass yuh an nuh one nuh nod.

Mother: An wehpaat yuh deh stay now? Mi hear seh dem place likkle likkle up deh, smaller dan a shoebox.

The visitor: Is one room wid a lickle kitchen corner, dat's di whole a it. Not even a proper yard, nutten fi call yaad. Some a dem picture inna dem head seh mi deh inna one big house wid staircase an all dat, but no, is one small small space, an rent alone tek out more dan half a wha mi a earn.

Uncle: More dan half? Fi wha, one room?

The visitor: One room, uncle. An everything cost more up deh too. Milk cost more, bus fare cost more, even a lickle bag a rice cost more dan wha unu a pay yah so. Some ting cheap though, like di electronics dem, but food an rent will tek out yuh pocket quick quick.

The eldest: So people up deh friendly, or dem jus stay keep to demself?

The visitor: Dat one nuh have no straight answer. Some a dem nice, real nice, will help yuh carry bag or show yuh di way if yuh look lost. But some a dem nuh see yuh atall, walk pass yuh like yuh invisible, nuh "good evening", nuh nutten. It different from yah so weh everybody know everybody business an ask after yuh mumma. Up deh yuh can live nex door to somebody five, six year an nuh know dem name.

Auntie: Steups. Dat sound lonely to mi.

The visitor: It can lonely, yes, but it also have people from all bout, all kinda language a call out inna di street, an some a dem warm same way like home once yuh find dem.

Cousin: An weh unu buy unu food? Market deh deh?

The visitor: Nutten run like Coronation up deh, cousin. Dem call it grocery store, everything wrap up inna plastic pon shelf, price tag pon everyting, nuh higgler a call out "come mek a deal", nuh haggling, nuh bargain. Yuh jus tek wha yuh want an put it inna cart an gwaan go pay. Mi did miss dat, di back an forth, di noise a di market, people a call yuh "yes bossy" an "come dung nuh".

Uncle: An how yuh get wuk up deh? Yuh haffi know somebody, or how it go?

The visitor: Nutten like yah so, uncle, weh yuh cyaan even fi get a job unless yuh have a connection. Up deh yuh apply pon computer, send resume, sometime yuh nuh even si a face till dem call yuh fi interview. Some job yuh get through agency, dem place yuh inna work an tek a cut a yuh pay fi demself. Mi work through one a dem first, before mi did find someting steady pon mi own.

Grandmother: An yuh never get lost, first time yuh reach?

The visitor: Granny, mi did lost bad. Three, four time di first few week, tek di wrong train go a di wrong side a town twice inna one day, one time mi end up so far mi did haffi call mi workmate fi come find mi. It tek time fi learn how di place set out, weh street connect to weh, which train go which way. Now mi know it like mi know mi own hand, but back den mi did feel like one lickle pickney jus a wander bout.

The eldest: An di food? How di food stay up deh?

The visitor: Some part better, some part worse, believe mi. Yuh can get food from all bout di world, any country yuh call, dem have it up deh, an some a it good fah true. But nutten taste like yaad food. Dem patty nuh right, di curry goat hard fi find done proper, an nutten cook wid dat same love. So it better one way, an it worse di other way.

The eldest: So which one better den, up deh or yah so? Straight answer now.

The visitor: Mi cyaan give yuh a straight answer pon dat one, mi love. Dem two place nuh really stand up side by side fi compare so. One thing good bout up deh, one thing better yah so, an is like dat fi most a it. Cyaan seh one better dan di other outright.

Mother: Yuh always did dodge a question. Same way yuh did stay when yuh was pickney.

The visitor: Steups, mumma, mi nuh dodge nutten, is jus true seh it complicated.

Mother: Awrite den, answer mi dis one. Yuh a come back fi stay one day, or yuh gone fi good now?

The visitor: Mi nuh know really, mumma. Maybe one day, when time ready, when tings set up di right way. Right now mi haffi si how tings gwaan. Mi nuh seh never, an mi nuh seh soon. Is jus how it stay fi now.

Auntie: So dat mean yuh coming back Christmas den?

The visitor: Dat one mi can promise unu, auntie. Christmas mi deh yah, same time, same yard, same everybody a talk over one another same way unu doing right now.
