De aunty si mi from de corner an she done a call out befo mi even reach de counter good.

The aunty: Awrite nuh, is de usual? Cook-up rice, stew chicken, extra pepper sauce pon de side, nuh so?

Me: Yes aunty, but today gi mi fish instead a de chicken, nuh. Mi belly a wahn something different today, is all.

The aunty: (steups) Fish? Since when unu start change up pon me so. Awright, awright, siddung deh, mi ago fix it fu yuh now now, de fish deh a de pot already.

Me: Wha de business a do dis week, aunty? Mi si de line short taday.

The aunty: Rain messing tings up, chile. Customer dem nuh deh come out like befo, an de fish price gone up besides, so mi haffi raise it lil bit but mi nah tell everybody yet, is you self mi telling.

Me: Yuh know mi does come here bout tree four time a week, aunty. Yuh cyaan hide notten from me.

The aunty: (laughing) True, true, is why mi already start yuh order befo yuh open yuh mout good. Wha bout home, everybody deh awright?

Me: Dem deh awright. De pickney dem giving trouble wid school but wha to do, life a guh on same way.

The aunty: Dat is how it stay, love. Awright now, hold yuh food, hot hot, mind yuh nuh burn yuh finger dem.

Me: (handing de money) Tek dis, keep de change fu yuhself.

The aunty: Tanks, love. Guh careful now, an mi go si yuh same time, same corner, nuh true?

Me: Yuh already know it. Later, aunty.
