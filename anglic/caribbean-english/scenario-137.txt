Smady run go tell di captain seh di ingine room di flood out, an bai di taim e reach pan di dock di wata mi almoss to e knee. Di crewman dem mi deh stan-op deh a look, an di captain e face red so wen e come outta di wheelhouse. E p'int e finga right pan di one wa jus di kohn bak fahn di store wid e lunch eena e han.

The captain: A yu lef da valve di open! A yu mess op mi ingine dis mawnin!

The one accused: Nuh mi.

The captain: Doa gi mi dat, bwai. A yu mi deh ya wen dis ting stat, a yu wan!

The one accused: A swear pan mi mada grave, nuh mi dis.

Di res a di crewman dem jus deh stan roun, dem yeye pan di grong, dem mout shet tight. Di one wa did really lef di valve open di deh right deh mongst dem, no wod pass e lip. Nohn a dem gat fi ansa fi it, so dem jus deh quiet.

The captain: Somebody tell mi seh a yu wi di wuk pan it fus ting dis mawnin.

Di one accused kip e face same way, no expreshan pan it atall, jus stan deh tek it. E workmate steups lo an tun e ai bak da di wata, like e no waahn si natn.

The captain: Unu betta fix dis quick, cuz dis wahn cost wi nof money.

Nobody seh sorry. Nobody seh a dem. Di one wa get blame jus tek up e lunch, waak weh, an lef di denial stan right deh weh e put it down, same place, same way.
