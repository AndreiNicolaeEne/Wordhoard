Mi gaan kohn ketch mi gyament from di tailor shap Satideh evenin, di one mi mi order fi mi sista christening. Soon a step eena di dor, a si di ting deh pan di hanga an sohnting jos noh look right.

The customer: Wait nuh, dis noh weh mi order. Weh di third button deh? An weh mek dis hem a cut pan wahn angle so? Mi tell yu seh three button, straight hem, no angle pan i atall.

The tailor: Bwai, dat da exakly weh wi mi gree pan from di start. A noh change nutn from weh yu mi seh mi.

The customer: Noh sah, mi noh seh dat to yu. Da three button mi seh, straight down di front, an di hem fi run straight, no angle. Unu tailor dem awlaz waan change up peple ting an seh a dat wi order.

The tailor: Awrite, mek mi noh argue eena front a yu. Leh mi guh check di book weh ih got di measurement dem write down.

*(di tailor gaan luk pan di note, kohn back afta a lee wail)*

The tailor: A find i now. Pan di button dem, yu right, i write down three eena di book, so da mi mistek da deh, mi mi mek i two. Bot pan di hem, i write angle, noh straight, so da yu mi mistek pan.

The customer: Steups. Awrite den, mi wahn tek di angle hem, mi noh mine dat wan, bot dis button ting need fi fix quick quick, mi need i fi Sunday sarvis.

The tailor: No fraid, mi wahn put wan a di gyal dem pan i fos ting tomaro fi sew da extra button an match op di tred. Bai Wensday evenin i wahn dohn fi true, yu ku kohn back den.

The customer: Awrite, tenk yu. Mi wahn kohn back Wensday afta wok, an mek sure i dohn dis time.
