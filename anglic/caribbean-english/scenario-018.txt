Awright, awright, make everybody hold up dem glass one minute, cause a promise unu de whole story tonight, start to finish, nuh skip nutten. Meh partner already know a go tell it, dat mek e nah say a word, just siddung deh a smile like cyat weh get de cream. Watch am. E know wah a coming.

So. We nah meet no fancy way, nutten pretty bout it at all. A did deh a Stabroek Market, one Saturday marning, sun a bun mi neck bad, a di push through de whole crowd fu buy fish. And meh partner did deh right in front a mi a haggle wid de same fish lady over one pound a snapper like e whole life depend pon it. A nah know de haggling still a gwan, so a lean over e shoulder fu try get de lady eye. And meh partner turn round quick and seh:

My partner: Bai, is which part yuh come out from, yuh cyaan see people already deh ah line?

A never even deh ah no line, it was just crowd, but a laugh so hard de lady stop haggling to watch we two. And a tell am back, straight straight:

Me: If yuh mekking dis much noise over one pound a fish, a hope yuh does cook am good, cause all dis fuss better nah go to waste.

Dat de ting weh stick. To dis day, if unu ever hear one a we call de other "bai" outta nowhere in de middle a argument, dat right deh is where it come from. It nah leave we since.

Now a go be honest wid unu, cause dis is de real story nah de nice one. A ben hesitate. A ben get burn bad one time before, and a did tell mieself a nah putting mi heart out deh again fi nobody fi trample pon am. So when meh partner start come round, come find mi at de stelling, come find mi by de church gate, a did keep am at arm length good, steups, act like a nah see am even when a did see am plain plain. It tek a good while, weeks upon weeks, before a stop putting up dat wall. Wah change mi mind was simple, meh partner never push, never beg, never get vex when a give cold shoulder. E just keep showing up steady, quiet quiet, like e ben have all de patience in de worl fi wait pon mi. And one day a realize a was de one waiting pon miself, nah pon am. Dat is when a let am in proper.

De courting start real once we go out fu true, nah just meet-up-by-chance ting. First time we spend proper time togedda was one Sunday down by de seawall, wid a bag a channa and two soft drink, watch de water come in. But it nah easy from deh, cause meh partner deh from Berbice side, and dat crossing wid de ferry nah play, plus e ben deh helping run de family business over deh, and mi ben deh a wuk long hours in town. So weeks pass we cyaan see one anodda, just phone call and dem short short text. It test we bad. But we mek it wuk, meh partner used to catch de early ferry pon e day off, come sit wid mi de whole day, den gone back same night, tired out but nah complaining once. And a used to save up mi off days fu match up wid e. It nah come easy, but we push through am togedda, one crossing at a time.

And unu already know bout de snapper ting, so a nah go explain am again, every year since, somebody bringing up dat same fish.

De moment we know it did turn serious, funny enough it was mi modda weh see am before mi. A did tek down bad wid fever, and de rain did heavy dat week, real heavy, road dem did flood already. And meh partner catch de ferry anyhow, walk through water up to e knee fu come check pon mi. Mi modda watch am come een soaked to de bone and she jus stand up dey shaking she head, and after e gone she tell mi straight:

The mother: Chile, a man weh come out in dat kind a rain fi come see bout yuh, dat nah no casual ting. Yuh betta know wah yuh have deh.

And she was right. A did know it awready in mi heart, but she was de one seh am out loud first.

De proposal now, dat nah come wid no big show, no restaurant, no photographer hiding behind no bush. We was back down by de seawall, same place we did start, foot dem wet from de spray, and a jus turn to am and ask straight out, no fuss:

Me: So wah we waiting pon? Yuh gon marry mi or nah?

And meh partner nearly drop de soft drink bottle, start laugh, den seh yes before a even done finish de sentence good.

Was bout a year after dat we married. De families dem took it different different. Mi side come round quick, mi fadda did like am from long time already. But e side, e modda did tek time, she did want fu see if a was serious or if a was just one town gyal weh go play wid e heart and lef. It tek she a good while fu warm up, but she come round proper once she see how a stand up beside am through all de crossing and de wuk and everything. By de time we married, she was crying more than anybody else in de church.

De wedding day itself, plenty a it deh pon film, but de one ting weh stick nah deh pon no photo at all. De light gone off right in de middle a de reception, GPL do wah GPL does do, and everybody start bawl and laugh at de same time. But instead a panic, de whole yard start sing by phone light, one a de old aunty dem start off a hymn and everybody just join een, no music, no DJ, jus we voice dem in de dark. Nutten never capture dat. It was more beautiful than any first dance we did plan.

We weh a couple, we gone through we hard stretch same as anybody. De flood few years back tek plenty from we, water inna de house, tings we couldn't save. It was rough, real rough. But wah carry we through was jus staying side by side, bailing out water togedda, laughing when it did too much fi cry, and never once turning pon one anodda. Dat de ting dat hold.

And a go close wid dis, from dat first day at de market, argue over fish, to now, right dis minute, meh partner still de same one, still quiet quiet, still letting mi tell de whole story while e jus siddung deh a smile. Dat nah change one bit. So make we raise we glass, to de fish lady weh never know she start all a dis, to de families dem, to de water we cross and de water we bail out, and to de one weh still call mi "gyal" like e did dat very first Saturday marning. Cheers, allyuh. Drink up.
