Mi affi tell yuh bout dis one night wi did haffi get mi friend home, cause it did mad bad, and mi laugh bout it good still to dis day, but Lawd mi did fraid fi true dat night.

So di whole a wi did deh a one lickle spot inna town, di kind a place weh di music a play loud and everybody a drink and a laugh till dem belly hurt. Mi friend love a lickle drink, yuh know how im stay, but dat night im a drink like seh a race im a run, one drink afta di next, and di bottle dem jus a pile up pon di table till mi lose count.

By di time di night start wind down, im nuh look right at all, at all. Im a stagger from di bar to di table, a hold on to whosoever near im, and every time im open im mouth fi talk, di words come out all jumble up, like im tongue too heavy fi carry dem straight.

Di first ting mek mi know it did gone too far, a when im try fi get up offa di chair fi guh a di toilet and im two foot jus buckle straight unda im, like dem turn to jelly. Mi run go grab im before im lick im head pon di floor, and mi seh, "Bredda, yuh alright?" and im look pon mi, eye a roll, and im seh, "Mi fine, mi fine, nuh worry bout mi," but a lie im a tell, cause im foot still a wobble unda im even as di word dem a come out.

Mi try fi get im fi leave right deh so, but oonu know how dem stay when dem reach dat stage, im nuh want fi hear it. "Mi nah guh nowhe," im seh, "di night jus a start," and mi haffi coax im good, mi haffi seh, "Come nuh man, mi a walk yuh home, come mek wi guh," and im a pull weh from mi hand, a insist seh im good right deh. A good ten minute mi did haffi beg and tug before im final give een and let mi lif im up offa di chair.

So wi start di walk, and from di jump mi haffi keep mi arm roun im di whole a di way, cause widout it im woulda drop straight eena di gutter. Wi never reach far, maybe two street corner, before im seh im feel sick, and im bend over side a one wall and bring up everyting im did drink dat whole night. Mi jus hold im back, a pat im, a seh, "Let it out, let it out, yuh a go feel better after," and when im done, wi si dung pon a lickle wall fi one time fi mek im catch imself, im head a hang, sweat a run off im.

Wan man did a pass round dat time, and im si di two a wi, mi a hold up mi friend who look like im cyan even hold up imself, and di man jus steups long and loud, "Steups, unu young people and dis drinking ting, whole a unu a guh kill unuself," and im shake im head and jus walk off, nuh even stop, cause a nuff time im si di same ting pon dat same corner.

Wi get up back and continue di walk, but mi mind did tired too, mi a try fi hol up mi friend and watch weh wi a guh same time, and wi end up tun eena di wrong street altogether, one weh nuh lead nowhe near wi yaad. Wi did a walk good five minute before mi realize seh wi deh pon di wrong road, and mi haffi tun wi roun and guh back di way wi did come, mi friend a mumble, "Weh wi deh? Weh wi a guh?" and mi jus seh, "Nuh worry, mi know weh mi a guh," even doah mi self did lost fi a lickle minute deh.

Before wi reach di yaad, mi ask im if im want fi nyam something, tink maybe a food ketch im stomach, but im jus wave mi weh, seh im nuh want nutten. Den mi si seh im need water bad, and mi ask im, "Yuh want some water?" and im seh, "Nuh, mi nuh want nutten," and push weh mi hand, but mi nuh mek dat stop mi, mi keep pon im, "Drink it nuh man, it a go help yuh," and finally, after mi beg good, im tek one lickle sip, den a next one, and before yuh know it im a gulp it dung like im never see water inna im life.

A right bout dat time, walking di last lickle stretch, mi friend hold on to mi arm tight and seh to mi, seh, "Yuh know seh yuh a di best friend anybody coulda have, mi nuh tell yuh dat enough, mi love yuh bad bad," and mi haffi laugh, cause a di rum a talk, but it still touch mi lickle bit, and mi seh, "Mi know, mi know, come mek wi jus get yuh home first."

Wi reach di door and a whole nex struggle start, cause di key nuh want fi guh eena di hole straight, mi friend a try fi help but im hand a shake so till im cyan even hold di key steady, and mi haffi push im hand aside and do it miself, jiggling di key dis way and dat till di lock finally give.

Wi get eena di house and mi walk im straight to di couch and lay im dung easy, tek off im shoes dem, put a lickle basin side a im jus in case, and mi friend look up pon mi, eye half shut, and seh, "Mi nah drink again like dis, nuh more, mi done wid it fi good," and mi jus seh, "Awrite, awrite," but eena mi heart mi nuh believe one word a it, cause a nuh di first time mi hear dat same promise, and it nuh go be di last eeda.

Next morning mi guh check pon im, and im head a bust im, eye red red, and mi ask im, "So how much a las night yuh remember?" and im tink bout it good and long and seh, "Mi remember wi did deh a di spot, and... mi tink mi remember walking?" and a dat's about all im coulda call up fi imself. So mi haffi fill een di rest fi im, bit by bit, di stumbling, di wall wi si dung pon, di man weh steups after wi, di wrong turn, di water im never did want. And when mi reach di part bout weh im seh to mi pon di walk, im cova im face and groan, "Mi never seh dat," and mi seh, "Yuh seh it, and worse, yuh mean it too," and wi both bruk out a laugh, cause a so it go, and a so wi love im still, every mad drunk minute a it.
