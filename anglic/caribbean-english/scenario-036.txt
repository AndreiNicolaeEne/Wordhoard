Girl, mek I tell yuh de whole ting from de start because I know yuh only hear bits and piece from people and dem does exaggerate. This year was me time to host, yuh understand, everybody been telling mi "when it go be yuh turn" fuh so long, and I finally seh awright, dis Christmas a mi house, done. But de whole week before I couldn't sleep good at all. I lying down and my mind a run, run, run, suppose de food nah come out right, suppose people come and it nah have enough, suppose somebody complain. Meh heart been beating like I gon do exam.

So I siddown and I draw up dis menu, and yuh know how I stay, I nah do nutting small. I put down black cake, garlic pork, pepperpot, cook-up rice, macaroni pie, some kinda salad, chow mein because de youths dem like it, and I even write down I gon try mek a trifle from scratch. My workmate see de list and she tell mi straight...

The workmate: Yuh mad or wah? Yuh a one woman, not a whole catering service.

I laugh but deep down I did know she right. Because dat pepperpot alone tek all day pon de fire, and I still put down five more dish like I have extra hand hiding somewhere.

Now de guest count. First it did just gon be de close family, mi mother dem, mi one sister and she husband, maybe eight people total, nice and easy. Then mi aunty call and seh she coming and she bringing she daughter and de grandpickney dem. Then mi neighbour hear I hosting and she seh "gyal I coming too, I bring a dish nuh worry", and before I know it de eight turn into like eighteen, maybe more, because people kept adding on de last minute self, "oh I have a friend visiting from foreign, she could come?" Wuh I gon seh, no? So de list just a grow and grow and I nah have de heart fi turn nobody back.

De shopping trip was a whole event on e own. I gone Stabroek Market early early, before de sun even hot yet, and de place still pack like it a give away money. I get everything pon de list, de wiri wiri pepper, de cassareep, de channa, de rice, but I couldn't find a decent piece of pork tail nowhere fuh mi love, I gone one stall to de next, three different vendor tell mi seh dem sell out already, one man tell mi seh "come back tomorrow" like I have time fuh dat. I end up buying a next kind meat altogether and adjust de recipe right deh in mi head, standing up in de market.

Night before, I nah even lie down till like two in de marning. I deh in de kitchen chopping and seasoning and marinating till mi two hand tired, mi eye red red. I bin tell miself I gon go bed by ten, but ten come and gone and I still a wash pot, still a season de meat, still a mek de black cake soak in de wine one last time. Meh sister call round eleven fi see how I doing and I nearly bark she head off, I was so tired and stress out.

So de marning I bin write out a whole schedule pon paper, like eight o'clock start de pepperpot, nine o'clock rice, ten o'clock de cake go in, real proper, like I running a restaurant. But once de actual marning reach, dat schedule tear up itself in mi hand. De pepperpot alone tek longer than I plan because de pot wasn't heating even, and by de time I even start de rice I already behind by two hour.

And den, disaster. I did put de garlic pork fuh fry and I gone answer de door because somebody knocking, and by de time I come back de whole ting black, black, burn straight to de bottom of de pot, smoke a rise up and set off de smoke alarm and everything. I nearly cry right deh in de kitchen. I bin tek one look and seh to miself, "well dat done, throw way." I had was to run gone buy more pork right deh and start de whole marinating process over, praying it soak up enough flavour in time, because it nah have de days it need no more, it only have hours.

Right in de middle of dat madness, mi neighbour reach early, she wasn't even suppose to come till later, and she walk in and see mi face and she nah even sit down, she just tie she head and roll up she sleeve.

The neighbour: Wuh yuh want mi fi do, chop or stir?

I tell yuh, dat woman was a blessing straight from God dat day. She tek over de rice while I deal wid de meat, she nah even ask twice, she just work.

By now de stove was a war zone. Four burner and I need six, de oven have de black cake in one side and de macaroni pie a next, and everybody dish a fight fuh space and time. De pot dem stack up pon de counter waiting dem turn, and I gone from stove to counter to stove like a mad woman.

But yuh know wuh come out better than I even expect? Dat cook-up rice. I nearly throw way de coconut milk because I thought it did turn, but I use it anyway out of pure desperation, and Lord, dat rice come out so rich and creamy everybody bin fighting over de bottom of de pot after.

Meal was suppose to start one o'clock, it start close to four. By de time everybody siddown and start nyam, I did too tired fi even care no more, I just wanted people fi eat and be happy.

First bite gone round and I watching everybody face like a hawk. Mi uncle nod he head slow, chewing, and he seh...

The uncle: Awright, awright, dis have promise.

Den mi godmother, she nah mince word at all, she tek one taste of de garlic pork and she push she plate lil bit and seh...

The godmother: Chile, dis one dry out, yuh rush it nuh.

And she right, I know she right, but it still sting a lil bit.

But dat cook-up rice, gyal, it gone first, before anything else pon de table, people a come back fuh second and third helping till de pot did scrape clean. Mi sister husband tell everybody in de yard seh it de best cook-up he ever eat outside he own mother house, and mi mother just steups and seh...

The mother: Since when yuh does big up people food so, yuh nah tell me dat.

Everybody laugh till dem belly hurt.

By de time de last plate wash and de last guest gone home it did well pass midnight, and I drop down pon de couch like somebody pull out mi plug. Meh sister siddown next to mi and aks...

The eldest: So, yuh doing dis again next year?

And I tell yuh straight, I look at she and I seh "not a next year, gyal, allyuh find somebody else fuh host," but even as de word a come out mi mouth I already thinking bout wuh I would do different, wuh I would season better, how much less ambitious de menu could be. So watch me, by de time next Christmas reach I probably a do de whole madness all over again.
