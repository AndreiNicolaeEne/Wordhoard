Gyal, leh me tell yuh bout dis moving day nuh, since yuh miss de whole ting. Is a story and a half, believe me.

First ting first, we ben plan fu start seven o'clock sharp, everybody up, van deh outside, done by midday. Dat was de plan. But de night before, de baby ben teething and a bawl till near tree in de morning, so when de alarm gone off nobody nuh move. By de time me eye open good is nearly nine, and me a lie down deh still a tell meself five more minutes, five more minutes, till de mother start bang on de door seh, "Allyuh nuh hear de time? Get up nuh man, de van man wahn charge we extra fu waiting."

So we jump up, everybody rushing rushing, and dat's when de first ting gone wrong. Me open de front door fu start bring out de boxes and de whole handle jus come off in me hand, snap, like it ben waiting all dis time fu dat exact morning fu break. We ben lock in de house nearly twenty minutes, me and de eldest a wrestle wid a screwdriver, sweat already a run down we back and we nuh even reach outside yet. De mother a steups from de kitchen de whole time, seh, "See wha me tell allyuh bout starting late, everything does start wrong when yuh start late."

Anyway we finally get out, and who yuh tink turn up, nobody call am, nobody ask am nothing, is de neighbour from next door. De one wid de truck. Him jus deh dere in him merino and slipper, seh,

The neighbour: "Ah hear allyuh a move today, mek me help nuh, wha allyuh doing struggling wid dat by allyuh self."

And honestly dat man ben save we life, because widout him and him truck we woulda deh deh till night just wid de first load.

Now de ting weh break, and de shout weh gone up wid am, oh gosh. Is de mother good glass cabinet, de one she get from she own mother, wid she wedding china in am. We ben careful careful carrying am down de step dem, everybody holding a side, and den de youngest foot slip pon a wet patch and de whole cabinet buss loose from one side and gone down. De glass door shatter and two a de good plate dem inside gone in a hundred piece. De mother let out a shout yuh coulda hear from de next street, "OH GOD NUH, ME MOTHER CHINA!" and she jus stand up dere wid she hand pon she head, nuh move fu a good five minute, just a look pon de broken glass like she cyaan believe wha she eye seeing. Nobody say nothing, everybody just freeze up, until de eldest start sweep up de glass quiet quiet and dat's when we know fi move on.

Den it get worse before it get better, cause dat's when we notice one box gone missing. De one wid all de photo album dem and de old letter and ting, de one marked "IMPORTANT. DO NOT LOSE" in big marker. To dis day we still nuh find dat box. We ben ask de van man, we ben ask de neighbour, we search de old house top to bottom, nutten. Somebody musse leave am back a de old yard or it drop off somewhere pon de road and gone, we nuh know till now. Dat one still a hurt me heart when me tink bout am.

Den come de big argument, over de fridge. Big ole fridge, heavy like sin. De eldest seh we fi carry am pon de side, de van man seh nuh, upright or it wahn damage de compressor, de neighbour seh forget all dat, jus tie am down good and lif from de bottom. Everybody a bawl out different instruction at de same time,

The eldest: "Nuh man, pon de side, dat's how fridge does travel!"

The van driver: "Yuh wahn buss de gas line! Keep am straight up, straight!"

The neighbour: "Allyuh stop de noise, jus grab a corner each and lif nuh, we nuh have time fu debate."

Three grown people a pull tree different way wid a fridge in de middle, was a whole comedy show right dey pon de street.

But de real test come when we reach de new place and dat fridge jus refuse fu go through de kitchen doorway. We turn am dis way, we turn am dat way, we tek off de handle, we tek off de door, still nuh fit. We measure and measure and it look like it shoulda fit easy but somehow every angle we try it catch pon de frame. In de end we haffi tek out de whole door frame piece self before dat fridge coulda pass through, hour and a half gone jus pon dat one appliance.

And of course dat's when de weather decide fu turn pon we too. Sky ben looking grey from marning but round two o'clock it jus open up, real Georgetown rain, de kind weh come down straight and hard nuh warning. We still had furniture pon de open van and everybody scrambling fu find tarpaulin, plastic, anyting fu cover de ting dem, running back and forth in de rain soaked to de bone, cussing under we breath.

By de time de rain ease up we ben tired and hungry bad, and dat's when de mother workmate, who ben pass by fu see how tings going, seh she bring cook-up rice and some chicken curry in a big pot. We nuh even bother fu find plate or table, everybody jus grab a spoon and eat standing up right dey among de boxes, still wet from de rain, and gyal me a tell yuh dat was de sweetest food me ever eat in me life. Dat pot save de whole afternoon.

After dat we push through de last load dem but by evening everybody ben done in, legs shaking, back locking up, and is de last two three trip weh really break we down, walking up and down dem step like we hundred years old, nobody talking much no more, just a grunt and a groan.

Before we lock up de old place for good, me tek a walk through de empty room dem by myself. Strange how a house does look and sound different once everything gone out of am, jus we footstep echoing and de mark pon de wall where de picture dem used to hang. Me stand up a minute in de room where all a we used to sleep, and something inside me get heavy, even doh we ben happy fu move.

We nuh reach de new place till good after dark, headlight pon de van de only light we had fu unload by, mosquito a bite we up, and half de tings we start de day wid still nuh deh wid we, de china gone, de box gone, and everybody so tired we jus leave de rest of de boxes stack up and gone straight fu sleep. But we reach. Dat's de main ting, we reach.
