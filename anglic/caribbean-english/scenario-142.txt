Since mi wake dis marnin mi a groan already. Mi did know from last night seh today a di day, cyaan hide from it nuh more. Mi swear pon mi mother grave back a January seh mi a go clean out dat whole back store-room, top to bottom, one full day, no matter what, and today di day reach fi mi. Mi nuh want get up. Mi jus a lie down deh pon di bed a stare pon di ceiling like it a go change mi mind fi mi.

But mi get up. Mi grumble di whole way to di store-room, steups long and hard when mi push open di door and see how di t'ing pile up to di roof. "Lord, why mi ever open mi mouth and say dis," mi a mumble to miself, foot dragging like mi a walk go a funeral.

Mi start sort out di boxes dem, and every ten minute mi find someting fresh fi complain 'bout. Di dust, di heat, how di whole t'ing tek so long fi even start, how mi never learn fi keep mi mout shut when people a beg favour one time too many.

The mother: "How di store-room a look?"
Me: "Like it a go tek mi whole life, dat's how it look."

'Bout two o'clock mi hand tired, mi back hot, and mi did really wahn jus close di door and walk weh lef it. Mi stand up deh a look pon di half-finish pile, and mi seh to miself, nuh, yuh swear yuh would, yuh cyaan back out now, done. So mi push through di worst part, di heavy box dem down a di bottom weh nobody touch in years.

By evening time mi done, every last box, every last corner clean out. Mi tired bad, back a kill mi, but mi nuh quit, not once. Mi complain from marnin to night straight, but mi finish it, exactly like mi did seh mi would.
