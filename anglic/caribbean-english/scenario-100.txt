Mi a go tell yuh someting now, and mi want yuh siddung good and listen, because mi nuh talk bout dis one to nuff people. Out a all di years mi live yet, dat one deh did di worse. Mi nuh put no date pon it, mi jus call it "di bad year" inna mi head to dis day, and once mi seh dat, mi know exactly which one mi mean, no calendar no need fi tell mi.

It start simple simple. Di work weh mi did have, di one weh did steady, weh mi coulda depend pon fi pay di light and buy di food and put a lickle someting a side, dat gone. Nuh warning nuh really come, or if it come mi never si it fi wah it was. One day dem jus tell mi seh di ting done, seh dem nuh need di position again, seh "tings hard all bout, nutten nuh personal." Mi walk outta deh dat day still a tell miself seh someting else a go turn up quick. Mi never know how long "quick" coulda tek.

First ting fi go was di small comfort dem. Di likkle treat pon a Friday, di taxi weh mi did tek stead a walk, di data pon di phone weh mi use to buy heavy, all a dat get cut back one by one, and yuh tell yuriself seh it nuh matter, seh a discipline yuh a build. Dat part nuh hard yet. Mi still did have mi pride intact.

Is when it start reach di bigger tings dat it start bite. Mi haffi give up di likkle savings mi did a build fi someting better, use it up pon rent and current bill. Mi stop buy certain food altogether, di kind weh nuh necessary but weh mek a meal feel like someting, mi jus a nyam wah did fill mi belly, nutten more. Mi stop go certain places weh mi love, mi stop call people because mi shame fi dem hear seh mi voice change, seh someting heavy a siddung pon mi.

And den di part weh really test mi, mi haffi ask fi help. Mi never one fi borrow, from since mi likkle mi mother teach mi seh yuh work fi wah yuh want, yuh nuh go a nobody door a beg. But money run out and bill nuh stop fi come, so mi haffi go a mi cousin and jus tell har straight seh mi struggling. Mi remember mi voice a shake when mi seh it.

Cousin: "Yuh shoulda tell mi from before, wah kind a pride dat."

Mi couldn't answer har straight, mi jus seh thanks and tek wah she give mi, enough fi cova maybe two month if mi careful. Dat lickle bit a money did save mi life more dan mi ever tell har. But every day mi a carry it like a debt pon mi conscience, like mi owe more dan money.

Close to six month mi run like dat, likkle wuk here, nutten deh, a odd job pick up an drop straight back dung. Six month a beg fi wuk an nuh get none steady. Yuh start count di days different when yuh deh so, yuh nuh count dem fi wah yuh a do, yuh count dem fi wah yuh nuh have.

Di bottom day, mi remember it clear like it a yesterday. Mi did a walk to try fi collect a lickle money weh a fren owe mi, and when mi reach, di fren nuh deh, nuh answer nuh phone, an mi haffi walk all di way back inna di hot sun wid nutten inna mi pocket an nutten inna mi belly since marnin. Mi siddung pon di step outside di gate an mi jus bawl, proper bawl, di kind weh yuh nuh let nobody si. Mi seh to miself seh mi cyaan tek no more a dis. Dat was di bottom, right deh so.

But is like someting a watch out fi yuh sometimes, because di very next week, outta nowhere, a neighbour weh mi barely used to talk to knock pon mi gate wid a bag a food.

Neighbour: "Mi hear seh tings rough wid yuh, mi nuh have much but tek dis, an if yuh need someting jus come."

Mi neva ask har fi nutten, mi neva even tell har mi business, but sip-sip run an she hear. Dat bag a food, an di likkle she give mi after fi help wid a light bill, it kept mi from gwaan sink further. Mi still nuh know how she find out, but mi nuh forget it.

Not long after dat, wuk start come back, small small. A day yah, a day deh, cleaning someting fi somebody, running errand fi anodda. Nutten steady, nutten guaranteed, but it was someting, an someting was a whole heap more dan nutten. From dat likkle bit, it did tek close to eight month fi climb back to weh mi could call miself stable again. Eight month a grind, a save wah mi coulda save, a seh no to tings mi did want but nuh need, a build back di lickle cushion mi did lose.

An through all a dat, mi learn who really deh fi yuh. Nuh di one dem weh yuh expect. Some a di people mi did think would come through, dem disappear quicker dan smoke, dem stop answer, dem get busy all a sudden. But some people, people yuh never even close to, dem show up widout yuh haffi ask twice. Mi learn seh help nuh always come from weh yuh expect it, an dat teach mi fi nuh judge people too quick, an fi nuh hold miself too proud fi accept a hand when it deh reach out.

Mi learn someting bout miself too, someting mi did nuh know before. Mi learn seh mi stronger dan mi ever give miself credit for. Mi used to think if tings ever get dat hard, mi woulda buckle, mi woulda give up altogether. But when it reach, mi find seh mi still get up every marnin, mi still try, even when it nuh mek no sense fi try. Dat teach mi seh survival nuh someting yuh decide, it someting weh come out a yuh when yuh nuh have no other choice.

It nuh have no one day weh mi wake up an everyting jus better. It come likkle by likkle. But mi remember di first day mi si di bank balance an it nuh mek mi stomach drop, di first day mi buy someting mi want an nuh feel guilty after, dat a bout when mi realise seh mi survive it, seh mi come out di other side.

Now mi si yuh, an mi know exactly weh yuh deh, because mi did deh deh mi self. Mi nuh have to ask yuh how tings stay, mi can si it pon yuh face, mi hear it inna how yuh voice sound pon di phone. An mi jus want yuh fi know seh dis yah stretch nuh going to last forever, even doah right now it feel like it nah go end.

Mi nuh have no magic fi mek it easier, but wah mi have mi give yuh, mi time, whenever yuh need fi talk, day or night, mi phone deh pon fi yuh. If yuh short pon someting, come tell mi, nuh mek pride keep yuh back di way mi did try keep miself back. Mi deh right yah so, an mi nah go nowhere. Yuh nuh haffi carry dis by yuhself, mi walk wid yuh through it, step by step, till yuh reach pon di other side same way mi did reach.
