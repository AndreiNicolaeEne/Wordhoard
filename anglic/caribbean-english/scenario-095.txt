The eldest: Bey, da de ring right here, dis one. Mama ben tell me seh it fi me, straight up, long before she even get sick.

The youngest: 'E ain' tell you dat. Steups. You just seein it deh pon de dresser now and you want it fi you.

The eldest: I ain' mekkin it up. Mama seh it plain, wid 'e own mouth, in dis very room.

The youngest: Mama never promise nobody no ring. We ain't never even talk bout no ring. You does always do dis, tek tings and seh somebody give it to you after de fact.

The eldest: Well since we talkin bout tekkin, wha bout de money den, de account? Mama had close to three thousand dollars in dat account and it come up short a'ready. You did deh a de bank fore me, ain't it so.

The youngest: I ain' tek nutting from dat account wasn' mine fi tek. I pay for de flowers out my own pocket too, you forget dat? You never even ask wha dat cost me.

The eldest: I ain' seh you tek de whole ting. I just seh it come up short and you de one had de card dem.

The youngest: You know wha, dis ain' bout no ring or no money at all. You just vex cause you wasn' deh when she pass. You wahn mek up for dat now wid a ring.

The eldest: Dat nuh fair fi seh.

The youngest: Nutting bout dis fair.

The husband: Yinna two, wha yinna a do, fightin so? Come sit down, e'nuff now. Yinna sistas, not two stranger. Mama jus deh pass and yinna out here mekkin sip-sip loud enough for de whole street to hear. Mama woulda steups jus fi hear yinna right now.

The eldest: Lord, listen to we.

The youngest: 'E right doh. Listen to we, fightin over wha lef of she.
