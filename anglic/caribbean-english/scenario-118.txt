Awright, come in, come in close, all a unu, tight round me now.

Mek me seh it straight, nuh sugar pun top a it. We down by three. Dat a de score, plain so. Leh it siddown pun unu fuh a second before we move on.

Now hear me good. A nuh one man mek dis happen, yuh hear me? Nah de keeper alone, nah de back four, nah nobody one man. We all slip together, little by little, every man pun de field give weh a little something, and dat a how it come to three. So nobody deh here carrying dis one alone, no man a go hang him head like a he one drop de ball. We slip together, we rise together same way.

Unu remember dat game, two season back, when we did down by four gine into de last quarter and everybody outside seh it done finish? We nah panic. We hold de ball, we mek dem run fuh once, and we tek it in de end, unu remember dat? Dat deh inna unu still, right now, dis same team.

So a one ting a want change fuh second half: we hold de ball longer, we nah rush de pass, we mek dem come to we. Simple so, nutten more complicated dan dat, nobody can misread it.

Now leh me go round quick. Captain, hold de line steady, dat one job a yours. Keeper, talk loud, mek dem hear you de whole half. De one up front, first touch, doh t'ink twice bout it. Young one pun de wing, run at dem every time. Big fella a de back, win every ball weh come inna de air, dat alone.

Dis nah just bout de number pun de board. We a de side weh keep playing no matter wah de score seh, dat a who we be, plain and simple.

Win or lose, when dis done, we a nyam together after, we a walk out together, nobody leaving one man behind, whichever way it gwine.

So mek we go now. Up. Out. Together!
