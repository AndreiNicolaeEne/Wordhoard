Aright mi wahn tell unu how di ting mi gwaan dat day, from di very start, cau if mi nuh tell it right unu wahn think mi di exaggerate, an mi swear pon everything mi nuh di exaggerate one bit.

Di mawnin mi jus regular. Hot, sticky, di way Belize City stay befo rain. But bout two a clak di sky start do sopm mi nuh like. It gone still. Nuh wind, nuh bird a fly, di leaf dem pon di mango tree jus a hang deh like dem fraid fi move. Mi granmodda used fi seh when di sky da stil like dat an it look kinda green-grey, unu betta watch out, an true anuff, by di time an hour pass di wind mi start pick up serious.

Mi seh, “oonu come eena di house now, dis nuh lookin good.” An mi an di husband run go outside quick fast fi tie dung weh could tie an bring een weh could bring een. Di plastic chair dem, di wash basin, di likkle bike weh di yungess lef outside, all a dat mi a grab an fling eena di veranda. Di zinc pon di neighba shed a already rattle like it a try fi tek off pon eena wan.

By di time we don wid all dat di wind really a rise now, a whole nex level, an mi call di pickney dem, “come, all a unu come sit een di hallway, dat da di safess part a di house.” So we all gather up close, di modda dem, di children dem, everybody pack up together een dat likkle stretch a hallway weh nuh gat no window. An it nuh long befo dat di current gaan. Lights out, jus so, an di dark come dung pon top a di storm an mek everyting worse.

Mi wahn tell unu, di wind get so loud unu couldn even hear yuself talk. Mi da try seh sopmen to di eldess an she jus a look pon mi mouth move, cau nutn nuh a come through. It mi like di whole worl a groan. An den, bwoy, wan tree lick dung, nuh far, mi could hear it lan, dat heavy crack an den di whole ground kinda shake. Mi heart drop. Mi nuh seh nutn to di pickney dem bout it but mi grab di yungess close an mi hold on.

Di roof start peel. Unu ever hear zinc a peel back eena wind? It soun like sopmen a rip. Wan piece a di roof jus gaan, tear weh off an gaan eena di dark, an rain start pour eena di back room like sopmen throw wata outta bucket. An di window eena di front room, di one weh mi nuh get fi board up een time, it crack, mi hear di crack straight through di wind, but it hold. It hold, thank God, cau if dat glass mi bruk we woulda in real trouble wid all dat flying debris outside.

Mi wahn admit sopmen now weh mi never seh dat night: mi mi fraid bad. Real bad. Mi nuh let di pickney dem see it pon mi face, mi jus a hold dem an a seh “we good, we good, jus wait it out,” but eena mi head mi a pray straight, mi a beg God fi mek di roof hold, fi mek di tree nuh fall pon di house, fi mek di likkle wan dem come out safe.

Di eldess, she brave doah, she hold di yungess hand di whole time an she nuh cry, but mi coulda see her lip a tremble.

The eldest: “Mommi, di roof gwein fly weh?”

Me: “Nuh worry unu head, we deh right ya, we nuh movin.”

Mi seh dat but mi nuh even know if it mi true.

Den, aroun bout di worse a it, when mi tink it couldn get nuh worse, everyting jus… stop. Like sopmen switch off a switch. Di wind drop, di rain ease up, an dis quiet come dung pon di place weh mi nuh could describe. Aftah all dat noise, dat quiet mi almos loud eena itself. Nobody nuh move fi a good minute, we jus a sit deh eena di hallway a listen fi mek sure it done fi true.

Di husband stand up first. “Mi gwein go look.”

Me: “Wait, mek mi come wid yu.”

We open di door an step outside, an. Lawd. Di whole yard change up. Right deh eena di front, di big tree weh mi tell unu bout, it dung across di whole gate, blockin di way out complete, we nuh coulda pull no vehicle out till dat get cut up an clear. Part a di roof weh mi tell unu bout, dat mi nuh no likkle patch job, dat da real work, dat gwein need new sheet zinc an a whole crew fi fix.

But den we look ova pon di neighba yard, an dem fence, weh used fi stand up nice, it jus flatten out complete, laid dung flat pon di grung like a giant step pon it. An mi haffi seh, as bad as fi we damage stay, dat neighba fence look worse off eena wan way, nutn lef standin a tall. Mek unu tink bout how it coulda been.

Di sip-sip start di very nex mawnin, everybody eena di street a talk bout who lose weh, who tree fall pon who yard, who roof fly clean weh. We spend di whole week aftah dat pon cleanup, di husband an di neighba dem man tek chainsaw fi cut up di tree piece by piece, di pickney dem help drag branch dem to di side a di road, an mi mi busy try save weh mi could eena di house from di wata damage. Every day sun come out an we jus wuk, wuk, wuk, sweat an sawdust an dat wet-wood smell everyweh.

An now, when mi tell people bout it, mi still cyaan get through it widout mi voice raise up pon di worse part. Mi seh, “an di tree lick dung, bwoy!, mi tell unu mi heart nearly stop!” Mi still deh pon di edge a mi seat when mi tell it, even doah mi da di one weh live it. Mi steups sometime when mi memba how close dat glass mi come fi bruk. But we mek it through, all a we, an di roof fix up nice now, better than befo if unu waan know di truth, an every hurricane season since, mi first ting mi do soon as dem start talk bout wan storm a come, mi run go check mi zinc nail dem tight, cau mi nuh gwein go through dat again unprepared, nuh sah.
