Bwai mek I tell yu wa happen to mi da di office one time, cau yu wouldn believe it.

I mi need mi good-conduct paper stamp dat same day self, cau di job seh if I nuh bring it back Monda mahnin dehn gwehn gi di spot to smadi else. So I gaan down early, tek mi likkle line, an wen I reach da di counter di man tek di form, look pan it, look pan mi, an jus siddown deh a flip tru di page dem slow slow. Him nuh seh notn straight out, him jus seh,

The one behind the counter: Dis kina ting, it tek time fi push tru... unless a smadi know how fi mek it move fast.

Me: Mek it move fast how, sir? Ai, I nuh know bout dat.

I jus mek maiself sound simple an let it hang deh eena di air, di two a wi jus deh deh lookin pan wan anodda. He steups likkle bit, shove di paper roun, an seh it plainer di second time.

The one behind the counter: Some people, wen dehn want ting done same day, dehn understand fi lef a likkle something pan di side.

Me: Oh, but dis a government paper, sir, unu supposed to process it fi free, no? An mi cousin work eena di next office deh, mi wahn ask ahn how di rule set up.

Him face change one time. He seh he wahn check inna di back an he gaan wid di form fi a good while, mi jus deh deh a wait, nuh know if I wahn get it or if I wahn haffi come back nex week.

But wen he come back he stamp it, no extra cyaa, an gi mi it back straight.

So dat a how I get weh widout payin fi notn weh sopoze to come free anyway. People weh let demself get press like dat, dehn di feed di same ting fi keep happenin to di next one.
