Tenant: Good evening, sah. Mi nuh disturb yuh?

Landlord: No man, come een, siddung. Yuh a hear how di rain a beat dung out deh all week?

Tenant: Yes sah, mi nuh get nuh sleep from it pon di zinc. Cyaan complain doah, betta dan drought.

Landlord: True dat. So wah bring yuh dis evenin?

Tenant: A di rent mi come fi talk bout, sah. Mi affi tell yuh straight, tings tight dis a time, mi have pickney a school and everything a rise same time.

Landlord: Mi hear yuh, but everybody tight nuh.

Tenant: Mi know sah, but hear mi out. From mi move een a three year now mi nuh miss one payment, every month it deh pon di dot. And di bathroom pipe mi ben tell yuh bout from di start still a leak, water a run guh a waste and nutten nuh do bout it yet.

Landlord: Mi know bout di pipe.

Tenant: So mi a ask fi a fifth off wah it deh now.

Landlord: (steups) A fifth? Mi cyaan do dat, di bill dem a rise pon mi side too, sah, taxes an all.

Tenant: Three year steady nuh mean sumn?

Landlord: It mean sumn, mi nah seh it nuh mean nutten. Mi can gi yuh bout a tenth, dat a as far as mi can guh fi now.

Tenant: A tenth lean far from a fifth, sah, dat lean far fah true.

Landlord: Mi know, but unu haffi understand, a nuh easy fi mi side neitha.

Tenant: Alright, wah bout we settle roun an eighth, somewhere between di two?

Landlord: Mi nah promise nutten dis minute, mi wi study it ova di weekend an tek di ansa come gi yuh.

Tenant: Alright sah, mi can live wid dat. Jus nuh forget bout di pipe eeh.

Landlord: Mi nuh forget. Walk good.
