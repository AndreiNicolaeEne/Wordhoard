The eldest: Awright, before di whole a dem reach, mi ago show yuh how fi play, likkle pickney. We need four a we at leas fi start, more betta, but four can gwaan.

The younger one: Weh di boundary deh?

The eldest: From di gate all di way to di mango tree, dat a one side. Di other side, a from di fence right dere to weh dawg tie up. Yuh pass dat line, yuh outta bounds, done.

The younger one: An who ago chase first?

The eldest: We dumb school fi dat. Whoever lose, dem a it. Watch mi han, one, two, three, whoever show di wrong sign, dem haffi chase.

The younger one: How yuh know when somebody really tag, doh?

The eldest: Two han pon yuh, nuh just one lickle brush. Mi haffi ketch yuh proper wid mi whole han. Mi tell yuh seh a lickle finger tap nuh count, yuh hear mi?

The younger one: Steups. Dat strict, eeh.

The eldest: A so di game set. Now, wah if a runner run outta di line when dem a flee di chaser?

The younger one: Dat weh mi wan know.

The eldest: Straight so, dem out. Step pass di mango tree or pass di fence deh, same ting as if di chaser tag yuh. Nuh argument bout dat.

The younger one: An wah happen when dem tag mi?

The eldest: Yuh freeze right deh, likka statue, yuh nuh move a inch. Yuh haffi stay so til one a di odda one dem weh still a run come tap yuh loose. Dem touch yuh, yuh free fi run back out.

The younger one: When di round done?

The eldest: When everybody freeze up an nobody lef fi come tap dem loose, chaser win. Di one weh tag first a di chaser fi di nex round.

The younger one: Wah if mi mess up dis time?

The eldest: Nuh badda worry, first round nuh ago count 'gainst yuh. Mi promise. Jus watch an learn.
