Me: Boy I have to talk to yuh serious nuh, not de ole talk. Wha happen by de Priority Bus Route dis marnin coulda done yuh, yuh know dat.

The friend: (steups) Doh start dat again man. Look at meh, I standing up right here, I orite.

Me: Yuh standing up cause de maxi brake in time, not cause yuh careful. I was right dey watching yuh a race de bike through de traffic, no helmet, weaving through de car dem like yuh eh have no sense, and de front wheel come up off de road, and I t'ink dat was it, dat was de last time I go see yuh alive. Meh heart was beating so till it want to jump out meh chest, and after, meh hand was shaking, fuh true, I eh lying to yuh.

The friend: Is nuttin, I doing dis long time already, I know wha I doing.

Me: Yuh know wha yuh doing? Yuh forget yuh bruk yuh wrist doin de same t'ing by de savannah, two years now, de bone still not sit right, yuh cyar even make a proper fist wid dat hand! Dat was a warning and yuh eh listen. Dis time yuh get away. Next time yuh eh go get up walking, yuh go get up in a chair for de rest a yuh life, if yuh get up at all.

The friend: ...

Me: And is who go siddung by yuh bed when dat happen, eh? Is who go bury yuh if it worse than dat? Yuh mother go bury she own son, dat is who. She go stand up over de box and everybody go seh, "he coulda stop, somebody coulda tell him." Well I telling yuh now, so doh mek dat be true.

The friend: (long steups, quiet) ...Awright, awright, yuh mekkin sense. I eh promising I stopping de bike altogether but I go wear de helmet, and I go slow down, especially by de Bus Route. Dat is wha I have fi give yuh right now.

Me: Dat go have to do. But doh mek meh hear seh yuh racing again, yuh hear meh.

The friend: I hear yuh, I hear yuh. Leh we go lime nuh, doh worry yuhself so.
