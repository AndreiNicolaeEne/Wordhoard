The husband: Girl, yuh deh sit down there since morning and yuh ain't touch yuh cocoa-tea yet. It getting cold in the cup and yuh eye still lookin some place far. What happen to yuh.

The wife: Is nothing, is just... I still thinking bout the dream I had last night, I cyah shake it off meh head at all. It following me since I wake up, it deh with me still.

The husband: A dream. Well tell meh nuh, doh keep it lock up in yuh head so. What kind a dream have yuh so far gone this morning.

The wife: Boy, is a strange one. It start off simple simple, nothing to it. I was just walkin home, same way I does walk every evenin, past the shop, past the church, nothing outta order. But the road, it just kept goin and goin. I was walkin and walkin and the house wasn't turnin up where it does turn up. I pass the same corner I know good good and it still had more road after. I ain't feel fraid at first, is only after a while I start to notice, eh, this walk takin longer than it should.

The husband: Mm hm. And then.

The wife: And then finally, finally I reach. But when I reach and I go inside, the house had a room in it I never see before in meh life. Never. I done live in that house since I small and I know every corner of it, but this room was just deh, like it always been there and I just never notice.

The husband: Wait wait, what kind a room it was. Like a bedroom, a kitchen, what.

The wife: I cyah even tell yuh proper what kind a room, it wasn't like no room I could name straight. It ain't have no particular furniture I could point to, it just... was a room. And inside it, sittin down calm calm like she never leave this earth at all, was meh mother.

The husband: Yuh mother! But she gone from... how long now, five years?

The wife: Six. Six years this September gone. And she was sittin down there in that room like it was any regular Sunday, like nothing happen, like time never pass at all for she. She lookin the same, she skin the same, she voice the same. And she was talkin to me.

The husband: Talkin bout what? What she tell yuh?

The wife: That is the part that vexin me the whole mornin. I cyah remember the actual words. I know she was tellin me something, something that feel important, I could still feel the weight of it now, like a message left with me, but the words themself just gone. Like a hand open up and let go.

The husband: But try. Try and remember, close yuh eye and go back in it slow.

The wife: I tryin, boy, I tryin since I wake up. It was somethin bout... nah. It start to come and then it slip right back out. It like a word deh right there on the tip a meh tongue and it just refuse to land. All I have left is the feelin of bein told, not the tellin itself.

The husband: That does happen so, eh, the feelin stay and the words gone. Leh we hear the rest, doh trouble yuhself with that part no more.

The wife: Awright. So after that, the dream shift up on me again, sudden so, like a page turn. And now I searchin for somethin. I in a hurry, I lookin and lookin, openin up drawer after drawer, movin thing round, and whatever it is I lookin for just ain't deh where it should be. Nothing turnin up. And the more I search the more frantic I gettin, meh heart start to beat up fast even inside the dream self.

The husband: What yuh was lookin for though. What was it.

The wife: That is the thing, I cyah tell yuh, cause even inside the dream I ain't know what it was! I just know I had was to find it, it urgent, it pressin on me, but the actual thing itself never show itself to me. I was just searchin blind, pure panic and no name for what I searchin.

The husband: Steups. That does be the worse kind, when the dream got yuh runnin bout for somethin and it ain't even tell yuh what.

The wife: Yes boy, and it just kept climbin and climbin, the panic, till it break the sleep clean off me. I wake up sudden, heart still gallopin like I really was runnin down a road somewhere. And I lay down there a good while, not movin, just breathin, and I couldn't even tell right off which one was the real one, the dream or this bed I wake up in. For a good few second there I ain't sure at all.

The husband: Lord. That sound like a heavy dream fu true. But tell meh, when a person gone so long and dem turn up in a dream lookin so calm and ordinary, sittin down like nothing pass, what unu say that mean? I hear people say all kind a thing bout that.

The wife: I dunno, eh. Meh aunty always sayin when the dead come to yuh calm so, is a good sign, is she come to check on yuh, come to see how yuh farin. But then meh next door neighbour, the one does go by church hard, she say if yuh cyah remember what dem tell yuh, is cause it ain't fu you to know yet, it ain't time.

The husband: Or it could just be yuh head doing what a head does do in the night, stringin up whatever it pick up through the day and mixin it round with what yuh missin. Yuh miss she, plain and simple, and yuh head give yuh a lil visit.

The wife: Could be that simple, yes. But then why the room though, why a room that never exist in this house at all. If is just meh head mixin up mih missin she, why it build a whole new room fu the thing.

The husband: Ha, now yuh askin meh questions I cyah answer! Maybe is a room yuh does need and yuh ain't have one yet, so yuh head build it fu yuh.

The wife: Boy, hush yuh mouth, doh get all deep and philosophical on meh this early mornin. Drink yuh tea.

The husband: I drinkin it, I drinkin it. But I tellin yuh, if she come back one more time and yuh remember the words this time, yuh better wake meh up and tell meh right away, dream or no dream.

The wife: Yuh mad or what. I not wakin nobody up in the middle a the night for that. But if it happen again, and I remember, yuh go be the first one I tell, promise.

The husband: Awright then. Now drink yuh cocoa-tea before it get cold complete, doh let a dream spoil good tea too.

The wife: Yes yes, I comin, I comin. Is just... it does stay with yuh, eh, when somebody gone appear so peaceful. Like they tellin yuh not to worry, even if yuh cyah remember one word of how they say it.

The husband: Well then maybe that self is the message. Doh worry. That could be it right there.

The wife: Maybe. Maybe so. Anyway, pass meh the sugar nuh, this tea gone lukewarm awready and I still ain't drink a drop.
