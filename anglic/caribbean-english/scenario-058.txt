Me: Bwai, dis roof a gimme trouble bad. Di leak worse pahn di back corner, right ovah weh di bed deh. Wen ih rain haad las night, wata jos a run dong di wall, mi haffi put tree bucket unda ih and stil ih a overflow.

The friend: Yu tell di landlord bout it already?

Me: Yes man, from di fos time mi raise it, ih tell mi seh ih wahn sen a man fu look pahn di zinc. Dat mi been asking him bout tree, four month now. Mi aks him again di second time las week, ih seh di sehm ting back, "mi wahn come dis week," notn nuh come fahn.

The friend: Wow. How long dis ting di gwaan fu chroo, and weh exakly ih promise yu?

Me: Since roun April. Ih neva put notn eena riting, jos tork.

The friend: Steups. Dat's how dem landlord dem stay, dem tork sweet and duh notn. Ok, hear mi nuh. Yu could withhold di rent, but if ih vex ih could try fu put yu out, and dat mean cort. Or yu could guh to di city fu file complaint bout di house condition, dat tek time but ih deh pan record. Or yu jos pack up and guh, but weh yu wahn find dis time a year wid di rain a come?

Me: True dat.

The friend: Mi neighba landlord did do di sehm sinting to har, seh ih wahn fix di gate fu monts, she haffi call di city inspector fi him fu move.

Me: So weh yu tink mi shoulda do?

The friend: Write him wahn letta, put di date and everyting yu aks him fah eena it, and gi him a copy. Dat way if it reach cort, yu gat proof yu did tell him seh.
