Mek me tell yuh how de checkpoint gwan dat time, and mi nah gon mek it bigger than how it really deh, cause it nuh need dat.

We ben a go up de road fu de interior, de whole bus pack, an everybody know already dat somewhe halfway dem gon stop de vehicle. Dem seh de checkpoint deh pan dat stretch cause a all de mining business wha gwan up dat side, dem got fu check who a go een and wha dem a carry, so nuh care how big or how small de vehicle, all got fu stop and dem check yuh proper. Been like dat fu a good while now, everybody accustom to it, but accustom nuh mean yuh nuh feel nutten when yuh see de line.

And Lawd, de line ben long dat day. By time we reach it done stretch back down de road, people out dey a stand up eena de sun, some a lime by de side, some jus a look pon dem foot, nobody a talk too much. We wait good half hour before we even reach near de front, and all dat time mi belly a turn lil bit, mi nuh gon lie. Yuh always tell yuhself yuh got nutten fu hide, yuh papers een order, but still de heart a beat lil harder de closer yuh come to de front. Mi a watch de officer dem, how dem a stand, how dem a look eena people face, and mi a try fu keep meself calm, but nerves is nerves, dem nuh ask yuh permission.

When we reach up to de front, mi han over de papers straight, nuh fumbling, cause mi ben practice dat een mi head already.

The officer: Where yuh heading dis morning?

Me: A go up to de interior, sir, fu wuk.

Dat one nuh trouble mi at all, mi answer plain, straight out, cause it true and it simple. But den de next one come, and dis one mi haffi tink pon lil more before mi open mi mouth.

The officer: And who yuh deh wid up dey, who yuh reporting to?

Mi haffi mek sure mi get de answer right, cause if yuh stumble pon a question like dat dem start fu look pon yuh different, so mi tek a breath, mi run tru it een mi head quick, and mi gi de answer steady, nuh too fast, nuh too slow.

Me: Mi deh wid a crew already up dey, sir, dem expecting mi dis week.

De officer nuh say nutten back straight way. He tek up de papers and he a look tru dem, one page after de next, and dat lil silence wha come after, dat de worst part, cause yuh nuh know wha a gwan eena de man head, yuh jus haffi stand dey and wait. Mi a hold mi face still, mi nuh let nutten show, but eena mi head mi a run tru everyting mi got, wondering if it een order.

Den he ask bout one ting mi ben near fu forget, a lil detail bout when mi last register fu travel dat road, and mi haffi tink quick and gi it to him right dey pon de spot, cause it nuh deh write down pon nutten mi got een mi han, it jus something mi haffi remember and seh out loud. Mi tek a second, mi find it, mi gi him de answer, and he nod lil bit, nuh mek nuh big ting bout it, jus write something down and move on.

While all dat a gwan, mi eye ben pon de man wha ben deh front a mi eena de line. Dem hold him back longer dan de rest, dem a ask him question after question, and de officer dem a look pon him proper hard, checking him bag, checking him face gainst de paper, but nobody nuh seh nutten out loud bout it, nobody nuh point nuh finger. Everybody jus a watch quiet, cause is not we business, and yuh nuh want fu draw attention pon yuhself by a look too long neither. Mi jus tek it een from de corner mi eye and mi keep mi own face straight.

Trute is, mi haffi wuk hard fu keep meself steady tru de whole ting. Mi voice ben come out even, but eena mi chest mi heart a beat like it want fu jump out, and mi han dem ben lil cold even doh de sun ben hot dat morning. Yuh nuh want fu let dem see yuh shaking, cause dem read dat kind a ting quick, so mi jus hold on, breathe slow, answer wha dem ask and nuh more dan dat.

But mi wi seh dis, de officer nuh rough wid mi at all, he even gi mi a lil courtesy, when he see mi a stand dey wid de heavy bag pon mi back, he tell mi mi can put it down side a mi foot while he a check de papers, nuh need fu hold it up de whole time. Small ting, but it mek a difference when yuh nerves already high.

After a while, he fold up de papers back and he han dem to mi.

The officer: Yuh good, go on.

Nuh more dan dat, jus a nod fu go, and dat nod carry more weight dan any long talk coulda gi mi. Mi tek back mi papers, mi tuck dem safe, and mi walk on back to de vehicle, keeping mi pace normal, nuh too fast like mi eager fu run, nuh too slow like something wrong.

Is only after we ben pass a good twenty minute down de road, de vehicle a move steady again, houses and bush a pass by de window, dat mi feel de whole ting let go outta mi body. Is like all de tension wha ben build up while mi deh eena dat line, it jus wait til mi safe fu really hit mi, and when it hit, mi feel it eena mi shoulders, eena mi han, mi realise how tight mi ben hold meself de whole time widout even knowing. Mi close mi eye lil bit and mi jus breathe.

Mi tell dis story careful, nuh mek it bigger dan how it ben gwan, cause nutten drastic nuh happen, nobody nuh get lock up, mi papers ben een order de whole time. But mi nuh mek it smaller either, cause de wait ben real, de fear ben real, even doh nutten come a it. A so life pon dat road stay, and mi jus a tell it how it gwan, no more, no less.
