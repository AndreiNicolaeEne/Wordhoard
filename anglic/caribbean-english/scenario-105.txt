Me: Yuh know wah, mi tired a talk bout it an nuh do nothing bout it. Trinidad. Panorama. Carnival. We a go, an we a go do it pon a thousand dollar, not one cent more. Dat a di budget, full stop.

The friend: Beg yuh nuh gwaan like yuh nuh serious. A thousand fi di whole trip? Airfare an everyting inna dat?

Me: Airfare an everyting inna dat. Watch mi nuh. First ting first, di getting deh, di flight. Mi check it aready, four hundred dollar return di two a wi. Dat a di biggest piece a di whole ting soh mi a start right deh.

The friend: Four hundred. Awrite, dat nuh too bad. So we lef six hundred fi everyting else.

Me: Yes, mek wi write it dung. Four hundred. Now, how much day wi affi stay fi it worth wi while? Mi nuh flying all dat way fi four day an come back.

The friend: Weh yuh a seh, five? Seven?

Me: Seven too short, wi jus a settle in good an wi affi lef.

The friend: Den fifteen? Mek it a proper trip.

Me: Fifteen too much, wi nuh have di money fi stretch dat far, an yuh know wi affi come back a work. Mi a seh ten. Ten day give wi time fi settle in, do di ting dem wi waan do, an still come home widout wi pocket a beg.

The friend: Ten day. Mi cyaan argue wid dat, dat sound bout right.

Me: A one ting though, an it nuh negotiable, wi haffi deh a Panorama. Mi nuh care wah else drop off dis list, wi affi deh deh when di pan dem a play inna di Savannah. Dat a di whole reason mi a book dis trip inna di first place.

The friend: Mi nuh disagree wid dat, but yuh know seh di ticket nuh free.

Me: Eighty dollar di two a wi, mi check it aready. But if wi a put eighty deh pon someting extra like dat, someting affi come out somewhere else fi mek room.

The friend: Downgrade weh wi a stay den. Wi nuh need no fancy hotel wid pool an breakfast an dem someting deh, a lie dung wi a lie dung, guesthouse can do fine.

Me: True dat. Twenty a night fi guesthouse, times ten night, a two hundred. Mek wi run di tally so far: four hundred flight, two hundred lodging, eighty fi Panorama, dat a six hundred an eighty already gone.

The friend: Six-eighty out a one thousand, we still have three-twenty lef. Nuh bad, nuh bad.

Me: Wait, one more ting mek mi seh before wi move on. If wi manage get di flight fi less, like say wi book early an it drop to three-fifty, wi coulda stretch it to eleven day instead a ten.

The friend: Dat woulda sweet fi true. But mi nuh trusting dat price fi jus drop like dat, so mek wi plan pon ten day fi now, an if di flight cheap off later, a bonus we tek.

Me: Fair enough. Now food. Mi a seh twenty dollar a day di two a wi put together fi eat, cook some a wi self, buy some doubles an roti an street food, dat kinda ting.

The friend: Nyam doubles every day, mi cyaan complain bout dat.

Me: Twenty a day times ten day, a two hundred more. So now wi deh pon, six-eighty plus di two hundred food, eight hundred an eighty.

The friend: Eight hundred an eighty out a one thousand. One hundred an twenty lef.

Me: An wi affi put someting aside fi when tings gwaan bad, yuh know how trip stay, someting always drop pon yuh weh yuh nuh plan fah.

The friend: A hundred dollar emergency money, put it aside, nuh touch it unless someting really gwaan wrong.

Me: A hundred. So dat tek wi straight to nine hundred an eighty.

The friend: Nine-eighty. Twenty dollar lef ova. Dat nuh bad at all, we can breathe easy.

Me: Wait nuh, mi never done. Mi did also waan put fifty dollar aside fi one splurge, one nice dinner, someting fi wi self one night, nuh haffi count every cent di whole trip like wi a prisoner.

The friend: Fifty dollar pon one splurge when wi barely have room fi breathe? Mi seh save it, put it back inna di emergency instead, dat betta safe.

Me: Nuh, mi nah gi up di splurge. A whole year wi save fi dis trip, mi waan one night weh wi nuh haffi think bout money at all. Wi wi manage widout it, trust mi.

The friend: Steups. A soh yuh stay, yuh never give up nutten. Awrite, awrite, mi hear yuh, di splurge stay in. But mek wi check di tally good now, cause mi feel like it nuh a add up right no more.

Me: Awrite mek wi run it whole from di top: four hundred flight, two hundred lodging, eighty Panorama, two hundred food, hundred emergency, fifty splurge...

The friend: Add it up nuh, mi nuh have patience fi guess.

Me: Four hundred, plus two hundred, a six hundred. Plus eighty, six-eighty. Plus two hundred food, eight-eighty. Plus di hundred emergency, nine-eighty. Plus di fifty splurge, a one thousand an thirty.

The friend: Mi tell yuh! We over by thirty. Kiss mi teeth, always someting nuh line up right di firs time.

Me: Awrite nuh badda fret bout it. We cyaan gi up Panorama, an we jus done fight ova di splurge so dat nah move neither. Food an lodging already lean as dem can go.

The friend: Den a di emergency money haffi tek di cut. Wi nuh really need a full hundred sitting deh doing nothing, cut it dung to fifty, dat save wi fifty right deh, an fifty still betta dan nutten if someting gwaan wrong.

Me: Fifty off a hundred. So now, one thousand an thirty, minus di fifty, nine hundred an eighty.

The friend: Nine-eighty out a one thousand. Twenty dollar lef ova, real an true dis time, an dat can jus stay pon di side fi drinks or someting small wi nuh even plan fah.

Me: Nine hundred an eighty dollar. Ten day. Trinidad, Panorama an all, an we nuh even have to give up di likkle splurge.

The friend: Wi did it fi true. Mi cyaan wait fi lime dung deh, hear di pan a beat, nyam wi doubles, an nuh haffi think bout money di whole time.

Me: A dat mi a tell yuh from di start. Wait till mi tell unu people bout dis, dem a go jealous bad. Book di flight tomorrow before it change pon wi.

The friend: First ting inna di morning. Nine-eighty, twenty spare, an we deh a Panorama. Mi cyaan wait.
