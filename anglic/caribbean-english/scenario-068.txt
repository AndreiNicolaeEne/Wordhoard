Lisen nuh, mi waahn tell yu bout wahn ting weh di run pan people jus come to di City, so yu nu fall eena it.

Da wahn sweet-mouth man, always smiling, always ga wahn deal fi offer yu. Him deh bout di market, di bus stop, anywhe new people deh stand up a look lost. Him friendly bad, aks yu how yu di stay, seh yu look like good people, seh him waahn help yu settle. Den him start tell yu bout wahn piece a lan, or wahn boat, or wahn job weh pay real good, and all yu need fi do da put down likkle money right now fi hold di spot.

Dat deh a di whole ting - if yu pay eniting op front, yu nu wahn si dat money again, and yu nu wahn si him again needa. Him gaan like smoke, phone dead, face gone, like him neva did deh a-tall.

Mi memba wahn fella, jus come dong from di village fi work, di man tell him seh him ga wahn container full a stuff comin een, and if him put in five hundred dollar today, him wahn double it by di weekend. Di fella nyam it op, gi him di money, and dat was di last time anybody si da man face. Cost him near all weh him bring fi start fresh, and him neva get back wahn cent.

So yu fi watch fi di signs before it get dat far: wen a man too friendly too fast, wen him only di talk bout money and nu answer straight wen yu aks question, wen him waahn cash right eena him han an nu paper, nu receipt, nu nothing fi show fi it, and wen yu question him, watch how him steups an change di subject quick quick - dat a red flag right deh.
