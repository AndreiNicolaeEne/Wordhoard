Girl, doh even ask meh how long it take meh to sit down and write this, but I promise yuh I was go tell yuh everything from the top, since yuh couldn't make it and I know yuh vex vex about that still. So sit down good, because this go long.

Meh eye open before four in the morning, before the alarm even ring, and I just lying down there looking the ceiling like it have answer for me. Couldn't sleep back, nerves had meh stomach turning. By the time everybody up and moving I try to nyam a lil piece of bake and saltfish the mother make, but I only manage two three bite, throat just closing up on meh. My sister say, "Yuh have to eat something nah, yuh cyah stand up in front people and faint," and I tell her I orait, but is lie I lying.

Now hear this, right before we ready to walk out the house, real real last minute, somebody bawl out the garter gone missing. Whole house upside down, cushion flying, bag turning inside out, meh godsister on she knee looking under the bed like she looking a lost dog. Turn out it was in the pocket of the robe the whole time, and everybody just steups and laugh at the same time because we nearly leave late over a lil piece of lace.

When we reach, that was the first time in my whole life I see both family standing up together in one place, his side and my side, all in one yard, and it hit meh different than I expected. His mother and my mother end up standing side by side near the front before anybody even organize it so, just so, like the two of them find one another on their own. And is his mother who cry first, believe that. The minute she see meh coming she bawl out loud, hand pon she chest, and that set off the whole front row one time, my mother, meh aunt, even meh uncle who does act hard was wiping he face with he handkerchief pretending it was sweat.

The vows part, girl, I go be honest with yuh, I lose the line. I had it memorize good good, practice it in the mirror plenty night, but standing up there looking he face everything just gone blank for a good few second. The pastor had was to lean in soft and give meh a lil word to start meh back off, and once I catch the first word the rest just come back like it was there waiting all along. Nobody laugh at meh, well, except meh own self after, because in the moment it feel like the longest silence of meh life.

Then the exchange, when we say the words that seal it and put on the ring, that part gone so quick after all that build up, like the whole day was leading up to one lil minute and then it pass just so. But it was the part that really seal it for true, and I feel it right down to meh foot.

After that was the papers, sit down at a lil table, sign where they show yuh, and yuh have to have one witness stand up for each side. He brother stand up for him and meh best friend from since school days stand up for me, both of them signing they name serious serious like it was them getting married, and after they done they just hug up one another like old friends, though that was the first time the two of them meeting.

Then, girl, the photographs. Lord have mercy, the photographs. We was out there posing for so long meh foot start to hurt in them shoes. The one taking picture kept trying to arrange cluster after cluster of family just right, "grandmother side come, no not so close, aunt move a lil, allyuh cousin dem line up," and every time she get one group settle somebody wandering off to talk or somebody child running loose in the background. Take nearly two hour just to get through everybody, and by the end we was all just laughing because nobody had the patience left to hold a straight face.

At the reception it had a seating mix-up that had meh laughing and cringing same time. They put two people who does not see eye to eye sitting up right next to one another, elbow to elbow, and the whole table could feel the tension, everybody talking extra loud and extra polite trying to smooth it over. One of them lean over to meh after and say low low, "Yuh coulda warn meh nah," and all I could do was steups and apologize and beg them make peace for the night.

Food, girl. The macaroni pie gone in about ten minutes flat, I ain't even get through one plate before somebody come back for third helping. But the one nobody touch, believe it or not, was a whole tray of some fancy lil thing the caterer insist we add, some foreign-looking appetizer, and it sit there the whole night looking pretty and untouched while people was fighting over stew chicken and callaloo.

Toast time break meh down proper. His best friend get up to talk and he couldn't get through it, voice cracking, had to stop twice, wipe he face, start back over, and by the time he done half the room was in tears including me who was suppose to be holding it together for the pictures.

All the while it had one dancer, meh husband auntie, who simply would not sit down the whole night. Every song come on she out there, fast one, slow one, didn't matter, she out there wining and calling other people to come join she. Somebody try to get she to rest a minute and she just wave them off and keep going till the band pack up.

And of course somebody had to drag out the old family story, the one everybody done hear a hundred time, about how the grandfather nearly miss he own wedding because he get lost looking for the church, and the whole table sit there and listen like it was the first time, laughing in all the right place even though half of them could tell it word for word theyself.

By the time the last guest leave and the lights start turning off one by one, it was just the two of us standing up in the middle of all that mess, plate and cup everywhere, flower petal all over the floor, one shoe of mine kick off under a chair somewhere. We ain't even clean up nothing right away. We just stand up there quiet, holding on to one another, looking at the whole wreck of a beautiful day, and it was the first real quiet minute we had since four that morning.

So that is the whole thing, start to finish, the way I would tell it if yuh was sitting right down there next to meh. Wish yuh was there for true, but now yuh have it all the same.
