Bai, dis whole year me tek up a plot down at di scheme, one lickle square a ground weh dem rent out to anybody who want grow sinting fuh dem self, and today me siddung fi tell allyuh how di whole ting gone, start to finish, di good and di bad and all di lickle politics weh come wid it. Da fi mi plot dat, right deh next to di old fella one, and from early in di year me did already study pon it.

Early in di year, when di mornings ben still have dat lickle chill pon dem before di sun climb up good, me start walk down de fi look pon di ground and study wha fi put in. Me nuh believe in rush yuh plotwork, so me tek time, watch how di sun a fall pon di land, ask di old fella weh have di plot next to mine wha he seh bout it. Him tell mi seh dis piece a ground good fuh bora and boulanger, and pumpkin wahn run good pon di back where it get more room. So a dat me settle pon, bora, boulanger, tomato, and a lickle corn corner fuh mi one. Me wait till di cold mornings gone good, bout a few week after, before me put di seed dem in di ground, cause plant early inna cold ground and dem juss siddung deh nuh grow.

And bai, once me put dem in, di weather gimme a real present. Sun out nice, lickle rain come through in di evening time juss enough fi wet di ground, and everything jump up outta dat ground like it ben waiting. Di bora vine dem start climb di stick before me even done tie up di last one, tomato bush thick up green green, and me proud, me a walk down de every evening after wuk juss fi come check pon it. Sometimes di old fella tek walk come look how mi crop dem a do, and me tek walk go check pon fi him one too, di two a we standing up in di sun praising up how good everything a bear.

But nutten nuh stay sweet too long. Bout a month in, me start see lickle hole inna di bora leaf dem, and when me look good me see a lickle green worm a nyam way. Me try wha di old fella tell mi, mix up soap water wid a lickle pepper and spray it pon di leaf dem, and dat lickle deal wid some a dem, but dem worm stubborn, dem keep a come back every week or so. Me end up haffi pick dem off wid mi own hand, one one, cause di soap water nuh finish di job, it juss slow dem dung.

Den di dry spell hit. Rain juss stop fall fuh weeks straight, sun a beat dung pon di ground till it crack up hard. Me haffi tek bucket and go fetch water every single day, marning before wuk and evening when me come back, hauling water from di pipe dung to di plot, back and forth, back and forth, till mi shoulder dem a hurt mi bad. Weeks dat gone on so, juss fi keep di plant dem alive, cause if yuh miss a day or two inna dat kind a heat, everything wither up quick quick.

Now dat pipe deh dung a di scheme, and dat's weh di real trouble start. It only have di one tap weh serve bout four a we plot, so we tek turn, supposed to tek turn, but one a di plot-holder, di one weh have di ground next to di old fella, start tek more time dan she fi tek, filling up drum and container fuh so, and by di time she done, half we marning gone and di sun already hot fuh true.

Me: "Neighbour, is how long yuh deh pon dis pipe? A fi mi turn since marning."

The neighbour: "Wait nuh, me nearly done, me just haffi fill dis last drum."

Me: "Yuh seh dat same ting yesterday, and di day before dat. Allyuh cyaan tek up di whole marning every time, some a we haffi go wuk after dis."

The neighbour steups, but she nuh answer straight, she juss tek she time and move off slow slow.

Dat gone on fuh a good two week, till di old fella call we all together one Saturday and seh we haffi write it dung, fair and square, who get di pipe wha day, wha time fi start, wha time fi stop, and if yuh nuh reach yuh time yuh lose it, next person tek over. Him post it up pon a lickle board by di tap. It nuh solve everything sweet sweet, but it settle di worse a it, and after dat me and di neighbour did civil to one another, even if it never went back to how it was before.

Now outta all a wha me plant, di bora was di real winner, hands dung. Dat ting bear and bear and bear, me could hardly keep up wid picking it, giving way basket to people weh pass by di plot juss fi get a shot a it. Even di pickney dem from di next yard woulda come stand up watch mi wuk, ask if dem could get a lickle bora fi tek home. But while di bora a do so good, di tomato dem start gi mi problem, after all dat rain and dry spell mix up so, di fruit dem start rot pon di vine before dem even ripe good, black spot spreading through di whole row before me could get to harvest half a dem. Me lose a good portion a dat crop right deh so, and nutten me try coulda stop it once it start.

Den mid-year, di big storm come through. Wind and rain fuh so, all night it beat dung pon di plot, and when me reach down de next marning, whole row a corn flat pon di ground, di tomato stake dem knock over, bora vine tangle up inna one another like rope. Me stand up deh look pon it and me heart juss drop. But nuh time fi cry ova it, so me spend di whole a dat Sunday, and di next one after, staking back up wha coulda save, propping di corn back straight, tying back di vine dem, digging out di plant dem weh did drown out from di water sitting too long. Some a it come back, some a it never recover, but me nuh lef nutten deh pon di ground to rot weh me coulda save.

Some people down deh siddung fi lime and pass time, but a nuh dat me deh fi do, a real wuk me deh pon, and dem know it too, cause dem always a come round when it time fi harvest.

By di time di year a wind dung, me tally up wha me get, and it did decent, plenty bora, good amount a boulanger, di pumpkin dem weh run wild pon di back come out big and sweet, and even wid all a wha me lose, still had more dan enough fi mi own kitchen. Me trade off a big basket a pumpkin to a friend weh grow ground provision pon she plot, cause she had cassava and sweet potato me been wanting bad, and dat exchange work out good fuh both a we.

Come di end, me clear off wha did left, tun over di ground, lef it fi rest through di dry months till it time fi start back. And when me siddung and study pon di whole year, me haffi seh it was worth it, di pest dem, di dry spell, di fuss wid di neighbour ova di pipe, di storm weh nearly wipe out half wha me had, all a dat was real work, real headache sometimes. But when me tink pon di bora weh nuh done bearing, and di basket me exchange, and how di whole plot come back green after dat storm try knock it dung, me seh it good. Next year me a go do di same ting again, and dis time me already know weh fi watch out fuh.
