Mi call unu here today fi one reason, and mi nuh call unu just fi nutten, so mek wi start. Unu can already si it pon mi face, and unu right fi si it, because something a come, and it come from far back. Is something mi did keep back years now, and it high time, past time, mi seh it out loud inna dis room wid all a unu hearing it di same time. So mi beg unu, siddung, and jus listen till mi done. Nobody nuh haffi seh a word yet.

Unu remember the year Mama go a rest. Unu remember weh happen right before dat, di pardner money weh di whole a wi did a save up week after week fi bury har proper, weh gone missing two days before wi did fi collect it. Everybody did a look pon everybody dat time, and it never tek long fi di finger settle. It settle pon my brother, because him did a hold di money fi safe-keep dat week, and when it come up short, everybody jump straight to him tief it, or him gamble it weh, or him drink it weh, whichever version dem did want fi believe worst. Nobody never siddung and ask him proper, nobody never si a scrap a proof, dem just condemn him right deh so at di kitchen table, and once dis family condemn yuh, it stick to yuh like pitch. Yuh cyaan wash it off.

Mi remember how di whole yard bwile up dat week, real anger, di kind weh nuh cool down easy. Auntie dem stop talk to him. Him own children hear people call him tief inna dem school yard. And even when time pass and everybody move on wid dem life, dat anger never really done, it just gone quiet, it siddung deep, and every so often somebody woulda bring it back up, half-joking, half-not, and my brother woulda just tek it, because weh him fi seh. Him did already lose the argument before it ever start.

So mi a go seh it plain now, one time, no dressing it up: it never was him. My brother never tek dat money. It was my sister.

Mi know because mi si it wid mi own eye. Mi did deh a di house dat night, mi never did sleep, and mi si har come out a di back room wid har hand inna har bag, and mi never think nutten of it till di money come up missing di next morning. And then years after, when har own life get hard and she couldn't hold it in har no more, she come to mi one night and she tell mi seh she was di one, seh she tek it fi pay off a man weh did threaten fi come tek something worse from har if she never find di money fast, seh she was frighten fi her life dat week and she never see no other way. She beg mi seh it deh between wi, and mi promise har. Mi keep dat promise long past weh it was ever right fi keep it.

Mi nuh have no clean excuse fi why mi hold it so long. Part a it was fraid, straight up fraid, fraid fi crack open something weh did finally gone quiet, because unu remember how bad it did get dat first time, and mi never want fi bring dat kind a anger back inna dis family. Part a it was mi did want fi protect har, because mi did si how frighten she did was, and mi never want fi be di one fi mash up har life more when she was already carrying so much. And part a it, if mi honest, was mi never want fi be di one who cause more damage by opening mi mouth. Mi tell miself keeping quiet was di safe thing, but keeping quiet nuh mean it was di right thing.

There was a time, years back now, when mi almost tell it. Unu remember when my brother marriage did a fall apart, and har family did bring up di old tief story like it was fact, like it settle who him is? Mi did a stand right deh, and di word dem was right deh pon mi tongue, and mi open mi mouth and nutten never come out. Mi close it back and mi walk weh. Mi think about dat moment more time than mi can count since.

And carrying it tek a toll pon mi mi cyaan even properly explain to unu. Mi watch mi own brother live small under a name weh was never his. Mi watch him stop trust himself round money, stop volunteer fi hold nutten fi nobody, because him did learn seh trust get thrown back inna yuh face. Mi never sleep good nuff nights, knowing weh mi know and watching him carry weh was never his fi carry.

So dat a it. Dat a weh mi keep back all dis years, and mi sorry, and mi ago come back to dat.

*

Di room gone dead quiet after mi seh it. Nobody nuh move.

The youngest: Wait, unu serious? All dis time?

Me: Mi serious. Mi certain. Mi si it wid mi own eye, and mi hear it from har own mouth after. Mi nuh have no doubt bout dis, none.

The niece: But everybody did seh... all dese years, everybody did seh it was him.

Me: Mi know weh everybody did seh. Mi si unu face right now and mi know some a unu still nuh want believe it, and mi understand dat, but a di truth mi a give unu, and mi nah tek it back.

Mi si di cousin shift up inna him seat, and mi know exactly weh a rise up inna him, because him was one a di worst fi bring it up over di years, half-joking, needling my brother bout it at every gathering. Mi see him mouth open like him a go seh something, and mi cut it off before it reach him lip.

Me: And before anybody start fi feel some way bout how much dem used to tease him bout it, or how dem used to look pon him different at dem own table, mi a tell unu now, dat weight nuh belong pon him no more. It never did belong pon him from di start.

My brother nuh seh nutten fi a long time. Him just siddung deh, and mi watch it move through him, and it was nuh one simple thing. It was relief, real relief, mi could si it loosen something inna him shoulder dat did tight up so long him probably forget it was tight. But right behind dat come a next kind a anger, fresh one, worse in some way, because relief cyaan give him back di years. Him lose people over dis. Him lose trust inna himself over dis. And nobody can give him dat back, not even truth.

The brother: So mi carry dat fi... how long now. Fi years. And unu all did know?

Me: Mi did know. Nobody else. And mi sorry, real sorry, because it was years too long, and mi shoulda tell yuh long before now.

My sister start cry right deh at di table, no hiding it, and she nuh mek no excuse fi harself.

The sister: Mi was frighten. Mi was frighten fi mi life dat week and mi never see no way out, and after, mi was too shame fi ever face unu wid it. Mi sorry. Mi sorry to yuh most of all.

And then di old anger, weh did siddung quiet so long, it come back up inna di room, but dis time it did know exactly weh fi land. It never land pon my brother again. It turn, finally, and it land pon di one who did actually earn it, and mi watch dat happen and mi feel someting mi cyaan quite name, not glad exactly, but something like di weight a years finally settling inna di right place.

Wi nuh fix nutten today. Dis family nuh come out di other side a dis clean and tidy, and it woulda foolish fi seh so. Some a unu still vex, and some a unu still hurt, and some a unu probably still need time fi even believe it proper. But wi done standing round di truth. Wi standing pon it now, finally, and dat a more than wi did have dis marnin.
