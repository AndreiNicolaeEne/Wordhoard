Di two a dem deh pan di step outside di friend yard, evening cool ketchin een, radio a play soft eena di back.

Me: Bwai, mi nuh waan mek dis awkward, but yu owe mi three hundred dollar since Febrary. Dat da five month now weh mi neva si wan cent a dat money.

My friend: Ah cousin, no bada mi wid dat right now nuh. Di store cut mi hours dis month an tings tight tight fi mi, yu no si how tings stay.

Me: Mi si how tings stay, but yu di tell mi seh yu wahn pay mi back before Eester done. Eester gaan wan good while now an mi neva si nutn come.

My friend: Bwai yu di come to mi lakka mi da some deadbeat. Mi seh mi wahn pay yu an mi mean it, jus gimme a likkle more time.

Me: Da noh di fus taim yu seh dat to. Yu mek di same promise back a Christmas, an befo dat to, wen yu seh yu wahn pay mi soon as yu get yu bonus. Mi patient wid yu, but mi patient nuh reach fu ebba.

My friend: Steups. Yu tink mi di lie to yu, cousin? Afta all wi been through since wi lee bwai, yu come a mi yard fi grieve mi bout money lakka mi noh yu fren.

Me: Da cause yu mi fren mek mi haffi seh someting. Wan stranga mi wouldn deal wid dis way, mi woulda done tell dem fi pay up long time now.

My friend: Awrite, awrite, hole on nuh man. Mi got fifty dollar pan mi right now, tek dat. Mi wahn get yu di rest by di en a di month, dat a promise.

Me: Mi hear yu. Mi wahn tek di fifty fi now, but doh vex if mi call yu every week till di rest a it come, cause dis time mi noh trustin nutn till mi si it eena mi han.
