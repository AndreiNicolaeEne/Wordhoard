The mother: Unu come yah now, mek one time, all a unu. A who break di lamp inna di front room?

The eldest: Nuh me, Mama. Mi neva touch it, mi swear pon mi life.

The mother: Yuh answer quick fi somebody weh nuh guilty.

The middle one: A true, a nuh me either. A di wind, Mama, di window did open likkle bit an' a breeze jus' come een an' knock it off a di table. Or maybe a di cat, him always deh pon dat table when unu nuh deh look pon him.

The mother: Wind cyaan knock over notn dat heavy off a solid table, an' dat cat nuh deh inna dis house since marnin, mi mi lef him outside miself. So try again.

The eldest: (steups) Ah true doah, is har weh always deh round di front room wid har phone, a she yuh fi ask.

The middle one: Nuh call mi out so! A yuh did deh a front room first, mi si yuh come outta deh wid someting inna yuh hand.

The eldest: Mi neva have notn inna mi hand!... well, mi did pick up a book off a di chair when mi pass through, dat a all dat happen.

The mother: A so? Jus' now yuh seh yuh neva touch notn a-tall, an' now yuh a tell mi seh yuh did deh inna di room a pick up book. Mek up yuh mind, one a di two a dem a lie.

The eldest: Mi jus' did deh deh fi a minute, Mama, mi nah lie to yuh.

The youngest: Steups, unu a gwaan like a me one deh yah, mi never even...

The mother: Mi nuh have time fi hear who did deh weh, an' nobody nuh waan own up. So a fi all a unu now, since nobody nuh have di courage fi seh a fi dem fault. Not one a unu a go watch television dis whole week, an' dat a final. Unu can steups all unu waan, mi mind set pon it.
