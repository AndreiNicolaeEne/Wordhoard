Him come stand up pon di verandah one evening when di light a drop soft an di yard settle down quiet, an him nuh waste no time roun it.

The one who came to ask: Listen nuh. Mi have something fi tell yuh, an mi nuh know how fi mek it pretty, so mi jus a go seh it straight. Mi want be more than weh wi is right now. Mi tired a hold it in.

Mi nuh cut him off. Mi jus deh deh a hear him out, mek him finish every word, cause when a person work up dem nerve fi come seh a thing so plain, di least yuh can do a give dem yuh full ears. When him done, mi teet suck one time an quiet tek ovah di yard a likkle before mi come answer him back.

Me: Bless up fi tell mi so straight, y'know. Nuff people woulda talk roun it fi a whole year an never land pon it. So mi a go give yuh di same.

Me: Di answer a no. An it nah go change down di road eidah, mi nuh want yuh a hold on to no hope weh nuh have no bottom.

Me: But hear mi good now, it nuh have nutten fi do wid yuh being no good man. Yuh have a heart weh generous bad, yuh check pon people when dem nuh even ask, an whoever God set fi yuh a go lucky bad. A jus di fit an di timing nuh deh deh fi wi two, an mi nuh see it a come roun eidah.

Me: Wi can still link up, still nyam a Sunday dinner togeda, still walk go a di shop, mi nah throw weh weh wi build.

Me: So walk good, my friend. Nutten spoil between wi.
