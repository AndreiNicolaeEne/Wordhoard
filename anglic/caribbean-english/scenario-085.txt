The first elder: Yuh si dis knee a mine? A no likkle ting mi a deal wid. From mi did a fifty-odd it start fi ache, an now a gone tirty-odd year mi deh wid it. Every marnin mi haffi get up guh rub it down before mi can waak to di gate, an is fi mi own fault mi tink, all di salt meat mi did nyam ova di years.

The second elder: Steups. Knee cyaan tell mi nuttn bout suffering. Every month when di weather change, tree, four night straight mi nuh get no rest, mi jus a toss pon di bed. Di pain deh right cross mi back like fire dem light under mi. If it nuh ease soon mi wahn haffi guh a doctor, an dat wi cost mi more dan a night sleep. Yuh knee tek weh yuh sleep? Mi back tek mine complete.

The third elder: Unu two a gwaan like unu jus discover pain. Mi did have dis chest ting since mi ben a small pickney, before either a unu did know mi face. Di doctor did tell mi seh a asthma, an back den dem never have di right medicine fi treat it right, so mi jus live wid it so till today. Sixty-odd year now, longer dan unu know one anodda.

The first elder: So a who out a di tree a wi really have it worse, eeh? Unu tell mi dat nuh.

The second elder: A fi mi win dat, fi sure. Mi night dem worse dan anybody knee.

The third elder: Unu nuh know weh unu a talk bout. Mi did have dis before either a unu did born, near about.

The first elder: Wait nuh, mi never seh mi did born before yuh! Unu keep on so, mi wahn lef unu two right yah fi talk to unu self.

All a dem bruk out inna one big laugh, nobody a give een, nobody a change dem mind, an di argument jus roll on so, same as it always do wen di tree a dem sit dung together.
