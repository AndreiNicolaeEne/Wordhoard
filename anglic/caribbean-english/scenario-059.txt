Light gone out inna the yard just after dusk, no warning, no nutten. One minute the television a play, next minute black out everything and the whole street gone quiet like it hold dem breath. We nuh know if a JPS cut it or if a the wire inna the back a the house dat mash up again, but light nuh come back and we affi deal wid it.

Everybody start scramble same time. The mother a call out seh, "Find the matches, dem deh inna the drawer!" The eldest a grope round the kitchen a knock over pot dem a look for the candle box, and true enough we find one lighter and a few match but only one candle stub left, likkle piece wax nuh longer than mi finger, the rest done use up long time and nobody buy back none.

So we affi share the one stub round the whole household, pass it hand to hand like a lickle warm coal. The youngest keep buck up pon the same chair leg inna the dark, same chair, over and over, till the eldest seh, "Mi tell yuh already, move di chair!" and everybody bruk out a laugh even though it nuh really funny.

Nobody nuh bother fix nutten dat night, we jus give it up, tek the little light we have and gather round the table, close up together, the candle a flicker in a saucer a wax. Grandmother start tell one old duppy story, and the pickney dem stop fret and start listen instead, eye big up inna the candlelight.

We siddung deh long time, talk and laugh soft, waiting fi see if light a come back, but it nuh come. One by one dem head start drop and we jus blow out the little stub and gone a bed inna the dark, feeling round for the bed frame, nuh mind it. Next morning light was deh waiting, like it never gone a foreday.
