Me: Aarait, unu come sidong close, leh me tell unu jus how it gwaan, straight, no lef out nating, cau unu fi know it too, jus like how mi know it.

Mi mi deh dis wid ih inna di last hour, jus mi wan wid ih, nobady els deh de yet. Fus ting ih seh noh bout no dying atall. Ih seh, "Bwai, dis fan noh di mek noise since maanin, unu neva fix it?" Jus so, easy, like any oda evening we di sidong.

Den ih come serious pan mi. Ih tell mi seh mek sure unu tek dong di lampi dem pan di line before di rain ketch dem, noh leave dem deh soaka wet. Clear, no mistaking, like ih been tinking pan dat ting good while.

Den ih call mi da likkle name ih always call mi, jus wan more time, same way since mi mi likkle pickney.

Den ih aks mi something mi neva expect. Ih aks mi if mi mi happy fi true. Mi tell ih di trute right deh, mi seh, "Yes, mi happy, ih mek mi."

Di las whole sentence ih manage fi seh was, "Unu look out fi one anodda, yaw, all a unu." Dat mi keep whole, neva change wan word since.

After dat was jus di quiet. Mi han deh inna ih han, mi hold on till ih drift, easy, like ih jus a go sleep.

Dat da everyting, straight, so unu can have it too, same way mi wahn have it told to mi.
