The traveler: Excuse mi, a wich part di market deh?
The stranger: Yuh see dat corner weh di likkle shop deh? Tun lef deh so.
The traveler: A right, and after dat?
The stranger: Yuh a guh straight till yuh reach a di next corner, weh di big tree deh, den yuh almost deh pon it.
The traveler: How long it tek fi walk it?
The stranger: Bout fifteen minute pon foot, if yuh nuh dawdle.
The traveler: Big up, mi soon reach. Tank yuh, eh.
The stranger: Walk good, mi friend.
