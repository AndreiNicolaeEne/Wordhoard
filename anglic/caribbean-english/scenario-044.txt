Been on this dock cracking conch since I small, and this morning I got the new hand next to me, showing 'e the first thing before anything else. I don't talk too much bout i, I just take up a shell, turn i belly up, and punch the knife through right by the third whorl, twist e out one time, clean. That da the spot, right there. I show 'e twice slow so 'e could watch.

Older hand: Alright, you try this one now.

'E take the shell, punch too high up where the shell thick and hard, and the whole thing bruk up in 'e hand, meat mash up with shell bits all in i.

Older hand: Nuh, bey, not up there so. Right by the turn, where I show you. Try the next one.

New hand: Wait, which turn you mean, the one by the lip?

Older hand: I just show you twice! Same spot I punch the first time. You wasn't even watching me do i.

'E go again, punch the same wrong place, worse this time, conch water running down 'e wrist and the shell all bruk up for nothing.

Older hand: Steups. How many conch we go waste this morning? You standing right up on me and you still punching wrong every time!

New hand: I sorry, I thought I had it, I was watching, I just...

Older hand: Alright, alright. Lemme show you a next way. Forget the knife first, you feel round wid you finger, find where soft. Don't punch till i give under you hand. Then and only then the knife go in.

'E feel round slow this time, find the soft part, punch clean, twist e out whole.

Older hand: There you go now! That's exactly i. See how i give when you find the right spot?

New hand: Yeah man, I feel i that time.
