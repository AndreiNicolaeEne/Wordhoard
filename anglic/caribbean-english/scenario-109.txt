Di gate did stick same way it did stick eight year ago, and mi a wrestle wid it wid one bag pon mi back and one inna mi hand when di front door fling open before mi even reach di step.

Di mother stand up deh so inna di doorway wid har hand cover har mout, like she see duppy. Har eye dem stretch wide, and fi a whole second nutten nuh come out har mout at all, like di sight lock up har voice.

The mother: ...is... a who dis? Lawd God, a really you?

Me: Is me, Mama. A really me.

The mother: JESUS AND MARY! IT'S HIM! IT'S HIM STAN UP DEH SO!

She bawl it out so loud di whole yard musse hear, and before mi coulda put down di bag proper she done fly out di door and grab mi up, squeeze mi so till mi couldn't get no air, and she nuh let go. Nuh let go one bit. Mi a pat har back and she a still hold on, rocking side to side like she a nuh believe mi flesh and blood real if she let go.

The mother: Mi baby. Mi baby come home. Eight year, and mi baby come home.

Me: Mama, mi cyaan breathe.

The mother: Yuh cyaan breathe? A eight year mi cyaan breathe! Hold still likkle more.

By di time she finally release mi, di eldest come charging out from inna di kitchen wiping har hand pon har frock, and she stop dead two step from mi, eye dem running mi up and down like she a inspect goods.

The eldest: Lawd have mercy. Look pon yuh. Look. Pon. Yuh.

Me: Wah do mi now.

The eldest: Yuh get big! Yuh did lef here like one lickle maaga bwoy and come back look like yuh a nyam gym! And beard? Since when yuh grow beard?

Me: A eight year, nuh. Man haffi change.

The eldest: Change fi true. Come yah mek mi hug yuh proper, mi nuh get nuff di first time.

Behind dem di aunt come out slower, one hand pon di doorframe, and har face already a crumple before she even reach mi. She a fan harself wid har other hand like dat a go stop di tears.

The aunt: Mi not crying. Steups. Mi have something inna mi eye, dat's all.

Me: Auntie, come here man.

The aunt: Mi seh mi not crying! ...Lawd, a really you. Look how yuh mek mi bawl inna mi own yard.

Everybody a talk one time now, one voice pon top di other, question a fire from every direction so mi couldn't even answer one before di next one lick mi.

The mother: Yuh nyam already? Yuh mus hungry, come nyam something. Yuh eat pon di plane?

The eldest: How long yuh deh here fah? Yuh going back Tuesday or wah?

The aunt: Yuh see how much yuh favor yuh father now? Mi neva see it before, but Lawd, it deh right deh inna yuh face.

The mother: Mi did tell unu him did coming, unu neva believe mi! Mi seh him deh pon di flight, and unu seh mi mad!

Right in di middle of all dat one likkle head did peep out from behind di eldest leg, one small girl-pickney wid har finger inna har mout, watching di whole commotion like she nuh sure if she fi run or stay.

The little one: Who dat man?

The eldest: Come, come si. Dis a fi wi... dis a family, love. Yuh nuh remember him, yuh did too small.

The little one: Him a shout too much.

Me: Mi nuh bite, yuh nuh haffi hide.

But she duck right back behind di leg anyway and just peep out one eye, nuh ready fi come out and face all a dat commotion yet, and nobody push her, dem just let her be, watching from far like she always do wid people she nuh know good yet.

Right about den mi start fi smell it, di thing mi been smelling since mi step out di taxi but didn't say nutten bout, and now it hit mi full force standing right deh inna di doorway.

Me: A curry goat mi smelling? Unu really cook curry goat today?

The mother: Course wi cook curry goat! Yuh think mi nuh know seh a today yuh coming? Mi been pon di stove since marning.

Me: Same smell. Same exact smell like when mi likkle. Nutten change bout dat one.

The mother: Some thing nuh haffi change. Now come inna di house, mek wi nuh stand up inna di gateway like beggar.

The bags did still deh right deh by di step, forgotten complete, nobody even look pon dem twice, cause di mother already a pull mi by mi hand toward di couch and di news start fi come out fast, one pon top di next, like she been saving up eight year worth and it all a spill out one time.

The mother: Yuh remember yuh cousin? Har weh live down by di corner? She married now, married two year now, gone married one nice man from Portmore. And yuh remember di little one you did carry pon yuh shoulder? Look pon her, she in high school now, top a har class!

The eldest: And yuh nephew, di one weh did just born when yuh lef? Him nearly big as yuh now, taller than mi already, growing like weed.

The aunt: And mi nuh know if yuh hear, but di family down by di old house dem, dem move gone country side, sell up di place and gone.

The mother: So much gone on since yuh lef, mi nuh even know weh fi start.

Me: Unu nuh give mi chance fi even look round di place yet.

The mother: Well look den! Wah yuh see?

Me: House small. Everything look smaller than how mi remember it, di ceiling, di kitchen, everything. But it still feel same. Same picture pon di wall, same crack up by di window.

The eldest: We keep meaning fi fix dat crack.

Me: Nuh fix it. Leave it deh.

The aunt: And how yuh find wi? We change much?

Me: Unu look di same to mi. Older, but di same. Only ting really change a har.

Mi point toward di little one still peeping out from behind di leg, and everybody laugh, and even she smile one little smile before she duck back out di sight again.

The eldest: So how long yuh here fah dis time? A month? Two week?

Me: Three week. Mi haffi go back after dat.

The mother: Three week nuh nuff! Mi did want longer.

Me: Is wah mi coulda get, Mama.

The mother: Well three week, wi ago mek every day count den. Sunday wi ago do a proper gathering, di whole family fi come round, everybody weh yuh nuh see yet.

The eldest: And nuh forget fi tell yuh uncle dem fi bring di sound system.

The mother: Course! Big gathering, big food, everybody welcome. Mi ago start plan it right now dis minute.

The aunt: Yuh still tek yuh tea wid condense milk, or dem foreign people change yuh?

Me: Yuh know mi cyaan change dat one, Auntie. Yuh know mi too well.

The aunt: Mi did know it! Same tea-drinking bwoy from before, some ting nuh change fi true!

And di aunt bust out laughing, and di mother laugh too, cause dat old joke been riding between dem two ever since mi did small, di tea-drinking bwoy, and nobody never haffi explain it to nobody, everybody already know weh it mean and why it funny, even di eldest laughing along like she remember it same way.

For a moment right after dat, everybody just gone quiet all at once, no question, no joke, nutten, just everybody standing round looking pon mi like dem still cyaan believe mi really deh deh, like if dem look too long mi might vanish back to weh mi come from. Even di little one peep out both eye dat time, watching quiet wid di rest a dem.

Then di mother clap har hand together and break it.

The mother: Well! Nuh mek di food get cold. Come nyam, everybody, come nyam, wi ago talk more over di table.

And just so, di whole house start move again, slower dis time, everybody settling back into demself, di aunt wiping har eye one last time, di eldest pulling out di chair dem, di mother already a fix up di plate dem, and di little one finally letting go di leg and coming to stand lickle closer, close enough fi look up pon mi proper for di first time.
