Marning time de water already deh at de door, but a nutten much, jus ankle deep, so me nuh mine i too tough. Me seh to meself, "de drain go tek i back once de sun come up good," and me gaan bout me business, slippers pon, foot cool in de lil water.

Bout an hour after me check back and de water rise up nuh joke, i deh higher than befo, so me start move t'ing off de floor straight away, de rice bag, de radio, de shoes dem, whatsoever me could lif me lif am up pon de bed and pon de table befo i tek everyt'ing.

Neighbour: "Yuh see how i a come up? Dis nah no normal flood dis."
Me: "A true wha yuh seh, mi a move up already, mi nah tek no chance."

By mid marning de water reach knee height and me a hice up everyt'ing pon top a everyt'ing, chair pon table, bag pon chair, anyt'ing stand highest me put am deh. De water nuh stop, i keep a come, keep a rise, till bout early evening, no, straight through till early afternoon i reach i highest, pass every single t'ing me did raise up, pass de table top self, right up to me knee where me stand pon de bed.

De whole afternoon me jus deh so, a sit up high, a listen to de water a lash 'gainst de wall, a knock and a suck like i vex wid de house. Me nuh do nutten but wait, de pickney dem quiet quiet, nuh bawl, jus a watch de water shine in de doorway. Steups, wha a way fu spend a day.

Bout four hour after i did peak me start notice de mark pon de wall a drop lil by lil, water a pull back slow slow, and by night i done reach back down to nutten but a damp floor.
