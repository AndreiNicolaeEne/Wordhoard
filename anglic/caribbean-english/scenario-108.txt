Sibling,

Mi nuh eevn waahn tell yu how lang dis lata di tek mi fi si dong an rait. Mi staat i wahn heap a taim now, get soh far as "Dyaa Sibling" an den put i dong an waak weh, bikaz wen mi tink bout evryting weh apn since yu lef, mi jos nuh noe wehpaat fi staat. Bot tonait di rain a fall pahn di zink an evryting kwaiyat, soh mi siddong wid di lamp an mi seh, aarait, tonait a di nait.

Let mi staat wid di haws, cuz yu wuda waahn no dat fos.

Yu memba how di back gate mi a lean pahn dat wan hinge fahn befo yu eevn lef gaan? Well i finally fix. Di man weh liv dong di road, ih kohn wid ih welda an ih straighten out di hole ting, put een a nyoo hinge weh nuh rattle no moh wen wind ketch i. Bot a tell yu, soon soon aata dat, di pipe anda di sink bos. Wi mi jos fix wan ting an anada ting mi tek ih chance fi go bad, dat da how i go eena dis haws, yu shuda memba dat kaina ting. Wi haffi kyaal a man fi kohn tek out di ole pipe an put in PVC, an now wen yu wash op, di sink nuh flood di flo no moh. An mi plaant wahn cashew chroo eena di kaana wehpaat di ole banana chroo mi ded, i lee bit already, tall pass mi wais.

Now di big ting, di ting weh chaynj since yu gaan. Di aunty, shi nuh liv dong bai di lagoon no moh. Shi muuv op dis said, klosa to town, bikaz di wok weh shi mi du, dem shet dong di ofis, an shi haffi fain wahn nyoo wan. Tek ar bout tu maant fi settle, bot now shi wok pahn di neks street ova, an shi kohn by wi haws pahn Sondeh moanin laik i nating.

Yu no wat mi memba tonait, sohn out a nowe? "Di chikin da mayor." Mi laff to miself rait ya soh, alone eena di haws, laik wan fool. Nobady eeva no wat mi di laff bout if dem si mi.

Now di neyba dem. Di wan family weh mi tell yu bout, dem gaan, muuv rait out, an dem lef di haws empti fu a good while til wahn nyoo family kohn tek i. Dem gat pikni dem, three a dem, an dem lee wan run roun di yaad hala laik ih di call fu evribady. An yu memba di wan weh yustu liv bihain wi, di wan wid di lee mongrel dog? Ih dawta jos gat wahn nyoo bebi, so now ih haws gat fo pikni eena i, ih ova full, bot dem hapi.

Two peple aalways aks bout yu. Di aunty da wan a dem, evri taim mi si ar shi seh:

Aunty: "Yu tak to yu sibling? Wa dem seh?"

An mi ohl wokmate, di wan weh yustu wok wid mi bak wen, ih ketch mi a di maakit las wik an ih aks mi di sayn ting, "Unu still tak?" laik unu di bruk op or nating.

Mi wahn confess wahn ting rait ya eena dis lata weh mi kyaahn seh out lowd to nobady face. Di adda die mi did a klin out di lee room, an mi kohn krak pan wan a yu ole shirt weh mi mi mi memba fi gi yu bak befo yu lef, an mi jos siddong pahn di flo an baal, plenti plenti, laik smadi ded. Nobady si mi. Mi wipe mi face an gwaan wid mi wok laik nating apn. Mi nu no if mi eeva tell yu dat eena wan fon kaal, so mi tell yu ya, eena raitin, wehpaat i safe fi seh.

Di seezn mi rof pahn wi dis yaa. Rainy taim kohn haad, di reeva rise ova di bang tuwaisz, an wi mi ha fi lif evryting out a di lee room fu tu wik til i go dong bak. Bot wi lucky, di big win weh dem mi di taak bout pahn di rediyo, i tun weh, nu tetch wi said. Sohn a wi neyba dem nu soh lucky, dem lose dem roof, bot da God mi luk out fu wi.

Now mi gat kwestyan fu yu, plenti a dem, cuz yu nu tell mi nating eena yu lata dem, jos "mi fain" evri taim laik dat a nof.

Ih kold dehso rait now? Yu gat waam clothes fi kova yu, o yu di shiba tru di nait?

Wa unu di nyam dehso? Yu di fain di rait pepa fi put pan yu fud, o evryting bland laik dem seh?

Yu wok stil di gud to yu? Dem pipl dehso trit yu rait, o dem mek yu wok laik dog?

An di ada ting mi waahn no, yu gat frenz fi taak to, o yu deh alone tumoch?

Aarait, mi haffi lef aff ya, bikaz di lamp a get lo an mi eye di tayad.

Mi go sen yu di hat sos yu aalways aks fu, di wan weh mi mek miself, wid di lee scotch bonit weh mi plaant bihain di haws. Mi seh dat evri taim an nu du i yet, bot dis taim mi meen i, mi go pak i op dis wik sef an sen i gaan wid smadi weh di chraval.

Rait mi bak wen yu ketch taim. Mi tink bout yu haad.
