Meh gon tell yuh de whole a it now, since yuh never deh dey fi see none a it wid yuh own eye, and is a story meh like fi tell anyhow, cause every time meh tell it meh laugh all over again.

Yuh remember how long meh did a talk bout learning fi swim and never doing nothing bout it? Well one year meh jus seh, nuh, dis time a nuh talk, a do. Every last person roun meh coulda swim since dem been lil pickney, even meh own younger cousin dem, and here it deh have meh, big woman/big man so, cyaan even put meh face een water widout panic tek meh. Meh nuh know wah mek meh wait so long, shame maybe, or jus fraid a de deep end, but dat year meh decide enough a dat, meh a go sign up fi lesson like everybody else.

First lesson, awoah, was hopeless straight through. De mind seh kick, de leg dem seh nuh, dem nuh listen to nutten. De instructor been a patient somebody but e nuh sugarcoat nutten neither, e watch meh flail bout een de shallow end and e come tell meh plain.

The instructor: Awright, meh gon be honest wid yuh, it gon tek yuh longer than it tek dem lil one over deh. De body harden up when yuh get older, it nuh tek to new tings so quick. But dat nuh mean yuh cyaan do it.

Meh nod like meh understand, but inside meh heart drop small.

And true to wah e seh, within a few day de pickney dem, half meh age or less, was already floating and kicking cross de pool while meh still deh a hold on to de side rail like it was meh mudda. Dat sting more than meh expect it to, watching lil ones weh nuh reach meh waist swim past meh easy easy while meh still deh a choke up pool water. Steups. Meh nuh gon lie, dat first week was a humbling ting.

De whole first week meh body ache een place meh forget coulda ache at all. Muscle weh meh nuh use in years start mek demself known every time meh move, every time meh sit down, every time meh try fi get up off a chair. Meh walk bout like old, old somebody, stiff foot and all, and everybody roun meh did wan know why meh a walk so.

Den de fall come, and dis one meh nuh forget till dis day. Meh did try fi push off de wall proper de way de instructor show meh, and instead meh miss de timing complete and gone down flat pon de water, belly first, hard. De air gone clean out a meh, jus so, meh couldn't even bawl out, meh jus deh a float dey a minute like somebody drop meh from a height. De whole pool gone quiet fi a second, den people start ask if meh awright, and meh lay dey een de water, breath gone, certain een dat exact moment dat dis whole ting was nuh fi meh, dat meh done, dat meh a go home and nuh come back.

Meh tell people so too, straight out, meh seh meh done wid it. But meh cousin, weh been dey watching dat day, come talk to meh after.

My cousin: Yuh cyaan stop pon one bad fall so. Everybody weh ever learn fi swim get one lick like dat. Come back one more time, jus one, and if yuh still wan stop after dat, awright, stop.

Meh grumble and steups but meh went back. And a good ting too, cause if meh did leave it right dey, meh would never know wah come after.

De progress after dat was slow, and plenty time humiliating, meh nah go lie bout dat part. One small ting gotten right after weeks a getting it wrong, and de whole time a lil crowd a onlooker deh pon de pool side watching, some a dem cheering meh on, one or two clearly amuse by de whole spectacle a big somebody struggling wid wah lil pickney do easy. Meh hear de giggling. Meh nuh blame dem, if meh did see somebody else een meh position meh probably woulda laugh too.

De only ting weh ever help meh through it was fi stop comparing meh self to dem quick learner dem and start comparing meh self to how meh been a month back. Cause if yuh put yuhself side by side wid somebody weh pick up de ting een a afternoon, yuh gon give up before yuh reach anywhere. But if yuh look back one month and see say yuh coulda float now weh yuh couldn't float before, dat different altogether. Dat de only measure weh mek sense.

Round de fourth month, someting click. Meh cyaan even explain it good een word, it was more a feeling een de body than anyting meh mind understand first. Meh push off de wall one regular evening, same as every other evening, and sudden so de water jus start hold meh up, de arm dem start move on dem own widout meh haffi think bout every stroke, and meh realize meh been swimming a good lil stretch before meh even notice it. Meh stop een de middle a de pool, shock, and meh start laugh out loud right dey by meh self. De ting weh been impossible dis whole time jus turn difficult, and difficult meh coulda work wid.

Meh never did catch up to de fast learner dem, not by de end, not ever. Dem still swim circle roun meh even now. But by de time de whole ting done, meh mek peace wid dat, cause meh proud a wah meh coulda do weh meh couldn't do at all when meh start out, and dat proud feeling nuh need nobody else fi measure up to fi mean someting.

And now, when meh study pon de whole season, meh nuh study it sour at all, meh study it fond. De fall, de sore muscle, de giggling pon de pool side, all a it fold up together een someting meh glad meh gone through. If yuh did tell meh dat first week, when meh deh a lay een de water wid de breath knock clean out a meh, dat one day meh woulda look back pon de whole ting and smile, meh woulda tell yuh seh yuh mad. But here meh deh, telling yuh de story wid a big grin pon meh face, and dat, more than de swimming itself, a wah meh really glad fi.
