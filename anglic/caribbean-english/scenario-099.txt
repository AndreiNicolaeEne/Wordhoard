Me: Eh-eh, but who dis! Ent a yuh dis, nuh, standing up by di stelling like yuh a wait pun boat!

The classmate: (steups) Yuh nuh see mi a wait pun mi sista, she seh she a come but she a come since one hour now. Look pun yuh nuh, time nuh do yuh nutten at all. Yuh still favour how yuh did favour long time.

Me: Ent so! Is mi a look pun yuh a seh di same ting. How di family dem stay? Yuh mother still deh a di same house side di canal?

The classmate: Yes man, she deh dey, cyaan move she from dey even if house a sink. Wha bout yuhself, yuh still deh a wuk wid di same company?

Me: Nuh man, mi move on since two, three year now. Different wuk altogether, but a nuh dat mi wan gaff bout. Look how long since wi see one anodda. Since when, since school time?

The classmate: Since school, yes. Long long time. Mi ent even memba di last time wi did talk face to face like dis.

Me: Yuh remember school days doah? Mi been thinking bout dem lately, cyaan seh why.

The classmate: Yuh mean when wi did deh a di primary or when wi guh up a di secondary?

Me: Both, but is di secondary mi a talk bout. Yuh remember di teacher dem use fi call "Sir"? Di one wid di stick?

The classmate: (long steups) Aye, mi cyaan even seh di name out loud without mi belly a turn. Dat man was a terror, a straight up terror.

Me: E was terror fi true. Yuh remember di rule? If yuh come een pun e class late, even by one minute, yuh get lick, no question, no explaining. E nuh care if bus never come, if rain a fall down, if yuh mudda sick. Late a late, and late get lick.

The classmate: And if yuh talk out a turn, forget bout it. E used fi mek yuh write out one line, one hundred time, every single time, wid no exception. "I must not speak when I am not spoken to." One hundred time, and if e ketch a smudge or e seh di handwriting messy, yuh haffi start over from one.

Me: Mi remember e used fi stand up in front di whole class and seh, "In this room, discipline is not a punishment, it is a mercy I am extending to you." E use fi seh dat exact ting, almost every week, like a chant. Wi use fi mock it behind e back after, seh it out to one anodda in e voice.

The classmate: (laughing) Yes! And e had dat ting e do wid e glasses, tek dem off and clean dem real slow before e call pun anybody, like e a build up di suspense. Wi use fi watch fi dat and steups low so e nuh hear.

Me: Mi remember one time, yuh was dey too, mi sure, when e mek one boy stand up front di whole class and write line pun di board because di boy nuh do e homework, and di boy hand start fi shake so bad di chalk drop out e hand twice. Whole class quiet like grave, nobody move, nobody laugh, because if yuh laugh e come fi yuh next.

The classmate: Mi remember dat. Mi remember mi own self used fi freeze up whenever e call pun mi name fi answer someting. Even if mi know di answer good good, mi mind go blank soon as e seh mi name. Up till now mi cyaan explain it, e voice alone did paralyze wi.

Me: Same wid mi. Even now if somebody call pun mi sudden so in a meeting mi heart jump first before mi tink. Dat man leave e mark pun wi, seh what yuh wan seh.

The classmate: But yuh remember di one time, mi still cyaan believe dis one till dis day, when one gyal pon we class talk back to e? Straight up talk back, in front di whole class!

Me: Oh gosh yes! Tell mi if mi remember it right. E did seh someting bout she work being sloppy, and she stand up and seh, "Sir, wid all due respect, you never even read it properly before you said that." And di whole class hold dem breath, waiting fi e explode.

The classmate: And e never explode! E just stand up dey, look pun she long time, and den e pick up di book and actually read it through, right dey in front a wi, and den e seh, "You are right. I apologize." Sir! Apologize! Wi never see notten like dat before or since.

Me: To dis day mi nuh know how she get away wid it. Mi tink bout it sometime and mi still shake mi head. Any one a wi try dat, wi woulda dead pon di spot.

The classmate: She was braver dan all a wi combine.

Me: Yuh remember di kind side though? Because e did have one, even doah e hide it good.

The classmate: Yuh talking bout when di boy modda pass and e neva seh notten to di boy fi weeks?

Me: No, mi mean di time when one gyal come to school widout lunch money, and e never say notten in front di class, but after school e call she aside quiet so and gi she someting, and e tell she seh, "Don't let this happen again, and don't tell the others." Nobody know until she tell wi years after.

The classmate: Mi ent know dat one. But it soun like e, in a way. E was hard, but e never was wicked.

Me: No, e was never wicked. Strict to di bone, but nuh wicked.

The classmate: Yuh ever hear wha become a e? Mi hear tell e retire long time and gone somewhere up di coast, but mi nuh know if dat true.

Me: Somebody tell mi e move to live wid e daughter, somewhere abroad, Canada or someplace cold so. But mi nuh sure at all, is just wha mi hear pass mout to mout. Nobody really know fi certain.

The classmate: Whatever e deh, mi hope e alright. Because looking back pun it now, is nuh fear mi feel when mi tink pun e, is more like respect. Real respect.

Me: True wud. Mi did fraid di man wid mi whole heart back den, but now, looking back, e mek wi better dan wi woulda be otherwise. Wi laugh bout it now, but back den nobody was laughing.

The classmate: (laughing hard) No sah, nobody was laughing! Mi still get a lickle chill up mi spine even now when mi tink bout e voice seh mi name. Straight true. Even now.

Me: Same yah, same yah. Old fear nuh easy fi shake off, even when yuh know it done long time.

The classmate: But enough bout school, tell mi wha gwan wid yuh life now. Yuh still deh single or wha happen?

Me: (steups) Doah start wid dat one. Mi deh wid somebody bout two years now, tings going good. Wha bout yuh, mi hear yuh guh England fi work?

The classmate: Yes man, mi did deh dey bout three years, but mi come back home last year. England cold too bad, and mi miss di food, mi miss wi people. Yuh cyaan get real cook-up rice over dey, dem tings come out a can and taste like paper.

Me: (laughing) Yuh mad fi true. But mi glad yuh back. Wi haffi lime one time properly, catch up good, nuh just standing up by di road so.

The classmate: True dat. Gi mi yuh number nuh, mi go call yuh, wi go sit down proper, drink someting cool, gaff till late.

Me: A deal. Wi go plan someting soon, doah keep saying "soon" and let a next ten years pass before wi see back one anodda.

The classmate: (laughing) Nuh worry, dis time wi go mek it happen fi true.
