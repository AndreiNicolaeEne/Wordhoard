Well is dat night I stay out by de lime longer than I say I go, and by de time I leave, dark done fall proper, not de dark you does see wid streetlight all de way, real dark, de kind dat make yuh own foot sound loud in yuh ears.

I ent walk far when I start feel it, footstep landing right behind mine, like dey following de same beat as me. I doh want to look back straight so, so I try test it quiet quiet. First ting I do, I slow up, like I lookin for someting in my bag, and de step behind slow up de same time. Den I pick up de pace little bit, casual so, like I just in a hurry to reach, and dat step pick up too, same distance like it tie to mine wid string.

Is right dere I decide, doh go by de shortcut behind de church tonight, dat lil dark track wid no light and only two house on it. I cut instead by de main road, where de shop still open and de lime still going by de corner, plenty people out, plenty light.

I take one glance back, just one, quick so, and I ent see nutten certain, just shadow and de road bend where anybody coulda be walking dey own way home same as me. Dat tell me nutten and everyting same time.

Last stretch home I walking fast fast, faster than I woulda ever admit to unu dat night, and when I reach and push dat door shut and hear de lock catch, Lord, de relief.

To dis day I doh know if it was somebody real following me or just my own fear walking behind me. I still tell de story though, and every time I reach de part where I glance back, I have to steups at myself, how sure I was dat night, sure like I coulda swear to it in court.
