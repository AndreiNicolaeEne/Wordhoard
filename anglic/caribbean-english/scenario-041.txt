Two years and some odd months, dat is how long I was in dat office before I finally get up de courage to walk out. Two years, girl. And is only now, sitting down wit allyuh, dat I go tell de whole ting straight, because de day I tell it in dat office I never tell dem de real story at all, I tell dem de nice one.

It never was one big blow-up dat send meh, yuh know. Nothing dramatic like dat. Was more like a long line of small tings, one riding on top de next, until de line reach a certain length and it just done, it cyah stretch no further. De final straw itself was so small it almost shame meh to say it out loud, was a next meeting call for a Friday evening, five-thirty, after I done tell dem already I have to reach and pick up de little one from lessons. Dat's it. Dat's what break it. Not de pay dat was staying de same for two years running, not de overtime dem does forget to pay out, not de way certain people in dat place does get praise for de exact same work I doing quiet in de back. All dat did build up long time already. De Friday meeting was just de last drop dat make de cup run over.

So before I even go and say a word to anybody, I rehearse it. Lord, I rehearse dat speech like I preparing for exam. In de car, in de shower, walking to de bus stop, I saying it over and over in meh head, smoothing out every corner so nothing come out rough. I want it to sound calm. Professional. Not like somebody vex, even doh I was vex, deep down I was real real vex. I want it to sound like a decision, not like a complaint.

Monday morning I reach in early and I ask if I could get a word wit de manager. De manager say come, sit, and I sit down across de desk and I say de line I did practise so much it barely sound like meh own voice no more:

Me: It's not really about any one thing in particular. I've just been thinking a lot, and I feel like it's time for me to move on.

Dat was de opening. Careful. Diplomatic. Not one raised word in it. And is true it wasn't about one thing, it was about forty things, but I say it dat way because I never want to stand up in dat office and read out de whole list, de meeting on de Friday, de pay dat never move, de praise dat never come, all of it, one by one, in front of somebody I still have to see every day for two more weeks. I hold back because I never want to give dem de satisfaction of watching meh face while I say de real ting. I never want to cry in front of nobody either, and I know if I start listing it out proper, I woulda cry.

De manager surprise, or acting surprise, I couldn't tell which one true.

The manager: Wow. I really didn't see this coming. Is there something we can do? We could look at your salary, or if it's the hours, we could talk about adjusting your schedule.

And I sit down there and steups inside meh head, not out loud, doh, I hold dat one back too, because two years I was asking quiet quiet for dem tings and nobody move, and now, de morning I say I leaving, all a sudden it have money to find and schedule to change. Funny how dat does work. But I only tell de manager:

Me: I appreciate that, I really do, but I think this is just the right time for me personally. It's not about the money.

Which, again, wasn't de full truth, but it was easier to say than "allyuh coulda find dis money two years now and allyuh choose not to."

We agree on two weeks. Standard. Proper. I even feel a certain pride saying it, like I doing everything right, giving dem time, not leaving dem stranded.

But dat same night, home, in meh bed, I get a lil doubt catch meh. A real cold one. What if I make a mistake. What if two years of steady, even if it wasn't much, was better than whatever coming next. I lie down there staring up at de ceiling fan going round and round and I asking meheself if I was too hasty, if de Friday meeting really was worth all dis, or if I just tired and confusing tired for sure.

De two weeks pass fast. Fast fast. And den it was de last day.

De goodbyes was mostly what yuh expect. People saying "we go miss yuh eh", "keep in touch", de standard tings people does say when dey doh really mean it but dey doh want to seem hard-hearted either. Somebody bring a small cake. Somebody sign a card everybody sign without really writing nothing personal in it, just "good luck!" and a smiley face. It was polite. It was hollow. Everybody smiling and nobody saying de real ting, which was dat plenty of us in dat office was tired de same way, just some of us find de courage to walk and some ain't reach dat point yet.

But it had one moment, right at de end, when I was collecting up meh few tings from de desk, dat come close to honest. One of meh workmate, de one I sit next to for de longest of dem two years, come by and she doh say "we go miss yuh" like de rest. She just look at meh straight and she say:

The workmate: Girl, I real happy for yuh. Somebody had to go first.

And I nearly, nearly, tell she everything right dere. I nearly tell she about de Friday meeting and de two years of asking quiet and de pay dat never move and how it wasn't really about no one thing but was about all ah dem things together. It was right dere on meh tongue. But instead I just laugh a small laugh and I say "yeah man, allyuh go be alright" and I pick up meh bag and I walk out.

Since dat time, and it have a good few months now. I could say straight up, is relief winning out over any regret, and it winning by a long way. It have days I does think back on de people, de ones I did actually like, and I miss dat small piece of it. But de rest, de meetings, de way I used to feel meh stomach tight up every Sunday night thinking bout Monday, none ah dat I missing at all.

If I was to sit down have dat same conversation again, knowing everything I know now, I woulda say de real ting. I woulda tell de manager straight, no polish, no rehearsing it smooth in de shower first. I woulda say two years I ask allyuh quiet for what I was worth and allyuh only find it de day I was leaving anyway. But I go be honest wit allyuh too. I not vex I do it de diplomatic way. Sometimes de smooth version is what does let yuh walk out wit yuh head straight up, and dat mattered to meh more than saying de sharp ting outright. Still, if it come round again, I think I woulda find a way to say a lil more of de truth, and hold back a lil less.
