Me: Yuh see when him come een though? Mi neva reckernize him at all, at all. Him used to stay so mawga back a school, an now him jus a big man wid a belly an everyting.

My friend: Mi see him! Mi seh to miself, "a fi him dat?" But den mi look pon him eye dem an mi seh yes, a fi him dat, jus a whole heap bigger.

The cousin: Wait nuh, a who unu a talk bout now?

Me: The one weh used to sidung a back a di class, always a draw pon him book. Him change up so till mi couldn't call him name outta mi mouth.

My friend: An den yuh have the other one, same so from long time, nuh change one bit. Same voice, same laugh weh yuh can hear from outside di gate before yuh even si har.

Me: Yes! Har deh right deh a di same style, straight back, still a tell di same joke dem. Nutten nuh move pon har.

The cousin: A true? Weh 'bout the one dem used to seh nah go be nutten, him show?

My friend: No man, him nuh come. Him tell we seh him woman sick an him haffi stay a hospital wid har.

Me: But guess wa wi hear bout the one everybody used to laugh after though. Him gaan a foreign, an him a run him own business now, big time, pickney dem big awready an one a go university.

The cousin: Get out!

My friend: Mi tell yuh! Nobody nuh believe it till dem show wi di picture pon di phone.

Me: True thing. It mek yuh tink bout yuhself too. Wi did jus stand up a look pon everybody an mi seh, "wi change?"

My friend: Steups. Not so much still. Yuh still a gwaan the exact same way, a talk the same foolishness yuh always did talk.

Me: Same to yuh, yuh know! But fi real doah, mi tink wi favour how wi did stay, jus a likkle more grey inna wi head an less energy fi lime late.

My friend: True dat. But wi affi do this again, nuh mek it tek so long like last time.

Me: Yes man, next year same time, mi a hol yuh to it.
