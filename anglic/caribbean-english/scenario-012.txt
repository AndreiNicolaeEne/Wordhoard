The eldest: "Go up nah, all the way this time, no backing out again."

Me: "I going, watch meh, I going higher than allyuh even."

Somebody: "Talk is cheap, climb it and see."

The eldest: "Doh tell nobody 'bout the tree, doh tell nobody, we go get in real trouble."
