Gyal, mek me tell yuh de whole account, nuh hold back nutten.

A de school book money weh start it, mek no mistake. School jus a open back and me tek out a piece a de likkle savings we ben a save up fi de light bill, fi buy de pickney dem book and dem uniform, cause de teacher seh she nuh gon tek dem een class widout it. Me tink me go pay back de light money after me get pay, but de light man come cut we off before dat. A him bring it up first, nuh me. He come home from wuk, see de candle a bun eena de front room, an he start straight, 'Wus happen to de light money, wey it deh.'

Me tell him seh de pickney dem book more important dan light dis week, an dat a wen he drag back de ol' foolishness bout how me ben gi money to dat box-hand woman years back an she run gone wid all we contribution, how me "still cyaan manage money todeh same as den." Dat had nutten to do wid dis one, but he pull it out anyhow.

De sharpest ting, a him seh it: 'Yuh mout run faster dan yuh sense, same way like befo.' Me nuh answer him back, me just steups long an loud and walk go a de bedroom.

We nuh talk fi bout two whole day, sis. We nuh really nyam good needa, everybody vex een dey stomach. De pickney dem carry message back an fourth, "Daddy seh..." "Mommy seh..."

Third day he come home wid a likkle piece a fish, put it eena de fridge widout a word, den he seh soft, 'Me gon pay de light tomorrow, doan trouble yuhself.' Dat a fi him way fi seh sorry, an me tek it so.

Me verdict, sis? De light bill was never de real quarrel. A de ol' box-hand shame he still a carry roun, an he tek it out pun me. Money short, but he nuh have no right fi drag dat up again.
