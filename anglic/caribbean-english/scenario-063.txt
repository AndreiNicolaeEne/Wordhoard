Mek Ah tell yu how dis fence bizniss run, cause fi tell di trut, it neva easy from di jump, an yu jus a sidong deh a lisin mi, no seh notn, so Ah wahn tell yu di hoal ting front to back.

Dat night di wind mi rough, yu memba, di kine a wind weh mek di zinc dem pahn di roof a chatta all night an yu cyaahn sleep fi notn. Ah mi deh a mi bed a lisin how di trees dem a swing an a tel miself, "well, tomorrow gwehn av wahn mess fi clean." An true to dat, when day clean an Ah gaan outside wid mi cup a tea, deh di fence, flat dong pahn di grong, wan whole section a it mash op onda wan big branch. Ah stan op deh pahn di back step an Ah jus look, cause fi tell yu straight, Ah neva expect notn dat bad.

So Ah waak roun fi si which tree da di one weh drop, an dat wehn di question staat. Cause di tree weh fall, ih mi deh right pahn di line, ih root dem grow up unda both yaad, but di trunk, di trunk mi lean more into fi mi neighba yaad, so evrybody da di street know seh da fi ih tree. Ih deh deh fahn befo eeda a wi mi buy wi haus, big ole tree, an ih branch dem mi di hang over pahn fi mi said good an proper, so wen ih come dong ih neva jus lik di fence, ih tek out a piece a fi mi clothesline post an ih.

So Ah gaan knock pahn di neighba door dat mawnin, an dis da how di fos conversation gone.

Me: Marnin. Yu si weh di wind du wi las night?

The neighbor: Ya, man, dat mi wahn rough wan. Ah yeri di crash but Ah neva know seh a fi mi tree dat.

Me: Well ih fall right cross di fence, tek out a good piece a it. We gwehn need fi split di cost fi fix it back.

The neighbor: Bwai, a jus di wind du dat, yu no can control weh di wind du. Dat noh so seh a mi fault.

Ah stan up deh an Ah feel di heat a come op inna mi chest, cause dat answer neva sit right wid mi at all. Cause dis tree neva jus fall outta clear sky, unu. Ih mi di lean fi wahn long time, mi husban did tell ih bout it good wan year befo, seh di tree a lean tumuch pahn fi wi said an ih need fi trim it back or prop it, an di neighba jus mi seh "aa, ih fine, ih deh deh long time, notn no gwehn happen to it." So wen Ah bring dat op...

Me: But dat tree been a lean fi a long time now. Mi husban tell yu bout it, memba, seh yu need fi cut back di branch dem befo wan bad wind come an tek it dong. Yu neva du notn bout it.

The neighbor: Ah memba ih seh someting, but dat noh mean nobody mi noh gwehn happen. Tree deh a lean fi years, dat noh mean seh ih gwehn drop.

An so we stan deh, both a wi wid wi han pahn wi hip, di sun a get hat, an neitha wan a wi mi waahn back dong fos. Ah mi feel seh, well, a fi ih tree, so a fi ih responsibility, an ih mi feel seh a jus wedda an ih noh gwehn pay full price fi someting God sen. We gone bak an foat like dat fi bout a week, sometime we no eevn talk, jus wahn nod pahn di street an gaan wi way, an mi one time steups an tun mi head wen Ah pass ih gate, cause Ah mi vex bad. Di broken fence jus deh deh di whole time, board dem scatta pahn di grong, an evribady pahn di street cyan si wi bizniss like it deh pahn display.

Da wahn old-time neighba, wan man weh live pahn di corna longa dan eeda wan a wi been pahn dat street, ih da di one weh come sort it out, an nobady neva ask ih fi come.

The elder neighbor: Awrite, awrite, mek Ah tell unu someting. Ah been pahn dis street since befo eeda a unu build. Ah si how tings run. Now, di tree, ih grow pahn di line, so tell mi someting, weh side a di root dem tek moas a fi wi rain wata offa unu roof? Cause dat tree been helpin cool bot yaad dis whole time, ih neva jus wan man tree.

The neighbor: Well dat true, ih shade did reach ova pahn ih said tu.

The elder neighbor: So mek unu split it clean een di middle, no fi cause a fault, jus cause a tree weh serve bot a unu now gone an tek out a fence weh serve bot a unu tu. Nobady lose face, notn no seh nobady wrong, a jus wan tree bizniss between neighba, an neighba fix tings tugeda.

Ih seh it so plain, so kine, seh nobady mi cyan get vex bout it no moa. Ah look pahn mi neighba an ih look pahn mi, an bot a wi jus seh "awrite" mos di same time, an dat mi di en a di whole standoff, jus so, wan ole man weh tek time fi come talk sense eena wi bizniss weh neva mi eevn ih own.

So wen di money side settle, di real wok mi staat, an dat mi a hoal nadda ting. Fos couple day, mi husban an di neighba wuk side by side pahn di fence, but Lawd, ih mi stiff bad. Nobody neva seh much, jus "pass mi di hama" an "hold dis ya post straight," notn moa. Yu cud yeri di hammer knock an di saw a cut branch an dat da all weh mi deh pahn dat yaad, no laugh, no story, jus wok.

But den, roun midday, di neighba wife kohn out wid a plate a food, rice an beans an stew chicken, an ih neva even ask if enybady mi hungry, ih jus set it dong pahn di bench an seh, "unu betta nyam befo unu drop." An dat lickle ting, Ah tell yu, dat mi di one weh crack it open. Cause afta dat, mi husban start pass di tool dem to di neighba wen ih si ih need it, no waatin fi ask, an bai evenin dem mi a joke an laugh like nutten neva happen atall. Wan a dem eevn call fi wan pickni fi run fetch nara cole drink fahn di shap.

By di time we finish, di fence weh stand up deh stronga dan di one weh mash dong, cause dis time bot a wi mi put een good post, treated wood, an we mi dig di hole dem deeper tu, cause we neva waahn go through dat again.

An now, well, tings deh good between di two yaad, betta dan how it mi deh befo eeven. Di pickney dem run back an foat cross dat fence like nutten eva separate dem, an sometime een di evenin unu si di two a wi jus a sidong pahn wi own step, but a talk cross di fence like famili. So no, a noh jus back to how it mi deh, it deh betta, an all a dat fahn wan tree weh Ah still tink bout evritime a good wind blow.
