The cousin: Bey, wha happenin, I hear seh you ain't well. How bad it really is, tell me straight nuh.

Me: It ain't too bad, but it ain't good neither. Free day now since it start, first was a ache in me joint dem and me throat scratchy scratchy. Then yesterday a fever come right on top a that.

The cousin: Lawd have mercy. And how you deh today?

Me: Today a lickle easier than yesterday, thank God, but I still far from right, ya. Body still heavy heavy.

The cousin: Wha you been doin fuh it?

Me: First ting, I does gargle some warm salt water fuh the throat, few time a day. Fever nuh wan break off by itself so I lay a cool cloth cross me forehead and keep changin it right through the night, every time it get warm.

The cousin: And you eatin sumn at all?

Me: Just broth and weak tea, nuttin heavy pon me stomach. Sleepin whenever me body let me sleep, and that ain't much some hour.

The cousin: Well you best rest proper then, don't push through it, ya hear? Doh be tryin fi go do nuttin round the house.

Me: I hear you. I set me own line already, if the fever climb any higher, or me breathin start get hard, I gon go get seen bout straight, no question. But before it reach that, I stayin right here.

The cousin: I could bring some soup come by, or sit wit you a hour, keep you lickle company.

Me: Steups... yinna worry too much. You ain't have to do all that, but if you passin by anyway, I ain't go turn it down neither.

The cousin: Doh let it turn into sip-sip round the yard now, everybody askin me how you stay. Rest, and call me if it get worse, hear.

Me: I hear you, cousin. I gon call.
