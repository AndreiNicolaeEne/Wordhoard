Bwoy yuh really miss it, yuh know. Mek meh tell yuh how di whole ting gone down, from top to bottom, cause is a night wi ago talk bout fi a long time.

So is four a wi did deh round di table, meh, di cousin, di neighbour man from next door, and di young one wha does always come round when e hear cards a rattle. Wi siddung, agree di stakes first before one card even lef di deck, small small money, nutten wha go mek nobody vex if e lose. Everybody put in wha wi agree and wi start.

First few hand dem did just so-so. Nobody clearly ahead, di luck a jump bout, one hand meh win small, next hand di young one tek it, then di cousin get one. Normal ting, how card does go. Wi a nyam wi channa and drink wi ting, a mek noise, a joke, nutten serious a gwan.

Then di neighbour man luck start to turn. E win one hand, awright, dat can happen. E win a next one, hmm, a get a likkle lucky. But e nuh stop deh, yuh know. Four hand straight e tek, one after di next, and by di fourth one meh mind start work. Cause when yuh a play long enough yuh know how luck does move, e does come and e does go, e nuh does hold so steady so long widout something.

Di cousin, e a watch di whole time and e mek a likkle remark, e seh, "Bredrin, dat shuffle look real clean, yes." E seh it soft soft, half joke half not. Nobody really tek it up, wi jus laugh and gone on wid di game. Di neighbour man neva even answer, jus deal out di next hand like e neva hear.

But when it come round to e turn to deal again, dat a when it change. Meh a watch di cards a lef di bottom a di deck, and something bout how dem a come off nuh sit right wid meh eye, too smooth, too matched up, like e know wha coming before e even reach yuh. And dis time nobody stay quiet bout it. Di cousin call it right out, straight, no beating bush. E point e finger and seh, "Yuh a deal off di bottom. Meh see it wid meh own eye, is you."

And di answer wha come back? Flat. No give in it at all. Di neighbour man seh, "Meh nuh cheat nobody a dis table, yuh can seh wha yuh want." Cold so, like e neva even blink.

Well dat a when di whole table split up. Di young one side wid di cousin, seh e see di same ting, di cards did look funny fi a while now. But meh, meh neva see nutten clear enough fi swear pon it, so meh did deh more pon di neighbour man side, seh yuh cyaan accuse man off a look. Everybody start go back over di hands wha done already play, a argue who bin win wha and how, a try work out if di earlier hands did rig too. But how yuh ago prove dat now? Cards already gone back in di deck, mix up wid di rest. Wi argue and argue and it settle absolutely nutten. Nobody change dem mind, nobody back down.

Still, wi keep playing, cause wha else yuh ago do, everybody already put in dem money and nobody want lef it deh so unfinish. But di ease wha did deh pon di table before, dat gone. Every deal after dat, everybody eye pon di neighbour man hand, watching how e a shuffle, how e a cut, how di cards a lef e finger. Nobody a laugh so free again. Even di young one, wha usually a mek noise di whole night, get quiet.

Wi play out di rest a di hands so, tense so, right til di last one. And is di cousin wha tek di final hand, funny enough, after all dat pointing finger, like e did determine fi show e still could win widout no accusation pon e own name.

When wi tally up di whole night, eleven hand total wi did play. And when everything done count, is di neighbour man, di one dem accuse, wha end up two hand ahead a everybody else. Two clear. Wha yuh mek a dat.

So dat a how wi lef it. Nobody settle nutten dat night, and nobody settle nutten since. Every time dat same four a wi siddung fi play again, and wi still does, cause wi is friend before wi is card partner, somebody go bring up dat night. "Remember dat shuffle." "Remember how e deal off di bottom dat time." And di neighbour man go steups, long and slow, and seh e tired tell allyuh e nuh do nutten. Di cousin go seh e see wha e see wid e own two eye. And round and round it go again, same argument, same two side, settling nutten, jus like dat first night. But wi still deh a di table every time, cause dat a how it stay wid we, di argument nuh dead, e jus does siddung deh quiet til di next hand start.
