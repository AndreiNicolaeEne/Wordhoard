Mi remember dat night good, good. Bout evenin time di pain start, likkle twinge yah an dere, an mi seh to miself, "a nutn dat, a just gas or someting." Mi wife tek een her likkle pain, walk bout di house, seh she alright, no big ting. But by di time night settle in proper, mi si seh dis a nuh no ordinary pain, she a bawl now, holdin pon di doorframe, an mi know seh a time. A fourteen hour it tek in all, from dat evenin straight to nex mawnin.

Wi did sen di big one go stay by her aunty already, cause wi did know it soon come, so a jus di two a wi in di house dat night, waitin an pacin, mi a hold her han, she a walk up an down, up an down, di pain a come an go, a come an go, no rest een between.

Wen it pass midnight, ting get rough fi true. She steups an seh she cyaan tek it nuh more, an mi did fraid bad, mi tell yuh di trute. A dat time mi run go call fi help, an di midwife reach.

The midwife: "Alright, hold her han, no leave her side. Everyting a guh come out fine, jus breathe wid her."

Mi stay right deh, no move. Di pushin part long, long, mi neva know pushin coulda tek so, an den, nutn. Bout five, six second, silence. Mi heart neva beat so hard in mi life, mi jus a wait pon dat cry.

Den di cry come.

The midwife: "Ya see, a bawl she a bawl, everyting alright now!"

Wid a minute mi did a hold di pickney, warm eena mi arm, an mi jus stan up deh a look pon her likkle face, an mi neva know a person coulda so full up like dat. Mi second pickney. Mi jus tell har, soft soft, "Yuh reach safe now, yuh reach."
