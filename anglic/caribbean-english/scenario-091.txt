Aarite, mek a tel yu how di ting gone, from di very stat, coz a know yu been deh a fone waitin fi ere it propa, an yu noh mi deh yah fi si non a it fi yuself.

Soon as di news lef di house, it did gaan roun tong fasa dan enibadi coulda run. A tel wan neighba, an befo a eeven don taak, dat neighba aready tel three moa. By evenin di whole street mi know seh di granmada gaan. Wi neva get no real res fi di whole two day afta dat, evribadi mi a scramble - some a organize di choch paat, some a kuk, some jos a stan op a bawl an cyaahn du notn els. Yu know how it stay wen it da fi wi own like dat, di time noh wait fi nobady, an evritin haffi ready eena two day flat.

Di relative weh liv fah, di wan wi mi tink might noh mek it - ih travel a hole day, chienj bus twice, an ih reach jos as di house mi a fill op good. Barely time fi put dong dehn bag befo pipl mi a poor een fram evriwe. Ih neva eeven chienj out a dehn travel clothes propa, jos wash dehn face an kohn stand op wid di res a wi like ih neva lef atall.

Bwai, by early mawnin di house mi aredi pak op solid. Food a kohn een fram evriwe, an noh wan a wi mi even aks fi half a it - pot a rais an beans, stew chikin, panades, hom bread, drinks a line op pon di step. Di kitchen kounta kova, di dinin tebl kova, dehn set plate pon di floor eeven, cyaahn find nohweh els fi put dehn. Nobady mi seh notn bout it, pipl jos bring weh dehn have, dat da how wi stay yah.

Evribadi mi wear plain dark clothes, notn fancy, an notn need fi seh bout dat - unu jos know weh fi du wen a time like dis kohn.

Befo di waak out, wi all pack op eena wan rum, evribadi eena di wan spies - di pickney dem included, though di lee wan dem mi noh really andastan weh a gwaan, dehn jos mi stan close to dehn mada laig. An it mi so kwaayat, a tel yu, a rum pak full lakka dat, yu wuda tink it wuda noisy, but it get so kwaayat yu coulda ere di clak pon di waal a tik.

A lee handful a wi kyari ahn out tugeda. Wi shoulda di box, hole ahn steady, step by step, out a di dor, an nobady seh a wod di whole way.

Partway tru, wan a wi jus brok dong, outa nowe, noh warnin atall, an fi a second nobady mi move fi tap it. Wi jos let it appen, coz weh els yu fi du wen someting like dat tek ahn?

Sohnbadi get op an taak, an afta dat, wan lee ting weh dehn seh get pass roun fram person to person, ova an ova, like it stick eena evribadi mine. A cyaahn eeven tel yu ezakli how it soun wen it fus kohn out, but a tel yu, evribadi weh mi deh deh a repeat it since, an dehn seh it di exack same way, like da noh dehn own wod.

An den, right eena di miggle a all a dat, sohnbadi memba someting weh jos noh mi able fi stay serious - a lee ting fram bak wen, an di laaf weh bos out afta dat ketch evribadi off gaad, di wan weh laaf ehself neva expek it fi kohn out like dat. Wan lee steups fram sohn a di ohl leddy dem, like fi seh "unu bad", but even dehn couldn hole back a smile. Wan second wi mi a bawl, di neks wi mi a laaf, an nobady coulda eksplain how da appen so quick.

Di waak to di restin place feel lang, langa dan a rememba it, an a walk dat rood plenty time befo dis. Mi fut mi jos noh waahn fi muv fast dat day.

Di wod weh seh deh so mi plain, shaat, notn dress op. Jos di stret trut, notn moa. An dat mi anof.

A lee handful a dut get let go, an dat mi da di end a dat paat.

Di waak bak feel shaata dan di waak deh, evribadi ready fi get bak an put dehn han eena sohn oda kine a bizniz, enitin fi keep unu min bizy.

Di food weh dehn lay out afta, hoo puttup togeda bai whoeva mi close to di family - nobady haffi aks, dehn jos start kuk fram di mawnin. It mi anof fi feed evribadi twice ova, easy, plate afta plate, nyam till evribadi belly full an still deh moa lef.

Wan relative weh nobady mi expek fi si show op anyway, kohn een quiet, stand a di bak, an it mi min moa dan enibadi seh out loud. Nobady mi mek a big ting bout it, but wi all know weh it mean.

Bai evenin di house did empty out, one by one, evribadi gaan bak to weh dehn kohn fram, an di kwaayat kohn all a wan time, heavy, heavia dan di noise mi been all day.

A wish yu mi deh deh fi si how evribadi kohn tugeda widout enibadi haffi aks - how di food jos kohn, how di han jos reach out wen it need to, how di hole tong show op fi wan ohl leddy weh mi teach half a dehn how fi live right. Da di paat a wish yu did si fi yuself.
