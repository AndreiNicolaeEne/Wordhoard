The child: Mommy, mi a beg yuh, buy di phone fi mi nuh. A every day mi a ask yuh.

The mother: Mi hear yuh, an mi ago tell yuh straight since yuh nuh wan tek di hint. Wi nuh have di money fi buy nuh phone right now, an dat a di plain truth. Light bill deh pon mi head, schoolbook still a wait fi September, an wi need wah lickle wi have fi nyam, nuh fi phone. Mi ben tell yuh dat same ting last month.

The child: But mi ago do more chore! Mi ago wash di whole yard, tek out di garbage every day, if yuh jus get mi di phone.

The mother: Pickney, dem ting yuh suppose to do already whether phone come or nuh come. Mi nuh trading chore fi phone, di two ting nuh di same.

The child: Wa bout when mi birthday come roun? Yuh coulda save an get it fi mi den.

The mother: If money deh right bout dat time, an if yuh still a do good a school, we might look pon it. Mi nuh seh nuh fi good, mi seh nuh fi now. Dat can change, but nuh guarantee deh pon it.

The child: But di girl from down di lane already have one! Har mother tell har seh she a get one las week!

The mother: Wah a gwaan inna dem yard nuh settle wah a gwaan inna fi wi yard. Mi nuh know how dem manage dem money, an dat nuh put nuh cent inna mi hand. Every yard have dem own story.

The child: (steups) So mi cah get nutten.

The mother: Mi neva seh dat. Yuh can tek mi old phone Saturday when mi nuh need it, so yuh can text yuh friend dem. Dat a wah mi have fi offer right now.

The child: ...Awright. But mi still waan mi own one, yuh know.

The mother: Mi know, mi know. Wi deh pon it, likkle by likkle.
