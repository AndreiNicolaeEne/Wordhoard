The eldest: Da true wa a tell unu, an a swear pan it: unu pickney dem wahn grow big, dehn wahn nyam sweet, dehn wahn si good day come afta good day. Steups, now yah di uda side, an a swear pan it di same: unu pickney dem wahn grow bitta, dehn wahn nyam hungry, dehn wahn neva si sweet day. Weh a seh, a seh, nuh lie: di blessing an di curse bound tugeda, dem cyaan separate.
