Bout haaf pass twelve now, di house dis doun to jus di fyah wan a wi - di daughta, di son, di eldest gyal-pickney, di neighba fahn nex door weh dis stay wid wi since evening, an me. Everybody weh mi deh pan di poach an eena di yaad done gaan home aready. Di lamp lowa now, di dominoes push tu wan side pan di taybl, an fi a lee wail nobody nuh seh nothing, jus di fan a turn slow eena di corner an di night bug dehn a mek dehn noise outside.

Da di daughta weh brok di kwaiyat fus.

The daughter: Unu memba how Ma mi stay boil di rice? She wouldn tek no shaat cut wid i. Every mawnin, same ting - wata fus, den di rice, den she stand op deh watch i laka she fraid i wahn run weh pan har.

The son: Nuh, nuh so i mi gwaan. Da di beans she mi stan up watch, nuh di rice. Di rice she mi jus lef pan di fyah an gaan bathe, come bak fi ketch i jus in taim.

The daughter: Steups. Yu right, yu right, a mi always confuse dat one wid di oda. She use tu fuss pan mi bout i tu, seh mi kyaahn get nothing straight eena mi head bout kukin.

The neighbour: (laughing all a sudden, den ketchin sheself) Sorry, sorry - a nuh mean fi laugh out like dat. A jus memba how she use tu fuss pan mi bout dat same rice wan taim, seh mi da wahn hopeless kuk an mi betta stik to salad.

The son: Nobody nuh mind, gwaan laugh. Dis a suppose to be di kinda night weh wi laugh good.

Me: Unu memba weh she stay seh when smady come tell ih bad news? She nuh badda cry out loud fus ting. She jus seh, "Well, dat da God wok, wi kyaahn du nothing bout i but pray."

Di eldest quiet fi a second, den ih seh i bak, same slow way, same tone, laka ih did practice i inna ih head fi years.

The eldest: "Well, dat da God wok, wi kyaahn du nothing bout i but pray."

An fi wahn second nobody seh nothing. Ih come out ih mout so exact dat wi aal jus hear Granny vais eena di room fi a lee second, an nobody rush fi brok i.

Den di eldest seh smaddi dehn neva expek.

The eldest: Unu know seh befo she mi marid to Grandpa, she mi run gaan a Cayo wid wahn fisherman fi three whole week, cause fi ih fambily neva want har fi marid Grandpa? Ih tell mi dat wan evening when wi mi deh alone eena di kitchen.

The son: Wat?! Nobody neva tell mi dat one deh eena mi laif!

The daughter: Ay-ay. A true dat? Ma neva tell mi dat, an she mi tell mi evriting.

The eldest: Ih tell mi noh fi tell nobody, seh i da har business an nobody business fi know how she mi come tu marid Grandpa. Ah figga tenight aarait fi tell i.

Di room quiet doun agen, di kind a quiet weh sidong pan yu chest a lee bit. Da di neighba weh brok i, standin op tu pass tings roun agen, jus fi gi ih han sohn ting fi du.

The neighbour: Aright, mek wi pass di bred roun wahn nex taim, an di rom deh pan di taybl - nobody kyaahn sidong an cry pan empty belly tenight. Tek, tek, di plaintain deh gud stil.

Wail di plate a go roun, di son seh sohnting to di daughta bout wahn ol matta dat neva have nothing fi du wid tenight.

The son: Yu memba di lan question, bout weh side a di yaad fi Uncle mi seh mi mi entitle to -

The daughter: Nuh tenight. Please. Wi nuh di go deh tenight a aal.

The son: Aarait, aarait, sorry. Mi nuh mean nothing bai i, i jus come tu mi head.

Nobody press i no further. Ih jus seddong deh fi a second, den di taak move on laka nothing neva pass.

Da di neighba weh tell di stubborn story, di wan everybody mi waitin fi somebady fi tell.

The neighbour: Unu memba how she neva wahn change dat ol chair, di wan wid di leg weh mi tie op wid wire fi like fifteen yaz? Mi offer fi buy har wahn new wan, seh Christmas gift, she cuss mi out good, seh dat chair been fine since befo aal a wi mi born an i wahn stay fine til she seh otawise.

The daughter: Ih neva let nobody touch dat chair, not even fi clean i good. Ih seh yu wipe i wid too much wata di wood wahn suffa.

The son: Stubborn bad, dat wan. But Lawd, ih mi right bout evriting else, so nobody neva mi have no room fi argy.

Everybody laugh soft pan dat, no edge lef eena i, jus di kine a laugh weh come from lovin smaddi dat mi hard-head so.

Widout nobody seh i outright, seem laka wi aal come roun tu di same wan ting tenight. Nuh di chair, nuh di rice, nuh di rom - da how she neva change ih mind wen she noh ready, an how she neva let nobody tell har how fi run ih own laif, right op til di en. Dat da di ting wi wahn memba har by, mo dan aal di res.

Partway tru dis, di daughta start cry kwaiyat. Tears jus a run doun ih face wid out ih mek no sound, ih nuh even wipe dehn, an di taak nuh stop fi har. Nobody mek no big show a i. Dehn jus kip pan gwaan roun har gentle, di son pull ih chair a lee closer, di neighba pass har wahn nakpin widout seh nothing bout i.

Bai bai di taak start tun practical, laka i haffi.

The son: Aarait. Tumaro mawnin ah wahn go a di funeral hows fi settle di paypa dehn. Yu wahn call di Fada fi arenge di sorvis?

The daughter: (wipin ih face, ih vais steady nou) Ah wahn du dat. Yu tek care a di food fi afta so. An yu - (to di neighbour) - yu know evribady roun ya, yu wahn help mi call roun tu let dehn know?

The neighbour: Yes, nuh worry bout dat, lef i to mi, ah wahn start fus ting.

The eldest: Ah wahn stay wid Ma tomorro so she nuh haffi du nothing bai iself.

Bai den di neighba an di son gaan lie doun, an di eldest gaan check pan ih lee wan dehn eena di back room. Is jus me lef sidong wid di lamp, di rom bottle haaf empty pan di taybl, an di house quiet fi true nou, nuh fan, nuh voice, jus mi own breat.

Ah pour wahn lee bit eena di last cup an hold i op to di kwaiyat room, cause somebady haffi close di night out an is jus mi lef fi du i.

Me: Fi yu, Granny. Rest gud nou, yu earn i. Wi wahn tek care a wan anada, jus laka how yu mi teach wi, an wi nuh wahn forget nothing, not even di rice.
