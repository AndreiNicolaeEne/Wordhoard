The foreman: Awright, before allyuh scatter off, leh we settle tomorrow now, cause if we nuh settle it now e gon get forget and den we deh a yard waitin pon one anodda in de mornin like fool.

The eldest: Wha time we startin. Six too early fuh me, bey, dese knee dem done wid six.

The young one: Six good, man, beat de sun before e too hot.

The foreman: Nuh man, leh we mek it seven. Seven good fuh everybody.

Her workmate: Seven fine wid me, I could deal wid seven.

The foreman: Awright, seven e is. We back a de site by de church road, di same one we deh workin las week, roof still nuh finish.

The eldest: Who bringin wha. I got de level and de extension cord already, dat's mine.

The young one: I got de ladder dem, and I could bring de drill too if unu need it.

Her workmate: I on de cement mix, dat's my part, I does mix good, everybody know dat.

The foreman: Good. Eldest, you pon de roof corner again, cause you de one know fi do dat proper.

The eldest: (steups) Every time e mi, every single time, man. Unu know I done wid dat corner a hundred time already. Alright, alright, I go do it, but unu owe me big.

The foreman: We short pon nail dem too, somebody haffi swing by de hardware fore we start. And dem seh rain a come tomorrow evenin, so we need finish before it ketch we.

The foreman: Headcount now, who all deh comin tomorrow. Me, de eldest, de young one, and you.

Her workmate: Wha bout de next one, e comin?

The young one: Nuh, e tell mi seh e cyan make it, e got a doctor thing pon e pickney.

The foreman: Awright den, we four e is.

The eldest: And everybody owe me now for dat corner, don't mek me remind unu at payday.

The foreman: Yinna hear de man. Seven sharp, don't mek me deh dere by myself like a fool.

The eldest: Seven sharp. We deh dere.
