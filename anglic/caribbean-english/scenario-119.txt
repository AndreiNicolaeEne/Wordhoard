Bwai, mek mi tell yu bout dis ya bet, yu noh si notn a it, so mi ga mek yu si eena yu head.

It stat outta nottin, straight so, jos wahn Friday evening wi deh pan di corner, di domino board pan di old table weh somebody drag outta dehn yaad, and wi jus a play fi fun, di kine a play weh nobody nuh put nottin pan it. Mi seh, "Bet yu cyaant beat mi dis game," and da wahn joke, yu noh, cause wi always seh dat, back and foat, nuh meaning notn by it.

But di crowd dem staat gada roun, and unu noh how people stay, dehn nuh lova nottin more dan wen two man staat mek noise bout who beta.

The one keeping score: "A wa unu a bet pan? Cah if unu a taak big taak, put sinting pan it."

My workmate: "Put money pan it, noh! Mek it wort while."

And so it gaan from nottin to sinting, and nobody neva agree wa da sinting was, dehn jus a talk it up, di price a climb, and mi deh de a laugh cause mi confident, mi seh mi noh lose to dis man yet dis whole week. Di odda side neva soh sure, e deh de a scratch e hed, but e nuh back out, cause wa man ga back out front a di whole yaad?

Bai di time wi lock it in, twenty dolla mi seh, twenty dolla e seh, and everybody agree, small enuh fi nuh sting, but big enuh fi mek it real. Wi shake hand pan it front a everybody, and di eldest deh de a nod like e di witness fi wahn court case.

The eldest: "Awrite, unu shake pan it now, no bakout wen unu lose."

So wi staat play, and mi a tell yu, di game gaan back and foat wahn good while, nobody clear ahead, mi tek wahn hand, e tek anodda, di crowd a mek noise every time somebody drop a good piece, and mi still confident cause mi mi a play good. But somewhere in di middle a it, di ting turn. E turn hard. Mi neva si it comin, cah mi mi deh de a count mi pips and mi neva si how e mi a set mi up di whole time, quiet quiet, and by di time mi realize, di bet already lose befo di game itself done. Mi jus deh de a hole mi domino dem eena mi han, and mi noh have wahn move lef weh mi wahn wahn.

Wen it final done, mi groan out lowd, mi couldn't help it, and mi seh, "Aarait, aarait, unu win, mi lose, no point mi a pretend." Di whole crowd bruk out eena laugh, dehn a slap mi pan mi back, dehn a seh dehn know from di start.

Now ya weh it get strange, my friend. Mi mi tink mi jus wahn fi pay di twenty dolla and dat done, but di crowd dem noh let it res soh simple. Dehn all staat talk at wan time.

My workmate: "No man, forget di money, we waan sinting betta."

The one keeping score: "E ga do sinting front a everybody. Payment fi be wahn performance, right ya, no pay quiet quiet."

And dehn nuh jus lef it vague eeda, dehn name out exactly wa e ga du, piece by piece, laughing di whole time dehn a build it up. E ga dress like wahn certain kind a woman, e ga borrow wahn wig from somewhow, e ga sing wahn full love song, and e ga go down pan e knee front a di landlady weh siddown pan she porch every evening, and propose to har like e mean it, right deh eena di street, and e ga finish di whole ting off befo e get up.

Mi try fi bargain outta it, mi seh, "Look, mek mi jus pay di twenty dolla, no bada wid all a dis, mi wahn pay right now."

The eldest: "Stchuups. No sah. Yu tink yu wahn buy yu way outta dis? Too lat fi dat now."

Dehn laugh mi outta di whole idea, seh a deal da a deal, and once di crowd decide, notn mi could a seh mi wahn change it. So wi set a day, di Saturday comin, and wi set a place, right deh a di same corner weh wi mi a play, so everybody weh mi si pan a regular day could come out fi si it.

And bwai, wen di Saturday come, e do it proper. E come out eena di wig, wahn old flowery dress somebody wife lend, e even bring wahn radio fi play di song, and e staat walk down di street a sing, and mi tell yu, e cyaan sing fi save e life, but e mi a give it e whole heart, e a hole di note like e mean it. Di whole street come out, pickney dem a run behind, dogs a bark, everybody a laugh and a record pan dehn fone.

Wen e reach da di landlady porch, e go down pan e knee, right pan di concrete, and e hole out e han and e seh, "Miss, mi love yu from di first day mi lay eye pan yu, mek wi be togeda."

But ya weh get everybody, di landlady, she noh play fool, she jus seh, cool cool, "Bwai, get up outta mi dirt befo mi wet yu wid dis bucket," and she lif up wahn bucket weh mi noh even si she mi a hole, like she mi a wait fi dis exact moment di whole time, and she throw wahn splash a water pan e right eena e face, wig and all. Di whole street lose it. People deh de a fall down, dehn cyaan even stand up straight, some a dehn seh dehn belly a hurt from laugh so hard. E jump up soakin wet, di wig a slide off e hed, and e still a try fi finish di song, singing through di water pan e face, and dat mek it worse, cause now e look like wahn drown-out chicken a sing love song.

Dat right deh, dat da di moment. Not di dress, not di knee, not eeven di proposal, but di bucket. Nobody could a plan dat, and da mek it perfect.

So now, my friend, whenever wi group deh togeda, no matta how much time pass, somebody wahn bring it up. And every time dehn tell it, e get wahn likkle more pan it. Now dehn seh di bucket water mi cold like ice, dehn seh di wig fly clean across di street, dehn seh di landlady mi already dress up eena she raincoat waiting from marnin. Mi neva even bet fi money no more, mi lose wahn night a domino, but mi gain wahn story weh nuh dead yet, and every time dehn tell it, e grow wahn next likkle piece a theater pan top, till soon dehn wahn seh a whole brass band mi deh de dat night.
