The niece: Aunty, mi need yuh fi gi mi di real brown stew chicken recipe, di one from Granny, before it gaan to nuttn. Nobody nuh have it write dung nowhere.

The aunt: Steups. Yuh tink mi have time fi write dung recipe? A inna mi head from mi did a likkle pickney a stan up a watch Granny a di stove.

The niece: Well tell mi nuh, mi a wait pon yuh.

The aunt: Awright, hol' on mek mi tink good... First yuh tek yuh chicken, cut up, bout a pound an a half, sometimes more if a big family a come. Yuh season it wid garlic, black pepper, likkle salt, and, wait, mi almost fahget di scallion dem, yuh haffi put in di scallion, cyaan mek it widout dat, mi nearly lef it out an mi granny woulda come outta har grave pon mi fi dat.

The niece: And den?

The aunt: Season it, lef it fi si down, overnight better but a hour or two can do. Den yuh put yuh oil pon di fire an drop in yuh brown sugar fi mek di browning, an dis a di part yuh cyaan rush, granny always seh nuh rush di sugar, if yuh rush it, it bun an it bitter an spoil di whole pot. Yuh drop di chicken piece dem in deh mek dem tek on di color, den yuh put in yuh water, carrot, onion, pimento seed dem, an let it stew dung slow.

The niece: How yuh know how much sugar dough, yuh nuh give mi no measurement.

The aunt: Mi nuh measure dat one, mi just judge it by eye, di color tell mi when it right.

The niece: And how yuh know when di chicken done, if yuh nuh watch no clock?

The aunt: Nuh clock nuh deh inna dis kitchen. When di gravy dark an it a cling to di meat, an di meat a pull weh easy from di bone, dat a when it done.

The niece: Mi can use white sugar instead a di brown fi di browning?

The aunt: Yuh can, but it nuh come out di same, di color paler an di taste nuh as deep, so if yuh nuh have none, do it, but a next ting altogether from wah Granny mek.
