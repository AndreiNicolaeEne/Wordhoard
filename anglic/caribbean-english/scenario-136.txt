Di lee money weh mi ben kip bak eena di jaar pan tap di cyaabinet, fi peh light bill ahn buy grosri fi di munt, mi tek i out fi kount afta mi kom een fahn wok. Mi kount i wan taim, ih neva ad op rait, so mi kount i bak agen, slo, tekin mi taim, ahn ih stil kom out shaat. Mi kount i twice fi mek shoor, ahn bot taim ih di seh di seym nomba, ahn dat nomba nuh rait.

Mi go luk eena di oda spat weh mi sohnaim put i, onda di matres eena di bak rum, jos fi mek shoor. Notn neva deh de needa.

Mi kaal aal a dem eena di haas, izi soh, fus taim.

Me: Unu pickney dem, unu si eni a dat money weh mi ben kip pan di cyaabinet? Enibaddy tek i, ar muuv i, ar barra i ahn foget fi tell mi?

The eldest: Nuh mi, mada. Mi neva touch i.

The youngest: Mi noh notn bout no money. Mi neva eevn si i.

Me: Nobadi? Enibaddy a unu?

The eldest and the youngest, together: Nuh mi.

Mi tink bout i gud, ahn mi riyalaiz seh a wan pusn ben deh eena di haas alone di day di money gaan mising.

Me, to the one who stayed home: A yu wan ben deh eena di haas alone dat day. Weh di money deh?

The one who stayed home: Mi tell yu aredi, mi neva tek i. Mi noh si no money.

Me: No kom tell mi seh yu neva si i, cauz a onli yu ben deh eena di haas dat day!

The one who stayed home: Yu kyaahn jos put i pan mi cauz a mi wan ben deh home!

Me: Da fi mi money dat, nuh fi enibadi els, ahn now ih lite, ahn a onli yu kuda tek i!

The one who stayed home: Mi seh mi neva tek noh money! Yu wahn beliv weh yu wahn beliv, no matta weh mi seh!

Me: If noh badi bring i bak, wi wahn haffi go widout light dis munt, ahn a yu fi bliem!

The one who stayed home: Steups. Blame mi den, if dat mek yu fiil betta.
