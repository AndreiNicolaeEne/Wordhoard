The bride: Look ya, dis list a run over by close to t'irty an di place cyaan hold more than wi have.

The groom: Mi si it already. A time wi start cut somebody off.

The bride: Right, so mek wi start wid di far cousin dem an di coworker dem yuh nuh really talk to from Christmas come. Cut dem an wi down by half a di t'irty right deh so.

The groom: Yuh nuh touching mi Aunty doe. She haffi deh deh pon di day, no matter wha gwaan.

The bride: Steups. A always har name come up first when time fi cut somebody.

The groom: A mi blood dat, seh wha yuh waan mi fi do.

The bride: If she a stay pon di list, one from unu side haffi come off den, cause it nuh balance out.

The groom: Yuh a start dis again? Same argument every time wi plan someting, seh a fi mi family always get more seat dan unu.

The bride: A nuh me a seh it, a di paper a seh it. Count unu side, count mi side, unu side longer, plain an simple.

The groom: Wha if wi jus do a likkle second ting fi di people weh nuh fit, back a di yard, nyam an drink, small small, nutten fancy?

The bride: Nuh bother wid dat. Wi nuh have nuh extra money fi run two function pon di same day. Forget it, dat done.

The groom: Awright, awright. Mek wi count back di whole list nuh.

The bride: Wi still ova. Bout a dozen more haffi go before it right.

The groom: Mi cyaan move mi Aunty, but mi wi tek off two a mi work friend dem, di one dem weh never even come a wi yard yet.

The bride: Dat a someting. Still short, but a someting. We wi haffi finish sort it out before di week done.

The groom: It soon done, nuh worry yuhself.
