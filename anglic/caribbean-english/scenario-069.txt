Allyuh see mih cat? Gone three days now, ain't come back yet. She grey and white, black tip on the one ear, tail does curl like a question mark. Last see she was by the standpipe corner, evening time. She shy with strangers, run and hide quick, but she does come out fast if it have food. Small money for whoever bring she back safe. Strips with the number tearing off below, doh call too late nah.
