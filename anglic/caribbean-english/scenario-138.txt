Neighbor: Mi hear seh the whole entire ting start when the pickney buck up pon one a har teacher dem, an dem seh she box the teacher back, hard, right deh inna the classroom in front a everybody. A fi dat mek dem put her out a school same day self, nuh letter send home first, notten.

Mother: Hold up, hold up, mi friend. That nuh weh happen. Not even close, yuh hear mi.

Mother: Yuh know a fi mi daughter dem a chat bout, right. Mi deh a the school when it gwaan, is mi dem call fi come. Two girl did a fight inna the yard, an mi daughter never box nobody. She push een between the two a dem fi stop it, an one a dem accidentally ketch her in the mix. One a the other one dem run gaan tell the teacher seh a she start it, an the teacher never see the first part, so she believe it. Dat's it. Dat's the whole story. Dem never put her out, dem never even give her no note. Mi come, mi talk to the teacher, dem see seh she never do notten, an she deh back inna class the very next day same way.

Mother: So by the time it reach yuh, "she stop a fight" turn into "she start one an get expel." Dat is whole heap a distance fi one story fi travel.

Neighbor: Steups, mi sorry eeh, mi never mean fi carry it so. A mi cousin tell mi, she seh she hear it from the lady weh sell by the gate.

Mother: An she probably never even deh there either. Is so it stretch, mouth to mouth, everybody add a likkle more.

Neighbor: True, true.

Mother: Alright den. From now, if anybody ask yuh bout it, tell dem how it really gwaan, exactly so, nuh more, nuh less. She stop a fight, she never get put out. Tell it straight every time, whoever ask.
