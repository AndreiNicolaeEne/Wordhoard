Aayoh my friend, mi owe yu di whole story bout Satideh nite cuz yu neva deh deh fi si it and mi still nuh believe misself weh mi get on. Lemme try piece i tugeda fi yu, cuz half a it come back to mi like flash a lightnin, one piece yasso, one piece dehsoh, notn a mek sense between dem.

Ih mi di granny birthday, unu know how di ole lady stay, she seh she nuh wahn nutn big dis yaso, jos wahn likkle sinting fi di family, and den before yu know it half a Yabra deh eena har yard, di grill a smoke since aftanoon, di soun sistem a bex, everybody dance and nyam till dem foot swell. Di granny herself did deh eena har chair like she da wahn queen, wavin har hand tellin everybody "nyam, nyam, no lef notn pan di plate," and yu nuh cyan tell dat ooman no, yu jos cyaan.

Mi mi tell misself, mi wahn eat wahn plate and lef early cuz mi ha church di nex day fi help set up. But di granny grab mi han and seh,

The granny: "Bwai, weh yu di guh? Siddown, yu nuh si how much stew chikin lef? Yu fraid fi nyam?"

And weh mi fi seh to dat. So mi tek anodda plate, den anodda one afta dat, and somebody push wahn cup a rum-punch eena mi han and mi seh awrite, jos wahn, and dat wahn tun to tree fo, mi nuh eeven mi di count no mo. Di music a play and mi mi deh a di middle a di yard a wine and jump up wid everybody, and mi memba clear so, laughin till mi belly hurt, di granny a clap fi everybody pan di dance flo, and dat da di last ting mi memba clear.

Afta dat notn deh eena order no mo. Mi si piece: somebody han mi wahn next cup, mi si misself a sing loud loud fi wahn song mi neva even know di wods to, mi si di neighba deh deh too and mi seh sinting to har, some big mouth sinting mi cyaan even ricall right, but mi feel eena mi bones seh it neva nice, it neva kind. Mi si misself a laugh, mi si somebody help mi fi walk straight, and den notn. Blank. Like somebody jos tun off di light.

Wen mi wake up di nex mahnin, my friend, Lawd God. Mi head mi a beat like drum, wahn heavy heavy drum dat nuh stop. Mi belly mi a tun ova jos fi tink bout food, notn, not even smell a bredfast could pass near mi nose widout mi wahn heave. Di likkle light weh come tru di winda mi feel like sun itself, and evrybody voice eena di house soun like dem a shout eena mi ear. Mi couldn even siddown pan di bed widout it tek all mi strent, mi jos deh deh, flat down, eyes shet, prayin fi di worl fi stop spin.

Di mother did up before mi, movin bout like notn happm, sweepin di yard, warshin wahn cup, and yu know har, she cyaan miss wahn chance fi lik mi wid har mout.

The mother: "Wa gaan wid yu? Yu look like sinting di cyat drag een. All dat wine and jump up las nite, weh it gi yu dis mahnin, eh?"

Me: "Mama, mek mi res, no."

The mother: "Res? Yu wasn resin las nite wen yu di sing loud pan top di speaka. Mi hear yu clear from mi room."

She di laugh, nuh badhaat bout it, jos teasin mi soft, but every wod she seh mi mi feel eena mi head like nail a knock. Mi jos lie deh, mi nuh move, and mi mek misself wahn promise right deh so, flat pan mi back, dat mi wahn neva let wahn nite guh so far gone again. Mi seh i to misself serious, serious. But mi know good and well seh dis nuh da di fus time mi swear dat same promise, and mi laugh eena misself, wahn sad likkle laugh, cuz mi know how dis stohri go.

Aal mahnin, likkle likkle ting weh normally tek notn, dem feel like mountain. Mi mi try guh outside jos fi wahn short walk fi clear mi head, and mi neva mek i pass di gate before mi haffi tun bak. Wahn simple choa, jos fi wash di plate dem, mi look pan i and mi jos couldn. Mi body neva ready fi notn til way pas midday.

Roun noon mi mek misself eat wahn dry bread wid wahn likkle bit a soup, notn rich, notn wid pepper, jos sinting fi settle mi stomach, and mi keep quiet, mi keep still, mi nuh watch no phone, no television, notn, jos siddown eena di dark room til di worst a i pass ova.

And eena all dat quiet time, piece a las nite keep comin back to mi, flash by flash. Di one weh trouble mi most da weh mi seh to di neighba. Mi memba now, clear as day, mi mi tell har someting bout har husban weh neva mi bizniz to seh, loud enuf fi haf di yard hear, and mi si har face change wen mi seh i. Mi cyaan even tell yu weh mek mi seh sowah ting, di rum tek hol a mi mout and mi neva check misself. Now mi owe har wahn apology, and mi noh di procrastinate bout i, mi jos di turn i ova and ova eena mi head, embarrass bad, tinking how mi wahn face har, weh wod fi seh fi mek i right.

By evenin time tings staat fi come back to normal. Mi mi able fi eat wahn real plate a food, rice and beans wid stew fish, and mi tink straight again, no mo drum eena mi head, no mo tun eena mi belly. Mi si di granny lata pan and she jos laugh and seh mi favor har own self back eena har day, and mi laugh too, but eena misself mi di feel some kine a heaviness still deh deh.

Cuz true talk, my friend, mi know weh mi promise misself dis mahnin da wahn good promise, but mi also know misself. Nex time somebody deh celebrate, nex time di music sweet so and di family deh roun mi so, mi wahn probably fall right back eena i again, seh yes to one more, den one more afta dat. Mi nuh proud a i, but mi honess wid yu. Fi now doh, mi ha wahn apology fi mek to di neighba, and mi wahn hold on to dis lesson jos likkle while, til di nex bram come roun and tempt mi again.
