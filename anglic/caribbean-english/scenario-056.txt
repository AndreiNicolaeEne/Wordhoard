Seven day dem gie mi fi mind de house, and a go tell unu straight, from di fus night someting neva right wid dat place.

Di owner dem lef fi go a foreign fi a wedding, and dem seh, "It nuh hard, jus water di plant dem, feed di dog, lock up good, and check de gate." Mi seh awright, no problem, a mi mind big house before. But di fus night, mi lay down pon dem bed and di whole house a mek noise. Not one big noise, jus di kind a settling noise ole house does mek, but it neva sound right to mi. Di fridge a hum diffrent, di fan a knock, and outside di dog a bark at nutting fi bout ten minute straight. Mi lay deh eye open till near two a marnin, thinking, "wa dis," but nutting neva come a it. Mi seh to miself, is jus di house nuh use to mi and mi nuh use to it.

Second day, di real trouble start. Mi go outside fi feed di dog and someway, someway, mi neva latch di gate proper, and dat dog bolt straight out inna di road like it goin somewhe fi collect prize. Mi bawl "come! come!" and run down di road in mi slipper, one hand still holdin di food bowl, people pon di street a look pon mi like mi mad. Neighbour man come out he yard.

Neighbour: "Wa gwaan, why yu a chase di dog like dat?"

Me: "He loose! I mindin di house and he get out!"

Di dog cut cross by di trench, gone round by di corner shop, and by di grace a God one lil boy from up di road ketch him and hold him collar till mi reach. Bout an hour altogether, mi heart neva stop poundin. Mi tell di dog, "if dem come back and yu nuh deh, is trouble fi mi, yu hear," like he understand English better dan he understand him own bark.

Now di plant dem, dat one neva happen sudden, dat one creep up pon mi slow slow. Fus two three day mi a water dem like dem seh, but di sun in dat yard hot bad, and by midweek mi start notice di leaf dem a droop, di soil a crack up like desert. Mi try fix it, water more, water less, put shade cloth over one pot, but by day five one whole croton just give up and drop half a im leaf dem pon di ground. Mi steups, real hard, cause mi nuh know if is too much water or too lil, and dem plant nuh come wid instruction.

Den di fridge, di same fridge weh mi hear hummin dat fus night, it start mek a different sound altogether by Wednesday, den by Thursday marnin it jus stop cold. Mi open it and everyting inside warm, di ice cream a mash down inna one soft mess. Mi call up di owner phone but nuh answer, dem a foreign, time diffrent. Mi try unplug it, plug back, nutting. Mi had was to move di perishable dem to a cooler wid ice mi buy from di shop, and every day after dat mi runnin to buy more ice like it a mi job.

Same week, in di middle a all dis, one relative a di owner just show up, no call, no warning, straight to di gate.

Visitor: "Good day, a who yu, and where mi cousin dem deh?"

Me: "Dem gone a foreign, dem ask mi fi mind di place. Yu want come in?"

Visitor: "Yes man, mi come fi collect someting from di back room, dem tell mi it deh."

Mi neva even know weh dis someting was, and mi standin up deh watchin a next-person go through di house like it fi dem, but wa mi coulda do, dem seh a family. Turn out was some paper dem needed, and off dem gone same way dem come, sudden sudden.

Den come di real fright. Mi a clean up round di dresser and mi come across one lil velvet box, and inside it look like gold ring, di kind dem does keep special. Mi put it down pon di dresser fi finish wipe, and when mi look back fi it, di box nuh deh! Mi search high, mi search low, mi move di whole dresser, look under di bed, mi heart a beat like drum, mi thinking "God, dis a fi dem good ring and mi lose it, dem a go kill mi." Mi search near an hour, sweat runnin down mi back, till mi find out di box did drop down between di dresser and di wall, small lil gap, box did jus slide down deh so. Mi hold mi chest and thank God out loud, cause mi did already start plan wa mi a go tell dem.

Now di lock-out, dat one was pure foolishness pon mi own part. Friday afternoon mi step out fi buy someting quick from di shop, door lock behind mi, and mi realize di house key deh inside on di table weh mi lef it. Mi try every window, every door, nutting movin. Mi call one neighbour fi help, and dem seh dem nuh have no way fi get in either, so mi jus haffi sit down pon di step, in di hot sun, waitin till a locksmith mi call finally reach, bout three hour later. Mi vex bad, but mi laugh too, cause who else but mi self self woulda do dat.

Dat same night, rain fall heavy, and mi remember mi had lef one window open inna di small back room fi get air. By di time mi wake up and check, water did soak di floor good, di lil rug near ruin, papers pon a shelf get wet round di edge. Mi mop and mop and put fan pon it fi dry, prayin di whole while it nuh leave no mark pon di wood.

Den one night, real late, mi hear noise from outside, someting a bang round by di back. Mi heart jump, mi grab a flashlight and go check, careful careful, thinkin worse. Turn out was jus a cat gone through di garbage, knock over di bin, tin can a roll all bout di yard. Mi pick up di mess, steups, and go back a bed, but mi neva sleep good after dat, ears open fi every lil sound.

By di sixth day mi decide enough a dat fridge foolishness. Mi call up a fixture man, and him check it, seh a small part gone bad inside, him change it out right deh, and finally di fridge start hum proper again, cold like it should. Mi glad fi see di back a dat problem, cause mi did tired a buyin ice every day.

Last day before dem come back, mi run round like mad, tidy every corner, mop floor, wipe dresser, put every plant weh look decent to di front and hide di droopy one dem behind di house, straighten di rug in di back room so di water mark nuh so notice, fix di curtain, spray someting nice smellin through di whole house. Mi even wash di dog.

When dem finally reach and walk through di door, di owner look round and seh, "everyting look good, house nice and clean, thank yu bad."

Owner: "How di week gone, everyting good?"

Me: "Yes man, everyting fine, quiet week."

Mi neva mention di dog run out, di plant weh droop, di fridge weh dead, di cousin weh show up, di ring weh lost and find back, di lock-out, di rain inna di room, or di cat inna di garbage. Mi jus smile and carry mi bag out di gate, thinkin to miself, next time somebody ask mi fi mind house, mi go think twice, real twice.
