Come, mek wi start from di gate, cause yuh cyaan understand di house widout yuh understand di gate first. Yuh push it and it a gi weh dat same lickle screech every time, and di dog dem from up di road start bark before yuh even foot touch di yard. Dat sound deh, di screech and di bark together, dat a di sound weh mean seh yuh reach home. Every time mi hear a gate creak like dat anywhere inna town, mi heart jump likkle bit, like it a call mi back.

Yuh come een past di gate and yuh a step pon di likkle concrete path weh crack up round di edge, and grass a push up tru di crack dem cause nobody never bother fix i. Straight up deh so a di verandah, and dat a weh yuh always find somebody sidung, whether a di grandmother a shell peas or a one a di neighbour dem come fi borrow someting and end up sit down a gossip fi one hour.

Inna di main room now, wi call i di hall, though it nuh big so, dat a weh everything used to gwaan. Di settee did have plastic pon i so long dat when yuh sidung inna hot afternoon i did a stick to yuh back a yuh shirt. Dat a weh wi watch television, dat a weh homework get do pon di floor, dat a weh visitor get siddung and offer likkle something to drink. Yuh coulda hear whole heap a argument bout politics inna dat room, cause di uncle dem love dem politics talk.

Uncle: Yuh see wa di man seh pon di radio dis marning?
Mother: Hush yuh mouth nuh, mi nuh waan hear bout dat again.
Uncle: Mi jus a seh...
Mother: Mi seh hush.

And everybody woulda laugh, and dat woulda done dat.

Now come mek wi go een a di kitchen, cause di kitchen a di heart a di whole house, everybody know dat. Di smell weh live een deh, that a one smell yuh cyaan never wash out, escallion and thyme and pimento a bruk down inna oil, and pon a Saturday marning di whole yard smell like soup a bwile. Di grandmother did have one habit inna dat kitchen, every single day she woulda dash a lickle bit a di pot pon di ground before she serve anybody, one lickle offering, and if yuh ask her bout i she woulda jus seh, "Dat a fi di dead dem, dem haffi eat too." Nobody question i, it jus did deh so, part a how di kitchen run.

Grandmother: Come nyam, di food ready.
Me: Mi nuh hungry yet, granny.
Grandmother: Yuh nuh hungry? Bwoy, tek di plate before mi lick yuh wid di spoon.

Everybody did sleep two-three to a room, cause di house nuh big enough fi gi one pickney one room to demself. Mi and mi brother did share, and mi did always end up pon di edge a di bed cause him did love spread out like him a king. Di girl dem did share di other room, and dem woulda argue whole night bout who tek up more space pon di sheet, or who a hog di fan.

One corner a di house did belong to di aunty, one and one, and everybody know fi leave i alone. She did have one likkle table wid her sewing machine and her church hat dem line up pon top a di wardrobe, and if yuh so much as touch dat table yuh woulda hear bout i fi days. Dat corner did feel different from di rest a di house, quiet, likkle bit cool even when di rest a di place hot.

Mi did have mi own spot too, though nobody know bout i really, behind di water tank round di back, likkle gap between di tank and di wall weh a pickney coulda squeeze inna and disappear from di whole world. Mi used to go deh wid a comic book or jus fi hide when mi did nuh waan do no chore. Mi did feel like nobody coulda find mi deh so, though a lie, cause everybody did know exactly weh mi deh most a di time.

Outta di back door now, dat a di yard, and di yard did have more life inna i dan di house sometime. Chicken did run bout, clothesline stretch cross from one post to di next, and one big mango tree weh drop fruit pon di zinc roof every likkle breeze wid a bang weh coulda frighten yuh outta yuh sleep. Yuh used to go outside inna mango season and pick up di ripe one dem before di ants get to dem.

Di neighbour dem did close so till yuh coulda hear dem business plain plain. Di one nex door did have one radio weh play gospel every marning bout six, so before yuh even open yuh eye good yuh a hear singing come tru di wall. Di one pon di corner did sell bag juice and bulla, and pickney from all bout woulda gather deh bout evening time.

Same time every marning, bout half five, six, yuh woulda hear di bread van a come down di road, blowing dat same horn, and everybody woulda run out wid dem lickle change fi buy bulla or hard-dough bread while it still hot. And same time every evening, bout dusk, yuh woulda see di same ole man a walk him goat dem down di road, slow slow, like him and di goat dem nuh have nowhere in particular fi be.

Di one thing bout dat house weh did always broken, or always a get talk bout, a di standpipe. Water woulda cut off same time every week and everybody woulda stand up round di standpipe wid dem bucket, and somebody woulda always seh dem a go call di parish council, but nobody never call nobody, and di pipe woulda jus fix itself somehow or wi woulda jus mek do.

Neighbour: Mi tell yuh, dis pipe a go dead one a dese day.
Mother: It deh so from since mi married inna dis yard, and it nuh dead yet.
Neighbour: Steups. One a dese day.

Season did change di place too, though wi nuh really did have four season like foreign, wi did have wet time and dry time. When di rain come down hard, di gully behind di house woulda run brown and loud, and pickney woulda run out fi watch, though wi mother woulda box wi if she catch wi too close. When it dry, di dust woulda rise off di road every time a car pass, and everybody woulda cuss bout it, and everybody woulda water dem plant morning and evening jus fi keep di likkle life inna dem yard.

Right out pon di street a weh di real gathering did gwaan. Evening time, when di sun start go down, all a wi pickney dem woulda come out and play, dandy shandy, hopscotch chalk pon di concrete, cricket wid a tennis ball and a piece a board fi bat, and if a car did come, everybody woulda holler "car!" and scatter till it pass, den come right back to di same game like nutt'n never happen. Di grown-up dem woulda stand up pon dem gate a talk, and yuh coulda hear laugh carry all di way down di road.

Di last clear memory mi have a standing right deh pon di verandah, packing up di last box, and di grandmother sidung inna her chair watching mi, and she jus seh, "Mind how yuh carry yuhself out deh, and memba weh yuh come from." Dat a di last time mi did really feel like di house did belong to mi.

Di place still deh, but it change bad. New owner buil up round it, di mango tree gone, dem cement over di yard. Some a di family scatter, foreign, uptown, wherever life tek dem. Yuh cyaan really go back and stand inna di same doorway and feel di same ting, di feeling more dan di house.

So dat's di whole tour, from di gate straight tru to di back a di yard. Yuh see now why mi always talk bout it like it still deh so, standing right deh waiting pon wi. Mi know it change, mi know time move pon, but every time mi tell it, mi deh back deh, right deh inna di doorway, pointing tings out to yuh same way mi jus do now.
