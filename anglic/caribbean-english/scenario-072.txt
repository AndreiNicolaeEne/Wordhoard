Me: Wi ago meet a di bar pon Half Way Tree side tonight, like eight a night gud fi yuh?

My friend: Eight? Nuh man, mi cyaan mek eight, mi haffi tek mi pickney guh drop a mi madda yard first, an dat tek time.

Me: Awright, mek wi push it likkle den. Nine gud?

My friend: Nine better fi mi. Same spot, or wi a guh somewhere else?

Me: Same spot, di one wid di TV dem outside, everybody know it aready.

My friend: True. Yuh check di res a di gruop yet?

Me: Mi check har a work, she seh she done by eight so nine nuh problem fi har.

My friend: Wa bout him, di one weh always deh pon time?

Me: Him deh pon it, him seh him a go come fi sure.

My friend: An di one from di office, yuh call him yet?

Me: Mi call him dis marnin, him seh him cyaan come tonight, him haffi wuk overtime, di boss nuh let him lef early.

My friend: Steups. Dat man cyaan ever get a break.

Me: True still, but a wa fi do, a money him a look.

My friend: So a nine wi a settle pon now?

Me: Mi did waan seh eight-tirty, but nine easier fi most a wi, so lickle late a nuh nutten.

My friend: Nine dun den. Yuh waan mi guh tell di res a unu fi yuh, so it nuh fall pon yuh one?

Me: Yes man, guh tell dem seh nine a Half Way Tree, same spot as always.

My friend: A how much a wi dis anyway, six?

Me: Round six, seven maybe, depen pon if har fren come or nuh.

My friend: Awright, six seven den. Wi nuh haffi count exact, wi jus deh deh.
