Doux-doux, I a write yuh dis late in de night and I not beating round no bush, is you I loving, plain so, and it have a good while now I know it for true.

Yuh remember dat evening we was just liming by de corner, waiting on de doubles man, rain start to fall light-light and we ain't even move? Wasn't no big occasion, no fete, nothing name-brand, just an ordinary evening like any other. Yuh lean back, roll yuh eye at some stupidness I say, and laugh dat laugh yuh does try to hide behind yuh hand. Is right dey, in dat little nothing moment, meh heart turn over and I know seh dis done gone and pass fun and games.

Nobody but meh know how yuh hum off-key when yuh ironing, always de same ole soca yuh swear up and down yuh doh even like. Small ting. But is mine to keep, and I keeping it close.

Since yuh come in meh life, meh days does run different, lighter, sweeter, even de traffic on de highway doh vex meh so bad no more. But leh meh tell yuh straight, when we apart de days does drag long long, and meh nights deh empty for true. Steups, meh eh shame to write it down, but is de truth and I giving it to yuh plain.

It have one ting I never tell yuh out meh own mouth, sometimes I fraid dat I not good enough to keep yuh happy, dat one day yuh go get tired of ordinary me and just gone quiet-quiet. I never say it loud to nobody, it just deh sitting in meh chest.

Still, I promising yuh dis one ting for sure: whatever life bring, rough or smooth, I not running nowhere. I staying right here beside yuh, through everyting, no matter what come.

Is yuh doux-doux, now and after.
