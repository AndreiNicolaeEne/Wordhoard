Gyaff nuh, mek me tell yuh bout de worse trip me ever tek in me life. Up to now when me studying it me haffi laugh, though when it a happen me tell yuh me nearly cry straight.

Dis trip we plan good good, weeks before, not no last-minute ting. Me and me pardner book we seat pon de Lethem bus, pay in full, get de receipt, everything set proper. De man tell we three o'clock sharp de bus a leave, so if we deh dey by two-thirty we straight, we wahn reach Lethem befo evening done, sleep in we own bed dat same night. Dat was de plan, and it was a good plan, mi tell yuh. We even ben hear seh dat bus company does run late sometime, but we seh no, dis time different, we early, we ready, nutten cyaan go wrong.

First lick come early early. We reach de park two-thirty like de man seh, bag pack, water bottle full, roti wrap up fu de road. Three o'clock come, no bus a move. Half four, de bus still deh dey park up, engine off, driver nowhere fi find. Come to find out de man a wait pon one more passenger cargo, some drums a rice de fella from Charity a bring, and till dat load pon top, we cyaan move a inch. So we sit dung pon de bench inna de dark, watching de sky lighten up lil by lil, and already me feeling seh dis trip start off pon de wrong foot.

Bus finally roll out gone six, three hour after we suppose to move, and everybody pon board a grumble already, though nobody nuh saying nothing loud. Yuh just tek it, yuh know how it go.

We reach de trail alright, dust flying, forest tight round de road, things a run smooth till we pass Kurupukari and cross de river pon de pontoon. Not even one hour pon de odder side, de bus start knock knock, and next thing black smoke a come out de front and de driver pull over quick quick. Engine mash up, done, finish. De conductor open de bonnet, look pon it, steups, shake e head, and dat tell we everything we need fi know.

By de time we come outta de bus, three, four next minibus deh line up behind we already, because de trail so narrow, one bus mash up block de whole line. Bit by bit de crowd start build, people from every bus gather side de road, pickney dem running bout in de bush grass, one lady selling boil egg and channa outta she basket like she know dis a gwine happen all along. We nyam some a de roti we bring while we wait, because who know when next we go eat. Bare people, and every last one a we stuck deh so together.

Hour pass, two hour pass, three hour pass, and nobody, and me mean nobody, could tell we when de bus a go move again. De driver dem deh pon de phone but out dey signal come and go like it own we nutten, so half de time dem cyaan even reach de office fi ask fu next bus fi come tek we. De conductor holler out one time seh allyuh mus just hold strain, e nuh have nothing more fi tell we, and dat was dat.

And as if dat nuh enough, de sky start turn dark quick, and rain start a fall pon we like it vex too. Not no lil drizzle, real hard rain, de kind wha turn de dirt road to soup in ten minute flat. Everybody scramble fi cover, some a we duck unda de bus, some a we crowd up inna de one next bus wha still running, and me and me pardner end up soak straight through, standing up unda a tarpaulin some fella tie up between two tree.

Is right dey, inna all dat confusion, me realize me pardner grip nuh deh with we no more. We ben tek de bag off de bus when de smoke start come out, put it dung side de road wid de rest, but wid all de people a shift bag round fi find shelter, some odder bus wha still running must a load up de wrong grip and gone. Twenty minute we run bout inna de rain, up and dung de line a bus, asking every driver seh dem see a grip like dis, me pardner near cry, and me heart a beat like it a gwine jump out me chest, because inna dat bag was de money and de papers dem.

Was a next passenger, a old man wid a cutlass pon e belt, a fella we never see before in we life, wha run go flag dung de bus wha pull off wid we bag on top e roof rack. E hold up e hand in de rain and mek dat bus stop, climb up pon de step sheself, and pull de grip dung, hand it straight back to we like it wasn't nothing at all. Up to today me still tink bout dat man. E never ask fu nothing, never even wait fi we say thanks proper, just nod e head and gone back fi e own seat. Some people just born good like dat.

By de time we settle back dung, we haffi pay one a de other bus driver fifty extra just fi hold two seat fu we pon e bus, because we ben lose we place inna de whole confusion, and dat was fifty we never budget fu at all, none a we.

And is right dey de argument start, because me pardner seh we should wait fu we own bus fi fix, since we ben pay fu dat one already, and me seh no, forget dat, we tek wha coming and go, because de sky nuh looking like it a clear up soon and we cyaan sit up inna dis mud all night waiting.

Me: We nuh have time fi wait pon dis bus, e mash up bad, come we go.
The companion: And wha bout de money we already pay fu de seat? We just a throw dat way?
Me: Better we lose de fare dan we lose de whole night sitting up in dis rain.
The companion: Yuh always so quick fi run off, yuh never tink tings through proper.
Me: And yuh always so quick fi hold on to something wha done finish. Mek up yuh mind, de bus a leave now now.

So we haffi come go find weself a next ride, and we ben get in wid a fella heading Lethem way inna e truck. We tink we lucky till de driver decide fi tek a shortcut e swear e know good, and next thing we deh lost pon some track inna de bush fi two hour, back and forth, turning round pon we own self, before we find we way back to de main trail. By dat time me nuh have no more temper fi argue wid nobody, me just tired, tired straight to me bone, wet clothes dry up hard pon we body, dust cake up on top a dat, hungry bad, and me and me pardner just sit up in dat truck bed not saying one word to one another, only watching de tree dem pass, too tired even fi vex proper.

Dat was de lowest point, me tell yuh straight, sitting up in dat truck bed inna de dark, no talk, no laugh, just waiting fi mawnin come.

But we luck ben turn, because just before daybreak we buck up pon a Land Cruiser wid two seat free, gwine straight Lethem, no more stop. De driver was a friend a de truck fella, and e tek we on nuh question asked, and dat vehicle fly, mi tell yuh, like it know we done wid dis road already.

We reach Lethem when de sun already up good, nine hour after de time we suppose to reach de evening befo. De two a we, dust up head to foot, clothes wrinkle up like paper, eye red, barely able fi stand up straight, and de first ting we do soon as we foot touch de ground, we jus buss out laugh, because wha else lef fi do. Nine hour late, one lost bag, one broke-dung bus, one wrong turn, fifty gone we never plan fu, and one argument we still a gyaff bout to dis day, but we mek it. And every time me tell dis story now me haffi laugh, though God knows dat night nuh have one ting funny bout it when it was gwine on.
