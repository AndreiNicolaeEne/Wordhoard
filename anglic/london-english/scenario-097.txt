Phone never stopped, people coming through the yard morning to night, someone's auntie doing tea for forty man, someone else on the phone to the crematorium, forms and forms and more forms, and you're signing your name on stuff you can't even read proper coz your eyes won't focus. Swear down, I thought sumfin was wrong wiv me, that I wasn't sad enough. Nah. Just no space.

Then everyone goes home. That's the bit nobody tells you about. Everyone gassed to help for like a week, casseroles and hugs and "call me anytime", and then one evening it's just you and the house and the door's shut and it's gone dead quiet. First time I was properly alone in there after, I swear the silence had a weight to it, like it was sat on my chest. No telly could touch it. That's when it actually landed, not at the funeral, not wiv all the people, after, when there weren't nuffin left between me and it.

Body went mad after that an' all. First month was the maddest one though, coz the world just kept moving, didn't it. People back at work moaning about their commute, adverts on the telly, someone cracking jokes on the bus like nuffin happened. And you're stood there thinking, do you lot not know? How is everyone just carrying on. That messed wiv my head more than the actual sad bits some days, just how fast everything closes back over, like water.

I remember exactly where I was standing an' everything, and I just went. Funny how it's never the big date that gets you first, it's the nothing day.

Months on from that, it don't even need a reason. A smell off someone's coat on the tube, some daft little object in a shop window, and bam, wave comes right out of nowhere. No pattern to it whatsoever, that's the bit that used to scare me, coz you can't brace for sumfin you can't predict.

Middle stretch of it, though, that's the long bit nobody warns you about neither. Not even the sharp sad, just a fog, innit, dulling everything down, even the days that were meant to be good ones. Birthdays, nice weather, mates trying to get you out, all of it came through this grey film. Longest chapter by a mile.
