The first: Bloody knees again, that's my lot. Been at me since ninety-three, dull achin' right be'ind the cap, worse when it's damp out, and round 'ere it's always damp, innit.

The second: Knees, leave it out, I'd 'ave your knees any day of the week and give you change. It's the hip does me in of a night. Three, four nights a month I'm up and down like a bleedin' yo-yo, can't get comfy whichever way I turn, end up wiv a cup of tea at four in the mornin', telly on wiv the sound off. That's my life now, that is.

The third: You two don't know you're born, God's honest truth. My back's been givin' me gyp since before either of you two knew me, forty year now if it's a day, started as a little twinge and now it's moved in permanent, like a lodger what won't pay rent.

The first: Forty year? Leave it out, you're 'aving a laugh.

The second: Go on then, who's got it worst out the three of us, be 'onest for once in your life.

The third: Me, obviously. Weren't that a stupid question.

The first: You always say that, every single time we 'ave this.

The second: My nights though, three, four a month, that's proper sufferin', not just a moan.

The third: Try forty year of it, love, then come and tell me about your little nights.

The first: Taking the mick now, ain't he.

The second: He's always taking the mick, that one, been doing it fifty year and more.

The third: Can't 'elp meself. Worst back in the whole borough, that's me, official, and nobody's arguin'.

The first: Porkies, the lot of it, from the both of you.

The second: 'Ere, whose round is it then anyway.

The third: Yours, seein' as you've got all that sleep going spare.

The first: Gawd, the state of us, listen to us.
