Me: Ohh you ain't heard the fox story yet. Rah, sit down then, this one's long, but it's a good one, you get me. Right, so.

First time anyone said anything about it, it was the old boy who lives up the road, coming back from the pub proper late one night, going round telling everyone he's seen this thing down by the bins, big, orange, just staring at him dead in the eye. The other half saying nah, that can't happen, can't have some wild ting roaming about where there's kids and that, someone needs to sort it out proper, deal with it before it's too late.

Then the whole ting turned peak when someone's creps went missing off the step where they's left out drying, and turned up the next morning dragged halfway down the road, chewed to bits, done for. And obviously everyone's pointing straight at the fox, no question asked, even though nobody actually seen it happen with their own eyes. Could've been anything if I'm being real, could've been next door's dog, could've been some kids messing about, but nah, man had already decided it was the fox and that was the end of that conversation. Argued about it for days. No proof either side, but try telling that to people once their mind's made up.

So there's a whole street meeting called after that, everyone piled into the hall, and rah, it got heated, bruv. One side shouting we need a trap down, get pest control involved, sort it before it does something worse than take someone's creps. Other side shouting allow it man, it ain't done nothing dangerous, you're all being dramatic over some shoes. Voices going up, people talking over each other, someone nearly walked straight out over it. Proper par how it got, grown adults arguing like it's a war over one fox.

Since then, road's changed a few little things about. Bins have all got straps and clips on now so it can't go tipping them over no more. It's just the fox now, the one that's always been about, long before any of it was ever a big deal, and long after everyone stopped caring it was even a story worth getting worked up over.
