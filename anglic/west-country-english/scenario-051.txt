It were properly dimpsy out, sat on the step with a can of zider between us, when the talk turned to what we'd do if some money just landed on us out the blue, no reason, no warning.

Me: First thing I'd get is a new pair of daps, mind, then straight down the club for a proper session.

My mate: Get you, you'd have it gone in a week, babber. Gert daft plan, that is. I'd get the car sorted, one that idn't held together with gaffer tape, 'cause her's on her last legs as it is.

Me: Boring. Where's the fun got to in that, then?

My mate: Fun's dreckly, my luvver, when the roof's caved in and you're stood there in your fancy daps with no motor to your name.

Me: Tis my money though, idn't it, I'll do as I like.

My mate: You do go on the same every time, and every time you're skint again by dimpsy on the Friday.

Me: Alright, alright. Say we put half away safe, and keep the rest for us.

My mate: Now that's proper job. Half away, half for a laugh.

Me: I still reckon we ought build a boat and sail off round the world with it, mind.

My mate: You're mazed, you are, proper teasy in the head. You gets seasick on the ferry over to Wales.

Me: True enough. Wisht old idea, that.

My mate: Half away it is then. Rest on a caravan down the coast, bit of scrumping in they orchards backalong like we used to as kids.

Me: Proper, that'll do us.

My mate: Mind you don't go telling the whole street though, else they'll all want a share.

Me: See, that's the only sensible bit sorted, anyhow.
