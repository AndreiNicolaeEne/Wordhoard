I goes to put me daps up on the table and the eldest near has a fit.

The eldest: Don't ee put they up there, my luvver, tis gert bad luck.

Me: Give over, idn't nothing gonna happen.

The eldest: Someone's dead 'fore the year's out, true as I'm stood yer, believe it or no. Turn 'em back over and knock the table, that puts it right.

Me: Proper mazed.

Turned 'em over and knocked anyway, mind.
