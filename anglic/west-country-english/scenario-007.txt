The one counting: Ickle ockle, blue bockle, fishes in the sea, out goes she.
The one caught: Oi, that idn't fair, you do count too gert quick, do it again, mind.
Another child: You start arguin' and we go right back to the top, babber.
The one counting: (louder) Ickle ockle, blue bockle, fishes in the sea, out goes she.
The one caught: Proper stitch-up, that were.
Another child: You're it now, proper job, see.
