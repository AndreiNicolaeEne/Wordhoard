Woke up and it hit me straight off what today were, right before I'd got both eyes proper open, and my stomach went down into my daps. Swore it, di'n't I. Months back, after being asked one time too many, I swore plain as anything I'd give the whole day to it, no matter what come, and today's the day. Tis.

Didn't want to get up, not one bit of me. Lay there a good while hunting for an excuse that'd hold water and never found one, so up I got, grumbling the whole time, moaning to myself like some mazed old soul nobody could talk sense into. Whole way down there and all, muttering under my breath, why I ever said such a gert daft thing, what was I thinking of, agreeing to a whole day of it for nothing but being nagged. Felt like backalong, when I were a nipper and got caught scrumping and had to own up regardless, you don't get out of what's said just 'cause it's hard.

Her'd let that garden go to ruin summer on summer, brambles near up to the shed roof, and there I was first thing grubbing 'em out near enough bare-handed, grumbling about the length of it before I'd hardly started. Then grumbling about the timing of it and all, why her couldn't have had me out there first thing 'stead of leaving it till the sun were full up, dimpsy hours and hours off yet. Teasy as anything by dinner, and wisht with it, proper wisht, hands tore up summat shocking.

The Aunt: Where's that fork got to, my luvver? You alright there? Don't have to finish it all today.
Me: I do go on same as I said I would. Said a day, meant a day.

Got to about two, back gone, and thought, that's it, I'm off, her'll never know the difference. Then I stopped that thought dead. Swore it, though, di'n't I. Said it plain in front of the lot of 'em, and a promise idn't worth nothing if it only holds when it suits. So I picked the fork back up.

Gritted through the worst of it, the compost heap turned, the path swept, right down till it were properly dimpsy.

The Aunt: Proper job, that. Proper job.

Walked home shattered, sat down after with a zider, and it's done, mind. Whole thing, see.
