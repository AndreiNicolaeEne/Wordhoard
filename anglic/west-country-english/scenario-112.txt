The traveler: Alright my luvver, what's it to town then, afore I get in?
The driver: Twenty, and that's fair.
The traveler: Twenty? Gwan, I'll give ee twelve.
The driver: Twelve idn't proper, babber, I got to eat an' all, tis a fair way that.
The traveler: Alright, sixteen, split it down the middle.
The driver: Sixteen, gert, done. Hop in then, mind yer head.
The traveler: Proper job.
