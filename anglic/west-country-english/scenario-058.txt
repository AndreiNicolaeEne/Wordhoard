Me: Ere, you got a minute? This roof of mine's proper doin my head in.
The neighbour: Come in then babber, what's gone on.
Me: It's over the back bedroom, right over where the chimney breast is. Tis worse when the wind's up off the wrong way, her's been leakin something chronic. I do put a bucket under it most nights now, and the ceilin's gone all soft and bubbled where it's got in.
The neighbour: Have you said anything to him bout it?
Me: Said it twice already. First time I told him, he said he'd get a man up there, no trouble, said it proper casual like it weren't nothin. Second time, same story, word for word near enough, "I'll get someone up," and still nothin done.
The neighbour: How long's all this been goin on then, start to finish?
Me: Gettin on four month now. First time he said a fortnight, no more.
The neighbour: Right. Well, you got a few ways you could go bout it. Write it down and send it him proper, so he can't say after he never knew. Or ring the council, they'll come out and make him do it, but that's slow, and he might get teasy with you once they're on him. Or hold some rent back till it's mended, only mind, that idn't without risk, he could try and turn you out for it even though he shouldn't.
Me: Don't fancy a row with him.
The neighbour: I know babber. I had a landlord backalong done just the same, water comin in over my kitchen, kept promisin and promisin till I was fair mazed with it. Wrote him one letter in the end, said the council next if he didn't shift, and he had a man round within the week, made a proper job of it too.
Me: I'll try that first then, get it in writin.
The neighbour: That's it my luvver. Do that, and if he comes back with more promises and nothin to show, straight to the council. Mind you keep a copy. See.
