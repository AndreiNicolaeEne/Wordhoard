I were out the back hanging washing when I seen it first, just gone dinner time, this thin grey line up over the hill, miles off it looked, and I thought nothing of it, just watched it a bit like you do of an afternoon. Tis always summat burning somewhere this time of year. Sat there with me cuppa watching it, no wind to speak of, proper lazy it was.

Then within the hour the wind got up something wicked and that smoke come rolling down quick, and me eyes started stinging something chronic, streaming down me face, and I thought, right, this idn't right no more.

The neighbour: You seeing this then?
Me: I am, my luvver, and I don't like the look of it, it's coming on gert fast now.
The neighbour: Reckon we ought to go?
Me: Reckon we ought.

Twenty minutes I were out that door, didn't take nothing but what mattered, chucked it all in the car, never even laced me daps proper, near enough out in me socks. Throat gone raw as sandpaper by the time I got in the car, and the heat pressing on me skin like an oven door left open, proper frightening it were. Looked back once as I pulled away and the whole hill were lit up orange, gert big glow of it climbing up the sky. Mind you don't look too long at a thing like that, it gives you the wisht feeling right through you.

Two nights we was away, stopping in with the sister, not knowing nothing, mazed with worry the pair of us, teasy as anything, everyone on the phone asking have you heard nothing yet, and there weren't nothing to hear.

Then word come it were out, and we got back in, and there it all were, standing same as backalong, not a mark on it, spared proper. Could have cried right there in the road, I could. See, you don't know what you got till you'm stood there thinking it's gone up in smoke, and mind, that were the longest two nights of me life.
