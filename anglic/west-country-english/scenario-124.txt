I was later leaving than I meant to be, and by the time I got out the door twas already proper dark, dimpsy long gone and none of that blue left in the sky at all. Normal I do go the quick way home, through by the allotments where there idn't hardly a light the whole stretch along, but I hadn't got far down the road when I got this feeling, someone behind me, footsteps landing about the same time as mine, like we was walking in step on purpose.

Didn't turn round, not straight off. Slowed up a bit first, casual like, just to see, and they slowed an' all. Picked it back up quicker after and blow me if the steps behind didn't pick up too, same as before, near enough exact. That done it for the allotments. I went the long way instead, up by the main road where there's shops still open and cars going past, proper busy compared to my usual cut, and I thought right, better that way tonight.

I did let myself one look back, just the once, by the bus stop, wondering where they was to exactly, and there was someone there alright, far off though, could've been anyone, could've been nobody to do with me at all. Told me nothing either way, that.

Last bit home I went at a right old pace, faster than I'd ever own up to normal, daps slapping the pavement hard, half mazed with it by then. Got in, door shut, bolted it, and stood there a minute getting my breath back.

Never did find out what that was about, mind. Nothing ever come of it, and I never seen a soul to ask. But I still tell it now half laughing at myself, my luvver, how gert sure I was that summat was coming, when backalong it was probably nothing at all, just some other poor soul wanting to get in out the dark same as I did, see.
