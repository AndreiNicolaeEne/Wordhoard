The niece: Aunt, don't mind knockin' proper, her's 'ad it!
The aunt: Never! When was this now?
The niece: Just gone six this mornin', gert quick an' all.
The aunt: Is her alright, and the babby?
The niece: Proper job, the two of 'em. Third one now, idn't it.
The aunt: Three under one roof! Right, coat on, we'm off up there now, not dreckly.
The niece: That's it, she's waitin' on us, mind.
