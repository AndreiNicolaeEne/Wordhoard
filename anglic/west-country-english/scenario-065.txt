The neighbour: Ere, my luvver, your goat's out, gone up past the hedge, gert lick on 'er, headin' straight for the road!

The other: Ess never, not again! Which way'd 'er go to?

The neighbour: Up by the gap where the fence come down. You cut 'er off afore the top, I'll block the gap so 'er can't come back through.

The other: Right y'are, I'm gone. Mind that road, mind!

The neighbour: Go on quick then, 'fore 'er's out proper.
