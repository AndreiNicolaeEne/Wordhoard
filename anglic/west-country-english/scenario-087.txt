Went in to work today and everyone wanted to know how the night went, so here's how it was.

Come dimpsy yesterday evening I clocked her off her feed, wouldn't touch the bowl, and moving all wrong, hunched up funny in the corner of the run. I said, right, I idn't leaving her out there on her own tonight, whatever anyone reckons, and brung her in by the fire.

First hour or two, nothing changed at all. Just me sat there watching her, and her looking back at me, wisht little thing. Tried getting a drop of water into her, coaxing, holding the bowl right up under her chin, but she wouldn't have none of it, turned her head off every time.

Nan rang about midnight to ask how she was doing.

Nan: Her still poorly then?
Me: Her idn't touched water nor nothing, Nan.
Nan: You do keep her warm, mind, and don't you go to sleep on her.

Round three she took a proper bad turn, breathing all wrong, gone mazed looking about the eyes, and I thought, that's it, I've gone and done wrong keeping her home 'stead of running her down the vet's this time of night. Gert wave of it come over me, worry like I've not had in years.

Stayed right there anyway, kept her wrapped up warm, talked to her low the way you would to calm anything frightened, on and on, nothing important, just the voice of it.

Near dawn her ear give a little twitch at a noise out the back, first normal thing all night, and her breathing come easier after. By full morning she was steadier. Not right yet, mind, but steadier, see.

Only once I was sure of that did I go to pieces myself, shook right through, sat there on the floor with her.

Workmate: Alright my luvver, rough one then?
Me: Proper job in the end. Backalong at three this morning I weren't sure of that at all. Proper glad of a drop of zider once she settled, mind.
