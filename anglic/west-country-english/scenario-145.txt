Babber: What has got a gert big face and two hands and never lifts a finger, then?
My luvver: A statue, up the green?
Babber: Nah, idn't that. Tis the clock, mind.
My luvver: Ah, proper job. Right, my turn now. What do go up the chimney down but can't go down the chimney up?
Babber: Dunno, my luvver, give us it.
My luvver: Tis a brolly, see.
Babber: Proper job, that's a good'n. Go on then, one more round.
