I do call in the tailor's most Tuesdays after work regular, and went round straight off to fetch it, all done up nice on the hanger, and knew straight off summat idn't right.

Me: Ere now, this idn't what I ordered.

The tailor: 'Course it is, my luvver, that's exactly what we agreed. Come and have a proper look.

Me: I have had a proper look. Three buttons and a straight hem is what I asked for, not two buttons and that angled thing you got going on there.

The tailor: No, no, I'm sure we said two buttons, angled hem, looks gert smart like that.

Me: I'm telling you now, tis three buttons and straight hem, never an angle to it, never have wanted one.

The tailor: Well hang about then, I'll go and check to the book, see what her says.

Me: You do that, cos I idn't paying for summat I never asked for, and it's got me proper teasy just looking at it.

The tailor went off round the back with the ledger and come back a minute after, turning the pages.

The tailor: Right, here 'tis... ah. You're right, babber, look, three buttons, straight hem, wrote down plain as day. Must've got yours mixed in with another job backalong, my mistake proper.

Me: Thought as much. So what we doing about it then?

The tailor: Give us two days and I'll have her altered proper, buttons on and the hem straightened right out. Come back Wednesday and she'll be sat here waiting for you.

Me: Proper job. Mind you get her right this time.

The tailor: I will, don't you fret, my luvver. See you Wednesday, mind.

Me: See you then.
