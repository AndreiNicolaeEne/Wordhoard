Week I minded that house for they, I do go round that way most days anyway so I said I'd have it, no bother, her said. Should've known, weren't no bother my backside. One thing after another, whole seven days, start to finish.

First night, soon as I put the key in, summat wasn't right. Heating clicking on and off like it had a mind of its own, and no dog barking when I come in, which idn't like him at all, he do bark at anything, that one, postman, wind, his own shadow. Found him hid under the table looking proper wisht, ears flat back, like he'd seen something out the garden. Had a look round with the torch, nothing there far as I could tell, but I didn't sleep easy that first night, kept hearing the boiler tick and thinking it were footsteps.

Second day the animal got out. Left the back gate open not five minutes while I brought the bins round, and he were gone up the road before I'd even turned round. Proper panic that was, running up and down calling him, half the street out looking, me still in me daps from doing the garden. Old girl two doors up found him in the end, sat in her front bed eating her pansies like nothing happened.

Neighbour: Her's out again, is her, your one?
Me: He idn't mine, I'm only minding, but yeah, he got out.
Neighbour: Well he's stopped eating my pansies now, look, he's had the lot.

Took about the hour all told, felt like the whole day, and he'd gone all teasy with me after, sulking behind the sofa like it were my fault he run off.

Garden started going a bit off from about the Tuesday. Little thing at first, leaves gone yellow on the tomatoes, and I thought, well, it'll be the heat, give it a drop more water. By Thursday the whole lot was drooping and there was these little white flies all up in gert clouds when you shook the plant, and I hadn't a clue what to do with it, so I rung her.

Me: There's flies all over your tomatoes, whole cloud of them comes up when you touch it.
Owner: Oh no, that's whitefly, you want to get some of that soapy spray on, there's a bottle under the sink somewhere.

Found the bottle, gave it a good going over, but by then half the leaves had gone that far I don't reckon there weren't much saving them. Felt bad about that, I did.

Washing machine went and packed in on the Wednesday and all, right in the middle of a cycle, water everywhere over the kitchen floor. I'm on my knees with a towel thinking, right, this is it, they're coming home to a flooded kitchen and a dead machine and it'll be all down to me. Turned out it were only the drain hose come loose off the back, but I didn't know that at the time, I was proper mazed, on the phone to her practically in tears over it.

Thursday dinnertime, knock at the door and it's her sister, turned up out of nowhere, no warning at all.

Visitor: Alright my luvver, didn't mean to give you a fright, only I was up this way anyway, thought I'd pop in.
Me: No, no, you're alright, come on in, kettle's just gone on.

Had to sit there in me painting clothes with the dog going mental at her and make small talk over a cup of tea I never asked to be making, hour and a half that took out my afternoon.

Same week I near enough had a heart attack over the fish and all. Went to feed him Friday morning and couldn't see him nowhere in the tank, water gone all cloudy from where I'd overdone the food the day before. Stood there going, where's he to, where's he to, tapping the glass like a fool, thinking, that's it, I've killed the fish, how am I meant to tell them that. Turned the tank light off and on about six times looking before I found him hid down behind the little castle ornament, right as rain, not a care in the world. Proper relief, I tell you, I sat down after on the sofa proper shook, went and had a zider to settle me nerves, I'm not ashamed to say it.

Saturday I locked meself out. Popped out the front for two minutes to put summat in the recycling and the door swung to behind me, and course the keys was sat on the side in the kitchen where I'd left them like a proper fool. Stood there a good few hours on the step, tried every window, tried the back and all, nothing doing, in the end had to sit on next door's wall till her workmate come round with the spare set the owners had left with her.

Neighbour: You been sat there long, babber?
Me: About three hour now, if you can believe it.
Neighbour: Proper daft, that. Mind you don't leave it in the door next time, eh.

Rain got in the spare room and all. I'd left the little window open upstairs on account of the damp smell in there, forgot about it clean, and we had that downpour Sunday night, come in all down the sill and soaked the carpet under it good and proper. Spent the Monday with towels down and the heater going trying to dry it out before it turned to mould.

Noise in the night woke me the Monday too, this banging out the back, proper loud, had me up out of bed with the torch again thinking somebody's in the garden. Turned out it were only the gate, catch had worked loose and it were swinging and banging in the wind, but I stood there a good while before I worked that out, heart going like the clappers, dog barking his head off behind me for once when I could've done with him keeping quiet.

By Tuesday, next to last day, I'd had enough of that gate going all hours, so I found a bit of rope in the shed and tied it back proper, wedged a brick against it and all for good measure. Sorted that one at least, proper job, held the whole rest of the week after that, not a peep out of it.

Last day was all tidying, mad panic tidying, hoovering round, getting the towels off the radiator, doing the windows where the dog had had his nose all up them, mowing what was left of the lawn, trying to make the tomatoes look less like they'd been through the wars, putting a few bedding plants in over where the pansies had gone, the lot. Some of the neighbour's kids had been round and all, scrumping what apples was left off the tree down the end while I weren't looking, but I let that go, wasn't worth starting on with everything else. Ran round like a headless chicken all afternoon, dimpsy coming down outside and me still going with the hoover indoors.

When they got in that evening I just come out with the whole of it, one after another, didn't hold nothing back.

Me: Look, I got to tell you now before you find out yourselves. Dog got out the second day but he were back within the hour. Machine packed in Wednesday but 'twas only the hose come off. Tomatoes have gone over with the whitefly, I done what I could for them. Your sister come round Thursday, she's alright, I made her a tea. Fish gave me a proper fright Friday but he's fine, he were only hid behind the castle. I locked meself out Saturday for about three hour on the step. Rain got in the spare room Sunday night through the window, I've had towels on it since. And the back gate were banging half of Monday night till I got out there and tied it off.
Owner: Blimey. Anything else?
Me: No, that's the lot. House is standing, animal's alive, and I idn't never house-sitting again long as I live.
Owner: Ah, don't be like that, you done alright by the sound of it, considering.
Me: Proper knackered, mind. But 'tis all sorted now, see, nothing left broke that I couldn't fix or fess up to.

And that were the week, backalong now but I still think on it every time somebody asks me to mind a place. Gert long week that were, and I'm not joking. Never again quite so quick to say yes, mind.
