Wait now till yous hear the rest of it. Sure I was up at half six and I wasn't due in til nine at all, standing below in the kitchen with the tea gone stone cold in me hand, the nerves eating the head off me. Left the house desperate early anyway, walked round by the corner and back twice so I wouldn't be the eejit standing outside the door before anyone had the shutters even up.

Himself that runs the place came down to meet me. "How's yourself, you're grand, follow me so," and that was the whole of the induction, in the door and straight into it. Handed me the till first off, said sure it'd be nothing to it, only I hadn't a clue what half the buttons were for. More than I was braced for if I'm honest, I thought I'd be shadowing someone the whole morning and I was after being left on me own by eleven, sink or swim.

Made a right hames of an order in front of the whole floor and all, gave a fella his change back to front. Yer man only laughed. "Ah you're grand, dat happens to everyone, don't be minding it."

At the break I was kilt with the cold above in the stockroom, sitting back sizing up the rest of them. This one does be giving out about the manager the minute his back's turned, another one acting the maggot over nothing at all, and there's a culchie beside me above, in from Kerry, amn't I only after starting meself and already I'd say she has notions. But there was one, the eldest of them on the floor, came over without me asking a thing and showed me the trick with the till that has everyone else banjaxed.

The eldest: You're after doing that the hard way altogether. Come here to me and I'll show you.

Pace of it was fierce faster than I'd braced for, no standing about at all, and the nine hours went by desperate quick, I never looked at the clock til it said five to.

Walking home after, there's a grand stretch in the evenings now, so I'd a bit of light to be going over the whole of it in me head, the till, the mistake, the lot.

Sitting down with them tonight I told them straight.

Me: Honest to God, it was deadly.

The mother: Was it hard going, love?

Me: Fierce busy. But grand. I'll manage.

The father: Are you going back in the morning?

Me: I am. I will so.
