The tailor: How's yourself. I have it hanging up for you there, all finished.

Me: (holding it up) Hold on now. This isn't what I asked you for at all. Three buttons and a straight hem, that's what I said. This has two buttons on it and the hem's gone off at an angle.

The tailor: Sure that's exactly what we agreed between us, love, I have it written down and everything.

Me: I do not think so. I'm dead sure it's three buttons I asked for, straight across, for the removal on Friday. I wouldn't be getting an angled hem done, no way.

The tailor: Would you look at that now. I'll check the docket so, hold on a second.

The tailor: Right, I have the measurements here from the first day itself... and God, you're right, it says three buttons plain as day on the ticket. Yer man that does the cutting must've read it as two, I don't know how he made such a hames of it.

Me: I knew I wasn't imagining it.

The tailor: You weren't at all, no. Desperate carry-on, that, and I'm mortified over it, I am. I wouldn't be codding you, it's our own fault, not yours.

Me: And how long would it take you to sort it, because I do be needing it for Friday, no messing.

The tailor: Two days, no bother at all. I'll have it grand for you Wednesday evening, I will so, even if I have to stop up over it myself tonight.

Me: Are you sure now, because I amn't in a position to be running back and forth to yous every hour God sends.

The tailor: I am, don't be worrying yourself over it. Give it here and I'll have himself out the back put it right, three buttons, straight hem, the way you wanted it in the first place.

Me: Grand so. I'll call back Wednesday, and it better be right this time.

The tailor: It will, don't you fear. Sure it's only a small thing in the end, we'll have you sorted.
