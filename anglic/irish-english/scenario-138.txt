Me: ...so anyway, I'm after hearing off yer one in the shop that yer man's young fella was banjaxed in a smash below at the roundabout, langers he was, and the guards had him in the cell overnight for it, and himself had to go down at all hours and bail him out, and...

The neighbour: Stop there now. That is not what happened, not even close.

Me: Is it not?

The neighbour: It is not, and I should know, I do be talking to herself above every day of the week. There was no smash at all first of all. The young fella backed the car into the wall below the flats, that's the height of it. No drink in it, none. And there was no cell, no guards even, only the fella from the insurance came out to look at the bumper and away with him again.

Me: God, I'd it all wrong so.

The neighbour: You'd it all wrong. By the time it got to you the story was after doing three laps of the estate and coming home twice the size it went out.

Me: Amn't I only saying it the way it was told to me, in fairness. Where did I even hear that, though, off her wan in the shop?

The neighbour: Ask her that yourself. I'd say she got it off whoever was in the queue behind you the Tuesday, and I'd nearly put money on it that one embellished it fierce along the way, sure that's always the way with it, one mouth to the next and it gets bigger every time.

Me: Suppose it does.

The neighbour: It's making a hames of the poor young fella's name you all are, and he only after a bang to a wall. Tell it the right way now, whoever asks you. A scrape in a car park and a call to the insurance, that's the whole of it, no more and no less.

Me: I will so.
