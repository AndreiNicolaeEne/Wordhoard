Friend: How's yourself! I was passing so I said I'd call in and see how ye're all getting on.

Mother: Grand, sure we're grand. Aren't we.

Father: We are, yeah.

Friend: Are ye getting any bit of sleep at all?

Mother: I am not, if I'm honest with you. She's up four or five times a night, five some nights, and if I get three hours in the one stretch I'd be doing well out of it.

Father: Tell her about the back as well while you're at it.

Mother: Go on so, you tell her about the hands.

Father: Me hands does be shaking with the tiredness of an evening, honest to God, I can't hold a cup of tea steady on me.

Friend: Ah, God love the two of yiz, it's wrecked the pair of ye must be. That's an awful cross to be carrying.

Mother: And I made a right hames of things yesterday and all. I'm after putting me phone in the press with the tins, don't ask me why, and I was searching the whole gaff for it till your man rang it and I heard it going off next to the beans.

Friend: Ah, you're codding me.

Mother: I amn't, God's honest truth.

Father: She'd forget her own head only it's screwed on.

Friend: Right, that settles it so. Gimme her here Saturday afternoon, the two of yiz are going for a sleep and no arguing over it. I'll bring up a bit of dinner an' all so ye needn't be cooking.

Father: Ah, we couldn't be asking you to.

Friend: Yiz didn't ask, I'm telling yiz. Two o'clock alright?

Mother: We will so. Tanks be to God for you, honestly, we're only banjaxed altogether.

Friend: Not a bother on me. Sure isn't that what I'm for.
