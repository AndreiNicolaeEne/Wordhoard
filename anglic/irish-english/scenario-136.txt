The mother: (to the room) Did anyone move the housekeeping? It does be in the press behind the tins, and I checked the tea caddy an all in case it was shifted, and it's not there either.

The eldest: Wasn't near it.

The middle one: Nor me. Amn't I after telling you I've no notion where that money does be kept half the time.

The mother: Well someone's notion of it is a hundred quid lighter, so.

The father: I'm only in the door meself, don't be looking at me.

The mother: Nobody's saying a word about you. It's yourself was above in work the whole day, sure everyone knows that.

The mother: It's only the youngest was alone in this house on Tuesday. Everyone else was out.

The mother: (turning) Did you take anything out of that press?

The youngest: I did not.

The mother: Are you sure now. No harm if you borrowed it and meant to say, that's all I want to hear.

The youngest: I said I did not.

The mother: Somebody's after taking near a hundred euro out of that house and there was only you here to do it!

The youngest: And I'm telling you it wasn't me, so stop saying it like it's a fact!

The mother: Who else was in it then, will you tell me that? The place doesn't rob itself!

The youngest: I don't care who was in it, I'm not taking the blame for something I never touched! You've some neck accusing me over money you probably mislaid yourself!

The mother: Don't you dare turn this on me in my own kitchen!

The youngest: I'm not sitting here being called a thief in me own home!

The mother: Nobody believes you, not one of us!

The youngest: Believe what you like so!
