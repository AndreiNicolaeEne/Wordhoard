The aunt: How's yourself, are you still poorly? I heard off yer wan below you'd been felled with something.

Me: Ah I'm banjaxed if I'm honest with you. Started three days back, an ache in the joints and the throat gone scratchy, and then yesterday didn't a fever land on top of it as well.

The aunt: God love you, that's fierce. Is himself minding you above, is he keeping an eye on you at all?

Me: He's trying, bless him. It's a small bit easier I am today, I'll say that much, but I'm far from right still, amn't at all myself.

The aunt: And what have you been doing about it, sure you can't be doing nothing.

Me: I'm not, don't worry. First off it was a gargle of warm salted water for the throat, that always does something. And when the fever wouldn't break off on its own I'd a cool cloth across the forehead, changing it through the night every time I'd wake.

The aunt: Are you managing to eat at all?

Me: I am, only broth and a weak drop of tea, nothing heavier than that. And I'm after sleeping whenever the body would let me, which does be less often than you'd think.

The aunt: Well you're to rest properly now, do you hear me, and not go pushing through it like an eejit making a hames of yourself entirely, or I'll give out to you something fierce.

Me: I hear you. If the fever climbs any further or the breathing turns hard on me, I'll go and be seen, but not before that. I'm not codding, I mean it.

The aunt: Will I bring you over a bit of dinner, or sit with you an hour? I've a drop of soup in the press already.

Me: Ah you needn't go to bother... go on so, an hour wouldn't go astray, I'm kilt trying to do the messages myself in this state.

The aunt: Right, I will so. Mind yourself and don't be having notions you're grand when you're not.

Me: I won't. Tanks a million. Talk to you later so.
