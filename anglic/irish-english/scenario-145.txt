Yer man: Here's one for you. What has a face and two hands, no legs on it, and it does be up on the wall watching you all night?
Yer wan: Is it the telly?
Yer man: It is not, ye eejit, it's the clock.
Yer wan: My turn so. What has teeth galore and never bit a soul?
Yer man: Tell me, I'm banjaxed.
Yer wan: The comb.
Yer man: One more so.
Yer wan: I will so.
