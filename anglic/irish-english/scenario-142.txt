Woke and for about two seconds hadn't a notion what day it was, then it landed on me like a bag of wet cement. Today's the day. Months back, worn down from being asked one time too many, I swore it plain in front of the mother and all. I'd do it, so I would, a whole day given over to helping the brother shift house, no matter what. And the second that came back to me lying there the regret near flattened me. Amn't I only after doing a full week meself, and now this. Not a bother in me wanted to get up.

Got up anyway. Legged it out the door forgetting even to check the immersion, cursing the whole way over in the car, muttering to meself about what sort of eejit swears a thing like that months in advance when he's not thinking straight at all. The traffic does be desperate round that road at the best of times. Barely in the door and I'm giving out already, boxes everywhere, the lift in the new gaff banjaxed, three flights of stairs, and himself standing there acting like it's grand.

How's yourself, says I, no enthusiasm whatsoever in it.

The brother: Good man yourself for coming.

Me: I said I would. Never said I'd enjoy it.

His mate: Are yous jackeens stoppin' for de day so, or are ye running back to town first sign of trouble?

Me: I am.

Fierce stretch of it after that. A wardrobe that wouldn't go through the door no matter what we did to it, a fridge that near kilt the two of us on them stairs, yer man from next door, the gobshite, giving out advice and doing feck all else, acting the maggot the whole time. Made an awful hames of the sofa going round the bend on the landing and had to start again. It's fed up I was by dinnertime, hadn't even time for the jacks, no craic in it whatsoever. Around three, legs gone, back gone, I near said sure that'd do, I'd done enough. Then I remembered. I swore it. No notions of quitting on me after that, none at all.

Gritted it through the worst of the afternoon, the last load in the rain, and finished the whole job proper, every box in the press where it belonged.

Himself: Tanks be to God dat's over.

Grand stretch of an evening after, sitting there knackered, on to the mother giving out about me back, but it was done. Said I would. And I did.
