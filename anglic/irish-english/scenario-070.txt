She lands in on top of me Tuesday evening, still in her work gear, and I've the kettle barely on when she starts.

The first friend: I'm after ringin' her four times and not a word back. Four times. And then I hear off yer wan below in the shop that she's callin' me two-faced. Two-faced! Sure I've done nothin' only try and sort the whole thing out meself.

Me: Right. And what started it at all?

The first friend: It's her that started it, I'm tellin' you. Always was.

I said nothin' more, only nodded, and let her away with a cup of tea in her.

Not an hour later herself arrives, banjaxed lookin', straight in the door.

The second friend: How's yourself. Did she land on you already, I'd say.

Me: She did, aye.

The second friend: Well whatever she told you, it's a different story here. It's me that got the blame for the whole hames of a thing and I wasn't even there half the time.

Me: Were you askin' to be included in it, or was it more you felt left out once it started movin' without you?

The second friend: ...I s'pose. I do be the one always sortin' things and this time it went on without me and I only heard after.

Me: Is that the real thing bothering you, more than what she said below in the shop?

The second friend: ...I'd say it is, yeah. I gave out to her for the wrong reason, if I'm honest.

Me: Would ye think about talkin' to each other, the two of ye, face to face, instead of the pair of ye comin' to me?

The second friend: I would, so. Get her to come round mine and I'll ring you after.

Me: I'll sort that, grand.
