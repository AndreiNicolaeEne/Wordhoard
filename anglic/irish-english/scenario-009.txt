The eldest: Jaysus, would you look at the head on you.
The younger: I'm after getting it done, it's savage so it is.
The eldest: Savage? Yer man off the shampoo ad had a fright and you went and copied him.
The younger: Better than yours, sure you'd frighten culchies with that mop.
The eldest: I will so tell the mother you did it yourself with the shears.
The younger: You're only codding.
The eldest: Amn't.
