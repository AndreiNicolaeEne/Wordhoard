One neighbour: Did you hear that racket out on the road last night, after midnight it was?
The other neighbour: I did, sure, near woke me stone mad. What was it at all?
One neighbour: I'd say 'twas some animal after knocking the bin over, only I'm not sure now.
The other neighbour: Could be a fox, could be yer man below banjaxed coming home from the pub, you'd never know.
One neighbour: True enough. If it happens again tonight I'll keep an ear out.
The other neighbour: I will so.
