The foreman: Grand stretch in the evening still, look at that. Right, before yiz all scarper home on me, we need tomorrow sorted, or it'll be forgot till we're standing round like eejits in the morning.

The sparky: Eight, same as always, sure.

The young fella: Ah here, not eight, I'm banjaxed as it is. Half seven at the earliest.

The eldest: Seven, and no more haggling, I've a head on me from the dust today.

The foreman: Seven so. It's the attic conversion we're on, above in the estate. Himself that owns the place wants the roof closed in before the weekend, so there's no dossing about it tomorrow.

The young fella: I'll bring the barrow and the shovels so. You're grand for the mixer?

The foreman: I am, and there's bags of sand in the back of the van already.

The sparky: I'll bring me own leads and the drill, don't be minding me for anything else.

The eldest: And who's doing the flashing, so, because I amn't doing it again on me own, I was up there Tuesday and near kilt with the wind.

The foreman: Ah you're the only one that can do it right, sure, don't be acting the maggot on me.

The eldest: It does be three hours every time and yous all know it. I'll do it so, but yiz owe me a good few pints after.

The sparky: There's fierce rain forecast for the afternoon and all, so if it's not done by twelve it's a hames of the whole day.

The foreman: We'll manage. Are we all sound then, where's yer man the block-layer?

The young fella: He's not coming, the eejit's after picking up a start on another job.

The foreman: Grand, we'll manage without him so. Are you right for seven?

The eldest: I am. And yiz better not be late on me, or I'm doing the flashing meself and charging the lot of yiz double.

The foreman: You're only codding.

The eldest: I am not, but sure I will so. Seven it is, and good craic tomorrow if the weather holds.
