Right, I'll rokker you how it went, this'll make you laugh. Needed a paper stamped down the council office, the bit that lets us pull onto the atchin tan proper, not just parked up rough. Had to have it that same divvus an' all, cos the site was full up otherwise and we'd be jalling on in the rain with the chavvies half frozen in the back of the wagon, no atchin tan for us that night.

So I'm stood there at the counter and this fella's looking through the forms ever so slow, sucking his teeth, going "these things can take a while to process, you know, unless..." and just leaves it hanging there like that. Didn't say nothing after the unless. I just dicked him like I never heard it, asked him again what time he reckoned it'd be done by. Kushti actor I am when I want to be.

He tries it again, a bit plainer this time, summat about "some people find it moves quicker if there's a little something for the trouble," looking at me proper this time, waiting.

I says to him, straight-faced, oh don't you worry, I know the rules on this, my sister-in-law's fella works two offices down and he told me this exact form's meant to be same-day, no charge, council's own leaflet says so. And if it's not doable today I'll just jal on back Thursday, no bother to me, got nowhere I need to be.

Tense old minute that, mush, him just looking at his own paper like it'd changed colour. Could've gone any way. Then he just stamps the thing, slides it under the glass, not a word more out of him.

Got it done, didn't pay a penny, and I always tell the young ones this one, you don't need to chor nothing off no gorja and you don't need to hand nothing over neither, you just stand there dinlo-faced long enough and let the wonga stay in your own pocket where it belongs.
