The traveller: 'Scuse me, mush, which way's the market, please?

The stranger: First left past the chapel, that's your turn. Then dick the corner with the big clock, keep that on your right, and you're close.

The traveller: How long's that on foot, would you say?

The stranger: Quarter hour, no more, walking steady.

The traveller: Kushti, thank you kindly.

The stranger: No bother, mush. Safe on your way now.
