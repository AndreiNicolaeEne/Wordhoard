The cousin: You alright in there? Heard off the aunt you ain't been kushti this last few divvus, tell me straight, how bad is it really.

Me: I ain't dying if that's what you're asking, but I ain't right neither. Started three divvus back, ache right through the joints like I bin dragged behind the grai all night, and me throat scratchy as owt. Yesterday the fever come on top of that, proper burning up I was, couldn't get comfortable no way.

The cousin: And today, any better?

Me: Bit easier today than yesterday, won't lie, but still miles off right. Head's thick as anything, legs gone to water.

The cousin: What you doing about it then, you ain't just sat there letting it rokker through you I hope.

Me: Course not. First off, warm salt water, gargle it good for the throat, few times a divvus. Fever weren't breaking on its own proper so I had a cool cloth across me head, changing it through the night when it went warm again. Kept to broth and a weak drop of tea, nothing heavy on the belly. Slept whenever me body let me, which weren't a lot of it if I'm honest.

The cousin: You want to rest proper then, not go pushing through it thinking you're some hard mush. You'll only drag it out longer that way.

Me: I know, I know, I ain't stupid. If that fever climbs any higher, or me breathing goes hard on me, I'll jal down and get seen, no messing about. But not before that, I ain't sitting in there for hours over an ache and a cough.

The cousin: I'll bring some hobben over if you want, or sit with you an hour, keep you company through it.

Me: Don't fuss yourself over it, love, I'm alright on me own... go on then, an hour won't hurt nobody, bring us something if you're passing.
