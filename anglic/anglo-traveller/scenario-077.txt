Me: Now then, mush, what's put that grin on you?

My friend: You won't believe it. Money's landed, out the blue, this morning.

Me: No! Get away, that's kushti, that is, really kushti!

My friend: Straight up, no word of a lie.

Me: How much we on about, roughly like?

My friend: Just over four grand, all in one go.

Me: Four grand... how's that even come about then, sudden as that?

My friend: Some old claim finally paid out, years it took.

Me: Must be nice. Some of us are still grafting every divvus just to keep the vardo on the road and the chavvies fed, and it just falls in your lap.

My friend: You've gone quiet on me. Summat wrong?

Me: Nothing wrong with you, mush.

My friend: Go on, out with it.

Me: Alright then, since you're asking straight - it's envy, plain and simple, nothing worse than that. Seeing it land on you and me still counting out for hobben and diesel, it does sting a bit, I won't lie.

My friend: Fair play to you for saying it to my face.

Me: I mean it though, I am glad for you, proper glad. You've had it hard as anyone I know, so if it's dropped on somebody it might as well be you. Put the kettle on, we'll have a rokker about what you're doing with it.

My friend: Kushti. Chavvies'll want new coats before the cold sets in, and about time an' all.

Me: There you go then, mush. Kushti divvus for you today, and no dinlo's going to tell me different.
