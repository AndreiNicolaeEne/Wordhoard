Me: ...so I heard it off the atchin tan up by the crossroads, they're not letting nobody stop there no more, on account of the young mush getting in a right barney with the gavver, knocked him clean on his back in the road, and they had to send two more cars out before it was done...

The eldest: Now hold up a minute. That is not what happened. Not even close.

Me: No?

The eldest: No. There was no barney and nobody got knocked down. What happened is the gavver come round asking after a chor'd battery off somebody's trailer, nothing to do with the mush at all, and he stood there calm as anything and told him straight he never touched it. That's the whole of it, start to finish. One gavver, one question, no cars, no fighting, no nothing.

Me: I heard two of them dragged him off in the van.

The eldest: Dragged him nowhere. He went home after, had his hobben, same divvus as any other. The only true bit in what you've been told is there was a gavver there at all.

Me: So how far off is it then, really.

The eldest: Far enough off it's not the same story no more, only the same man's name stuck on it.

Me: Where'd you even hear it, that version.

The eldest: Where'd you hear it.

Me: Off my workmate, down the yard.

The eldest: And she got it off who.

Me: Her sister, I think. Or her sister's mort that's up near the atchin tan.

The eldest: There you are then. Comes down from the atchin tan to the sister to your workmate to you, and along the way it's grown two more gavvers and a fight that never was. Trace it one step further and you'll find whoever first opened their mouth on it was stood twenty yards off and never dick'd a thing proper.

Me: Fair enough.

The eldest: So mind you tell it the true way from here. One gavver, one question about a battery, mush never laid a finger on him, walked home and had his tea. Nothing more to it than that, whoever asks you.
