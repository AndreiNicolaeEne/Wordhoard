Me: Two short here, love. Seven was due back and only five come to me hand.
The one at the counter: Never made no mistake, counted it out meself, proper and all.
Me: Dick again then, two of your wonga's missing, five sat there where seven should be.
The one at the counter: (counting again) ...comes up short right enough. (hands it over, grudging) There, take your two, and count quicker next time yourself.
