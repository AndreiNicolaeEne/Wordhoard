Woke up this morning and it hit me straight off what divvus this was. Months back I swore blind, in front of half the atchin tan practically, that I'd give the man a full day sorting that scrap heap out proper, top to bottom, no messing, and today's the divvus I said it'd be. First thing I thought was, what a dinlo I was to open me mouth like that. Lay there a good five minutes not moving, wishing I'd never rokkered a word of it to no one.

Got up anyway. Didn't want to, not one bit of me wanted to, but a mush's word is a mush's word round here, kushti or not, and I'd said it plain enough for the lot of them to hear.

Walking down there I was grumbling the whole way, muttering to meself like a right dinlo, going on about who asks a fella to sort a whole tip in one divvus, and why I ever let that come out me mouth in the first place.

The eldest: You still on about that scrap?

Me: Told him I'd do it and I'll do it. Don't mean I got to like it.

Hours in, back aching, hands black to the wrist, and it's nowhere near done. Kept dicking at the pile thinking there's no end to it at all. Right point where I could've just jalled off home and no juk would've barked at me for it, I stopped meself dead. Said it out loud even, to no one but meself. Swore it, didn't I. That was that, no more thinking on it.

Ground through the worst of it past dinner, hobben forgotten, sun gone hot and horrible on the back of me neck.

My friend: Still at it, mush?

Me: Nearly.

Finished the whole lot, every last bit, exactly as I said I would, not a corner left for the gorja to point at and say a traveller's word's worth nothing. Walked home moaning the entire way back same as I come down, but moaning's all it ever was.
