Younger neighbor: Kushti divvus this, in't it.

The elder: 'Tis. Better than Tuesday, that was proper cold, rain right through, couldn't hardly get the washing dry.

Younger neighbor: Mm, that it was, bitter that day.

The elder: Puts me in mind of a summer years back, dry as this one, chavvies playing out round the atchin tan till it near went dark and nobody fretting.

Younger neighbor: You reckon it holds tomorrow, then?

The elder: Might cloud over come morning, but I'd call it kushti again.
