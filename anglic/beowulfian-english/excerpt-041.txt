7 þæs ymbe .xiiii. niht gefeaht æþered cyning 7 Ælfred his broþor wið þone here æt Basingum, 7 þær þa Dæniscan sige namon.
7 þæs ymb twegen monþas gefeaht æþered cyning 7 Ælfred his broþor wið þone here æt Meredune, 7 hi wæron on twam gefylcum, 7 hi butu geflymdon 7 lange on dæg sige ahton, 7 þær wearð mycel wælslyht on gehwæðre hond, 7 þa Dæniscan ahton wealstowe geweald, 7 þær wearð Heahmund biscop ofslægen 7 feola godra manna.
