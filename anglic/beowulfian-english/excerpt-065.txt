Her for se ilca here innan Myrce to Snotengaham 7 þær wintersetl namon.
7 Burhred Myrcna cing 7 his witan bædon Æþered Wessexena cing 7 Ælfred his broðor þæt hi him gefultumedon þæt hi wið þone here gefuhton.
7 ða ferdan hi mid Wessexena fyrde innan Myrce oþ Snotingaham 7 þone here ðær gemetton on þam geweorce 7 hine inne besæton, 7 þær nan hefelic gefeoht ne wearð, 7 Myrce frið namon wið þone here.
