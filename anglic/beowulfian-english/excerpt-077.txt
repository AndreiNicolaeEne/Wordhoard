Of Ealdseaxon comon Eastsexa 7 Suðsexa 7 WestSexan.
Of Angle comon, se a siððan stod westi betwyx Iutum 7 Seaxum, Eastengla, Midelangla, Mearca 7 ealle Norðhymbra.
Her Hengest 7 Horsa fuhton wiþ Wyrtgeorne þam cyninge, in
þære stowe þe is gecueden Agęlesþrep, 7 his broþur Horsan
man ofslog; 7 æfter þam Hengest feng to rice 7 Æsc his sunu.
