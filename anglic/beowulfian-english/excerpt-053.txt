Her Æþelstan cyning 7 Sihtric Norðhymbra cyng heo gesamnodon æt Tameweorðþige .iii.
Kalendas Februarius, 7 Æþelstan his sweostor him forgeaf.
Her oðeowdon fyrena leoman on norðdæle þære lyfte.
7 Sihtric acwæl, 7 Æþelstan cyning feng to Norðhymbra rice.
7 ealle þa cyngas þe on þyssum iglande wæron he gewylde, ærest Huwal Westwala cyning, 7 Cosstantin Scotta cyning, 7 Uwen Wenta cyning, 7 Ealdred Ealdulfing from Bebbanbyrig, 7 mid wedde 7 mid aþum fryþ gefæstnodon on þære stowe þe genemned is æt Eamotum on .iiii.
