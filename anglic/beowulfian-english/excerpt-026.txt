Her on þis tima leot Ceolred abbot of Medeshamstede 7 þa munecas Wulfrede to hande þet land of Sempigaham to þet forewearde þet æfter his dæi scolde þet land into þe minstre, 7 Wulfred scolde gifen þet land of Sliowaforda into Medeshamstede, 7 he scolde gife ilca gear into þe minstre sixtiga foðra wuda 7 twælf foður græfan 7 sex foður gearda 7 twa tunnnan fulle hlutres aloð 7 twa slægnæt 7 sex hund hlafes 7 ten mittan Wælsces aloð 7 ilca gear an hors 7 þrittiga scillinga 7 ane næht gefeormige.
