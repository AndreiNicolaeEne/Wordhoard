Se ðe hine þonne nedes ofsloge oððe unwillum oððe ungewealdes swelce hine god swa sende on his honda. and he hine ne ymbsyrede sie he feores wyrðe and folcryhtre bote gif he friðstowe gesece.
Gif hwa ðonne ofgiernesse and gewealdes ofslea his þone nehstan þurh searwa. aluc ðu hine from minum weofode to þam þæt he deaðe swelte.
