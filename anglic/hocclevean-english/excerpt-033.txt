By þe king Worshipful fader in god / right trusty and welbeloued / we grete yow wel / And for asmuche as þe trewes
bitwix vs / and oure broþir þe duc of Bretaigne been proroged vnto þe feste of alhalowmesse
next comyng / and for wekes after / in þe same fourme as þei were taken þe last yere at Alencon /
we wol þat in al hast ye do proclame in due fourme þe said prorogacion in al places nedeful and accustumed
in suche cas / And god haue yow in his keping Yeuen vnder oure signet in oure Castel of Rouen þe xxx day of Ianuer
