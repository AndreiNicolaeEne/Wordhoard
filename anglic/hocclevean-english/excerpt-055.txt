By the king Worshipful fader in god / right trusty and welbel oued / We sende yow closed wiþ ynne þees a supplicacion putte
vnto vs by Thomas Gray / grocier and marchant of oure Cite of London making mencion of certain dettes whiche the lord Talbot /
þat is god betaught shuld owe to hym and to his wyf as is more pleinely contened in the same supplicacion / Wherefor we wol and
charge yow / þat þees oure lettres with the saide supplicacion seen by yow / ye doo comme tofore yow / the enfeffours /
þat þe same supplicacion maketh mencion
of / and seeth / þat þay contente þe forsaide Thomas:
of al that thay been bounde by right and Reson to contente hym of touching the Dettes abouesaide / And god haue yow in his
