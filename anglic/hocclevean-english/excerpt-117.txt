By the King: Worshipful fader in god oure trusty and welbe loued / We wol and charge yow þat vnto oure Welbeloued clerc Iohn Hethe
/ ye doo make out writtes of Liberate and Allocate / from terme to terme / wiþ the clause / non obstante /
þe preferrement / of þannuite of xx Marc / which we haue granted hym by oure lettres patentes
/ of the fee ferme / of oure town of Iarnemouthe / to take hit by þe handes of oure baillifs of the saide toun for
the tyme beyng / And seeþ tat þis be doon / notwiþ standyng / any commandement / yeuen vnto yow / to þe contrary
here afore / Yeuen vnder oure signet / at oure town of Rouen þe xxviije day of ffeuerer Andrieu
