By þe kyng Worshipful We grete you wel And for as muche as þe trewes taken b etwen e vs and
oure broþer þe Duc of Bretaigne: been proroged vnto halowmesse next coming. we wol þat þer
vpon ye ordeine that þer be proclamacion maade in alle oure portes and in suche places as hit haþ been
vsed and accustumed to be doon in like caas afore þis time / And god haue yow in his keping yeuen vnder oure signet
in oure hoost before Roan þe ix. day of Aust.
