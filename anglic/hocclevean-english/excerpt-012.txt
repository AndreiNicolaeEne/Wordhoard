By þe kyng: Worshipful fad er in god / riȝt trusty and welbeloued / We wol / þat to sir
Nichol Wynbush clerc of our petit bagge / ye do haue / oure lettres of collacion Vnder oure grete seel in due forme /
of a prebende in Chichestre / þat voided now late and longeþ vnto oure yifte / by cause of þe
temporaltees of þe bishopriche of Chichestre / in like wise as oure Chamberlain wrote vnto yow now late fro faloiȝe /
for þe said sir Nichol / vnto þe prebende a forsaid / Yeuen vnder our signet in oure Cite of Baieux þe xxij. day of Marche.
