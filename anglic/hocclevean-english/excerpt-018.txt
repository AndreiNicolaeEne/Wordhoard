By þe king Worshipful fader yn god. right trusty and welbeloued / We sende to yow closed wiþynne þees oure
lettres a supplicacion putte vnto vs yn þe name of þe priour and Conuent of oure house of Ihesu of Bethle em
at Shene of þordre of Chartuse makyng mencion how þat þe barons of oure Eschequer at Westminstre wol
not discharge hem of certeine sommes of moneye rennyng on hem at oure saide Eschequer for diuerses dimes and quinȝimes
granted vnto vs by þe clergie and comunes of oure Reaume of Englond. we wol þat þe saide supplicacion seyen /
and vnderstanden by yow / and oure lettres patentes made vnto hem þerevpon / ye doo ordeine and see /
þat þai be discharged of þe saide dymes and quinȝimes after þeffect of oure saide lettr es
patentes and if þat þe said priour and cou ent may not be discharged by þe vertue of oure same lettres
patentes þanne we wol / þat ye ordeine yn þe best wyse þat ye can / þat þai be discharged as for þ is
tyme of þe dymes and quinȝimes abouesaid / Also we pray yow / spekeþ vnto þarchebisshop of Canterbu ry
/ þat oure lettres patentes made vnto þe saide priour and Conuent touching þe discharge of þe saide
dymes and quinȝimes be entred in his Registre / if ye þinke hit goodly to be doon / yeuen under oure signet yn oure Castel of Caen þe xx. day of May:
