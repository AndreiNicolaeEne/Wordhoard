By the kyng Worshipful fader in god / oure Right trusty and welbeloued We wol and charge yow. that at what tyme / oure welbeloued
knight Iohan Cornewaille / bringeth vnto yow / oon called Mareschal Des Rues. for to swere þe pees that is made betw een ou re
fader of france and vs: ye take his ooth and lat him swere tofore yow þe same pees / after þe fourme of
þe copie whiche we sende yow closed wiþin thees / Yeuen vnder oure signet in oure hoost at Ruthueil beside Meaulx þe xiiij. day of Nouembre. Shiryngton
