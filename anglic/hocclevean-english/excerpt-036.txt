By the King: Worshipful fader in god / oure trusty and welbeloued / for asmuch a
Patente closed vnder oure signet / and ensealled with oure grete sea Syon / þe whiche Patente / fore haste myȝht not be
enrolled here enrolled in England. Not wiþstanding / þe two names rased Yeuen vnder oure signet / at
oure town of Rouen / þe ije day of March / Another hand The two names aforesaide been þerles of Arundell and of
warrewik þe whiche wer Rased / because þey wer myssette And for asmuche as oure saide patent is of a þyng perpe tuel:
we wol þat ye do write hit ayein of a more substancial hande / and seel hit vndre oure seel beyng yn youre warde. And now
þat we haue satisfied to þe desire of þayme of Syon as towardes þaire profession. we wolde
þat for any thyng þe profession wer hasted. wherto we pray yow to stere and to help þat ye may.
