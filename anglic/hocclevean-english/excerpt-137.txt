Worshipful fader in god / oure right trusty and welbeloued / We wol and charge yow / that ye make oute oure writtes to oure
viconte of hamptshire / for to make Proclamacion in oure saide vicontee / in alle place accoustumed / that alle man ere
men that haue any grond or land with ynne oure toun of Port esmouth / with ynne suche a certein day as ye shal li m it
by youre discrecion / comme and chalenge / eche man his owen grond / So that hit may be knowen sekirly what grond
is longing to euerych of hem / And that grond that is not chalenged / with ynne the saide day limited / we wol ye ordeine /
that hit be saised in to oure handes / yeuen vnder oure signet / at Saint liȝ the .xviij. day of Iuyn / Toly
