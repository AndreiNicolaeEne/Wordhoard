By the kyng Right trusty and Welbeloued brother / We grete yow often tymes Wel / And for as moche as We haue vnderstande that
maistre Iohan Chaundeler that was Deen of Salesbury is chosen Bisshop of the same chirche. Wher of we hald vs wel agreed / and therto we
yeue oure assent Roial / and we wol wel that after the consecracion of the said Elit / he haue liueree of his temporaltees /
And the holy gost haue yow in his keping / yeuen vnder our signet in oure hoost afor ffaloise / the .xvij. day of Decembre /
