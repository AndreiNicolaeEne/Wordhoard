By þe king Worshipful fader in god / oure right trusty and welbeloued / We grete yow wel / And we wol ye wyte / þat þ e
priour of oure hows of saint Bartholomew / in Westsmythfeld of London haaþ poursued vnto vs here on þis side þe
see / for confirmacion of alle þe yiftes grantes / priuileges / franchises and libertees graunted vnto oure saide hows by oure
progenitours and predecessours / And for asmuche: as ye might by vertue of youre office haue doo maad hym oure lettres of
confirmacion wiþoute þat hym shulde haue neded to poursue ferther vnto vs / we suppose þere bee sum cause
resounable / why ye haue not doo hit / in cas þat he haue pour sued vnto yow þerfor / wherfor if ye knowe any suche cause why
ye aught not to make hym oure forsaide lettres / we wol þ at ye certiffie vs what hit is And if þere noon bee / þ at
þanne ye doo maake vnto þe forsaide priour and to þe conuent of oure saide hows of seint Bertholomew /
oure lettres patentes of confirmacion vnder oure greet seel in due fourme of alle þe yiftes / grantes / priui leges
/ franchises and libertes / whiche þay haue / as is aboue said of þe yifte or grant of oure progenitours and predece ssours
