By þe kyng Worshipful fader yn god We wol þat to oure clerc Robert Shiryngton ye do haue our lettres
patentes vnder owr grete seal en due forme / of þe preb ende þat was maistre Richard Derehams in þe chirche of
Chichestre / þe whiche is voide by his deeþ and longeþ to owr yifte be cause of þe temporaltees of þe
bisshoprich of Chichestre / of whiche matere we haue writen to yow afore þis tyme / yeuen vnder oure signet at oure Chastel
of Alencon þe second day of Nouembre
