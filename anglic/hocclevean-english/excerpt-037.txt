vnderstande by yow þe matere contened in þe supplicacion / whiche we sende yow closed here wiþin
/ touching certain þinges granted vnto oure welbeloued squier. Ianico dartasse: as ye may se more pleinly. by þe
saide supplicacion: ye doo ordeine þat he haue suche writtes as may Lawefully be hadde / and suche as yow þen keþ
resounable in þe cas. And god haue yow in his keping. Yeuen vnder oure signet in oure town of vernon þe vj. day of Aueril S Shiryngton
