By the kyng Worshipful fader: in god Right trusty and Welbeloued. We grete yow ofte tymes Wel. And for as muche as we haue vnderstande
that maistre Iohan Chaundeler that was Deen of Salesbury is chosen bisshop of the same chirche / Wherof we hald vs wel agreed / and
therto we yeue our assent Roial and we wol wel / that after the Consecracion of the said Elit he haue liueree of his
temporaltees / And the holy goost haue yow in his keping: yeuen vnder our signet in our hoost afor ffaloise. the .xvij. day of Decembre
