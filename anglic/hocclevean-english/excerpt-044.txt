By þe kyng Worshipful fader yn god / oure right trusty and welbeloued / We grete yow wel / And we sende yow closed wiþ yn
þees / a supplicacion put vnto vs / by oure poure ligeman Iohan Bone. vpon whiche supplicaion / we wol
þat ye doo hym haue þat that right and lawe wollen yn þe cas / but þat oþersyde beeth wel avysed or to
grete duresse be shewed vnto hym / or to any of oure suggettes yn persone or yn goodes / for þe suggestions or appech ementes
of himþat calleth hym self person of wortham. vnto þe tyme þe verray trowþe be clerely
examined and knowen / of his accusacions / oþer elles þoo þat he accuseth. han be noted afore tyme of suche defaultes.
for þe forsaid person is holden to besy in suche maters / And god haue yow in his keping / Yeuen vnder oure
signet in oure town of Mante þe xj. day of Iuill S
