By þe kyng Worshipful fader yn god riȝt trusty and welbeloued. For asmoche as we yaf now
Robert Chicheley Aldermen of london wiþ oþer / in an annuitee of xx.marc Recluse of Berkyng. by cause she
miȝt nat haue þannuitee her self: for of london. as in oure lettres patentes / it is more clerly contened /
we wol of liberate courrant & allocate dormant / natwiþ standyng any preferrement we write
at þis tyme vnto þe quenes conseil / for to deliuere
certain endentures vn Syon at Shene / touching certain money þat is due to her / of þe ferme of saint endowed vnto oure said howse: So we pray yow þat ye be helpyng to þat matere deliuer ance of þendentures aforsaid / yeuen vnder oure signet in oure Cite of Baieux day of M arche
