By the kyngTrusty and welbeloued: ffor as muche as we be enfourmed / Thomas de la Croix hath sent hors and certain
Armerers and hernois for vs vn to our town of Caleys We wol and charge yow expresly þat ye ordeine þat þe
saide hors and þe men þat ar comen wiþ hem come to vs in al seur haste / and send sum trusty man for to go wiþ
hem / þat may haue þe ouersighte and gouern ance of hem alle / And þat be seurly and saufly conduyed fro
thens to Seintomere / and so to Arras / And fro thens to Amyas / there abiding vn to þe tyme they haue seur conduyt from our
brothir of Burgoigne or fro sir Iohan de Luxemburgh. to þe whiche we haue writen for her sauf passage fro thens to Aubiualle
fro whens our garnisons wiþ goddes grace shullen conduit hem to Parys where we wol they abyde til þey haue woord of
our wille / And þey comen to Parys we wol he þat shal come fro yow with hem / sende vs woord of their comyng thider. yeuen
vnder our signet in our Oost at seint Denys de Moronval beside Dreux the laste day of Iuyll: Depeden
