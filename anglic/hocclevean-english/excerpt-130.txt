By the king Worshipful fader in god: oure right trusty and welbeloued / for asmuche as we haue granted vnto oure welbeloued
clerc Robert Rolleston oure warderober the chirche of warton in oure Conte of Lancastre / whiche standeth voide in lawe / as we
been enfourmed / and longeth to oure yift / we wol and charge yow
/ þat ye doo make vnto oure saide clerc / oure lettres
of collacion of þe saide chirche / in due fourme / And god haue yow in his kepyng / Yeuen vnder oure signet in oure hoost / afore Meleun / the / xxij. day of Septembre / Toly
