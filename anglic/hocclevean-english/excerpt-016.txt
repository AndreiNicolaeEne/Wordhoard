By þe kyng W orshipful fader yn god right trusty and Welbeloued / We sende
yow closed wiþ ynne þees a
supplicacion put vnto vs yn þe nam of oure welbeloued Roger Waltham / by þe whiche he haþ prayd vs for
certein causes contened yn þe saide supplicacion / we wold graunte hym to be oon of þe barons of oure Eschequer / yn
þe stede of Robert Sandford þat is god betaght / Considering þat he might be of power to doo vs þe bett er
seruice yn oþer occupacion as we haue sette hym ynne for oure prouffit / as ye may be more clerly enfourmed
by þe saide supplicacion / So we wol þat ye see and vnderstande clerely al þe matere contened þer
ynne / and þervpon ordeine suche prouision as hit semeþ best to youre Discrecion for oure prouffit and auantage
yn þis cas: yeuen vnder oure signet yn oure Castel of Caen þe furst day of May /
