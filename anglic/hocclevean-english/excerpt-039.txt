By þe king. Worshipful fader yn god oure right trusty and welbeloued / We grete yow wel / And we sende yow closed wiþ
ynne þees oure lettres. a supplicacion of greuous compleynt / put vnto vs by. sir Rogier wodehill person of
Strete / as ye may see more clerely by þe same supplicacion / wherfor we wol þat þe forsaide supplicacion wel
vnderstanden and considered by yow / ye doo calle before yow bothe parties speciffied in þe same supplicacion / and þaire
causes herd / þat ye doo vnto hem boothe / right and equite / and in especial þat ye see þat þe porer
partye suffre no wrong / but þat ye make suche an ende in þis matiere / þat we be nomore vexed hereafter
wiþ taire complaintes / And god haue yow in his keping / yeuen vnder oure signet / at oure town of Vernon þe .xxviij.
day of Auerill
