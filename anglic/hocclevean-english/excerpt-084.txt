Duk of Burgoyne oure rebell with his puissans of ffleming water of Gravelyng and willyng and disposyng
hym to gete oure toun of Caley þere the whuche if so were to grete an hurte and perpetuell shame. We
therfore willyng to resiste the malice of the said callyng hym Duk dispose rescous to oure said toun and marches. Wherefore
we desire and pray you hertly as oure feod man in wise & as at this tyme make you redy and al the poeple that ye
can and may and drawe you theder toward best array for the seid oure foed men and true subgitts to whom we yeven
vnder oure priue seal at Westminstre þhe last day of callyng hym Duk hath take oure fortalys
