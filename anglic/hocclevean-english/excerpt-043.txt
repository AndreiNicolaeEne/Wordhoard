By þe kyng S Worshipful fader yn god / oure right trusty and welbeloued / We grete yow wel And forasmuche / as we lete sende
for maistre Richard garsedale oon of þe contendentes of þe prouoste of þe Oriell to þat ende
þat for his partie / shulde no thyng be poursuyd neither at þe Courte of Rome ne elles where but þat /
þat controuersie shulde be put in Respit vnto oure comyng hoom wiþ goddes grace / for oure occupaci on
is suche þat we mow nat wel entende to suche materes here / Wherefore we wol þat ye make booþe þe
said garsdale whiche comeþ now hoom be oure leue / and also Lentwardyn / com afore yow / and þat ye take seurte souf ficeant
of bothe þe parties / þat neither of hem shal make ferþer poursuyt of appelle at Courte of Rome / ner no
manere of poursuyt þere or elles where / as touching þe said contrauersie vnto oure comyng as before / at
whiche tyme oure entent ys / to put þe same controuersie to a goode and rightwyse conclusion
and þe said partie
yn Rest. And yf any of hem haue þe saide poursuyt of apelle hangyng yn Court þat þey abate hit / and sende to Revoke
hit yn al haste / and þat þay make al suche as been þ aire attornes or doers yn Court spirituel or temporel to
surcesse. And we wol ferþermore as touching oure said College of þoriell / þat ye put hit yn suche gouernance /
as semeth to yowre Discrecion for to doo vnto oure Comyng. And god haue yow yn his keping / Yeuen vnder oure signet in oure town of Mante þe vij day of Iuill S Shiryngton
