By þe Kyng:Trusty and Welbeloued. By a supplicacion of greuouse compleinte. putte vnto vs yn name of þe Priour and
Couent of oure cathedrale chirche of Bathe. We haue vnderstande how albeit þat of tyme passed mynde it hath be vsed and
acustumed þat ye of þe Cite of Bathe. shulde ryng no belle / no day in þe week þil þey of
þe s aid chirch e hadden first rong þair belles. Neither at night aftir þe tyme þat þey
had rong / yit aȝeinstonding þe long continu ance of þe same vsag and custume. ye of late tyme haue do
þe contrary and wilfully do fro day to day. yn greet disese and preiudice of þe forseide Priour and Couent. but if
remede couenable be ordeined þer vppon. Wharfor we wol if it so be. and charge you expressely þat ye cesse of
al suche manere newe and wilfulle gouernance yn þe matire forsaide. and conforme yn to þe gode
custume of olde tyme vsed and continued here a fore. vnto oure comyng with þe grace of god ynto our Realme
of Englond. þat we may haue ful knowloche of þe matire and to ordeine suche remede þer vppon
as we shal be þan auised. by wey of trouthe and of right. Yeuen vnder our signet at oure towne of Caen þe xxiij. day of Septembre
