By the kyng Worshipful fader in god / oure Right trusty and welbeloued / We sende you closed wiþin thees oure lettres
a supplicacion put vnto
vs by marchantes of Paris / Rouen / Troyes / and Brugees / as ye may see þerby / And wol and
charge yow / þat ye enfourmed of þe matere contened in þe same supplicacion / ordeineth that Right be doon vnto
þe saide marchantes after þe lawes and coustumes of oure Rewme of England / wiþowte any long delay /
Yeuen vnder oure signet in oure ost at þe toun of Meaulx. þe xxviij. day of Marche / Shiryngton
