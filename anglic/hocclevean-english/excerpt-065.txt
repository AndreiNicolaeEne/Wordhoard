thees: a supplicacion put vnto vs
/ by Thomas compleinyng him / how þat he beyng here in oure seruice oon Robert Roklee Squier haþ
dissesed him of þe manoir with þappourtenanc es in york shire / as in þe same supplicacion hit is
more clerely contened / Wherfore we wol þat euidences of bothe parties ordeine so for þat matere þat
his beyng here in oure saide seruice be nat harmeful nor but þat he stande in as goode condicion as he did at
his comyng owt wiþ vs Yeuen vnder oure signet saint faron beside Meaulx þe xv day of ffeuerer
