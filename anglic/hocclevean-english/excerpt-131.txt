By þe kyng R yht trusty and welbeloued broþer. We grete you wel. and we do you to wite þat it
is oure wil and entente þat ye d eclar e a parlement to be holde monday þe second day of
Decembre next for certaine causes þe whiche we wol do you to haue knowlech of in al haste. And also we wol and charge you
þat ye se þat Iustices of pees. Shereues. Eschetours. Coroners and suche officers as shul be maad. be suche persones
as ben able & worthy þerto & þat ben no troublers in þaire contrees. and þat þey be chose wiþ oute
brocage or fauour of persones or any oþere vnleeful. meenes. after þestatut & ordennances maad þervpon.
