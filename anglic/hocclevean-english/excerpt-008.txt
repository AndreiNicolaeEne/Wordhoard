patentes we han granted and assigned to oure welbeloued Richard Whytyngdon and Richard harowedon monk of Westmins
Vc. mark yeerly vp on the issues of the hanaper of oure chancellerie for the byldynge of oure chirche of Westmins
ye doo maake writtes of liberate with the clause that folweth her after / Preferramento decem milium librarum Regi in parliamento suo nuper facto non obstante /
yeuen vnder oure signet in our hoost beside oure toun of ffaloise the xviij day of Januer
