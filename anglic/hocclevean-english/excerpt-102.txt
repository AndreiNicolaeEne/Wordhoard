By the Kyng:Worshepful fader in god we wyl that ye doo make writtes of proclamacion in to alle oure portes of
Englond al maner men that wil bryng vitailles vn to oure tovn of Caen for the Refresshing of vs and of our hoost in our
Duchie of Normandie that they schul paye therfor no custume / so that they fynde sufficeant seurte.
that they shal brynge the sayd vitailles vn to our said tovn of Caen for the refress of our seyd hoost / yeuen vnder our
signet in our Castel of Caen the .xxij. day of Septembre
