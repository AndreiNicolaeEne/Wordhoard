To þe hyghe and myghty Prynce þe duc of Glouce ster and other of þe councell of oure souueraigne lord
þe kyngeȝ Lyke vn to þe hyghe and to þe descrete councell of oure souueraigne lord þe kynges
to graunte vnto Ioan Asteley norysse of oure said souueraign lord þe kyng xx li by yere at þe terme of here lyve
to be paied yn what place þat þe said discrete councell woll assign for þe goode seruyse and entendauntȝ þ at
þe said norysse hathe doen fro þe day of þe berthe of oure said souueraign lord vn to þis day consid eryng
þat þe said Ioan hath neyther reward ne fee by fore graunted of oure said lord for þe wathe and labour
þat sheo hath had fro þe day as hit is a boven rehersed vn to þis day. Where fore and hit lyke þe said
hye and discrete councell to graunte þe said Ioan Asteley þe said xx li for þe terme as hit is a boven wreton yn
þe wey of charite
