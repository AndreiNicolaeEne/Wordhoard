By þe kyng Right trusty and Welbeloued broþer. We sende yow closed
wiþ ynne þees / a supplicacion put vnto vs: by William Mynours. yeman huissher of oure Chambre. And hit ys oure wille:
þat ye be as fauorable to him yn þis matere: as ye may resounably: And þat al þe fauour be doon vnto
him / bothe afore oure chief Iustice of oure benche / and also afore oure Chaunceller : that may be doon / by Lawe
conscience and reson.
And broþer see ye þerto. Consideryng his beyng here in oure seruice. yn wyse as ye wote. Yeuen vnder oure signet
in oure towne of Rouen þe .v. day of Ianuier S Shiryngton
