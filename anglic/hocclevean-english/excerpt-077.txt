To þe hygh and myghty Prynce þe Duc of Gloucester and oþer of þe councell of our souueraign lord
þe kyng Lyke vn to þe high and discrete councell of oure souueraign lord þe kyng to graunt to
Morgrete Brothnam Chambrer and Wesher of oure said souuera ign lord þe kyng C s for as long as þe said descrete
councell lykys and to be paid yn what place þat þei will Assign for þe goode seruice and entendantȝ
þat þe said Chambrer hath don fro þe day of þ e birth of oure soueraign lord vn to þis day consideryng
þat þe said Morgrete hath noþer reward nor fee be for graunted of oure said lord for þe wach
and labor þat scho hath had fro þe day as yit es aboven rehersid vn to þis day wherfor and yit like þe
said hye & discrete councell to graunt þe said Morgre te Brothnam þe said C s in þe wey of Charite and Almous.
