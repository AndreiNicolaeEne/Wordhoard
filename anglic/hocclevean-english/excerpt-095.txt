To þe kyng our souerain lorde Bise cheth humbly Richard Erl of Salisbury of your grace espec ial
to graunt hym þe keping of your towne & Castel of Carlele & of þe West march toward Scotland to haue fro
þe ende of þe terme þat þe bisshop of Carlele in theym now hath / to þende of ten yeres þen
next filowyng in such fourme as þe said bisshop þe saide keping
hath Takyng þe said Erl for þe same keping
euery yere during þe same x yeres Ml li in such maner e fourme & places & with such surties & preferrementȝ
of & to Paiementȝ of þe somme aforsaide / as þe said bisshop hath for þe keping of þe said towne Castel &
march by indentures and your lettres patentȝ þervpon to be made in due fourme / to þe said Erl /
And if it happen þe said bisshop. in eny wise to leve þe keping of þe towne Castel & march aforsaide. afore þende
of his saide terme. þat þen þe said Erl. may haue þe keping of þe same towne Castel & march for x
yeres next filowyng after þat levyng / takyng þe said Erl for þe same keping euery yer of þe same x yeres
Ml li in such manere fourme place & wyth such surties & preferrementȝ to hym by your lettres
patentȝ to be made / as It is abouesaid. And þe said Erl shal within iiij yeres next after his entree / to þe said
keping do repair þe dungeon of þe said Castel. so þ at he yerly haue during þe same iiij yeres l. li to be
taken of þissuȝ profitȝ & Reuenuȝ commyng of þe shir of Cumberland / by þe handes of
þe shirif þere for þe tyme beeyng by your lettres patentȝ aforsaide of graunt also þer of
to hym to be made. consideryng þat if þe said dungeon wherof partie be downefallen: hooly fal downe / as It is liche
to do but if hasty reparacion þerof be made / wol cost yow Ml li & more / for And þat ye yif nowe
