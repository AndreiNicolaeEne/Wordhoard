By þe kyng S Worshipful fader yn god / oure Right trusty and welbeloued / we
grete yow wel / And for asmuche as we haue granted vnto oure welbeloued clerc of oure Chapelle / Thomas Gyles / þe pre bende
whiche / Iohn Cooke late clerc of oure saide Chapelle / hadde wiþin oure free Chapelle of hastynges. We wol þat
þervpon ye do make oure lettres patentes in due fourme. vnder oure grete seel / as þe cas asketh / Yeuen vnder
oure signet in oure town of Mante þe xxv. Day of Iuill S Shiryngton
