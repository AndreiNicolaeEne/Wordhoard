To the kyng oure souuerein lord Bisecheth lowly youre pore Ianyn Cassons squyer of my lord of Wynchestre. that sith the Office
of Viergerschup in the kynges Chapelle of Wyndesore is voide by the deth of Iohan Clifford of whom god haue mercy.
like youre highnes by thaduis of my redoubted lord of Gloucestre and of al youre noble graunt your saide
bisecher the same office of vierger with al the fees and appurtenances that longeth ther to in wise like as the said
Iohan þerme of life for goddes sake. and as he shal euer praie for youre high estat and for al your noble lignie
