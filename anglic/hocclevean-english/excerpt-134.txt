By the king Worshipful fader in god / oure right trusty and welbeloued / For asmuche as hit is doon vs to vnderstande that
the bisshop of London is passed to god / we lat yow wite / that we haue granted vnto oure welbeloued seruant Iohan Hanham.
clerc of oure Caterye the pension of hym that shal be next bisshop of the same place wolnyng & chargyng yow / that there
vpon whanne tyme commeth ye doo make hym oure lettres vnder ou re gree t seel in due fourme / Yeuen vnder oure signet in oure oost
