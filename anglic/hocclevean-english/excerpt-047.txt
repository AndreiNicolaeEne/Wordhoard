By þe king Worshipful fader in god oure right trusty and welbeloued We wol þat ye sende hedir vnto vs sir Thomas
Suthwell preste / in al resonable haste. And ferþermore we wol and also charge you þat ye ordeyne þat þat
be effectuelly doon in dede. þat we wrote vnto you as touching þe Duc of Orliens as oure trust is to you. for the
cas is so grete þat ye ne couthe not ymagyn hit gretter. And as touching þe chirche of Lughtbourgh in contie of
Lercestre longyng to oure yift: we wol wel þat oure welbeloued clerc Robert ffry remembred to vs by youre lett res
haue þe saide chirche / So þat he be preste or atte leste infra sacros wiþ yn þis yere aftre þe
tenour of þe comun lawe Yeuen vnder oure signet at oure toune of Mante þe first day of Nouember.
