By the King Worshipful fader in god oure right trusty and welbeloued. We wol þat ye doo al the fauour and ese þat
ye may lawfully vnto Iohan Spendeloue bringer of þees touchyng þe bille whiche we sende vnto yow closed wiþynne
þees oure lettres. And if ye may lawfully doo al þ at þe same bille conteneth / we wolde ye dide hit. And we wol þ at
ye doo delyuere vnto maistre Roger Whelpdale Elit of Karlell þe temporaltees of þe bisshopriche / as sone as his
bulles bee comen / not withstandyng þat he hath not made homage þerfore to vs / Yeuen vnder oure signet at oure town of Rouen þe xxj day of Ianuer:
