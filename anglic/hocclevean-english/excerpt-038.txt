By þe king S Worshipful fader in god / oure Right trusty and welbeloued. We grete yow wel / And we wol þat after þe
teneur of a supplicacion. whiche
we sende yow closed wiþ ynne þis / ye do make oure lettres of saufconduit. vnder
oure grete seel in due forme / duryng for a yere / And god haue yow in his keping / Yeuen vnder oure signet in oure towne of vernon þe xxthe. day of Auril S Shiryngton
