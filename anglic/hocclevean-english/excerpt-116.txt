By þe king. Worshipful fader in god right trusty and welbeloued / We grete yow wel / And doo yow to wite þat
we haue yeuen vnder oure greet seel here / þe chirche of Morton / whiche as we were enfourmed voyded be þe deces
of oon maister Richard Penelles and longed to oure yift / be cause of þe beyng of oure Cousin Courrenays landes
yn oure handes / the whiche oure yift we signiffie vnto yow to þat ende / þat ye witt / we wol hit take
effect / yeuen vnder oure signet / in. oure town of Rouen. þe .xxx. day of Ianuer Toly
