Ho, that night I when leave work way later than I like, was hana pau long time already but I stay talk story wit da one at da counter and neva notice da time. By da time I when walk out, was dark-dark, not just dusk kine, real dark.

My usual way home get one shortcut, cut behind da old cane field, but dat night someting inside me when say no. Not far down da road already I get da feeling, steps behind me, landing same time as mine, like echo but not echo. I neva turn around. I just slow my pace little bit, like I stay checking someting on my phone, casual kine. Da steps behind me wen slow too. Chicken skin, brah, all up my arms. Then I when speed up quick, like I remember someting urgent, and same ting, da steps wen speed up right after, no even one second late.

Right dea I when decide, no can risk da shortcut tonight. I cut over to da main road instead, where get da store still open, get streetlight, get people still out. Halfway down I let myself look back, one time only, quick kine. Nothing. Just da road, empty, dark. But that neva make me feel better, if nobody stay, then what was making da steps, yeah?

Da last stretch I when walk so fast, almost running, but to anybody who wen ask I was just walking brisk, no lie. Got to da door, key shaking in my hand, and when I when shut um behind me, ho, da relief. Whole body just let go, like I was holding my breath da whole way.

Me: Nevah find out what was.

The eldest: Probably was one cat, or your own steps bouncing off da wall.

Me: Garans not one cat, auntie. But yeah, I tell um now half laughing, cause how sure I was that night, running from nothing, maybe. Still get days I wonder.
