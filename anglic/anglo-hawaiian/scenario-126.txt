Me: Eh brah, I gotta tell you someting, and no give me dat lolo face before I even start.
Brah: What kine face. You look like you wen see one ghost or someting. You was probably half asleep, tired from work, brain stay make up stories.
Me: No, no, I stay wide awake, garans. Was maybe eleven, I sitting on da steps waiting for da dog fo come back, thinking clear, not even one beer in me.
Brah: Okay okay, so what wen happen.
Me: I hear da drums first, far off. Den get one line of light coming down da old cane road, torches, small kine flickering, and get one conch shell blowing too, low kine sound, like from way back time.
Brah: Ho, dat's probably just some guys walking home from da bar wit dere flashlights. Dat road still get people cutting through sometimes yeah?
Me: No can be, brah. Dat road stay all fenced off since da mill wen close, get pig wire all da way around um. I wen check da lock myself dat same morning, garans. Nobody stay walking through there.
Brah: Maybe was cars den, headlights from da highway bouncing off da clouds or someting.
Me: Highway stay too far for dat, and cars no make drum sound, brah. And get no wind dat night, was dead calm, I stay telling you. I wen get chicken skin all up my arms, all da way to my neck, and it nevah go away till da whole ting wen pass.
Brah: I dunno. Still sound like you was dreaming to me.
Me: I swear on my grandmother's grave, every word I telling you stay true. I nevah move from dat step till da whole line wen pass by and disappear down by where da old mill stay. Wasn't no dream, wasn't no imagining, I was sitting right there wide awake da whole time.
Brah: (shaking head) I still no believe you, brah. But okay, no need get all worked up about um.
