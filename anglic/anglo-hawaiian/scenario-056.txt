Okay so my workmate and them wen ask me watch da house while dey stay Big Island for one week, seven days, funeral stuff on dat side. I said shua, no problem, I stay right down da road anyway, easy fo walk over. But garans I nevah know what I wen sign up for, brah. One week turn into one whole saga, I tell you.

First night already something not right. I wen open da door, da house stay all dark, get one smell inside like somebody wen leave da rice cooker on fo three days, but nobody was cooking nothing. I check da kitchen, nothing. I check da bedroom, nothing. Turns out was da dog blanket, da dog wen pee on top da blanket before dey left and nobody wen catch it. So dat's how da week start, me on my knees at ten o'clock at night scrubbing one blanket, thinking to myself, eh, seven more days of this, or what.

Second day, da chicken wen get loose. Dey get one small coop out back, couple chicken, one rooster dat like crow at anykine hour, and I wen leave da gate open little bit when I was filling up da water thing. Nex thing I know da hen stay out in da neighbor yard, running around like she won da lotto. Chase um for about one hour, me and da neighbor kid both, up and down da carport, under da car, into da ti leaf, before we wen corner um by da rubbish can. My shirt was soaking wet after, but at least I nevah lose da chicken for good, dat would've been real bad news to tell dem.

Da neighbor:
Ho, you like help? I get one net in da garage.
Me:
Eh yeah, bring um quick, dis chicken lolo, she no like get caught.

We wen laugh about dat one after, but in da moment my heart was going.

Da garden part wen creep up on me slow, I no even notice at first. Dey get plumeria and one small patch where dey grow tomato and green onion, and I was watering um every morning like I said I would, but somehow by day three da leaf stay turning yellow on da tomato and I no can figure out why. By day five half da plant stay drooping, and I stay googling on my phone like one lolo, too much water, not enough water, bugs, whatevahs. Never did fully fix dat one, just wen cut back da watering and hope for da best. I figured dey go understand, plant stay plant, sometimes she just do her own thing.

Da appliance wen go down on day three, da washing machine, right when I was doing da towel from da dog blanket incident. Machine wen stop mid cycle, make one grinding noise, then nothing. I wen open um up, get water everywhere on da floor, towel stuck half wet half soap. No can call da owner, dey stay dealing with funeral stuff, I no like bother dem over one washing machine. So I wen call my uncle who stay retired, he used to fix stuff for da plantation camp back in da day.

My uncle:
Eh, sound like da belt or maybe da pump, you like me come look?
Me:
Eh, if you not busy, yeah? I no like mess up somebody's washing machine more than already stay messed up.

He wen come by dat evening, poke around, say need one part, but for now he wen jerry rig um so at least I could hand wash and wring out da rest of da towels. That machine stay half broke da whole week after that, just sitting there like one reminder.

Day four, get one visitor show up, no warning, nothing. I stay in da kitchen eating leftover grindz when I hear knock knock on da door. I open um up, was da owner's titah, da sister, she wen come from town side just to check da house because she nevah could reach her sibling on da phone and was getting worried.

Da sister:
Eh, sorry fo just show up like dis, I was trying call and call, no answer.
Me:
Oh, dey stay fine, dey just busy over there, everything cool over here, well, mostly.

I nevah tell her about da washing machine or da dog blanket, just said mostly, and she wen laugh, said dat's how family stay, always something. We wen talk story for maybe one hour on da porch, she wen tell me stories about growing up in dat same house back when da whole street was still plantation camp houses, everybody's door open, everybody borrowing rice from everybody. Was nice actually, one bright spot in da middle of all da craziness.

Da jolt of alarm wen hit me day five. I was looking for da spare key dey wen give me, da one fo da shed, and I could not find um anywhere. I wen turn da whole junk drawer inside out, check my own pocket like five times, check da car, check under da mat. My stomach wen drop, I was already thinking how I going tell dem I wen lose their key, how much dat going cost fo change da lock. Turns out da key was in da other pocket of my slippers bag da whole time, but garans I nevah sleep good dat night from da stress alone.

Den day six morning, I wen lock myself out. Went outside real quick just fo bring in da trash can, door wen swing shut behind me, and da door stay one of dose dat auto lock. No spare key on me dis time cause I wen leave um inside like one lolo after all dat drama finding um da day before. Stuck outside for about three hours, sun beating down, until my uncle could come back with his tool and jimmy da window open little bit. I was so hot and so annoyed, sitting on da step, nothing to do but watch da cars go by and think about how much more can go wrong.

And more did go wrong, cause dat same day, get one big rain come through in da afternoon, and I wen forget I had left da window open in da back bedroom fo air um out from da dog smell earlier in da week. By da time I remember, da carpet by da window stay all wet, curtain stay dripping. I wen mop um up best I could, put one towel down, ran da fan on top um for da rest of da day. Chicken skin thinking about explaining dat one too.

Da noise in da night wen happen day six also, like everything just wanted to pile up together at da end. About two in da morning I hear one loud bang from outside, sound like somebody wen drop something big by da carport. I wen get up, heart going, grab da flashlight, go check. Turns out was just da rubbish can, da wind wen knock um over, rolling around on da concrete. Nothing was there, no burglar, no nothing, just da wind, but I nevah go back sleep easy after dat.

Day six, next to last day, I finally wen land on one fix fo da washing machine situation, my uncle wen come back with da part and get um running again proper, no more half broke. Felt like one small victory after everything else dat wen go sideways.

Last day before dey come home, I was frantic, cleaning like crazy, trying make everything look like nothing wen happen. Mop da bedroom floor good, hide da wet towel from da window, straighten da tomato plant best I could even though half um still stay yellow, make sure da chicken stay all present and accounted for in da coop, scrub every corner. By da time dey wen pull up in da driveway I was exhausted but da house wen look pretty good, considering.

When dey wen come home I wen tell dem da whole thing, start to finish, and dey was just laughing da whole time, shaking dey head.

Da owner:
Ho, one week, look like one week's worth of drama for real, yeah?
Me:
Eh, I tell you, nevah one dull moment. But everybody still stay alive, chicken included, so I figure dat's one win.

Dey wen thank me plenty, gave me one plate of grindz to take home, and I told dem straight up, nex time dey like go somewhere, dey bettah find somebody else watch da house, cause I stay pau, done, one week like dat was enough for one lifetime.
