The eldest: I bless you dis, I swear um: da road stay open fo you, get plenty inside da house, days goin come so good dey give you chicken skin, garans, um goin come true.

Da turn.

I curse you dis too, same swear: you go walk dat road wit nothing, da house stay empty, no can rest, no like see your face come back, garans, um goin come true.

One wen bless, one wen curse, sealed same word.
