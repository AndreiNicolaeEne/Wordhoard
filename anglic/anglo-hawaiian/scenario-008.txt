The child: Mom, I stay asking you again, can I get da phone o what.

The mother: I told you already, no can. Not right now.

The child: But how come.

The mother: 'Cause we no get da money fo dat, dass how come. Rent wen go up dis month, an de light bill still not pau paying off from before. I not going make up someting fancy, dass da real reason, plain an simple.

The child: I go do extra chores. I do da dishes every night an I go mow da grass too, garans.

The mother: I hear you, an I like dat you offering, but chores no turn into money, love. Dat not how dis ting work.

The child: Okay den what if I wait. Like till my birthday. Can get um den?

The mother: If tings stay better by den, maybe. If your dad's overtime still coming in an de bills stay caught up, we go talk about um again. I not saying yes, I not saying no. But if not, den not, yeah?

The child: But da kid down da street already get one, an his family no even work as much as us...

The mother: I no care what dat family do o no do. Dat dea house, dis hea house, an what dey can afford not what we can afford. No compare us to dem, dass not going change one ting in my wallet, brah.

The child: ...

The mother: Eh, no make dat face at me. I cannot get you da phone right now, but I go get you one shave ice Sunday, after church, your favorite kine, choke syrup on top. Dass someting, even if not da ting you wanted.

The child: ...okay. Fine. But when I get big I buying my own, garans, watch.

The mother: Ho, now dat's da kine talk I like hear. Come, pau, your grindz stay getting cold already.
