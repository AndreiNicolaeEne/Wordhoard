Eh, let me tell you what wen happen at the family table, aftah church, ovah by the grandmother's house.

Get choke grindz already by then, brah. The kalua pig wen stay all pau, the lau lau wen come and go, the poke wen finish first like always, and everybody had two three plates in um already, sitting back little bit, top button popped, that kine full where you no can even think about moving. Broke da mout, all of um. Everybody stay comfortable, talking story nice and easy, laughing about small kine stuff, guard stay all the way down. Even the haole in-law, the one that married into the family few years back, he stay relaxed too, just sitting there smiling, not really following half the talk story but happy fo be there.

But get one thing, yeah. One thing this family nevah talk about at that table. Nobody evah wen stand up one day and make one rule out loud about um. Just everybody wen learn, from feeling what happens when um come up one too many times, that you just no go there. So mostly everybody stay steering around um, quiet kine, like one hole in the road everybody already know where um stay.

So we all sitting there, comfortable, plates half pau, and one uncle, he wasn't even trying start nothing garans, he just wen make one comment, sideways kine, half joke half not, something he wen throw out while reaching fo the rice. Wasn't even about da subject direct, just wen brush up against um a little, yeah? Just passing.

And I telling you, the whole table wen go quiet fo one second. Not dead quiet, but everybody kine froze, forks stay hanging in the air halfway to the mouth, cause everybody, and I mean everybody, wen already know exactly what was coming before one word of argument even wen come out yet.

Get one auntie, she the one couldn't let um pass. She wen say something mild first, just testing, like:

The auntie: I mean, you no can really say that, though, yeah?

And that was all it wen take. One other uncle, he wen counter hard right away, no even hesitate:

The uncle: What, you the expert now?

From there the voices wen start climbing fo real, and no was even five minutes yet. Get the whole charge coming out plain:

The uncle: You don't even know what you stay talking about, you know that? You not even close.

And the auntie wen fire right back, flat, no give one inch:

The auntie: I know exactly what I stay talking about, more than you evah will, so.

By then everybody else at the table just stay looking down at their plate, or looking at each other like, eh, here we go again. The mother, she wen try change the subject, bless her heart, she wen jump right in with something about the dessert, the haupia, whether get enough fo everybody...

The mother: Eh, who like more haupia, get plenty still in the fridge...

, but nobody wen even look her way, the argument wen just roll right ovah that like she nevah say nothing at all.

And then, brah, get one old grievance wen get dragged right back in, from some argument they had, I dunno, couple years back, same exact subject. The uncle wen bring um up quick:

The uncle: This just like that time you wen say the same kine thing, and look how that one wen turn out.

That's when the grandmother, she wen put her foot down, quiet kine but everybody wen hear um clear:

The grandmother: Eh. We said we no talk about this at this table. We said that already, all of us.

But even that nevah work. The argument just wen keep rolling right ovah her words, like she wasn't even talking at all.

The uncle who wen get accused, he wen push back from the table then, chair scraping. Nevah leave the room, nevah storm out or nothing, just wen scoot back little bit, put some space between him and the rest, arms crossed, looking off out the window at nothing.

And then get this silence, brah, the kine silence where you can hear every single fork against the plate like somebody wen turn up the volume on um. Chicken skin kine quiet, garans. Choke uncomfortable. Nobody talking story no more, everybody just eating, or pretending fo eat, staring at the same three grains of rice on the plate.

Little while later, the titah, she wen try smooth um ovah, threw out something about, I dunno, the price of gas going up again, or some da kine news she wen see on her phone, something about one cousin's new job ovah in town. And it wen work, little bit, people wen start talking again, small kine, but the table nevah go back to how relaxed um was before. You could feel um, that stay different kine quiet underneath everything, even when everybody stay laughing again little bit at the end.

The haole in-law, he wen just sit there through all that not saying nothing, probably no even fully understand what wen just happen, but even he wen feel that um was one lolo idea fo say anything more at that point, so he wen keep his mouth shut too.

And that's how um wen end, brah. Nobody wen say sorry, nobody wen even really talk about what wen just happen at all. The family just wen quietly agree, the way um always do, fo nevah bring that up again, not at that table. Everybody know, though, garans, deep down, this go happen again. Some other meal, some other Sunday, some other holiday down the road. Always do. Same table, same quiet, same thing nobody like say out loud, just waiting fo somebody fo brush up against um again without even trying.
