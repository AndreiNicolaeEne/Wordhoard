Eh, dis cold wen come our place and no like leave, stay eleven days straight, no joke, no break even one day. First day already had one bad feeling.

Me: Dis no feel right, da grass get frost on top by da carport, I nevah seen dat since I was one small kid.

Normally by ten o'clock da sun stay warming everything up nice, but dat whole week da cold jus stay, hanging on top us like one wet blanket dat nevah dry.

We wen keep da wood stove going around da clock, das why nobody was sleeping proper, somebody had for get up every two hours fo feed um more wood or else da whole house go freeze up fo real. My father wen stay up most nights, blanket wrapped around him like one burrito.

The father: No can let um die out, yeah? Once um die out, too hard for start um again dis kine weather.

Den da pipe outside by da hose bib wen freeze up solid, first time evah dat wen happen to us. He wen try turn um on fo water da chickens, zero, nutting coming out. Had to melt um with hot water from da stove just fo get water going again.

The eldest: I stay live here since one small girl and I nevah seen um like dis. Get one time back in da day, one bad cold spell, but dis one broke da mout. Dis one worse, garans.

Da worst night was da seventh one I tink, so cold get chicken skin jus from stepping outside fo check da fire. Nex morning we wen find three of da hens stiff, no wen make um through da night. My mother nevah say nutting, jus wen look at um long time before she wen go back inside.

By day eleven da sun wen come back out proper, warm on top da skin, and everybody wen go stand outside, no talk story, jus feeling um. Pau, finally.
