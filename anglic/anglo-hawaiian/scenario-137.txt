Was Sunday, everybody stay loading up da van fo bring da grindz up to da hall, choke pans of rice and da lau lau all stack up by da back gate. Somebody wen leave da latch open and da whole stack wen slide out onto da driveway. Rice all over da concrete, one whole pan of lau lau bus up, and everybody just stay standing there when da foreman wen come out see da mess.

The foreman: Ho. Who wen do this. You was right there loading, ah?

Me: Wasn't me.

The foreman: No give me dat. I seen you standing right by da van just before.

Me: Wasn't me.

The foreman: Same ting though, you was right there when everything wen fall.

Me: I nevah touch nothing by dat gate. Garans.

After dat nobody wen say notting. Da two guys who was pushing da handtruck too fast round da corner, dey just stay by da wall looking down at their slippers, quiet as da wall itself. The foreman stay looking at me hard, like he waiting fo my face crack, but I no give him one ting, just stay same, flat, looking back at him steady. He shake his head, mutter someting about kids these days no like own up, and go back inside fo call up more rice.

Nobody wen say sorry after. Nobody wen say nothing more about um either. I wen pick up da broom and start sweep up da mess, and that was that, the ting still sitting there just how I left it, cause that's how it was, wasn't me, no matter how many times he go ask.
