Okay you guys, quiet down small kine, let me talk story before we eat, da food not going anywhere, get plenty, choke grindz today, aunty wen make three kine and everyting stay broke da mout already just from da smell.

Every year you folks make me tell dis story, so I go tell um proper tonight, start to finish, cause my wife ova dea just smiling, she leave da telling to me. She always do dat. Okay.

So how we wen meet, nothing fancy, no big setup o nothing like dat. I was working yard shift down at da mill back den, and every day after, before go home, I stop by da store fo one cold soda, cool off small kine. Get dis girl behind da counter, ringing up everybody, and she wen give me hard time from day one.

First time I go up dea I ask her fo one extra bag ice, cause my cooler stay leaking bad, and she wen give me one da kine look, no smile, nothing, and she tell me...

The wife: Eh, you like da ice fo free too, o what. Dis not one plantation store, dis one business.

, and I nevah even had one comeback, I just wen laugh like one fool. And from dat day I stay coming back every single day, even da days I no need nothing, just fo see if she going give me hard time again. Till today, all dese years later, she still tell dat story like she wen catch me stealing.

Now, she was da one nevah sure, and I no blame her fo dat. She had one real reason, she was fixing fo go mainland fo nursing school, ticket practically already bought, and she no like start nothing wit one mill boy when she leaving in few months anyway. She wen tell me straight, no sugar on top...

The wife: No make sense fo start someting, brah. I stay leaving.

And das dat, I figured. But I nevah give up easy. I wen write her, even before she left, and after too, every week, rain o shine, tell her all da small kine stuff happening round da camp, who wen get married, whose dog wen run off, nothing important, just so she know I stay thinking about her. Nevah push her fo nothing. And dat wore her down more than any big speech could of. She wen tell me later she start looking forward to da mailman more than her own paycheck, yeah?

Courting time, first time we really wen spend time together proper, not just me buying soda at her register, was when she come home one summer break and we wen go movies in town. And das when da real obstacle wen come, da distance. She stay mainland most da year, I stay here working, and back den one phone call over dea cost you near one whole day pay, garans, so no can call every day like dat. Was mostly letters. Nevah easy, I no goin lie, plenny nights I wen wonder if I was writing to nobody. But we wen push through um, one letter at a time, till she wen pau school and come home fo good.

And das when da slippa thing wen happen, some of you guys already know dis one so I no need explain um too much, but das da night, walking her home from da movies, one rain wen come outta nowhere, and I wen step wrong right into da drain, lost my slippa completely, had to walk da rest da way barefoot while she stay laughing so hard she almost fall down too. Titah still ask me sometimes...

Titah: Eh, wea your slippa.

, and everybody at dis table know exactly what dat mean.

Da moment I knew was serious was when I wen catch myself checking da mailbox every single day, even da days da mailman no even come. Nevah even think twice about it, just wen become one habit. But my titah wen figure out I was serious before my wife did, she wen catch me out dea by da box one too many times and she wen write my wife one letter, tell her, "eh, your boyfriend stay lolo ova here, waiting fo your letter like one lost dog." Das how she wen find out, through my own sister ratting me out. She always did have one big mouth, dat titah.

Da proposal, nothing fancy, no down on one knee at da beach o nothing like dat. Was one regular day, we stay eating plate lunch same kine we always get, and I just wen ask her straight out...

Me: Eh, you like get married o what.

, and she wen laugh so hard she almost choke on her rice, and den she wen say yes just like dat, in between bites. Nevah even had one ring ready, had fo save up fo dat after.

Was about one year between dat and da wedding. Her family wen take little while fo come around, her fadda especially, he had big plans fo her, no like see her marry one mill boy when she had one whole education going fo her. I nevah argue wit him though, I just wen keep showing up, fix da leaky pipe, help um wit da yard, come around even when nobody wen ask me, and slowly he wen come around too. My side, dey nevah even blink, dey wen know her family from church long time already, so was easy fo dem.

Da wedding day, get one thing I remember more than any picture wen catch. Right in da middle of us saying our vows, da generator fo da sound system wen konk out, just like dat, dead quiet, no mic, nothing. Instead of stopping everyting we just wen keep going, talking soft, and everybody wen lean in close fo hear, and all you could hear behind us was da wind and little bit ocean. Was more chicken skin than any photo could ever show, garans.

We had our hard stretch too, like everybody get one. Da mill wen pau few years after we married, shut down fo good, and plenny families round here wen struggle bad, us too. But what wen carry us through was da same ting dat wen carry us dis whole time, we nevah try figure um out alone, we always wen talk story about um, every night, even da bad nights, especially da bad nights. Family wen help too, plenny grindz coming from all sides back den, nobody round here wen go hungry.

But you know what, stay exactly da same since dat very first day at da store. She still give me hard time bout everyting, and I still come back fo more, every single time, garans. No can even walk past dat same corner today without her telling me someting about free ice.

So, to my wife, ova dea just smiling like she always do, letting me tell da whole ting probably wrong. Cheers, you guys. Now let's eat before da food get cold, choke grindz waiting.
