Wen wake up dis morning and da first ting I stay tink of, before even my eyes open all da way, was da carport. Ho. Right away my whole body wen get heavy, like somebody wen sit on top me. Months ago I wen swear, in front everybody too, dat one day, whichever day, I go give up and clean out dat whole carport fo my mother, top to bottom, no leaving nothing fo later. And of course today stay dat day.

I nevah even want get up. Just wen lay dere little while, tinking maybe get one way out, but no can, cannot, I said em already, garans I said em in front da whole family dat one Sunday when she wen ask me fo da hundredth time.

Walking down da road I stay grumbling da whole way, muttering fo myself. "Why I wen open my big mout. Why. Coulda just say nothing dat day, yeah?" Get one neighbor stay watering his plants, he go, "Eh, where you off to so early, so grumpy for?"

Me: Going do one promise I wen make, dat's where.
Neighbor: Ho, chicken skin just hearing dat kine tone. Good luck, brah.

Get to da carport, da mother already get her broom out waiting.

The mother: Bumbye you no show up I was gon call your titah.

Me: I stay here, I stay here.

Choke junk in dat carport, I telling you. Old freezer nobody wen use since forever, broken chair, one whole shelf of da kine bottles and boxes nobody can even remember what stay inside. By midday my back stay broke, my hands all cut up, and I stay tinking, just leave da rest, come back one nother day. But den I remember. I wen swear. So dat kine tinking, I wen shut em down quick, no let em go further.

Kept going anyway, through da worst part, da heavy stuff, sweating like crazy, no stopping. By evening, pau. All done, all hauled out, carport stay empty and clean fo real.

The mother: See, nevah kill you, yeah?

Me: Almost did.

Grumbled da whole day. Nevah once wen quit.
