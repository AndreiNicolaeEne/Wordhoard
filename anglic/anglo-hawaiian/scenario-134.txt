Eh, this was the year, garans. Everybody still stay talking about um, and I no blame them, cuz nothing evah come close, before or since.

Was clear early too, from the first pick you could tell. The count wen come in almost double what we normally get, and everybody was like, brah, something stay happening this season fo real. But get one crop that wen go beyond everything, blow past all da rest by so much, was so much we had fo call in extra hands just fo keep up, before the whole ting wen turn on us. Extra hands wen come from all ova, cousins, neighbor's braddahs, anybody could hold one basket and no complain.

And that count, get nothing close to um from before. The old best, from way back years, was already one good number, but this one wen blow right past um, no contest.

Once the house had enough fo eat and give around to family, still had choke left ova. Some we wen store fo later, some we wen trade out, get one guy down the road who take extra fo his pigs, get others who wanted fo sell um off the truck.

The oldest grower present, the one been doing this longest than anybody, wen walk the rows slow, and he tell, "In all my years, I nevah seen um like this." He credit the rain, wen come right on time, not too much, not too little, and one mild stretch right through the season, no big storm fo knock everything down.

Now, every year somebody bring up one good crop, and first thing anybody say is, "Yeah, but no can compare to that year." Garans that year still the one everybody measure against, the one you talk story about when the young ones stay listening, chicken skin every time somebody tell um right.
