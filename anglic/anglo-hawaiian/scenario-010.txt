So the lamp stay broken, the one from the front room, get crack all down the shade, and nobody wen see nothing, yeah. I call all three come sit down the kitchen table, told them nobody get up till somebody tell me what happened.

Me: Who wen broke the lamp.

The eldest: Not me. I neva even go by there today.

Me: Not you, huh.

The second: Not me either. Was probably the wind, the door stay open this morning, get one big draft come through, or maybe was the cat, she stay jump on top everything lately.

Me: The cat stay outside since this morning, and the door was lock the whole time. So no give me that kine story.

The second: ...oh. Maybe was the eldest then, I seen um by the front room fo real.

The eldest: Lolo, why you lie like that. You the one always by there, playing catch inside when I tell you no can.

The second: Wasn't catch, was, okay maybe I wen go by there quick, but I neva touch da lamp, garans.

The youngest: (looking down) I never said nothing.

Me: Little while ago you said you neva even go by the front room. Now all a sudden you seen your braddah there. Which one true now.

The second: ...both, kind of.

Me: Kind of no good enough, brah. Nobody like own up, everybody get one different story, and none of them match up. So, all three of you, no more TV this week, and no can go beach Saturday either. When one of you like talk story fo real, come find me. Till then, everybody pay.

The eldest: But we neva even...

Me: Pau. That's it, no more talking.
