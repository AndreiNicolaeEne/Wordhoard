The mother: Olgeta pikinini, kam hia kwiktaim! Mi lukim glas bilong windo long sitrum i bruk pinis. Em i bikpela samting we mi baim long stua, na mi laik save stret husat i mekim.

The eldest: Mama, i no mi tru. Mi wantaim narapela wantok, mitupela i stap ausait taim dispela samting i kamap, mitupela i no lukim wanem samting.

The second one: I no mi tu, Mama. Ating strongpela win, em i save blowim strong olsem taim ren i laik kam, na em i bagarapim windo.

The mother: Win? Em inap long bagarapim glas olsem? Tasol mi lukim liklik mak bilong bal i stap klostu long ples we glas i bruk. Win i no gat han bilong kisim bal na tromoi long windo.

The second one: Ah... ating pusi bilong haus i wokabaut antap long windo sil na em i pundaunim wanpela samting, na dispela samting i paitim glas.

The eldest: Yu tok win pastaim, nau yu tok pusi. Yu giaman tumas, brata.

The second one: Yu no ken tok olsem long mi! Ating yu yet i sutim bal i go insait, mi lukluk yu na yu holimpas bal long han bilong yu taim mi kam insait long rum.

The youngest: Mi no gat wok wantaim dispela samting. Yutupela i save toktok planti tasol, na nau yutupela i wari long putim asua long mi nating.

The mother: Olgeta, harim mi gut. I no gat wanpela bilong yupela i laik tokaut stret. Yupela olgeta i save wanem samting i kamap tasol yupela pret long tokaut. Orait, olsem na bai yupela olgeta karim hevi wantaim. Nogat TV dispela wik, nogat gutpela kaikai long Sande, na yupela olgeta bai wasim ol dis na klinim haus, inap wanpela bilong yupela i redi long tokaut husat tru i mekim.
