Yu askem mi wanem i save hapen long dis haos moning taem, orait, yumitupela i sidaon smol, mi bae wokbaot yu insaed long hem, be yu jes lisin nomo, mi bae talem stret olsem i save hapen, semfala olsem evri narafala de.

Fastaem long evriwan, i gat Bubu. Bifo eni alam i save singaot, bifo skae i klia gud, hem i wekap. Mifala evriwan i slip yet, be Bubu hem i no wet long nara man. Hem i pulum blanket isi isi, hem i putum leg blong hem long graon, hem i wokbaot i go aotsaed taem haos i stap kwaet olsem nating. Hem i save mekem sem smol wok evri taem: hem i tekem paea, hem i putum tin wota antap, blong tea i save redi bifo narafala i wekap gud. Dog blong mifala i save wet long doa blong kitin, tel blong hem i save muvum isi taem hem i harem Bubu i kamaot, tufala i save sidaon smol, waet skae i statem kam.

Namba tu man we i save wekap, hem i Papa, be hem i no wekap kwaet olsem Bubu. Evri moning, kok blong niabor i krae, mo Papa i save talem semfala tok:

Papa: "Kok ia, hem i no save tingting long man we i wantem smol slip moa."

Semfala tok, evri taem, be hem i no save se hem i talem semfala samting evri moning blong laef blong hem.

Afta hemia, kontes i save statem, blong wasrum. Papa i mas redi blong wok, Bigwan i mas redi blong skul, tufala i save agiu long doa, evri singel moning, i no gat wan we i save win gud.

Bigwan: "Papa, mi fastaem, skul i no save wet long mi!"
Papa: "Yu gat sikis wik moa blong skul, mi mas go wok distaem, no gat mani, no gat kakae!"
Bigwan: "Yu save mekem was long naet, o long tumoro moning!"

Tufala i toktok toktok, be long en wan i wet, wan i go, semfala olsem yestede, bae i semfala olsem tumoro.

Taem hemia i stap hapen, mi mi wekapem ol pikinini, long sem oda, evri taem, evri moning. Fastaem Bigwan:

Mi: "Bigwan, wekap nau, skul i no wet long yu!"

Afta, mi go long Smolwan, mi tajim leg blong hem isi isi:

Mi: "Kam, kakae i redi, be waswas fastaem, yu no save go long skul olsem doti pikinini."

Semfala tu toktok, semfala oda, evri moning blong mifala.

Wantaem, wan agiu i save kamap, hemia blong bred. Smolwan i wantem las pis, Bigwan i talem se hem i bigwan, em i mas kakae moa:

Smolwan: "Hemia bred blong mi, mi luk hem fastaem!"
Bigwan: "Mi bigwan, mi mas kakae moa blong mi gat strong blong skul!"
Smolwan: "Yu save talem semfala toktok evri moning!"

Mi save lukluk olgeta tu, mi save laf isi, from mi save se long faef minit tufala bae fren bakegen olsem samting i neva hapen.

Dog blong mifala i save gat wan wok blong hem tu long tispela taem, hem i save sidaon aninit long jea blong Smolwan, i wet long wan pis we i save foldaon. Taem Papa i openem doa blong go aot, dog i save bak wan taem, i no moa, semfala wan bak evri moning, olsem hem i talem gudbae long hemwan fasin.

Mitripela, Smolwan, Bigwan, mo mi, mifala i save sidaon raonabaot long tebol taem Papa i stap faet yet long wasrum. Mi save mekem semfala kakae evri moning: raes samfala taem, o bred, tea wetem tin milk. Papa, taem hem i kam aot long wasrum blong kakae kwiktaem, hem i neva jenisim oda blong hem, i sem toktok, evri moning:

Papa: "Tanks, be no sugar."

Mi save laf, mi talem: "Mi save finis, tu wik, tri wik, ten wik, evri wik, semfala toktok."

Wan samting we i save lus evri moning, hemia shus blong Bigwan. I no save wea, be evriwan i save go lukluk long sem treples: aninit long bed, biaen long doa, insaed long wasrum.

Mi: "Bigwan, mi talem yu finis, putum shus long semfala ples!"
Bigwan: "Mi mekem finis, be i lus bakegen, mi no save wanem i hapen!"

Taem kitin i statem gud, ol nois i mikim, be trufala saon we i talem mifala evriwan se moning i statem stret nau, hemia trak blong pablik we i save pasem long rod aotsaed, hem i hon hon wantaem, tri taem, semfala mak evri moning. Taem hemia, evriwan long haos i save mekem hariap hariap.

Papa, hem i wan man we i save leit smol, tu minit, tri minit, evri moning, from hem i stap agiu longtaem tumas long doa blong wasrum. Taem hemia, evriwan i ron: kot i pulum kwiktaem, bag i givim i go long han blong pikinini, be i no gat wan samting we i save lusum, from mifala evriwan i save mekem semfala rot evri moning, han i save wea samting i stap bifo tingting i statem gud.

Wan de long wik nomo, evri samting i jenis, hemia San De. Nogat hariap, nogat toktok agiu, haos i stap kwaet olsem narafala haos. Bubu i save sidaon longtaem long veranda, i lukluk solwota, mi save smelem win blong solwota i kam insaed long haos, i defren tumas long narafala sikis de blong wik. Long San De, Bubu i save go tok smol wetem wantok blong hem long fenis, tufala oldfala meri i save toktok isi isi long taem we i no gat hariap insaed.

Be long ol narafala de, taem evriwan i stanap long doa blong go aot, semfala toktok i kamaot, evri taem, evri moning: "Yu lukaotgud, mi lavem yu, go isi long rod." Evriwan i talem, semfala wan wan toktok, i no save jenis, i no save mestem.

Mi save laf taem mi tingbaot olgeta samting ia, from mifala i save mekem semfala shou evri moning, olsem wan pija we i pleplei bakegen evri taem, be mifala i neva taed long hem. Mi, mi wan meri we i save ronem semfala rot evri moning blong laef blong mi, be mi no save wantem jenisim hem. Yu, we i neva lukim wan moning olsem, bae yu tingting se mifala i krangkrang tumas, be hemia i stret laef blong mifala, hemia haos blong mifala, hemia famili blong yumi evriwan we i save kasem semfala kaen moning, eniwea.
