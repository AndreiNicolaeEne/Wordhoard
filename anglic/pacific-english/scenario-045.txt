Me: Ei, brata, dis wan i no matchim wanem mi bin oda. Mi bin tok tri baton wantaim stret hem, no dis tupela baton wid slant hem ia we stap long dis wan.

The tailor: Wanem yu toktok, brata. Dat wan stret wanem yu bin askim mi. Mi save wanem yu bin tok, mi no rong.

Me: No, lisin gud. Mi stanap ia insaid dis shop dat taim mi bin oda em, mi bin tokaut stret, tri baton, stret hem, no bend nating. Dis wan yu wokim i gat tupela baton nomo, en hem i slant ia, luk yu wan.

The tailor: Orait, orait, no kros yet. Larim mi go lukim de not, mi bin raitim measurement blong yu dat taim, bai mi save wanem tru.

(em i go insaid, i stap smol taim, i kam bek gen holem pepa)

The tailor: Aa, mi lukim nau. De not i tok tri baton, dat wan yu bin tok, em i stret, em i tru. Bat long hem, de not i raitim slant, dastawe mi bin wokim olsem. So baton, em i mi bin rong; hem, em i de not bin rong, i no yu.

Me: So baton nomo we bin raitim rong, hem tasol we mekim dis kamap olsem.

The tailor: Yeah, tupela samting bin miksapim wantaim, bat mi wan i mas fiksim nau. Sori tumas, brata. Givim mi tu de, bai mi putim wanpela moa baton en stretim dis hem gud, bai i redi bilong yu.

Me: Orait den, tu de nomo ah, mi mas werem em Sande kam.

The tailor: No wari, bai mi wok pinis befo dat. Yu kam bek, em bai stap redi, mi promis.

Me: Orait, tenkyu brata. Mi go nau.
