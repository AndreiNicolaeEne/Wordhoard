Meri blong kakae stoa long saed rod, klosap long maket long Vila, i save kastom man ia gud tumas, from hem i kam tri taem long wik, sam taem fofala taem sipos wok i no bisi tumas. Taem hem i wokbaot i kam klosap, meri ia i stat putumap kakae blong hem finis, bifo hem i talem wan wod.

The owner: Ei, yu bakegen! Rais wetem jikin kari, wetem kabis, olsem yu save oltaem, yes?

The customer: Yes, be tede, putum extra chili, mi wantem strong.

The owner: Oraet, bae mi putum nomo. Yu stap oraet? Wok i olsem wanem?

The customer: Mi stap gud nomo, be wok i had tumas dis wik. Olgeta pipol oli wantem samting kwiktaem nomo, i no gat taem blong sidaon.

The owner: Yu wok tumas, brata. Yu mas kakae gud, nomata.

The customer: Hemia nao mekem mi kam bak long yu, blong wanem mi save kam tri taem long wik.

The owner: Mi save yu tumas, taem yu no kam wan dei, mi ting se yu foget mifala finis!

The customer: Nating, mifala i wantok, mi no save fogetem yu.

The owner: Yumitufala i olsem brata nomo, i tru!

Taem kakae i stap kuk, tufala i stap toktok isi isi, olsem oltaem.

The owner: Bisnis, be? I stap go slou slou dis wik, be i stap oraet, from plante man olsem yu i save kam bak.

The customer: Yu save mekem kakae i swit tumas, hemia nao mekem mifala i save kam bak.

The owner: Kakae i finis, hemia. Rais, jikin kari, mo kabis blong yu, strong olsem yu wantem.

The customer: Tank yu tumas. Hamas?

The owner: Faef handred vatu nomo.

The customer: Hemia. Bae mi kam bak, sem dei, sem taem, wik ia i kam.

The owner: Yu oraet, brata. Lukim yu bakegen!
