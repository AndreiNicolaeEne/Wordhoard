The traveler: Excuse me, mi laik askim, wanem rot bai go long maket?

The stranger: Yu tanim lep long dispela fas korna, orait wokabaut i go stret, bai yu lukim bikpela stoa long namba tu korna, dispela em i mak bilong yu.

The traveler: Hamas taim bai em i kisim, sapos mi wokabaut tasol?

The stranger: Em i no longwe. Inap fiftin minit yu kamap pinis.

The traveler: Tenkyu tumas, brata.

The stranger: Nogat wari. Gutpela wokabaut long yu.
