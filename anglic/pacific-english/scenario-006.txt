The eldest: Orait, lisin gut, liklik brata, bipo ol narapela wantok i kam. Yumi nidim sikispela man i go inap eit, no moa, nogut planti tumas na gem i kamap kranki.

The little one: Orait. Banis i stap we, long dispela sait?

The eldest: Long dispela sait, banis i go inap long bikpela mango tri, yu no ken pasim dispela. Long hapsait, banis i stap long solwara sait, i go daun inap long dispela drum. Ol i mas stap insait long displa banis oltaim.

The little one: Na sapos wanpela ranawe i ran i go ausait taim chaser i ranim em?

The eldest: Sapos em i go ausait taim yu ranim em, em i stap aut stret, nogat gutpela eskius. Displa lo i strong tumas, i no gat wei raun long en.

The little one: Na chaser bilong fes raun, olsem wanem yumi makim em?

The eldest: Mipela olgeta i statim wanpela liklik singaut, wan-tu-tri, na lasman i kisim tas em i statim. Sapos mitripela tasol i kam pastaim, orait mitripela i ken statim smol gem, bihain ol narapela wantok i joinim.

The little one: Na tas, em wanem kain? Mi save lukim ol narapela i tasim liklik, i no tru tas.

The eldest: Lukluk gut nau. Tas i mas tasim skin, han o sol bilong yu tasol, i no ken tasim klos, dispela i no kaunt. Sapos em i tasim tru bodi bilong yu, yu frisim wantu, yu mas sanap stret, lek i no ken mubim liklik. Yu stap frisim inap narapela runner, wanpela i stap ran yet, i kam tasim yu na singaut "lus!" - dispela taim tasol yu ken ran gen.

The little one: Raun i pinis wataim?

The eldest: Raun i pinis sapos olgeta pikinini i frisim pinis, o sapos chaser i les tumas na em i givap. Namba tu chaser em i namba wan man ol i tasim long raun bipo, dispela lo i stret olowe.

The little one: Sapos mi mekim rong long fes raun?

The eldest: Nogat wori, ba. Fes raun bilong yu, mi promisim, em i no bai kaunt.
