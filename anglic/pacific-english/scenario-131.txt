The rain comes down just gone midday, after weeks of drai spel nomo.
The eldest: Ren i stap kam, kam luk, ale!
Everyone rushes for the window, then out the door onto the wet grass.
The mother: Em i tru rein nao, mifala i wet tumas, tangkyu tumas.
The uncle throws his arms out wide to the sky.
The uncle: Ren, yu kambak blong stap nomo, bai mi swim long yad!
They stap ausaed nomo, harem ren i foldaon.
