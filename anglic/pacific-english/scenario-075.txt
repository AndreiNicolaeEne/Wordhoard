Mi stap long queue long stoa long Point Cruz, wet nomoa, taem mi harem wanfala voice singaot strong long mi baksaed.

The old friend: Ei! Weitim, hem yu nomata?! No, no, hem no fasin blong mi, mi no bilivim ai blong mi!

Me: Ah, hem mi ya tru! Bro, wanem nao yu duim raonabaot long hia, mi tingim yu stap Auki nomoa!

The old friend: Mi stap Auki bifor, distaem mi shiftim kam Honiara nao. Bro, hao long nao iumitufala no lukim each other?

Me: Mi tingim hem moa long ten yia nao stret. Last taem iumi storyim long em, iumitufala stap long primary skul yet.

The old friend: Tru ya! Skul pinis, evriwan go seleva rod. So tell mi, wanem nao stori blong yu distaem? Yu wok wea?

Me: Mi wok long haospitol nao, mi mared pinis, mitufala garem wanfala pikinini gele, hem smol yet. Yu yasef?

The old friend: Mi wok long wanfala kampani long taon, mi mared long meri blong mi tu, mitufala garem tu pikinini nao.

Me: Ei, yu tingim taem yu fol daon long solwara from canoe blong yu, taem iumi olketa go swim long aftanun?

The old friend: (laf) Ah, no askim mi long em! Olketa boi bin laf long mi tumas, mi wet baed for wanfala wik!

Me: Mi save tu, mi laf yet distaem taem mi tingim.

The old friend: Bro, yu save wanem nao happen long wantok blong iumitufala long skul, wea hem stap distaem?

Me: Mi no save nomoa. Last mi herem, hem go stap Ostrelia o samwea, bat no wanfala save talem stret.

The old friend: Sem ya, mi askim planti pipol pinis, no save.

Me: Orait, distaem iumitufala mas kipim contact, no mekem sem mistek olsem bifor.

The old friend: Yes stret, givim namba blong yu, bae mi ringim yu.

Me: Hemia namba blong mi. Bae mi promisim yu, distaem bae mi followim tru, mi no forgetim olsem bifor ya.

The old friend: Orait bro, bae iumitufala storyim moa long fon.
