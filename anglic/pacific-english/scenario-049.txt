Me and this wantok bin knowing each other since primary school time, same island, same footy team long time back. He stap owing me two hundred and fifty dollar for that outboard part I bought him in the wet, close on eight month gone now. I see him coming up from the wharf that arvo and I call out to him easy, no rough voice, just straight up.

Me: Cuz, that money you owe me, the two-fifty for the motor part, eight month now since the wet season. I not chasing you hard, but I gotta ask you today, you got something for me?

My friend: Ah bala, sorry ay. This week been one hard week. Missus and them pikinini, school thing, everything come up same time, tumas expense.

Me: You bin say that one already. Remember Easter time you tell me next pay for sure, true God, and that pay come and gone, nothing. Now you giving me the same story again.

My friend: Ah come on now cuz, I not lying to you. Things just tight, that's all. You think I forget you or what?

Me: I not saying you forget me. I saying the date you give me already pass one time, and now you giving me new date. How I know this one different from before?

My friend: Eh, why you talking to me like that now, like I'm some kind of no-good. I'm your wantok, I never run from you yet.

The wind come up off the solwara and neither one of us say nothing for a bit, just the ferry horn out past the wharf. Then he look down, quiet now, and dig in his pocket.

My friend: Look, I got fifty dollar on me. Take that liklik bit now, and I give you the rest bai end of month, true God this time.

Me: Alright, I take the fifty. But end of month I coming to find you. Not waiting on you to come find me this time.
