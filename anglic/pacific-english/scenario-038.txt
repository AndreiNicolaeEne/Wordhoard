Ei wantok, sidaon fastaem, letem mi tokim yu stori bilong dis fala day, from stat kasem en, from seven kasem seven, dat mekem ten hour stret we mi wok.

Moning tumas mifala stat, san no kam ap gud yet, body bilong mi no eni wam, leg blong mi stil slip, bat boss i singaut kwiktaem, "Iufala redi o nomoa," so mifala mas go stret, no eni taem for warmap. Fastfala samting mifala duim, hem i karem blok en sand kam long trak go daon long ples, en dat wan i setim pace bilong evriwan narafala samting behaen, boss i wantem evri sak pinis befor ten o'clock.

No longtaem nomoa, hand blong mi start complainim, skin i katkatim from rop bilong sak, en back blong mi tu, hem i stat sing singaut long mi. Mifala kisim smol brek raon leven, kaikai wanfala biskit, dring smol wata nomoa, bat hem i sot tumas, body i no rest gud, mifala mas go bak long wok kwiktaem.

Aftanun ia hem i wosfala taem tru, san i strong tumas, sweat i ron olabaot, mi tingting for kwit go long haos, bat mi tingim pikinini bilong mi stap wet, so mi stap, mi stap.

Wanfala gudfala samting nomoa, digim hol bilong septik, mitufala wetim narafala wakaman finisim kwiktaem winim wanem mifala tingting, dat wan i wanfala smol lift long had day ia. Boss i givim mi foti dola stret for dat wan wok seleva, mi hapi long dat.

Tumoro mi no save yet, bae hem i sem wok o bae mi restim body bilong mi, from mi barava taed nau, wantok, wok hem i pinis, en mi laik slip nomoa.
