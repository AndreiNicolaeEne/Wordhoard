Mipela bin go solwara side early, chasing wanpela bigfala fish out near the point, and the motor i stap orait all the way till we come round by them rock. Then i cut out. Cut out three time, four time, and we drifting close before the skipper save fix em again. By time we tie up longside the jetty everybody wet, everybody cranky, and that whole mob from the shed come down lukluk wanem bin happen.

The skipper im face hot already, im come straight up holding the empty fuel can, no wasting time.

The skipper: You the one bin forget the fuel, ah. Drum empty and I bin tell that mob before we go, check em drum, check em drum. You no listen.

The young one: Not me.

The skipper: I not joking now. You bin loading gear that morning, you bin forget fuel bilong the motor, mipela nearly drown out there because bilong you.

The young one: I swear, not me.

That two cousin what bin loading the boat that morning, them just stap there, lukluk down, kicking sand round their feet, not one word coming out from them mouth.

The skipper im waiting for some big talk back, some cry, but the young one face i stap same way the whole time through, no shame there, no fright there, just still, like solwara before wind come up.

The old lady from the shed she call out, ei, leave em, kaikai stap getting cold, but nobody move straight away, everybody just stap lukluk.

The young one no say nothing more. Im pick up im bag, im turn, im walk off up the jetty, and that denial i stap there still, standing exactly where im bin leave em, nobody touching em, nobody taking em back.
