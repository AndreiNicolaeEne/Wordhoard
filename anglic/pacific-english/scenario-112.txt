The traveler: Draeva, hamas nao fea go long taon?
The driver: Twenti dola nomoa.
The traveler: Twenti i tumas ya, bae mi peim twelef nomoa.
The driver: Twelef i smol tumas ya, mi no satisim wetim datwan, rod hem longwe tu.
The traveler: Orait, hamas nao yu wandem stret?
The driver: Sikstin nomoa, mi lusim fofala blong yu nomoa.
The traveler: Orait, sikstin nao, hop insaet nau.
The driver: Yes, sindaon gud, mifala go stret long taon nau.
