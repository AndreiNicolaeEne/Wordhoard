Dat mani blong da haus i stap oltaim insaid da tin long kitchen shelf, la hondred dola, moa o les, em i blong dis mont kaikai an light bill an nara liklik ting we i mas kamap. Mi go for tekem samting, mi lukluk, an mi si em i lait tumas, i no rait. Mi kaunim. Mi kaunim gen, isi isi, no wanpela mistek insaid. Mi go luk long odarwan ples we mi save putim sometaim, insaid da drawer aninit da bed. Nating stap dea tu.

Mi kolim ol insaid haus, isi fastaim, no wandem meikim big ting.

Me: Enibodi bin tekem mani from da tin, o muvim em go somwe? Sipos yu bin tekem em fo lukim nara samting, orait, tok mi nomoa.

The eldest: No mi wan, mi no tasim dat tin.

The second one: Mi no sabe wanem mani yu toktok.

The youngest: Mi no bin stap kolsap dat tin, mi bin stap sku.

Ol i sem denai, flat wan, no hesitate insaid.

Bihain, mi tingting stret: wan pipol tasol i bin stap wan haus, taim dis mani i go lus. Mi go stret long em, no isi tumas dis taim.

Me: Yu wan tasol i bin stap ya dat de. Nomoa. Yu tekem dis mani.

The second one: No! Mi no tasim em, mi tok stret long yu.

Me: Husat narawan i go tekem sipos i no yu? Mani i no wokabaut wan wan.

The second one: Yu jas wandem someone fo blemim, cos mi wan i bin stap! Dat i no minim mi tif!

Voice i go antap nau, no bekim isi.

Me: Mi no wandem blemim nating, mi wandem trut!

The second one: Trut em dis, mi no tekem! Yu tingting mi tif long yu wan haus, orait, tinktink olsem!

Nomoa toktok bihain dat. Ol i stap kwaet, ol i vex, an nating i solve.
