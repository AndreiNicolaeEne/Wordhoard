Me: Wanfala samting i hapen long mi last naet, i tru tumas, mi mas tokim yu.
My wantok: Ating yu slip nomoa, hed bilong yu i wokabaut tasol long naet, o yu dring tumas.
Me: Nogat, brata! Ai blong mi i open stret, tingting blong mi i klia olgeta, mi no drimwin wan samting.
My wantok: Orait, tokim mi stret wanem samting yu lukim.
Me: Yu save, mitufala i save wokabaut planti taem long datfala solwara saed. Mi stap aotsaed blong smok taem mi lukim wanfala laet i stap muv i go antap long liklik hil klosap long solwara, i no gat wanpela man i holem.
My wantok: Ating em fisherman nomoa, em i holem tos laet bilong em, i wokabaut i go daon long solwara.
Me: Nogat tru! Taed i go daon pinis, solwara i draem pinis, olketa kanu i stap taehed long haus, no wanpela man i stap aotsaed. Mi lukluk raonabaot, mi save wanem mi lukim.
My wantok: Maet em i laet bilong bot i stap longwe, yu lukim reflection nomoa long wota.
Me: Nogat! Bikfala mango tri i stap namel long mi wetem solwara, laet i go stret pas long tri ia, i no save kam from solwara saed, tumas!
My wantok: Yu tingting tumas nomoa, brata, hed blong yu i save mekim trik long yu taem yu taed.
Me: Mi no giaman wanpela samting! Mi swearim tru antap long pikinini bilong mi, olketa toktok ia i tru wan wan. Bai yu luksave taem yu lukim seleva.
My wantok: Mi harem toktok blong yu, tasol mi no bilivim, samting olsem i no save hapen long ples ia.
