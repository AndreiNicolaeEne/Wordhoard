Me: Your mum, ey. Im bin start on the tucker soon as we bin sidown. "Dis one bin cook it too salty," im bin say, right there longa everybody, no shame. Nobody bin aks her.

My old man: Ah, that's just how im talk. Oldpeople way. Im don't mean nothing by it.

Me: Im mean something every time doe. Every time we go there dat mob all bin look at my plate like I done something wrong.

My old man: Im proud woman, that's all. Grow up different way, that generation.

Me: And your dad, ey. That story 'bout the buffalo and the ute, im bin tell it same way, same words, three time now. I could tell it miself, longways, start to finish.

My old man: Im like that story true. Im proud of it, that one.

Me: I know im proud. But three time, bala. My face bin start hurting from smiling.

My old man: ...True. Three time brabli too much, that one.

Me: See.

My old man: I never bin say you wrong now.

Me: And then dat goodbye longa door! Forty-five minute. Garra go, garra go, still standing there. Your mum garra tell you 'bout every cousin, every one dat sick, every one dat gonna have baby...

My old man: True, forty-five minute, unna. I bin standing there thinking, we already bin say goodbye ten minute ago, why we still here longa this door.

Me: See, you know I'm right now.

My old man: I never bin say you wrong. Dem oldpeople just lonely I reckon, that's why they hold on longa the door like dat.

Me: I know, I know. Still, next time I'm waiting inside the car.

My old man: You always say that one.

Me: This time true, bala.

My old man: Alright, alright. Forty-five minute. Deadly.
