The elder: Kam, mi holem yu smol taem nomoa, bifoa yu go.
The elder: Yu lukluk gud long yu seleva, no wakabaot long naet, kaikai gud, orait?
The traveler: Bae yumitufala tok gen stret taem plen i lan, promis.
The elder: Tekem displa selen fastaem, wantok, hemi liklik nomoa but bae hem helpem yu.
The traveler: Taem mi kam bek, bae mi bringim gudfala samting blong yu.
