The aunt: Mi kam story smol nomoa. Yestadei toktok i go raon long haos blong misinari, mi harem stret, wanfala sista blong mi long church i tok, datfala young man, hem i gud fren tumas, no eni bikhed samting long hem.

The niece: Mi harem nomoa, Aunty.

The aunt: Wan, waka blong hem i stret. Hem i stap waka long wharf, pei blong hem i kam evri fortnaet, no wanfala dei nomoa hem i les fo go waka. Tu, tingting blong hem i kwaet tumas, hem i no fren long dring o raoa, hem i save sidaon gud, hem i no eni kaen bikhed man. Tri, famili blong hem, olketa gud pipol, church pipol, no wanfala trabol i stap insaed long famili ya. Trifala samting ya, hem i naf fo mekem yu tingting gud long hem.

The niece: Mi save, Aunty, mi save.

The aunt: So wanem nao rong long hem?

The niece: I no olsem samting i rong, Aunty. Waka blong mi hem i fulap tumas distaem, mi no redi yet fo tingim datkaen samting.

The aunt: Yu tok olsem, bat mi rimembarem cousin-sista blong yu, sem mak nomoa. Hem i tok "mi no redi yet, mi no redi yet," mifala storyim hem tumas taem. Lukim hem nao, hem i hapi tumas, hem i sei sapos mifala no kipim strong insaed long hem, bae hem i lusim wanfala gudfala samting.

The niece: Mi save, Aunty, bat mi no sem case olsem cousin-sista blong mi.

The aunt: Orait, mi no fosim yu. Toktok blong mi i pinis nao, bat ofa ya bae i stap. Eni taem yu redi, yu talem mi nomoa, bae mi go bak long famili ya.
