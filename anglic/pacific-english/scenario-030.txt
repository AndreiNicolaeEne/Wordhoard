The pot is already going on the back burner and I got my phone out this time, propped against the sugar tin, because every Christmas I say I bai write down the kaukau pudding and every Christmas I forget.

Me: Aunty, dispela kaukau pudding bilong yu, mi laik save nau, the real one, not the shortcut one you give the pikinini when you're rushing.

The aunty: Orait, orait, no rush mi first. Number one ting, yu kisim kaukau, the orange kind, na maybe three, four big ones, depends how planti wantok bai kam kaikai.

Me: Three or four, orait. Then?

The aunty: Yu boilim em pastaim till em i soft tru, then yu mashim, smooth smooth, no lumpy. Eh, mi lusim wanpela samting there, sori. Yu mas putim de coconut cream, no forget dispela wan, mama bilong mi always tok, sapos yu lusim em, em i no kaukau pudding, em i just mash kaukau nomoa.

Me: How much coconut cream we talking.

The aunty: Liklik, one smol tin, fresh one better sapos yu gat. Then sugar, yu putim, yu testim, yu putim moa sapos i no swit inap.

Me: And how much sugar exactly?

The aunty: Ah, dispela wan mi never measure em, tru. Mi lukluk nomoa, mi test long tongue, sapos i swit inap long mi, em stap orait. Cannot give you exact number, sorry, na my eye na tongue i save, not my hand.

Me: Fair enough. So the order, start to finish?

The aunty: Boilim kaukau, mashim, putim coconut cream, putim sugar, mixim strong, wrapim long banana leaf, then steam em, that's the last one and that's the one you never rush.

Me: That's the step grandmother always warned about?

The aunty: Yes tru, she'd get cross quick. She always tok, no rush the steam, sapos yu rush em, em i stap raw insait, outside i luk orait but insait i no cook.

Me: How you know it's done then, no clock?

The aunty: Smell bilong em i change, banana leaf i go dark, and yu press em liklik long spoon, em i firm now, i no wet. That's how, nose na eye tell you, not the time.

Me: Can I use margarine instead of the coconut cream if I run out?

The aunty: Mmm, yu can, liklik bit, but em i no gonna taste the same tru. I'd say yes, but only sapos yu gat nothing else, never first choice.
