Dijan cold we bin gerrim this raun, i bin one brabli hard one, en i bin mo kol than wi bin expectim, from di very first day already. Yumi sabi how kol i can get roun ya, bat dijan wan, i bin samting els.

Mipela bin garra keep di faya bon-bat raun di clock, day en night, no stop, cos soon as im go daun lilbit, cold i kaman insaid en kutum you rait through. Mipela bin tekit tantaim, wanbala sidan garram di faya while najawan slip, den swap oba wen di taim kaman. Dat mob pikinini bin rag up nekst to faya, no muv from deya, blanket ap to dei chin.

Bin eleven day laik dat, straight, no brik, no warm day kaman between. Ol pipe bilong wi outsaid, im bin freeze solid, wi neva sin dat before, ebala. Wata i no stap kaman from di tap nomo, so mipela bin garra go daun la riba garram bucket jis blong wash en drink.

Wi oldbala la haus, im sabi toktok bout oldtaim wen im bin lilgel, im bin sidan garram im blanket rong im shoulder, lukluk outsaid.

The oldest: "Ai bin sabi one cold laik dijan, longtaim, wen ai bin small gel. Bat dijan wan, i beatim dat wan. Dijan wan i wosbala."

Di seven night, di middle wan, bin di wosbala wan bilong ol. Frost bin lay oba ol di grong laik sheet, en wi bodi bin shekshek from kol, no matter bigmob blanket wi bin garram. Dat night mipela bin lus wanbala chicken, i bin jis die stiff, wi faindim im in di morning, no muv, no sing out.

Wen di eleven day bin op pinis, en di san bin kaman ap strong, no wind, en di warm bin tach wi skin agen, evriwan bin kaman autsaid, sidan la san, laughing. I bin brabli gudwan, laik mipela mob bin cam bek to laif.
