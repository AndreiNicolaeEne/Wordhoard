Dijan one bin the best gadin year we ebber seen longtime, true. Early on, when the first lot start comin off, mob could tell straight up this one gona beat every other one, comin in near double what we oldei get off that same block.

One thing stand up more than anythin else - the pumpkin. That wan bin give bigmob, more than mob could count first up, so we bin count im proper: forty-odd bag off that one patch alone. Before that, best we ebber had was maybe twenty-two, twenty-three bag, and dat bin years back now, longtime before the young ones even born yet.

Had to bring extra mob in just fo pull im all in before it start turnin bad longa the vine, dumaji it don't wait for nomo. Family mob come up from downriver, some come in from town, everybody out there mornin til dark, pullin, packin, loadin im up longa the truck. Whatever we never need fo weself, we store some, put im way fo later, and some we sellim on, trade im to mob from other community, get bit a money and bit a swap back.

The eldest, one bin gadin longa dat same ground since young fella time, look at im all longtime and said:

The eldest: "Bin gadin longtime now, me, from lilbit girl. Never seen im like dis one. Rain bin come right time, not too much not too little, and no big hot spell fo burn im off longa the middle a the season. Dat's why im give like dat."

Everybody agree, dat mob who bin there and dat mob who only hear about im aftawed. Even now, when mob toktok bout gadin, they measure im up against dat year. "Was it good like dat year?" they say. "Was it near dat one?" Najing come close since, but mob still oldei hopin.
