Mi wekap bifo kokorako i singaot yet, taem sky i no gat laet nomo, hem i blak stret. Mi harem papa i wokabaot long haos, i openem doa blong kitchen, i singaotem evriwan.

The father: Yufala wekap nao. San i klosap kamap, yumi mas go bifo hem i hot tumas.

Mifala evriwan i wekap slo slo, boyfala tufala blong mi tufala i stap sluk long mat, mama i stap boelem wota blong tea finis. Papa i stanap long dua, i tokbaot wanem wok nao bae i stap tude. Hem i talem se tude yumi mas go finisim gaden blong yam mo taro long ples antap, from wik i kambak antap mo rein i save foldaon strong.

Papa i givim smolfala wok long smolboe, hem i yangfala tumas, i no save wok wetem naef yet. Papa i talem se:

The father: Yu, smolboe, yu karem basket, yu go pikimap ol kokonas we hem i foldaon, mo yu putum long wanfala hip klosap long trak. Yu no digim samting, yu jes karem nomo.

Smolboe i hapi tumas long dispela wok, hem i ronron i go, hem i save singaot i kam bak we hem i faenem kokonas we i bikwan.

Bigfala wok, we hem i strong wok tumas, papa i putum long biggestfala boe blong mifala, we hem i strongfala man nao, mesol blong yad. Papa i talem se hem mas digim graon blong taro long saed antap, from graon ya hem i hadhad, i gat plande ston. Bigboe i teksem digstik mo naef, i no toktok tumas, i jes stat digim wan, digim tu, swet i stap ronron long fes blong hem stret we san i kamap.

Mitufala narafala, mi wetem sista blong mi, mifala i go pulumaot ol liklik yam long saed daon, we hem i isi wok, graon i sofsof. Mifala i wok wok, i putum ol yam long lain, i no talemaot naf yet.

Taem yumi finisim fastfala pat blong gaden, evriwan i sanap wan minit, i lukluk raon. Papa i chenjim wok, i talem se:

The father: Nao yumi go long narafala pat. Yufala wetem naef, yufala go katemdaon ol liklik bus we i stap kavremap ol taro. Mi bae mi go wetem bigboe long saed antap.

Mifala i muv olsem, evriwan i jenisim wok blong hem, i no stap wanples tumas taem.

Long taem sun i klosap long midel blong skae, sista blong mi cousin, we hem i yangfala meri, i stat go slo slo, i no save keskip wetem yumi. Mifala i lukim, be i no gat wan we i tok raoni long hem. Nomo, big sista blong mifala i muv kwaet i go stanap klosap long hem, i stat digim sem lain wetem hem, tufala i wok tugeta, i no gat toktok, i jes wok. Afta smol taem, cousin i fil gud bakegen, i save kambak strong.

Long midel de, mama i karem kakae i kam, wetem smolgel we i save help long haos. Mama i singaot longwe:

The mother: Yufala kam kakae! Laplap i stap, mo tea i stap hot.

Evriwan i sanap, i wasem han long wan bucket wota, i go sidaon andanit long big tri. Mifala i kakae wetem han, i toktok isi isi, i spel wan haf aoa. Bigboe i tok stret long naeba blong hem:

The eldest: Yu digim tumas slo, brata. Mi save finisim tu lain taem yu finisim wan nomo.

The workmate: Ha! Yu tok olsem from yu no karem heavi basket olsem mi. Yu wet, taem yu karem basket ya bae yu no tok bigmaot moa.

Evriwan i laf, i gat smol arguwe long stael blong digim, wan i talem se yu mas digim raonabaot fastaem bifo yu pulumaot, narafala i talem se nomo, yu jes pulum stret, i seif taem. I no wan bigfala kaskas, i jes toktok blong pastaem, evriwan i laf mo go bak long wok.

Wetem taem, klaod i stat kambak long saed blong solwota, i blak tumas, win i stat kam strong. Papa i lukluk antap, i wari:

The father: Klaod ya i luk nogud. Bae rein i foldaon. Yumi mas jenisim plan, yumi no save go long farawe gaden nomo, yumi mas stap klosap, yumi mas kwiktaem finisim ples ia festaem.

Mifala i chenisim plan olsem hem i talem, evriwan i wok kwiktaem, be klaod i pas long narafala saed, win i stap nomo, rein i no foldaon. San i kambak aot long klaod, i hot moa. Papa i talem se:

The father: Klaod i pas. Yumi save wok moa. Be yumi mas fasfas, taem i no plande.

Mifala i wok tru long apinun, i no spel tumas, evriwan i stap wetem wok blong hem.

Long apinun, samting i gowrong. Naef blong bigboe i brekem, hendel blong hem i splitim taem hem i digim wan bigfala ston. Bigboe i singaot:

The eldest: Naef blong mi i bagarap!

Papa i ronkam, i lukluk, i talem se i no gat narafala naef long ples ia. Mifala i stap wari smoltaem, be papa i tekem naef blong hemwan, i givim long bigboe, i tekem wanfala olfala naef we i stap blong katem kokonas, i yusum olsem. I no gat bigfala trabol, mifala i jes chenisim wok, bigboe i go daon i helpem mitufala pulumaot yam, mifala i lego naef wok i go long hem.

San i stat go daon, laet i stat go grei. Papa i singaot bikmaot:

The father: Yufala fasap nao! San i klosap go daon, yumi mas finisim las pat bifo tudak i kam.

Evriwan i lego wanwan wok blong hem, i kam tugeta long las pat blong gaden, we i gat sam yam we i stap yet. Mama, papa, bigboe, mitufala, cousin, smolboe, evriwan i wok wantaem long sem ples, wan i digim, wan i pulumaot, wan i karem i go long trak, i no gat taem blong spel.

Taem las yam i kam aot long graon, evriwan i sanap, i lukluk basket, sak, mo bag we i fulap long trak. Papa i kaontem, i talem se:

The father: Yumi karem samwe long ten bag taro, faef bag yam, mo tri bag kokonas. Hem i gudfala de.

Evriwan i harem tayad tumas, be i harem hapi long haet blong hem. Mifala evriwan i sidaon long graon, i no muv, leg blong mi i pen, han blong mi i swet mo dat, bodi blong mi i hevi olsem ston. Mama i luk raon long evriwan, i smael isi.

Bifo eniwan i save go sidaon stret long haos, mama i lukluk long smolboe, i talem se:

The mother: Yu, boe, bifo yu go spel, yu go karem wanfala bucket wota kam, mifala i mas wasem leg blong yumi. Afta yu save go sidaon.

Smolboe i grone smol, be i stanap, i karem bucket, i go daon long wota. Nomo bihaen dispela, evriwan i save sidaon, san i go daon finis, mo tudak i kavremap gaden.
