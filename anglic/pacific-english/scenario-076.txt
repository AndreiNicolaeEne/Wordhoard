Me: Wantok, mi mas tokstret long yu. Dis fasin bilong yu fo klaem antap long tray bilong trak taem hemi ranabaot fast fast long rod, hemi mas stop nao. Mi lukim yu yesta, hemi barava klosap kilim yu dae, no gammon.

My friend: Eh, cool daon. Mi save duim dis samting longtaem finis. Nating hem happen long mi.

Me: Nomoa dis taem. Mi standap long saed rod, mi lukluk long yu taem trak hem swing raon long korna, yu barava foldaon go, wan han bilong yu nomoa i holem, and mi no save duim wanem. Hat bilong mi hem ranabaot fast fast, han bilong mi shakem shakem, mi no save stopem. Yu no lukim datfala fasin from insaed, be mi lukim, and hemi spolem mi tumas.

My friend: Orait, orait, be nating hem hapen long mi. Mi olraet nomoa.

Me: Yu fogetim tu yia bifoa nao? Taem yu jumpim daon from trak ia, yu brekem han bilong yu, wik faef yu no save duim wanfala samting. Dat wan hem smolfala samting nomoa. Sapos yu foldaon andanit long wheel bilong trak, hemi no bae han nomoa. Bae hemi hed bilong yu. Bae yu no gat sekan chance moa fo laf, fo talem sori, fo duim eni samting.

My friend: Mi harem tok bilong yu.

Me: Sapos samting olsem hemi happen long yu, huia bae stap krae krae? Mami bilong yu bae stap wetim yu kam haos, yu no kam. Pikinini bilong yu bae askem, "Wea nao dadi?" Mifala olketa wantok bilong yu bae stap sore tumas, oltaem, no save finisim.

My friend: Orait, orait. Yu raet, tok bilong yu i tru. Mi no promisim mi bae stopem evri taem, be bae mi trae fo lukaotem mi seleva. Mi no bae jumpim trak taem hemi ranabaot fast olsem, and bae mi holem gud taem mi klaem antap.
