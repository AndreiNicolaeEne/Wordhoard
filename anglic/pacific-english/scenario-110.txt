The uncle: Orait, kandere, yu stap long hia nau, tokim mipela stret. Em i olsem wanem, dispela taun bilong yu?

The cousin: Em i bikpela tumas, uncle. Yu no inap tingim. Mosbi em i liklik ples nating sapos yu putim em i stap arasait long dispela taun. Mi save tingim taim mi go pas long em, ai bilong mi i op nabaut, mi tingim, olsem wanem bikpela ples i stap olsem?

The aunty: Bikpela olsem wanem stret? Yu ken raitim long mipela?

The cousin: Orait, tingim olsem, long Mosbi yu ken drangan long kar i go long wan sait i go kam bek long narapela sait, em inap wanpela aua nating, tasol long dispela taun bilong mi nau, sampela de em i winim wanpela aua stret, mi tok tru, bilong go long wanpela sait bilong taun i go kamap long narapela sait. Ol trafik i pas olgeta, olgeta kar i stap wetim wetim, i no muv.

The young brother: Nois tu, em i olsem wanem? Mipela long hia i save harim rooster tasol long moning na sampela dok i krai.

The cousin: Nois i stap oltaim tumas, brata. Ol trak i wok long ran de na nait, ol pipia trak, ol siren bilong ambulans, na ol pipel i wokabaut i mekim nois long ol rot. Long fes wik mi no inap silip gut, ia bilong mi i no save long em. Tasol nau bodi bilong mi i lainim pinis, mi save slip nabaut long nois, i olsem em i stap tasol, mi no harim moa.

The grandmother: Na haus bilong yu, em i we? Mi tingim bikpela haus wantaim gaden bilong yu, i olsem haus bilong tumbuna long hia.

The cousin: Nogat, tumbuna, haus bilong mi em i liklik tru, wanpela rum na kitchen wantaim, i no gat gaden. Ol i kolim "unit". Em i sindaun antap long narapela haus, olsem em i wanpela bilong dispela bikpela bilding. Long Mosbi mipela save gat spes bilong wokabaut raun long ples, tasol long hia haus bilong mi em i smolpela tru, tasol i stap klostu long wok bilong mi, olsem em orait tu.

The uncle: Na prais bilong ol samting, em i olsem wanem, i winim bilong hia o nogat?

The cousin: Em i antap tumas, uncle. Rent bilong dispela liklik haus bilong mi wanpela wik, em inap baim planti kaikai long maket bilong hia inap tu mun. Kaikai long stua tu i dia moa, tasol pei bilong wok, em tu i bikpela moa. Olsem na taim yu skelim gut, sampela samting i orait, sampela i no orait.

The aunty: Orait, na ol pipel long hia, ol i pren o nogat? Ol i luk gut long yu o ol i les long ol niupela man i kam long ples bilong ol?

The cousin: Em i no isi long tok stret, aunty. Sampela taim ol i amamas long toktok wantaim yu, ol i givim smail long yu long rot, ol i halp yu taim yu askim rot. Tasol i no olsem long ples, we olgeta man i save yu na yu save olgeta man. Long hia ol pipel i stap wanwan tumas, olsem wanwan man i gat rum bilong em yet, em i no save toktok wantaim narapela man i stap arere long em. Mi bin stap tripela yia pinis, tasol mi no bin toktok wantaim man i stap arere long haus bilong mi, em i olsem samting i strong long mi long lukim. Tasol taim yu paulim rot bilong yu na askim, ol i save halpim yu stret, oltaim. Em i olsem tupela sait bilong wankain kok.

The young brother: Na maket bilong hia, em i wankain olsem maket bilong hia o nogat?

The cousin: Nogat tru, brata. I no gat maket olsem maket bilong hia we meri i sindaun aninit long amrit wantaim basket bilong kaukau na banana na tomato. Long hia ol samting i stap insait long bikpela haus, olgeta samting i stap long stret laen, ol i putim prais long ol pepa antap long kaikai, na yu no ken toktok baim daun prais, olsem prais i stap fiks. Mi les liklik long dispela pastaim, mi bin traim toktok baim daun prais long man i salim kaikai, em i lukluk long mi olsem mi longlong. Nau mi save pinis, mi save wokabaut stret, kisim samting, baim, go.

The grandmother: Na wok bilong yu, olsem wanem yu painim em? Yu save wokabaut nabaut long taun na askim ol haus bilong wok, olsem long hia?

The cousin: Nogat, tumbuna, em i narapela pasin olgeta. Yu no save wokabaut nating long dua bilong ol kampani. Yu mas raitim pas, ol i kolim "resume", i tokim olgeta samting yu bin skulim na wok yu bin mekim bipo, na salim long ol long intanet. Sapos ol i laikim tok bilong yu, ol i singaut long yu long toktok. Mi bin salim planti pas bipo mi kisim wanpela wok. Em i hatwok long painim, tasol taim yu kisim, wok yet i no hatwok tumas, i gat gutpela taim bilong lusim wok na kisim moni long taim yu sik o taim yu laik lukim ol.

The uncle: Ating yu bin lus rot planti taim long fes taim, a? Mipela harim tok olsem long ol niupela man.

The cousin: Yu tok tru stret, uncle! Fespela mun, mi bin lus rot tripela, fopela taim, mi save go long trein i go long rong sait, o mi kam daun long rong stop na mi wokabaut longwe tumas long san i strong. Wanpela taim mi bin lukluk raunraun inap wanpela aua, mi no save we hap bilong mi i stap, mi sem tumas long askim, tasol las mi askim wanpela lapun meri long rot na em i soim mi stret rot. Nau bihain long tripela yia, mi save pinis long olgeta rot, olgeta sotkat, mi no lus rot moa, tasol long stat em i tru hevi.

The aunty: Na kaikai long hia, i winim kaikai bilong hia o nogat?

The cousin: Em i tupela sait, aunty. Sampela kaikai i gutpela moa, ol frut na ol samting long stua i klin na i fres oltaim, na yu inap kisim kaikai bilong olgeta kantri, Tailan kaikai, Item kaikai, Sainis kaikai, olgeta i stap klostu. Tasol kaikai i no gat testim olsem kaikai bilong hia, kaukau bilong hia, aibika, saksak, mumu, ol samting olsem i no stap isi long hia, na taim yu painim, em i dia tumas o em i no gat gutpela testim. Mi save mis long testim bilong mumu tru.

The young brother: Orait, tokim mipela stret nau, em i gutpela moa o nogut moa long hia bilong Mosbi?

The cousin: Ah, brata, yu askim mi hatpela kwestin! Tupela ples i gat gutpela sait na nogut sait bilong ol yet, mi no inap putim wanpela i antap long narapela olsem. Wanpela taim mi tingim ol samting long hia i winim, narapela taim mi tingim mi laikim moa pasin bilong ples. Em i olsem solwara, sampela de i kwaiet, sampela de i strong win, yu no inap tok stret em i gutpela o nogut olgeta taim.

The grandmother: Pikinini, bai yu kam bek long ples bilong yu long stap oltaim, o nogat?

The cousin: Tumbuna, mi no save yet stret. Bai mi lukluk isi isi. Sampela taim mi tingim mi laik kam bek, stap wantaim yupela olgeta, harim nois bilong ol rooster long moning, kaikai kaukau stret long gaden. Tasol wok bilong mi i stap long hia, na sampela samting mi laikim tu long dispela taun. Bai yumi lukim, taim i go, ating rot bai i kamap klia long mi. Nau tasol mi laik stap liklik taim wantaim yupela, kaikai gut, toktok gut, na larim yumi no wari long tumora.
