Me: Yu save, datfala kaikai ba long maket, hem i namba wan stret, no arafala i klosap long hem. Fish an chips blong Aunty long maket, i swit tumas, evri taem mi kaikaim, mi hapi tru.

My friend: No, brata, yu toktok krangke nao. Datfala kaikai ples long Kukum, hemi winim tru. Wanem gud samting stap long maket, ah?

Me: Yu jas no save kaikai nomoa. Kokoda blong Aunty long maket, hemi save mekim gud wetem solwara fis, coconut milk blong hem i fresh stret, no wanfala man save mekim olsem hem.

My friend: Tru fasin, bat mi talem yu wanfala samting. Wanfala de mi go kaikai long maket, fis blong mi i kolkol nomoa, oil i pas olsem sospen i stap tude fainis. Bel blong mi i sik strong, tru stori ia, mi no gohed moa.

Me: Datwan wan taem nomoa, i no evri taem olsem. Bat datfala kaikai blong Kukum, olketa fis i smolfala tumas, mi kaikaim wanfala, mi hangre yet, mi go bae baem araot smolfala kaikai moa.

My friend: Datfala smolfala wan, hem i swit moa, i no fo quantiti nomoa. Ples blong Kukum hem i stap oltaem fulap wetem pipol, olketa lain i go raon moa long haos blong Praem Minista.

Me: Ha, yu toktok bikmaos tumas nao, tumas fasin. Olraet, bat mi save wanfala samting - datfala liklik stall blong olfala mami klosap long korna long Chaenataon, kaikai blong hem, nomoa wanfala save tok nogud long hem.

My friend: Tru, datfala wan, mitufala evriwan i agri, hem i swit stret, i no gat trabol wetem hem.

Me: Orait den, yumitufala go kaikaim tufala ples blong yumi bak agen, mekem shua wanem wan i tru winim.

My friend: Yes, bae yumi go, bat mi save fastaem - kaikai blong mi bae win.
