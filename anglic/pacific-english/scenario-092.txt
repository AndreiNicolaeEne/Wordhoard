Da haus i quiet now, real quiet, not like earlier wen e been full up wit people, plate an cup everywe, ol pikinini running roun outside an ol big mob standing up long da yard tokin low. Round about two, three o'clock ol dat mob i gone home long dea own bed, an five of us left stap, da wife, da sista, da eldest, da youngest, an me, wantok blong da whole family since scool time. We all sidaun roun da kitchen table, cup a tea stap cold long ar hand, nobody i wandem go slip yet, body too tired for slip.

Da eldest i start em first, real soft kine, jus wan small memory, nothing big.

The eldest: Yufala rimember how e save get up before daylight, before anybody, an e go sidaun long da veranda wit e cup, jus lukluk long da solwara. E no talk nothing, e jus stap.

The wife: Yu mixim em up now. E no drink tea long morning, e drink coffee. Tea na night time, dat one.

The eldest: Aa, yu right, yu right, mi always mixim dat one up, coffee long morning, tea long night. Mi never get em right, not even now.

Da sista i laugh one kine laugh she never mean for let out, quick she cover er mouth.

The sister: Sori, sori, i not funny ting, i jus... e sound so real wen yu tok em like dat.

Nobody i mind. Small laugh good ting long dis kine night, everybody savve dat.

Den da youngest i tok, quiet.

The youngest: Yufala rimember how e always tok, "No rush, de sea i no go anyweh." Any time somebody hurry hurry, e tok dat wan.

The wife: "No rush. De sea i no go anyweh."

Everybody i quiet small time after dat, jus lukluk long da table, hearing em in dea head, same way e used to say em.

Den me, I tok one ting I never bin tell nobody yet.

Me: Yufala savve, before e sick propa, e bin tok long me e wandem go sidaun long da point one more time, jus watchim da tide turn, one more time. E never bin tell yufala dat wan?

The wife: E never bin tell me dat.

Room i go real quiet, jus da fan turning, jus dat.

Da sista i da one break em, she get up, pass roun da biscuit tin, top up ol cup wit more tea, something for ar hand for do.

The sister: Yufala wandem more tea? Kaikai stap plenty still, we no finish em.

Everybody tekem li bit, jus for something.

Den one old ting surface tween da wife an da sista, something bout who bin suppose to check da old man boat wen e first bin get sick, nothing to do wit tonight at all.

The wife: You know you never bin come check dat boat dat time, wen mi ask yu.

The sister: Dat one old story now, sista. No leh we go dea tonight.

The wife: Yeah. No leh we go dea.

Da eldest tell da famous one den, bout how e stubborn.

The eldest: Yufala rimember wen e refuse blo go long hospital, jus small cut long e hand, an e tok, "Mi tuff, mi orait." E wrap em up wit e own singlet an keep on working three more day before mama force em go.

Everybody laugh real fond dis time, no edge lef in em, jus laugh.

The wife: Stubborn olsem rock, dat man. Always e own way.

Widout nobody tok em straight out, we all seem for agree dat's da one ting we bai always rimember em by, im stubborn hed, im own way or no way at all.

Partway through, da youngest start cry quiet, tears jus come down er face, she no make big noise bout em, she no even wipe em quick. Talk keep going soft roun her, nobody stop for her, nobody make big ting bout em, jus let her cry an keep tok gentle.

The eldest: An yufala rimember e save fix everyting wit e own hand, never call nobody for help, even wen e should.

The wife: E save do dat, yeah. Too proud for ask.

Later, tok i turn practical, plain, no ceremony bout em.

The wife: Bai who go handle da paperwork long Centrelink an dat?

The eldest: Mi can do dat one tumoro morning, first ting.

The sister: Mi go organize kaikai for da gathering Sunday, mi got time.

Me: Mi go tokim da rest of da wantok mob, let ol dem savve wen an weh for come.

The youngest: Mi go clean up long da veranda tumoro, wea e used to sidaun.

Nobody i say nothing more bout dat, jus nod, jus agree, plain like dat.

By den night i really late, everybody else half slip long dea chair, only me still wide awake, cup long mi hand gone cold long time ago. Mi lifting em up anyway, small toast, nothing prepared, jus what i come long mi head.

Me: Wel... to da old man. E stap wit yumi long dis haus still, mi reckon, e stap wit yumi long every kaikai time, every tea time, every time somebody tok "no rush." To em.

Nobody i say nothing back, jus lif dea own empty cup small bit, jus dat, an we all sidaun together long da quiet, till daylight start come up over da solwara.
