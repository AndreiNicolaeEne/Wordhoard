Em i wanpela nait, klostu tulait i pinis, na wanwan taim tasol olgeta samting i go blak. Television i dai, fan i dai, lait long kitchen i dai, olgeta wantaim. Mipela i no save stret wanem i kosim, maybe PNG Power i katim paua bilong dinau i stap, o maybe wanpela waya long powa poles i bruk long win. Nogat toksave i kam.

Papa i kirap kwiktaim, i go painim torch bilong em long drawer, tasol batri i dai pinis, i no wok. Mama i go insait long kitchen, i pilim raun long tabol bilong painim maset. Em i painim tu-tri candle, tasol tupela i sot tumas na wanpela tasol i stap gutpela liklik, na em i liklik hap nating, i no inap long olgeta rum bilong haus.

Mother: Yumi mas sharim dispela wanpela candle, i no gat narapela.

Olsem na em i putim candle long namel tebol, na yumi olgeta i sindaun klostu klostu long em, ol pikinini tu, bihainim liklik lait i go long fes bilong yumi.

The eldest: Mi paulim leg bilong mi long sofa gen, sem hap!

Em i bin naispasim leg bilong sofa tripela taim insait long dispela dark, na yumi lap liklik long em, tasol em i pilim pen tu, i mekim yumi soim sori long em.

Papa i traim lukluk long fuse box wantaim torch bilong fon, tasol i no save wanem samting i bruk, na em i tok, "Bai yumi lukim tumora long de taim san i kamap, nogut yumi bagarapim samting moa long dark nating."

Olsem na yumi givap long fixim long dispela nait, na yumi sindaun stap tasol, storian isi isi, kaikai kol supper wantaim, ol pikinini i go slip klostu long mama long wanpela bed.

Candle i go daun liklik liklik, i dai klostu long ten klok, na lait bilong haus i no kam bek. Yumi go long bed olgeta long dark, harim ol dok i singaut ausait, na tingting olsem, bihain long dispela kain nait, yu save gut moa long famili bilong yu taim olgeta arapela samting i dai raun long yu.
