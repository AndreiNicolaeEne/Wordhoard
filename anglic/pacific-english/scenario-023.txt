My dear, sori mi leit for tok stori long yu bat mi promis-im yu long fon se bae mi raetem evri samting go long yu, from strat kasem las, since yu no save kam. So sidaon gud, diswan bae longfala wan.

Mi wekap tumas earli, before kokorako singaot nomoa, maet fofala klok long moning. Mi jes stap long bed, ai open, hat i go bigwan bigwan, mi no save sleep bak. Mi trae kaikai smolfala rais wetem tea, bat bele blong mi i no wantem kaikai tumas, mi jes kaikai lelebet, samfala spun nomoa, then mi pushim plet i go.

Nomata mi ovaslip tingting, evrisamting stap redi olsem mifala arenjem, bat wanfala samting mifala fogetim tumas: nogat sus! Mi stap wetem bilas evri samting, hair pinis, mek-up pinis, then wanfala small kasin i singaot, "sus blong yu, sus blong yu, hem i stap wea?" Mifala evriwan lukluk raonabaot long haos, no faenem, and taem stap sot tumas nau. Nara kasin jamp long trak go bak long haos blong mami for lukim, hem drive olsem crezi man, hem kam bak jes taem nomoa, sus stap long hand blong hem, evriwan singaot sori tumas.

Taem mifala arrive long ples, dat wan i mekim mi cry-im tumas insaet. Diswan fastaem taem tufala family i stanap tugeta long wanfala ples, wanfala pati blong mi wetem pati blong hasban blong mi, evriwan wetem gutfala klos, evriwan lukluk wanwan narafala olsem oli askem, "hu nao diswan famili, bae iumi save gud o nomoa?" Bat i no longtaem nomoa, evriwan i smael kam, olketa antie stat storystori olsem oli fren fo longtaem pinis.

Mami blong mi hem fastaem cry. Mi jes wokabaot insaet, mi lukim hem, hem i pulum wanfala tishu from bag blong hem, hem i tanem fes blong hem go narasaet mekem mi no lukim, bat mi lukim nomoa. Then papa blong hasban blong mi i cry tu, and afta diswan, evriwan i stat cry nomoa, olsem wanfala rein i foldaon.

Long taem blong vow, mi stanap, han blong mi shake, mi tingting go emti. Mi stat gud: "mi teikem yu fo mi hasban / mi wife, fo gudfala taem, fo..." then mi lus-im, tingting blong mi i go nating, olgeta pipol wet, no eni saon. Pastor hem i wetim smol, hem no singaotim mi, hem jes standap kwaet. Mi lukluk go long ai blong hasban blong mi, and diswan i mekim tingting kambak: "...fo nogud taem tu, mi promis bae mi stap wetem yu."

Diswan taem exchenj ring i sil-im evrisamting. Hasban blong mi puttim ring long finga blong mi, han blong hem shake tumas nomoa moa long mi, and mi laf smol insaet taem mi putim ring blong hem. Pastor i singaotem mifala hasban wetem waef, evriwan i klap han, sam pipol whistle tumas.

Afta, mifala go saenem paper. Wanfala witness kam from saed blong mi, wanfala witness kam from saed blong hasban blong mi, tufala saenem, mifala saenem, hand blong mi stil shake taem mi raetem nem. Pastor smael, hem se, "nau, evrisamting stret pinis, according to lo."

Then kam foto taem, and ah, diswan i longwan tumas. Wanfala fren blong mifala hu holem kamera hem trae arenjem clasta clasta long famili, fastaem saed blong mami, then saed blong papa, then olketa auntie wetem uncle, then olketa pikinini stanap fran, evriwan i muv, i sidaon, i standap moa, "yu go lelebet left, no, yu tufala klosap moa, smol pikinini kam fran hia." Wind i blow kam from solwata, evriwan hair i mess-up, olketa gele holem hair blong olketa taet taet, samfala man wipe sweat, bat evriwan laf nomoa, no eni cross.

Long resepsen, seating hem i gat wanfala problem. Olketa arenjem tebol rong, wanfala uncle blong hasban i sidaon tumas klosap long wanfala antie blong saed blong mi we tufala i no gud toktok wetem narawan fo longtaem pinis, from wanfala olfala problem long bak. Tufala jes sidaon, fes stret, no toktok, evriwan raonabaot lukluk laf smol bat no seiv eniwan.

Kaikai, diswan wan gutfala. Fish wetem coconut cream, hem finis kwiktaem, olketa pipol go bak fo moa, tumoas plet i emti insaet ten minit nomoa. Bat wanfala salad, gril vegetable wan, hem stap same ples long taebol, nomoa wan tas-im. Mami blong mi hem se afta, "mi tinghim tumas about diswan salad, hemi gutfala tumas, bat nobodi lukim!"

Toast taem, papa blong mi trae, bat hem no fit finisim. Hem stat gud, hem tok about mi taem mi pikinini, hem tok about hao mi grow big kwiktaem, then vois blong hem i braek, hem jes standap dea, glass long han, evriwan wet kwaet, then hem jes se, "mi tumas hapi," and hem sidaon, ai blong hem wet. Evriwan klap han strong fo helpim hem.

Long dans, wanfala small kasin, hem i no sidaon full naet, tru tumas! Musik i pley, hem dans, hem dans, evriwan taiet hem stil go, olketa askem hem for res, hem se, "nomoa, mi orait, ple moa musik!" Las song, hem stil dans wanwan bae hemself, evriwan lukluk hem, laf tumas.

Then wanfala olfala story kam aot, storyi blong hao papa mit mami, bak long taem, wei papa i lus rod go long ples blong mami fastaem, hem askem rong haos, olfala uncle i tok-im diswan story long full tebol, evriwan laf, sam pipol hirim diswan story fastaem, sam hirim planti taem pinis bat laf sem laf.

Las, taem evriwan go pinis, mifala tufala jes stap, ol chair i stanap rong we, plet stap evriwhea, flawa stap fol daon long graon, mifala jes lukluk raonabaot, no toktok wan minit, then hasban blong mi holem han blong mi, mifala jes stap kwaet long dat mes, tumas hapi insaet, no eni need fo toktok.

Diswan hem full de, my dear. Mi wish yu bin stap, bat nau yu save evrisamting olsem yu bin stap.
