Well, God, I stap here now, quiet time before I close my eye.
Thank you for this day, for the good wind blong the boat and the fish we bin haul up.
I thank you tumas for my old aunty, my dear one, she stap strong watching over all of us.
I askim you one thing, keep her safe, keep her strong for plenty more day.
That's all now. Amen.
