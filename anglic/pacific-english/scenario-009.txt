The eldest: Ei! Lukim dispela haircut bilong yu, husat i katim?
The younger: Em i nais tumas, noken tok nogut long en.
The eldest: Nogat tru, hea bilong yu i luk olsem kakaruk i stap aninit long bikpela ren!
The younger: Yu yet, lukluk long glas pastaim, hea bilong yu i olsem bikpela bus i kamap.
The eldest: Orait orait, dispela wan yu winim mi tru.
The younger: Bai mi tokim mama long dispela tok bilong yu, yu save.
