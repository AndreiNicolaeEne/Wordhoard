Love, mi mas talem yu stret nao, mi no save haidim insaed long haat bilong mi eni moa: mi lovem yu, tumas, moa long eni samting mi save tingim.

Mi stap tingting bak long wanfala apinun, longtaem pinis nao, taem yumitufala sidaon nomoa long stesin, wet long bas i kam. No eni bikfala okasen, jes wanfala nomol apinun, rain i stat foldaun smol-smol, ol pipol raosem olketa seleva go anda long ruf. Yu openim smol basket bilong yu, tekemaot wanfala mango, yu brekem hem tu-hap, givim mi hafwan bifo yu kaikaim yu seleva. Long dat smol samting nomoa, mi save insaed haat bilong mi, dis filing hem i no smol samting nomoa, hem i kamap bikfala pinis, mi no save go bak.

Yu save wanfala samting, taem yu tingting-tingting long samting i had, yu save skrasem lelebet skin blong ia bilong yu long lef saed, smol samting nomoa bat mi save lukluk gud long yu, so mi save.

Evri de wetim yu stap insaed laef bilong mi, kaikai i swit moa, waka i no hevi tumas, mi save toktok isi long haat bilong mi taem mi tingim yu nomoa. Bat taem yumitufala longwe, day hem i draggem-draggem, solwara hem i openim bikfala namel long yumitufala, naet hem i longfala tumas, mi no save sleep gud.

Wanfala liklik wari mi neva talem yu bifo: sometaem mi fraet olsem laef bae pulim yu go long narafala ples, mi fraet mi bae stap seleva bakegen. Mi no laik dis wari, bat hem i stap insaed nomoa, so mi raetem long pepa.

Bat harim mi stret: mi promis long yu, yumitufala bae wakabaot tugeta go kasem laswan de bilong mi, mi bae holem han bilong yu strong, mi bae no lusim yu eni taem.

Swithaat.
