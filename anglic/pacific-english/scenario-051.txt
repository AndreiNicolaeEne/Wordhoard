my cousin-brother: Dat easy one. Me I garra buy im new troopy, big one, four-wheel drive, brabli deadly, drive im up to Katherine every weekend.

my cousin-brother: Bank! Now you sound like whitefella. Money i come, money i go, dat's how e stap.

my cousin-brother: Aright aright, but I still garra buy meself one big flat-screen TV, biggest one longa Katherine, watchim football every Friday night.

my cousin-brother: True true. But listen ere, wanem if yunmi buy im dat old cattle station up the river, we two run im, become proper bosses, get plane, fly im everywhere, no more troopy even!

Me: Ay you gone too far now, cousin-brother! We don't even save how for run cattle, we gonna go broke first year!

my cousin-brother: Secondhand! From dat wrecker mob?

my cousin-brother: (laughing) Yeah, better than nothing. Anyway dat money never even bin come yet, we sittin ere talking big for nothing!

Me: (laughing) True, yunmi bin dreaming brabli hard for nothing, eh.
