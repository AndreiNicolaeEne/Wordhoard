Ah my friend, you mis the whole saga, true. I stap tell you now, from the start, coz you no bin come and honestly maybe that was the lucky thing for you.

Plan i bin simple. Truck i sud kam eight o'clock, everyone i sud stap redi, by ten we finis everything, by lunch we stap na long niu haos drinking tea like big man. That was the plan. God i laf long yumi, true.

Morning i stat wrong stret. I wekap, I go for shawa, no wota. Tank i stap empty since Sarere, nobody i tinktink for chekim. So mi stap wasim bodi wetem wan bucket bilong wota we mi keepim long kitchen, cold cold, and mi stap shivering, thinking dis day i no wantem stat gud from the first.

Truck man i kam leit tu. He bin tok eight, he kam kwata pas nain, and mi jes stap long doa wetem wan haf kap coffee, wasing taem. By the taem yumi redi for lodim, sun i kam antap already, hot i stat kam.

But then, samting gud i hapen. Mi no bin askem hem nating, but the neighbour blong mi, hem i jes kam wokabaot insaed long yard blong mi wetem wilbarrow blong hem, and hem i tok:

The neighbour: Mi harem se yu muv tede. Mi kam help.

Mi no bin askem hem. Hem jes kam, olsem se em i save se dei olsem ia, wan man i mas gat wan wantok fo helpem, sapos nomo. Mifala i glad tumas fo hem, coz truk man wetem draeva tufala nomo, and haos blong mi i gat planti samting.

Naoia, from here evri samting i go kranki wan wan.

Faswan bigfala samting we i brek, em i the glass cabinet blong mama, we hemi karem long haos blong hem taem hemi marit, longtaem finis, i gat ol niu china plet insaed. Mifala i stap karem hem daon long steps, tri man holem, and wan leg blong table i sitim antap long wan step we i no fasin gud, i slip, and cabinet i go daon, glas i brok olgeta, sound olsem bom. Ol i sanap, no man i tok nating fastaem, jes sailens. Then mama, hemi stap sanap antap, hemi singaot:

The mother: Aiyo! Ol plet blong mi! Plet blong mama blong mi tu!

Em i singaot longwe, ol niebor i harem, wan dog i stap bak long yad blong narafala haos. Mifala jes stap luk long ol pis glas long graon, no man i save wanem fo talem. That cabinet i stap wetem famili blong mifala fo fotiwan yia, en naoia i pinis, jes olsem.

Wan bokis tu, i lus. Mi no save wanem i bin insaed, mi tink em i gat sam olfala foto wetem sam buk blong skul, mi neva save fo shua. Mifala i putim ol bokis long trak, i kaontem, i kaontem gen taem i kam daon long niu haos, en wan i sot. We i go, hu i lusim, nobody i save. Mifala i lukluk aninit long trak, insaed long ol narafala haos, be nogat. Em i jes gowe, olsem solwota i tekem hem.

Naoia, bigfala hard samting, em i faet abaot fasin fo karem heviwan samting, olsem fridge. Fridge ia i bikpela tumas, hevi tumas. Brata blong mi hemi tok wan fasin, husband blong mi hemi tok narafala fasin.

The brother: Yumi mas turnim hem long saed, no long fes.

The husband: Nogat, sapos yumi turnim long saed, motor i save spoelem. Yumi mas karem stret, evriwan i pulim.

Tufala i stap agyu longtaem, long medel rod, sun i hot, fridge i sanap namel, evriwan i wet. Mama hemi stap kolim tufala:

The mother: Yutufala i no save agyu, jes karemap! Sun i kilim mi hia.

Long las, tufala i mekem wan we blong tufala, karem stret, be dat i no mekem eni beta, coz doa blong niu haos, em i smol tumas.

Dat doa, tru, em i wan spirit blong bagarapim mifala. Fridge ino save go pas, wetem eni fasin. Yumi turnim raet, yumi turnim lef, yumi tilim antap, yumi tilim daon, i jas no wantem pas. Wan aoa mifala i stap faet wetem wan doa nomo, sweat i ronem daon, ol man i lukluk, wan i tok:

The driver: Sapos yu tekemaot doa fastaem, bai i isi.

En em i tru, be nobody i tinktink long dat bifo, mifala jes faet wetem doa fo wan aoa fastaem olsem krangke pipol.

Klaod i stat kam taem mifala i stap haf-wei. Solwota i smel kam strong, win i jenis, en biaen i no longtaem, ren i foldaon, no smol ren, bigfala ren, ol samting we i stap aot, ol boks pepa, ol samting i wetgud, mifala i ronraon olsem krangke, trae kavarem ol boks wetem plastik, wetem taon, be planti samting i wet finis, karton i sofmap i brok.

Long dat taem, taem evriwan i wet, i taed, i kros, meri blong narafala haos, wan wantok blong mifala, i kam wetem wan bikfala pot blong rice wetem chikin, wetem sam kumara, en hemi tok:

The neighbour's wife: Kaikai fastaem, ol samting bai i stap yet.

Mifala evriwan i sanap namel long ol boks, no jea, no tebol, jes sanap, kaikai stret long han, laf smol taem, forget smol taem long fridge wetem doa wetem ren. Dat kaikai, mi tingbaot hem, em i sevem afternun blong mifala tru.

Be biaen, taem san i stap godaon, en mifala i stap karem las ol boks, taedness i kasem mifala strong. Leg blong mi i pen, han blong mi i sekseg, tok blong mifala i sot sot nomo:

Mifala i jes laf smol, no gat pawa fo kros nating.

Mi luk kona we pikinini i yustu slip, mi luk mak long wol we mi neva rabim aot, mi stanap longtaem long kitchen, tingting long ol dinner, ol pati, ol trabol, evri samting we i hapen insaed long ples ia.
