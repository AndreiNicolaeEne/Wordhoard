Early arvo mipela bin sidong longa camp en mi lukim dat smok comin up longa horizon, way way off, jus one liklik grey line sittin up there. Mi bin tink, ah, dat one no close yet, im stap longa udda side, might burn im grass en go quiet. Mi en mi wantok bin jus lukluk, lukluk, no wori.

Bat windi bin change quick. Im i pick up brabli strong en start blowin dat smok dis way. No even wan hour bin pas en already im bin stingim mi eye, mekem em run water, mekem mi kofkof.

Den mi savi im no gonna sidong quiet no more.

My wantok: Auntie, we gotta go now, dat faya close up tumas!

The old auntie: Yu right, grabim wat mattarem, lipim ol res.

Mipela bin muv quick quick. Twenti minit, dat wan, en mipela bin out longa dat place, liklik bag, water, pikinini blanket, faya bin no wait for nomo. Big mob bin loaded up longa kar, dog runnin behind, ol neighba comin out same taim.

Mi trot bin go raw, feel like sand sit up inside. Dat heat bin press up longa mi skin from behind, hot hot, mi no luk back til mipela bin get clear longa road.

Wen mi bin luk back, mi lukim im, dat faya light up bikpela, red light up all longa sky, like sun bin fall down early taim. Mi jus stan there liklik bit lukluk, den mi jump back longa kar quick.

Tu naet mipela bin stap longa dijan town, no savi wat bin happen longa mipela place. Mi jus sidong worri worri, wonderim if house bin stanap yet o if im bin gone finish.

Third dei ol ranger bin tellim we can go bek. Mipela bin drive longa road quiet, nobody toktok. Wen mipela bin come up longa turn-off, mi lukim, house stap gud, tree stap gud, ebrijing bin spared. Faya bin go raun, no touchim. Mi jus stan there, cry liklik, glad tumas.
