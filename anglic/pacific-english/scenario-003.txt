The father: Mi tingim wanfala nem finish - iumi save callim pikinini ya "Nathan".

The mother: Nomoa, mi no laekim datfala nem. Nathan i olsem nem blong olketa waet man long TV nomoa, hem no garem rot insaid long famili blong iumi, hem i no minim nating long mifala.

The father: Orait, sapos iu no laekim, wanem nao iu tingim?

The mother: Mi tingim "Moses" i gud tumas.

The father: Moses? Nomoa wei. Brata blong mi i garem wanfala fren, nem blong hem Moses, and tufala i no fren gud long taem bifo. Mi no wantem pikinini blong mifala i kerem datfala nem, bae hem i mekem mi tingim datfala trabol nomoa evritaem mi coleim hem.

The mother: Orait, orait. Wanem sapos iumi callim hem long nem blong bubu blong iu? Datwan i soem respect, and bubu bae i hapi tumas taem hem herem.

The father: Mi save tingim tu, be wet firstaem - aunty blong iu and cousin blong mifala, tufala i garem datfala nem finish. Sapos iumi callim pikinini olsem tu, bae hem i kamap trifala long famili wetem sem nem, and evritaem olketa bae confusim hu nao iumi storyim.

The mother: Tru tu... be mi no save wanem nara nem bae fit nao.

The father: Iu tingim tumas long saed blong iu nomoa, no long saed blong mi.

The mother: Hu nao i garem las toktok long diswan - iu o mi?

The father: Yumitufala nomoa, mi tingim. No wan narafala.

The mother: Sapos yumitufala nomoa, why nao evri nem mi tingim, iu jes tromoim stret?

The father: Mi no duim olsem.

The mother: Iu duim.

Tufala i stap kwaet nomoa, taed tumas finish for datfala toktok. Pikinini i slip pinis long kot, nem blong hem i stap nating yet, and tufala i save toktok ya bae i kamap gen tumoro.
