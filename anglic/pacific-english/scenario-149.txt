The eldest: Dis blessing I tok, em i sworn, em i baimbai true: solwata i stap kind long yufala, garden i grow prapa strong, pikinini blong yufala i neva hangre. Nau I tanem. I tok di odarwan, curse tu i sworn, i baimbai true olsem: garden blong yu i die, solwata i tekem yu boat, pikinini blong yu i cry hangre tumas. Wan mouth bin tok dis tupla, wan sworn i join dem.
