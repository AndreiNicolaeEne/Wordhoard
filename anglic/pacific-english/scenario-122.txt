Dis stori, im always come up same way. Late evening, wen the mob bin finish kaikai and the fire stap low and everybody jus sidong quiet, someborn young one gon aks why the old people always warn im blanga that walk, why nobody gu down there wen it dark, wen the moon nomo stap. That's wen I tell it. Not before. Yu bin aks proper tonight, straight up, no gammon, so I garra tell im to yu proper too, start to finish, en yu jus sidong en listen now, orait.

Dat thing, nobody ever bin lukim im true, not one time, not eva. Yu nomo gon see im first. Im come as a voice, en only after, if im come at all, yu might feel someting stanup close, someting wid weight to im but no shape yu can hold in yu ai. Dat's how im always bin telled to me en dat's how I telling im to yu. Voice first. Shape nomo, or shape brabli lilbit, jus a darkness dat's darker than the dark aroun im.

En wat im do, im kolim yu. Im kolim yu name, but nomo in some strange voice, im use the voice blong somebody yu brabli love. Yu granny, yu uncle, yu little brother, whoeva yu ai gon turn for quick quick without yu even garra think about im. Im use that voice cos that's the only voice gon pull yu off the track. Im wanchi teikim yu away from the path, away from anybody who might bin help yu, out into the dark wea nobody can hierim yu back.

En im only come afta midnight. Nomo before im, no matter how dark early evening get. En im only come on the nomoon night, wen there's najing up there to light the track, when the whole bush stap black black en yu can't even lukim yu own hand proper. Any other night, moon-time night, yu safe. Everybody save that.

The spot blong im, here, is dat bend wea the road goes down close by the river before the old crossing. I nomo gon tell yu im stap all over the place, im blong big country, cos dat's not true en I nomo garra gammon yu. Im blong dat bend. Dat's im place. Nobody roun here reckon im walk no further than dat.

I bin first hierim dis stori wen I was lilbit gel, from my granny, my mother's mother. She bin tell im to me the same way I telling im to yu now, sidong by the fire, en she nomo bin laugh, not one lilbit. Straight face, eye serious the whole way through, like she telling me someting I garra carry proper, not someting for make me jump en scream en forget by morning. I bin ask im, "Granny, yu bin hierim true?" En she jus bin look at me longtime before she answer.

Now the proof, the one everybody talk about first. Dat patch of groun right on dat bend, najing eva grow there. People bin try. Grass nomo come up. Somebody bin plant someting there long time back, jus for see, en im nomo grow, not one green ting, while everyting aroun im green green, brabli green wet season time. Dat groun jus stap bare, like someting underneath im nomo let anyting live on top.

Second proof, dat one always come secondhand, the way these ting always come. My cousin-brother, im got a countryman from further up, en dat countryman bin tell him im uncle-side family bin hierim someting kolim out from dat bend one night, longtime ago, wen dey bin driving past late, en nobody bin out there, no house, no camp, najing. Dat's how dis one always get telled, not "I bin hierim," but "somebody bin hierim, en dey bin tell somebody, en dat somebody bin tell mi."

But the one story everybody tell in full, every single time, no matter who telling im, is bout my relation, years back now. This relation bin walking home late, on foot, past dat bend, en dey hierim dey own name kolim out from off the path, from the dark side wea najing stap but bush. En the voice, dey bin swear to dis, en dey still swear im to dis day, the voice bin sound zactly like somebody dey bin lost, somebody dey bin love brabli much. Dey bin turn straight away, without even garra think, en dey bin walk three, four step off the path toward im, toward that voice, before someting, dey nomo can tell yu wat, jus someting, bin mekem dem stop. Dem stop dead, en someting bin turn dem back roun, en dey bin run, brabli fast, all the way home, en dey nomo bin look back, not once.

Wat dey swear to dis day, word for word, the way we always repeat im in the family: "I bin hierim im call my name true, en I bin start to go, en someting bin grab me heart en turn me roun before I get too far." Dat's how it get telled every time, exact them words, cos nobody in the family gon change im.

The one detail that mekem even the doubters go quiet lilbit, the voice dat dey hierim, im nomo jus sound similar. Im bin use a lilbit sayin, a lilbit way of callin dis relation's name, dat only that one lost person eva bin use, someting nobody else, not one soul still living, bin ever call dis relation by. How dat thing sposta know dat, if im not real? Dat question, im shut people up quick.

The other detail, jus as real, dat give the doubters dem opening, dis relation bin walking home from a lilbit drink dat night, en the dogs from the camp bin howlin big mob right about dat same time, cos wild dog stap thick roun dat bend too. So the ones who nomo believe, dey get im easy: dog howlin, man bin had a drink, dark night, bush playin tricks. Dey laugh im off quick quick, en I nomo blame dem.

Now I garra be honest wid yu, cos yu bin ask me proper. Dis stori, im change lilbit dependin who telling im. Some of the old people tell im soft, like a warning wrapped up gentle so the young ones nomo get too fright. Others tell im hard, brabli hard, no soft edge on im at all, so it sit in yu chest en stay there. I bin hearim told both way, my whole life, en I nomo eva minded the difference. Both way still true underneath, I reckon. It's the same thing, jus wearing different clothes dependin whose mouth im coming out of.

En dis is the lilbit thing we still do here, quiet quiet, cos of dis stori. If yu hierim yu name kolim from somewhere dark, from off any path, yu nomo answer im. Not one word. Yu wait, yu check wid yu own eye, proper, who really callin yu, before yu eva open yu mouth back. Every one of us do dis, even the young ones who reckon dey too smart for old stori.

En me, I garra tell yu true, since yu bin sidong en listen so proper: I nomo fully believe dis stori meself. I can't tell yu I do, hand on heart. But I never bin broke dat rule, not one time in my whole life. Not once.
