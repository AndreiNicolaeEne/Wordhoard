The buyer: Eh, chenj ya i no stret. Seven nao yu sud givim mi, bat faevfala nomoa yu putim, tufala i sot.
The shopkeeper: Nomata, mi kaontim stret finis, no gat mistek long mi.
The buyer: Tufala dola nao i sot, mi kaontim tu, hem i no rong.
The shopkeeper: (kaontim gen) ...aye, i sot tru. Tekem ya, bilong yu nomoa.
