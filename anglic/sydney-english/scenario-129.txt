So it was early arvo, just after lunch, and I'm out the back having a ciggie and I clock this smudge of smoke way off on the ridge. Real distant, just sitting there, not moving much. Rang my brother, told him, nah it's miles off, we're right.

Within the hour though the wind swung round and just pushed the whole lot up towards us, heaps faster than you'd reckon, and my eyes started stinging, that sort of prickly sting, and the smoke had gone from a smudge to this big dirty smear right across the sky. That's when it stopped being watching and started being oh mate, we need to go.

Me: right, that's it, we're going now.

The eldest: what about the...

Me: leave it, leave all of it, just get in the car.

Twenty minutes, tops, and we were out. Grabbed the esky with whatever was in the fridge, grabbed the important papers, grabbed the dog, and that was it, everything else just stayed where it was. Throat was raw the whole way out, proper raw, like you'd been smoking forty a day, and the heat was pressing on the car windows even with them shut, you could feel it on your skin through the glass practically.

Got to the top of the rise and looked back once, just the one look, and the whole hill behind us was lit up orange, and I thought, righto, don't look again, just drive.

Two nights we were at my sister's not knowing a thing, phone going but no one could tell you anything solid, just no dramas, no dramas, she'll be right, which nobody fair dinkum believed. Went back the third day and the fire had gone round us, spared the place completely. Bloody lucky, that's all it was.
