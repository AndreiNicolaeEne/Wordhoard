Woke up and it hit me straight off, before I even had me eyes open properly: today's the day. Months back, sick of being asked one more time, I swore black and blue I'd do the whole shed out, top to bottom, no more putting it off. And now here it is and I do not want to get up. Not one bit of me wants to get up.

Got up anyway. Didn't even have brekkie first, just walked out the door muttering, oath, why'd I ever open me big mouth, coulda just said no like anyone with sense. Bloke at the servo getting a coffee: G'day, big day ahead? Me: Yeah nah, big day of regret, mate, that's what it is.

Shed was worse than I remembered. Heaps worse. Dad's old tools gone dodgy with rust, some fridge that's been dead since god knows, boxes from three house moves ago. Whinged about the size of it first, then the heat, then about meself for ever swearing to a whole day of this in the first place. I reckon that's the driest way going to spend a day off, honestly.

Mate rang round two, wanting to know.

The mate: Youse still doing the whole shed today? Come down the pub instead, no dramas.

Me: Can't, mate. Swore I'd finish it.

The mate: Keen to see that, fair dinkum.

Round then, dragging the dead fridge out on me own, quitting looked heaps reasonable. Told meself flat out, no, swore it, and that's that, no fortnight of excuses getting me out of it now. Gritted through the worst of it, the mozzies, the insulation, all of it.

Finished it proper as the light went. Every box sorted, floor swept. Chucked the thongs on, cracked one from the esky, sat there half dead in the doorway. She'll be right. Never swearing to a whole day again though, but. Not never.
