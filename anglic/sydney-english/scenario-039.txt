The foreman: Righto, before youse all wander off - tomorrow, what time we starting.

The chippy: Six, same as always.

The labourer: Nah, six's mental, mate. I'm not even outta bed at six, but.

The apprentice: Seven's better, gets us in before the heat's on without being stupid early.

The foreman: Seven it is then, and don't be arguing it again in the morning.

The chippy: Fair enough. Marrickville job still, is it, finishing off the deck?

The foreman: Yeah. You're bringing the compressor and the nail gun.

The chippy: Already in the ute.

The foreman: Sparky's coming through after lunch to sort the point near the shed, so mind that trench stays open for him.

The labourer: Speaking of which, we're heaps short on decking boards. Only got enough for half of it if the order don't turn up.

The foreman: It'll turn up.

The labourer: She'll be right, till it's not.

The apprentice: And there's rain forecast for the arvo an' all. Bit keen to get it closed in before that hits.

The foreman: Bit of drizzle, won't stop us.

The chippy: So who's digging the last of them footings then, cause that's the shocker of a job.

The foreman: That'd be you, mate.

The chippy: Course it is. Every fortnight it's me down the hole and youse standing round having a squiz.

The labourer: No dramas, we'll bring the esky over after.

The foreman: Right, that's four of us sorted then. We all good? Where's the other fella, he in tomorrow?

The apprentice: Nah, chucked a sickie, reckons his back's gone.

The chippy: Bad back my arse.

The foreman: Whatever. One down, we'll manage.

The labourer: Righto, seven then. And you owe me a beer for them footings, mate, not forgetting that one.

The chippy: Owe you nothing, you still owe me from last Friday.

The foreman: Sort it out over another round. Seven sharp, don't be dodgy about it.

The apprentice: G'day to that.
