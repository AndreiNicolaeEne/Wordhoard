The one who wasn't there: So how'd it go then, don't leave anything out.

Me: Oh mate, heaps good actually. Better than I thought it'd be, going in.

The other one: Reckon. Bit nervous beforehand, ay, but it was heaps good once we got there.

Me: You would not believe the bloke who used to sit up the back, quiet as anything, but. Walked straight past him at the esky, didn't even clock it was him till he said g'day.

The other one: Couldn't recognise him at all. Whereas the one who ran the whole social committee, she's exactly the same. Down to the laugh, down to bossing everyone round.

Me: Exactly the same. Back then she was organising the whole show, telling everyone where to stand for photos, and now apparently she runs her own business, still telling everyone where to stand.

The other one: Word was the one who used to captain the footy didn't show. Someone reckoned he's crook, someone else reckoned it was just work on, who knows.

Me: Heard both those. But get this, the quiet one from a few doors down, nobody thought she'd amount to much, ended up running a whole department over at Centrelink, if you don't mind. Wouldn't have picked it in a million years.

The other one: Fair dinkum?

Me: Fair dinkum.

The other one: What about us then. We changed much or what.

Me: Nah, not really. Reckon we're pretty much the same, bit greyer maybe.

The other one: Yeah, more or less. She'll be right.

The one who wasn't there: So youse doing it again?

Me: Keen to, before another twenty years goes past, be lucky if we'd know each other by then.

The other one: Definitely doing it again. Don't leave it a fortnight this time, mate.
