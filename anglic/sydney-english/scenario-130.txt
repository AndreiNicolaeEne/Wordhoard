Water was at the door first thing, real early, only up round me ankles, so I didn't think much of it, reckoned it'd go down soon as the rain eased off. Went back an hour later and nah, it'd come up heaps already, so I started shifting stuff off the floor while I still had time, dragging the telly up onto the bench, chucking the rugs over the clothesline out back.

By midmorning it was up round me knees and I was flat out, everything I could lift went up onto whatever was highest, the fridge shelf, the top of the wardrobe, the ironing board even. Rang me mate down the road.

Me: You right down there?
Workmate: Nah mate, we're gone, it's up past the couch already.
Me: Same here, she's still climbing.

Kept climbing right through till early arvo, went past the highest thing I'd put up, water just found its way up over the lot, over the bench, over the wardrobe, the whole lot gone under. Peaked round two-ish and after that it was just the wait, sitting up on the stairs listening to it slap against the walls, the whole place creaking, wondering if it'd come up the stairs an' all.

Few hours after the peak it finally started dropping, real slow at first, you'd hardly notice, then all a sudden it was down past the powerpoints and you could see the letterbox again.

Went back the next arvo to have a squiz at what it left behind. Big brown line right round every wall, like the whole house had a tide mark on it. Mother stood there just shaking her head.

Mother: Look at the state of it, love.
Me: Yeah. Coulda been worse though, eh.
Mother: Coulda been worse. Reckon we'll be right, we'll just have to get stuck into it.
Me: No worries. She'll be right.
