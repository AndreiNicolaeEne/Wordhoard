Right, so I minded that house for the week while they were up the coast, and honestly the whole seven days was just one thing after another, start to finish, I'll tell it the way it went.

First night already I knew something was off. Walked in with me bag and the esky full of stuff for the fridge, whole setup different to what I'm used to at home, and the second I got the key in I could hear this hum coming from somewhere that shouldn't have been humming. Turned out to be the hot water system doing something dodgy, this groaning noise like it was warming up to have a whinge all of its own. Texted the owner straight off.

Me: hey all good here, just the hot water's making a weird noise, that normal?

The owner: oh yeah it's always done that, she'll be right, ignore it

Right, okay then. First night in someone else's house and already lying there listening to the hot water have a moan to itself. Didn't sleep heaps, I'll be honest, but you settle in.

Second day the dog got out. Not my fault, or maybe a bit my fault, left the side gate on the latch while I was bringing the bins in, and next thing I look up and there's no dog anywhere. Heart absolutely dropped. Spent about an hour walking up and down the street in me thongs calling out, feeling like an absolute bogan doing it, till a bloke a few doors down spotted her sniffing round his garden and gave me a hand getting her back. Fair dinkum thought I'd have to ring the owners on day two and tell them I'd lost their dog, that would've been a shocker of a phone call.

Neighbour: mate she's just after the cat next door, does it to everyone

Me: oh thank god, cheers mate, I owe you one

No worries off him about it either, which was decent, cause by that point I was heaps rattled.

The garden was the slow one, crept up on me. Didn't notice at first, watered it the way they'd said, but by about day three the tomatoes out back were looking a bit sad, and by day five there was this white sort of mould creeping over half of them. Reckon I'd been overdoing the water in that heat, or underdoing it, honestly no idea. Tried a bit of this and a bit of that, ended up pulling off the worst leaves and hoping for the best. Told meself she'll be right, but by the end of the week half that veggie patch looked like it'd been through something.

Dishwasher died on about the fourth day. Just stopped mid cycle, this little beep and a light flashing, and when I opened it up there was water sitting in the bottom not draining anywhere. Rang a mate who does a bit of appliance stuff on the side.

Me: it's just sitting there full of water, won't drain, won't do a thing

Mate: pull the filter out the bottom, betcha it's full of gunk

Fair enough, it was. Sorted it myself in the end, but not before I'd had a proper little panic about breaking something that wasn't mine to break.

Then the visitor. Wednesday arvo, no warning at all, knock at the door and it's the owner's sister, just turned up cause she was in the area, didn't even know I was minding the place that week, bit of a mix up on their end.

The sister: g'day, sorry, didn't realise you'd be here, everything going alright?

Me: no worries at all, come in, just having a cuppa and some brekkie leftovers, want one?

Ended up having a decent chat, she was sound, but it threw the whole afternoon out, I'd been keen to get a few jobs done and instead we sat there having a whinge about this and that for a good hour and a half.

The real jolt though was the ring. Doing a bit of a tidy round the bedroom on about day five, spotted this ring sitting on the dresser, obviously not cheap, went to move it while I was dusting and next thing it's not there. Gone. Turned that whole room upside down, under the bed, in the drawers, pockets of me own jeans just in case, heart going like anything, thinking how on earth do I explain to these people I've lost their ring. Found it in the end, it'd rolled off the dresser and gone down behind the skirting board, took a coat hanger to fish it out. Never been so relieved in me life, fair dinkum.

The lockout was its own disaster. Took their car down the servo on the Thursday for ciggies, rego sticker still current thank god, only meant to be five minutes, and the door swung shut behind me and I realised halfway down the driveway I'd left me keys sitting right there on the kitchen bench. No spare anywhere I could find, tried every window, nothing budging. Ended up sitting on the front step for about three hours in the heat waiting on a locksmith, ringing round trying to find someone who wasn't booked out solid, absolutely gutted, thinking about chucking a sickie off the whole house-minding gig and just going home. Locksmith finally rocks up about half four.

Locksmith: this happens more than you'd reckon, mate, no dramas, we'll get you in

Cost a bit, but she'll be right, put it down to experience, just keen to get inside by that point and never leave the keys out of reach again.

Left a window cracked in the back room one night for the mozzies to clear out and forgot about it entirely, and of course it bucketed down overnight, proper storm, woke up to a puddle right across the carpet in there and the curtain soaked clean through. Spent the next morning with towels down trying to dry the whole lot out before it started smelling.

Middle of one of the nights there was a noise, a proper bang from out the back, and I'm lying there thinking right, this is it, someone's broken in, had to get up and go have a look with just me phone torch, heart going. Turned out to be nothing more than the esky lid off the back deck that'd blown off in the wind and gone thumping against the fence. Felt like a right idiot standing out there in me thongs at two in the morning over an esky lid, but you don't know till you check, do you.

Even threw a barbie on one arvo out back just for something to do, felt a bit odd cooking up someone else's snags in someone else's yard, but she'll be right, waste not.

By the second last day I was sick of the hot water carry-on, still groaning away every night, so I actually got under the house with a torch and found the pressure valve thing stuck, gave it a proper turn and that was the end of that noise, first quiet night all week. Should've sorted that day one, honestly felt like I'd been there a fortnight by then, not seven days.

Last day was frantic. Hoovering, wiping benches, trying to sort the garden so it didn't look a total write off, hiding the evidence of the dishwasher drama, getting the dog's fur off the couch, the works, heaps of running around for one afternoon.

When they finally walked in I just gave them the whole rundown straight up, no point hiding any of it.

Me: look, it was a decent week overall, dog got out but we got her back quick, dishwasher played up but it's sorted, tomatoes took a bit of a hit I'm sorry to say, and don't go looking too close at that back room carpet

The owner: mate, honestly, don't even worry about it, she'll be right, we owe you heaps for even doing it, see youse back here for a proper thanks meal soon

No dramas in the end, but fair dinkum, never again in a hurry, not without asking for danger money first.
