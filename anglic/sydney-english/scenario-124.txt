Left heaps later than I meant to, and by the time I actually got moving it was already darker than I like it to be walking home of an evening. Not far down the road I got this feeling there was someone behind me, footsteps landing pretty much the same time as mine, like an echo that wasn't quite an echo.

Didn't want to spin round like a goose so I just slowed off a bit, easing up like I was checking my phone, and the steps behind slowed too, same time near enough. Sped up again a bit after, heart going, and yeah, they sped up as well. Could've been nothing, could've been some bloke just walking the same pace as me, but I wasn't about to bet on it.

Normally I'd cut through the lane behind the servo, shaves five minutes off and it's my usual way, but no way I was doing that in the dark with someone on my tail, so I stuck to the main road, the one with the shops still lit up and the odd person out walking their dog. Figured if it did turn dodgy at least there'd be someone round to see it.

Let myself one glance back near the lights, quick, and there was just some fella on his phone, could've been him, could've been nobody, told me nothing either way really.

Last stretch I absolutely legged it, wouldn't admit that to anyone at the time, but I was hoofing it, no dramas about the pride. Got the door shut and locked behind me and just stood there a sec, heart going like mad, pure relief.

Flatmate: You right? You look like you've seen a ghost.
Me: Reckon someone was following me the whole way back.
Flatmate: Fair dinkum? Or reckon you were just spooked.
Me: Dunno, honestly. Probably nothing. She'll be right.

Never found out either way, and I still don't know now. Every time I tell it I go a bit red, because in the moment I was that sure, and it was probably just some poor bloke walking home same as me, keen to get in out of the cold same as I was.
