G'day. Righto, sit down, I'll tell ya about it, cause fair dinkum I'm rooted.

Started before I was even proper awake, six o'clock, still dark, and the body's not ready for that, never is. First thing was shifting the whole stack of pavers off the back of the ute round to the side yard, and that set the pace for the rest of it, dead set, cause you do that heavy right out the gate and everything after just piles on top.

Hands went first. Had the wheelbarrow going up that slope for what felt like hours, blisters coming up under the gloves by brekkie time. Then the back started chiming in too round about ten, from hauling the mixer round the side over and over. Had a break about half nine, ten minutes, barely enough to get a cuppa into ya before it's back on.

The arvo though, that's what nearly did me. About two, sun flogging down, I was seriously thinking, nah, that's it, I'm knocking off early, this is too much for one bloke. But ya don't, ya just keep going, one more barrow, one more panel.

One good thing, the fence panels I reckoned would eat the rest of the day, I smashed out in under the hour, heaps quicker than I thought, felt like a proper win in the middle of all that grind. Boss said that one was worth forty on its own, on top of the day rate, so no complaints there.

Ten hours all up, door to door. Tomorrow's either the same again or he gives us the day to sort the body out, hasn't said which yet. She'll be right either way, but I'm heaps keen on it being the second one, I'll tell ya that for nothing.
