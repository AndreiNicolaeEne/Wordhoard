We were on the wall of the site, the two cobs grazing the verge and the young lad walking one round for the sulky, and I couldn't be biting my tongue on it any longer, so I said it to him straight out.

Me: I'll say it now and have it said. That's two hundred and fifty euro you have off me since before the last Christmas, and not a penny back off ye yet.

My friend: Ah here now, don't be at that, I'm good for it, sure amn't I always good for it.

Me: You said the very same to me above at the mission hall, and you said it again the time of the fair, that you'd have it for me by the March, and the March came and went and came round again and still no sign of it.

My friend: Things is tight on me, ye know rightly things is tight.

Me: I know things is tight for the whole of us, but I'm after asking ye now three times civil, and I'm not for asking a fourth time civil. That gred was for the child's shoes, God forgive me having to say that to ye at all.

My friend: Don't be putting that on me now, don't be making me out like I done ye some wrong, sham. I never done nothing only ask ye one small favour one time.

Me: One small favour sitting there a year and more, and me looking at ye every day of the week on this site like there's nothing between us.

My friend went gammy over it then, wouldn't hardly look at me, staring off down the tober like he'd sooner be anywhere else. After a minute he took what notes he had out of his pocket.

My friend: I've forty on me now, I'll bug ye that below, and I'll have the balance for ye after the next fair, on me solemn word.

Me: On your word. Aye. God bless it, I heard that word off ye before.
