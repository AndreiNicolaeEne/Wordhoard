The eldest: What has a mouth but never ates a scrap, and talks the whole night at every kena door?
Sham: The wireless?
The eldest: It's not. Tell ye?
Sham: Go on, I'm after guessin enough.
The eldest: 'Tis the door itself, creakin.
Sham: Fair play. Here's one back, what runs the whole tober and never lifts a foot?
The eldest: The sulky wheel, is it?
Sham: It is, God bless yer wit. Round to me, go again?
