I'm after losing the cat, three days now and no sign of her. Last seen of an evening below at the wall, black as the ace of spades, the one white paw on her and a nick out of her ear from a scrap. Friendly enough with strangers, no bother in her at all, God love her. A few bob for whoever finds her and brings her home safe. Number below, tear off a strip, and God bless ye.
