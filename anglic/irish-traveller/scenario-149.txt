The eldest: May the tober rise easy under ye and the kena never go cold, God bless ye, and may ye have gred in yer pocket till ye're old, that's sworn, not wished. But if ye wrong this family, may yer tober turn to bog and every kena bar its door on ye, may ye go skimished and gammy till the shades lift ye off it, sworn the very same. One oath, two roads: mind which ye walk.
