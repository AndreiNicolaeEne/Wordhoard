Me: Will ye come here to me a minute and gloke at me proper. I'm after standing at the wall of the site this whole morning watching ye out on the tober with that sulky, and me heart was banging so hard I thought it'd come up out of me mouth.

The friend: Ah would ye stop, it's grand, sure I've the horse well trained, I do it every week of me life.

Me: Grand. That car come round the bend and ye were only a hand's width off going into the ditch, I seen it, I'm not making it up for ye. Look at me hands, they haven't stopped shaking since, and that's an hour ago now.

The friend: Nothing happened though, did it. I'm here amn't I.

Me: Nothing happened THIS time, God between us and all harm. Do ye not remember above at the other site, the collarbone your own cousin broke off a sulky that went over, six month he couldn't lift a mug of tea for himself, couldn't dress himself, and that was only a bend like the one this morning. That's not nothing. That's a man lucky he's still got the use of his arm at all.

The friend: ...

Me: The next time it won't be a collarbone. It'll be your neck off the tober, under a car, and then what. Who's left standing above at the mission saying the rosary over ye. Your own mother, is it, and your children after ye, and me as well, I'd be the one left with it in me head the rest of me days, seeing it happen and not able to stop it.

The friend: (quiet) I hear ye. I do. I'm not saying I'll give it up altogether, but I'll keep off the main tober going round the bends, and I'll slow it, God's honest truth. Will that do ye.

Me: It'll have to do me. For now.
