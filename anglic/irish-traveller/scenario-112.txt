Me: What's the gread for the tober out to the site, before I get in atall?
The driver: Twenty, and that's fair enough for the length of it.
Me: Twenty, is it? Ah stop now, I'll bug ye twelve and not a bob more.
The driver: Twelve? God help us, sure I'd be after losing the price of the diesel on that alone.
Me: Split it so, sixteen, and we're away.
The driver: Sixteen so. Get in, and God bless the road.
