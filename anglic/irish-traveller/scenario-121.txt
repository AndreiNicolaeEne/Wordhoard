Me: Did ye hear that racket outside last night, after midnight?
Neighbor: I did, God bless us, what was it at all?
Me: I'm after thinking 'twas an animal at the bin, but I'm not convinced.
Neighbor: 'Twas out in the tober though, not near either kena.
Me: Could've been some feen skimished, stumbling home, or one of the shades' dogs.
Neighbor: Well, if it comes again tonight, give a shout and I'll do the same.
Me: I will, sure.
