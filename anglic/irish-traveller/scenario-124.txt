I was after leavin' later than I meant to, and the evenin' was after goin' dark quicker than I'd like, so I only set out anyway, sure I'd be grand.

Wasn't far along the tober when I got the feelin' there was a feen behind me, the feet of him landin' near the same time as my own, like there was an echo trailin' after me that wasn't rightly an echo at all. I wouldn't turn round for nothin', so I slowed the step down easy, like I was only strollin' - and didn't the steps behind slow an' all, the very same. So then I picked it up a small bit, not runnin' like, only quicker, and God between us and all harm, they picked up too, same beat, same bit of distance behind me the whole time.

I said to myself I'd not misli be the field the way I'd usually cut through, I'd go the long road instead, where there's the lights on and the chipper open and a few about still. Gammy oul feelin' to have on ye, not trustin' a road you know since you were a child.

I let myself the one look back, quick, over the shoulder, and sure there was nothing certain in it - a shape, maybe, could've been anyone at all, or nothing only the shadow off the lamp. Told me not a thing.

The last bit up to the site I near ran, though I'd never let on to that at the time, and the relief of gettin' the door of the trailer shut and bolted behind me, God, there's no describin' it.

Mother: What ails ye, look at the colour of ye.

Me: Nothin', only I walked fast, the evenin' got away on me.

Never found out to this day was there a soul behind me at all or was it only myself and the dark playin' tricks, please God it was only that. I tell it now laughin' at myself, half embarrassed at how sure I was there, in the moment, that I had a feen on my tail the whole way home.
