Landlord: Well now, how's things above at the site? Grand day for it anyway, thank God.

Me: Not too bad. The young fella's after starting back at the school, God bless him, so that's one thing settled.

Landlord: Good, good. And yourself, keeping well?

Me: Keeping well, please God. Listen, I won't be keeping ye long, I know you're a busy man, but I wanted to have a word about the rent.

Landlord: Go on so.

Me: I've paid ye on the nail every month for coming on four years now, never a day late, never an excuse made. And the whole time that back window's been gammy, the frame rotted out of it, letting the rain in on the child's bed. I'm after asking three times to have it seen to.

Landlord: I hear ye, I do, and I'll get a man out to look at the window. But the rent, sure the rent's the rent, I can't be moving on that.

Me: I'm not asking for nothing for nothing. Take a fifth off it and I'll not bother ye again about the window, I'll see to it myself.

Landlord: A fifth's a lot of gred to be finding, sham. I could go ye a tenth, and that's stretching it as it is.

Me: A tenth won't cover what that window's after costing me in oil alone, trying to keep the heat in on the child.

Landlord: I know, I know. I'm not saying no to ye outright. Let me think on it over the weekend and we'll talk again come Monday.

Me: Fair enough. I'll leave it with ye so, and God bless.

Landlord: God bless.
