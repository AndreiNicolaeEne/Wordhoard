I come up to the van the same as always and the owner's already reaching for the tray before I even open me mouth.

The owner: There she is. Two sausage, chips, brown bread and the tay, no sugar, is it?

Me: Ah near enough, only throw a fried egg on it today, will ye, I've a powerful hunger on me this morning.

The owner: An egg now. Get you, living the high life. Sit down there so, I'll not be a minute with it.

Me: Sure I'm in no rush. How's the gruber treating ye this weather?

The owner: Ah, quiet enough, quiet enough. The rain has the whole tober empty since Tuesday, not a sinner passing only yourself and the shades doing their rounds.

Me: The shades were up at the site again an' all, poking round.

The owner: Were they now. And what were they after this time?

Me: Nothing only themselves, checking the tax on the vans. Sure amn't I here three times in the week now, and every time it's them or the rain following me.

The owner: Three times, is it. I'd have you down as me best customer only for the fella with the sulky, he's in and out of here morning, noon and night since the trotting started up.

Me: How's that gammy horse of his doing anyway, is he after winning anything at all?

The owner: Divil a bit. Skimished more nights than he's sober, that man, and still backing the same horse every week. God help him.

Me: God help us all so, the state of him.

The owner: There y'are now, love, that's hot. Four fifty for ye.

Me: There's a fiver, keep it for yourself.

The owner: Good woman yourself, God bless. Mind how ye go now, and I'll see ye Thursday please God.

Me: Please God. God bless.
