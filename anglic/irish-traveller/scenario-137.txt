The horse was after breaking clean through the wire beyond in the field and the trap smashed to smithereens off the wall of the site, gred and all wasted on it now, and there wasn't a sinner said a word only look from one to the other and then land the gloke on the young one, standing there with his hands below in his pockets and not a thing on him only being there.

The father: You. You had that horse the whole morning, don't be telling me different now.

The young one: I didn't touch him.

The father: Ye were down at the field with him not an hour ago, sure the whole site seen ye there.

The young one: I didn't touch him.

The aunt below at the door of her trailer, arms folded on her, said nothing only watched, and the two lads that had the horse out chasing round the far field earlier, God help them, they said nothing either, only stood looking at their boots like there was something wrote on the ground for them to be reading.

The father: I'll ask you the once more and I want it straight, in front of the whole lot standing here, did you let that horse out or didn't you.

The young one: I swear to God I never went near him.

He said no more after that. Not a word more out of him, his face set the same as it was the whole time, no reddening, no raising of the voice to meet the father's, nothing given back only the flat of it sitting there exactly as he'd put it down.

The father stood a minute, gloking at him like there was more owed, but there was no more coming. The young one turned and misled off up the tober for the kena, aisy as you like, and left the whole crowd of them standing there with the gammy trap in bits and no answer only the one he'd already given, and not a soul putting a hand up to say different.
