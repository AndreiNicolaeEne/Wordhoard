Me: Ah here now, don't be talking to me about that yoke beyond the gate, the burger van is only shockin. The one at the top of the field, that's your only man for a feed.

My friend: Would ye go 'way outta that. Sure the yellow van's chips do be gammy half the time, soggy as anything. It's the one below by the trotting has it, the burgers are massive and the onions done proper, not thrun on raw like yours.

Me: Raw onions me eye. I'm after having a burger off that van of yours three weeks back and I was sick as a small hospital after it, God forgive me for eating it at all. The one above is the queen of chips, I'm telling ye, and the sham that runs it always throws in a few extra for the young ones.

My friend: Sick is it. Sure I seen your man skimished behind the counter of the top van of a Sunday, dropping burgers on the ground and solking them back up onto the grill same as ever. Gloke at the state of him. At least below they'd wash their hands between one order and the next.

Me: G'way, that never happened in your life, ye're only making that up now for the sake of it.

My friend: I am not, I'm after seeing it with me own two eyes, cross me heart and hope to die.

Me: Sure if that did happen he'd have poisoned the whole halting site be now, we'd all be inside above in the hospital, the whole lot of us, feen, beoir and child.

My friend: Ha, would ye stop, that's a right fright of an exaggeration now.

Me: Anyway what about the fish van beside the two of them, nobody could say a bad word about that one, the batter's lovely and the fella's never short with ye.

My friend: True enough, can't fault that one at all, God bless it, sure it never done wrong by anyone.

Me: Right so, we'll misli down the tober the morrow to the three of them and settle it proper, one feed after another, and no crying after if ye can't finish yours.

My friend: Deal so. Bring the gred, I'm skint after the weekend, and bug us a couple of chips off yours while we're waiting on the burgers.
