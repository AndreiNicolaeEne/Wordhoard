The mother:
Hush now, hush now, my little lackeen, you're after roarin' this whole hour and the site gone quiet around ye. Hush now, hush now, I'll not quit your side the night long, not till the grey comes up over the tober. Look, there's the moon ridin' easy above the tober, steady as she goes - the same aul' moon mindin' ye still. Hush now, hush now, alanna, an' God bless ye down to sleep.
