Me: Well, stranger, hardly gloked you there behind the beard, wasn't sure was it yourself or your brother.

The friend: Ara it's only me, the wife has me demented to shave it off but I'm leaving it now for the weather that's in it. How's herself and the childer beyond?

Me: Grand, thank God, touch wood. The young one's after starting school beyond in the town, roaring crying going in every morning, you'd swear we were killing her.

The friend: Ah they settle, they always settle, sure my own were the very same. Is the cob still trotting well for ye?

Me: He is, please God, we're bringing him up for the sulky trotting Sunday if the weather holds. The tober out of the site is in a shocking state though, potholes the size of his hoof in it.

The friend: Don't be talking, I nearly lost the axle on the trailer over one of them Tuesday... Listen, I won't be beating round the bush with you, you're a busy man yourself. Any chance at all you'd see me right for the loan of three hundred, just till the end of the month?

Me: Three hundred's a good bit of gred. What's after happening you, is it bad?

The friend: Ah it's nothing at all really, only a small bit needs doing to the trailer before we'd go anywhere in it, and I'm caught a small bit short this week with one thing and another, ye know yourself how it is.

Me: I'll do it for you so, but I want it back the day ye're paid, not a week after, and no arguing with me over it.

The friend: You'll have it Thursday week, please God, soon as the check clears, I give you my solemn word on that, on my own mother's grave.

Me: Alright so, that'll do.

The friend: You're a gentleman, a proper gentleman so you are, God bless you and every one belonging to you, I won't forget this now as long as I live, God bless you and keep you...

Me: Would ye stop now, you're only after making me blush, the shades'll be coming up thinking there's a wedding on with all the blessing going on.

The friend: Ara stop, aren't you only delighted I didn't tell you I was skimished last night and it's for the drink I want it!

Me: Get away out of that, I know rightly what you'd be doing with three hundred, ye robber ye.
