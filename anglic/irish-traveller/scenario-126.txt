Me: I'm telling ye now and I'm not putting a tap on it. I'm after seeing a light out on the tober past the kena last night, low down along the wall, moving steady as if some feen was carrying it, and there wasn't a soul carrying anything.

The friend: Ah here, ye were only half asleep in the bed and dreamt the whole of it on top of yerself.

Me: I was not asleep, I was wide awake standing at the door of me own trailer having a smoke, me head as clear as it is this minute, and I gloked at that light the whole way down past the wall till it went from me.

The friend: Or ye'd a few cans in ye and yer eyes were only playing tricks on ye.

Me: I hadn't touched a drop, not one drop, I was stone sober, I'd me tea an hour before and nothing after only the smoke, so don't be saying skimished at me.

The friend: Well maybe 'twas a car so, some sham up the tober with the one headlight gone on him.

Me: There's no road runs along that wall at all, only the field, ye know that as well as I do, and there was no engine in it, not a sound out of it, no exhaust, nothing, only the one light gliding low and steady and then it was gone into the field like it never was there.

The friend: Maybe a torch so, one of the young ones out at the cobs mitching about in the dark.

Me: A torch doesn't glide like that, doesn't hold steady with no hand shaking it, no beam swinging about, and there wasn't a young one within an ass's roar of that field, I'm after checking after, not a soul out.

The friend: I still say 'twas a dream in ye, or the drink, or something.

Me: I'll swear it to ye on me own mother's grave, God rest her, and on the candle lit for her this minute in the kena, every word I'm after telling ye is true, I seen what I seen and no one's changing me on it.

The friend: I don't care what ye swear on, I'm not believing a word of it, God help ye.
