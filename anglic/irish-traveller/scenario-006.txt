The eldest: From the step of the kena there over to the big wheel, that's one side. Other side's the wall down to where yer man's trailer's parked, and ya don't go near the tober at all, that's out of bounds altogether, d'ya hear me now.

The young one: What if I'm bein chased and I go over the tober by mistake?

The eldest: Then that's you out for the round, gammy, ya don't get caught fair, ya just go on and freeze where ya stepped over, simple as that.

The young one: And what counts as a real tag so?

The eldest: Two full hands now, none of this brushin off ya with one finger and sayin ya got me, that's no tag at all. Two hands or it doesn't count, God bless the mark.

The young one: And if he does get me proper?

The eldest: Yer frozen solid where ya stand, can't move a foot, till one of the ones still runnin comes over and taps ya loose, then off with ya again quick as ya like.

The young one: How does the round end?

The eldest: When the whole lot of us is froze and there's no one left runnin to tap us loose, that's it done. Whoever got froze the very first is on chasin next round.

The young one: I might get it wrong the first time.

The eldest: Ah don't be worryin yerself over that, sure the first go doesn't count against ya no matter what ya do, amn't I after tellin ya the whole of it now anyway.
