The younger: Isn't it fierce out today, the head near lifted off ye.
The elder: It is, love, it is. Worse than Tuesday, and that was bitter enough.
The younger: It was, aye, it was.
The elder: I'm after minding the same years back, above at the old site, the tober froze over and the kena rattling with it. Nothing new under God.
The younger: D'ye think tomorrow'll ease off?
The elder: It might, please God. We'll see.
