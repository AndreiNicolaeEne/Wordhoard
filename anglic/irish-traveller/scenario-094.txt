The mother called the two of them in off the road, from beside the wall of the site where they were pucking a ball about near the cob, and she brought them into the trailer and sat them down on the bunk, in tight against her on both sides. She held onto their two hands a small minute before she said anything at all.

The mother: Now loves, I have to tell ye something and I want the both of ye to listen to me proper. Yeer grandad's after dying, God rest him. He went in the night, peaceful.

The eldest sat forward on the bunk, working it over slow.

The eldest: But what does that mean though, after dying? Like is he still anywhere at all, or is that it now, is he just gone?

The mother: His body's stopped, love, and that's the hard part of it, he won't be sitting at the fire with us or shouting after ye for running wild again. I won't tell ye different, that part is real and it's sore. But we believe, and yeer grandad believed it stronger than any of us going, that his soul's gone on to God, and that's not nothing at all, that's everything to him now.

The younger pulled at the mother's sleeve then, wanting his own bit of it.

The younger: Will he be cold though, below in the ground?

The mother: No pet, he won't feel cold nor warm nor anything else ever again. That's all finished with him now.

The mother opened her two arms and drew them in against her, tight as she could get them, and she put her hand over the head of the younger, the sublik, still holding the pair of them close.

The eldest asked it low then, not lifting her head off the mother's shoulder.

The eldest: Will we ever see him again though?

The mother: We will, love, please God, not the way we're used to, but we'll be with him again someday, every one of us, and that's a promise I hold to hard.

The mother: Now over the next few days there'll be a wake for him, and we'll all misli down together, the whole site near enough, in and out of the kena the whole time, and after that we'll bury him. It'll be a sad old few days, but ye'll not be on yeer own through one minute of it. Go on now, finish yeer game, and God bless him.
