Me: Did ye hear off yer one, only an hour ago, cancelling on us again for the morrow.

My friend: Ah for the love of God, not again. What's the excuse this time?

Me: Says she's after being called in sudden for a shift, some class of an emergency below in the work.

My friend: Ah here now, isn't that the very same one she gave the time before last.

Me: It is near enough, God's honest truth. And I'm after counting it up in me head there, that's four times now, four times she's after standing us up.

My friend: Four times! Ah that's shocking so it is. And every time it's some gammy old excuse wouldn't wash with a child, let alone the two of us.

Me: D'ye think there's any truth in it at all, or is she only saying whatever comes into her head to get out of it easy.

My friend: I couldn't tell ye, in fairness the work does be mad busy this time of year for her. But four times running, that's not bad luck anymore, that's a pattern and no mistake.

Me: I know well. God love her, I do think an awful lot of her still, but sure you can only be let down so many times before ye stop asking altogether.

My friend: True for ye. Would ye even ask her the next time, or would ye leave her out of it altogether?

Me: I'd ask her, I would, I wouldn't want to be the one leaving her out over it. But if she cancels on us again, that's it now, we go on without her and no more said.

My friend: That's the head on it. We'll ask her one more time, and if she's after cancelling a fifth time, we just misli on without her, the two of us, and she can do whatever she likes then.

Me: Exactly right. Sure what else can ye do.
