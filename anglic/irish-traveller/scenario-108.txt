The first thing you'll want to hear is the step is done. Finally. The step is solid now, thank God, you could dance a set on it. Only of course no sooner is that sorted than the water pump's gone gammy on us, making a noise like a beoir screaming below the sink, and himself says it's the seal but he's said that about three things now that turned out not to be the seal, so we'll see. The big news, and you'll have heard some of it off the mother on the phone already I'd say, is that my workmate is after moving the whole operation. It come about sudden enough in the finish, one week he's cursing the council up and down and the next he's signing for the new place, so it goes. I'm below there three days now instead of the two, which is no harm for the gred, God knows we can use it.

Don't be worrying, I never once mentioned the tin of beans to anyone, not a word, so your secret's safe as it ever was.

On the neighbours now. I do get a terrible dose of the lonesomeness some evenings, worse than I let on to anyone here, and I sat out the back of the trailer one night last month and cried like a young one over nothing at all, only the cob put his head over the fence looking at me like he knew rightly what was wrong, and I felt like an awful fool after, so I never told a soul only I'm telling you now.

The last while has been middling only, if I'm honest with you. But the last few weeks turned soft and warm, thank God, and the sulky trotting started up again of an evening, so there's a bit of life back in the place, fellas down at the wall watching the trials the way they always do, arguing the toss over whose cob has the better turn of speed.

Now I've a fair few questions for you and you're not to be leaving them unanswered the way you do.
