Cousin: God bless, are ye there. I heard off the mother ye're gammy this while, how bad is it going on ye?

Me: Ah I'm after having three days of it now. Started with an ache in the joints, a scratchy throat with it, thought nothing much of it at the start. Then yesterday didn't a fever come on top of the whole lot.

Cousin: Ah God love ye. And how's it sitting on ye today, any easier?

Me: 'Tis a small bit easier than yesterday, I'll say that for it, but far from right still, I wouldn't be saying I'm grand at all.

Cousin: And what have ye been doing for it, are ye minding yerself?

Me: I am. First thing I done was a gargle of the warm salted water for the throat, morning and night. And when the fever wasn't breaking off itself I put a cool cloth across the forehead, changing it through the whole night, up and down to the sink with it.

Cousin: And are ye eating anything at all?

Me: Only the broth and a drop of weak tea, nothing heavy on the stomach. And sleeping whatever hour the body lets me, I'm not fighting it.

Cousin: Well ye're to rest yerself properly now and not be pushing through it, d'ye hear me, none of yer nonsense.

Me: I hear ye. I've it set in me own head anyway, if that fever climbs any higher on me, or the breathing turns hard on me at all, I'm away up and getting seen, no messing about. But not before that, please God it won't come to it.

Cousin: Will I come over to ye, bring ye a bit of food, sit with ye an hour even?

Me: Ah ye needn't be putting yerself out, sure I've enough here to be going on with, though I won't say no if ye're passing, a bit of company wouldn't go astray on me either.
