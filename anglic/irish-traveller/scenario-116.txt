Me: Did ye see that at all? Plain as the nose on yer face, the ball was over the line before your man got the boot near it, and yer referee's standing there shaking the head on him like he seen nothing.

The cousin: Ah would ye stop. He had the best view in the whole ground, better than you on the settee anyway, and if he's after calling no goal then no goal it is, end of.

Me: Best view, God help us. Sure the same fella let your man off a stone wall of a foul earlier in the half and never reached for the pocket at all, and I said it to ye then, I did, that referee's gammy, so he is, gammy from the first whistle.

The cousin: And what about the free ye got below in the second half, went yer way fair and square and not a peep out of ye then. Quiet as a mouse ye were that time.

Me: That's a different thing altogether, that is.

The cousin: It's only different in your head, boy.

Me: Start to finish the man was shook. Cards he never gave, cards he'd no business giving, offsides called that weren't there and the real ones let go. A gloke of a showing if ever I seen one, and I've seen a few.

The cousin: Sure the sham below us in the stand said it after, said the ref had an awful stinker of a day alright, but the big call, the one everyone's on about, he called that one right. Middle of the road man, wouldn't say boo to a goose, and even he's not fully with ye.

Me: He's not fully with you either so.

The cousin: He's not, no.

Me: There ye go.

The cousin: There's no talking to ye at all at all.

Me: Nor you to me, love.

The cousin: God bless us and save us, the whole shower of them's useless. Every referee in the country cut from the one cloth.

Me: Amen to that. Useless, the lot of them.
