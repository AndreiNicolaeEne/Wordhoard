The traveller: God bless, I'm only after moving onto the site above, would ye know the road to the market at all?

The stranger: I would, love. Take yer first left by the chapel there, and watch for the corner with the auld pub, that's yer second turn.

The traveller: Is it far, would ye say?

The stranger: Ah no, only about fifteen minutes on the foot from here.

The traveller: God bless ye so.

The stranger: Safe home to ye.
