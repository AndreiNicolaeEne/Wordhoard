I go up to the tailor for the good suit for the wedding, and no sooner do I lay me eyes on it below on the hanger than I know well it's not right.

Me: God bless the work, but that's never what I left in with ye. Three buttons I said, and a straight hem. That below has two on it, and the hem gone off on the angle altogether.

The tailor: Sure that's exactly the way we agreed it above, love. Two buttons, hem cut on the slant. I have it took down meself, I'm telling ye.

Me: I stood in that door with the child on me hip and said it plain, three buttons and no messing with the hem. Have another gloke at it, go on.

The sham: She's right so, God's truth. I was after standing there the whole time with her.

The tailor: Hold on now, I'll go below and get the docket, we'll see what's wrote.

The tailor: ...Well now. There it is in me own hand, plain as day. Three button. Amn't I after telling meself different a minute ago, God forgive me, the eyes going on me with the small writing below in the light.

Me: Didn't I say it to ye. And what about the hem so?

The tailor: The hem's not marked clear at all under that, could be read either way if I'm honest with ye, but sure I'm not leaving ye standing here arguing the toss over an inch of cloth. I'll run it straight the same as ye're after saying, and the buttons put back the way they should be.

Me: Fair enough so, as long as it's sorted before the day.

The tailor: Give me the two days on it and it'll be below in the van to ye, right as rain, and not a gred off ye for the bother, God knows it was my own fault.

Me: God bless ye now, and mind ye don't be reading the small writing without the glasses on ye the next time.
