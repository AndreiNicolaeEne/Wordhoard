So okay, I have to tell you about this house-sitting week because it was seven days straight of stuff going sideways, like one thing after another, and I'm still not fully recovered honestly.

It started the first night already feeling off, and I couldn't even tell you why. Like I get there, I've got my bag, I've got the folder they left me with the wifi password and the feeding times and all that, and I'm walking around turning lights on because the house just felt weird. Not haunted weird, just, I don't know, a house makes different noises when it's not your house. The fridge was doing this hum that would cut out and then kick back on, and every time it did that I about jumped out of my skin. I texted the friend like, "dude I don't think I'm gonna sleep at all this week," and she's like, "you'll be fine, it's just a house." Easy for her to say, she wasn't the one lying there at midnight listening to a stranger's pipes.

Second day the dog got out. I still don't totally know how, I think the gate latch doesn't catch all the way unless you really lean on it, and I was hauling the trash cans out and didn't hear it swing open behind me. I turn around and I'm like, wait, where's the dog. And then I see this streak of him going down the sidewalk like he's late for something. So I'm chasing him in my socks, no shoes, down the block, and the whole time I'm going, hey, hey, come here, buddy, in this super calm voice I did not feel at all. Caught him maybe an hour later, he'd just gone and parked himself under some neighbor's car like it was the most normal thing in the world. My heart was going so hard. I texted the owners something vague like "all good here, dog's having a great time," which, I mean, technically true.

The plant thing crept up on me and I feel bad about it because it wasn't even dramatic, it was just slow. There's this row of pots on the back patio and I was watering them like the note said, every other day, but by like day four they're looking kind of sad and droopy and I'm thinking, am I overwatering or underwatering, because with plants it looks the same either way, it's just sad. By day six one of them had just given up completely. I didn't say anything about that part till later.

Then the garbage disposal died on me, I want to say day three. I'm rinsing a plate and I flip the switch and it just makes this one sad little grinding sound and then nothing, total silence, and there's water starting to back up in the sink. So now I'm googling garbage disposal reset button at eleven at night, find this little red button underneath the thing, push it, nothing. I ended up just not using that side of the sink for like four days and washing dishes like it's 1950.

The visitor showed up on maybe day four, no warning at all, just a knock on the door, and I about had a heart attack because I wasn't expecting anybody. Turned out to be the neighbor from next door.

Neighbor: Oh hey, sorry, didn't mean to scare you, I saw the lights on and figured I'd just check who's staying here this week.

Me: No, all good, I'm just watching the place for them, they're out of town.

Neighbor: Oh nice, yeah they mentioned that. You need anything, I'm right there.

It was fine, it was nice actually, but for about ten seconds when that knock happened I thought something was really wrong.

The real jolt though was day five, when I thought I'd lost this ring the owners keep in a little dish by the sink, like a family thing, and I'd picked it up to move it while I was cleaning and set it down somewhere and then could not for the life of me find it. I tore that kitchen apart. I was already writing the text in my head, like, "so, funny story," and I was totally panicking, and then I find it, it had rolled under the dish rack, sitting right there the whole time. I sat down on the kitchen floor for a second after that one.

Getting locked out was its own whole thing. I went to grab something from my car and the door swings shut behind me and it's one of those doors that just locks on its own, no thinking about it, click, done. And of course my phone's inside, my keys are inside, everything's inside. So I'm standing in the driveway like an idiot for a solid three hours until the friend could swing by with the spare I'd left at her place way back at the start of the week, thank god I actually did that. Sat on the porch steps that whole time just watching cars go by.

Somewhere in there it rained, which never happens, and of course I'd left a window cracked in the back bedroom because it was stuffy in there, and I come in from somewhere and there's this puddle on the windowsill and a wet patch spreading on the carpet underneath it. I threw every towel I could find at it and propped a fan in there for like two days straight.

And then the noise. Middle of the night, maybe day six, this thumping sound from somewhere in the house, and I am lying there frozen going, okay, it's probably nothing, it's probably the water heater or something, but you have to go check, you can't just lie there. So I get up, grab the closest heavy object, which was a hairbrush, incredible weapon, and go creeping down the hall, and it's the dog, who had somehow gotten into the closet and knocked something off a shelf and was just standing there looking extremely pleased with himself.

The fix, finally, came on day six, for that garbage disposal. I found a little hex wrench thing taped to the underside of the unit that I hadn't even noticed before, stuck it in the bottom, wiggled it around like the video said, hit reset again, and it roared back to life like nothing ever happened. I felt like I'd personally saved the whole house.

Last day I was in full-on tidying mode, like vacuuming at nine in the morning, wiping counters that were already clean, moving the dead plant to the side yard where hopefully nobody would notice right away, fluffing pillows that don't need fluffing. Total chaos energy but the quiet, invisible kind.

When they finally walked in I gave them the whole rundown, the nice version.

Me: So, it was a great week, the dog was an angel, everything's good.

The owner: Yeah? No issues at all?

Me: I mean, the disposal did this weird thing for a couple days but I got it sorted, it's totally fine now.

The owner: Oh no, sorry about that.

Me: Nah, don't even worry about it, it was easy.

I did not mention the plant. I did not mention the window. I did mention the neighbor stopping by because that one made me look responsible. Anyway, house is still standing, dog's fine, I'm never house-sitting again for at least a month.
