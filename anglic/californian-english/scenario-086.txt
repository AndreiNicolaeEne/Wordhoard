okay so I know it's been like four days but I still have to tell you about my sister's birthday because it was A Whole Thing and I'm honestly still recovering emotionally if not physically lol

so the plan was just dinner, right, like a low key thing at her place, some appetizers, maybe a toast. that lasted about forty five minutes before it turned into a full blown situation. her husband had this whole spread going, and my mom brought like three different desserts because god forbid there's only one option, and everybody kept doing these little toasts to my sister all night, like every twenty minutes somebody's standing up with a glass going "to the best sister/daughter/whatever" and every single time I'm like okay yeah, one more, for her, obviously, how could I not

and that's kind of how the whole night went. one more plate because her husband made too much and somebody had to finish it. one more glass because we were toasting. one more because her workmate showed up late and we had to "catch her up." I wasn't even tracking anymore at that point, I was just saying yes to everything put in front of me because it was my sister's night and I love her and also, let's be honest, because it was really good and I have zero self control once we get going like that

last thing I actually remember clearly, and I mean clearly clearly, is us all out on the patio doing this ridiculous group toast where my mom made everybody say one thing they loved about my sister and it turned into this whole emotional moment and I was crying a little and laughing at the same time. after that it's just pieces. like snapshots. somebody put music on. I remember being in the kitchen at some point doing what I can only describe as interpretive dance with a wooden spoon. I remember singing along to something super loud with my sister's workmate, both of us just screaming the words, no idea what song. I remember somebody's cousin trying to teach me some dance move in the living room and me absolutely not getting it but trying anyway for like twenty minutes straight. and then just, nothing. total blank until the next morning.

which brings us to the morning. dude. I have never in my life wanted to just not be a physical body more than I did lying there. head was pounding like something with an actual heartbeat, stomach doing this slow rolling thing where even the idea of food, any food, made me want to lay back down and not move again. and the light, it wasn't even that bright a morning i don't think, but it felt like somebody had installed stadium lighting directly over the bed. every little sound in the house, like a cabinet closing two rooms away, felt like it was happening inside my skull. sitting up took actual planning. like I had to think about it first, brace myself, and then do it in stages

my sister was already up, obviously, moving around normal like a functioning human being, which felt personally insulting given the circumstances. she comes in with a glass of water and just takes one look at me and goes

The sister: oh wow. okay. good morning sunshine

Me: please don't

The sister: I'm just saying, you had a whole moment with a wooden spoon last night, I hope you know that

Me: I do not know that, and I would like to continue not knowing that

The sister: too late, it's already a whole story now, mom has it on video probably

and she wasn't being mean about it at all, she was cracking up the whole time, and honestly I would've done the exact same thing to her if the roles were reversed, but lying there in that state I just groaned and put the pillow over my face and made some kind of promise to the ceiling, or to whatever's listening, that I was never letting a night get away from me like that again. like actually never. and even as I was thinking it I knew, I knew, that I have made that exact same promise before, probably more than once, and here we are again, so take that for what it's worth

the rest of the morning was just survival mode. my sister suggested we take a walk around the block, just to get some air, and I want to be clear this is a walk I have done a hundred times and normally it's nothing, ten minutes, no big deal, and that day it felt like a full hike. every step felt like a decision I had to actively make. I also told myself I was going to do one load of laundry since it was just sitting there, and that did not happen until like four in the afternoon, and even then it took me three separate attempts to just get up and start it

I made myself some toast around noon, plain, nothing on it really, just enough to get something in my stomach without setting anything off, and I sat on the couch not really watching whatever was on tv, just kind of existing, waiting for my body to forgive me. which it did, slowly. by early evening I actually felt like a person again, ate a real dinner, could hold a conversation without wanting to lie back down halfway through it

and in between all that, throughout the day honestly, these little flashes kept coming back to me. and one of them, dude, one of them I really need to deal with. apparently at some point I told my sister's workmate, the one I was screaming song lyrics with, that her ex was, and I'm quoting what my sister told me because I genuinely don't remember saying this, "not even that interesting, honestly, kind of a boring guy if we're being real." to her face. unprompted. I don't even know this woman that well. I've thought about it probably a dozen times since and every single time I get this hot embarrassed feeling all over again. I'm gonna have to say something to her next time I see her, like actually apologize, because that is not my business to have an opinion on out loud like that, drunk or not

so yeah. that was the night. so good in the moment, worth it in a lot of ways because it really was my sister's night and she deserved every bit of the fuss, and also just, a lot, in every direction, the next day. I sat there most of that morning thinking real hard about pacing myself better, being the person who says okay I'm good after this one, and I mean it when I think it, I really do, but if I'm being honest with you and with myself, I'll probably end up right back in that same spot again next time somebody I love is celebrating something. that's just kind of how it goes with me
