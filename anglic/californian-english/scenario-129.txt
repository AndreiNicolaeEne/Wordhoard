So okay, it started off super chill, honestly. I was out on the patio around like one, one thirty, just doing nothing, and I see this little gray smudge way off on the ridge, real far off, like not even a thing yet. I'm just watching it, sipping my coffee, like whatever, they'll knock it down before it goes anywhere. Anyway I texted my roommate like hey you seeing that, and she's like yeah probably nothing, it's so far.

Within like an hour, no joke, the wind just picked up out of nowhere and shoved that whole thing straight at us. My eyes started stinging before I even smelled smoke, that's how fast it was, the sting came first and then the smell hit right after. And I was still kind of like, do we actually gotta go, or is this just gonna blow past us. Then the neighbor came banging on my door.

The neighbor: You gotta go, like now, it jumped the ridge.

Me: Wait, seriously? It looked so far like ten minutes ago.

The neighbor: Seriously, don't grab anything dumb, just go, go.

And I moved so fast after that, dude. Twenty minutes tops and I was out the door, grabbed my laptop, the dog, my meds, and that was it, didn't even think about anything else. My throat was already raw just walking to the car, and the heat was pressing on my whole face, like somebody left an oven door open right next to me. I looked back once, right when I got onto the main road, and the whole hillside behind me was just glowing orange, and I didn't look again after that, just drove.

Two nights at my friend's place not knowing anything, just refreshing my phone over and over, so on edge the whole time. And then finally they let us back in, and the place was just fine. Untouched, totally fine. I stood in the driveway and just started crying, no joke, didn't even mean to.
