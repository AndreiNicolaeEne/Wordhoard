So I wake up and for like two seconds I don't remember, and then it just lands on me all at once, this heavy dumb feeling, like oh no, today's the day. Months ago my sister asked me for the millionth time to help clear out our mom's storage unit and I finally just snapped and swore, out loud, in front of her, that I would give up one whole entire day and get it done, no excuses. And she goes, okay, I'm holding you to that. And now here we are.

I lay there for a good ten minutes just complaining to the ceiling. Told myself this was the dumbest promise I ever made in my life. Nobody needed me to swear anything, I coulda just said soon, but no, I had to go and make it a whole thing.

Whole drive down the 405 I'm muttering to myself, why did I say a full day, why not a couple hours, who even talks like that. Traffic's not even bad and I'm still mad about it.

Get there and the unit is worse than I remembered. Boxes stacked to the ceiling, everything smells like dust and old cardboard. I'm sorting through it and complaining about literally every box, this one's too heavy, this one's pointless, why do we even still have this.

Her workmate stops by around noon to drop off tape and goes, you doing okay?

Me: honestly, no, but I said I would so.

Around hour six I'm hot, my back hurts, and I'm seriously thinking, I could just leave, say I'll finish tomorrow. But then I just tell myself, straight up, no, you swore, that's it, done thinking about it. And I keep going.

Finish around eight that night, unit totally empty, swept out and everything. Complained the entire day. Never once actually stopped.
