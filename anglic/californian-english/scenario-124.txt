So I left way later than I meant to, like closer to eleven than ten, and I knew that the second I stepped outside. It gets dark early enough already and I was not trying to be walking that stretch by my usual block, but whatever, I was already out the door.

Wasn't even two minutes in when I started getting that feeling, you know, like footsteps behind me landing right on top of my own, almost too on beat with it. First I just told myself it's nothing, everybody's walking somewhere, it's a sidewalk, chill. But it kept nagging at me so I did the thing where you slow down a little, not obvious, just easing off like you're checking your phone, and the steps behind me slowed too. Same gap, same rhythm. Okay. Then I sped up, not a jog, just a quicker walk, and yeah, they matched that too. That's when it stopped being nothing in my head.

I wasn't about to turn around and make it a whole thing, so I just decided, forget the shortcut through the alley by the church, I'm cutting over to the main street instead, more lit, more people, closer to actual businesses still open. Once I hit the corner I let myself do one glance back, real quick, like I was just checking for cars. Didn't see anybody close, didn't see nobody far either, just headlights and a guy on a bike, so it told me literally nothing.

Last block or two I was hauling, like power walking so hard it was basically jogging, and I would never in a million years admit that pace to anyone's face. Got to my door, hands kind of shaking getting the key in, and the second that door shut and locked I let out this breath I didn't know I was holding the whole way.

Never found out what it actually was. Could've been somebody just going the same direction as me, could've been nothing at all. I still don't know, honestly, and when I tell people now I'm always kind of laughing at myself, like, dude, I was so sure in the moment, and I still can't say for certain I was wrong.
