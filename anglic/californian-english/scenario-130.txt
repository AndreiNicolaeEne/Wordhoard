Okay so like, I gotta just walk you through the whole day because it was actually insane. Checked again like an hour later and I'm like oh no, it's already up more, so I started hauling stuff up off the floor while I still had time to actually think about it and not just panic-grab whatever.

By midmorning it's at my knees, which, dude, that is not nothing, that's a totally different situation. So I'm moving everything I can onto the counters, onto the dresser, whatever's highest in each room, and it just keeps coming. Didn't stop. Early afternoon it peaked and it went past the highest stuff I'd moved, like the water just didn't care what I'd decided was safe.

The eldest: is it still coming up?
Me: I don't know, I really don't know, just stay up there.

That part was the worst honestly, just sitting there listening to it hit the walls, this constant sloshing sound, and you can't do anything, you're just waiting it out. Hours after it peaked it finally started going back down, real slow, like you'd stare at it for twenty minutes and go huh, okay, maybe an inch.

Went back the next day and there's this line all the way around every wall, right where it sat the longest, like a little watermark showing exactly how high it got. Just stood there looking at it for a while. Super weird seeing it in daylight, like the house remembers something you lived through hour by hour and now it's just this one flat mark on the wall.
