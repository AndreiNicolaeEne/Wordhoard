So okay, you know how I always say the worst trip of my life happened and I never actually tell the whole thing? Fine. Sit tight, 'cause this one's long, and I promise by the end you're gonna be laughing even though none of it was funny while it was happening.

Bus tickets bought early, hotel booked, dress hanging in a garment bag, the whole deal. My roommate was coming with me 'cause she didn't wanna drive up alone and honestly neither did I. That was the plan.

First delay, and we hadn't even left the city yet. We're on the 405 trying to get downtown to the station and traffic is just parked, not moving, six in the morning and it's already a parking lot, I don't even know why. We get to the station and the bus that's supposed to leave at six is still sitting there with its hazards on, and the driver's like, "we're running about forty minutes behind, just some paperwork stuff," which, yeah, okay, forty minutes, whatever, we can eat that.
