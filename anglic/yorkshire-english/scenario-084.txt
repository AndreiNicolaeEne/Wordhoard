The stranger: Ey up, sorry to bother thee, love, but tha wouldn't have a drop o' water spare? Fair mafted out 'ere.

The one asked: Here, get that down thee.

The stranger: Oh nay, just a sip'll do me, don't want to put thee out.

The one asked: Give over, sithee, sup t'lot, there's plenty more where that come from.

The stranger: Ta ever so. That's champion, that is. Right good of thee.
