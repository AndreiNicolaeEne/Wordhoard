I were sat wi' me tea when there were a knock, and in she come, coat wet through, wanting me to hear her side of it afore owt got twisted round.

First friend: Ey up. Tha got a minute? I just need someone to hear us out, I'm not being funny wi' thee.

Me: Go on then, love, sit thissen down. I'll listen but I'm not taking sides, mind, not till I've heard it all.

First friend: Well it started over nowt, honest. She started chelping at us in front of everyone at work and I'd had enough of it, that's all.

Me: Right. And that were t'first of it, were it?

First friend: Aye. I never raised me voice first, I swear on it.

She went not long after, and I'd not even fettled t'table from us tea when there were another knock, and it were him this time, stood on t'step looking fair put out.

Second friend: Now then. Has she been round? Bet she's telled thee I started it.

Me: I'm saying nowt about what were said afore thee got here. What's thy version then?

Second friend: She started on us, calling us idle in front of t'whole shop. I only said summat back cause I were sick of it.

Me: So neither of thee started it, and yet here's both of thee on me step in one afternoon. Is this about t'shift swap again, or is it summat else under it?

Second friend: ...It's about her never saying ta for owt I do for her. That's what it is, if I'm honest.

Me: Aye, reckon that's nearer t'truth than either version thee's give us.

Second friend: Maybe I could've said it different, tha knows. Weren't just her fault, not really.

Me: Why don't thee two talk to each other direct, sithee, stead of coming to me separate. I'll set it up if it helps thee both, get thee round mine, side t'table proper and sort it plain. It'll be reet.

Second friend: Champion. Aye, go on then.
