The sister: Now then, how you keeping on? Our mam said tha's badly.

Me: Ey up. Aye, been rough three day now. Started wi' me joints achin' and me throat all scratchy, and then yesterday a fever came on top of it an' all.

The sister: Bloody hell. And today, how's tha finding it?

Me: Today's a bit easier, tha knows, not champion but better than yesterday were. One minute nithered, next mafted, can't mek me mind up which.

The sister: What tha been doin' for it then?

Me: Gargled warm salted water for me throat first off, that's allus t'first thing. Then when t'fever wouldn't break on its own I put a cool cloth on me forehead, changed it through t'night, reet through till mornin'. Kept to broth and weak tea, nowt else has gone down right, and laiked at sleepin' whenever me body'd let me.

The sister: Well tha wants to rest proper, love, none o' this pushin' through it like tha allus does.

Me: I know, I know, I'll not push it, I've nowt to prove. If t'fever climbs any higher or me breathin' goes hard on us, I'll get missen seen to, but not afore that, sithee.

The sister: Sithee thissen an' all. I can fetch thee summat over, or sit wi' thee an hour if tha wants company while tha's laid up.

Me: Go on then, a breadcake wouldn't go amiss. Don't fuss thissen too much though, love, I'll be reet.

The sister: It'll be reet, aye, but I'm bringin' it anyroad, no arguing. Side t'table for us when I'm there and I'll fettle owt wants fettling.

Me: Ta, flower. Champion, that.
