The eldest: Ey up, I've a good un, tha knows. What goes round t'house all day and nivver sees no light?
The one at the counter: A clock?
The eldest: Nay, soft ha'porth, it's t'cat, laikin under t'table.
The one at the counter: My turn. What has a face and hands but nobbut walks when tha winds it?
The eldest: That's t'clock on t'mantel.
The one at the counter: Champion. Another round?
The eldest: Go on, love, it'll be reet.
