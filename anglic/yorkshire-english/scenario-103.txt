Traveler: Now then, sorry to stop thee, does tha know t'way to t'market?

Stranger: Ey up, love. Tha wants first left just past t'chippy, then keep on while tha spots t'big church on t'corner, tha can't miss it.

Traveler: How far is it, or how long's the walk?

Stranger: Bout quarter hour on foot, no more. It'll be reet, tha's nearly there once tha's past t'corner.

Traveler: Champion, ta very much.

Stranger: No bother, sithee.
