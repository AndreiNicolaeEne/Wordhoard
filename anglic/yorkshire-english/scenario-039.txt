The gaffer: Reet, afore we all sup up an bugger off home, tomorra's not sorted, an it'll not sort itsen.

The eldest: Thought tha were seein to that at dinner.

The gaffer: Summat came up, didn't it. Ey up, listen in, this is it.

The young lad: Go on then. An it's not eight, I've told thee, I'm not standing about in t'dark for nob'dy.

The joiner: Get thissen up, tha's twenty-two, not eighty. Six is what it wants, sun's barely up but at least tha's not laiking about half t'morning.

The eldest: Six is for masochists an milkmen. Split it, call it seven, champion, everyone's happy.

The gaffer: Seven it is then, no more chelp. It's t'roof job ovver by t'canal, finishing off afore t'weather turns.

The joiner: I'll bring t'ladders an felt, tha knows I've room in t'van.

The young lad: I'll fettle t'genny an bring us brews.

The eldest: I'll sort t'scaffold boards, an sithee, mind we're low on fixings, we want more or we're stuck by dinner.

The gaffer: Aye, I'll pick summat up first thing. Now then, forecast's siling it down after twelve, so whoever's up top wants to be quick.

The young lad: Why's it allus me stuck up there in t'wet, freezing me shins off. Nithered to death for a laugh, that's me.

The joiner: Tha volunteered thissen last week, lad, don't come roaring to me.

The gaffer: Right, headcount. Me, thee, thee, an thee, that's four. Ow do, what about thi labourer, is he in?

The eldest: Nah, he's off to t'doctor's, back's gone again. Said not to wait on him.

The gaffer: Fair do's. Four's plenty for that job anyroad.

The joiner: So who owes who now, since I'm bringing t'ladders again.

The eldest: Tha's owed nowt, tha's had thy round bought twice this week.

The gaffer: Reet, that's settled. Seven sharp, don't be late, love, or tha'll be walking.
