Two of them sat in of an evening, telly on low, kettle just boiled, siling it down outside so nobody were going anywhere.

Me: Ey up, tell thi summat, if I got a right load o' brass tomorrow, first thing I'd do is get thissen down t' club and buy a breadcake and a can for every bugger in't place.

My workmate: Ow do, dreamer. That's it then, is it. Whole windfall on breadcakes.

Me: Nay, I'm only saying. Then I'd get a static caravan up at coast, laik about all summer, none o' this clocking in of a morning.

My workmate: Grand plan, till first winter comes and tha's nithered through and through, stuck in a tin box wi' no telly signal. Sithee, tha wants to put some by first. Side away happen half of it, don't touch it, let it sit.

Me: Half? Tha's gormless if tha thinks I'm sat on half a fortune doing nowt with it.

My workmate: I'm not saying doing nowt, I'm saying not blowing the lot on breadcakes and a caravan tha'll be mafted in come August anyway.

Me: Aye, alright, how about this then. Buy summat daft first, just the once, get it out us system. I were thinking, tha knows, a proper indoor swimming pool, heated, built onto back of t' house.

My workmate: In Leeds? Tha'd need to put t' wood in t' oil an' all just to keep it running, never mind rain getting in through roof. That's not sensible, love, that's chelp, that is, not a plan.

Me: Champion idea though, while it lasted.

My workmate: It were, while it lasted. Right, side t'table, get kettle back on, fettle us a brew and we'll talk sense. New boiler, week away somewhere, and put rest by. Call it reet.

Me: Aye. It'll be reet.

My workmate: Sensible allus wins wi' folk like us in t' end, love.

Me: Ey, don't go roaring about it, we've still to win it first.

My workmate: True enough. Offcumdens can keep their swimming pools. Kettle's boiled, flower, side thissen up to table and have a spice biscuit, and we'll say no more about it.
