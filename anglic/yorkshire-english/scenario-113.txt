Ey up. Now then. Ow do, love, get thissen sat down, sithee, I've got to tell thee about last night, proper carry on it were and tha missed the whole thing.

Four on us round t'table, same as always, none on us offcumdens, been playing together donkeys' years so we all reckoned we knew how it'd go. Stakes were agreed afore a single card went out an' all, nowt daft, just coppers really, enough that losing wouldn't sting nob'dy, we were only laiking for us own amusement if I'm honest wi' thee, that's all it were ever about.

It were siling it down outside summat shocking, and us nithered right through from t'walk round, so we got t'wood in t'oil sharpish and put kettle on. Host had sided t'table already, fettled it lovely, cloth down and everything, bowl o' spice out an' all, tha knows, like his mam used to put out, and breadcakes going spare from us tea so nob'dy went short. Kettle going and four on us crowded round meant it were fair mafted in there inside ten minutes, but nob'dy were moving for owt.

First few hands went by ordinary as owt. Nobody up nor down much, hand here, hand there, going round t'table, no bother, just t'usual chelp between us, nowt in it really. I had meself a champion hand early on, took a couple running, thought I were laughing, but tha knows that never lasts.

Then it come round to t'host dealing and summat changed. Took that hand. Took t'next an' all. Then t'one after. Then a fourth, four hands running, and by 'eck it were starting to look less like luck were carrying it and more like summat else were doing the carrying for it.

The workmate: That shuffle looked reet clean, that.

Just said it quiet like, half a joke really, and it got brushed off straight off.

The host: Get on wi' thee, it's cards, that's all it is.

And we let it go, said nowt more about it while it happened again, which didn't take long neither. Nobody wanted to be gormless about it over coppers, it'll be reet, we thought, carry on.

Except it come round to t'host dealing again after, and this time summat about how they were coming off t'bottom of t'deck caught t'lad next door's eye proper. He were watching close, right, and this time he didn't keep it to missen.

The lad next door: Tha's dealing off the bottom. That's what tha's doing, dealt straight off t'bottom there, I saw it wi' me own eyes.

Said it right out, love, named him straight, no messing about it at all.

The host: I am not. Take that back, that's a lie that is.

Flat as owt, no give in it. And that were it then, table split right down t'middle over it. Workmate sided wi' t'lad, reckoned he'd seen summat funny an' all on that last one. I were sat there not knowing which way to look, didn't fancy calling nob'dy a liar to their face, but summat did look off if I'm honest wi' thee.

We went back over t'hands an' all, every one on 'em, arguing t'toss, who'd had what and when, whether that four running were even possible fair, and it settled nowt, love, nowt at all, just went round and round like t'rain outside, everybody getting more narked and no closer to owt.

The host: I'll not sit here and be called that in me own house.

The workmate: Then don't deal off t'bottom and tha'll not get called it.

Proper chelp flying by then, and for a minute I thought t'lad next door were about ready to bray somebody, sat forward wi' his fists on t'table like that, but nob'dy threw owt, thank God, just words, sharp ones mind.

Thing is, love, we kept playing. Daft as that sounds. Nob'dy got up and went home, nob'dy threw t'cards down for good, we just carried on, dealt t'next hand and t'one after that, but it weren't t'same table no more, tha knows what I mean, t'ease had gone right out of it. Every deal after that had somebody watching t'host's hands like a hawk, and him going red about it, chelping back every time somebody so much as looked his way.

Eleven hands in all we played afore we called it, and when we tallied up at t'end, host were up by two over t'rest of us put together, two hands clear, which didn't help owt, as tha can imagine, it just poured salt on it. Nob'dy were roaring over a few bob, mind, it were never about t'money, it were t'shuffle that got everybody's back up and stopped there.

Last hand of t'night, for what it's worth, went to t'lad next door, t'very one who'd called it out in t'first place, took it clean and grand an' all, and there were summat almost funny in that if anybody'd been in a mood to laugh, which nob'dy were by then.

We packed up sensible enough in t'end, said us ow do's and off home, but I'll tell thee summat for nowt, flower, it's never once been settled. Every single time t'four on us sit down to play since, somebody'll say summat about clean shuffles or watching hands close, and off we go again, same argument, same two sides, same nowt decided. It'll be reet, we keep saying to missen, and happen it will be one day, but I'll not hold me breath while then.
