The eldest: Ey up, what's tha done to thi head?
The younger: It's a fade, in't it. Thirty quid, that were.
The eldest: Looks like tha's stuck thi head in a hedge trimmer, love. Proper gormless.
The younger: Tha's one to talk, tha's got a mop like a mafted haystack thissen.
The eldest: Say that again and I'll bray thee.
The younger: Aye, and tha'd still have worse hair.
The eldest: Ha, get kettle on then, tha daft article.
