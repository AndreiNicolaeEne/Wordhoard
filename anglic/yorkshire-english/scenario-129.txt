Right, I'll tell thee how it went, cos it were proper daft looking back on it now.

Start of it were early afternoon, I were out back side t' table pegging washing out, and I clocks this bit o' smoke away over t' hill, miles off, just sat there on t' skyline, thin and grey. Didn't think nowt of it, tha knows, there's allus summat burning somewhere this time o' year. Carried on wi' me business, put t' kettle on, had a brew, watched it out t' window now and again.

Then within the hour wind got up proper, come round hard, and all of a sudden that smoke weren't sat still no more, it were rolling right at us. Started catching in me throat and me eyes were stinging summat rotten, watering like onions.

The neighbour: Ey up, tha wants to think on getting thissen off, look at state o' that sky.

Me: Aye, I know, I know. Just give it a minute.

Weren't a minute though. That were the moment watching turned into leaving.

I went through t' place like a mad thing. Twenty minutes, tops, and I were out, grabbed what mattered, papers, phone, dog, and left rest of it where it were. Didn't even side owt away, just went. Me throat were raw as owt by time I got in t' car, and heat were pressing on me face and arms, proper mafted, like an oven door left open. Looked back once, just t' once, and whole hillside were lit up like town on bonfire night.

Stopped wi' me sister two nights, neither of us knowing owt, just sat waiting on news, saying it'll be reet, it'll be reet, over and over, while summat come through.

Then word come we could go back, and place were standing, untouched, champion. Kettle still where I'd left it.
