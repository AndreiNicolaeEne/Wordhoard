The mother sat them down at the table, proper, close in beside her, before she said a word.

Mother: Right, sit yourselves down here wi' me a minute, both of you. Come on, love, budge up next to your brother.

She waited till they were both looking at her.

Mother: Now then. I've got to tell you summat and it's not easy telling. Your grandad's died. Died in his sleep this morning, and there were no pain in it, tha knows.

The eldest: But what does that actually mean though, dying? Like really mean. Where's he gone?

Mother: It means his body's stopped, love, stopped for good, it can't work no more. He's not coming back and he's not poorly, he's just gone from us, proper gone. It's the hardest thing there is and I'll not pretend it's owt else. It's alright not to understand it proper. I don't understand it proper missen half the time, if I'm honest wi' thee.

The youngest: Is he cold?

Mother: He might be, love, aye. But he's not feeling it. He's not feeling nowt now, not cold, not sad, none of it. That bit's done wi' for him.

She held her arms out then and pulled them both in tight against her, the youngest right up under her chin.

Mother: Come here, flower. Come on, both of you, get in here.

The youngest's voice went right small then, right down into her jumper.

The youngest: Will we see him again though?

Mother: Not in this life, love, no. I'll not tell thee different, there's no sense in that. But you'll carry him wi' you, in how you remember him, and that's not nowt, that's a real thing, that. He's not just vanished off the earth. He's in you, and in me, and in your nana.

She held on a minute longer before she let them go.

Mother: Right. Over next few days there'll be folk coming round, and we'll be sorting a funeral, so it'll be a bit different here, a bit busier than usual, and that's normal, that's just what happens. If you want to roar you roar, no shame in that, and if you want to go and laik same as any other day, that's alright an' all. It'll be reet. Go on now, get yourselves sorted for your tea.
