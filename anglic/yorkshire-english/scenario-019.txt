My friend: Ey up. Now then, I'll not beat about it, I heard thee and thi partner going at it summat rotten through that wall Thursday. Proper shouting. Tha has to tell us what all that were about.

Me: Aye, go on then, seeing as tha's asked. It'll be all round t'street otherwise, might as well hear it right.

My friend: So what were it even about? Weren't owt daft, were it.

Me: It were daft an all, that's the thing. It started with us tea. Partner said they'd be back for half six, I'd got it all on, done for half six on the dot, and then nowt. No text, no ring, nowt. Half seven, tea's dried up in t'oven, I've took it out twice thinking they're just round corner. Gone eight they walk in like nowt's up, coat still on, chelping away about work like it's any other night.

My friend: And that set thee off.

Me: It did, I'll not lie. I said summat like, "tea's been sat there an hour and a half, could tha not have text us," and I said it sharpish, I know I did, I raised us voice first, no use pretending different. And they come back at me just as loud, summat like "I've had a day of it and all, I don't need this the minute I walk through door." So then it's off, both of us going, neither listening to t'other, just talking over top.

My friend: What were said, then, that tha shouldn't have?

Me: There were one bit, right in middle of it, I said "you'd rather be anywhere but here, wouldn't you", proper daft thing to come out with, I never meant it, not for one second, but it just went straight in there like a knife and I couldn't take it back once it were out. And then, because it were getting nasty by then, it come up, the thing we don't usually touch, they brought up us losing us job back in spring. Said summat like "maybe if you had summat to do all day you wouldn't be stood here counting minutes till I'm home." That's not summat that gets said normal, that's left well alone usually, and it landed exactly where it were meant to.

My friend: Bloody hell.

Me: I know. And I were that wound up by then I've took mug off side and banged it down proper hard, cracked handle clean off it, and then I've gone through to kitchen door and pulled it to behind us so hard t'glass rattled in it, whole frame shook. Weren't meant for owt but somewhere for it all to go.

My friend: And then what.

Me: Then nowt. That were it. I've stopped answering. They asked us summat, I don't even remember what, and I just didn't say owt back, went straight past them upstairs. And that were us for rest of night and best part of next day and all. Them on t'settee, me up in bedroom. Passed on t'landing once or twice, neither of us said a word, just carried on like t'other weren't there. Proper cold. Whole house felt it, tha knows how a house goes when nobody's talking in it.

My friend: How long were that for, all told?

Me: Better part of a day. Night through and all next morning, into afternoon. I laid up there thinking summat rotten, going over it all, then just laiking on us phone doing nowt useful with it, just to have summat to look at that weren't t'ceiling.

My friend: So who give in first, then.

Me: They did. Knocked on t'door, soft like, not proper knocking, just tapping, and come in with two mugs, one for each of us, and just said, plain as owt, "I've put kettle on if tha wants one, and I'm sorry an all, sat here stewing's doing nobody any good." That were it, that were t'opening.

My friend: What did they say sorry for exactly, then, not just a sorry for sorry's sake.

Me: No, they were straight with it. Said "I shouldn't have brought job up, that were out of order, I know what that knocked out of thee and I used it against thee on purpose because I were narked, and that's not fair, and I'm sorry for it." Proper said it like that, not mumbled, looked right at us saying it.

My friend: And tha said summat back.

Me: Course. Said I were sorry for what I'd said an all, that "you'd rather be anywhere but here" bit, said I never meant it, not one word of it, said it just come out because I were that wound up about tea going cold and being sat waiting not knowing where they were, and none of that were their fault really, they'd just been held up, that's all it were in t'end. And I said sorry for door and mug an all, said it were a daft thing to do, taking it out on summat that never did owt to me.

My friend: And that squared it?

Me: Not all at once, but it started it off right. They called us flower, then. First time in ages. Used to call us that all t'time when we first got courting, hadn't said it in months, tha knows how a word like that just stops getting used and neither of you notices till it comes back. Soon as they said it I knew it were mending properly, not just papering over.

My friend: What sealed it, then.

Me: They went out, no more said, and come back twenty minutes on with a breadcake and chips off corner shop, my favourite, still hot, handed it us without a word, just put it down side of us. That were it really. Sat there eating chips out of paper between us like nowt had happened, only better, because we both knew summat had and we'd got past it proper, not just let it drop.

My friend: So it's all sound now, then, between thee two.

Me: Champion, aye. Better than it were before if owt. Only thing is now every time we've a proper laugh together I think, well, friend heard t'lot of that and all, didn't they, through that wall, may as well have had thee sat on us settee with us. I says to partner now, when we're having a laugh, "keep it down, tha knows who's listening through there," and we both go on about it, it's a right joke between us now. So aye, tha got thi story. Whole thing, warts and all. It'll be reet from here, I reckon.
