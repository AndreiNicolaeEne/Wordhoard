Neighbor: Ey up. Tha hear that racket outside last night? Summat going off in t'street, well after midnight.
Me: Aye, I did an' all. Thought it were an animal knocking summat over, but I weren't convinced, sithee.
Neighbor: Could've been owt, foxes, or some offcumden stumbling home. Weren't near either house though, were it.
Me: Nay, right out in t'street. If it goes again tonight, tha knows, give us a shout and I'll keep an ear out.
Neighbor: Aye, we will. It'll be reet.
