I had t'house to missen for a week, minding it while they were off somewhere hot, and I were meant to just water t'plants, feed t'cat, and not touch owt I didn't need to. Simple as. That's what I thought, any road.

First night were already wrong and I couldn't tell thee why. Nowt broken, nowt missing, just t'house didn't sit right round me. Every creak had me sat up in bed listening. I put t'wood in t'oil twice over before I'd settle, and even then I were laid there thinking, well, it'll be reet, it's only a house, tha knows. Didn't sleep proper till gone two.

Second day, and I'm letting t'cat out back same as they'd told me, and it's through t'gap in t'fence and gone before I've so much as shut t'door behind me. I'm stood there in me socks shouting ey up, ey up, puss puss, like a right gormless article, and it's nowhere. Took best part of an hour, up and down t'street, round back of next door's shed, before I got it out from under somebody's car, filthy and looking dead pleased with itself. I carried it home like a sack o' spuds, saying nowt to nobody, and fettled t'fence best I could with a bit of trellis so it couldn't happen again.

Garden were fine to start, then bit by bit through t'week I noticed summat weren't right with it. Tomatoes going a funny colour, hanging basket by t'back door drooping summat rotten. I were watering same as they said but it clearly weren't enough, or were too much, I couldn't tell thee which. By Thursday half of it looked mafted to death and I'm out there morning and night with t'watering can like it'll make up for four days of not noticing.

Then t'washer goes. Halfway through a cycle, this Wednesday it were, and it starts making a noise like it's trying to take off, then stops dead with water still in it and a full load of somebody else's washing sat there going nowhere. I had a look under, tha knows, poked about, found nowt I could fix and didn't dare force owt in case I made it worse, so I just left it shut and hoped. Bit of me wanted to bray it.

Visitor turned up Thursday an' all, no warning whatsoever, knock at t'door and it's her sister off t'estate, come to drop summat round for them not knowing they were away.

The sister: Now then, love, they in?

Me: Ey up. No, they're off, I'm just minding t'place for 'em.

The sister: Oh, didn't know that. I'll leave it wi' you then.

Left a bag of spice on t'side for the kids and were gone again in five minutes, quick as she came. Offcumden or not, she were no bother, but it put me right on edge for an hour after, wondering who else might just turn up.

Friday I were near enough roaring, not gunna lie. I couldn't find t'spare key they'd left me for t'shed, looked everywhere, turned t'kitchen upside down, were dead sure I'd lost it or worse, put it somewhere daft and it'd gone through a gap in t'floor never to be seen again. Tore t'place apart looking, heart going like nobody's business, thinking how am I meant to tell them I've lost their key. Found it in t'end inside a wellington boot by t'back door where I must've dropped it missen. Sat down on t'step after and just breathed for a minute.

Same day, would you believe, I locked missen out. Popped out to t'bin wi'out me key, door swung to behind me in t'wind, and that were that. Stood there in t'garden like a right lemon for the best part of three hour, freezing, absolutely nithered, while a neighbour let me sit in her porch and have a brew. Had to ring round half t'street before somebody turned up who could get us back in.

Rain got in an' all. I'd left t'bedroom window on t'latch one afternoon, thought nowt of it, and that night it were siling it down summat shocking. Woke up to a right puddle on t'carpet and t'curtain soaked through. Mopped it up as best I could at gone midnight, towels down, window shut, thinking please let this dry out before they're home.

Middle of t'night an' all, some racket downstairs had me up wi' me heart in me mouth, creeping down in t'dark not knowing what I'd find. Turned out to be nowt but t'cat, having a laik with summat it'd knocked off t'side. Stood there in t'hallway at three in t'morning feeling a right fool, but you don't know till you've looked, do you.

By t'Saturday, day before last, I'd had enough of that washer sulking and had another proper go at it, and this time I found t'filter thing right at t'bottom, absolutely stuffed, and once that were sorted it ran champion, no bother at all. Should've looked there from t'start, tha knows, but there it is.

Last day were all hands to it, side t'table, fettle t'garden as best I could, hoover through, get every last towel washed and dried and back on t'rail like nowt had happened. Cat fed, brushed, looking respectable. Bit of a scramble, not gunna lie, running round like a headless thing trying to make a week's worth of mess disappear in an afternoon.

When they got back I told 'em straight, more or less.

Me: It were grand on t'whole. Cat got out on t'second day but I got it back sharpish. Washer played up summat awful but it's sorted now. Had a bit of a moment over your spare key an' all, thought I'd lost it, but it turned up.

The owner: Ey up, sounds like you've earned a cuppa, love.

Didn't mention every last bit, mind, no need frightening 'em. Put kettle on and sat down, and I thought, aye, well, it'll be reet, it always is in t'end.
