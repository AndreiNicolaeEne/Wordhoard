Ey up, love, tha's gone off us for good, old dog, and t'house sat quiet as nowt without thee. I can see thee yet, laikin' down t'ginnel, tail going champion, chelpin' at every soul as passed. Ey up, come back, love, ey up, come back. It nithered me of a morning waiting on thee, and mafted us both silly of a summer teatime. Ey up, come back, love. It'll be reet, tha knows. Sleep easy now, old lad.
