Now then. Sithee, sit yourselves down a minute, all of you, because I'm going to tell it you straight, the way it wants telling, and you should have it same as I had it, no bits missed out.

First thing she said were nowt to do with owt serious at all. She looked past me out t'window and said, look at yon lad of yours, laiking out in t'yard in this, siling down an' all, he'll catch his death, that lad. Just that. Plain as anything, like any Tuesday.

Then she come out with it proper. She said, tha knows that gutter round back's still hanging off, get our lad's husband to see to it before it goes altogether and takes half t'roof with it. Said clear as owt. No mistaking what she meant by it.

Then she turned her head to me and said, come here, flower. Same as she'd called me since I were nowt but a bairn, and she said it just the once more, same as ever.

Then she asked me summat I weren't expecting at all. She asked, have I been a good mam to thee. And I told her honest, holding her hand right there, I said, aye, tha has, best there is, and I meant every word, no messing.

Last whole thing she said were, it'll be reet, love, don't thee fret thissen over me. I've held that whole ever since, word for word, and I always will.

After that it were just quiet. No more talking. Just me holding her hand, hers going that bit colder in mine, sat with her till she went, easy as owt, like drifting off of an evening.

That's it. That's all of it. Champion woman, she were. Wanted you to have it same as I had it.
