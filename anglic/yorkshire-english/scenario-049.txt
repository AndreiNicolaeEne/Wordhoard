Me: Now then. Tha knows I don't like askin' but it's been gettin' on three month now, that eighty quid, love, off before Christmas.

My mate: Eighty? I thought it were more like sixty...

Me: It were eighty, I've still got it wrote down on t'back of an envelope. Sat there since afore Christmas an' all.

My mate: Aye, well, it's been mad busy, work an' that, tha knows how it is.

Me: I do know how it is, but this is third time tha's said summat like that. Tha said tha'd have it for me end o' March, remember, we were sat in t'pub an' tha swore blind.

My mate: I know what I said.

Me: Well it's August now an' nowt's changed.

My mate: Don't go on at us. I've got enough on wi'out thee chelpin' on about eighty quid every time we're out.

Me: I'm not chelpin', I'm askin' for what's mine, that's all it is. It's not nowt to me either.

My mate: Tha's actin' like I've robbed thee.

Me: I'm not actin' like owt. I just want it sortin', that's all, sithee.

My mate: Fine. Here. I've thirty on us now, tha can have that off us today, an' I'll get thee rest by end o' t'week. Promise.

Me: End o' t'week. Aye, well. We'll see if it's like last time, or if tha means it this time.

My mate: It'll be reet.

Me: It'd better be, love, cause I'm not askin' a fourth time.
