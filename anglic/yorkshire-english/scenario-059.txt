Light went off right after dusk, no warning at all, telly on one minute and then everything black as t'inside of a boot.

The sister: Ey up. Summat's gone with the leccy.

Looked out the window and the whole street were dark and all, so it were the leccy off proper, not owt we'd done ourselves.

Our dad were straight into the drawer where all t'odds and ends live, batteries dead three year since, a ball of string, and one candle stub left from Christmas, no bigger than my thumb. Mam went hunting for matches. Our lass were shouting down the stairs for the torch off her dressing table, only that had gone and died an' all, typical, nowt in this house ever works when tha needs it.

So it were one stub of a candle between the five of us, and not much of one at that. Dad stood it on t'sideboard and we had to gather round it like it were summat holy. Our lad went straight into the arm of the settee going for his coat, then did it again not five minute after, same leg, same spot, roaring the second time proper.

The father: Well tha's gone and done it twice now, tha gormless article. Do it a third time and I'll bray thee. Sit thissen down and stop laiking about in t'dark.

We had us tea by candlelight, breadcake and whatever were cold in t'fridge, seeing as owt hot were off the cards with an electric cooker. Nobody bothered trying to fettle it that night, there were no mending a cut from t'front room, so we gave over and just sat round the one flame, talking low. A neighbour came knocking to see were we all reet.

The neighbour: Now then, tha's an' all in t'dark then, love?

We said aye, sithee, whole street's the same. Played cards we could barely see the pips on, more chelp between us than sense, our lass moaning there were no spice left for owt to nibble on.

Light never did come back before we turned in. Up to bed by candle and phone-torch, the lot of us nithered because the heating had gone off with it too. Left the stub burning down to nowt on the landing. Woke up to find it all back on, kettle going, like nowt had happened at all.
