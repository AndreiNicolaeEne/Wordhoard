Daughter: Ey up, ey up, it's come, it's here!
Mother: Nay! Give us a chance, I've not even got t'door shut. What's come?
Daughter: Babby, that's what, lad, born this morning just gone six.

Mother: Well that's number three now int' family, tha knows.
Daughter: It is an' all. Come on, get thi coat, we're off up there now, not putting it off till later.
