The counter: Reet, get thissen in circle. One potato, two potato, three potato, four, five potato, six potato, out tha goes.

The one caught: Nah then, that were too quick, tha skipped our lass, do it ower!

Another: Tha knows chelpin means startin again fra t'top, love.

The counter: Reet then. (louder) One potato, two potato, three potato, four, five potato, six potato, OUT THA GOES!

The lad: Ha! It's thee that's out, missen's safe.
