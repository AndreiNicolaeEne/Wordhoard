Now then, love. Sithee, tha's nobbut just got here so I'll tell thee this once and then it's up to thee what tha does wi' it.

There's a fella knocks about round here, friendly as owt, allus got a deal on the go. Ey up, ow do, pal this, love that, calls thee flower before he's even got thy name off thee. He'll tell thee he's got summat going, work, a van going cheap, tickets, a flat to let, whatever's going spare that week, and it's dead cheap, too good to be stood there askin questions about. That's the whole trick, tha knows: if it sounds too champion to be true, it is.

He'll want a bit up front, just to hold thy place like, nowt much, twenty quid, happen fifty. Tha hands it over and that's the last tha sees of him or thy brass. Gone. Vanished like he were never here at all.

There were a lass down our street, new like thissen, gave him money for a flat that were never his to let in t'first place. Cost her the best part of a month's wages and she were roaring about it for days after. I'm not saying it to chelp about her, just so tha knows it can catch owt anyone, not just the daft ones.

Signs to watch for: he's too friendly too fast, he's allus got summat on the go, he wants cash and he wants it now, and he'll not give thee an address nor owt tha can check up on.

If one of them comes chelping at thee, tell him tha'll think on it, then get thissen off sharpish. Don't hand nowt to no one new asking for money up front, not while tha's asked at t'corner shop first, the one who's been stood fettling that counter twenty year. He'll put thee reet.
