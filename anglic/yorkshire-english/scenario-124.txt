I were later leaving than I meant to be, on t'phone to me mam about nowt in particular, and by time I got out t'door it were properly dark, darker than I like it when I'm walking back on me own. Weren't two minutes down t'road when I got that feeling, tha knows the one, like someone's right behind you, footsteps landing same time as mine, near enough.

Didn't turn round outright, didn't want to look daft stood there gawping at nowt. Just slowed up a bit, like I were checking me phone, and the steps behind slowed an' all. So I picked up again, quicker, casual as owt, and they picked up an' all, same beat, same distance back. That were enough for me.

Usual way I'd cut down the ginnel by the allotments, no lights down there, dead quiet, but I weren't having that, not tonight. Went the long way round instead, past the chippy and the bus stop where there's always somebody stood, better lit, more folk about even at that hour. Told missen it were nowt, just somebody walking same way, coincidence, but I weren't taking the chance.

One glance back, at the corner by the shop, quick as owt, and there were just a shape, could've been anybody, offcumden or neighbour, couldn't tell you, told me nowt either way. Last bit up our street I fair legged it, wouldn't admit that to a soul at the time mind, walking fast like I weren't bothered while me heart were going ten to the dozen. Got the door shut behind me and stood there a minute, proper relief, that were.

Never did find out what it were. Might've been nowt at all. I tell folk now and go right red telling it, laughing at missen, how sure I were that summat were coming up behind me.
