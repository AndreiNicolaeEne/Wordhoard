It were siling it down when she come round, dripping wet through, banging on t'door like summat were on fire. Put t'wood in t'oil quick before owt got in, and there she stood on t'mat, coat dripping, grinning like a gormless article.

My friend: Ey up, ey up, tha'll never guess what's happened to me!

Me: Ow do. Come in out of that, you're soaked through. What's got into thee?

My friend: I've gone and won some money, haven't I! A right sum, an' all.

Me: Bloody hell, that's brilliant that, tha! Champion news, that, absolute champion!

My friend: I know! I still can't believe it missen, tha knows.

Me: Go on then, don't leave us hanging. How much we talking?

My friend: Four grand, love. Straight in me account. Four grand, just like that.

Me: Four grand. Bloody hell. So how'd tha come by that then, exactly?

My friend: Ah, scratch card off the wife, would you credit it. Mad, isn't it. I mean it's not like ours, is it, everything held together wi' sellotape and hope.

Me: Aye. Some of us are still counting out for a breadcake and a bag o' chips, sithee.

My friend: Tha alright? Summat up wi' thee, tha's gone right quiet all of a sudden.

Me: No, no. I'm reet.

My friend: Tha's not reet, I can tell. What is it, then?

Me: Alright, I'll be honest wi' thee, it's nowt tha's done wrong, none of it. I'm just proper jealous, if I'm honest. Green as owt, that's all it is. No more to it than that.

My friend: Aye. I can see that. I'd be same, other road round, tha knows.

Me: I am made up for thee though. Really am. It'll be reet, this'll set thee up champion, that will. Proper made up, that.
