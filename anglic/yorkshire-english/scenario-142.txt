Woke up this morning and it hit us straight off - today were t'day. Told our mam back i' t'spring, after her going on and on about it, "I'll sort your garage out for thee, one full day, I swear it, now leave us be." And now here we are, and I could've roared.

Lay there a good ten minute just staring at t'ceiling thinking what a gormless thing that were to swear to. Nobody twists your arm to say tha'll do owt, tha does it thissen, and then tha's stuck wi' it whether tha likes it or not.

Got up mafted already just thinking on it, dragged missen out of bed, no breakfast bar a breadcake wolfed down going out t'door, muttering the whole way down the road about ever opening me gob in the first place. Sky were siling it down an' all, grand and miserable for it.

Mam were stood at the door.
Mother: Ey up, love, tha's here then. Champion. Kettle's on.
Me: Aye, well, I said I would, didn't I.

Started on t'garage about nine. Boxes going back to when I were a lad, tha knows, junk on junk on junk, and every hour summat fresh to grumble on - the dust, the cold, nithered in there something rotten wi' the door open all morning, me own back playing up something shocking from all the lifting.

The eldest turned up dinnertime to help shift the big stuff.
The eldest: Sithee, you've hardly made a dent in it.
Me: Don't chelp at us, I've been at it since nine, thank you very much.

Got to about three and I were that done in I nearly said forget it, leave the rest for another day, it'll still be there. Then I thought, now then, tha swore it, that's that, no arguing wi' thissen over it. Put t'wood in t'oil on the thought and just got on wi' it.

Worst of it were the boxes right at back, soaked through, heavier than owt in the place. Ground through it though, no stopping, no sitting down for it neither.

By seven it were done, proper done, garage cleared top to bottom, not a thing left in it.
Mother: By heck, tha's done it. Grand job, that, love.
Me: Told thee I would, didn't I. Now where's us tea.

Complained the whole day, start to finish. Never once walked off it though. Said I would, and I did.
