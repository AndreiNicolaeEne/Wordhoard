Whole tray of breadcakes were ruined, oven left on all night, and t'wholesale order for the school gone with it. Gaffer come through from t'back still in his coat, and everyone stood round by the counter went quiet, the way they do when they knows summat's coming.

Gaffer: Now then. It were thee left t'oven on, weren't it. Only one in first that morning were thee.

The one blamed: Weren't me.

Gaffer: Aye it were. Tha's the only one had a key that early, don't tell me it weren't thee.

The one blamed: I swear on my life I'd nowt to do with it.

Nowt more said after that. She didn't say it twice, didn't raise her voice to match his neither, just stood there with her face give away nowt, not a flicker, though he were fair bawling by then, wanting her to crack or cry or summat. Two of the lads who'd swapped the late shift between 'em stood by the fridges the whole time, saying nowt, looking at t'floor like it were the most interesting floor in Leeds.

Gaffer: Well summat's not adding up, and I'll get to bottom of it.

The one blamed: Aye. You do that.

He'd nowt else to say to that, so he went back through to t'back still muttering, and none of t'others said owt neither, not one word to put it right. She fettled the counter, side t'trays that were left, and walked off out the front like it were any other morning, denial just sat there where she'd left it, nobody having moved it an inch.
