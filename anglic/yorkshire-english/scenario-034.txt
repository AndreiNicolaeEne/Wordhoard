I'm dahn there three, four times a week, dinnertime mostly, and by t'time I've got to front o' t'queue he's already turnt round wi' t'tongs in his hand.

The owner: Now then. Breadcake, bacon, brown sauce, no beetroot, is it?

Me: Aye go on then, but chuck an egg on an all today, I'm mafted from walking and want summat proper in me.

The owner: Egg an all? Champion. Sithee, tha's spoiling thissen.

Me: Have to, don't I, weather like this taking it out on thee an all.

The owner: Tell me about it, love. I were siling wet through setting up this morning, brolly turnt inside out afore I'd even got t'griddle on.

Me: How's trade been then, otherwise?

The owner: Mustn't grumble, lad. Steadier than summer, tell thee true, but them offcumdens from t'new offices don't know what a breadcake is, keep asking me for a roll. I says tha wants a breadcake, flower, this is Leeds, not dahn south.

Me: Gormless, some on 'em.

The owner: Ey up, don't be starting or I'll bray thee wi' this spatula. How's thi mother keeping, any road?

Me: Champion, ta for asking. Roaring at t'football Saturday mind, they lost again, nowt new there.

The owner: Aye well. Reet, there tha goes, get that dahn thee while it's hot, don't let it go cold nattering to me.

Me: Ta very much. What's the damage?

The owner: Three eighty, love, and put t'wood in t'oil on thi way out, it's parky out there today.

Me: Will do. See thee Thursday then, same as ever.

The owner: Aye, tha knows where I am. Sithee, lad, mind how tha goes.
