The eldest: Sithee, tha'll wed reet and t'bairns'll come champion, house full o' laughter while tha's grey and past caring, that's sworn on mi own life, not wished. Now then, mark this an' all: cross me and tha'll wander nithered and empty-handed, nowt but bad luck laiking round thy door while tha's nowt left to lose, that's sworn same as t'first. One word said, both stick. Tha knows which tha's earned.
