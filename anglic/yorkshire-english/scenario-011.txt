The grandmother: Ey up, don't put them shoes on t'table, tha knows!
Me: Why not, they're only shoes.
The grandmother: Do that and someone in this house dies, sure as owt, sithee.
Me: (half-laughing) Nowt happens, it's just talk that.
The grandmother: It's true whether tha believes it or not, love.
Me: So what fettles it then?
The grandmother: Off sharp, three knocks on wood, and it'll be reet.
Me: (rolls eyes, knocks three times anyway) There. Champion, satisfied?
