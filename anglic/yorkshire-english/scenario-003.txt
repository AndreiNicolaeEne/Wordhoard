The father: Ey up, sithee, I've been thinking on it while thy were feeding her. What about summat like Nevaeh, tha knows, different like.

The mother: Nevaeh? Gerraway. That's heaven spelt backwards and wrong un at that, love. I'm not calling us daughter after a word tha can't even say straight off. Sounds like summat off telly, not a name for a bairn round here.

The father: Reight, fine. What's thy bright idea then.

The mother: I were thinking Amber. It's nice, it's normal, nobody'll ask thee to spell it twice.

The father: Amber? There's an Amber works on t'till at t'Morrisons, gob on her like a fog horn, chelping on all shift. I'm not having folk thinking on her every time somebody says us lass's name.

The mother: That's gormless, that is. One Amber dun't mean owt.

The father: Well what about thy mam's name then, eh. Show a bit of respect, side wi' family for once instead of chelping at every name I come up wi'.

The mother: We've two already called that, tha soft aporth, thy sister's lass and t'woman two doors down. Third one'll be nowt but confusion at Christmas, whole table shouting same name at once.

The father: We can't just sit here and wait while she's christened wi' nowt down on paper. Somebody's got to have final say on it.

The mother: Oh aye, and who's that meant to be? Thee?

The father: I don't know, do I. I'm knackered, I'm nithered an' all, and babby's due a feed again.

The mother: It'll be reet. Just not tonight it won't. Put wood in't oil, I'm off up.
