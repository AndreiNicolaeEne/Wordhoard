Weeks it'd been dry, then just after dinner the first drops come.

The eldest: Ey up, is that rain?

The mother: It is an' all, get t'door open!

They piled outside, the youngest with her arms flung out to it.

The youngest: I'm havin' every drop, me!

The father: Tha's gormless, tha'll catch thi death.

The mother: Nay, let her be, it were needed summat rotten.

They stood there, nowt said, just listening to it siling down.
