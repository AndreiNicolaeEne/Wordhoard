The tenant: Ey up. Tha got five minutes, or is tha mafted wi' all this cleanin'?

The friend: Now then. Come in, love, I'm only side-in-t'-table. What's up wi' thee, tha looks like tha's carryin' t'weight o' t'world.

The tenant: It's t'roof again. Corner ovver t'back bedroom, above where t'wardrobe is. Every time it siles down proper I've a bowl under it and it's still not enough, water's got into t'ceilin' now, gone all soft and brown.

The friend: How long's this been on, then, exactly? None o' this "ages" chelp, I want proper time.

The tenant: Since afore Christmas, so what, seven month now. First time I told him he said he'd get a fella round "next week." Nowt happened. Asked again in't spring, same answer, word for word near enough, "next week, love, I've got a fella." No fella's ever showed his face.

The friend: Sounds like our old landlord, God rest whatever's left of his patience wi' us. He did t'very same ower t'kitchen window one winter, all draughty and rotten, and it were "next week" for the best part of a year while we were nithered every night wi' t'wind comin' through. In t'end I wrote it down, everything, dates and what were said, and sent it him proper, and stuck a copy up wi' t'council an' all.

The tenant: So what dost tha reckon I should do. I could just keep chelpin' at him on t'phone, but that's got me nowhere so far, has it. Or I ring t'council and let them have a look, only then he might turn nasty about it, take against me somehow.

The friend: Ringin' council's t'safest road, tha's covered then, but tha's right it can rile him. Just leavin' it be, it'll not get reet on its own, that's certain, roof'll not fettle itsen.

The tenant: Nay, it won't.

The friend: Right then. Do this first, get it all down in writin', dates and all, same as I did, and send it him that way so he can't say he never knew. Give him a fortnight. If nowt's changed, straight to t'council after, no more chelpin' back and forth on t'phone.

The tenant: Aye. That'll do. Champion, that. Ta, love.

The friend: It'll be reet. Put kettle on afore tha goes.
