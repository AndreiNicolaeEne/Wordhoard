Reet, I'll tell thee about this one. Had to get a paper stamped down at t'office, form for me mother's bit of land it were, and it had to be done that day else we'd have missed us slot and had to start the whole palaver again from nowt. So I'm there dead early, papers all in order, folder fettled up nice, and up I go to the counter.

The one at the counter: Now then. This is all in order, aye. Only these things do take a while to process, tha knows. Might not get done today, this one.

Me: Oh aye?

I just left that hanging in't air. Said nowt, looked at him proper gormless, like I hadn't a clue what he were on about. He tried again, bit plainer this time.

The one at the counter: Some folk bring summat for the lads, like, for a brew. Gets it moved along a lot quicker, that.

Me: Nay, I've nowt on me, love. I'll just wait while it's ready then, no bother.

Told him an' all I knew someone up there, one of me mother's old workmates fettles the filing on that floor, and I'd just pop back Thursday when she were in. He went quiet at that. Proper quiet. Tha could've sliced that pause wi' a knife, could've gone either road. Then he just stamped it there and then, no more said, told me it'd gone through champion after all.

Never cost me a penny, that. Just playing dumb and stood me ground, and a name dropped in at the right minute. Folk like that only get on if tha lets 'em, sithee. Say nowt back, don't roar about it, don't chelp, just wait em out. It'll be reet if tha's got the patience for it, and I had.
