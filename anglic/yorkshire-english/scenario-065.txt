The neighbour: Ey up, ey up! Your goat's out, gone down past t'ginnel, ower t'field, headed straight for t'road!

The other: Oh no, not now -

The neighbour: Nay time, love, get thissen down t'lane, head it off afore it hits t'road. I'll nip back and block t'gap it got out on.

The other: Reet, tha go left, I'll cut round back, go on, go, quick!
