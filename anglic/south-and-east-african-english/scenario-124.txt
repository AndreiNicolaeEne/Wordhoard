I was busy closing up at the shop and by the time I locked and set out it was already darker than I like, no moon that night, just the one bulb at the corner doing its best. Not far past the stage I got the feeling, someone behind me, footsteps landing almost the same time as mine, like an echo that is not quite an echo. I didn't turn, I'm not mad. I just slowed my pace small small, pretending to check something in my bag, and the steps behind slowed too, is it. Then I picked up again, quick quick, and hey, the steps picked up as well, same as before, matching me both times.

That is when I decided, no, I am not passing by the swamp path tonight, the usual short way, dark dark with nobody to see you if something happens. I turned instead for the main road where the rolex guy was still frying, the boda boys still parked waiting for a fare, people still moving up and down. Safer there, even if it is longer.

I allowed myself one look back, just one, and there was a person there, true, but walking normal, and I could not tell you if it was somebody following or just somebody also going home, same as me. That glance told me nothing certain, nothing at all.

The last stretch to the gate I was moving faster than I would ever admit to now, and when I got the door shut behind me and the bolt across, the relief was something else, hey.

Me: I'm just tired, that's all.
The mother: Is it the thieves you saw, is it?
Me: No, no, nothing like that.

I never found out if it was a person meaning me harm or just an ordinary person walking my direction and my own fear doing the rest. Even now, telling it, I laugh small, shame, at how sure I was that night, how certain, and how in the end there was nothing to show for it, or maybe there was and I will never know. Now-now, that swamp path, I still don't use it.
