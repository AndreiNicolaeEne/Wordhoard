Well done, my sister, sit, let me tell you the whole thing before it reaches you somehow twisted.

That rent money for this month, the one folded inside the tin, that is what caused all this. The wife took the whole of it and bought a new gas cylinder for her rolex stand, because the seller told her if she doesn't pay now-now the price jumps by Monday. She never said one word to me about it. I only knew when the landlord was standing at the gate asking for his money and I was there looking foolish, saying let me check and call you back, not sure myself what I was checking.

I'm the one who brought it up first, that evening, when I saw the cylinder shining there in the kitchen and asked her where the rent had gone. She was busy explaining the business needs before I even finished the question.

Me: Where did this month's rent go, because the landlord was just here.
The wife: The business has to eat first before we eat. If that stand closes, what will we even use to pay rent with next month?
Me: Fine, but why can't you tell me? Am I not your husband, are we not together in this thing?
The wife: Who is even talking about telling people things, is it not you who threw your whole sacco payout at your cousin for his "sure" business, and up to now that money is still walking somewhere in this Kampala?

Imagine, she said it standing under my own mother's photo, as if to call a witness on me. That one cut me, hey.

After that we didn't greet. Two full days going on the third morning, the house was just quiet quiet, even the radio felt loud in it. Then that Thursday she put my tea on the table without my asking and called me to come eat, and that is how I knew she was the one softening first. I ate, and I said sorry for shouting, and that finished it.

My verdict, my sister: the money itself was nothing much, it's only the fear of not having for rent that makes both of us somehow. She should have told me plain, simple as that, but me too, I should let that cousin matter rest, it's finished, it's gone, why must it keep coming back to visit us.
