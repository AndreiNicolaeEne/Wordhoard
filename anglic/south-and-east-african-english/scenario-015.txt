Love,

Me I am not going to walk around it, let me just say it plain: I love you, and it has grown too big now to fold small inside me any longer.

You remember that evening, it was not even a big thing, we were just sitting outside your gate, you were busy peeling maize for your mother and you did not even look up, you just said, is it, when I told you the matatu was late again. Something in me turned right there. Not the roses-and-candles kind of turn, just, this person, hey, this is the one. Small small like that, but it did not leave me since.

There is that thing you do that only someone close would know, when you are thinking hard you press your thumb into your palm like you are counting something only you can see. Nobody else has caught it, not even your own sister, but I have, and I love it more than this paper can carry.

My days are not the same since you came. Even work goes somehow easier, the matatu queue feels shorter somehow, I cannot explain how that happens but it does. And when you are not here, the days have become heavier than I let on when we talk on the phone, because on the phone I am always sharp-sharp, fine fine, but the house is too quiet, and even a flash from your side at night is not the same as you sitting there.

Let me tell you the small thing I have never said out loud: sometimes I fear this love is too easy for me, and one day it will ask a price I have not prepared for. I am not telling you this to worry you, I just needed to put it somewhere outside my chest.

But hear this promise, si I am not just talking: whatever comes, now, now-now, or just now, I will not run. Slowly slowly we will build this thing, together, sindiyo?

Yours, always,
mpenzi.
