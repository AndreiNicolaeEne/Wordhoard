Sasa, let me tell you about that harvest, the one everyone still measures the others against. Si from the start of the season we were already seeing it, the rains came exactly when they were needed, not too early to rot the seed, not too late either, and by January the whole shamba was looking different from any year before. Is it, people were asking each other at the market, is it this one is going to be like that. And it was.

By the time we finished bringing in the maize we already knew, this thing had beaten every year we could remember, almost double what we are used to getting in a normal season, and that is not me talking big, that is what the sacks were telling us themselves.

But it's the beans that finished everybody, hey. That year the beans did numbers nobody was expecting, way past the usual, past even the good year from way back, the one we used to bring up before this one came and took the crown for good. Everybody was standing counting sacks and shaking the head, is it, we kept saying, is it this shamba has done this.

We had to call people, extra hands, because if you leave that harvest even a day more in the field it starts turning, and nobody wants to lose what God has given like that. Relatives came, neighbours came, even people from town who don't normally touch a panga came to help, and we were paying them small small from what came in.

After the house was covered for the year, the rest, some we stored properly for the dry months, some we sold off, the price wasn't even somehow that season because everyone had grown well.

The oldest grower: In all my years on this land I have not seen a season give like that one gave. Not since I was a young one myself.

The oldest grower: It's the rain, it came when it was needed, and after that the weather stayed mild the whole way, no long dry spell to punish the crop. That is what did it.

Even now if someone wants to complain about a slow year, another will bring up that harvest, is it as bad as that year, remember that one, and everyone just laughs because they know exactly which year you mean. That one set the mark, sindiyo, and nothing since has come close.
