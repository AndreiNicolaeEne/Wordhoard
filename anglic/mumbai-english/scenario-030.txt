Me: Aunty, that mutton you made last Sunday na, that one only, tell me the full recipe, I want to write it down properly this time.

Aunty: Arre this recipe, my mother's recipe only, she never wrote anything anywhere, everything was in her hand only, in her feel only. Okay okay, do one thing, first you take onion, four medium ones, not too small not too big, chop fine-fine like that only. That is the first thing that goes in the hot oil.

Me: Onion first only? I thought ginger-garlic goes first.

Aunty: No no wait, ginger-garlic paste comes after, small small quantity, two spoon roughly, that goes in after the onion turns brown, proper brown, not white-white. And onion browning, that you cannot rush, that one thing my mother always used to say, don't rush the onion, sit there and stir only, if you rush it the whole curry will taste raw only, no matter what you do after.

Me: Okay okay. Then?

Aunty: Then tomato, two medium ones, arre wait, I'm forgetting, coconut also, fresh coconut, half cup, grind it and add, otherwise the gravy will be thin, no body it will have. Without coconut it is not our curry, my mother used to say, it is somebody else's curry.

Me: How much masala you put, exactly?

Aunty: That I don't measure yaar, by eye only I judge, colour must come like brick, not too dark not too light, that I cannot tell you in spoon-spoon, you have to stand and see and learn, that's it.

Me: And done, how to know it's done, done?

Aunty: No clock, no watch, nothing. You smell it only, when the raw smell goes off and the oil starts floating separate-separate on top, that time the meat is falling off the bone itself. Bas, ready.

Me: Can I use chicken instead of mutton?

Aunty: Chicken, hmm, can do, but time will be much less only, mutton needs long long cooking on slow flame, chicken half the time, taste will be little different, but can do, no harm in that.
