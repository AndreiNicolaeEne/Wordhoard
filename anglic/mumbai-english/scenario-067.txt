Arre come, come, I'll show you all, no need to ask, I'll tell you everything itself.

See this side, from the peepal tree till the blue gate, you can leave your scooter there, no problem, whole street parks there only, even the delivery van stands there sometimes for timepass. But that empty patch in front of the yellow compound, never park there, na. Looks empty, but that is the corner uncle's spot only, his old Santro sits there since fifteen years, engine doesn't even start now but nobody moves it, so you also don't move it, achha.

And see that low wall behind the tulsi plant, that side is the neighbour aunty's ground, no? Nothing is written, no fencing, no board, but nobody crosses that line, not even for the cricket ball, we just stand outside and call, "Aunty, ball andar gaya, please throw," we don't go in ourselves. Old rule, older than me.

Two hours everybody keeps quiet without any sign, two to four, afternoon, that is sleeping time for whole street, no hammering, no loud bhajan, nothing, even the milk bhaiya doesn't ring the bell in that time. And after ten at night, bas, off the TV volume, only low low sound, that we all just know, isn't it.

Passing on the street, elders first only, whoever is older you greet first, don't wait for them, and the watchman always, because he is seeing everything, everybody greets him like family only, then friends, then everybody else, that order is fixed, nobody taught it, we just know it.

Borrowing is simple, do one thing, small small things like sugar or one screwdriver, just go ask, "didi, sugar is there kya," she'll give, no hesitation, but bring it back same day, next day latest. If you don't return, nothing is said, nobody will fight, but next time you ask, she'll say "abhi nahi hai" even if it is there, everybody in the lane will know by evening itself, what to do, this place is like that only.

And this last one, everybody does it sooner or later, even the strictest uncle, come na, I'll tell you: after eleven, when nobody is watching, that same empty spot near the yellow gate, people park there ten minutes only, "just now I'll move it," and nobody says anything, because everybody has done it once.

That's it, bas, now you know the street, haan.
