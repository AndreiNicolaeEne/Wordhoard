Arre yaar, I have been meaning to tell you this since two days only, but I was so senti about it I didn't know where to start. You missed a proper mad one, I'm telling you. The cousin's engagement function, na, it was supposed to be a small small thing, just close family and two-three friends, that's what everybody kept saying, "chalo, just a quiet one," but you know how it goes in our house, nothing stays small.

So the whole evening the aunty kept coming with the plate, "eat na, eat na, you have become so thin," and I kept saying no no I'm full, but she is not the type who listens, she will just keep standing there with the plate until you take one more. So one more piece became one more helping, and that helping became another, and by the time the actual sit-down dinner started I had already eaten like three rounds of starters only. Then the uncle brought out the drinks, and he is the one who feels bad if you have just one, he will keep topping up the glass himself, saying "arre this is a celebration, drink properly na, what is the use otherwise," and everybody is toasting every two minutes for some reason or the other, first for the couple, then for the families, then for no reason at all, just for timepass, and I am like okay fine, one more won't hurt, and that is exactly the thought that gets a person into trouble, no?

Then the music started and honestly that finished me off completely. You know I cannot sit still when there is proper music, and the DJ they had hired was actually mast, ek number selection, old songs mixed with new ones, and everybody pulled everybody onto the floor, the didi from upstairs, the cousin's friends, even the uncles were doing their own version of dancing which was more comedy than dancing but full entertainment. I had told myself, one hour of dancing and then I will slip out quietly, I have to give exam prep tomorrow anyway, but that one hour became who-knows-how-many hours. The last thing I remember clearly is standing near the speaker with a glass in hand, laughing at something the cousin's friend said, something about the groom's family, and after that, yaar, it is all in pieces only. Small flashes here and there, like photos somebody has shuffled out of order, no proper story connecting them.

I woke up the next morning and honestly for the first few minutes I didn't even know what was happening to me. Head was pounding like someone is playing dhol inside it non-stop, no break. Even the small light coming through the curtain was feeling like someone switched on a flood light directly into my eyes, and every little sound, the fridge humming, someone's chappal on the floor, was sounding like it is right next to my ear, too loud, too sharp. Just to sit up on the bed took real real effort, like my whole body had become heavy overnight, arms and legs not listening properly. Stomach was turning at the mere thought of food, forget eating, even the smell of tea from the kitchen was making me feel like running to the bathroom.

And of course the younger brother was up before me, moving about normally like nothing happened, humming some tune, and the moment he saw my face he could not resist.

The brother: Arre look who is up. What happened to you, ghost has entered or what.

Me: Please, not now, my head is bursting.

The brother: Yesterday you were the biggest dancer at the function, now this state. What to do, this is what happens when one only says yes to every round.

Me: Bas yaar, don't start.

The brother: I am not saying anything, just observing only. Drink some water, it is there in the fridge.

He wasn't being nasty about it, just his usual teasing, but in that moment even his normal voice felt too loud, too cheerful, like he was doing it on purpose. I just lay back down and told myself, right there, lying absolutely still, that this is it, never again, I am never letting a night go like this again. Of course I have made this same promise to myself I don't know how many times before, this is probably the fourth or fifth "last time" only, but in that moment I meant it with my whole heart, I'm telling you.

The whole morning, small small things that normally take nothing at all felt like a whole project. Just walking to the bathroom and back felt like I had done one full local train journey standing in the general compartment. Mother had asked me to just go down and get milk from the society shop, one two minute job, and I could not even manage that, I just said, "give me five minutes," and that five minutes became one hour of just lying there staring at the ceiling fan going round and round, which also was making me feel more dizzy somehow.

By afternoon I managed to eat something very plain, just curd rice, nothing rich, nothing with masala, because even the smell of oil was too much for me. I kept mostly quiet, didn't even look at the phone properly, just stayed still on the bed with the curtains closed until the worst of the pounding passed.

And in between all that lying still, pieces of the night kept coming back to me in flashes, and one flash in particular kept coming back and making me cringe properly. I remember, somewhere near the end, saying something to the cousin's friend, something about her own relationship, some comment I thought was funny at the time but now, thinking about it clearly, it was actually quite rude, uncalled for, none of my business at all. I don't even remember her exact reaction, just my own voice saying it, over and over in my head, and every time it comes back I feel this hot embarrassment rising up in my chest. I owe her a proper apology, I know that, I have to message her, I keep telling myself today itself I will do it, but two days have passed already and I still haven't found the courage, what to do.

By evening though, finally, I started feeling like myself again. Ate a proper meal, dal chawal, nothing fancy but real food, and my head had cleared up, I could think straight again, no more heaviness anywhere. It was such a relief, honestly, after a whole day of feeling like a wet towel someone had wrung out.

But sitting here now, writing this to you, there is this regret sitting underneath everything, quiet but definitely there. I know I will apologise, I know I will feel proper bad about it for some more days, and I also know, full well, that some day, some function, some celebration, I will again say yes to one more helping and one more round and one more song, and the whole thing will happen again exactly like this. That's the funny part, no. You make the promise so seriously lying there in the morning with your head splitting, and by the time the next one comes along you have conveniently forgotten how bad it actually felt. Anyway yaar, that's the whole story as far as I can piece it together. Next time you better come, at least then there will be someone to remind me at the right moment to stop.
