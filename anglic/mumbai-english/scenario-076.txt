Me: Come na, sit for two minutes, I want to talk to you seriously.

The friend: Arre what happened now, why that face you are making.

Me: Face I will make only. Since two days I am wanting to say this, yesterday you were doing rash driving on that bike, no helmet, weaving here-there near the signal, and that BEST bus was two inches from your elbow only. The friend: Chalo yaar, nothing happened na, I got down safe only, why you are so senti about it.

Me: Nothing happened is what you told last time also, and that time your wrist got fractured for three months, remember. You couldn't even hold the chai cup, someone had to feed you like small small child. That was luck only, this bindaas attitude will not save you every single time.

The friend: Bas na, you are being too filmy about it.

Me: I am not filmy, I am telling practical thing, do one thing, listen first, achha. Next time it will not be wrist, it will be head on that road, and then what. Who will be left crying, tell me, your mother, your sister, this me only, standing near hospital doing nothing, feeling so helpless, and you will not even be there to see what to do about it.

The friend: (quiet) ... okay okay, don't start now itself, yaar.

Me: I am not starting anything, I am serious only.

The friend: Fine, fine. I'll go slow, promise. Helmet I'll wear, and signal-jumping I'll cut down also, at least on your society road. Happy now?

Me: Not fully happy, but chalo, that is something.

The friend: Ek number drama you do, yaar. Come, let's have cutting chai, my treat only.
