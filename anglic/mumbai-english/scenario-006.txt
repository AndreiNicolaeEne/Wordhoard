The elder: Okay listen, before everyone comes na, quick quick I tell you the rules only.

The younger one: Haan tell, tell.

The elder: See, minimum six players we need to start. Five also can adjust, but six is proper number. Boundary is there, from that gate till the cycle stand on this side, and up to the society wall on that side only. You go outside that, it's not allowed, understand?

The younger one: And if while running I go outside the line, what happens then?

The elder: Simple only, boundary crosses, you are automatically out. Den doesn't even have to touch you, the line itself catches you. So don't be over-smart thinking you'll escape outside, it doesn't work like that here.

The younger one: Achha. And tag is what exactly, any touch?

The elder: No no, not any-how touch. Proper tag means two fingers, on the back or the shoulder, and you say "tag" loudly. Otherwise players will only fight..."I didn't feel it, I didn't feel it." Slow-slow brush doesn't count, arre.

The younger one: Okay. Who becomes den first, though?

The elder: We do ekka-dukka, everybody shows hand front or back together, whoever comes odd one out, that one is den for first round. Fair only, no fixing-fixing.

The younger one: And once den touches me, then?

The elder: Then you freeze. Stand like statue exactly wherever you are, legs open, hands up, don't move, don't talk too much. Only when some other running player comes and taps your hand, then you are loose again, you can run.

The younger one: And if nobody comes to tap me?

The elder: Then bad luck, you stand there only till round finishes. Round finishes when everybody is frozen, den wins that one. Whoever got caught first, that one is den next round, chalo, that way turns keep coming for everybody.

The younger one: And if I do some silly mistake in the first round...

The elder: Relax yaar, first round doesn't count against you, I'm not that strict type. Just run properly, have fun, small small mistakes are fine, no tension. Bas, listen, they're all coming down now, let's go.
