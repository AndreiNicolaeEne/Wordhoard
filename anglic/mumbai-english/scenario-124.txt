That day the meeting got preponed and then it dragged on so late, what to do, by the time I came out it was already properly dark, darker than I like it to be. I was thinking only, let me finish this walk fast fast and reach home.

Not far from the station only, I got the feeling someone is there behind me. Footsteps landing exact same time as mine, na. Didn't want to turn around like some scared item, so I slowed my pace once, casual only, like I am checking my phone, and the steps also slowed down. Then I picked up little bit speed, like I am in hurry for no reason, and the steps also picked up. Same rhythm both times. My heart was doing its own filmy drama by then.

Usual shortcut is through the society compound and behind the shut shops, dark dark lane, not one streetlight working. That night I thought no, better the main road side, where the vada pav stall and the cutting chai wala are still open, autos still standing at the signal, some crowd is there only. Safety in crowd, na.

One time only I looked back, quick quick, at the turn. Couldn't tell anything for sure, some figure, maybe just someone also going home, walking normal pace. Told me nothing, that glance, only made my imagination worse.

Last stretch I will not lie, I was almost running, but who admits that at the time. Reached the building, and that sound of the door shutting behind me, bas, best sound in the whole world that night.

Whether it was actually someone following or just some uncle also going home same route same time, I still don't know till date. Nothing happened, nothing proved, no lafda in the end. Now when I tell this to my friend she is like, arre you were just being senti that night only. And I say, no, the steps were matching too well for coincidence, though half the time even I am not fully sure I wasn't imagining the whole thing.
