The eldest: Come na, listen, this blessing I am swearing, not wishing only: promotion will come itself, house will be full of laughing, mark my words, it is decided.

But same mouth curses also, turn proud, forget this old one, and this too is sworn: peace will not be there for you, only lafda after lafda, mark my words, it is decided.

Whichever you earn, that only comes, this mouth never lies twice.
