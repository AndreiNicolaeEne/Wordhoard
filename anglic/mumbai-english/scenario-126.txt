Me: Yaar, something happened to me last night, near our society gate itself, I'm still not over it.

My friend: You must've been half-asleep only, no? After double shift at the hotel, eyes half closed, coming home, mind is playing tricks, that's all.

Me: Arre no, I was wide awake, thinking clear-clear, not even a little bit dozing, I'm telling you. Whole thing I remember step by step.

My friend: Achha, so tell, what happened.

Me: I got down from the auto at the signal, walking towards the gate, and near the compound wall this figure was sitting, all in white, not moving, not talking, facing the temple side only. I called out twice-thrice, "kaun hai," no answer, no movement, nothing.

My friend: Bas, some aunty must be sitting there waiting for someone, or watchman's relative only, do one thing, don't overthink these things, come na, go sleep.

Me: No yaar, gate was locked from inside, chain-lock and everything, watchman himself had gone for his cutting chai, key was with me itself, I checked twice. Nobody could go in-out from there, that side is boundary wall only, no other way.

My friend: Chalo, maybe some homeless person only, resting small small time on the wall.

Me: That I also thought, itself, but the moment I blinked, gone, completely gone, no footsteps sound, nothing. I ran to that spot in two seconds flat, nobody there, you tell me, how person can vanish like that in two seconds only.

My friend: I'm not believing, yaar, sorry. Some trick of light only, streetlight was flickering at that time or what.

Me: Streetlight was working properly, no flickering, nothing, haan. I swear on my mother, I swear on the Ganpati sitting in my house, every single word is true, what to do, you don't believe, fine, don't believe, but I know what I saw with my own eyes.

My friend: Bas yaar, I'm still shaking my head only, this kind of story I'm not buying, sorry, no.
