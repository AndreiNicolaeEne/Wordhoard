Arre yaar, don't ask me about that Diwali, seriously, whole one week I am still recovering only.

So this time everybody is saying, at least once host it na, you also, why always eating at somebody's place. So I thought, chalo, let me do it, first time only, how bad can it be. That was my thinking. Big mistake, I am telling you.

From the day I said yes only, nerves are there, proper. Two weeks before the date I am not sleeping properly, mind is running the whole menu again and again. My workmate is asking, what happened, you are looking senti, and I am like, arre nothing, just this hosting tension only.

Menu I made was too much, seriously too ambitious, no sense at all. I sat with a paper and started writing, one dal, two sabzi, one non-veg for the ones who eat, puri, some rice item, then I thought sweet dish also I should make from scratch, not buy from the shop, and then some starter also, cutlets, and then I am thinking one more chutney, one more raita, list is going and going. My mother saw the list and said,

The mother: Itna sab kaun khayega, do din laga do isko banane mein.

Meaning who will even eat all this, it will take two whole days to cook, but I didn't listen na, I was in that mood only, everything I want to make, show off a little bit, what to do.

Guest count also, don't get me started. First I invited eight, ten people only, small small gathering I was thinking. Then one uncle is calling, can we also come, then that uncle's whole family attached, then my friend is saying her cousin is in town, can she bring, then society wale aunty next door heard and said, arre beta, hum bhi aa jaayen kya, what to say, I can't say no na, so by the end count has gone to like twenty two, twenty three people, for eight people's menu. I am sitting there doing maths, this dal will not be enough, that rice will not be enough, everything double karna padega.

Shopping trip was a whole saga separately. Went to the market, bhaiya at the vegetable stall, I am buying everything in bulk, he is like, aaj shaadi hai kya, is it a wedding or what, so much you are buying. Everything I got, but one thing, seriously, one single thing I could not find anywhere in whole Mumbai that day, some particular besan for one sweet my grandmother used to make, fine quality wala, not the normal one. Three shops I went, one hotel-supply type shop also I tried, bas, nowhere it was there. Finally some far aunty's contact gave jugaad, sent through her domestic help, reached me only at eleven in the night before the function. Full lafda for one packet of besan, I am telling you.

Night before, forget sleep. I was up since ten in the night doing prep, chopping chopping, marinating, whole kitchen looking like bomb only. By two, three a.m. I am still there, hands paining, and I am like why did I take this project. My eldest came in yawning and said,

The eldest: Tu so ja na yaar, kal subah phir uthna hai.

Go sleep, tomorrow morning also you have to wake up, but bas, so much left that sleeping was not an option.

Morning I had made whole schedule on paper, timing everything, six a.m. this, seven this, eight this, very organised I was feeling, proud even. That paper survived maybe one hour into actual morning, then everything went for a toss. Cooker whistle count I lost, one phone call from delivery bhaiya asking where is the society, then gas cylinder is behaving funny, flame is low low, what to do.

Then the real disaster came. I had put the puri dough resting and one big pot of some gravy on slow flame and got distracted doing something else, and by the time I came back it is properly stuck at the bottom, that burnt smell has spread through the whole kitchen, whole house smelling like something died. I am standing there almost wanting to cry, thinking whole dish is gone, what will I serve now. Full scramble started, I am calling my mother in panic, she is saying,

The mother: Ro mat, dusra bana, time hai abhi.

Don't cry, make another one, there is time still, and somehow between us we managed one quick jugaad version, not as good, but saved the situation, what to do, this is what happens only.

In all this tension, one guest came early, much earlier than told, I had said come by one, this one landed by eleven thirty itself, and the moment she entered the kitchen and saw the state, she just rolled up her sleeves without even asking, started cutting vegetables, arranging plates, whole helper mode on. I was so relieved, I am telling her,

Me: Arre you didn't have to.

The guest: Chup kar, kaam kar, baad mein baatein karna.

Stop talking, work now, gossip later, that kind of practical support only, saved my whole morning.

By then stove and oven both are having zero space, every burner occupied, one dish waiting for other dish to finish, this competing for time and fire, it was like signal jam situation in my kitchen, everybody wanting green light same time. Oven I had to off and on, off and on, one thing baking, then off, then on another thing, small small adjustments whole time.

Funny thing is, one dish which I had made least tension about, some simple raita type thing I threw together last minute because I was short of one side dish, that came out far, far better than I expected, everyone later was asking for the recipe only, ek number it came out, and I am like, this I made in five minutes, casual casual, and that turned out best, life is like that only.

Meal was supposed to start at one, by the time actual serving started it was almost two thirty, everyone sitting hungry, smelling the food since long, complaining lightly, is it ready or not, that kind of talk going around, but nobody really angry, more timepass joking only.

First bite reactions, that is the moment I was most tense for. People are tasting, nodding, some are saying wah mast, very nice, one uncle said,

The uncle: Bahut acha bana hai yaar, kaafi dinon baad aisa khana khaya.

Saying long time since he had food like this, that felt good.

But then there is always one blunt person na, my one aunty-type relative, she tasted that jugaad gravy dish, the one I had rescued from burning, and straight up said,

The relative: Yeh thoda off hai, kuch missing hai isme.

This is a bit off, something is missing in it, no filter at all, everyone laughed but I felt a small pinch also, what to do, can't please everyone.

Dish which disappeared first, gone in like ten minutes flat, was that cutlet starter, loudest praise also for that only, people were like,

The cousin: Ek aur plate aayega kya?

Asking for one more plate, that felt like proper win, paisa vasool for all that effort.

By end of the day, everyone leaving, stomach full, house looking like cyclone came through, I am standing there tired to the bone, and my friend jokingly asked,

The friend: Next year bhi karegi kya?

Will I host again next year, and I said, half serious half joking,

Me: Bas yaar, is baar ke liye toh kaafi hai, next saal dekhenge.

Enough for this year, let's see next year, but honestly deep down I know I will do it again, though that day I was swearing never again, what to do, that's how it always goes only.
