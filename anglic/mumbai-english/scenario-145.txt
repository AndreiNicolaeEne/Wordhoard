The uncle: Arre, listen, round face, two hands, walking whole day, never going anywhere, what is it?
The aunty: Fan, no?
The uncle: No no. Clock, yaar.
The aunty: Achha, now mine, comes out only when the light has gone, stands quiet quiet in the corner, cries itself away whole night, tell?
The uncle: Ghost?
The aunty: Chup re, not ghost, candle, what else.
The uncle: Haan, correct. Bas, you win, chalo one more, no?
