Arre yaar, listen, this happened to me only, last month. I needed the domicile certificate stamped from the tehsil office, for passport, because they had preponed my appointment, otherwise I'm having ten more days but now it is tomorrow itself, what to do. So I go, and the line is there since two hours already, small small queues everywhere, one uncle in front, one aunty behind, everybody senti about their own file only.

When my turn comes, the one at the counter is turning the pages very slowly, very slowly, and he is saying, half to himself, half to me, "this month, work is so much, staff is not there, sanction is not there, file movement is very slow these days." I am just standing, playing dumb, giving one blank smile, not saying anything, letting it hang na. He looks up, waits, nothing from my side.

So second time he says it plainer.

The official: See, if you want it fast fast, some arrangement can be made, otherwise normal process is there, takes time only.

Me: Oh achha, arrangement, I see. Actually sir, no issue, whatever is the rule, I will follow only. Is there any form for urgent processing? I can fill.

That word "form" and "rule" did the trick, bas, his face changed. I also mentioned, casually, that my neighbour's brother is working in collector office itself, just dropped it like that, not threatening, just chalo, mentioning. There was one proper tense minute there, him deciding, me looking at my phone like I have all the time, no hurry no hurry. Then he just stamped it, gave me the paisa vasool feeling for once, no extra paisa gone from my pocket, and said come tomorrow for the other copy.

My verdict, yaar: these fellows, they only push if you show even little bit of fear or hurry. You stay bindaas, mention one rule, one contact, let it hang, they fold. Otherwise it becomes proper lafda, and who has time for that, honestly.
