So listen fully, from starting to end, then only you will understand why even now, any time our whole gang is sitting together, someone will bring this up and everybody starts laughing all over again like it happened yesterday itself.

So it was a normal Sunday evening only, nothing special, we were all sitting down in the society compound near the gate, some uncles playing cards on the bench, kids doing cricket with tennis ball near the parking, and our group had brought out the carrom board, the old one with one corner slightly chipped, everybody knows that board. Me and my friend sat down to play, just timepass only, nobody was thinking of any bet at that point, bas, one game, chalo let's see who wins type thing.

But you know how it starts, no? Someone from the side just said one small thing.

The neighbour's boy: Eh, if uncle wins you will do what?

And I said, laughing, arre nothing will happen, he cannot win only, don't worry. That is all it needed. Once you say like that, the whole group gets that gleam in the eye, they smell blood, and slowly slowly they start pushing.

One from the group: No no, something has to be there na, otherwise what is the fun of playing.

Another: Put some amount only, small small, twenty do.

Nobody had even decided anything properly, it was all happening on the side while we were setting the coins, but by the time we actually struck the first piece, twenty rupees was sitting on the table like it was always there. Twenty, nothing big, doesn't even sting, that was the whole point, everybody agreed easy on that, haan haan twenty is fine, done, chalo start.

Now the game itself, yaar, I am telling you honestly, it was going up and down up and down for a good half an hour. First I was ahead, then he pocketed three in one turn and came back, then I covered the queen, then he broke it open again. Small crowd standing around the board by now, uncles had left their cards, kids had left the cricket, everybody giving free commentary, ohh, arre wah, no no wrong shot, like that only. Whole compound ka entertainment for that time.

Then it turned. And it turned hard.

He had one clean line open, straight shot, and the moment he lined up his striker, everybody standing there could see it before he even hit it, the whole board just went quiet for two seconds. I could see it too, honestly, my hand went cold, I knew what was coming even though the game was not finished, three, four more pieces still on the board, but that shot was going to decide everything and there was nothing I could do sitting on my side.

He hit it. Clean. Straight in. And after that it was just formality, two more turns and it was finished, properly finished, no argument, no doubt, nothing.

I groaned so loud, put my head down on the board itself, and just said it out loud, no point pretending.

Me: Ho gaya yaar, I lost. Bas, take your twenty.

And this is where it goes strange, so listen carefully now. The group who had been standing around, they did not even let the twenty rupees come out of my pocket. The eldest of our group, he is the one who always has some jugaad idea ready, he just put his hand up.

The eldest: No no, forget money, money is nothing, we want performance.

Everybody around started clapping and hooting immediately, like they had all been waiting for exactly this. And then they sat down right there and decided, out loud, in front of me, exactly what this performance had to be, nothing left vague, they were very particular about it.

The eldest: Full song, start to end, no stopping in middle. And minimum one full filmy step also, not just standing and singing like a bhajan. And loud only, whole building should hear, that is the condition.

I tried, yaar, I tried to bargain my way out.

Me: Arre come on, let me just give the twenty na, what is the need for all this drama.

They all just laughed at that, full laughing, like I had said something very funny.

Another from the group: No chance, bhai, twenty rupees who cares, this we want, this is better than money.

The neighbour's boy: Bas decided, no changing now, come na, don't spoil the fun.

So that was that. No way out of it now. We fixed a day, next Sunday evening again, same compound, same gate area where everybody sits anyway after their tea, and the word went around the whole building like fire, even the flats on the sixth and seventh floor who otherwise never come down, they were saying they will come down that day itself, don't worry.

And came the day, whole compound was full, more people than any function I have seen there actually, aunties had brought their own chairs down, uncles standing with their evening cutting chai in hand, kids sitting right in front on the ground like it is some school assembly. Watchman uncle had even stopped his rounds to stand and watch, broom resting against the gate.

I stood in the middle, near where the carrom board itself used to be kept, that was decided as the spot, small small joke that the crime scene should be the stage also. Someone put a phone speaker down, full volume, song started, and I gave it everything, yaar, no shame left at that point, might as well do it properly since doing it anyway.

Song was going fine, I was managing the steps somehow, group was already laughing at just my seriousness on the face, but the moment that really finished everybody, the one that even now gets repeated every single time this story comes up, was this. Right at the big step of the song, when I turned around with the full filmy thumka, watchman uncle, standing there with his broom, he also turned with me, exact same timing, broom held up like it is a prop, dead serious face, not even smiling, just doing the move perfectly in sync. Whole compound lost it completely. Aunties on the chairs were literally wiping their eyes, one uncle spilled his chai laughing, kids were rolling on the ground. Even I stopped singing for two seconds because I could not stop laughing myself, looking at watchman uncle standing there so serious, doing better steps than me only.

That was it, that was the moment, paisa vasool for the whole evening honestly, whole building got their entertainment, better than twenty rupees, better than any picture also.

And now, whenever the group is sitting together, doesn't matter where, someone's terrace, someone's wedding, doesn't matter, someone will say, remember that carrom bet, and then it starts again, and every time, I am telling you, the story grows one more layer. None of that happened that day, but nobody corrects it anymore, what to do, the story has its own life now, bigger than the twenty rupees ever was, bigger than the game itself even.
