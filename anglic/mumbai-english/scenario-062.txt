Me: Arre come, come, sit, no need to stand near that gate like that, that side is not really our side to be standing at, better you know that today itself. You are asking about this wall business between us and them, and everybody on this lane will give you their own version only, so let me tell you my side, plain, one time, and after that whatever you want to think, you can think.

See, these two houses, ours and theirs, from my grandfather's time we are like this only, one house not knowing what is cooking without the other house also knowing. My father and their father, every evening, same step there, cutting chai from the corner hotel if the mood was there, discussing this politics that cricket, forty years of it, more. Every Ganpati we are going to their house for prasad, every Eid the mutton is coming from their kitchen to ours, small small things, tiffin exchange, "taste and tell if salt is correct" type of thing. One time light had gone for two full days after some storm, their fridge stuff was spoiling, my father ran one extension cord from our meter to theirs, on the whole line himself, didn't even wait to be asked. Their eldest and my elder brother did eighth standard together in the same municipal school, bunked the same lectures together also. That was the closeness, I am telling you. Life was mast in those days, ek number relations, nobody was thinking one wall could do what this wall has done.

Then, twelve thirteen years back, they are extending their kitchen at the back. Fine, their plot, their business, nobody said one word about that. But the drain pipe from that new kitchen they fixed running right along the boundary, and with it they are planting one coconut sapling exactly on the line, not one foot this side, not one foot that side, exactly on it. Small small thing you will say, a tree what will it do, but root doesn't ask permission no. Two three years and that root is going into our compound wall foundation, cracks are coming, small small first, then bigging up.

I asked politely. My mother went and asked politely. "Bhaiya, that pipe water is sitting on our side only, and the tree also, some root has gone into the wall, come na, look one time, we'll manage together, no fight, just move it few feet, that's it." First time, nothing, forgotten, no reply, we thought maybe busy, everybody has their own life no. Second time we asked, "haan haan will do, will do," and again nothing happened. Third time, and I am telling you this third time only had some edge in my voice, not too much, but there was some. I said, achha, fine, it is one month now, wall is not going to wait for your convenience only, water has been standing since one week in front of my kitchen door itself.

Now what came back to me, through the aunty who does all the up-down between both houses, always has some reason to be standing at both gates at once, was a completely different version by the time it reached me. By the time it came round it was I had gone and shouted, made full scene at their gate in front of some guests, things I never did, I am telling you, I only said what I said standing at my own gate, one time, in a normal voice. That is how a message changes hands on this lane, by the fourth hand it is a different animal only, that aunty can be pakau like that, always some update nobody asked for, and worse, twisted.

And when I finally went myself and asked straight, the lady of the house is looking at me like I have grown two heads, saying, "what ignored, when did we ever ignore you, you never even came and asked properly, this whole thing is in your head only." Three times word was sent, my mother went personally two times, and she is standing there at her own gate saying nobody ever came. Flat denial, like that. That thing hurt more than the crack in the wall, because fight is one thing, but to say something never happened when it happened in front of your own eyes, what to do with that.

So then I also did one small thing, quietly, no drama. Their scooter used to stand, parking, half on our side of the common passage, for years we never said one word about that, adjustment only. That week I put out two flower pots there, small small pots, nothing much, just enough that scooter could not come and stand like before. Some will call it a chindi thing to do, over flower pots, but what to do. Nobody said one word to me directly about it, but I heard, through the same aunty only, that it started some fire on their side too.

And one comment came back to me, through that same channel, that stung worse than everything else put together. Their lady had said to someone, casual only, over tea, that our whole family has always been like this, jealous types, since college days itself, never had our own success so busy measuring what others are having. That, I am telling you, that one line, thirteen years of wall business I could have forgotten, but that line I am not forgetting. Even today I am having that feeling somewhere sitting, folded up, like a note kept in a drawer, never sent again.

Still, that Diwali I made one plate, sent it through the same aunty, sweets, one small chit also, saying let bygones be bygones, kids are growing up watching all this, not good for them. Plate came back same evening. Not even emptied and refilled, sent back as is, not one sweet touched. That, I understood the message very clear. I gave one missed call also, once, after that, thinking maybe she'll call back on her own. She cut the call from her side instead. Didn't even ring back after. So what to do, I also stopped trying after that.

Rest of the lane, what to say, roughly half-half only it became. Some are coming to our side, saying the tree was clearly encroaching, root is root, proof is proof. Some are going their side, saying we made too much lafda over one small small thing, faltu tension for a tree only. Silent groups formed like that, nobody announcing which side, but you will know from who is greeting who at the common tap in the morning.

Then one day, common tap time, water pressure was low, whole lane standing there with buckets waiting their turn, and some remark passed. I don't even remember now who started that particular sentence, but within two minutes the whole street is hearing our two houses going at each other, full volume, years of things coming out in front of everybody, the bhaiya from the tea stall standing there with his kettle still in hand, not knowing where to look, full filmy scene, I am telling you, whole lane standing like audience for it.

And in that same shouting only, their side accused us that somebody from our house broke their outer light, and the mirror of that scooter also went missing, saying we did it in the night. I told them straight, on our mother's name, nobody from this house touched one thing of theirs, that light was hanging loose since months, anybody on this lane can say that, but they are not believing. Still today not believing.

It is running eight years now, this silence, complete silence, not one good morning crossing that boundary. Small small way it still shows, their post sometimes comes to our box by mistake, courier boy confuses the two gates, that parcel I keep outside their gate now, don't even ring the bell, they do the same for ours, quietly, like some jugaad arrangement we never discussed but both are following. Kids don't run in and out of both houses anymore like it was one house only. Civility is there, we don't show ill will on the face when we pass, but warmth, that is not there anymore, not even a trace.

You do one thing, don't go asking them also for their side, waste of your time only, everybody on this lane is filmy about their own story by now, mine included maybe. And you know, sitting here today, telling you all this so clearly, so sure of myself. I am not even hundred percent sure anymore, who actually started all this. The tree, or the way I asked the third time, or that aunty's carrying tales between two gates, I don't know. But nobody in this lane is going to hear me say that out loud except you, today, since you are new only.
