The mother: You are somewhere else only, na. Chai is getting cold in your hand and you are not even drinking.

Me: Sorry, sorry. I was thinking about the dream I had, that's all. Since I woke up only I am having this feeling, cannot shake it off.

The mother: What dream, tell me properly. Come na, sit, don't just stand there stirring the same spoon around and around.

Me: Okay okay. So it started very simple only, nothing scary in the beginning. I was walking home from somewhere, I don't even know from where, office or market, not clear. Just walking, walking.

The mother: Achha. Then?

Me: Then the road kept going only. Like it is not stopping. I am walking and walking, past the signal, past the society gate, past everything, and home is still not coming. In real life that road is two minutes only, you know that. In the dream it was going on and on, small small turns I have never seen, and I am thinking, arre, this is taking too long, but my legs are just walking, not even tired.

The mother: Baap re. And then you reached or not?

Me: I reached eventually, that's the thing. But when I reached, the house was having one room extra. A room we don't have in real life, ma. Not there in our flat, never was there.

The mother: Wait wait, what kind of room? Like bedroom, store room, what?

Me: That is what is troubling me only, I cannot even say properly. Not a bedroom exactly, more like a sitting room, small, with one window, light coming in soft like evening time. Just very ordinary looking, that is the strange part. Nothing spooky about the room itself.

The mother: Haan, go on, go on.

Me: Inside that room, Papa was sitting.

The mother: Arre bhagwan.

Me: Just sitting there, ma, calm like anything. Like he never went anywhere at all. Not thin like at the end, normal only, the way he used to be before, in his chair, one leg up like that only he used to sit. And he was talking to me.

The mother: What was he saying, tell me exactly.

Me: That is the problem, I am trying and trying since I woke up, the actual words are not coming back at all. I know he was saying something, I remember the feeling of it, like something important was being told to me, some message, but the words itself, gone. Poof.

The mother: Try once more, close your eyes and try.

Me: I am trying, ma. No, it's not coming. There was something about... no. Bas, it's not there, whatever I try. Only the feeling is there, that he told me something and I understood it fully in the dream, felt very settled hearing it, but now, nothing. Empty.

The mother: Chhod na, don't force it, sometimes it comes back later in the day itself, out of nowhere.

Me: Maybe. Then the dream shifted, like a scene change in a filmy movie, one moment to next. Suddenly I am searching for something. Some object, very urgent, must find it, that feeling only, but the thing itself I cannot say what it was.

The mother: Searching for what, though? You must have some idea, no?

Me: Genuinely I don't know, ma. Even inside the dream I did not know what I was searching for, that is the mad part. I am opening cupboard, checking under the bed, checking my own bag three four times, patting my pockets, and each time nothing is there, and I am getting more and more worked up, heart starting to go fast, and still I don't have one clue what object I am even looking for. Just this drilling feeling, must find, must find, and it is not there, it is not there, anywhere.

The mother: That sounds so tiring, yaar. Small small panic building up like that.

Me: Very tiring. It kept climbing and climbing, searching one room then another room, then outside, then back inside, faster and faster, and then it just broke, like something snapped, and I woke up. Straight up in bed, heart properly hammering, like I ran the whole local platform to catch a train.

The mother: Oh baba. And then?

Me: Then I was just lying there for one long minute, not moving, not sure which one is real. Whether I am actually still in that house with the extra room and Papa sitting there, or whether this bed, this fan, this ceiling is the real one. Properly confused for a minute, ma, I am telling you. Had to look at the fan turning round and round two three times before I believed I am actually awake.

The mother: Chalo, at least you are awake now and drinking chai with me, that is the real one, don't worry.

Me: I know, I know. But it's sitting in my chest still, since morning itself, this heaviness. What to do, cannot explain it to anyone who was not there.

The mother: I am telling you what it means, shall I?

Me: Tell, tell.

The mother: It means he is fine wherever he is, that's it. That's why he is looking so calm, so settled, sitting like that with one leg up, talking easy easy, no tension on his face at all. If something was wrong he would come looking worried, no? This one came looking bindaas.

Me: Ha, bindaas ghost, very filmy theory this is.

The mother: Not ghost, don't say ghost. I am just saying, when they come like that in dream, so ordinary, so calm, it is a good sign only. My own mother used to say the same, that if the person is smiling and sitting easy, it means all is well on that side, no complaints.

Me: Then why I could not hold onto the words, ma? If it was some message, some important thing, why is it not staying with me?

The mother: Who knows. Maybe some things are only for that moment, not meant to carry over into this side. Or maybe it is nothing at all, just your own mind doing timepass with old memories, mixing up walking and searching and your father, all jugaad, stitched together from nothing.

Me: Could be that also. Though it did not feel like nothing when I was in it.

The mother: Dreams never feel like nothing when you are inside them, that is their whole trick. Drink your chai now, it is properly cold, I will make one more cutting for you.

Me: Do one thing, make it a little strong today, my head is still heavy.

The mother: Will do, will do. And that searching part, what you think that means, since you are asking me everything else.

Me: I don't know, yaar. Maybe searching for the words itself, the ones he was telling me, and the dream was making me search for something else because it could not show me searching for words directly. Very senti explanation, no?

The mother: Very senti, but could be true also. Or maybe it means nothing, maybe it is just some lafda your brain was cooking at four in the morning and there is no meaning hiding underneath at all.

Me: Maybe. Anyway, what to do, I cannot solve it sitting here. Give me the chai, let it be strong, and let's leave it as it is only. Some mystery is fine, na.

The mother: Fine, fine. Chalo, drink up before it goes cold again, second time I am not making.
