I am not going to dance around it, so let me say straight, from the first line itself. I love you, and it is not some timepass feeling, it is serious, you know na.

You remember that evening, nothing big at all, no function, no occasion, just the current had gone in the whole society and we were sitting on the step outside sharing one cutting chai from the stall bhaiya, waiting since one hour for the light to come back. You were fanning yourself with some old newspaper, cracking a faltu joke about the building committee, and I was laughing more than the joke even deserved, only for you. That is the moment, sitting there in the dark like two fools, I understood this thing has turned serious now, not like that only, for real.

One thing nobody else notices, you get a small small crease near your left eyebrow only when you are pretending everything is fine and it is not. I have seen it hundred times by now, every time my heart does something funny.

Since you have come into my life, even the local at rush hour, everybody pushing here and there, I am not minding so much, itself feels less faltu somehow. But the days you are not there, what to do, they are dragging so slow, like time is just not there at all, and I keep checking phone for one missed call from you.

Here is one thing I have never said loud, not even to myself. I get scared sometimes that I am not good enough for all this, that you will see through me one day and go. Bas, saying it here only, on paper.

One promise, whatever comes, we will do some jugaad and manage, together, that is final, no ifs about it.

Getting too senti now, so I will stop.

Come na, close the door, sit alone, read this again.

Yours, always,
jaan.
