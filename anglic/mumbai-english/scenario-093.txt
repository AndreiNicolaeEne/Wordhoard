Me: Come na, sit, all of you, I'll tell it now, plainly, once, so you all also have it, the way I would want it told to me.

It was only me there in that last hour, no nurse, no ward boy, just me and Baba, and that monitor beeping soft soft, like a clock only. First thing he said, nothing about dying-viying, achha. He looked at the fan and said, "This is making too much noise, no? Tell them to change it." That's all, small small thing, just to ease into the room like that only. I said haan Baba, I'll tell them, don't worry.

Then he became serious. He said, do one thing, that society file, third floor maintenance, it is pending since two years now, you clear it, don't leave it hanging, what to do, somebody has to finish it only. I said yes Baba, I will do it, I am handling, don't take tension.

Then he called me beta. Just once, same as always, like when I was small small, coming home late from tuition. "Beta," he said, and I held his hand tight, tight.

Then, out of nowhere, he asked me something I was not expecting at all. He asked, was I a good father to you? Just like that, plain question, no filmy drama. And I told him true, right there, I said yes Baba, always, no doubt about it.

Last full sentence he managed, I am having it whole in my head since, word for word: take care of your Aai, and don't fight with your brother over small small things, bas.

After that, only quiet. Doctor had said some chance is there, but by then time was not there really. His breathing slow slow, my hand in his hand, holding on, holding on, until he drifted, just like that, no more words after.

So chalo, that's all of it, plainly, the way it happened. Nothing to add, nothing to hide. This is what I wanted you all to have, same as I have it.
