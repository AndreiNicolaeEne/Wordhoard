The moment my eyes open I am remembering it, no delay, straight remembering: today is the store room day. Months back, in that society meeting, after being told for the hundredth time, "you people never clean your corner only," I stood up and swore, bas, I will do it, one full day I will give, whatever happens. And now the day has come and my whole body is saying no, don't go, sleep only, what to do.

But I get up. Getting ready itself I am grumbling, muttering to myself in the mirror, arre why did I open my mouth that day, nobody was forcing me, I could have stayed quiet only, now see, whole Sunday gone for this.

Down at the gate the watchman is smiling too much.

The watchman: Aunty, today is the big day no? I have kept the broom ready for you. Do one thing, take the small broom not the big one, corner is small only.

Me: Bas bas, don't start, I am not in mood for your jokes today.

Store room is like one lafda inside, boxes since years not touched, small small things everywhere, old fans, somebody's old cycle, tiffin boxes with no lid, newspapers gone yellow, gone soft. I am sitting on the floor only, sorting, and every ten minutes finding one more reason to complain, itself the dust is giving me sneezing, itself the tube light inside is off, current is there, light is not there, what kind of building is this, and who keeps this much chindi rubbish for so long, I ask you.

By afternoon my back is finishing, my patience is finishing, everything is finishing, and quitting starts feeling like the only sane option. One aunty from the third floor comes, stands at the door.

Aunty: Beta, this is taking whole day it seems, come na, leave it for tomorrow, nobody will say anything.

Me: No aunty, I swore I will finish today itself. If I leave now what was the point of all that big talk that day. What to do, have to finish only, no other way.

She gives one sympathetic look and goes, must be thinking I am mad. Cutting chai from the corner hotel keeps me going, one after other, bhaiya bringing it up without even asking now. By evening arms paining, mind gone numb, but last box also empty, corner clean like new, jhakaas even I would say myself. Watchman claps once, mostly to tease me, but today I don't mind. Whole day gone, promise also done, only relief is there now, and one big sleep waiting, no complaints left to give.
