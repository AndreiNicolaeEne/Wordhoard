The mother switched off the fan, sat both of them down on the bed close beside her, one on each side, and held their hands first, before saying anything at all.

Mother: Beta, I have to tell you something, listen properly, okay. Your grandfather, he is not there anymore. He passed away, just now only. Hospital se call came.

The eldest: Passed away means what exactly, Ma? Like he actually died? Not just very very sick, actually died-died?

Mother: Yes, beta, actually died. His heart, it stopped, the breathing also stopped. He was having that trouble since two months only, you remember, but this is different, this is final. Doctor tried everything only, but some things nobody can fix, not even the best doctor. He is not in pain now, not feeling anything now, that much I promise you.

The younger one: But where he has gone then, Ma? Is he sleeping only?

Mother: No, beta, not sleeping. Sleeping people wake up no? This is different only. His body has completely stopped, forever. We will not see him walking, talking, doing his little small small things, ever again, not in that way.

She pulled both of them in tight then, one arm around each.

Mother: Come here, come na, both of you, come close, my chikoo.

The younger one's voice came out very small, right into her shoulder.

The younger one: Ma, we will see him again? Ever?

Mother: Not like before, beta, not in this body, not walking in and calling your name from the gate. That I will not lie to you about. But whatever he gave you, his love, his stories, all that time he spent with you, that is staying with you only, in your memory, always. That much is there.

The eldest: Okay.

Mother: Do one thing, both of you, listen now. Next two-three days it will be a little different at home. Lot of relatives will come, aunty-uncle types, some crying also will be there, don't get scared by that, it's normal only. One uncle is coming tomorrow morning to take us to his house, there will be some rituals, some prayers, that is how we say goodbye properly, our way. School I will inform, no tension about marks this week. Any doubt, anytime, you come, ask me, okay?

The eldest: Okay, Ma.

Mother: Chalo, go now, but stay close by only, na.
