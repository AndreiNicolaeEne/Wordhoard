Arre don't even ask, whole week I was minding that flat only, and every single day something-something happened, I am telling you. Not one quiet day, not one. My colleague had gone to her native place for the wedding, seven days, and she is like, bas you just stay there, water the plants, feed the cat, give milk to the bai in the morning, that's it, simple work only. Simple work my foot.

First night itself something was not right. I reached by nine, tired, train was packed like anything, and the moment I put the key in the main door the latch is not matching, like somebody has changed the lock or what. I am standing outside for twenty minutes, calling her, no answer, phone was probably switched off on the flight. The watchman came up, uncle from downstairs only, and he is like:

Watchman: Madam, this door sometimes sticks, you lift and push, don't push straight, na.

Me: Uncle I am lifting only, still not going.

We did some jugaad, he brought oil, put on the hinge side, twisted here-there, and finally it opened, but I am telling you, that whole first night I could not sleep properly, kept feeling something is off, like the house itself is testing me, whether I can manage or not.

Then second day morning, the cat. Small small thing, that cat, but so cunning. I opened the door to take the milk packet from the bhaiya downstairs and that cat just shot out like bullet, gone into the society compound, under the parked cars, no sign. I am running around in my nightie only, everybody in the society looking at me like what is this scene, aunty from the next building is standing on her balcony enjoying full tamasha. One uncle from ground floor, he is like:

Uncle: Beta, cats don't go far-far, they get scared and hide only, don't run behind, sit quietly, it will come.

I sat near the parking, kept calling na, come na, come na, with the treat packet shaking shaking, and within one hour, maybe little more, it just walked out from behind the scooter like nothing happened, so bindaas, tail up and all, as if I am the one who was missing. I nearly cried, seriously.

Then the plants. First two-three days I was watering like she told, but slowly slowly I am noticing the money plant leaves are turning yellow, and that one balcony pot, the tulsi, is looking very sad, drooping. I didn't know whether I am giving too much water or too less, nobody tells these things properly na. By day four the tulsi is almost finished looking, I am panicking, messaging her, no proper network there apparently, only one reply came, giving exam of patience she must be, ha ha. I asked the flower bhaiya near the signal, he said don't water daily only, alternate day is fine, morning time only, not afternoon, sun is too much. Did that, it recovered little bit, but that whole middle of the week I had this tension only in my head, what if the plant dies under my watch, what will I tell her.

Then Wednesday, the geyser. I am going for bath, switch on the geyser, and there is some burning smell, faint but there, and no hot water coming, just cold cold water. I off it immediately, called the building electrician, he came, opened it up, said the element has gone, needs replacing, cannot do same day, some part is not there in his shop. So two days I am having only cold water bath, in this weather, chi. Finally Thursday he brought the part, fixed it, and that evening onwards hot water was coming again, proper. That was the fix, actually, landed on the second last day only, because before that whatever he was doing was all temporary jugaad, not lasting.

In between all this, one afternoon the doorbell rang, out of nowhere, no call no message, and it was her aunty, staying nearby only, just dropped by to check.

Aunty: Beta everything okay na? I was just passing, thought let me see the house.

Me: Yes aunty all fine, come come, I'll make tea.

Inside I am thinking oh god the house is not that tidy, some clothes on the sofa, but she was very sweet only, had cutting chai with me, sat for maybe half hour, asked about my studies, my work, very filmy questions like when are you getting married, typical aunty style, but nice company that day, I was feeling little lonely in that house otherwise.

Then the big scare. I could not find the spare house key, the extra one she had given for emergency, I had kept it somewhere safe, too safe apparently, searched the whole house, every drawer, under the mattress, inside her cupboard even, my heart was going bum bum, I am thinking this is gone for good, what will I tell her, what if I lose the main one also. Full one hour I searched, senti ho gaya literally, nearly cried again, this week was making me cry left right. Then found it only, inside my own bag, in the small zip pocket, where I myself had kept it day one and forgot completely. What to do, that is my brain only.

Saturday, worst afternoon. I stepped out just for two minutes, to get vada pav from the corner stall, didn't take the key, thought door will stay open only, and some gust of wind came and it banged shut behind me. I am standing outside in my house clothes, no phone, no key, nothing, for almost three hours, waiting since three hours literally, until the watchman's shift changed and the new one had a duplicate key with him. Sat on the compound bench whole time, aunty from upstairs gave me tea even, out of pity I think, but full waste of an afternoon that was.

Same week, one night, rain came suddenly, proper Mumbai rain, and I had left the small bedroom window open because it was too stuffy. Woke up at midnight, heard water sound, ran in, whole floor near the window is wet wet, curtain also soaked, papers on the side table gone soft. Mopped it at midnight only, half asleep, cursing myself.

And then another night, some noise, like something falling in the kitchen, scared me completely, I got up, took the torch, walked slowly checking each room, heart beating, and it was nothing, only that cat had knocked one steel bowl off the counter, sitting there looking at me like what, so casual, as if it is not the cause of half my tension that week.

Last day, Sunday, before they were reaching that evening, I did full cleaning, mopping, dusting, arranging everything back exactly where it was, watered the plants one last time, threw out my own mess, made the house look like nothing happened at all.

When they finally reached, tired from journey, her mother asked me:

Her mother: So beta, how was the week, all okay na, house gave trouble or what?

Me: Aunty all bindaas only, nothing much, small small things here-there, otherwise all mast.

I didn't tell them everything, what to do, some things you just keep to yourself only, paisa vasool experience it was though, that much I can say.
