That anger from that time, I am not exaggerating even one bit, it did not stay small, it went big and it stayed big. After that, any decision was there to be taken in this family, uncle was not called, not even for timepass discussion, forget anything serious. Whole society also was talking, not just family, that shop-wala uncle, be careful with him, don't trust him with cash. Aunty also, she just went quiet after that, sitting in corners at functions, nobody asking her opinion also, like she also did something. This reputation followed uncle for years and years. Next morning the shortfall came out, and it was uncle's name that got attached to it, because uncle was the one doing accounts officially, on paper.

Carrying this, yaar, it is not small small thing. Papa is not there now to hear this, but if he was there, he also would want only truth, not this story we were all living inside for so long.

The eldest: Yes. It was me.
