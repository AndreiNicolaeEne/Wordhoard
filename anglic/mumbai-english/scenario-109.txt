Eight years, and the same bell only, still that same dying-battery sound, half a ring and then nothing, so I had to press twice. Bags on both shoulders, one more in my hand, and I am standing there like some delivery bhaiya outside his own house.

The door opens and it is the mother, and for one second, na, she is just looking. Not smiling, not saying, just looking, like she is checking is this really or some lookalike only standing at her door at ten in the night.

The mother: Arre, arre bhagwan, is it you or what...

Me: Ma, it's me only na, who else will come with this much luggage...

The mother: AAAAAA! Come, come, come inside, why are you standing there like a guest, come!

And then she is not letting go. I am trying to put the bags down and she is not letting go, one full minute, more, my father is coming from behind saying let the boy breathe, and even he is not really meaning it because he also comes and joins, three of us stuck together in the doorway like some traffic jam, nobody moving.

The eldest: (coming running from the kitchen, wiping hand on the dupatta) What is all this shouting, who has. OH MY GOD.

Me: Didi.

The eldest: You have become so thin-thin, no, or is it just the beard, come here, come here properly, let me see your face, turn this side...

Me: I am fine only, don't start...

The eldest: No no, you have changed a lot, this face is not that face, something is different, achha your jaw only has become like Papa's, see...

The father: Everybody is asking, nobody is letting the boy sit even, chalo chalo, first sit, first sit...

Aunty from next door: (already inside, uninvited, as usual) I heard the noise itself I came running, arre look at him, look at him, he was this high only when he left na...

The eldest: Aunty please, let him breathe...

Aunty: I am asking only, no harm in asking...

The mother: Sit sit sit, why is nobody making him sit...

Uncle: (from the sofa, not even getting up properly, just raising a hand) So, finally. Eight years I am hearing "he is coming, he is coming," and today only he is actually coming.

Me: Uncle, how are you...

Uncle: I am there only, what will happen to me, same same. You tell, how was the flight, how long the layover was...

The mother: Let him sit first, then flight-shite ask...

The father: (voice cracking a little, turning away to adjust the fan switch that does not need adjusting) All this time... eight years is not a small thing, ki...

The eldest: Papa...

The father: No no, I am fine, dust is there in the fan only, that's why...

Me: Papa...

The father: Bas bas, I am not like your mother, getting senti-senti for nothing, sit, sit, tell us...

A small voice, from behind the mother's leg, half hidden:

The younger cousin: (whispering to the mother) Who is this uncle?

The mother: Arre this is your own brother's, this is bhaiya only, don't you remember, I showed you photo so many times...

The younger cousin: (still not coming forward) He doesn't look like photo.

Me: Come na, come here, I won't bite...

The younger cousin: (hangs back near the door, one finger in mouth, shy of all the shouting)

The eldest: Leave her, she was two years only when you went, what will she remember, come, come, sit properly first...

Me: (sitting finally, looking around) Ay, this sofa is same, no? Same cover also, or you changed...

The mother: Same only, I got it re-done once, that's it, everything else is same, why will I change...

Me: And this smell: (sniffing), is this the same masala you are using, this smell is exactly like before, itself, nothing has changed in this house, I swear...

The mother: (laughing, wiping eyes at the same time) You remember the smell also, look at that...

The eldest: Forget smell, forget smell, tell us, how is job, how is that place, are you eating properly there or what, you look thin...

Aunty: Is there some girl also, or still...

The mother: Aunty, let him keep the bag down first, na...

The bags, meanwhile, are sitting exactly where I dropped them at the door, nobody has touched them, nobody remembers them, three bags and a shoe box just standing there like separate guests waiting to be invited in.

Uncle: You know what all has happened here, no, so much has changed, let me tell...

The eldest: I will tell, you always tell it wrong...

Uncle: Achha, tell then...

The eldest: Building-wale uncle's daughter, she got married last year only, remember her, small small girl, used to play cricket with you in the society compound...

Me: That one? Married already?

The eldest: Married, and one child also now...

The mother: And the one from fourth floor, the son, he has gone abroad, some Canada or what...

Uncle: Not Canada, Australia...

The mother: Canada, Australia, some cold place only, what does it matter...

The eldest: And your school friend, the one you used to bunk lecture with together, remember, he is doing very well now, has his own business, some export-import, jugaad type business only but doing very well...

Me: That fellow? He couldn't even give the exam properly in eighth standard...

The eldest: Exactly that fellow, now he is driving some big car, life is like that only, you never know...

Me: And this society, gate is new?

The father: New gate, old committee, same politics, don't ask...

Me: The chai wala outside, is he still there?

The father: Still there, still giving that same cutting chai, nothing has changed there also...

Me: Good, first thing tomorrow I am going, I am telling you, that taste I have not got anywhere in eight years, whatever they call chai there, it is not chai...

The mother: First thing tomorrow you are eating breakfast in this house only, then you can go wherever...

The eldest: So tell, how long this time? Full month or again just running-running visit like last time...

Me: This time properly, three weeks at least...

The mother: Only three weeks?

Me: Ma, job is there, I can't stay forever...

The mother: Do one thing, extend it, tell them some family function is there, what to do, they will understand only...

The eldest: Anyway, three weeks is there, so this Sunday we are doing something, whole family, everybody coming, I am already planning it in my head...

Aunty: I will bring the sweets, don't worry about that part...

Uncle: And drinks I will manage, don't tell your mother...

The mother: I am sitting right here only, uncle...

Uncle: (laughing) Just testing if you are listening...

The father: This calls for one proper get-together, na, eight years is not timepass, this is big thing...

The eldest: (suddenly, grinning) Oi, Bhidu, remember what we used to call you, or you have forgotten that also in foreign...

Me: Don't start...

The eldest: (already telling the aunty, who was not there for it, unexplained, just laughing) He used to...

Me: Didi, please, not in front of everyone...

The eldest: Arre it's nothing bad, just...

Me: Bas, bas, leave it...

For one moment then, nobody says anything. The mother just looking at me, the father looking at the mother, the eldest still smiling from the joke, the younger cousin peeping now from behind the doorframe a little braver, uncle and aunty both gone quiet also, and it is like the whole room takes one breath together, just looking, like they are making sure it is real, that I am actually sitting here on this same sofa and not some video call that will freeze any second.

Then the mother gets up.

The mother: Chalo, enough sitting, you must be hungry, I am heating the food, don't argue...

The eldest: I will help...

Uncle: I am going nowhere, I am comfortable...

The father: (finally smiling properly, patting my knee once) Good you are here. Bas, good you are here.

And just like that the shouting settles into something quieter, spoons in the kitchen, the fan going properly now, the bags still forgotten by the door, and everybody talking in normal voice at last, like eight years was one long day only, and this is just evening.
