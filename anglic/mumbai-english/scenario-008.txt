Mother: Beta, how many times you will keep asking same-same thing. Listen properly now, money is not there for a fifteen-thousand rupee phone, that is the plain truth of it. Child: But I will do so much chore only, I will go down for the milk daily, I will fold everybody's clothes, small small work also I will do, just get me the phone na?

Mother: Chore is chore, money is money, one doesn't become other like that. Achha, do one thing, if Papa's bonus comes this Diwali, and if your marks in the terminal exam are decent, not first-class, just decent, then we will see. I am not saying yes, I am saying we will see, samjha?

Child: But cousin already has one! Uncle bought for him last month itself, why not for me?

Mother: What Uncle does in his house is his business. His shop is doing well this year, ours is having its own lafda going on, you know that only. Don't bring what that child has and put here, it doesn't settle anything in this house.

Child: Then nothing for me only, is it.

Mother: Arre, who said nothing. I will get you that small speaker, the cheap one, for your cycle, that you were showing me on Papa's phone that day. Not the big thing, but something, no? Bas, that's the deal for now.

Child: Theek hai. But when marks come you won't forget na?

Mother: Haan haan, won't forget. Chalo now, homework pending since one hour, what to do, whole evening gone in this discussion itself.
