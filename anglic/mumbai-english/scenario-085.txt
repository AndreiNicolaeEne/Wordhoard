Evening, the compound bench, cutting chai from the bhaiya downstairs, and the talk goes where it always goes only.

The first: Arre these knees, yaar. Paining since ten years now, no? Every morning getting up from bed itself is one whole project. Stairs I am avoiding completely, lift I am taking even for one floor, what to do.

The second: Ten years, bas, that's it? Listen to my back once. Three-four nights every month, sleep is simply not there. I am lying there two-three in the morning, off the fan, on the fan, off the fan again, small small adjustments, nothing is working. Ceiling and I are having a proper staring competition daily.

The third: Both of you are children only, I am telling you. This gas and acidity I am carrying since college time itself, before even your marriages happened. Forty-something years, same problem, on and off, on and off, never once properly gone. Doctor also has given up, he is just saying uncle adjust, like that only.

The first: Adjust, he is saying, sitting in his AC cabin, giving one tablet, taking full fees. Convenient job that is.

The second: True only. So tell me one thing then, since we are all sitting here anyway, who is actually having it worst? You want a certificate or what, uncle?

The third: Obviously me na, longest-serving patient at this bench. Bring the trophy.

The first: Trophy for what, your knees also can't even climb to collect it.

The second: Bas bas, forget trophy, at least my problem is more dramatic, three-four sleepless nights, very filmy.

The third: Filmy nothing, mine is the original, you two are timepass only.

Nobody gives in, nobody ever does, and the three of them are still arguing when the chai finishes, laughing like the whole thing was decided long back and nobody bothered telling them.
