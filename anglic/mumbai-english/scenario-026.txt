Arre yaar, let me tell you the whole thing, what to do, it was silly-silly but it hurt like anything.

It started with the AC bill only. This July it came 4200, and he saw it lying on the fridge and started first, no warning: "Every night running it is, no? Even when nobody is there in the room." I said the child was having fever only, that's why, but he was not listening. So bas, I did off the AC remote myself and said achha fine, sleep without it then, see how you like.

That's when he dragged back that old faltu SIP scheme, the "guaranteed return" one his friend gave him, 40,000 gone like that three years back, money that is not there anymore, totally different matter, but he brought it in only, to balance his account against mine.

The husband: "At least I invested somewhere, you only spend on tiffin and this-that."
Me: "You are comparing tiffin to your jugaad scheme? What to do with such logic only, haan."

Then he said the sharpest thing, about my mother's family, that we are chindi people, counting rupees since childhood itself. That one sat in my chest whole day, I am not lying.

After that house was silent since two days, only "pass the remote" type talking, nothing more between us, whole society could feel the tension I think.

Third evening he came up with cutting chai from the bhaiya downstairs, kept it on the table quiet and said, "Come na, this small small thing is becoming too much." That's it, he backed down first, I did not even say sorry from my side, just took the chai.

My verdict to you only, haan: money quarrel is like the Virar local, delay hoti hai, packed and senti in the middle, but reaches eventually. He is not wrong man, just habit of dragging old ghosts. I am also guilty, keeping ledger in head. Do one thing, I told myself, let it go this time. Bas, chalo, moving on now.
