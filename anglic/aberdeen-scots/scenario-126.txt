Me: Ye'll no believe fit happened tae me the streen. I'm no jokin ye.

My friend: Here we go again. Fit wis it this time, were ye half-sleepin and dreamt it up?

Me: I wis wide awake, I'm tellin ye. Nae dreamin, nae drappin aff, naething like that. I hid the clock richt in front o me, quarter tae three, and I mind every thocht I hid afore it happened.

My friend: Aye, fit happened, then?

Me: The wardrobe door swung open on its ain. Slow, like. Naebody near it, naething tae push it.

My friend: Must've been a draught fae the windae.

Me: The windae's been sealed shut since the hairst, my friend, naething gets in or oot o that thing. I checked it masel twice that same day, I ken it wis shut.

My friend: The cat, then, cats get intae aathing.

Me: We've hid nae cat in this hoose since the auld een deed twa year back, ye ken that fine yersel, ye were greetin aboot it.

My friend: There's some rational thing ahin it, there's aye something, dinna be daft.

Me: There's naething vrang wi my heid, I'm tellin ye plain, it juist swung open slow like somebody wis stannin richt ahin it, watchin me.

My friend: Ye're haverin, ye must be.

Me: I swear on my ain grunny's grave, every last word o that's true, I saw fit I saw and naebody's tellin me different.

My friend: I'm no buyin it. No a scrap o it. Yer heid wis fu o sleep, whether ye ken it or no.
