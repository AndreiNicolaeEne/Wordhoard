The older hand: Richt min, watch me nae aince. Ye slip the knife in juist ahin the gills, like so, nae doon through the belly, and ye draw it up in the ae stroke. See that? Clean.

The new hand: Aye, I see it.

The older hand: Awa ye go than, gie's a shot.

The new hand: There.

The older hand: Na na, that's a' vrang, min, ye've gyan straucht doon through the guts, look at the mess o that. In ahin the gills is fit I said.

The new hand: Fit dae ye mean ahin the gills? Ye said doon the middle a meenit syne.

The older hand: I never said nae sic thing. Ahin. The. Gills. Watch again - see my thoomb, richt there, that's far ye start, nae ony farther doon.

The new hand: Right, richt, ahin the thoomb.

The older hand: Aye. Try it.

The new hand: There, is that better?

The older hand: Fit like are ye no listenin tae a wird I'm sayin? That's the third fish ye've ruined the day, we canna sell that, it's a' cut tae ribbons. Foo mony times div I hae tae show ye the same thing?

The new hand: Weel I'm tryin, ye're gyan affa fast, I canna see fit yer daein wi yer hands aa hunched ower like that.

The older hand: Richt, richt, fair eneuch, dinna fash yersel, quinie, I'm no roarin at ye for the fun o't. Come roon this side. See here - yer thoomb sits on the heid, like that, and the knife gyangs in juist at yer thoomb, nae past it. Try it wi yer ain hand first, nae the knife.

The new hand: Like that?

The older hand: Aye, noo the knife.

The new hand: There.

The older hand: Aye, that's it! Noo yer gettin it, see, clean as a whistle. Ye'll hae the hang o't afore hairst if ye keep that up.
