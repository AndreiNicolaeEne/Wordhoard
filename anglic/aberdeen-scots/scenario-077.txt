My friend: Ye'll nivver guess, I canna even sit doon wi it, I've had the maddest news!

Me: Fit like, fit's happened tae ye?

My friend: I've won! Money, a richt muckle sum o't, landed aa at eence intae the account, I'm still shaakin here!

Me: (a bit ower loud) Aw that's brilliant, that's affa brilliant for ye, honest tae God!

My friend: I ken, I still canna credit it masel.

Me: Fit's the scale o't then, gie's the actual number.

My friend: Seeven thoosand pound, near eneuch. I hinna seen that much in ma haun ever.

Me: Seeven thoosand... fit wye did that even come aboot, like? Foo does a thing like that jist happen tae ye?

My friend: A scratcher, if ye can credit it. Bocht it wi ma messages, near didna even look at it.

Me: Must be nice richt enough. Some o us are oot graftin the hale hairst for months and gettin nae mair than a sair back and a sma pey packet for wir trouble.

My friend: ...is there something wrang wi ye? Ye're spikkin awfa queer aboot it.

Me: (a lang meenit) Naw, naw, nothing wrang wi you, love. It's me. I'm jist... I'm affa jealous, if I'm honest wi ye. Real envy, nae mair than that, dinna fash yersel ower it.

My friend: Ach, I ken fine things hae been ticht for ye.

Me: Aye. But look at ye. Ye deserve a bit o gweed luck comin yer wye for eence. I'm richt pleased for ye, I really am, fae the hert this time.

My friend: Come here, gie's a bosie, ye daft mannie.

Me: Awa wi ye. But aye. Congratulations, richt enough.
