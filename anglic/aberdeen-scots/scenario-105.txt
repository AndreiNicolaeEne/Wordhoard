The man: Fit like?

The wife: No bad. Sit doon a meenit, A've somethin tae say tae ye. We're gaun on wir trip. The een we've aye spoken aboot, since afore we were mairit near eneuch. Iceland, the northern lichts, the hale jing bang. A thoosand pound, that's fit we've got for it, an no a penny mair.

The man: A thoosand. Richt. Fit wye a thoosand, fit wye no mair?

The wife: 'Cause a thoosand's fit's in the tin, chiel. We're no borrowin for a holiday, we're no daft.

The man: Fair eneuch, fair eneuch. Sae fit's first? The flicht, A'm thinkin, seein as that's the ane we canna dae withoot, whitever else we cut.

The wife: Aye, the flicht first. A wis lookin the ither nicht, it's comin in aboot fower hunner return, gin we gyang the richt week.

The man: Fower hunner. That's near hauf the hale thoosand awa afore we're even oot the hoose.

The wife: A ken, A ken, but there's nae ither wye there, is there, unless ye're thinkin o swimmin.

The man: Haud yer wheesht. Richt, fower hunner for the flicht, doon on the tally. Sae we've sax hunner left for aathing else, the bidin, the eatin, the lot.

The wife: Aye. Sae the question is foo mony days can we stretch that tae.

The man: Weel it depends fit we're spendin ilka day, disna it. Gin we're canny we could dae a fortnicht easy eneuch.

The wife: A fortnicht, on sax hunner, wi the bidin an aathing forbye? Ye're haverin. A wis thinkin mair like a week.

The man: A week's ower short for aa that flicht time, ye'd hardly land afore ye were awa hame again. We need mair days tae mak it worth the flicht.

The wife: True eneuch. Sae somewye atween a week an a fortnicht. Ten days?

The man: Ten days. A can live wi ten days.

The wife: Ten days it is then.

The man: Noo, there's een place we're no missin, whitever it costs. The Blue Lagoon. If we gyang aa that wye an dinna see it A'll never forgie masel.

The wife: Aye, A wisna gaun tae argue that een wi ye, that's the hale point o gaun. Sae we mak room for it somewye else.

The man: We could dae withoot a hotel, tak a hostel insteid, share a room, save a bit there.

The wife: Aye, that'll dae it. We're no gaun tae be sittin in the room onywye, we're gaun tae be oot lookin at the lichts an the meen.

The man: True eneuch. Twinty a nicht for the hostel, ower ten nichts, that's twa hunner.

The wife: Twa hunner for the bidin. An the Blue Lagoon itsel, that's aboot auchty pound the twa o's, wi the extras.

The man: Richt, pit it doon. Fower hunner for the flicht, twa hunner for the bidin, auchty for the Blue Lagoon. Fit's that come tae?

The wife: Sax hunner an auchty.

The man: Sax hunner an auchty, fae a thoosand. Three hunner an twinty left.

The wife: Noo, if we get the flicht cheaper, say fifty less, we could bide an eleebenth day. Jist a thocht, keep yer een on the flichts nearer the time.

The man: Aye, we'll see. Plan for ten the noo, an if a cheaper flicht comes up we'll think again aboot the eleebenth.

The wife: Fair eneuch. Richt, fit else. We'll need tae eat.

The man: Twinty a day atween the twa o's, that shid dae it fine, we're no eatin oot fancy every nicht.

The wife: Twinty a day, ower ten days, that's twa hunner.

The man: Twa hunner mair. Sax hunner an auchty, plus twa hunner, that's aicht hunner an auchty.

The wife: Aicht hunner an auchty. An we said a hunner set aside for onythin gyangs vrang, the caur breks doon, somebody loses their bag, fitever.

The man: Aye, ye canna gyang onywye withoot somethin gyangin vrang, that's jist the wye o't. A hunner for that.

The wife: A hunner for that, sae we're at nine hunner an auchty aaready, an we've no even spoken aboot onythin for wirsels yet.

The man: Aboot that. A wis thinkin, the last nicht, a richt gweed denner, the twa o's, nae worryin aboot the price. Fifty pound.

The wife: Fifty pound on een denner? We could save that fifty insteid, hae mair spare, or pit it towards the eleebenth day if the flicht comes in cheap.

The man: Aye, but fan div we ever get tae dae somethin like that, jist the twa o's, nae worryin? We're aye sayin we'll save it for later an later never comes.

The wife: A ken fit ye mean. Fine, the fifty stays in. We'll hae wir denner.

The man: Gweed. Sae fit's the hale tally the noo?

The wife: Nine hunner an auchty, plus the fifty, that's a thoosand an thirty.

The man: A thoosand an thirty. We're thirty ower.

The wife: Thirty short, aye. Somethin's got tae gyang.

The man: We could drap the caur hire. We didna really need it, the bus gyangs near aawye we're wantin, an it's affa dear onywye for fit little we'd use it.

The wife: Aye, drap the caur, that wis fifty. That pits us at nine hunner an auchty.

The man: Nine hunner an auchty, oot the thoosand. Twinty left ower.

The wife: Twinty left ower, no bad ava. We'll pit that towards a wee mindin or twa fan we're there.

The man: Aye. Ten days, the Blue Lagoon, the lichts, the meen, an twinty pound tae spare. No bad ava for a thoosand pound.

The wife: No bad ava. Noo dinna fash yersel ower it nae mair, we've done the sums, it's set. We're gaun.

The man: We're gaun. Aboot time an aa. Come here, gie's a bosie.
