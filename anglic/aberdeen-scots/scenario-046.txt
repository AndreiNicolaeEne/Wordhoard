Me: Fit like. Fit's the price on thae aipples the day?
The wifie: Twal for the batch, quine, gweed anes an aa.
Me: Twal? There's a bruised een sittin richt on tap. I'll gie ye echt, and tak the hail lot aff ye forbye.
The wifie: Echt's affa laich, I canna gyaan that far doon. Ten, and that's my last wird.
Me: Ten it is, aye, and thankit tae ye.
