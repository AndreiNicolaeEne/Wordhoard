Now fit like, ye'll be new aboot the place, so I'll jist tell ye this ance, plain, and ye can dae fit ye like wi it.

There's a chiel gyangs aboot - kens aabody's name inside the week, aye a smile on him, aye some deal on the go. Ye widna pick him oot in a crowd, looks jist like onybody's neebor. Freendly as onything, clap ye on the back, cry ye 'pal' or 'freend' afore he even kens yer ain name. First sign richt there. Naebody's that freendly that quick, no for nathin.

He'll speir early far dae ye bide, fit ye're deein here, fa ye ken aroon the toon. Jist bein sociable, ye'll think. He's takin yer measure.

Syne he'll come wi an offer - a room gyaan cheap, a van load o stuff half price cause his mate's clearin oot, a job that pays affa weel for nae much darg. Soonds affa gweed, disna it. Ower gweed tae be speirin questions aboot. That's the wye it works - if it soonds like ye're gettin the better o the bargain, ye're no.

Then he'll ask for a bit siller up front. Jist a deposit, jist tae haud yer place. Gie him that and ye'll niver clap een on him again. Awa, gone, like he niver stood there.

There wis a quine doon the stair fae me, new up fae the sooth like yersel, gied him three hunner for a flat that wisna even his tae gie. Grat aboot it for weeks after.

So mind: naebody that keen tae ken yer business, naebody that gweed a deal. And dinna fash yersel handin ower a penny tae onybody new asking for siller up front till ye've askit the wifie in the shop doon the road fit she kens o him first. She kens aathing that gyangs on here, and she'll pit ye richt afore ye lose a penny tae the vratch.
