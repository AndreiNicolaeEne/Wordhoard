The host: Fit like? Gie's a riddle, min.
The guest: Fit his an een but canna see, a tail ahin't, yet nivver gings a walk?
The host: A moose?
The guest: Nae, it's a needle, ye glaikit mannie.
The host: Aye, gweed ane. Ma turn, fit sits i the neuk affa het, gies ye a bosie o heat come the caul?
The guest: A cat?
The host: Nae, it's the fire.
The guest: Ye won that ane. Anither, quine?
