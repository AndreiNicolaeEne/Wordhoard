Me: Fit like the nicht? Ye're affa quiet there.

My mate: Jist thinkin, if the muckle win landed on's the morn, first thing I'd dee is buy a fitba park o ma ain. Nae mair stondin in the rain at the ropes wi aabody girnin.

Me: A fitba park? Ye're vrang in the heid, min. I'd get a hoose up by the hairst fields, a big steen biggin wi a gairden roond it, nae this shoebox I'm bidin in enoo.

My mate: Ay weel, that's fine an sensible, bit far's the fun in that? I'd hae a motor an aa, a richt fancy een, and tak some quine doon the road in it ilka Setterday.

Me: Ye'd hae it aa skint in a fortnicht, chiel. Ye need tae pit some by, intae the bank, for a rainy day. Dinna fash, I'm no sayin dinna enjoy it, jist keep some safe an aa.

My mate: Aye, aabody says that, bit fit's the eese o siller sittin deid in a bank? I've a better notion ,  we buy an island, jist the twa o's, wir ain sheep and neeps growin, bidin there aa the hairst lang, nae skweel, nae wark, jist us and the meen at nicht.

Me: An island? Ye canna even sail a dinghy withoot bein feart, ye'd be seek afore we cleared the harbour, ye widna last a day.

My mate: Aye, richt enough, that wis affa impractical. Fine then ,  hoose for you, motor for me, and the rest awa intae the bank, half an half, that dee ye?

Me: Aye, that'll dee fine. Fit wye we're sittin here plannin oot ilka penny o siller we'll niver see onywye is beyond me.

My mate: True eneuch. Back tae the claik then ,  fit's for yer tea?
