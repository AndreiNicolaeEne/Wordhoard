I hid them aa sit doon roon the table afore I said a wird, and I could see fine they kent somethin wis comin. Naebody wis takin a turn o their ain, they jist sat, the mither wi her hands foldit, the uncle wi his cap still on his knee, the eldest lookin fae me tae the faither and back, waitin.

Me: There's somethin I've keepit back a gey lang time noo, years mair nor I should hae, and it's time I said it. So I'm jist gonnae say it plain, and I'd ask ye tae let me get tae the end o it afore onybody spiks.

The uncle shiftit in his chair. Fit like, aa this for, he wis thinkin, I could see it on him, but naebody said it oot loud.

Me: Ye aa mind the fire in the byre, that hairst, lang syne. Ye mind fit it took fae us, the beasts we lost, the money we didna hae efter tae right the place, the twa parks we hid tae sell on. Ye mind the anger that come up in this hoose efter it, real anger, the kind that disna jist blaw ower. And ye mind fa got the blame for it.

I looked at the uncle when I said that, and he looked back at me steady, like a man that's been looked at that wye afore and learnt tae thole it.

Me: He wis telt he'd left the lamp burnin in the byre that nicht, that he'd been fu and careless, and naebody ever quite forgae him for it efter. He's cairrit that aa his days since, the chiel that couldna be trusted wi the beasts, said ahint his back at every merket and every funeral fae then tae noo.

The sister: We aa ken fit happened. Fit's the point in rakin it up the noo?

Me: The point is it wisna him. He didna dae it. I hae kent that the hale time, and I'm sorry, but I'm sayin it noo. It wis the faither.

There wis a silence then that I widna wish on onybody, a silence wi a weicht tae it, and I made mysel keep gaun afore it could swallow the room.

Me: I saw it. I wis a bit quinie, up in the hay laft that nicht, I wisna suppose tae be up there and I didna say so at the time either, but I saw the faither come in wi his pipe gaun, and I saw him drap the match, and I saw him look at it catch and no dae naethin aboot it, jist turn and gang oot the door. Naebody else wis near. It wis him, and it wis only ever him.

The eldest: That canna be richt. That's no fit happened, that's no fit we were telt.

Me: I ken fine fit ye were telt. I'm tellin ye fit I saw wi my ain een. I'm certain o it. I've been certain o it for near forty ear, and I'm sorry it's taen me this lang tae say so, but I am certain.

I could see the auld grievance risin up in the sister afore she'd even opened her mou tae let it oot, the wye her jaw gaed tight, the wye she wis aboot tae say somethin aboot aa the years she'd defendit the uncle tae folk that widna listen, and hoo it hidna been worth naethin if it wis true aa alang.

Me: I ken fit yer thinkin, that ye stood up for him and it wisna even in question, and that's no wasted, dinna think that for a meenit, that wis the richt thing tae dae the hale time, whether onybody else believed it or no.

I hidna telt onybody, no a soul, no even my ain man, aa this time. I wis telt it in confidence by naebody but my ain een, and I hae carried it since I wis a bairn, through my ain merriage, through the bairns I've hid mysel, through every merket day the uncle's name got said wi a bit sneer aside it. I mind aince, at his ain fiftieth, I near said it, I hid the wirds in my mou, and I lookit at the mither laughin and the hoose fu and I thocht, na, no the nicht, I'll no be the een that spiles this, and I swallowed it back doon and it sat there, sooer, for years mair efter that.

Me: I kept it aa this time for fear o openin up an anger that hid finally gaen quiet, and for the faither's sake tae, I'll no lee aboot that, and because I didna want tae be the een that caused mair hurt by spikin. But haudin it in has been nae easy load either. I've watched him cairry a reputation that wis never his tae cairry, and I've said naethin, and that's on me.

I lookit at the uncle then, and his face wis a hale mixter o things at aince, some relief in it, aye, ye could see the years fa awa aff him a wee bittie, but there wis a fresh anger risin up ahint that as weel, hard and cauld, at aa the ears he'd been made tae stand for somethin that wisna his tae answer for.

The uncle: Forty ear. Forty ear I've hid folk look at me sideweys ower this. Forty ear.

He didna shout it. That wis the waur pairt, he said it affa quiet, and it landit harder for that.

I lookit tae the faither. He hidna said a wird, he wis sittin wi his heid doon and his hands grippin the edge o the table, and when he did look up his face wisna a man denyin naethin, it wis a man that's kent this day wis comin his hale life and hid jist been hopin it widna.

The faither: Aye. It wis me.

Nae mair nor that fae him. Nae explainin, nae greetin, jist the twa wirds, and somehow that wis waur than a hale speech would hae been.

The anger that hid gaen quiet in this hoose forty ear syne come back up like it hidna been oot at aa, only noo it wisna aimed at the uncle, it wis aimed straucht at the faither, and the mither's face gaed fae disbelief tae somethin I'd nivver seen on her afore, a kind o cauld fury that she'd kept doon her hale merriage withoot kennin fit it wis kept for.

The mither: Aa these ears. Aa these ears I stood by ye.

The faither said naethin tae that either.

Me: I'm sorry. I should hae said this lang, lang afore the noo. There's nae excuse I can gie ye that maks it richt, I'm no gonnae try, I'm jist gonnae say it plain, I'm sorry for the years I let gang by wi this in me and said naethin, I'm sorry for fit that cost the uncle, and I'm sorry for makin ye aa sit wi it noo, cauld, when it should hae been said lang syne.

Naebody said it wis aa richt, and I didna expect them tae. It's no aa richt. We're sittin here the noo wi a truth that's forty ear late and nae less true for that, and it's no tidy, and it's no settled, there's a hale lot still tae be said atween the faither and the uncle and the mither that's no mine tae say for them. But we're staunin on it noo, the actual truth o it, insteid o roon aboot it the wye we hae been aa this time, and that's somethin, even if it's a sair somethin the nicht.
