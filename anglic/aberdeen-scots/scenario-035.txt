Me: Fit like? Ye ken fine the best stall doon the market's the burger wifie's, nae contest, disna even need sayin.

My pal: Awa an bile yer heid, the pakora chiel's twice as gweed, ye're haverin afore ye've even stertit.

Me: Haverin? I hid ma denner there last hairst an the burger wis affa fine, tatties crisp as onything, nae grease near it. Fit hiv you got tae say for that neep-heidit chiel doon the road?

My pal: I'll tell ye fit I've got tae say. Mind fin I got a poke o chips fae yer burger wifie? Stane cauld, like a steen aff the road, an the wifie hardly gied a look at us, jist claik wi the ither quine ahin the coonter the hale time.

Me: That wis wan time, dinna fash yersel aboot it. The pakora chiel gies ye a poke o nithin bit batter, nae meat tae be seen, it's aa a swick if ye ask me.

My pal: A swick? I'd tak his pakora ower yer burger a hunner times ower. It's the best in the hale toon, mebbe the hale country, naebody maks it better fae here tae London itsel.

Me: Fit? Yer heid's fair wrang, ye canna believe that yersel, ye great glaikit chiel.

My pal: I dae so.

Me: Weel fit aboot the fish loonie's van doon by? Naebody's ever said a bad wird aboot yon. Gweed fish, nae complaints fae onybody, aabody kens it, even you.

My pal: Aye, that's true eneuch, canna fault the fish loonie, he's affa gweed tae folk forbye.

Me: Richt, weel we'll jist hae tae gyang back an get baith again, yer pakora an ma burger, an settle it aince an for aa.

My pal: Aye awright, the morn then. We'll spik nae mair claik aboot it till we've tried them baith fair.

Me: Deal. Jist dinna greet like a feart bairn fin mines wins oot.

My pal: In yer dreams, chiel.
