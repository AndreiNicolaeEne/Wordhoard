Younger neighbour: Fit like? Affa caul the day, is it no.
The elderly neighbour: Aye, caulder than Tuesday, and yon wis bad eneuch.
Younger neighbour: Mm, aye, it wis that.
The elderly neighbour: Minds me on a hairst gey lang syne, ice on the steen dyke three mornins runnin, the neeps stiff in the grund. Jist the wye o't.
Younger neighbour: The morn'll be waur again, I'd say.
The elderly neighbour: Aye, waur, nae sign o't lettin up.
