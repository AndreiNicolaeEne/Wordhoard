The host: Come awa, get a good hansel o thae neeps intae ye, quine.
The guest: Ach, dinna fash yersel, nae the noo - I'm affa full, but yon tatties are richt gweed.
The host: Bit they're aff the hairst themsel, best neeps I've hid in years.
The guest: They look it tae, I'll gie them a try the morn, promise ye that.
The host: Aye, fair eneuch, the morn it is then.
