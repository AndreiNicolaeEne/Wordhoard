This wis the hairst tae end aa hairsts, I'm tellin ye. Fae early on ye could see it wisna gaun tae be like ony ither year, the parks were fair burstin, and by the time we got the coonts in it cam oot near double fit we'd get in a normal year. Aabody kent it afore the combines were even oot the shed.

But it wis the neeps that stood abeen aathing else. The park that gies us maybe eicht ton an acre in a gweed year gied us near saxteen this time, and the best afore that wis awa back some fifteen year syne, when there wis a park naebody's beaten since, till noo.

We hid tae fee in extra chiels jist tae get it aa lifted afore it turned, for wi a hairst like that ye canna bide aboot, it winna wait on ye. Fit wis ower and abeen fit we needed got stored in the sheds, and the rest we traded on tae fowk that were shorter that year.

The auld mannie that's been fairmin langer than onybody mind o, he stood at the edge o the park and said tae me:

The eldest: Fit like, I've seen affa near eichty hairsts noo, and I've nivver seen the likes o this ane. Nae in aa my days.

He put it doon tae the rain comin jist richt, nae ower much nae ower little, and a fine mild stretch aa through the growin season that nivver let up.

And that's fit fowk aroon here still spik aboot. Ony ither year that comes near, aabody says, aye, but is it as gweed as yon year? It's the year we measure aathing else against noo, and I dinna think that'll chynge onytime soon.
