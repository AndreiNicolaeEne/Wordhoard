Me: Ye winna believe't, the loonie sat doon last nicht an read the hale book, start tae finish, nae a word o help fae onybody.
Friend: Get awa, the same een that widna touch it a year syne?
Me: The very een. Gied up a dizzen times, near grat wi the fashin o't. The nicht up she stauns, prooder than I've seen her, "I did it aa masel." Gied her a muckle bosie, telt her I was affa prood.
