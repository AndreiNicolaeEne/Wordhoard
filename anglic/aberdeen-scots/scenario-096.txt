Aa the folk roon the grave gyaan quiet, waitin, nae a body sayin a word. Ma throat gings tight a saicont afore I can spik. Aabody here kens this aboot her, she'd gie ye her last penny, nae maitter fa ye were. Ma dear wifie, I caaed her, jist the once. Weel, that's you awa hame noo. Lettin the earth fa fae ma haun tae close it.
