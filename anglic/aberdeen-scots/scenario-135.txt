Ach, ya bloody, fit! Nae, nae, nae, that wisna me, that wisna, aa ower the fleer, smashed tae buggery, the vratch o a thing. Christ, feck the hale lot o't! ...Wheesht. Onybody hear that? Naebody aboot, jist me an the cat gawpin. Heh, hark at yersel, roarin like a bairn ower a bit o crockery. Sigh, doon on yer hunkers noo, pick up the bits, canna fash yersel ower spilt gweed.
