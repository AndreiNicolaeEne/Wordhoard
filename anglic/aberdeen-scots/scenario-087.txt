Workmate: Fit like wis yer nicht, then? I heard the claik ye wis up aa nicht wi the beastie.

Me: Affa lang, I'll tell ye that. It wisna richt aa yesterday tea-time, aff its feed and nae wint tae shift itsel, jist lyin there wi its heid doon and its een half shut. I kent strecht awa I wisna leavin it its lane the nicht, so I brought it ben aside the fire and made up a bed for it there.

First oor or twa, naething. Nae change ava, jist me sittin watchin it, ma hert in ma mooth ilka time it gied a shudder. There wisna hairst on, sae there wis nae ither soul aboot the place tae gie a haund, jist me and it. I tried gettin some watter intae it, coaxin it wi ma hand cause it widna tak it on its ain, jist dabbin roon its moo till it lickit a wee.

Aboot the middle o the nicht it took an affa bad turn, breathin aa vrang, shakin sae muckle I gied it a bosie tae try and haud it still. I'll nae lee tae ye, I sat there thinkin, hiv I deen the vrang thing here? Should I hae cairtit it oot tae the vet insteid o bidin here fashin ower it ma lane? But I couldna face takin it oot intae the cauld like that, sae I jist happit it up warmer and keepit spikkin tae it, low, the wye ye wid tae onything feart, tellin it dinna fash, dinna fash yersel.

There wis a gweed meen oot, and I mind thinkin its een wis shinin like twa steens in the licht fae it. Near daylicht I catcht the first sign, its breathin gaun steadier, and its lug gied a twitch at a soond the wye it aye did afore. By full mornin it wis stronger, nae richt yet, but a gweed bit steadier. Only aince I wis shair o that did I feel masel gyaan aa tae bits, deen in, that tired I couldna hae kent a neep fae a tattie.

Workmate: Puir chiel. Ye'll be needin yer bed the day, nae mistake.

Me: Aye. Nae argument fae me on that een.
