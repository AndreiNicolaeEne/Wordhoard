The eldest: Fit's that? Listen!
The loonie: It's rain, come see!
They aa rushed tae the windae, syne oot the door intae it.
The father: Aye! We were affa needin that.
The eldest flung her airms oot tae the sky, roarin at it tae come on doon.
The mother: Ye'll be drookit, ye daft quine.
The eldest: Drookit and gled o it!
Syne they aa stood, quate, jist listenin tae it fall.
