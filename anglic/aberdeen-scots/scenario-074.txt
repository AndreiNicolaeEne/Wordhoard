Me: Ye ken fit she's went an did noo? Cancelled on us again, half an oor afore we were tae meet an aa.

My friend: Vratch. Fit was the excuse this time?

Me: Said she wisna feelin weel, some heidache comin on.

My friend: Aye richt, a heidache. Mind the time afore that, she said her mither wisna weel an needed her at the hoose, an then she wis postin aboot bein oot wi some ither quine the same nicht.

Me: Dinna even. That fair got on ma wick.

My friend: An afore that again, fan we'd the table booked for her birthday near enough, an she jist didna show, nae word till the morn.

Me: So coont it up. That's the fourth time noo, is it no.

My friend: Aye, the fourth. I'm no jokin, I've been coontin.

Me: Fourth time. It's affa, so it is. Ye canna keep daein that tae folk.

My friend: D'ye think it's even genuine this time, or is she jist fashed tae come oot?

Me: Could be baith. But fower heidaches in a row, come on, that's ower muckle tae swallow.

My friend: I dinna ken, I feel a bittie bad even sayin it, she's no a bad chiel, jist... unreliable.

Me: Aye, but loyalty's twa wyes. We're aye there for her, she's never there for us.

My friend: True eneuch. So fit dae we dae, jist stop askin her?

Me: Naw, we ask her the same as aabody else. But if she cancels again, we're no waitin roond, we jist gyaan on athoot her.

My friend: Aye. Fair eneuch. Nae mair sittin in wonderin if she'll turn up.

Me: Exactly. Her loss if she disna come.

My friend: Richt, that's settled then.
