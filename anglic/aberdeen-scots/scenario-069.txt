Naebody's seen her since the Sunday nicht, doon by the back green. She's a wee grey cat wi white paws an ae bricht green ee. Affa shy wi strangers, she'll no come near ye, jist hide an waatch fae a safe distance. Gin ye spot her, dinna chase her, jist gie's a ring. There's a fair wee reward for whoever finds her. Number's on the strips below, tear ane aff.
