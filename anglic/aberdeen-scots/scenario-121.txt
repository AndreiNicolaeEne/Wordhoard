Neighbour: Fit like? Hear that racket oot on the street last nicht, efter midnicht?
The other: Aye, oot in the road, nae near either hoose.
Neighbour: Thocht it wis a beastie chappin ower somethin, but I'm nae sure.
The other: Could be, or some mannie stotterin hame fae the pub.
Neighbour: Affa loud for that, near feart me.
The other: If it comes the nicht, we'll keep an ear oot.
Neighbour: Aye, dinna fash, we'll suss it atween us.
