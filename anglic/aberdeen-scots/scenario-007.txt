The one coontin: Ickle ockle, black bockle, fit wye ye stan there gowkin, oot goes ye.
The loonie caught: That's vrang, ye gaed affa quick, ye skippit the wee quinie, dae it ower.
Another: Ye ken fine, argue an aabody stert the rhyme again fae the stert.
The one coontin, louder: Ickle ockle, black bockle, fit wye ye stan there gowkin, oot goes ye!
The quinie caught this time: Aye, dinna fash, that's me oot.
