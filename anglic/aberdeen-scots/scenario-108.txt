I ken, I ken, it's been months noo since I said I'd write, and here's the letter feenally gettin itsel written, months late and nae excuse for it barrin the ordinary yin, that I pit it aff and pit it aff till it turnt intae a muckle thing in ma heid, ower big tae stairt. So the hoose is quiet the nicht, the kettle's on, and I'm sittin doon tae dae it richt, the hale thing, no jist a scrat o a note.

Hoose news first, cause I ken that's fit ye'll want tae hear. Mind the gutter that's been hingin aff the back since afore ye left, wi the ivy growin intae it and the rone fu o muck? It's fixed. Feenally, efter aa this time. The chiel that did it took the hale day and chairged mair nor he said he wid, but it's up straucht noo and it disna drip intae the pantry ony mair, sae I'm no complainin ower muckle. Soon as that wis sortit, mind you, the back door stairtit stickin, sae noo ye hae tae pit yer shoother tae it afore it'll gie in, the frame's soakit wi the weet, they tell me, sae that's the neist thing on the list, whaniver we get roond tae it. And I pit in a raw o tatties doon whaur the aul shed wis, efter we pu'd it doon, and they've come on affa weel, better nor I thocht, sae we'll hae oor ain for the pot come the hairst if the slugs dinna get there afore us.

The muckle thing since ye went awa is oor cousin's flittit. Mind she wis aye sayin she'd nivver leave, that she'd nae mair thocht o gyaan sooth than flee'n tae the meen? Weel. There wis a sister post gaun at a hospital doon in Dundee, and she pit in for it mair for the practice o writin an application than onythin, nivver thinkin she'd hear back. They cried her for the interview within the wik, and she'd the job afore she'd time tae fash hersel ower whether she wantit it ava. She's doon there noo, in a flat wi a view o a roondaboot, she says, and soondin fine on the phone, though I ken fine she's greetin intae her pillae some nichts, same as onybody wid.

The meenister's still on the go, still no chipped.

I'll say nae mair on that, ye ken fit I mean.

The neighbours have shifted aboot an aa. The wifie next door, the aul yin that aye kept the front step scrubbit, she's intae a hame noo, her dochter cam and got her sortit efter the last fall, and there's a young couple in wi twa bairns that run up and doon the street on scooters aa hours, mair noise in a wik than that hoose's seen in twinty year. And doon at the corner, the lassie's had a bairn, a loonie, seeven pund summat, and the hale faimly's roond there aa the time noo, the grandmither included, sae there's aye a pram parkit ootside when ye gyang by.

Twa fowk are aye speirin efter ye. The mither, ilka time she's on the phone, first thing oot her mou afore even fit like:

The mother: Hiv ye heard fae her? Is she eatin richt? Is she cauld?

Me: She's fine, I tell her. She's aye fine.

The mother: Ye aye say that.

Me: Cause it's aye true.

And the wifie fae the shop, ye ken the yin, she aye asks an aa, though I dinna think she minds yer name richt, jist "yer sister that gaed awa" or "yer brither doon sooth," whichivver wye it comes oot, and then a hale claik aboot the price o things afore I can win oot the door.

There's a thing I'll write doon here that I widna say tae yer face, cause I'm nae guid at that sort o spik oot loud. I still gyang intae yer aul room some nichts and sit on the edge o the bed for a meenit, jist a meenit, I dinna richt ken fit for. It's no sad exactly. I jist dae it. I widna tell ye that ower the phone, but it gyangs doon fine in a letter, somewye, wi naebody watchin ma face while I write it.

The hairst wis a richt weet yin, ower muckle rain the hale back end, and the neeps doon at the field rotted in the grund afore onybody got them up, a richt sicht, aabody roond aboot fair scunnered wi it. But the simmer afore that wis affa fine, aabody oot in the gairden mair days nor no, sae it evens oot, I suppose, the wye it aye dis, ae season peyin for the neist.

Noo, questions, cause I hae a hantle o them and ye nivver answer half o fit I speir. Fit's the job like, richt enough, noo ye're a while intae it, is it fit ye thocht it wid be, or hiv they landit ye wi mair nor ye bargained for? Fit's the fowk like doon there, hae ye made ony proper freends yet, onybody ye'd cry a real freend and no jist somebody ye spik tae at the wark? And fit's the hoose like, or the flat, or fitivver it is ye're bidin in noo, is it warm eneuch, cause I mind fine ye aye felt the cauld affa quick, even here, even wi the fire on.

I'll close this the wye I aye dae, promisin tae send ye the thing I aye say I'll send and nivver dae. The tablet. I ken, I ken, I've said it every time I've written or phoned for a year noo, and it's nivver appeared, but this time I mean it, I'll hae it made and boxed and in the post afore the month's oot, and ye can haud me tae it, and gie me laldy doon the phone if it's no there.

Write back sooner nor I did. Or dinna, I ken fit ye're like an aa, aye the same. Jist ken ye're thocht on here mair nor ye'd credit, and there's a bosie waitin on ye whenever ye're hame tae get it.
