Left affa later than I meant tae, and by the time I wis oot the door it wis darker than I like it tae be, nae streetlichts on yet in ma heid though they were richt eneuch abeen me. Wisna far in when I got the sense there wis somebody ahin me, fitsteps landin near eneuch the same time as ma ain that ye couldna be sure. So I slowed doon aince, like I wis jist takin ma time, and the steps ahin me slowed an aa. Then I sped up a bittie, and them an aa, quick quick tae match. That wis eneuch for me. I widna gang doon the vennel I aye cut through, nae the nicht, so I took the lang wye roond by the road wi the lichts and the chip shop still open, mair fowk aboot even if it wis jist a chiel walkin his dog.

I let masel ae look back, jist the ane, and there wis naething I could say for certain either wye. Could hae been onybody. Could hae been naebody. But I wisna hingin aboot tae find oot, and the last bit hame I walked faster than I'd ever own tae, near runnin though I widna cry it that at the time. Got the door shut ahin me and stood there wi ma back tae it, fair peching, and it wis the plainest relief I've felt in a lang while.

Never did find oot fit it wis. Still dinna ken tae this day.

Workmate: So wis there onybody there ava, or wis it aa in yer heid?

Me: Fit dae I ken. I wis that sure in the moment, and noo I jist sound daft tellin it. Mibbe it wis naething. Mibbe it wisna. I'm nae gaun doon that vennel again either wye.
