Jist crossin the road, same as ony ither day, fan some chiel in a caur swerved in affa close wi nae warnin ava. My body jumped afore my mind kent fit wis happenin. Stood there a meenit, frozen, feart oot o masel, hert gaun like the clappers, close eneuch I swear I felt the air o it gyaan past ma face. Hame noo an I still canna spik richt, the hands juist winna stop shakin.
