The eldest: Mither, mither, the shed's afire, the shed's afire!
The mother: Fit?! Fa's fire, is the loon oot o it, foo did it start...
The eldest: I dinna ken, I jist seen the reek, affa thick, comin fae the back, an I, it wis, it wis roarin, the hale roof near gaun...
The mother: Richt. Richt. Jist... haud on a meenit.
The eldest: Aye. A meenit.
