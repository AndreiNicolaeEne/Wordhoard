Me: Fit like?

My friend: Aye, fine. Fit wye are ye lookin at me like that, like I've killt somebody?

Me: Cause I heard fit happened on the road last nicht. Ye nearly went intil the ditch again on that bike, nae helmet fastened richt, gaun God kens fit speed doon by the brig.

My friend: Ach, it wisna as bad as fowk's makkin oot. I had it aa under control.

Me: Under control? I wis staunin there fin the car cam roon the bend an ye swerved, an my hairt wis gaun like a hammer in my chest. My hands widna stop shakin efter, I couldna even haud my tea richt. Ye dinna ken fit it's like watchin that, feart oot yer wits fer somebody.

My friend: Ye're affa dramatic aboot it.

Me: Am I? Mind fin ye broke yer collarbone twa year back aff that same bike, near lost the use o yer airm athegither? That wisna nathing, that wis a warning, an ye didna listen tae it. Neist time it's nae a collarbone, it's yer neck, or yer heid, or ye're lyin on the roadside afore onybody gets til ye.

My friend: ...Aye, awright, ye've a point there.

Me: An fit dae ye think happens tae yer mither, sittin at hame waitin on a chap at the door that's the wrang kind o chap? Fit dae ye think happens tae me, haein tae staun at yer grave insteid o giein ye claik ower a pint on a Friday? I canna dae that. I'm no ready tae grieve ye ower a bike, dae ye hear me?

My friend: Aye. Aye, I hear ye. Dinna fash yersel, I'm no deif tae it, I've jist been a stubborn chiel aboot the hale thing.

Me: So?

My friend: So I'll slow doon. I'll get the helmet sortit richt, an I'll no be sic an eejit the morn as I wis last nicht. Canna promise ye I'll be perfect at it, but I'll be mair careful. That's aa I've got tae gie ye the noo.

Me: That'll dae, for a start. Jist dinna gie me a fricht like that again.
