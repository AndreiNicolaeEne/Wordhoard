Stranger: Fit like. Sorry tae fash ye, but is there a wee drappie o watter ye could spare? Affa het the day and ma throat's like a steen.

The wifie: Aye, nae bother, mannie, here's a fu cup for ye.

Stranger: Och, jist hauf wid dae, I dinna want tae tak yer aa.

The wifie: Wheesht, tak the lot, ye look fair deen in wi the heat.

Stranger: That's affa kind o ye. Thank ye, I mean that fae the hert.
