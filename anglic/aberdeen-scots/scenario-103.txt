The traveler: Excuse me, fit wye tae the mercat fae here?
The stranger: Aye, nae bother. Turn left at the first corner up by the auld pub, syne keep gaun till ye see the muckle grey kirk on the second corner, that's yer marker.
The traveler: An foo lang tae walk it, dae ye ken?
The stranger: Aboot fifteen meenits on fit, nae mair.
The traveler: Thanks affa much, that's richt kind o ye.
The stranger: Nae bother ava. Aa the best tae ye, mannie.
