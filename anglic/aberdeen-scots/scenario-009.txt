The eldest: Michty me, fit's that on yer heid?
The teased: Jist got it cut, it's the new spik.
The eldest: New spik? Ye look like a neep pulled half oot the hairst, aa hacked tae bits.
The teased: Aye, weel, better a neep than a mannie wi nae hair ava, ye affa cheek.
The eldest: Dinna fash, quinie, I'm only spikkin.
The teased: Spik on an I'll tell the mither fit ye said the day.
