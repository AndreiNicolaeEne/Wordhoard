Fit like, min. Ye missed yersel a sair day at the hairst, I'll tell ye that for naethin.

Startit afore the body wis onywye ready for it, half seven in the park and the dew still on the drills, and I wisna weel warmed up afore the gaffer had's aa on the riddle, pickin the stanes and the bad eens aff afore they gaed intae the box, in case onything gaed vrang and the hale load wis spilt for naethin. That wis the first job and it set the pace for aathing efter, ye couldna slack a meenit or the hale line wid back up ahint ye.

By aboot nine the hands wis complainin, aa scratched fae the stanes, and no lang efter that the back joined in an aa, that dull ache atween yer shooders fae bendin ower the box. We got a break aboot ten, quarter of an oor wi a flask, barely time tae sit doon nivver mind rest yer legs, syne it wis richt back tae it.

The efternoon wis the ill bit. Aboot three, wi the rain threatenin and the gaffer wantin the park deen afore it cam on, I wis affa near chuckin it and awa hame, I'll no lie tae ye, but ye jist dinna, ye ken foo it is.

Ae park went faster nor onybody thocht, mind, deen in nae time wi a young loonie fae doon the road giein's a haun, and the gaffer slipped me forty for it forbye.

Ten oors, start tae finish. The morn's either the same again or a day tae let the back mend, and dinna fash, I'm no carin fit wye it gyangs, jist gled this yin's deen.
