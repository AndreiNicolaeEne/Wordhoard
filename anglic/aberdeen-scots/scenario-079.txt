Richt, aabody, quiet a meenit. We're aa here the nicht for ma friend, so haud yer wheesht and listen. Noo, this ane's the kindest quine ye'll ever meet, aye first tae gie awa her ain, never a thocht for hersel. Mind yon nicht wi the neep and the burst tyre? Aye, that. Weel, quinie, health, happiness, and aa the gweed things - lang may yer lum reek. Table, raise yer gless and gie's a cheer!
