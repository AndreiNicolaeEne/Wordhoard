Cradle for sale, affa gweed yaised, did the wee mannie and the wee quinie near fower ear atween them. Nae hidin naethin: the ae rail gaed a bit o a crack and got mended, ye'll see the mark gin ye look close. Strap's some worn but hauds fine. Fifteen, teen as she stauns. Come the morn and hae a keek, or gie's a shout and cairry her awa yersel.
