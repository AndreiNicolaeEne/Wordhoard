Guest: That neep an tattie mash wi the mince, affa gweed, fit did ye pit in it gars it taste that wye?

Cook: Ach, jist a bittie magic, nae mair tae it.

Guest: Come awa noo, dinna fash yersel keepin secrets fae a hungry chiel, fit like, fit's the trick?

Cook: Weel... there's a hurl o nutmeg gings in. Rest o it bides wi me.
