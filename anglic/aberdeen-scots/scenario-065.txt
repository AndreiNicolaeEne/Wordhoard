The neighbour: Yer beastie's oot, affa quick doon by the dyke, heidin straight for the road!

The other: Oh vrang, fit wye did it get oot?

The neighbour: Gap in the fence doon by the park. Rin an heid it aff afore it reaches the road, I'll bide here an block the gap.

The other: Richt, I'll gyang doon the loanin masel, you haud the gap, dinna let it throu again.

The neighbour: Aye, gyang fast, mind yersel.
