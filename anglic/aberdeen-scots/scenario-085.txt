Three o them sittin thegither, cups gaun cauld, and the claik turns, the same as ilka time, tae fit's sair on them.

The first: Fit like, the pair o ye? Ma knees is fair vrang the day, nippin somethin affa. Fifteen year this his been at me noo, since I wis still on ma feet aa day at the fish hoose. Canna get doon tae the gairden athoot a groan comin oot o me, and canna get hame frae the shops athoot a rest on the wye.

The second: Fifteen year, is that aa, quine? Dinna fash yersel wi that, try haein this back o mine. Three, fower nichts a month I'm up and doon like a flea in a bottle, widna sleep a wink for the sair o it, lyin waukin tae the meen's up and the birds startin. The bed's hard as a steen forbye, disna help.

The third: Aye weel, the pair o ye dinna ken fit sair is. This chist o mine's been at me since afore either o ye kent me, since I wis a loonie rinnin the hairst park barfit, afore I'd even left the skweel. Langer than yer merriages, the baith o ye.

The first: Fit wye dae ye aye think ye're waur aff than me, ye auld chiel? Gyang on then, fa's got it warst?

The second: No me that's backin doon, mannie. Ma back's the vratch o the hale toon, feart tae even lauch in case it gings.

The third: Aabody roon this table thinks they're the warst aff. Aathing's a competition wi you twa.

Nane o them gied an inch, no ane, and they sat there aa three, lauchin thegither ower a fresh cup, nae nearer an answer than they'd ever been.
