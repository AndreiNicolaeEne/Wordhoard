The eldest: Richt, we'll jist dee this ane box at a time, nae need for onythin tae get atween us.

The younger: Fit's happenin tae the clock, though. The wee steen ane aff the mantel.

The eldest: Fit dae ye mean fit's happenin tae it, it's gaun in the box wi the rest.

The younger: She telt me yon clock wis mine. Afore she gaed intae the hame, she said it tae ma face, "that's for you, quine."

The eldest: She niver said nathin o the kind tae me, and I wis here mair days nor no. I'd hae kent.

The younger: Weel ye werena there the day she said it, wis ye, sae ye widna ken.

The eldest: Dinna start wi me.

The younger: And fit aboot the three thoosand oot the account? Ye'd taen a fair bit o that afore ony o us even kent she wis awa.

The eldest: I took fit I wis awed for runnin her tae the doctor's a hunner times in the hairst, ye canna caa that takin fae onybody.

The younger: Aye, that's fit ye aye say. Aabody kens fit ye did.

The eldest:. I jist wintit her tae say it tae me aince. That I mattered as muckle as you did tae her. That's aa this is, and it's no aboot a clock.

The younger: Aye. I ken.

Her man: Right, that's aneuch noo, the pair o ye, sit doon. She's no cauld in the grund and here's you twa at it like this.

The eldest: ...

The younger: We'll leave it. We'll leave it the noo.

The eldest: Aye. Leave it.

Neither o them said onything for a fair meenit efter that, jist sat there feelin foolish and sair baith at aince, kennin fine hou it soonit, fechtin ower her things wi her jist gone.
