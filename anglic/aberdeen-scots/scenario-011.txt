The grandmother: Haud on, quinie, dinna pit thae sheen up on the table.

Me: Fit wye? Fit happens if I jist dae it onywye?

The grandmother: It's a daith comes tae the hoose afore the year's oot. Affa true, whether ye believe it or nae.

Me: Aye richt, fit dae I dae then?

The grandmother: Chap the table three times wi yer knuckle richt efter, and it's undone.

Me: rolled her een, chappit it onywye, jist in case.
