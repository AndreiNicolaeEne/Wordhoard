Richt, I'll tell ye fit happened, this is a true story mind. I needed a paper stamped for the caur, some form for the licence, and it hid tae be in that day or the hale thing wid lapse and I'd be back tae square een, weeks o waitin again. So in I gyang tae the office, quine at the front sends me through tae this mannie ahin a desk, aal and dour lookin, and I hand him the form.

He's leafin through it, affa slow, and he says, oh this is a busy time o year fer us, affa lot o forms tae get through the day. And he jist leaves it hingin there, looking at me ower his glesses. I ken fine fit he's efter, but I jist say, aye, must be affa thrang for ye, and I say naethin mair, jist stand there like a chiel that disna understand.

He tries again efter a meenit, says, some folk fin a wye tae mak things gyang a bittie quicker, if ye ken fit I mean. I says, oh I dinna ken foo that wid work, is there a form for that? And I keep my face gey straucht.

There wis a lang pause there, richt enough, ye could feel it in the air, him waitin tae see wid I bite. I says tae him, I'll jist wait my turn like aabody else, and onywey I ken the wifie that rins the ither office doon the road, she'll sort it if this yin canna. That did it. He stamped the paper, nae mair said, handed it back ower like naethin had happened.

I got it dane, and nae a penny paid oot forbye fit I wis due. That's the wye ye deal wi thon kind, ye jist dinna gie them the openin.
