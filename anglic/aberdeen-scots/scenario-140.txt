Me: Thank ye. Thank ye, I dinna even ken fit tae say, that's nae eneuch, no for that.
The rescuer: Ach, wheesht noo, dinna fash yersel, it wis nithing.
Me: (shivering) God, I wis that affa near it, I wis that near--
The rescuer: Aye, weel. Ye're oot noo.
Me: I mean it though, I dae, there's nae words big eneuch, ye've nae idea fit that--
The rescuer: Ach awa wi ye, ony chiel wid hae dane the same, stop yer greetin and get hame.
