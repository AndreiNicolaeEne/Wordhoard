Me: Fit wye can ye nae see that wis a stanin free-kick? The linesman wis lookin richt at it an still gied nithing.

The workmate: Awa an bile yer heid, it wisna even close. The chiel gaed doon ower easy, the ref made the richt cry there.

Me: The richt cry? He's been vrang the hale gemme, mind that een in the first half fan their striker wis clean offside an the flag nivver went up?

The workmate: Aye, but mind oor ain een an aa, fan the ba caught yer loon's airm in the box an naebody said a wird? Ye didna complain aboot that een.

Me: That's different, that wisna even a shout.

The workmate: It wis a shout tae onybody wi een in their heid, ye jist didna care cause it gaed your wye.

Me: Weel the hale performance wis affa, missed calls fae stert tae feenish, bookins that shoulda been reids, corners gien as goal kicks. The chiel's nae fit tae ref a bairns' gemme, he wis feart tae gie a card the hale efternoon.

The workmate: My brither-in-law wis sayin efter, said it wis six o een an half a dizzen o the ither, said neither side could complain ower much.

Me: Aye weel he's vrang an aa, that kine o middle-o-the-road claik convinces naebody.

The workmate: It convinces me fine.

Me: Ye wid say that, ye're jist takin his side cause it suits ye.

The workmate: I'm nae takin naebody's side, I'm jist sayin that een cry wisna the robbery ye're makin it oot tae be.

Me: Fit like can ye defend him fin he's been shooglie the hale efternoon?

The workmate: Cause the ba's roon an it disna aye faa yer wye, dinna fash yersel ower it.

Me: I'm nae fashin, I'm jist richt.

The workmate: Weel richt or nae, refs the day are aa the same, useless gowks the lot o them, nae a een amang them's fit tae haud a whistle.

Me: Aye. Useless, the hale lot o them.

The workmate: Aye. We'll be at this again the morn an aa, nae doot.

Me: Nae doot at aa.
