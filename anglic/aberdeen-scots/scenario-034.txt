The wifie at the stall kens fine fa's comin up the road afore they've even won the length o the counter - same waalk, same jaiket, same time near eneuch ilka day.

The owner: Aye aye, fit like the day? Roll on square sausage, broon sauce, tea nae sugar - that's you sorted, is it?

The customer: Aye, ye ken me affa weel by noo. Gie's broon AND red the day though, I'm feelin adventurous.

The owner: Adventurous! First time in months ye've said onything different fae that order. Fit's brocht this on, then?

The customer: Ach naething muckle, jist fancied a change for aince. Foo's the business been gaun onywye? Gey quiet oot there the day.

The owner: Could be waur, could be better. The mornin trade wis daecent eneuch bit fowk's nae spendin like they eesed tae, nae since the hairst wis in. Ye're ma best customer aa the same though - fit is it noo, fower times a week?

The customer: Five, if ye coont the Setterday.

The owner: Five! Ye'll be needin yer ain steen tae sit on suin, at this rate.

The customer: Dinna fash yersel, I'll jist bide here athegither, save ye the bother o makin it fresh ilka time.

The owner: Awa ye go, ye chiel. Foo's the faimly keepin? Yer mither still gien ye claik aboot yon job?

The customer: Aye, ilka Sunday withoot fail. She's nae blate aboot it either, I'm telt.

The owner: That's mithers for ye, aabody's the same. Richt, here's yer roll noo, mind it's het.

The customer: Foo much the day?

The owner: Same as ever, three pound twenty.

The customer: There ye go. Cheerio then, see ye the morn nae doot.

The owner: Aye, nae doot aboot it. Tak care o yersel, quinie.
