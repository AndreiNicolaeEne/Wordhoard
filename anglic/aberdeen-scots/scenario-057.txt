The tenant: Fit like?

The landlord: No bad ava, come awa in, sit yersel doon. Affa dreich oot there the day.

The tenant: It is that, rain since the back o six. Foo's the wife keepin?

The landlord: Aye, fine, fine, thrang wi the grandwean maist days. And yersel, ye still up at the skweel?

The tenant: Aye, same as ever, nae complaints there. Onywye, I widna keep ye lang, I ken ye're a busy chiel. It's the rent I wintit a wird aboot.

The landlord: Oh aye?

The tenant: Aye. I've peyed ye on the dot every month for three ear noo, never missed, never late, and I've said naethin aboot yon windae in the back room that's nivver shut richt since I moved in. But wi the price o aathing the noo, I canna keep on at fit it is.

The landlord: I hear ye, but I canna jist drap it, I've costs o ma ain, the roof, the rates, aa that.

The tenant: I wisna askin ye tae drap it a lot, jist a fifth aff fit I'm peyin the noo. Seems fair, gien I've nivver gied ye a day's fash.

The landlord: A fifth's an affa lot, mind. I could manage ye a tenth, nae mair than that, times bein fit they are for aabody.

The tenant: A tenth's gey little for three ear o payin on time and a windae that lets in the hairst wind.

The landlord: Fair eneuch, fair eneuch. Look, I'll no promise ye the noo, but I'll think on it ower the week and gie ye a proper answer come the morn's morn. And I'll get somebody in aboot that windae forbye.

The tenant: That'll dae fine. Thankit tae ye.

The landlord: Nae bother. Mind and shut the door, it's snell oot there.
