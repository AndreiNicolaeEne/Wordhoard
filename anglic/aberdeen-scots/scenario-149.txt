The eldest: Gweed be on this hoose ,  I sweer it afore aabody, the bairn'll hae a lang, walthy life, gweed hairst ilka year and a warm hame, this I sweer, sure's the meen comes up. Noo the ither side o't, sworn the same wye: gin ye wrang me, yer days'll gang aa vrang, yer skweel and yer wark baith fail ye, and ye'll bide feart and alane, this I sweer. Baith are sworn thegither, and baith will haud.
