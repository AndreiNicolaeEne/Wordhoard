Richt, I'll tell ye fit happened, cause it's still gie fresh in ma heid. It wis early efternoon fan I first saw the reek, jist a grey smudge awa on the horizon, nae mair than that. I mind stannin at the winda thinkin, aye, that's some fire, wonder far aboot. Ye jist watch it, ken, cause it's affa far awa still.

Bit within the oor the win got up, and it wis pushin the reek richt this wye. Ma een startit stingin afore I'd even smelled onything. I phoned the neebor.

Neebor: Fit like? Ye seen the reek?

Me: Aye, it's comin this wye affa fast, I dinna like the look o it.

That wis the meenit watchin turnt tae leavin. Naething mair tae say aboot it, jist up and awa. I wis oot the door in aboot twenty meenits, only fit mattered, the wee dog, the box wi the photos in't, ma phone and chairger. Didna even lock the back door richt.

Ma throat wis raw by the time I wis in the caur, the heat pressin on ma skin like an oven door left open. I lookit back eence, jist eence, and the licht o it against the sky wis somethin I widna forget, aa orange and roarin, and I thocht, please dinna tak the hoose, please.

Twa nichts I wis awa, bidin wi ma sister, no kennin a thing, jist waitin on the news and fashin masel seeck. Sister: Ye'll ken suin enough, dinna torture yersel. Easy said.

Fan they let us back the hoose wis stannin, aabody's wis, spared, the fire had turnt afore it got tae us. I jist stood there greetin like a bairn, affa relieved, affa tired.
