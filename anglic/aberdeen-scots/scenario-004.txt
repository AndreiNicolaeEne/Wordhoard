Wheesht wheesht, my wee quinie, fit's aa this fyke the nicht,
nae mair greetin noo, I'm richt here aside ye, I'm no gyaun awa.
The meen sits saft abeen the hairst park, blinkin in tae mind ye sleep,
an I'll bide wi ye aa the nicht through, till the morn comes creepin in.
Wheesht wheesht, my ain wee quinie, the meen's abeen us still,
sleep noo, sleep noo, my lammie, let the fyke gang doon.
