Mither, ye're gone, and the hame's affa teem wi'oot ye. Ye're gone, mither, aa gone. I mind ye at the fire wi the neeps on the boil, spikkin the aul spik, lauchin fit the loonies got up tae. Gone, gone, ma ain wifie o the hoose. Naebody tae fash ower us, naebody tae gie's a bosie afore bed. Nae mair. Gone, mither, aa gone. Bide a meenit langer in ma mind, and lat ye lie easy, awa hame.
