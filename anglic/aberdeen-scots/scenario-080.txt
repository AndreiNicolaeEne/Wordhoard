Me: Fit like! Whit's that ye've got there?
The friend: Jist got it, look, canna haud masel, it's affa braw!
Me: Michty me, that's some thing, afore ye've even shown me it richt! Fit did it cost ye?
The friend: Twa hunner, near eneuch, nae messin.
Me: Foo did ye manage that, then?
The friend: Saved ilka penny since the hairst, proud o masel.
Me: Ye lucky chiel, I'm affa jealous, nae word o a lee.
