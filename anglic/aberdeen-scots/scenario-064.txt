Fit like, ye aa richt? Jist a wee note tae say fit a rare night ye hid on ,  the hoose wis affa lively, near three the morn afore it settled doon, no the first time this year mind ye, ken foo it is. Nae bother ava, jist gie's a chance tae haud it doon efter midnight neist time, easy deen. Aa the best tae ye baith, take care o yersel.
