The injured: Ah! Ma hand, it's affa sair!
The other: (running) Haud still, loon, press this hard.
The injured: It winna stop.
The other: Up abeen yer hairt. Dinna fash yersel.
The injured: (shaky) Like this?
The other: Aye, jist that. Bide thegither.
The injured: It's slowin.
The other: Gweed. Aamost done, quine.
