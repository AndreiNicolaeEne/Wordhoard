The mother pulls the loon in for a bosie, haudin on a meenit afore she lets go.

The mother: Richt. Tak care o yersel on that affa lang road, dinna fash yersel, mind.

The mother: Send's word the verra meenit ye land, ken.

She pits a wee packet o pieces intae his hands afore he can turn fur the door.

The mother: Here, pit that awa in yer pooch, ye'll be needin it afore ye win onywhere.

The loon: Aye, my ain mither, I will that. And I'll bring ye back somethin fae ower yonder afore lang.
