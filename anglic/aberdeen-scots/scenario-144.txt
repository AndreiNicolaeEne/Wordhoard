The grandmother: Sit doon, quine. I ken fine fit's adae, ye're fair doon in the mou since ye got yer jotters. Trouble's like a steen in yer shoe, quine: affa sma, but it maks ilka step sair. Sae shak the steen oot an waak on; dinna cairry it a mile mair than ye maun. Time's a great mend. An that's the hale o it: ye're nae yer trouble, it'll pass, an sae will ye.
