I'm on the porch round early afternoon and I catch this little smudge of smoke way off on the horizon, look like somebody burning leaves or something, ain't thinking nothing of it yet. Sky still blue everywhere else. Ion even say nothing to nobody at first, just watching it sit out there. Give it a hour though and that wind done picked up and pushed all that smoke in close, close enough my eyes start stinging and watering up like I'm cutting onions in the kitchen. That's the moment it stopped being a thing to watch and turned into a thing I'm finna deal with.

My auntie call me up:

The auntie: You smell that? It's a lot of smoke out here now, more than it was.
Me: I been smelling it for a minute. Finna pack up right now, ion like how close it look.
The auntie: Don't nobody know how bad it's gone get. Better you go on and move.

I ain't play with it after that. Grabbed what mattered, the box with the pictures, papers, charger, wasn't studying nothing else, and I was out that place in under twenty minutes flat. My throat done gone raw before I even made it to the car good, and the heat pressing up on my skin the whole way out, like something breathing on you from behind. I look back one time getting clear and see that light glowing up orange against the sky, whole horizon lit up wrong.

Stayed with mama'n'em two whole nights, don't nobody sleeping right, everybody just on they phone the whole time, calling round asking who all made it out and what all got left behind, talmbout is it gone reach this side or that side. Been sitting there stressed, not knowing if the place still standing or if it's gone for good.

Third day they finally let us go back through, and it's still there. Ain't touch it, spared clean, everything just how I left it. I stood out in the yard a minute just breathing, ain't say nothing to nobody, just glad.
