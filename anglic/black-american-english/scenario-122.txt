The young one: Tell it. Go on and tell the one about what be callin out past the bend when it get dark.

Me: It always come up the same way. Somebody new to the place, ain't been here but a summer or two, done heard the old folks say don't be walkin down past the bend after dark, and they want to know why, like it's some rule somebody made up just to keep the young ones scared and inside. It ain't that. It never was that. It come up late, usually, nine, ten o'clock, everybody done ate, porch light on and the bugs beatin theyself up against it, and that's when it be true, right round then, cause that's when the ones who know start talkin low instead of loud.

Won't nobody tell you they seen it. Not straight out, not lookin you dead in the eye and sayin I seen a shape stood there. What they tell you is they felt it before anything else had a chance to be felt, felt the air go different, felt they own name comin at em out the dark before it ever had a face to go with it. That's how it come, if it come at all. It take a voice long, long before it take any kind of shape.

And the voice ain't no stranger voice, that's the part that get people every time. It sound just like whoever you'd come runnin for. Mama'n'em voice, or a sister's, or somebody long gone you still catch yourself listenin for some nights. It call you by your own name in that voice so you don't even think, you just move, and 'fore you know it you finna be three steps off the road and headed for the trees. That's the whole point of it. It ain't tryna be seen. It's tryna draw you off the path and off away from anybody could reach you if you needed reachin.

Now it don't come whenever it want. Ion care what nobody else tell you 'bout seein things at dusk or hearin things at noon, this here only come after midnight, and only on a night ain't got no moon in it at all. Any light up in that sky and it stay quiet, stay still as anything. That's just how it is, been how it is.

Round here it's the bend down past where the old road curve by the water, where the crick run up under the low part of it and the trees lean all the way over like they tryna keep the light out theyself. I ain't gon tell you it's every bend like that everywhere, cause it ain't. It's just that one. Ours. It don't belong to nothin bigger than right here.

I first heard it from my own granddaddy, settin right where I'm settin now, and who all was there with us I couldn't even tell you no more, just that it was hot and the fan was goin and he told it plain, told it like he was tellin you what time the store closed. Wasn't no laugh in it nowhere. Now some of em tell it grinnin the whole way through, so you can't hardly tell if they playin with you or not, but he ain't laugh, not once. That's how I knew to believe him before I believed anybody else who ever told it to me after.

First thing anybody show you for proof is that spot right at the bend where don't nothing grow. Ain't no grass there, ain't even weeds, and weeds'll grow anywhere they get half a chance. Somebody done tried plantin somethin there more than once, just to see, and it either come up wrong or it don't come up at all, and can't nobody tell you why. They just walk you out there and let you look at the dirt your own self.

Second thing, and this the kind you always hear from somebody who heard it from somebody else, is that a man drivin through late one night thought he seen somebody walkin in the road right at the bend, and pulled over to check on em, and when he got out wasn't nobody there. Just his own headlights and the crick runnin under the road like always. Ion know who that man was myself. Wasn't nobody I ever met could say for sure they was him. But everybody know somebody who know him.

But the one they tell start to finish, every single time, is what happened to a cousin of mine, years back now. Comin home late, walkin, cause wasn't but the one road out that way then, and heard they own name called clear as day, off in the trees, in a voice they'd have known anywhere in this world. And they started toward it. Three, four steps off the road before somethin in em just stopped, turned em back around, and to this day they can't tell you what it was that stopped em. Just that somethin did, and it did it in time.

And they swear to it, same words every time, we done heard it told a hundred times and it don't change on us: wasn't nobody out there could've called em like that, sound just like they own mama's voice, and it knew they name the same way they own mama know it. That's it, word for word, that's how it's always told in this family and it ain't never gon change.

Now the part that make even the ones who don't believe none of it go quiet for a second is that wasn't a soul missin from home that night, everybody was accounted for, every last one, and it still knew the name and still knew the voice to use with it. But the part that gives em they opening to laugh it off anyway is that it was dark, real dark, no moon like I already told you, and can't nobody swear on what they hear clean and clear when they already half spooked walkin home by theyself that late. Here go somebody talmbout it was just an owl, or the wind comin off the water funny. Let em say it. Don't cost me nothin.

Now I ain't gon lie to you, it don't come out the same from every mouth round here. Some tell it soft, more like a warnin than a scare, somethin you tell a child so they don't wander. Some tell it hard enough to make a grown man take the long way round on a Friday night. Depend who raised you, and depend what they was tryna teach you with it. I never once minded the difference. Truth got room in it for more than one voice tellin it.

But here what we still do, and it ain't hardly even a thing no more, it's just how we live out here: don't nobody round here answer to they name bein called from somewhere dark til they done looked with they own two eyes and seen who it is doin the callin. Don't matter how much it sound like somebody you love. You look first. Always look first.

And I'll tell you the truth, since you asked me for it 'stead of just the story: I don't believe it. Not all the way, not settin here in the light with you, fan goin, everything plain as day. But I ain't never broke that rule neither. Not one time in my whole life. Ion care to be the one who finds out.
