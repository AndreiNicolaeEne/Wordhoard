So my cousin and them people she work with asked could I stay at they house a whole week while they went up to see the wife family for some reunion thing, and I said yeah, easy money, house sitting ain't nothing but watching TV in a house that ain't mine. I ain't know what I was walking into. Seven days. Let me tell it from the top cause by the end of that week I was a different person, I'm not even playing.

First night already felt off and I couldn't tell you why. House too quiet, then not quiet enough, the ice maker going off in the kitchen like somebody knocking, and it's a floorboard by the hall closet that pop every time the AC cut on. I ain't scary but I ain't finna lie, I slept with the hall light on. Kept telling myself it be like that in a house you don't know, everything sound different, every house got they own voice and I just ain't learned this one yet.

Second day the dog got out. I'm talmbout I opened that back door to get the trash can and that fool shot between my legs like he been planning it all week. I'm out there in the yard in my socks hollering his name, neighbor two doors down come out on her porch just watching, ain't offer to help or nothing, just stood there like it's a show.

Neighbor: Ooh, he got out again?

Me: Again? This the first time since I been here!

Neighbor: Mm-hm. He stay doing that.

Took me near about an hour, chased him clean around the block twice before he got tired his own self and let me scoop him up by the collar. After that I started double checking that door like my life depend on it.

Round about Wednesday I noticed the plants on the back porch looking some kind of way. Wasn't all at once, it crept up on me, first the one droopy, then a couple leaves gone yellow, then the whole row looking tired and sad. I'm out there every day giving them water so I couldn't figure what the problem was, turned out I was drowning them, roots sitting in water cause them pots ain't have proper drainage and I ain't check. By the time I caught it half of it was already gone too far to save. I felt terrible, real terrible, cause that's they tomatoes they been nursing since spring.

Then Thursday the garbage disposal went out while I'm standing right there doing dishes. Just this grinding sound, then a burning smell, then nothing. Dead. Under my watch. I'm calling my sister like...

Me: Girl what I'm finna do, this ain't even my house!

My sister: Did you push the little red button under there? The reset one?

Me: What reset button?

My sister: Ion know, google it.

Turned out that's exactly what it was, a reset button up under the sink I ain't even know existed, but I ain't find that out till two days later, so for a minute I was walking round that house like a criminal.

Same week, out of nowhere, somebody knock on the door and it's the husband brother, just standing there with a duffel bag talking bout he in town for work and figured he'd crash a couple nights since his brother house closer to the airport than his hotel. Wasn't no call, no text, nothing, just showed up.

The brother: Hey, you must be watching the place. He ain't tell you I was coming through?

Me: Naw. Naw he ain't say nothing to me about that.

The brother: That's my brother for you. What all he tell you about me?

Me: Nothing! I ain't even know it was a you to tell me about.

He was cool though, stayed two nights, kept asking bout mama'n'em back home like we go way back, ate half the leftovers and left, no harm done, but my nerves was shot that whole visit cause don't nobody tell you a stranger finna be sleeping in the guest room when you the one responsible for the house.

Now the scare. Friday I'm cleaning up and I go to put back this little jewelry box off the dresser and it ain't there. Just gone. I'm turning that room upside down, checking under the bed, in the drawers, my heart racing cause I'm thinking this the one thing I done lost for real, how I'm finna explain that. I done called my cousin near about crying.

Me: I can't find the wife jewelry box, it was right here on the dresser, I swear it was.

My cousin: You check the bathroom? She be moving stuff in there to do her face.

And sure enough it was sitting on the bathroom counter the whole time, she must've had it in there before they left and I ain't never looked. I sat down on the edge that tub and just breathed for a good five minutes.

Saturday I run to the store right quick, don't even grab my key off the counter cause I'm thinking I'll be back in fifteen minutes, and that door lock behind me. Stood out there on that porch a good three, four hours waiting on a spare from the neighbor who wasn't even home either, had to wait on her to get off work. Hot as it was too. I called everybody I know just to have something to do.

That same stretch it rained one whole afternoon and I ain't think about the window I cracked open in the back bedroom that morning for some air. Come home from being locked out to a soaked carpet and the sill all wet, water done pooled right there by the baseboard. Spent that whole evening with towels down trying to dry it out before it could mold or nothing.

Then it's a noise in the night, had to be like two in the morning, sound like something knocking round in the kitchen. I'm laying there like I ain't hear it, then it happen again and I know I got to go look, so I creep down there with my phone flashlight and a broom like a broom finna save me from anything, and it's the dog, he done knocked a cup off the counter chasing after a roach or something. Scared me half to death for nothing.

By Sunday, day six, I was tired of that disposal acting up every other day, so I finally sat down and looked up the exact model online, found the reset button my sister was talking about, held it in, ran some ice cubes through it like the video said, and that thing ain't give me one more bit of trouble after that. Should've done it day one honestly.

Last day, Monday, I'm up at six in the morning going through that house like a whole crew. Vacuuming, wiping counters, fluffing them cushions back up how they was, moved what plants I could save back to the front where they get better light, hid the ones that ain't make it in the trash can outside so they ain't gotta see it right off. Wasn't nobody finna know all what went down in there if I could help it.

When they pulled up that evening I met them at the door all smiles.

The wife: How was everything? Y'all good?

Me: It was quiet. Real quiet. The dog was a angel.

The husband: Plants look a little thin.

Me: It's been hot, I been watering em good though.

They ain't press it too hard, just glad to be home, and I ain't tell them about the lockout or the flood in the back room or the jewelry box scare or none of that, cause what them people don't know won't hurt em, and I done learned my lesson about house sitting regardless. Seven days felt like a whole month. I'm still tired thinking about it.
