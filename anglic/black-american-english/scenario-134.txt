This the year mama'n'em still be talmbout. Wasn't even hard to tell early neither, the plants ain't wait around to show you, they just came in doing damn near double what they usually give, and everybody out there was like, oh, this the one.

The okra was what really did it though. Okra be giving a little something most years, don't get me wrong, but this go round it was ridiculous, bushel after bushel, way past what it normally do. Past even the year the fence line came down, and that was s'posed to be the best one before this ever touched it.

Had to call in extra hands just to keep up with it, cousins who don't even garden regular, folks from down the street who ain't picked nothing in they life. Everybody out there before it could turn on the vine. Whatever ain't get et right then or put up got shared out, some went in the freezer, some got canned, some just traded off to whoever ain't have none that year. Wasn't nobody round there going without, not that year.

The eldest grower out there, she been doing this since she was knee high to something, and she said she done seen a lot of harvests but ain't never seen nothing like this one in her whole memory.

Me: What all you crediting it to, for real?

The eldest grower: Rain, baby. Came right when it needed to, ain't overdo it neither. And the whole season stayed mild, wasn't no killing heat out there to knock nothing back. Ground just stay giving when the rain come right like that.

She still tell that story every chance she get, and don't nobody argue with her on it. It's a lot of people round here still measuring every other year against that one, like ain't nothing ever gon touch it again.
