I counted it twice, laid it out on the bed and went through it bill by bill, then I went and checked the coffee can too, case somebody moved it over there and ain't say nothing yet. Wasn't there neither.

So I start asking around the house, mild, cause I ain't trying to jump straight to nothing.

Me: Anybody been in that drawer this week?

The eldest: Not me, I ain't even thought about it.

The youngest: Ion know what you talmbout, I been gone all week.

Me: So don't nobody know nothing.

Then it hit me, wasn't but one person home that whole Tuesday, everybody else was at work or at they own place. So I go straight to that one, and this time it ain't mild.

Me: You was here by yourself all day Tuesday. Wasn't nobody in this house but you. Where the money at.

The one who was home: I already told you, I ain't touched it. I ain't seen it. I don't know what you want me to say.

Me: You finna sit here and lie to my face? It don't add up. It's nobody else coulda got in here.

The one who was home: So what you saying, I'm lying? I said what I said!

Me: I'm saying you the only one that was here! You been done spent it or what?

The one who was home: I'm not finna keep going back and forth with you about some money I ain't took!

Me: Then where it go?!
