I left later than I meant to and by the time I stepped out it was already darker than I like it to be walking home. Wasn't even that far in before I got that feeling, like somebody walking right behind me, steps landing almost the same time as mine. I ain't turn around though. First thing I did was slow my pace down, just a little, like I was checking my phone, and them steps slowed right on up same as mine did. Then I sped up, and don't you know they sped up too. I had done tested it twice by then, slow and fast, and both times it matched. That's when I knew it wasn't just in my head.

Ion do the shortcut when it's like that. The one that cut through past the lot, it be too dark back there and don't nobody be out that way this late no way. So I cut over to the main street instead, where it's a lot of people still out, where the gas station light be shining and cars still passing every which way. I let myself look back one time, one time only, and ain't see nothing but shadows and a long stretch of sidewalk. Couldn't tell you if it was somebody or wasn't.

That last block I was finna run for real, tryna act like I wasn't even hurrying, but I know good and well I was walking faster than I'd ever admit to at the time. Got to my door, got it shut and locked behind me, and I swear I ain't breathe right till I heard that lock click.

To this day I don't know if it was somebody or just some man walking the same direction. My workmate be like:

Workmate: You be talmbout somebody following you every time you walk home in the dark.

Me: Maybe so. But I'm telling you, that night them steps was matching mine step for step, and I still don't know what that was, and I been shook thinking about it since.
