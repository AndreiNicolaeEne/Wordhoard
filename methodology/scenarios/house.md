scenario: the whole saga of building or fixing the house
zones: past-narrative,numbers,body
intent: the one who did the work tells the whole saga of building or fixing up the house, start to finish.
beats:
- deciding the house had to be built or fixed, and why
- reckoning how many people and how many months it would take
- a wrong turn near the very start, work having to be undone and redone
- the day extra hands arrived to help who had not been asked
- the stretch of weather that stopped the work entirely for the better part of a week
- running short of what was needed partway through and making do with what was on hand
- the fall from height that came to nothing worse than a scare
- the argument over how one part of it should be done
- the wall that had to be rebuilt from scratch after it was found to be wrong
- the small win when the frame finally stood
- the last stretch, working on past dark for several nights running
- the day the work finally stood finished
- the tally taken afterward of how long it took and how many hands helped
- how it feels now to stand inside what was built
held:
- the reason the work began: the household had outgrown the old space
- reckoned at the start to take around eleven months and several helping hands
- the wrong turn near the start cost about two weeks of work done over again
- six different people helped at one point or another across the months
- the weather stopped work entirely for the better part of a week
- ran short of material partway through and made do with what was on hand
- the fall from height left nothing worse than a scare
- the argument was over how one particular part of the work should be done
- one wall was rebuilt completely from scratch after being found wrong
- the small win came the day the frame finally stood
- the last four nights were worked on past dark to finish
- the whole job ran to about eleven months start to finish, six hands helping in total
cast: the one telling it, who did the building or fixing; the people who helped along the way, kin and neighbors both, named only by their part in the work; whoever hears the telling stays out of scene.
text: You built or fixed up your house yourself, with help, and the whole job took about eleven months, six different people helping along the way. Tell the whole saga, start to finish: the household had outgrown the old space, which is why the work began; reckoning at the start how many people and months it would take; the wrong turn near the very start that cost about two weeks of work done over again; the day extra hands arrived without being asked; the stretch of weather that stopped everything for the better part of a week; running short of material partway through and making do with what was on hand; the fall from height that left nothing worse than a scare; the argument over how one particular part of the work should be done; the wall that had to be rebuilt completely from scratch once it was found to be wrong; the small win the day the frame finally stood; the last four nights worked on past dark to finish; the day it finally stood finished; and the tally taken afterward of how long it took and how many hands helped. Tell it the way you would to someone who was not there for any of it, all the way to how it feels now to stand inside what got built.
=== end ===

scenario: negotiating the rent down with the landlord, face to face
zones: money,numbers,face-work
intent: a tenant sits down with the landlord face to face to try to get the rent brought down.
beats:
- the small talk before the real subject comes up
- the tenant's case for why it should be lower
- the landlord's first refusal
- the tenant naming a figure
- the landlord's counter
- both sides careful not to give offense while pushing their own number
- how it ends, a number or a promise to think it over
held:
- paid on time for several years running
- one thing about the place that has never been right
- the tenant's opening figure asks for a fifth off
- the landlord's counter offers about half of that
- neither side raises their voice through any of it
- it ends on a figure somewhere between the two, or a promise to think it over
cast: the tenant; the landlord; they label their own turns however such people would address each other.
text: A tenant sits down with the landlord face to face to try to get the rent brought down. Set the scene: the small talk before the real subject comes up; the tenant's case for why it should be lower, paid on time for several years running, and one thing about the place that has never been right; the landlord's first refusal; the tenant naming a figure, asking for a fifth off; the landlord countering with about half of that; and the careful way each side avoids outright insult while still pushing their own number. Bring it to an end: a figure somewhere between the two, or a promise from the landlord to think it over. They label their own turns however such people would address each other. Write the exchange.
=== end ===

=== scenario 61 | house | M | m | control ===
scenario: instructions for the house, plants, and animals while away
zones: imperative,temporal,spatial
intent: instructions left for whoever is minding the house, plants, and animals during an absence.
beats:
- how many days the absence will last
- when and how much to water which plants, and where each one sits
- the feeding schedule for the animals and how much each gets
- the one plant or animal that needs handling different from the rest
- what to do about mail or deliveries left at the door
- which doors or windows to check morning and night
- what to do if something goes wrong that cannot wait
- how to reach the household if it is truly needed
held:
- gone for ten days in total
- the plants get watered twice across those ten days
- each animal is fed once a day, a measured amount every time
- one plant or animal needs handling different from all the rest
- mail or deliveries get left just inside the door, not out front
- the doors and windows get checked morning and night, both
- reachable only if something cannot wait for the return
cast: the one leaving the instructions; whoever minds the house, plants, and animals while they are away, addressee out of scene.
text: You are leaving the house in someone else's care for ten days and writing down what they need to know. Cover it all: when and how much to water which plants and where each one sits, watered twice across the ten days; the feeding schedule for the animals, once a day, a measured amount each time; the one plant or animal that needs handling different from the rest; mail or deliveries left just inside the door rather than out front; the doors and windows checked morning and night, both; what to do if something goes wrong that cannot wait for your return; and how to reach the household if it is truly needed. Write the instructions plain, the way you would leave them taped to the door or the counter.
=== end ===
