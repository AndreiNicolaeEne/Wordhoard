# Birth scenarios

=== scenario 1 | birth | M | m | corpus ===
scenario: the birth-room night, the labor and the first cry, told after
zones: body,past-narrative,kin
intent: a parent looks back and tells, after the fact, the whole night their child was born, ending with the first cry.
beats:
- the first pains starting that evening, mistaken at first for something ordinary
- the moment it became clear it was really beginning
- the hours that followed, waiting and pacing
- the point sometime past the middle of the night when it turned hard and frightening
- the extra pair of hands that arrived partway through to help see it through
- the last stretch, the physical effort of it
- the pause of several seconds that felt too long before the first cry came
- the moment, within a minute of the cry, of first holding the child
held:
- the labor ran about fourteen hours start to finish
- this is the second child
- the older child spent the night with a relative
- a stretch of several seconds passed before the first cry came
- one other person besides the parents was in the room throughout
- the hard turn came sometime past the middle of the night
- the extra hand arrived partway through, not from the start
- the first hold happened within a minute of the cry
cast: the parent telling it, addressed as you; the other parent; a relative who came to help through the night; the older child, elsewhere, out of scene.
text: You are looking back, now that time has passed, on the night your child was born, about fourteen hours from start to finish. Tell it the way you tell it after the fact: the first pains starting that evening, mistaken at first for something ordinary, the moment it became clear this was really it, the long hours of waiting and pacing that followed, sometime past the middle of the night when things turned hard and frightening, the extra pair of hands that arrived partway through, not from the start, to help see it through, the last stretch of effort, the pause of several seconds that felt far too long before the first cry finally came, and the moment within a minute of that cry when you first held the child, your second. Your other child was elsewhere that night, staying with a relative, so it was just the two of you and the one who came to help. Write the account as you would tell it now, after.
=== end ===

scenario: spreading the news of the child born this morning
zones: exclamation,kin,numbers
intent: a relative rushes to share the news that the baby arrived this morning.
beats:
- the messenger arriving too excited to knock properly
- the announcement blurted out at once
- the immediate excited questions back
- the details given, the hour it happened and which child this makes in the family
- the plan made on the spot to go see them right away
held:
- born this morning, just after six
- this is the third child in the family
- the messenger arrives before the household has finished breakfast
- the announcement is blurted out before a proper greeting is exchanged
- the plan is to go see them right away, not later
cast: the one bringing the news, a close relative; the one receiving it, an elder relative; they label their own turns however such people would call each other.
text: A relative arrives at another relative's door early in the day, too excited to knock properly, with news that the child was born that morning, just after six. Write the exchange: the announcement blurted out before a proper greeting is even exchanged, the immediate questions fired back, the details given, the hour it happened and that this makes the third child in the family, and the plan made on the spot to go see them right away, not later. They label their own turns however such people would call each other.
=== end ===

=== scenario 3 | birth | D | m | control ===
scenario: the argument over the baby's name
zones: negation,kin,interrogative
intent: two people go back and forth, unable to agree on what to name their newborn.
beats:
- one of them proposes a name
- the other turns it down and says why
- a second name is suggested
- that one is rejected too, for a different reason
- someone brings up naming the child after a grandparent
- that idea meets its own objection, since the family already has two people with that name
- someone finally asks outright who gets the final say
- the conversation ends with no name settled, both sides worn down for the night
held:
- three names are proposed across the argument
- the first name is rejected for one stated reason
- the second name is rejected for a different reason
- the third name is the grandparent's
- two living relatives already carry that name
- the question of who gets the final say is asked directly, not hinted at
- the argument ends undecided
- both of them are worn down by the end
cast: the couple; they label their own turns however such people would call each other.
text: A couple is going back and forth over what to name their newborn, and it is getting heated. Write the argument: one of them proposes a name, the other turns it down and says why, a second name is suggested and rejected on different grounds, someone brings up naming the child after a grandparent, that idea runs into its own objection because two people in the family already carry that name, someone finally asks outright who even gets the final say, and it ends with nothing settled, both of them worn down for the night. They label their own turns however such people would call each other. Write the exchange.
=== end ===

=== scenario 4 | birth | V | s | corpus ===
scenario: a lullaby for the child who will not sleep
zones: formulaic-verse,endearment
intent: someone sings a child down to sleep after it has refused to settle.
beats:
- the direct address to the child, using a tender term, naming the fussing outright
- a hushing sound or soft repeated phrase
- a promise that the singer will stay close through the night
- one steady image from the natural world, returned to more than once
- a refrain that repeats at least twice
- the closing wish for sleep
held:
- the child's fussing is named directly, not talked around
- the promise to stay close through the night
- one recurring image, returned to at least twice
- a refrain that repeats at least twice
- a tender term of address used for the child throughout
- the song closes on the wish for sleep, not before
cast: the one singing, close to the child; the child, unnamed.
text: A child will not settle and someone close to them sings to bring on sleep. Give the song the shape of a lullaby: an address to the child, using a tender term for them, that names the fussing outright, a hushing sound or soft phrase repeated like a held breath, a promise that the one singing will stay close through the night, one steady image drawn from the natural world that returns more than once to calm the child, and a refrain that repeats at least twice before it closes on the wish for sleep. Write the lullaby.
=== end ===

=== scenario 5 | birth | D | m | corpus ===
scenario: the new parents' exhaustion, confessed to a friend
zones: body,kin,tags-backchannel
intent: two new parents admit to a visiting friend how exhausted they really are.
beats:
- the friend asking how things are going, expecting the polite answer
- the polite answer given first
- the admission that it is worse than they are letting on
- the real numbers given, how many times up in a night and how little sleep is left
- a physical complaint added, an aching back or hands that will not stop shaking
- the friend reacting with real sympathy
- a small absurd mix-up confessed, something forgotten or put in the wrong place
- the friend offering to mind the baby for a while or bring food
- the parents, too worn down to keep refusing, accepting the help
held:
- up four or five times in a single night
- three hours of sleep in a stretch, if that, is what is left
- one physical complaint is named, an aching back or hands that will not stop shaking
- one absurd mix-up is named, something forgotten or put down in the wrong place
- the friend's offer is concrete, minding the baby for an afternoon or bringing food
- the polite answer comes before the honest one
- the friend's sympathetic reaction comes after the physical complaint is added
- help is accepted only after being worn down enough to stop refusing
cast: the two new parents; a close friend visiting; they label their own turns however such people would call each other.
text: A friend stops by to see the new parents and asks, mostly out of politeness, how things are going. Write the exchange: the polite answer comes first, then one of them admits it is worse than they are letting on and gives the real numbers, up four or five times in a single night, three hours of sleep in a stretch if that, the other adds a physical complaint of their own, an aching back or hands that will not stop shaking from tiredness, the friend responds with real sympathy right after, someone confesses to a small absurd mix-up caused by exhaustion, something forgotten or put down in the wrong place, the friend offers to mind the baby for an afternoon or bring food, and the parents, too worn down to keep refusing, accept. They label their own turns however such people would call each other. Write the exchange.
=== end ===
