beats:
- the traveler stops a stranger and asks the way, politely
- the stranger names the first turn to take
- a landmark given, the second corner, to watch for along the route
- a follow-up question about how far or how long the walk is
- the stranger answers with an estimate, about fifteen minutes on foot
- a quick thanks before the two part ways
held:
- the market as the destination
- the first turn given
- the landmark named, the second corner
- the estimate given, about fifteen minutes on foot
cast: a traveler asking the way; a stranger giving directions; they label their own turns however such people would.
text: A traveler new to the area stops a stranger on the street to ask the way to the market. The traveler asks politely, and the stranger names the first turn to take and points out the second corner as a landmark to watch for along the way. Asked how far it is or how long the walk will take, the stranger puts it at about fifteen minutes on foot. The traveler thanks them before the two go on their separate ways. They label their own turns however such people would. Write the exchange.
=== end ===
